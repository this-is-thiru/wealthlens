## Questions

- **Q1 — CG per category.**
  - **Q1a (recommended):** each `BucketOutcome` records its part, and the bucket's consideration, cost, improvement and
    expenses as computed. A source `FromCapitalGainsBuckets(parts, figure)` sums them. The CG node changes; outcomes
    grow four fields and a part.
  - Q1b: ManualEntry for consideration and cost; gains only, from the parts.
- **Q2 — deemed dividends.** The engine has one `DEEMED_DIVIDEND` kind; the form splits s.2(22)(e) from (f).
  - **Q2a (recommended):** `DEEMED_DIVIDEND` at 1a.ii; 1a.iii ManualEntry.
  - **Erratum (W-323 C4a, D-662):** `DEEMED_DIVIDEND` is the s.2(40)(f) / 2(22)(f) kind, a buy-back (Gazette 265620 p.10; `OtherSourceKind`), so Q2a placed it on the wrong row. It is now at 1a.iii; 1a.ii, a loan to a shareholder, is ManualEntry.
  - Q2b: both ManualEntry.
- **Q3 — PF interest.** `PROVIDENT_FUND_INTEREST` is one total; the form splits it four ways by proviso (1b.v – viii).
  - **Q3a (recommended):** the total at 1b.v, labelled "all four provisos together"; vi – viii ManualEntry.
  - Q3b: all four ManualEntry.
- **Q4 — CYLA / BFLA by head.** `HeadsAfterSetOff` keeps totals and the losses by head, not each head's figure
  after set-off.
  - **Q4a (recommended):** record each head's income after current-year and after brought-forward set-off in
    `HeadsAfterSetOff` (the set-off node changes), and fill the per-head rows.
  - Q4b: totals only; per-head rows ManualEntry.
- **Q5 — CFL by year.** The ledger holds losses by year in; the engine computes the year's carry-forward by head, not
  by year of origin.
  - **Q5a (recommended):** CFL's current-year row and total from the node; earlier years' rows ManualEntry.
  - Q5b: all ManualEntry.

**Proof at the build:** each new figure is checked by a persona derived by hand. That covers two properties (one let,
one own), mixed interest kinds, a CG persona with an equity sale (A2) and a brought-forward loss it absorbs (B-TI 8,
BFLA), and the loss persona for CYLA.
