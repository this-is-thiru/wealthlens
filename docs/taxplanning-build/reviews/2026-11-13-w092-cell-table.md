# W-092 — the cell table, drafted for the owner's adoption (TY 2025-26 ITR-1 and ITR-2)

Each row is a cell (W-092 C2a, D-654): defined once, placed by each form at the path its Gazette page prints.
Sources are the engine's nodes (`NodeIds`) or rules in the trace (C3a). **Drafted by Claude.** A cell becomes data only
once the owner adopts it. Paths and labels are from `sources/2026-11-13-itr-schedules-ty-2025-26.md`: ITR-1 is Gazette
271423 p.17 – 18; ITR-2 is 271435 p.41, 52 – 54 and 67 – 70.

Each mapping below is read from the node's own javadoc (`node/*.java`) and its TY 2025-26 rule rows. The 1961 Act
governs TY 2025-26.

## Open questions (need a ruling before the data is written)

- **Q1 — sums of figures the form does not print.** `SALARY_GROSS` (B1.i) is `salary.gross` + `salary.medicaltravel`;
  `SALARY_PERQUISITES` is three nodes; `SALARY_EXEMPT_U10` is two. As built, `SumOf` adds only cells the same form
  places (I21).
  - **Q1a (recommended):** also allow `SumOf` over **nodes** (a `FromNodes(list)` source, summing as `FromRules` does).
  - Q1b: hidden cells that a form "places" without printing.
- **Q2 — the s.17(1) / s.17(3) split.** The engine's components could be classed into s.17(1) and s.17(3)
  (compensation on termination, VRS, retrenchment: s.17(3)(i)), but no component carries that fact today.
  - **Q2a (recommended):** ManualEntry for both, with the guidance "split gross salary between s.17(1) and s.17(3)".
  - Q2b: a new component fact, as its own item.
- **Q3 — tax by basis.** D1 "Tax payable on total income" and B-TTI's slab and special-rate lines read `tax.bybasis`, a
  structured value (`TaxByBasis`: slab, and special rates by rule).
  - **Q3a (recommended):** a source `FromTaxBasis(slab | rule)`.
  - Q3b: ManualEntry for now.
- **Q4 — signs and losses.** "Enter nil if loss" (B-TI.2, 3c, 4a) and "payable / refund" (D13 / D14) are one figure
  shown in one of two cells.
  - **Q4a (recommended):** a cell's source may carry `"floorAtNil": true` for "nil if loss".
  - Payable and refund are two cells over `round.netpayable`, `"part": "POSITIVE" | "NEGATIVE"`.

Interest u/s 234A/B/C (D7 – D9) reads `interest.and.fees` items by rule (`INT-LATE-RETURN`, `INT-ADVANCE-SHORTFALL`,
`INT-DEFERMENT`). Those lines' outs in the trace are the interest, so `FromRules` serves once Q4's sign question is
ruled. HP's per-property rows, OS's items 1a – 1e and 2, and Part C's section-wise deductions come in the second pass,
after these rulings.
