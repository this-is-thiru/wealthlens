## What this settles, and what it does not

- **Settled:** the two Acts state the same exemption in the same terms; the only difference between them is
  numbering (S2 s.86 / 1961 s.54F) and the two ₹10 crore caps, which the 1961 section gained from 1-4-2024 and the
  2025 Act carries in its own sub-ss.(8) and (9). Both shipped years have the caps.
- **Settled:** for a sale bearing securities transaction tax, the gain is not recomputed
  (s.48's sixth proviso / s.72(3)(b), `CG-CHARGE-STT`), but whether that tax is *"expenditure incurred wholly and
  exclusively in connection with such transfer"* — and so reduces **net consideration** under s.86(10) / the s.54F
  Explanation — is **not stated**. The proviso's existence implies STT would otherwise be transfer expenditure; the
  Acts do not say so. This is OQ-38's question and it changes the exempt amount for every equity sale.
- **Not read:** any commentary, circular or ruling on the interaction between the s.86 exemption and the indexation
  measure W-088g added (s.197(3) / s.112(1) proviso). The plan takes the exemption off the gain and lets the indexed
  measure follow the same components, and says so as a decision.
