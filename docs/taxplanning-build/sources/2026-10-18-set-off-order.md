## The share's conclusions (not a decision — D-172)

- Q1 (OQ-28): neither 1961 s.71 nor 2025 s.109 prescribes an order between eligible heads; no rule, circular or ITR

## Reading

- **OQ-28:** no provision orders the heads (sources 4–6). Two Tribunal orders hold the order is the assessee's, one of
  them (source 2) on a single house-property loss and a choice of absorbing head. No High Court or Supreme Court
  ruling on the general point was found. Both judgments are Indian Kanoon copies.
- **OQ-29:** this year's set-off before brought-forward loss is now sourced (source 4, BFLA column 1). The order among
  years of origin, and of the attributable part, is prescribed nowhere and no authority gives the assessee a choice;
  *Eldorado*'s reason (s.71(1)'s *"be entitled to"*) was read on s.71(1) only.
