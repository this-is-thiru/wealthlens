# Holding period — short-term or long-term capital asset (W-088k1, 2026-10-21)

What W-088k3's `RS-CG-TERM` rows will be copied from:
- the period after which a capital asset stops being short-term;
- the assets with a shorter period;
- how the period of holding is counted for assets `portfolio` records;
- the assets deemed short-term whatever the period.

## Q1 — the boundary day (owner ruling D-303)

Both Acts: *"held … for not more than twelve months immediately preceding the date of its transfer"*. "Month" in a
Central Act means a month reckoned by the British calendar (General Clauses Act, 1897 s.3(35)). That is cited for the
reading below but not re-read this session.

Worked dates, computed (not reasoned):

| Bought | Sold | Days | (a) calendar months: long only after the same date 12 months on (`plusMonths(12).isBefore(sold)`) | (b) more than 365 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10-4-2024 | 10-4-2025 | 365 | short | short |
| 10-4-2024 | 11-4-2025 | 366 | long | long |
| 29-2-2024 | 28-2-2025 | 365 | short | short |
| 29-2-2024 | 1-3-2025 | 366 | long | long |
| 28-2-2023 | 29-2-2024 | 366 | long | long |
| 1-3-2023 | 29-2-2024 | 365 | short | short |
| **1-3-2023** | **1-3-2024** | **366** | **short** | **long** |
| 1-3-2023 | 2-3-2024 | 367 | long | long |

The readings differ only when the twelve months contain 29 February and the sale falls on the anniversary.
`portfolio` computes (a) (`HoldingPeriodResolver:115–116`).

**Ruling (D-303):** (a) calendar months — long-term only after the same date twelve months (or twenty-four) on; 1-3-2023 → 1-3-2024 is short-term. Owner, AskUserQuestion "Calendar months (Recommended)", 2026-10-21.

**Not raised:** the Acts date the period from acquisition to transfer; `portfolio` records trade dates, not settlement
dates. No rule here turns on the difference, and it is not ruled.

## The bonus-date gap (for W-088k2)

Row 4(f) / row 8 (C)(IV) count a bonus share's period *"from the date of the allotment"*. `portfolio` dates a bonus lot
at the corporate action's **ex-date** (`CorporateActionService:357`, `bonusSharesEntity.setTransactionDate(
corporateAction.getExDate())`). A demerged lot keeps the parent lot's date (`:571`, `setTransactionDate(
entity.getTransactionDate())`), which is row 4(g) / row 8 (B)(III)'s rule as recorded. The gap is W-088k2's to
decide: a fact to add, or an approximation stated in the row.

## For W-088k3

- **Both years, equity shares listed on a recognised exchange:** short-term if held for not more than **twelve
  months**; otherwise long-term. Unlisted shares and other assets: **twenty-four months**.
- **No transfer-date window inside either year:** row 3.
- **Deemed short-term:** row 5 / row 9. No engine part covers funds or bonds yet (D-302).
- **The boundary:** Q1, ruled (a) calendar months (D-303).
