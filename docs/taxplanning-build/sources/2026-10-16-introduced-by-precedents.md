the Act that set `effectiveFrom`. The method is as in `2026-10-16-introduced-by.md`: egazette by date bracket, with
a whitespace-insensitive "NO. n OF year" scan. egazette's numbers are not monotonic in date (2015's Act is at 163872,
below February issues), so windows were widened until a hit.
