## TY 2026-27 — Income-tax Act, 2025 (Gazette CG-DL-E-22082025-265620)

- **s.153, p.185** (Part *"D.—Deductions in respect of other incomes"*; marginal heading *"Deduction for interest on
  deposits."*), READ:
  - (1) *"An assessee who is–– (a) an individual, not being a senior citizen; or (b) an individual, being a senior
    citizen; or (c) a Hindu undivided family, shall be allowed a deduction from the gross total income, subject to
    conditions specified in sub-section (2), where it includes income by way of interest on deposits with–– (i) a
    banking company to which the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, applies (including any bank or banking institution
    referred to in section 51 of that Act); or (ii) a co-operative society engaged in carrying on the business of
    banking (including a co-operative land mortgage bank or a co-operative land development bank); or (iii) a Post
    Office as defined in section 2(k) of the Post Office Act, 2023."*
  - (2) *"(a) in case of an assessee mentioned in sub-section (1)(a) or (c), the whole of the interest up to a maximum
    amount of ₹ 10000 on deposits in a savings account, excluding time deposits; (b) in case of an assessee mentioned
    in sub-section (1)(b), the whole of the interest up to a maximum amount of ₹ 50000 on deposits in any account,
    including time deposits."*
  - (3), (4): no deduction to a partner, member or individual for a deposit *"held by, or on behalf of, a firm, an
    association of persons or a body of individuals"*.
  - (5) *"the expression “time deposits” means the deposits repayable on expiry of fixed periods."*
- **s.2(100), p.20**, READ: *"“senior citizen” means an individual resident in India who is of the age of sixty years
  or more at any time during the relevant tax year"*.
- **s.122, pp.158–159**, READ: (2) *"The aggregate amount of the deductions under this Chapter shall not, in any case,
  exceed the gross total income of the assessee."* (9) — income *"as computed under the provisions of this Act (before
  making any deduction under this Chapter)"* — is for a deduction *"under Part C of this Chapter"* only. s.153 is in
  Part D (OQ-25).
- **s.202(2)(a)(xii), p.227**, READ: the new regime's total income is computed without *"Chapter VIII other than the
  provisions of sections 124(1) and 124(2), or 125(2) or 146"*.
- **Finance Act, 2026 (egazette 2026/271439)**, READ: Part B (ss.35–122) amends no s.153, s.122 or s.2(100); s.35
  amends s.2 clauses (32) and (40) only; s.56 omits s.202(2)(a)(iii) (s.144). "80TT" occurs nowhere in the Act.

## TY 2025-26 — Income-tax Act, 1961

- **thc.nic.in consolidation** (`Central Governmental Acts/Income Tax Act 1961_.pdf`, 8,812,268 bytes, fetched
  2026-10-16; current to Act 7 of 2019), READ:
  - **80TTA, pp.424–425**, in Part *"CA.—Deductions in respect of other incomes"*: *"(1) Where the gross total income
    of an assessee (other than the assessee referred to in section 80TTB), being an individual or a Hindu undivided
    family, includes any income by way of interest on deposits (not being time deposits) in a savings account with—
    (a) a banking company …; (b) a co-operative society engaged in carrying on the business of banking …; or (c) a
    Post Office as defined in clause (k) of section 2 of the Indian Post Office Act, 1898 (6 of 1898), there shall …
    be allowed … (i) in a case where the amount of such income does not exceed in the aggregate ten thousand rupees,
    the whole of such amount; and (ii) in any other case, ten thousand rupees."* (2): the firm / AOP / BOI bar.
    *Explanation*: time deposits *"repayable on expiry of fixed periods"*. Footnotes: Part CA *"Ins. by Act 23 of
    2012, s. 31 (w.e.f. 1-4-2013)"*; the bracketed words *"Ins. by Act 13 of 2018, s. 31 (w.e.f. 1-4-2019)"*.
  - **80TTB, p.425**: *"(1) Where the gross total income of an assessee, being a senior citizen, includes any income
    by way of interest on deposits with— (a) … (b) … (c) …, there shall … be allowed … (i) in a case where the amount
    of such income does not exceed in the aggregate fifty thousand rupees, the whole of such amount; and (ii) in any
    other case, fifty thousand rupees."* (2): the firm / AOP / BOI bar. *Explanation*: *"“senior citizen” means an
    individual resident in India who is of the age of sixty years or more at any time during the relevant previous
    year."* Footnote: *"Ins. by Act 13 of 2018, s. 32 (w.e.f. 1-4-2019)"*.
  - **80AB, p.329**: the net-income rule is for *"any section … included in this Chapter under the heading “C.—Deductions
    in respect of certain incomes”"*, not Part CA (OQ-25).
- **Finance Act, 2012 (23 of 2012), s.31**, READ by eye in the reprint, Official Gazette, Government of Goa, Series I
