**Finding so far:** from 2019 to 2026, Chapter VII changed only for options and futures (serial 4, s.99) and for
unit-linked insurance (serial 5A, ss.97, 100, 101). Nothing touched:
- the delivery-based purchase or sale of an equity share on a recognised stock exchange, or its payer;
- the definitions of a taxable securities transaction or a recognised exchange;
- collection by the exchange.

Those rest on the pre-2019 text (row 1): REPORTED (1a, 1b, alike) and corroborated by the official amending Acts' quoted words (1c–1g); accepted by the owner as REPORTED (D-294).

## Q1 — "chargeable" and s.198's "paid" (owner ruling, D-294)

| # | Question | Status | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Does a side that is a taxable securities transaction under s.98 Sl. 1 or Sl. 2 — *"entered into in a recognised stock exchange"* and *"settled by the actual delivery or transfer"* — meet S2 s.198(1)(c)(i) / 1961 s.112A(1)(iii)(a)'s STT *"has … been paid"* when no breakdown records it? | Owner ruling (AskUserQuestion, 2026-10-20) | **Yes.** s.100(1): every recognised stock exchange *"shall collect"* STT from every purchaser or seller *"who enters into a taxable securities transaction in that stock exchange"*. s.100(4): an exchange that fails to collect *"shall, notwithstanding such failure, be liable to pay the tax to the credit of the Central Government"*. So STT on such a transaction is always paid. A reading, not a text that says so — every trace using it names this ruling. Exceptions to keep in data: s.113A (NPS Trust; an IFSC exchange with foreign-currency consideration, row 1f) and S2 s.196(3) |

**For W-088j2c:**
- A side is chargeable when it is a purchase (Sl. 1) or a sale (Sl. 2) of an equity share entered into in a
  recognised stock exchange and settled by delivery.
- A non-delivery sale is chargeable to the seller (Sl. 3).
- s.113A excludes the NPS Trust and an IFSC exchange with foreign-currency consideration.
- Both tax years have the same conditions: Sl. 1–3 and s.100 are unamended from 2013 to 2026 (rows 2–7), and S2
  s.196(1)(b) still cites Chapter VII.
- No rate is needed.
