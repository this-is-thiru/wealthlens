## Conclusion

Income from units of a Mutual Fund is not a dividend in either Act (U3, U5). No clause of s.92(2) or s.56(2) charges
it (U2, U4, U6 – U11). So it is charged by the residual s.92(1) / s.56(1) (U1, U4).

Family pension, commuted fund pension and a Government death gratuity received under other sources are likewise named
by no clause. They are charged by s.92(1) / s.56(1). Their *"one-third"* and *"the entire amount"* are deductions under
s.93(1)(d), (g), (h) / s.57(iia), with s.10(10A)(iii) and s.10(10)(i) excluding the latter two in TY 2025-26 (the rows'
`_note`s).

Interest on compensation is charged by s.92(2)(i) / s.56(2)(viii).
