## TY 2026-27 — Income-tax Act, 2025 (Gazette CG-DL-E-22082025-265620)

- **s.93(1)(d), p.141**, READ: *"for income in the nature of family pension (a regular monthly amount payable by the
  employer to a family member of an employee upon the death of such employee),–– (i) an amount equal to one-third of
  such income or ₹ 25000, whichever is less, where income-tax is computed under section 202(1); and (ii) an amount
  equal to one-third of such income or ₹ 15000, whichever is less, in any other case"*.
- **s.93(1)(e), p.142**, READ: *"any other expenditure (not being in the nature of capital expenditure) laid out or
  expended wholly and exclusively for making or earning such income"*.
- **s.93(1)(g), (h), p.142**, READ: *"(g) for income in the nature of commutation of pension received from a fund as
  specified in Schedule VII (Table: Sl. No. 3), the entire amount; (h) for income in the nature of gratuity as referred
  in section 19(2)(g), received on the death of the employee, the entire amount."*
- **s.19(2)(g), p.46**, READ: the Table Sl. 3 death-cum-retirement gratuity is one *"(A) received under the revised
  pension rules of the Central Government, or the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 2021; or (B) received under
  any similar scheme applicable–– (i) to the members of the civil services of the Union …; (ii) to the members of the
  all-India services; (iii) to the members of the civil services of a State …; or (iv) to the employees of a local
  authority"*.
- **Schedule VII Sl. 3**, READ: *"Any fund, by whatever name called, set up by the Life Insurance Corporation of India
  on or after the 1st August, 1996 or any other insurer under a pension scheme."*
- **Schedule III Sl. 15, 16, p.507**, READ: family pension of a gallantry awardee's family (Sl. 14's individual), and of
  the widow, children or nominated heirs of an armed-forces member who died in operational duties — excluded.
- **Finance Act, 2026 (egazette 2026/271439) s.44, p.43**, READ: substitutes s.93(1)(a) and s.93(2) only. Part B's
  sections (35–122) touch neither s.19 nor any other clause of s.93.

## TY 2025-26 — Income-tax Act, 1961

- **thc.nic.in consolidation** (current to Act 7 of 2019), READ:
  - **s.57(iia), p.310**: *"in the case of income in the nature of family pension, a deduction of a sum equal to
    thirty-three and one-third per cent of such income or fifteen thousand rupees, whichever is less. Explanation.—For
    the purposes of this clause, "family pension" means a regular monthly amount payable by the employer to a person
    belonging to the family of an employee in the event of his death"*. Footnotes: *"Ins. by Act 13 of 1989, s. 13
    (w.e.f. 1-4-1990)"*; *"Subs. by Act 26 of 1997, s. 20, for "twelve thousand rupees" (w.e.f. 1-4-1998)"*.

    Government or, as the case may be, the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, or under any similar scheme
    applicable to the members of the civil services of the Union … or to the employees of a local authority …"*.
  - **s.10(10A)(iii), p.72**: *"any payment in commutation of pension received from a fund under clause (23AAB)"*.
  - **s.10(18)(ii), (19), p.83**: the same two family-pension exclusions as Schedule III Sl. 15, 16.
