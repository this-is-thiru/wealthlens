## Method

1. Text extracted with pypdf 6.14.0. Every Income-tax Act amending clause was found two ways. The first was operative
   words ("N. In section X of the Income-tax Act"; for A10, its s.4 items "(N) in section X"). The second was
   marginal notes ("Amendment of section X."). Each marginal-note hit was assigned to its statute by the operative
   words on its page and the chapter heading over it.
2. For every amending clause on s.10, the set of clauses it touches was listed, and its end was checked against the
   next numbered clause.
3. Searched sections: 10(10), (10A), (10AA), (10B), (10C); 16(ii); 17(1)(vi); 17(2)(iii), proviso (vi) and its
   clause (vii); 22–27 (including 25A); 115BAC(2)(i); the Fourth Schedule Part A. "Fourth Schedule to the Income-tax
   Act" occurs in none of the twelve Acts.
