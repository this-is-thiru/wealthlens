## Reading (D-226)

- Both Acts aggregate across employers only for other gratuity and leave salary (S2 s.19(2)(a), (f); 1961 s.10(10)(iii),
  (10AA)(ii) provisos). Statutory gratuity (Sl. 5; s.10(10)(ii)) and another employer's commutation (Sl. 8;
  s.10(10A)(ii)) limit each payment on its own — **ruled** (owner, C1a).
- The commutation's gratuity condition names no employer (*"the employee has received gratuity"*; *"receives any
  gratuity"*): a gratuity from any employer counts — **ruled** (owner, C1a).
- Voluntary retirement (Sl. 12; s.10(10C)) and retrenchment (Sl. 10; s.10(10B)): the text limits each amount, but a
  lifetime ₹5,00,000 for voluntary retirement is widely stated and unread here — **to source** (D-227).

## Rulings (D-227, owner, 2026-10-18)

- **Voluntary retirement:** ₹5,00,000 per employee for the year — the year's payments together, in entered order
  (*"any scheme or schemes"* under one limit). Two schemes never give more than one limit.
- **Retrenchment:** per retrenchment — each employer's compensation takes its own least-of (*"at the time of his
  retrenchment"*; no once-only proviso).
- **Copies:** SAIL (Indian Kanoon, a learning copy) and Circular No. 640 (reported, 403 here) accepted as background;
  neither decides the two-employer case.
