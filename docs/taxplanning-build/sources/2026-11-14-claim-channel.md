**What follows (owner's ruling, session 97, option (a)):** HRA is claimable in the return because the exemption's own
conditions (C4, C5) do not include the employer, and s.392(5)(b) (C3) binds the payer only. That is an inference from
the two texts, not a sentence of the Act. `DR 22` C2's basis — s.392(4)(a) "at his option" — does not reach HRA (C1); an
erratum is recorded there.
