**Content changed at build from the adopted drafts** (each a legal statement read above):
- **Health, mistake:** the draft's *"claiming medical expenses and a premium for the same person"* is narrowed to a
  senior citizen (C11, s.126(7)); for anyone else the Act sets no such bar.
- **Health, step 1:** adds that a preventive health check-up may be paid in cash (C11, s.126(9)(a)).
- **Home-loan interest, step 2:** names the higher limit by `conditionsMet` (the rule's field for the s.22(2)(a) limit),
  with `anyOtherCase` and `aggregate`; the plan named `aggregate` and `anyOtherCase` only. All three are defined in
  both versions of `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-INTEREST-CAP`.
- **Leave travel:** step 1 says no assistance means nothing to claim (C4a); the last step covers assistance paid but
  taxed.
- **Basis references:** each 2025 basis ends with s.392(5)(b), as HRA's does, except home-loan interest, whose basis is
  s.392(4)(a) (C12).

**What follows:** for all six, the claim's own provision has no employer condition, and s.392(5)(b) binds the payer
only — the same inference as HRA's (C3 – C5), not a sentence of the Act; for home-loan interest, s.392(4)(a)(iii) is a
sentence of the Act.
