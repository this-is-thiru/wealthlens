## The amendment search

The twelve amending Acts after the thc copy (the list and numbers in `2026-11-01-land-sale-provisions.md`) were searched on
every page for sections 207 – 211, 218, 234A, 234B, 234C, 234F, and FA 2026 and TLA 2025 also for sections 403 – 409 and
423 – 428. The scanner was first proved on a known hit (FA 2020 s.27, `section 50C`, p.24). Hits other than rows 21 – 24 and
34 – 37: TOLA 2020 pp.4, 34 (pandemic-period relaxations, AY 2020-21 and earlier); FA 2022 pp.36 – 37 (s.140B and the
updated return's own interest rule); F(No. 2)A 2024 p.39 (new s.158BF, block assessments); FA 2026's "section 207 / 210"
references are the 2025 Act's special-rate sections, not advance tax. TLA 2025 (265618) is 3 pages; its headings amend 1961
ss.10, 16, 49, 80CCD and the Finance Act, 2025, nothing on these sections.

**Open:** 2025 s.423 counts "months" without "part of a month", so r.269(b) does not reach it by its words — **OQ-39**.
