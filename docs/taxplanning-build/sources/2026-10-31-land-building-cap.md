## What the two Acts say together

For a resident individual or HUF, a long-term gain from land or building acquired before 23-7-2024 and transferred on
or after that date is charged the lower of:

- **A** — the normal long-term rate on the gain: 12.5% (2025 s.197(1)(b); 1961 s.112(1)(a)(ii)(B)), after the
  shortfall shift of s.197(2) / the s.112 first proviso; and
- **B** — 20% of the gain computed with indexed cost (2025 s.197(3)'s formula; 1961 the same comparison against the
  Act as it stood before FA (No. 2) 2024, which for this asset was 20% with the second proviso to s.48).

Both years of the pack are the "on or after 23-7-2024" limb: a TY 2025-26 transfer (1-4-2025 – 31-3-2026) and a
TY 2026-27 transfer are both after the date. So the arithmetic is the same in both years; the enacting provision is
not (1961 s.112(1)(a) second proviso for TY 2025-26, 2025 s.197(3) for TY 2026-27).

Both Acts define the indexed cost the same way: cost × CII(year of transfer) ÷ CII(the first year the asset was held,
or the year beginning 1-4-2001, whichever is later). The index is the notified one, and the notification power describes
75% of the average rise in the CPI (urban).

**Not read here:** the notifications that fix the index for each year before 2023-24 — the pack's rule records those
for 2023-24 onward only (`RATE-COST-INFLATION-INDEX.json`, D-115); sourcing the earlier years is W-088g1's.
