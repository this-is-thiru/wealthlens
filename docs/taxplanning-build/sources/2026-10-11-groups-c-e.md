## Reading

By G1, the Income-tax Acts' references to the Payment of Gratuity Act s.4(2)–(3) and the Industrial Disputes Act
s.25F(b) are construed, for a separation or retrenchment on or after 21-11-2025, as references to the Code on Social
Security s.53(2)–(3) and the Industrial Relations Code s.70(b) (G2–G4). The Codes repeal and re-enact them "with
modification" (the notifiable days; the Code's own definition of "wages"). No different intention appears in 1961
s.10(10)(ii), s.10(10B) or 2025 s.19(1) Table Sl. 5, 10. This is the statutory basis for D-107, which the owner had
decided without it.
