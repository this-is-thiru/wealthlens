## What it shows

In both years the furniture addition is made to the value of Sl. 1 and 2 (the Government's, owned and leased
accommodation) and to nothing else. A hotel's value is its own column, with no furniture. `PERQ-ACCOM-FURNITURE`
therefore reaches `GOVERNMENT`, `EMPLOYER_OWNED` and `EMPLOYER_LEASED` (W-158b C2a, D-358).
