# Sourcing for W-088g1 — the Cost Inflation Index, 2001-02 to 2026-27 (2026-10-31)

**READ** means read in the primary document by this session. Text was extracted with pypdf 6.14.0; nothing was
executed. Every value below is copied from a Gazette copy held on the session's disk, whose hash is recorded so a later
session can tell whether it is looking at the same file.

The measure these years serve: `indexed cost = cost × CII(year of transfer) ÷ CII(the first year the asset was held,
or the year beginning 1st April, 2001, whichever is later)` — 2025 Act s.72(8)(b) and 1961 s.48 Explanation (iii), read
verbatim in `sources/2026-10-31-land-building-cap.md` rows 3 and 13. Nothing before 2001-02 is therefore ever needed.

- **A18** names P as principal — *"published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, vide number S.O. 1790(E), dated

- **A19** inserts **after serial number 18** but names *"S.O. 2413(E), dated the 13th June, 2018"* as the notification
  it amends. That is the drafting slip the 2019 copy carries: A18's own text says it amends S.O. 1790(E), and A20, A21
  and A22 each name **S.O. 1790(E)** as principal. Read together the chain is unbroken and P is the table.
- **A20, A21, A22** name S.O. 1790(E) as principal and insert after serials 19, 20 and 21 respectively.

Nothing in the six copies changes an earlier year's figure; each inserts one row.

## The base year

P's table begins at 2001-02 = 100. The reason is recorded, not assumed: the thc.nic.in consolidation of the Income-tax
Act, 1961, carries at s.48 Explanation (iii) a footnote that the words *"the year beginning on the 1st day of April,
2001"* were substituted for the 1981 base by **Act 7 of 2017, s.24** (`sources/2026-10-31-land-building-cap.md` row
13, read there). The 2025 Act says the same in its own words at s.72(8)(b) (same file, row 3).

**Not read here:** the Finance Act, 2017 itself. The base-year change rests on the thc consolidation's footnote, the
same standing the repo already gives it.

## What this leaves open, and what it does not

- **Covered:** every financial year from 2001-02 to 2026-27, each with a Gazette copy held and hashed. There is no year
  the measure can ask for that is not now sourced.
- **Not a gap:** years before 2001-02. The provision floors the divisor at the year beginning 1-4-2001, so an asset
  acquired in 1985 divides by 2001-02's 100.
- **Not settled here:** whether the pack gains a second index rule or extends the one `PerquisiteNode` reads. That is
  W-088g1's decision, argued in `plans/W-088g1.md`; the values above serve either answer.
