## Structure (F16-1 as amended by F16-2)

- **Part A** (pp.28 – 29), *"Certificate under section 203 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 for tax deducted at source on salary paid to an employee under section 192 or pension or interest income of specified senior citizen under section 194P."*
  - The header is a grid of headed boxes, not labelled rows:
    - "Certificate No.", "Last updated on";
    - "Name and address of the Employer/Specified Bank", "Name and address of the Employee/ Specified senior citizen";
    - "PAN of the Deductor", "TAN of the Deductor", "PAN of the Employee/specified senior citizen", "Employee Reference No./ Pension Payment order No. provided by the Employer (If available)";
    - "CIT (TDS)" with Address / City / Pin Code, "Assessment Year", "Period with the Employer" From / To.
  - Three tables. Unlike Form No. 130, they are in **Part A**, not Part B:
    - "Summary of amount paid/credited and tax deducted at source thereon in respect of the employee": Quarter(s); Receipt Numbers of original quarterly statement of TDS under sub section (3) of section 200; Amount paid/credited; Amount of tax deducted (Rs.); Amount of tax deposited/ remitted (Rs.); Total (Rs.).
    - "I. DETAILS OF TAX DEDUCTED AND DEPOSITED IN THE CENTRAL GOVERNMENT ACCOUNT THROUGH BOOK ADJUSTMENT": Sl. No.; Tax Deposited in respect of the deductee (Rs.); Book Identification Number (BIN), split into Receipt numbers of Form No. 24G, DDO serial number in Form No. 24G, Date of transfer voucher dd/mm/yyyy, Status of matching with Form No. 24G; Total (Rs.).
    - "II. DETAILS OF TAX DEDUCTED AND DEPOSITED IN THE CENTRAL GOVERNMENT ACCOUNT THROUGH CHALLAN": Sl. No.; Tax Deposited in respect of the deductee (Rs.); Challan Identification Number (CIN), split into BSR Code of the Bank Branch, Date on which tax deposited (dd/mm/yyyy), Challan Serial Number, Status of matching With OLTAS; Total (Rs.).
  - Then a verification.
- **Part B (Annexure-I)** (pp.29 – 31): "In relation to employees for tax deduction under section 192", "Details of salary paid and any other income and tax deducted".
  - Row A: "Whether opting out of taxation u/s 115BAC(1A)? [YES/NO]".
  - Rows 1 – 18 as F16-1 prints them (extracted text below), then F16-2's rows 19 – 21.
  - Column headings: "Gross Amount" / "Deductible Amount" for rows 10(a) – (j); "Gross Amount" / "Qualifying Amount" / "Deductible Amount" for rows 10(k) – (n).
- **Annexure II** (pp.31 – 33): "In relation to specified senior citizen for tax deduction under section 194P", rows A and 1 – 17. The printed heading is "Annexure II", without "Part B"; Note 5(iii) calls it "Part B (Annexure -II)".
- **Notes 1 – 7** (p.33). Note 5(i): each employer issues Part A for its own period. Note 5(ii): Part B (Annexure-I) *"may be issued by each of the employers or the last employer at the option of the assessee"*. Note 6: tax deposited is tax, surcharge and cess together.

## Savings — read (session 104, after D-740)

- **S2, Income-tax Act, 2025, s.536** (egazette 2025/265620, pp.491 – 492, 6,428,678 B):
  - (1): *"The Income-tax Act, 1961 is hereby repealed."*
  - (2)(b): *"nothing shall affect any right, privilege, obligation or liability acquired, accrued or incurred under the
    repealed Income-tax Act"*.
  - (2)(c): the repealed Act *"shall continue to apply … to any proceedings initiated on after the 1st April, 2026
    (including notices, assessment, …) in respect of any tax year beginning before the 1st April, 2026 and such
    proceedings shall be carried out as per the procedure specified in the repealed Income-tax Act"*.
  - (2)(j): a *"notification, order or rule"* under the repealed Act continues *"so far as it is not inconsistent"*, as if
    made under the corresponding provision.
- **S4, Income-tax Rules, 2026** (2026/271092, sha256 `af51e671…` matches the recorded hash):
  - p.1533: made under s.533 of the 2025 Act, in force 1-4-2026.
  - **No clause repeals or supersedes the Income-tax Rules, 1962.** A full-text search for "supersession", "except as
    respects" and "Repeal and sav" found none. "Income-tax Rules, 1962" appears only as the source of old forms to be
    attached (pp.1581, 1916).
- **Reading (INFERENCE, from the read text):**
  - A TY 2025-26 salary certificate is issued under the 1961 Act's s.203 and the 1962 Rules' rule 31 and Form No. 16.
  - The basis is the employer's obligation for tax deducted before 1-4-2026, kept by s.536(2)(b) and (c).
  - No provision says so in words. **Adopted by the owner (D-741, 2026-11-17).**
- **Name clash:** in S4, **"Form No. 16" is a different form**. p.1557 reads *"furnish to the donor, a certificate
  specifying the amount of donation in Form No. 16"*. A form number alone is therefore ambiguous across the two Rules;
  the year (pack) must always qualify it.
