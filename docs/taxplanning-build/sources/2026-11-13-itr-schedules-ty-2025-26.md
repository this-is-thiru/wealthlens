# ITR-1 and ITR-2 schedules for TY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) — Gazette text (W-092, session 91)

The forms' layouts, as the Gazette prints them, for W-092 (`ItrSchedule`, `FieldSource`) and W-093 (`ScheduleMapper`).
Both forms are the 1962 Rules' Appendix II forms as substituted on 30 March 2026, in force 31 March 2026 for AY 2026-27
(W-090d, D-652):

- **ITR-1 SAHAJ:** Gazette CG-DL-E-30032026-271423, Income-tax (Second Amendment) Rules, 2026, G.S.R. 226(E),
  https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/271423.pdf, English pp.15 – 19 (ITR-4 SUGAM follows from p.19).
- **ITR-2:** Gazette CG-DL-E-30032026-271435, Income-tax (Third Amendment) Rules, 2026, G.S.R. 227(E),
  https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/271435.pdf, English pp.38 – 71.

**How this was read.** Both PDFs were downloaded from egazette.gov.in on 2026-11-13. Their text layer was extracted
(pypdf) and is reproduced below by page, with the Hindi running heads and the page headers dropped. Pages 41, 42 and
52 – 54 of ITR-2 and pp.15 – 17 of ITR-1 were also read on screen in this session, and the extraction matches them.
A table's columns come out one cell per line, so a field's number can follow its label on the next line. Where
a wrapped label is unclear, the page cited is authoritative. TY 2026-27's forms under the Income-tax Rules, 2026
r.164 are not located.
