## What the Acts say

| # | Provision | Text | Level | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2025 s.72(1)(a), (b) | Deduct *"expenditure incurred wholly and exclusively in connection with such transfer"* and *"the cost of acquisition of the asset and the cost of any improvement thereto"* | READ | 117 |
| 2 | 1961 s.48(i), (ii) | The same two amounts | READ (thc) | 272 |
| 3 | 2025 s.90(1) | Defines the cost of **improvement**; it does not say what the cost of acquisition includes | READ | 134 |

**The search.** 2025 pp.115–140 and 1961 pp.270–305, every page, for `stamp duty`, `registration`, `brokerage` and (2025)
`commission` and `legal`. Hits: "stamp duty" only as the stamp-duty *value* or the valuation authority (2025 s.78; 1961
s.50C, s.56(2)); "registration" as the date of registration or a fund's certificate; "commission" in the University Grants
Commission's name; `brokerage` none. **Neither Act says which amount a property's expense is.** The later amending Acts were
not searched for these words: `2026-11-01-land-sale-provisions.md` searched them for ss.50C and 55 only.

## What this settles

Nothing by itself. The classification is **the owner's ruling, D-434 C1a, C2a**, the same footing as D-375 C2a for a contract
note's charges: paid on the purchase, cost of acquisition (2025 s.72(1)(b) / 1961 s.48(ii)); paid on the sale, transfer
expenditure (2025 s.72(1)(a) / 1961 s.48(i)). Rows 4 and 5 are consistent with that reading and are recorded only as what
was found.
