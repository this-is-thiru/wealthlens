# Statutory charge rates — STT, stamp duty, the SEBI fee and GST on the charge engine's statutory cards (W-163c, 2026-10-28)

This record is what the four statutory cards (W-163b, D-382) cite. Under W-163c C2a, an official copy was READ
wherever one could be reached. **READ** means this session read the document named, with pypdf text or, for a
scanned page, the image by eye. **REPORTED** means the fact rests on someone else's reading. Every rate below
**agrees** with the card that carries it. The window checked runs from 2025-04-01, the start of every card, to the
date of this record.

## Stamp duty: in force, and not changed since

- **Commencement:** FA 2019 s.11: Part I comes into force *"on such date as the Central Government may, by
  notification in the Official Gazette, appoint"*. S.O. 1226(E), 30-3-2020, egazette **2020/218957**, READ, under
  s.11 amends S.O. 115(E) (which amended S.O. 4419(E) of 10-12-2019): *"for the words and figures "the 1st day of
  April 2020", the words, figures and letters "the 1st day of July 2020" shall be substituted"*. No later deferral
  was searched for after 22-4-2020; the rates are in force before 2025-04-01 either way.
- **No later change to Article 56A**, READ in each Finance Act:
  - FA 2020 Part I (ss.142 – 144, thc copy pp.60 – 61): a s.9A proviso for IFSC exchanges and a new s.73B.
  - FA 2021 s.126 (egazette 2021/226208, p.66): s.8G, strategic sale.
  - FA 2023 s.166 (2023/244830, p.66): Article 47, insurance.
  - FA 2022 (2022/234693), FA 2024 (2024/252143), F(No. 2)A 2024 (2024/256436), FA 2025 (2025/262125) and FA 2026
    (2026/271439) do not name the Stamp Act.
  - Not searched: an Act other than a Finance Act amending the Schedule.

## Limits of this record

- **The SEBI fee between the 2019 amendment and the 2026 Regulations:** each amendment to the 1992 Regulations was
  not read. Both ends read ₹10 per crore. A secondary source (taxguru) reports the 2022 amendment
  (SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2022/73) as adding the Electronic Gold Receipt segment at the same rate.
- **The GST residual entry between 2019 and 2025:** each amendment was not read. The 2019 consolidated text and the
  2025 notification agree that item (vii) is the residual at 9 %.
- **The catalogue's own references** (`charge-catalogue.json`, `statutoryReference`):
  - `SEBI_FEE` cites "SEBI (Regulatory Fee on Stock Exchanges) Regulations, 2006". That is the fee the exchanges pay;
    the 2019 amendment above amends it separately. The turnover fee a contract note passes on is the Stock Brokers
    Regulations' fee (row 7).
  - `STAMP_DUTY` cites "Indian Stamp Act, 1899, as amended 2020"; the rates were inserted by the Finance Act 2019, in
    force 1-7-2020.

  Both are left as they are: they are not rates, and W-163c's plan does not change the catalogue.
