with a charge and a null or empty breakdown (`CapitalGainsSummaryService.java:95-96`). So **after W-348b every owner row with a broker
charge is refused**, not only today's §3.1 rows: under C3a the owner would re-enter every charged trade. Row 2 reads both numbers.
