## Plan for W-088a — `CapitalGainsNode`: the head per rate basis, and its set-off

**What this item is for (one sentence):** compute the capital-gains head from `EngineInputs.capitalGains` as gains per
(term, rate basis), set losses off within the head (S2 s.108(2); 1961 s.70(2), (3)) and from other heads into it
(S2 s.109(1); 1961 s.71(2)), never out of it (S2 s.109(2); 1961 s.71(3)). The surviving parts reach total income
and, for W-088b, the special-rate tax.

**This plan raises choices C1–C7 and one open question (OQ-32). Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (session 21, 2026-10-18)

- **The row (D-242):** *"buckets netted within the head by term …, no capital loss set off against another head …;
  the result carries the surviving gain per rate basis; wired into the set-off / gross-total-income chain"*.
- **The law** (`sources/2026-10-18-capital-gains-basis.md` rows 11–14, all READ):
  - **S2 s.108(2)(a):** a short-term loss *"shall be set off against the income, computed in respect of any other
    capital asset"*.
  - **S2 s.108(2)(b):** a long-term loss *"against the income computed in respect of any other long-term capital
    asset"*.
  - **S2 s.109(1):** another head's loss against *"any other head, including 'Capital gains'"*.
  - **S2 s.109(2):** a capital-gains loss *"shall not be set off against income under any other head"*.
  - **1961 ss.70(2), (3) and 71(2), (3):** the same (thc copy; unamended since).
  - **Neither Act orders the gains a loss absorbs.**
- **The rulebook:** `SETOFF-INTRA-CG-001` and `SETOFF-CG-001`, VERIFIED. `DR 13` R-7 sketches an absorption order with
  `CAPITAL_GAINS_SLAB` before `CAPITAL_GAINS_SPECIAL`, *"NEEDS VERIFICATION … whether the assessee may choose"*.
- **D-221 (OQ-28):** the assessee may choose the s.109(1) order (Tribunal: *Eldorado India*, *Nikhil Khandelwal*), so
  `SetOffOrderElection` prices every order. **Nothing rules s.108(2)'s order**, the case where a short-term loss meets
  gains on several bases.
- **The code:**
  - `SetOffNode` holds `heads` (salaries, house property, other sources) and `SETOFF-ACROSS-HEADS`'s
    `AbsorptionOrder` (the same three, declared). Its `_note` says *"The business and capital-gains heads are not
    built; their limbs bite when they are (W-085, W-088)"*.
  - It returns one `MoneyValue` total, read only by `GrossTotalIncomeNode` and three tests.
  - `NodeValue` is sealed (`MoneyValue`, `TaxByBasis`, `ComponentSet`, `SalaryBases`).
  - `TaxByBasisNode` charges rounded total income at slab rates only.
  - TS T6 `capitalgains.income` is ○.
  - The pipelines are in `law/regimes/*.json`.

### Findings

1. **The head must stay split by part `(Term, RateBasisHint)`.** W-088b taxes each special-rate part on what survives
   both set-offs, so `setoff`'s value must carry the capital-gains parts, not only a total.
2. **Within the head, the order a short-term loss absorbs the parts changes the tax.** Slab gains bear up to 30%;
   special ones 20% or 12.5%, the first ₹1,25,000 of listed long-term gains nil. No text read settles it.
3. **Until W-088b, a special-rate part reaching total income would be charged at slab rates,** a wrong figure, so the
   tax node must refuse one loudly.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the values:**
  - **(a) recommended:** two new `NodeValue` variants.
    - **`CapitalGainsHead`** (from `CapitalGainsNode`): the surviving gain per `CapitalGainsPart(Term,
      RateBasisHint)`, in the order of C2, plus the year's net short-term and long-term loss left (W-088d carries it).
    - **`HeadsAfterSetOff`** (from `SetOffNode`): the total, and the capital-gains parts after other heads' losses.
  - `GrossTotalIncomeNode` reads `total()`; the three tests are updated.
- **C2 — the order within the head:**
  - **(a) recommended:** a declared default on a new row, `SETOFF-WITHIN-CAPITAL-GAINS` (rulebook
    `SETOFF-INTRA-CG-001`), definition `CapitalGainsAbsorptionOrder`. It runs from the highest likely rate to the
    lowest:
    1. short-term slab;
    2. short-term listed equity;
    3. long-term other;
    4. long-term listed equity (its first ₹1,25,000 is free, so it goes last).

    A long-term loss takes the long-term parts in the same order. Every set-off line names the order, as `SetOffNode`
    does. **OQ-32** is raised: may the assessee choose the order under S2 s.108(2) / 1961 s.70, as D-221 held for
    s.109? If yes, an election over these orders is a follow-up row (W-088a2) you place.
  - **(b)** rule it now as D-221 did, and price every order here (an `Election` variant, about 100 more lines).
- **C3 — across heads:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `SETOFF-ACROSS-HEADS`'s heads gain `CAPITAL_GAINS`, last, in both years. A house-property or
    other-sources loss absorbs capital gains after the other heads, part by part in C2's order.
  - `SetOffOrderElection` then prices 4! = 24 orders.
  - A pack change, so a relock (law layer bump).
- **C4 — the guard (finding 3):** `TaxByBasisNode` refuses any non-zero special-rate part in `HeadsAfterSetOff`,

- **C5 — a net capital loss:** a MEASURE line *"not set off against another head (S2 s.109(2); 1961 s.71(3)); carried
  forward — W-088d"*. Nothing is carried yet; this year's tax is unaffected either way.
- **C6 — wiring:** `capitalgains.income` joins both regimes' pipelines before `setoff`, and `SetOffNode` requires it.
  In the new regime, the existing `ctx.inRegime` verdicts govern other heads' losses, as today.
- **C7 — records:** D-243; OQ-32; the pack row and its `_note` and `_sources` (sources rows 11–13); the relock; TS
  T6 (`capitalgains.income` ✅, the `setoff` value); WI.
