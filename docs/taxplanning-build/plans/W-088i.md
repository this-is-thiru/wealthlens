## Plan for W-088i — the house-reinvestment exemption (S2 s.86 / 1961 s.54F)

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a long-term capital gain on an asset that is not a
residential house is taken out of the charge, wholly or in proportion to how much of the sale's net consideration went
into one house in India bought within a year before or two years after the sale (or built within three years after),
unless the assessee already owns more than one other such house — and today the engine charges that gain in full.

sides, no contract-note charges recorded; a house bought 2027-01-15 for ₹60,00,000.

| gain | ₹80,00,000 | 1,20,00,000 − 40,00,000 |

**What the engine already does** (read, not assumed):
- `CapitalGainsNode:105-110` computes the gain (`CapitalGainsCharges.gainOf`), then the scope, then the term, then

- `CapitalGainsTerm:52-59` derives the term from `RS-CG-TERM` and passes it to a row as `capitalgains.term`, which

- `ItemFacts` throws on a fact the item does not carry, so the row's conditions may name only facts the node hands
  it; the window is therefore arithmetic over rule data, not a condition.
- `Money.scaledBy(Money numerator, Money denominator)` (`:116-124`) is the proportion, refusing a nil denominator,
  so a nil net consideration needs its own branch.

**What I am NOT doing (explicitly out of scope for this item):**
- **The deposit route** (2025 s.86(2)–(4); 1961 s.54F(4)) — a deposit made before the return as the deemed cost, and
  the charge on what is never used. Proposed **W-088i2**.
- **The charge-backs** (2025 s.86(6), (7); 1961 s.54F(2), (3)) — buying or building another house, or selling the new
  one, after this year: a later year's income on an earlier year's facts. Proposed **W-088i3**.
- **The ₹10 crore cap on net consideration** (2025 s.86(9); 1961 s.54F(4) second proviso) — it limits the deposit
  route alone, so it travels with W-088i2.
- **Houses outside `portfolio` as the original asset** — property and unlisted shares have no manager (the brief's
  M-08 gap); the condition that the original asset is not a residential house is rule data and is true of every
  asset kind `portfolio` records today.

**Risks or ambiguities I noticed:**
1. **The item is over the size envelope, and this is the split.** From the files above it is ~450 lines of Java plus
   ~250 lines of rule data — about one and a half items (§7). It does not split cleanly: the input record, the
   definition, the row and the arithmetic are one unit, and the bar cannot move out without a version that grants an
   exemption the law refuses. **Proposed:** W-088i is this item (purchase **and** construction limbs — the
   construction limb is a date test on the same record, not an item), with **W-088i2** (deposit route) and
   **W-088i3** (charge-backs) placed behind it. If you would rather it fit the envelope, the only honest cut is the
   construction limb as **W-088i1**, which saves ~40 lines and costs a whole item; I do not recommend it.
2. **`net consideration` and securities transaction tax — OQ-38.** The pack classifies each charge once
   (`RS-CG-CHARGE`: `TRANSFER_EXPENDITURE`, `COST_OF_ACQUISITION`, `NOT_DEDUCTED`), and `CG-CHARGE-STT` puts STT in
   `NOT_DEDUCTED` because s.48's sixth proviso bars it from the gain. But s.86(10) / the s.54F Explanation asks a
   different question — whether it is *"expenditure incurred wholly and exclusively in connection with such
   transfer"* — and the proviso's very existence implies STT would otherwise be. **I propose to read net
   consideration as the consideration less the charges the pack marks `TRANSFER_EXPENDITURE` only**, which leaves
   STT out and so understates the exemption by STT × the proportion for an equity sale. If you rule the other way,
   the change is a `transferExpenditure` flag on `ChargeDeduction` and the eight charge rows, plus its own item.
   **This is the one place where the figure depends on a reading, and I would rather have your ruling than mine.**
3. **No design document states this exemption's shape.** M-08 names it and nothing more. The shape in this plan —
   one entered fact per transfer, matched to the bucket by the transfer date, refused where the match is not unique
   — is mine, and worth your eye before any code.
4. **The exemption and W-088g's indexed measure.** A land bucket sold after 23-7-2024 carries a second measure for
   the 20 % cap. I apply the exemption to the gain and let the indexed measure follow the same components (it is
   computed from the chargeable gain), which exempts the same *amount* from both. The alternative — the same
   *proportion* of both — changes a rupee figure only for such a bucket. Neither Act says which.
5. **Matching by date.** A reinvestment names the original transfer's date; two buckets transferring on the same
   date make the match ambiguous, and the node will refuse rather than pick one. `CapitalGainsInput.Bucket` carries
   no disposal identity to match on instead.
6. **`EnteredFacts` grows a 40th component**, and every positional construction in the tests grows with it. The
   fingerprint picks it up unasked, which is the point of the seam.
7. **The one-year-before limb and the tax year.** A house bought before the transfer is a fact of the same year in
   most cases, but a purchase or construction *after* the year end, within the window, is legitimate and the fact
   carries dates outside the year; nothing in the engine refuses a date outside the packed year.

**Three things did not go as the plan said, and each is reported rather than quietly fixed:**

1. **The golden's figure was short by the nil-band shift.** The table above works ₹4,84,375 of tax and ₹5,03,750 payable
   from a ₹40,00,000 chargeable gain, but `RATE-CG-SHORTFALL-ORDER` reaches a long-term listed-equity gain and total
   income here is that gain alone, so the whole ₹4,00,000 zero-rate band stands unexhausted and comes off it
   (2025 s.196(2); 1961 s.112A(2) proviso) — the same shift the T13-1 golden applies, where 3,00,000 came off an
   11,00,000 gain. The gain charged is therefore ₹36,00,000, the special-rate tax 12½ % × ₹34,75,000 = ₹4,34,375, the
   cess ₹17,375 and **net payable ₹4,51,750**, which is what `golden/ReinvestmentExemptionTest` asserts. The step is
   worked from the rows, not from a run; the table above is left as written and this record corrects it.
2. **The row's cap was written a factor of ten too high** — `"newAssetCostCap": "1000000000"` (₹100 crore) instead of
   `"100000000"` (₹10 crore). The cap case caught it: 15,00,00,000 × 12,00,00,000 / 20,00,00,000 = ₹9,00,00,000 exempt
   where the hand-work says ₹7,50,00,000. Corrected in both versions, both packs relocked.
3. **`AssetType.LAND_OR_BUILDING` cannot carry the exemption's scope.** The plan's out-of-scope section says the
   condition that the original asset is not a residential house "is true of every asset kind `portfolio` records
   today"; W-088g2 has since added land or building, and the kind does not say whether a sale is of a house, a plot or
   a commercial building. The row therefore serves `capitalgains.asset = EQUITY` only, stated in the row's `_note` —
   an under-grant, never an over-grant — and the gap is placed as **W-088i4**.
