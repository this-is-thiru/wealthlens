## Plan for W-140 — boundary-case generator

**For:** when a pack moves a threshold, the build names every income boundary that now has no hand-worked golden case,
instead of the old cases silently testing the old law (`DR 15` C5; `16-getting-started.md:372`).

### What I read (session 48)

- **`DR 15` C5** (`taxplanning-design/15-review-findings.md`): the T1/T2 boundary inputs — at a threshold, one rounding
  step above, the crossover where a relief stops binding — are derivable from the pack. The generator yields **inputs**;
  expected figures stay hand-derived (`D-GOLDEN` not weakened). Acceptance (`16-getting-started.md:372`): "generated
  boundaries match the hand-written T1/T2 inputs exactly".
- **The hand cases** (`src/test/resources/taxengine/golden/T1`, `T2`; 21 files, each run in 2025-26 and 2026-27):
  rebate ceilings and +₹10 (`T1-N1`/`N2` 12,00,000/12,00,010, `T1-O1`/`O2` 5,00,000/5,00,010); the rebate relief
  crossover (`T1-N4a`/`b` 12,70,580/12,70,590); age-variant basic exemptions (`T1-A1a`–`f`); surcharge threshold and +₹10
  (`T2-1`/`2` 50,00,000/50,00,010); the surcharge relief crossover (`T2-3a`/`b` 51,61,190/51,61,200); the 5-crore band
  (`T2-4a`/`b`, `T2-5`).
- **Where the pack states each boundary** (timeline rule files): the step, `RATE-ROUND-TOTAL-INCOME`
  `rounding.toNearest` "10"; rebate ceilings, `REBATE-*` `relief.incomeCeiling` (1200000 new, 500000 old) and
  `REBATE-NEW-TAX-REGIME-EXCESS` `threshold`; surcharge, `RATE-SUR-*` `SlabLadder` band ceilings and
  `RATE-SUR-*-CEILING` `relief.thresholds` (5000000, 10000000, 20000000); age variants,
  `RATE-SLAB-FIRST-SCHEDULE-AGE-60TO79`/`-80` first-band ceilings. No figure is hard-coded: the sketch's `Money.TEN` is
  the pack's rounding step here.
- **No law is read or changed.** `BoundaryCaseGenerator` appears in prose only (`03-WORK-ITEMS.md:1619`).

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — which boundaries.** (a) The kinds T1/T2 already hand-work: each rebate ceiling and the step above; each relief
crossover (rebate, surcharge); each surcharge threshold and the step above; each age variant's first-band ceiling and the
step above — per regime, per shipped year. (b) Also every slab-band ceiling and every `Fixed` cap (C5's sketch): 20 slab-band
ceilings and 19 rules with a `Fixed` cap (counted over the timeline); only the
first-band ceilings the age cases sit on have hand cases, so most would wait on the awaiting list until worked.

**C2 — how a crossover is found.** (a) Bisection on the pack's rounding grid between the threshold and the next one,
running the real engine (`GoldenCaseRunner`) and reading whether the relief node is still non-zero: about 15 runs per
crossover, and no second copy of the pipeline's order. Its answer is checked against `DR 08`'s hand algebra
(12,70,580/590; 51,61,190/200). (b) Algebra over the shapes (`SlabLadder`, the relief shapes) composed in the
generator: a second copy of slab → rebate → surcharge → relief ordering to keep in step.

**C3 — what the build enforces.** (a) `BoundaryCoverageTest`: every generated boundary has a T1/T2 case with the same
regime, year, total income and age class — **or** is listed, with why, in a committed
`taxengine/golden/boundaries-awaiting-figures.json`. A pack change that moves or adds a boundary fails until a person
writes the case or lists it. And every T1/T2 boundary input is generated (the acceptance). (b) Only the acceptance
check: nothing fails when the law moves a boundary.

### Build (C1a – C3a)
1. Test sources, `taxengine/golden/BoundaryCaseGenerator`: reads the compiled pack per shipped year; returns
   `Boundary(regime, year, totalIncome, ageClass, kind, ruleId)`. Test-only: nothing in `main` would call it.
2. Crossover bisection through `GoldenCaseRunner`.
3. `BoundaryCoverageTest` and the awaiting-figures file, seeded with what the generator finds today without a case —
   expected: the 1-crore and 2-crore surcharge thresholds, their +₹10 and their crossovers, both regimes; the exact list
   is written from the first run and read by me before the report.
4. `03-WORK-ITEMS` W-140 row; the awaiting list becomes a placed row (hand figures to work), not a silent file.

### Build record

**Result:** the generator finds all 15 `DR 08` boundary cases in both years, the two crossovers included — 12,70,580 /
12,70,590 and 51,61,190 / 51,61,200, found by bisection through the engine and matching `DR 08`'s hand algebra. It
finds **46** boundaries with no hand case (23 per year): both regimes' 1-crore and 2-crore surcharge thresholds, the
step above and their crossovers (e.g. new regime 1,01,96,940 / 1,01,96,950); the old regime's 50-lakh threshold, step
above and crossover (51,95,890 / 51,95,900), and its 5-crore crossover (5,30,17,820 / 5,30,17,830); the old regime's
age-60 ceiling one step above (3,00,010). They are listed in `boundaries-awaiting-figures.json`; **W-140a placed** to
work their hand figures. The crossover incomes above are the engine's: none is a hand figure.

**Departures from the plan, each found while building:**
1. The bite check is a hidden hand case (T1-N1), not a pack copy with a raised ceiling: the packs are lock-verified,
   so an edited copy is refused before any boundary is read.
2. The first-run list is 46, not only the 1- and 2-crore points the plan expected: the old regime's 50-lakh and
   5-crore crossover and the age-60 step above have no hand case either.
3. Age matching is by the ladder the age falls in (the highest `AgeAtLeast` at or below the case's age), since the
   hand cases use ages 45, 65, 82 rather than the ladder's lower bounds.

**Tests:** `BoundaryCoverageTest` 3, about 1 second.

**Probes, reverted byte for byte (`cmp`):** step read as 1 → the generator cannot place the crossovers and the class
errors; crossover reported one step late → the `DR 08` check and the stale-entry check fail; T1-N1 hidden (source and
compiled copy) → the coverage check fails; a stale awaiting entry → the stale-entry check fails. A first run of two
probes printed nothing — the filter hid an error and the compiled T1-N1 copy survived — and both were rerun.
