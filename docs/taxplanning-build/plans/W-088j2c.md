## Plan for W-088j2c — STT chargeability decided on the tax side from rule rows

**What this item is for (one sentence):** where no breakdown records a side's STT (`UNKNOWN`), the engine judges from
that side's venue facts (D-295) and Chapter VII as rule data (D-294) whether the transaction was **chargeable**, and
`RS-CG-PART` accepts a chargeable side as the law and the Q1 ruling allow. That ends W-088j's blanket refusal for
trades whose venue is on record.

**This plan proposes a split (§7) and raises C1–C4.** No code until you approve.

### What I read (session 26, 2026-10-20)

- **The row** (`03-WORK-ITEMS.md`, W-088j2c), D-293, D-294 and sources row 8:
  - **chargeable:** a purchase (Sl. 1) or sale (Sl. 2) of an equity share *"entered into in a recognised stock
    exchange"* and *"settled by the actual delivery or transfer"*, and a non-delivery sale (Sl. 3, the seller);
  - **not chargeable:** s.113A's NPS Trust, and *"a recognised stock exchange located in an International Financial
    Services Centre where the consideration … is paid or payable in foreign currency"*;
  - **Q1:** a chargeable exchange-delivery side counts as "paid" for s.198.
- **D-295:** `CapitalGainsBucket.acquisitionVenue` / `transferVenue`, each `TradeVenue(exchange, segment)` as
  recorded; `UNRECORDED` on old rows.
- **The engine today:**
  - `CapitalGainsNode.factsOf` passes `stt.acquisition` / `stt.transfer` as `PAID` / `NOT_PAID` / `UNKNOWN` (W-088j).
  - `RS-CG-PART`'s five rows test those codes (D-290–D-292).
  - `BucketFacts` carries no venue (D-295 C5).
- **The repo's exchange vocabulary:** `NSE` and `BSE`. They are the only strings in the charge rate cards'
  `exchange` scope (`resources/data/charges`: 24 × NSE, 5 × BSE, 11 × null) and in `EntityExportService:82`.
  `ChargeScheduleEntity:67` says *"NSE and BSE levy different transaction charges"*.
  - What production holds is §3.4's query, which you run only at the deploy (D-287). So the mapping is built from
    sources and the repo vocabulary, and any other string stays `UNKNOWN`, not guessed.
- **Not sourced:** which exchanges are **recognised** under SCRA 1956 s.2(f) / s.4 (SEBI's list), and which are
  located in an IFSC (s.113A). R1/R2: a rule may not name an exchange as recognised from memory.

### The design

**1. Carry the venues into the engine.**
- `BucketFacts` gains `acquisitionVenue` and `transferVenue`, each a new engine record
  `BucketFacts.Venue(Optional<String> exchange, Optional<String> segment)`, `portfolio`'s strings passed as is.
- The adapter maps `TradeVenue` into it. That makes 8 components.

**2. An engine step derives each side's STT state from rule rows**, not a condition repeated in every part row:
- **Rule set** `RS-STT-CHARGEABLE` (`AT_MOST_ONE`, `REGIME_AND_CONDITIONS`), with item facts per side:
  `stt.side` (`ACQUISITION` / `TRANSFER`), `capitalgains.asset`, `trade.exchange` (the code, or `UNRECORDED`),
  `trade.segment` (the code, or `UNRECORDED`).
- **Rows**, each with a definition `SttChargeability(code)`:

  | Row | Conditions | Code | Law |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `STT-PURCHASE-DELIVERY` | side `ACQUISITION`, asset `EQUITY`, exchange ∈ recognised list, segment `DELIVERY` | `CHARGEABLE` | Sl. 1 |
  | `STT-SALE-DELIVERY` | side `TRANSFER`, … `DELIVERY` | `CHARGEABLE` | Sl. 2 |
  | `STT-SALE-NONDELIVERY` | side `TRANSFER`, … `INTRADAY` | `CHARGEABLE` | Sl. 3 |
  | `STT-IFSC-EXCLUDED` | exchange ∈ IFSC list (s.113A(b)) | `NOT_CHARGEABLE` | s.113A |

  The exchange lists are data, sourced in j2c1. Any side no row covers (an unrecorded or unknown exchange or
  segment) keeps `UNKNOWN`.
  - s.113A(b) also needs *"consideration … paid or payable in foreign currency"*, which no record holds. C3 decides
    whether the IFSC row may fire without it.
- **The step:** a new class beside `CapitalGainsNode` (`CapitalGainsStt`, named when built) asks the set only for a
  side whose evidence is `UNKNOWN`, per C2. The derived code replaces `UNKNOWN` in the side's item fact, and **a
  trace line names the row, the Chapter VII entry and, where it reaches s.198, the Q1 ruling (D-294)**.

**3. `RS-CG-PART` accepts the derived codes**, per C1(a), with a relock:
- `-SHORT-LISTED`: transfer `PAID` or `CHARGEABLE` (s.196(1)(b) says *"chargeable"*).
- `-SHORT-SLAB` and `-LONG-OTHER`: transfer `NOT_PAID` or `NOT_CHARGEABLE`.
- `-LONG-LISTED`: acquisition and transfer each `PAID` or `CHARGEABLE`. The acceptance of `CHARGEABLE` is the Q1
  ruling, in data, with the row's `_note` citing D-294.
- `-LONG-NOTIFIED`: transfer `PAID` or `CHARGEABLE`; acquisition neither `PAID` nor `CHARGEABLE` (S.O. 5054(E)(II),
  *"not chargeable"*).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what the step yields:**
  - **(a) recommended:** its own codes, `CHARGEABLE` / `NOT_CHARGEABLE`, with the part rows deciding what each
    accepts.
    - s.196's *"chargeable"* and s.198's *"paid"* stay distinct in data, and Q1 is a visible acceptance in the
      s.198 rows.
    - If Q1 is ever ruled otherwise, that is a data change.
  - **(b)** the step yields `PAID` / `NOT_PAID` directly. That is simpler (the part rows are unchanged), but it
    hides Q1 inside the derivation, where a reader of the s.198 row cannot see it.
- **C2 — precedence:**
  - **(a) recommended:** recorded evidence first. The step runs only on `UNKNOWN`, and a breakdown that says
    `NOT_PAID` stands.
  - **(b)** chargeability overrides a `NOT_PAID` breakdown (s.100(4): the tax is due however collected). That
    disputes the charge engine's own record; better as a later data-quality check than silently in tax.
- **C3 — s.113A(b) without the currency fact:**
  - **(a) recommended:** the IFSC row requires a fact no record holds (the consideration's currency). It is written
    with that condition, so it never fires today, and an IFSC side stays `UNKNOWN` and is refused. Honest, and
    ready if `portfolio` ever records the currency.
  - **(b)** treat every IFSC exchange trade as foreign currency. That is a reading; ask before it.
- **C4 — the split:**
  - **W-088j2c1:** source SEBI's recognised stock exchanges (SCRA s.4) with their stock-exchange status for equity,
    and the IFSC exchanges. Reading only, with a ChatGPT prompt if needed; the exchange lists are recorded with
    their sources.
  - **W-088j2c2:** the engine work above (1–3), both tax years, relocked. About 250–300 lines of Java plus rows and
    tests.
  - In W-088j2c's place, before W-088j2d. j2c2's plan is written when it is taken.

### Numbers I will use

None. There are no rates (D-294 row 8): chargeability, not the amount, decides.

### What I am NOT doing

- Recording the currency of consideration (C3).
- Overriding recorded evidence (C2).
- Off-market detection: `portfolio` has no "off-market" fact, and an absent exchange is unrecorded, not off-market.
- The user declaration (j2d).
- Any asset but equity.

### How I will verify

j2c1: the sources record (READ / REPORTED per row). j2c2 will name its own Verify, including bite probes on the
recognised list and on the Q1 acceptance.

### Post-deployment actions (R15)

None in j2c1. j2c2 changes rule data only; locks ship in the jar.

### Risks or ambiguities

1. **Until §3.4's query runs at the deploy,** the real exchange strings are unknown. The mapping covers sourced
   codes, the rest stays `UNKNOWN` and is refused, and §3.4 shows how much.
2. **Exchange strings in the wild** (`nse`, `NSE ` and the like): exact matching in data is deliberate. Normalising
   case or spaces would be a `portfolio` read-model decision; I note it, and propose nothing until the counts exist.
