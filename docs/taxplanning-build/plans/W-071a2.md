## Plan for W-071a2 — the Cost Inflation Index cap for continued accommodation

**What this item is for (one sentence):** value owned or leased accommodation an employer has provided to the same
employee since an earlier tax year — today refused (D-111 C5a) — by capping it at the first year's amount scaled by
the Cost Inflation Index, both years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6). C2 is a reading of the Rules' own words; nothing is coded until you
choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-071a2` row** (D-114). `DR 01` `PERQ-ACCOM-CAP-2026` (alias `PERQ-ACCOM-CONTINUED`).
- **TY 2026-27, S4 r.15(2)(d)** (Gazette 271092, p.1542, read here): *"Where the accommodation is owned or taken on
  lease or rent by the employer and the same accommodation is continued to be provided to the same employee for more
  than one tax year, the amount calculated in accordance with Table I: Sl. No. 2(a) or (b) shall not exceed the amount
  so calculated for the first tax year, as multiplied by the amount which is a ratio of the Cost Inflation Index for
  the tax year for which the amount is calculated and the Cost Inflation Index for the tax year in which the
  accommodation was initially provided to the employee."* **(g)**: the index is the one *"notified … under section
  72(8)(a)"*; *"first tax year" means the tax year 2023-2024, or the tax year in which the accommodation was provided
  to the employee, whichever is later*.
- **TY 2025-26, Rules 1962 r.3(1) third proviso and Explanation 2** (G.S.R. 615(E), p.6; the consolidated rule 3, V1
  pp.1–2): the same words with "previous year"; *"owned [or taken on lease or rent]"* — the bracket inserted by
  **corrigendum G.S.R. 636(E)** (T6, READ); the index *"notified … under clause (v) of Explanation to section 48"*;
  "first previous year" 2023-2024 or later.
- **The indices** (all READ in the Gazette): **348** for 2023-24 (S.O. 2571(E), U1), **363** for 2024-25 (S.O.
  2103(E), T4), **376** for 2025-26 (S.O. 2954(E), U2) — 1961 s.48 Expl. (v), table of S.O. 1790(E); **384** for
  2026-27 (S.O. 3889(E), T5) — 2025 Act s.72(8)(a). The 2026 notification lists 2026-27 alone; 2025 Act s.536(2)(j)
  keeps a 1961 notification in force as if made under the 2025 provision (READ, retirement-limits A4).
- **Code:** `PerquisiteNode.requireNoIndexCap` (`PerquisiteNode.java:165`) refuses owned or leased accommodation
  whose `EmployerAccommodation.firstProvidedIn` is before the tax year; the rent is taken off after the Table value;
  furniture is its own line (`PERQ-ACCOM-FURNITURE`). No rule shape or definition holds a table of values by year.

### Findings

1. **Two years in the formula, which may differ.** The amount scaled is the **first tax year's** (2023-24 or later),
   but the divisor is the index for the year the accommodation was **initially provided**. For accommodation first
   provided in 2023-24 or later the two are the same year. For accommodation provided before 2023-24 they differ:
   read literally, a 2023-24 amount is scaled by this year's index over an older, lower one, which inflates rather
   than caps.
   No text resolves it; the older indices are not read either.
2. **The first year's amount is a figure from another year** — its salary, band, rental. The engine sees only this
   year's facts.
3. **"The amount calculated in accordance with Sl. 2(a) or (b)"** — Table I column 3 ends *"as reduced by the rent,
   if any, actually paid"*, so the Sl. amount is after rent; furniture is column 4 / sub-rule (e), outside the Sl.
4. **The index is a table by year**, the first data of its kind in the pack; the TY 2026-27 pack needs 2023-24 to
   2026-27, the TY 2025-26 pack 2023-24 to 2025-26.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the first year's amount:** `EmployerAccommodation` gains **`firstYearValue`** (`Optional<Money>`): the
  amount calculated under Sl. 2(a)/(b) for the first tax year, after rent, as the employer computed it. Required for
  owned or leased accommodation first provided before this year; refused elsewhere.
- **C2 — accommodation first provided before 2023-24 (finding 1):**
  - **(a) recommended:** still refused, naming the question — proposed **OQ-11** (which year's index divides). Built:
    first provided in 2023-24 or later, where both readings agree.
  - **(b)** Divide by the first tax year's index (the purposive reading). Builds every case; a reading.
  - **(c)** The literal text: needs the pre-2023-24 indices (unread) and inflates.
- **C3 — what is capped (finding 3):** the Sl. 2(a)/(b) value **after rent**; the cap binds it at
  `firstYearValue × index(this year) / index(first year)`; furniture stays outside. The trace line names which bound.
- **C4 — the index as data:** a new `RuleDefinition`, **`IndexByYear`** (a year → integer table), on a new row
  **`RATE-COST-INFLATION-INDEX`**, one version per tax year: TY 2025-26 {2023-24: 348, 2024-25: 363, 2025-26: 376};
  TY 2026-27 the same plus {2026-27: 384}. A year the table lacks is refused, never read as zero. A new definition is
  a new class and a new permit, as `RuleDefinition` intends.
- **C5 — the cap as a rule:** a row **`PERQ-ACCOM-CONTINUED`** (both years) whose cap is the scaled first-year
  amount — `PercentOfBase` 100% of a base the node computes (`firstYearValue × ratio`) — so the line cites its own
  source. Its `whatToCheck` for TY 2025-26 names the consolidation (V1) and the three unread Acts, as the other rows.
- **C6 — scope:** TY 2025-26 covers owned **and leased** (G.S.R. 636(E)), correcting W-071a's "owned only" reading;
  the cap row applies to both in both years.

## As built — departures from the plan (for review)
- **C2a without a year in code:** accommodation first provided in a year the index table does not hold is refused
  naming OQ-11. The table starts at 2023-24 — the first tax year the Rules name — so every earlier year is refused,
  and no year is written into the node.
- **`Money.scaledBy(numerator, denominator)`** is new: the index ratio may exceed one, which `apportioned` refuses.
  It divides once and rounds once to paise, as `apportioned` does.
- **`IndexByYear`** is the sixth `RuleDefinition`; the six existing switches over definitions gained a refusing case.
  JSON: `{"type": "IndexByYear", "years": [{"financialYear": "2023-24", "index": 348}, …]}` — a misnamed or repeated
  year is refused. `TaxYearId` is not `Comparable`, so the table is an unordered map and the refusal lists its years
  in order.
- **Coverage:** only s.17 gains `PERQ-ACCOM-CONTINUED`. 1961 s.48 and 2025 s.72 stay `PLANNED` (capital gains unbuilt);
  the index row is read by the cap alone.
- **The TY 2026-27 index version is marked retrospective** — S.O. 3889(E) was notified 15-7-2026 and applies from
  1-4-2026, and `AmendmentRef` requires the flag to match the dates.
- **A mishap, repaired before anything was committed:** the first checksum run failed (the unordered-map bug above)
  and the lock script, run on its empty output, emptied both locks' rule maps. Both locks were restored from the
  committed W-071c state and relocked from a good run; the script now aborts on an empty run.
- **Locks:** law 2025-26.law.22 / 2026-27.law.29; exec unchanged.
