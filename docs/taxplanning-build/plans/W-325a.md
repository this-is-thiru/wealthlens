## Plan for W-325a — explanations for the salary lines (F37.2)

`content/explanations.json`. That covers perquisites, salary bases, retirement payments, allowances, gross salary,
tax on employment and the employer-contribution charges, so their trace lines read as plain language instead of a
rule ID.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: rows that are an exclusion.** These are `PERQ-CLUB-BUSINESS`, `PERQ-TELEPHONE`, `PERQ-MEAL-REMOTE`, `PERQ-CAR-PATH-OFFICIAL`, `PERQ-EDUCATION-FREE`, `PERQ-LOAN-PETTY`, `PERQ-ASSET-USE-EXCLUDED`, `PERQ-SPECIFIED-EMPLOYEE` and similar.
  - **C1a (recommended):** their `what` says the benefit is not taxed as a perquisite, and names no figure. Their `out` is nil, and a nil printed as "₹0" adds nothing.
  - C1b: the same sentence shape as a valued perquisite ("valued at ₹0").
- **C2: the "why" for these rows.** Their `limitedBy` is engine text, not a limit name, e.g. *"CLUB, less the recovery 0.00"* or *"not a perquisite: excluded by PERQ-CLUB-BUSINESS"*. Today it prints after the sentence as written: codes, and figures unformatted.
  - **C2a (recommended):** leave it as written in this item, and place **W-327**. That item would template each computed `limitedBy` (component codes in words, figures through `inIndianNotation`) by giving the nodes structured reasons instead of strings. It is an engine change, too large to fold into a content batch.
  - C2b: hide `limitedBy` for rows with no limits. That hides the reason a figure is what it is, so it goes against F37.3.
