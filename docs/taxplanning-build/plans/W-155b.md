## Plan for W-155b — the voluntary-retirement bars by rule data

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** the two bars on the voluntary-retirement deduction —
none after relief for arrears on the amount, and none once a deduction under the entry was allowed in another tax
year — are today `RetirementNode.voluntaryRetirementBar`'s `entry ==` test and two Java branches. This item makes them
rows of an ordered set, reached by the payment's facts, with the once-only history kept per entry (D-336 R3, C2a;
audit P5 `RetirementNode:239–250`).

**This plan raises C1 – C2; C2 changes two test assertions and asks your approval for them (R3).**

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the bars' shape:**
  - **(a) recommended: an ordered set of conditions-only rows.** `RS-RET-BAR` (`ALL_THAT_APPLY`, ground `REGIME`,
    ordered by `ruleSetPosition`). The node takes the first row, in the set's order, whose conditions about the item
    hold, as `SpecifiedEmployee.gates` tests a row's item conditions.
    1. `RET-BAR-ARREARS-RELIEF`: `retirement.entry = VOLUNTARY_RETIREMENT`, `retirement.arrears.relief.allowed =
       true` (s.19(2)(e)(iii); 1961 third proviso).
    2. `RET-BAR-ONCE-ONLY`: `retirement.entry = VOLUNTARY_RETIREMENT`, `retirement.allowed.in.another.year = true`
       (s.19(2)(e)(ii); 1961 second proviso).

    Both rows take their provisions and provenance from `RET-VOLUNTARY-RETIREMENT`. The order is today's (arrears
    first), now data. A barred payment is a nil line on its entry row, as today, naming the bar.
  - **(b)** an `AT_MOST_ONE` set with a `Not` on the second row so the two never overlap (W-154d's club pattern).
    That works, but it states a precedence as a negated condition rather than as an order.
- **The facts (C2a of D-336):**
  - `retirement.arrears.relief.allowed` is the payment's own field.
  - `retirement.allowed.in.another.year` is computed from the ledger, which becomes
    `deductionAllowedIn: Map<RetirementEntry, TaxYearId>` in place of `vrsClaimedIn`. It is true where the map holds
    the payment's entry with a year other than this one. No entry test is left.
  - `vrsAlreadyClaimed()` goes; it has no caller.
- **C2 — the nil line's words:**
  - **(a) recommended:** "barred by <bar row>", plus the ledger's year where the entry has one ("…; a deduction
    under this entry was allowed in 2021-22"). The year is a fact of the payment's history, written for any bar, not
    picked per bar.
    - `:149` keeps "2021-22" and gains the row id `RET-BAR-ONCE-ONLY`.
    - `:178`'s "relief" **changes** to the row id `RET-BAR-ARREARS-RELIEF`, because the generic words no longer say
      "relief". This is an assertion changed, not weakened, and shown here for approval (R3).
  - **(b)** keep today's words, chosen per bar in Java. That puts the bar's law back into strings.
