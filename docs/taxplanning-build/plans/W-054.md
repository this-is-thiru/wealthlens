### Findings

1. **Surcharge isn't a marginal ladder.** One rate, chosen by the band the income falls in, applies to
   the whole tax. `SlabLadder.apply` would be wrong for it; `W-056` adds the band lookup the row plans.
2. **Two income tests.** Clauses (i)/(ii)/(iv)/(v) test total income **including** dividends and the
   special gains; the 25% and 37% clauses test it **excluding** them. One ladder on one base can't state
   both. The clauses compose exactly as the **greater** of two ladders' rates:
   - excluding-income above 2Cr → 25% or 37%, which beats the 15%;
   - otherwise the including-income ladder's rate, where "not covered" → 15% is that ladder's 1Cr+ band
     continuing.
3. **Invariant I3 conflicts with a data-only item.** These rows have no node until `W-056`, so
   `rulesNotEvaluatedByAnyNode` would list them. D-043 says the pending list "may only shrink", and
   `W-055`'s relief rows have the same problem.
4. **Both years are one instrument.** FA 2026 states all four tables (s.2 for AY 2026-27, s.3 for TY
   2026-27), with identical figures, so each row is **one spanning version** from 2025-04-01 (the
   `W-052c` rule).
5. **Standing rules.** These are Finance Act provisions, not Act sections: no coverage or catalogue
   change. Both years are covered by finding 4; concept IDs are as C2.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the shape (findings 1–2).**
  - **(a) recommended:** per regime, **two rows**, each a `SlabLadder` whose bands are the Act's
    clauses:
    - on **total income**: (0, 50L] 0%, (50L, 1Cr] 10%, (1Cr, open) 15% (clauses (i), (ii), and (iv) or
      (v) "not covered → 15%");
    - on **total income excluding dividends and the special gains**, new base
      `TOTAL_INCOME_EXCLUDING_DIVIDENDS_AND_GAINS`: first schedule (0, 2Cr] 0%, (2Cr, 5Cr] 25%, (5Cr,
      open) 37%; new regime (0, 2Cr] 0%, (2Cr, open) 25%.
    - `W-056` takes the **greater** of the two rates (finding 2) and adds the band lookup. The "15% on
      that part" limit (`RATE-SUR-SPLIT-001`) stays `W-056`'s, with its bucket logic.
  - **(b)** One ladder per regime on total income alone (10/15/25/37). It's simpler, but it states the
    25%/37% test on the wrong income, which becomes wrong the day capital gains arrive (`W-088`).
- **C2 — IDs:**
  - `RATE-SUR-FIRST-SCHEDULE` and `RATE-SUR-NEW-TAX-REGIME` (from the `W-045d2` map) for the
    total-income ladders;
  - `RATE-SUR-FIRST-SCHEDULE-EXCLUDING` and `RATE-SUR-NEW-TAX-REGIME-EXCLUDING` for the other two.
  - Regimes: `OnlyIn` their regime; no conditions (Table 1 Sl. 1 names the persons; `HasPersonType`
    isn't needed while both regimes serve individuals only).
  - Provisions: `provision1961` FA 2026 s.2(4) Table Sl. 10 / Part I-A Para F Table 1 Sl. 1;
    `provision2025` s.3(4) Table Sl. 10 / Part I-B Para F Table 1 Sl. 1.
- **C3 — I3 (finding 3).**
  - **(a) recommended:** an **explicit, owner-approved exception to D-043's "may only shrink"**. The
    four rows (and `W-055`'s) join `NOT_YET_EVALUATED` with a comment "evaluated by `W-056` (D-084)",
    and `W-056`'s Verify asserts the set is empty again. A pack row without its node is then visible and
    time-boxed.
  - **(b)** Merge `W-054` and `W-055` into `W-056`, so rows and node arrive together. The invariant
    stays whole, but it's one large item.
- **C4 — the lock:** `2025-26` → `law.3`, `2026-27` → `law.12`; `RuleIds` regenerated; the inventory
  tests (10 → 14 rules).
