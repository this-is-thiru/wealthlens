## Plan for W-238j — dearness allowance in the provident fund's base

**For:** a user whose dearness allowance counts for retirement. Today, naming `BASIC` movable with a provident fund
contribution entered is refused, because nothing says whether the contribution is measured on basic alone or on
basic + DA. Row `03-WORK-ITEMS.md` W-238j (W-238d finding, C3a). Design: `03-advisory.md` A2.2 C8.

### What I read (session 73)

- **The refusal.** It fires where a share follows basic, DA is entered, and DA counts for retirement
  (`taxengine/optimise/AllocationPricing.java:155-159`, narrowed by W-238i).
- **The ratio.** It is taken over basic alone: `amountAt` = entered × basic′ ÷ basic, and `ceiling` uses the same
  denominator (`optimise/Following.java:37-57`). Nothing else reads `leaderEntered` (grep).
- **Two existing DA facts, and why neither answers the question:**
  - `termsIncludeDearnessAllowance` is the tax "salary" test: DA "if the terms of employment so provide". The RPF's
    twelve per cent limb reads it (`law/rules/SAL-SALARY-FOR-RPF.json:28`, `WHERE_TERMS_PROVIDE`).
  - `dearnessAllowanceCountsForRetirement` is the accommodation-perquisite test (`input/Employment.java:18-20`).
  - Both say how the law measures salary. Neither says what the employer actually pays its contribution on. W-238d
    refused rather than read one as the other.
- **A gap I found.** `DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE` is `SALARY` (`exec/components/DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE.json:6`), so it can be
  named movable today. If the contribution is measured on basic + DA, moving DA should move the contribution too, but
  only `BASIC` leads (`AllocationPricing.java:125-160`). Today that answer is silently wrong.
- **Placement.** W-238i put the provident fund terms on the request (`api/ProvidentFundTerms`). This new fact sits
  beside them, for the same reason: drafts store `EnteredFacts` whole, and a new primitive on `Employment` would
  stop old drafts from loading (D-498).

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1: the fact.**
- **(a) Recommended: a new optional fact, `ProvidentFundTerms.baseIncludesDearnessAllowance`** (`Optional<Boolean>`).
  It applies to both shares, since they are measured on the same wage. The request gains an optional
  `providentFundBaseIncludesDearnessAllowance`.
  - Where it is absent: today's behaviour. Refused where DA counts for retirement and a share follows; otherwise basic
    alone.
  - Where it is stated: it decides, whatever the retirement flag says.
- (b) Read `termsIncludeDearnessAllowance` as the answer. No new fact, but it treats the tax definition as the
  employer's payroll practice, which is the guess W-238d declined to make.

**C2: DA itself movable.**
- **(a) Recommended: refuse `DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE` as movable where a share follows and DA is in the base** (stated
  true, or absent with DA counting for retirement). This closes the silent gap above. I'll place a row for DA leading
  as well, so it isn't lost.
- (b) Let DA lead too in this item: `Following` becomes a base of several leaders, each with its own ceiling. That
  is wider than the row asks for.

**C3: refusals for a fact that does nothing.** **(a) Recommended, matching W-238i C3a:** a stated
`baseIncludesDearnessAllowance` is refused where `BASIC` is not movable, where no share follows, or where no DA is
entered. In each case it would change nothing.
