## Plan for W-327d1 — a plain "why" for house property and set-off

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** each of the 21 house-property and set-off sites publishes its
"why" as a reason with facts, and the content words it. Heads of income and capital-gains parts get plain words in the
content, because the set-off sentences name them.

**Probe (no code run):**
- **No test compares whole lines.** No test compares whole trace lines (searched for W-327b2), so no test moves.
- **Every site read.** I read each site, and the facts its text holds.
- **The set-off texts name heads and parts:**
  - Today's text reads "HOUSE_PROPERTY loss against SALARIES; order […]" and "short-term loss of 2023-24 against SHORT_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY".
  - `HeadCode` is a five-value enum (`rules/condition/HeadCode.java:17`).
  - `CapitalGainsPart` is open data: each year's `CG-PARTS` declares the parts (four today: `SHORT_TERM_SLAB`, `SHORT_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY`, `LONG_TERM_OTHER`, `LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY`).
- **The words already in the content:** "income from house property", "income from other sources", and "gain on listed shares and equity funds".

The set-off order and its basis (elected or the default) stay in `limitedBy`.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: words for heads and parts.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** content `terms.head` (all five `HeadCode` values) and `terms.capitalGainsPart` (every part any year's `CG-PARTS` declares). The compiler refuses a head or a declared part with no word, as it does for regimes (D-678). W-327d2 reuses the part words.
  - C1b: set-off sentences that name no head or part ("set against your other income in the order the law sets"). That needs no words, but loses where the loss went.
- **C2: the abroad-untaxed reason.** W-327c2 made `OS_UNTAXED_ABROAD`, and house-property interest now needs the same one.
  - **C2a (recommended):** rename it `UNTAXED_ABROAD` (code, content and tests) and use it in both places: one word per concept.
  - C2b: a second code with the same sentence.
