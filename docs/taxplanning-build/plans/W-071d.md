## Plan for W-071d — services, utilities and education

**What this item is for (one sentence):** value household services, gas, electricity and water, education for a
household member, and a carrier's free travel, as the employer provides them, for both tax years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C7). C1 (whom) extends the car's reading (OQ-10); nothing is coded until you
choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-071d` row** (D-110): S4 r.15(4) Table III; Rules 1962 r.3(3)–(5). `DR 01` `PERQ-SERVANT-2026`,
  `PERQ-UTIL-2026`, `PERQ-EDU-2026`.
- **TY 2026-27, S4 r.15(4) Table III** (Gazette 271092, p.1544, read here) — *"The value of benefit provided by the
  employer to the employee or any member of his household for goods, services or utilities"*; it names no limb of
  s.17 (Table IV names s.17(1)(e)):
  - **Sl. 1** sweeper, gardener, watchman or personal attendant: *"the total amount of salary paid or payable by the
    employer or any other person on his behalf for such services as reduced by any amount paid by the employee"*;
  - **Sl. 2** gas, electric energy or water: (a) bought from an outside agency — the amount the employer paid the
    agency; (b) from the employer's own resources — *"the manufacturing cost per unit incurred by the employer"*;
    each less the employee's payment;
  - **Sl. 3** education of a household member: (a) the employer's expenditure less recoveries; (b) the employer's own
    institution, and (c) free education elsewhere *"by reason of his employment"* — *"cost of such education in a
    similar institution in or near the locality"*, less recoveries, *"where the cost of such education or value of
    such benefit per child exceeds **₹3,000 per month**"*;
  - **Sl. 4** a carrier's free or concessional journey for an employee (*"not being an employee of an airline or the
    railways"*) or household member: the value at which it is offered to the public, less recoveries.
- **TY 2025-26, Rules 1962 r.3(3)–(6)** (consolidated rule 3, p.3, V3): the same valuations; r.3(5) proviso — *"nothing
  contained in this sub-rule shall apply if the cost of such education or the value of such benefit per child does
  not exceed **one thousand rupees per month**"* for the employer's own institution or free education by reason of
  employment; r.3(6) proviso — *"nothing contained in this sub-rule shall apply to the employees of an airline or the
  railways"*.
- **The Acts:** S2 s.17(1)(c) (a benefit or amenity — a specified employee, or a director or substantial-interest
  holder of a company employer), (f) (an obligation of the employee met by the employer — everyone); 1961
  s.17(2)(iii), (iv). D-113 read the car this way (OQ-10) and built `salary.monetaryincome` and
  `PERQ-SPECIFIED-EMPLOYEE`.

### Findings

1. **Table III, unlike Table IV, names no limb of s.17.** Its goods, services and utilities are benefits or amenities —
   s.17(1)(c), a specified employee's — **unless** the employer meets the employee's own obligation (the servant is the
   employee's, the connection is in the employee's name, the fees are the employee's), which is s.17(1)(f), everyone's.
   The same reading as the car's (OQ-10), no text stating it.
2. **Education's limit applies only to Sl. 3(b)–(c)** (the employer's own institution, or free by reason of
   employment), per child per month, and is all-or-nothing: at or under it nil, over it the whole value. Sl. 3(a)
   (fees the employer pays) has no limit.
3. **The limit's measure — "the cost of such education or value of such benefit per child"** — is before or after
   the recovery? The words give both.
4. **An airline's or the railways' employee's free travel** is outside Sl. 4 / r.3(6) — "shall not apply"; whether it
   is then nil or valued another way is not stated.

  - **(b)** Everyone, whatever the arrangement.

  valued on (the salary paid, the amount paid to the agency or units × manufacturing cost, the expenditure, the
  similar institution's cost, the public fare); the recovery; and for the education limit, the months and the child.
- **C3 — the education limit (findings 2, 3):** per child per month, on the **cost before recovery** (the "cost of
  such education" the Rule names first); over the limit, the whole value less recovery; at or under, nil. A row
