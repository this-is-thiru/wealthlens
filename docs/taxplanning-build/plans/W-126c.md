### What the Gazette and the design say

- **Source:** Gazette 271092 (sha256 `af51e671…`, re-fetched and matched today), pp.2274 – 2275 (Part B) and 2279 – 2280
  (Annexure-II). I read the text with pypdf 6.14.0 and checked it by eye against the pages rendered to PNG. No amendment
  to 12-11-2026 touches the form (`sources/2026-11-16-form-130.md`).
- **Annexure-II has the same shape as Annexure-I** (`DOC-F130-C-B`, `01-rulebook.md:1509`). It opens with the heading
  "PART C (Annexure-II)" and a sub-heading, "In relation to specified senior citizen for tax deduction under 393(1)
  [Table: Sl. No. 8(iii)]". Its rows are A and 1 – 17:
  - **Columns.** 8(a) – (d) print "Gross Amount" and "Deductible Amount". 8(e) – (h) and 9 print "Gross Amount",
    "Qualifying Amount" and "Deductible Amount".
  - **Repeating lines.** 8(g) is a heading over repeating "section …" lines.
  - **Wording that differs from Annexure-I:** 8(f) reads "section 153(2)(b)"; 8(g) reads "Amount deductible under any
    other provision(s)".
  - **Kept as printed:** row 5's "[(2-4]", row 9's "Act[8(a)" with no space, and row 6's curly quotes.
  - **Printed sums** (target = signed terms):
    - p.2279: 4 = 3(a) + 3(b); 5 = 2 − 4; 7 = 5 + 6.
    - p.2280: 9 = 8(a) + … + 8(f) + 8(h), Deductible Amount column; 10 = 7 − 9; 15 = 11 + 13 + 14 − 12; 17 = 15 − 16.
  - Row 2, "Total amount of salary received", prints no sum, but row 5 fixes it, as row 3 fixes Annexure-I's 1(d).
- **Part B is three tables, not labelled lines** (`DOC-F130-B-001`, `01-rulebook.md:1507`):
  - **(i) The quarterly summary**, under "Summary of amount paid/credited and tax deducted at source thereon in respect
    of the employee". Columns: Quarter (s) · Receipt Numbers of original quarterly statements of TDS under section
    397(3)(b) · Amount paid/credited · Rate of deduction of tax · Amount of tax deducted · Amount of tax deposited/
    adjusted. Then blank rows, one per quarter, which the issuer fills, and a "Total" row whose Amount paid/credited and
    Rate cells are merged into one.
  - **(ii) "I. Details of tax deducted and deposited in the central government account through book adjustment".**
    Columns: Sl. No. · Tax deposited (refer Note 5) · a group "Book Identification Number (BIN)" over Receipt numbers of
    Form No. 137 · DDO serial number in Form No. 137 · Date of Transfer voucher (dd/mm/yyyy) · Status of Matching with
    Form No. 137. The header crosses the page break: "137" is printed on p.2275. Then a "Total" row.
  - **(iii) "II. … through challan".** Columns: Sl. No. · Tax deposited (refer Note 5) · a group "Challan Identification
    Number (CIN)" over BSR Code of the Bank Branch · Date on which tax deposited (dd/mm/yyyy) · Challan Serial Number ·
    Status of matching with TIN 2.0. Then a "Total" row.
  - **Notes 3 – 5:** government deductors fill item I, other deductors item II. Note 3 prints "item II of Part **A**"; the
    form has no such item, so Part B is meant. Note 5: tax deducted = tax + surcharge + cess (`DOC-F130-TAXSUM-001`).

### Findings

1. **Annexure-II fits W-126a's model as it stands.** The probe added 1 part, 30 lines and 7 sums to `FORM_130.json` with no
   Java change; the validator accepted them and the relock moved only `2026-27.forms.8` → `.9`. Three tests moved, each
   because a list it locks grew:
   - `Form130LayoutTest`'s headings test (`:125`) and sums test (`:170`).
   - `Form130ParserTest.read_whenOnlyPartAIsIssued_findsPartAAndSaysPartCIsAbsent` (`:142`). The fixture still prints
     Annexure-II, so 42 fields are found instead of 13.

   Every other parser case passed with Annexure-II printed empty: no inference fired and no note was added.
2. **Part B does not fit.** A data row has no printed label, because the issuer writes the quarter or serial number, and
   W-126b's parser finds lines only by label (`LabelMatch.find`, `LabelMatch.java:40`). Part B needs three new things:
   a table in the layout, a parser step that reads a table's rows, and a way to put each word in its column. That is
   more than ~300 lines on its own (§7).
3. **Sub-headings are missing from the layout (an existing gap).** Each annexure prints a sub-heading ("In relation to
   …"), and Annexure-I also prints a "Particulars" column heading. Neither is in the layout, and the fixture prints
   neither (`Form130Fixture.java:36` – `:58`). A real certificate would therefore leave them unclaimed inside the part.
   `unmapped()` (`Form130Parser.java:233`) would report them and make the reading `PARTIAL` (`:118`). This is my
   reading of the code; no test proves it yet.
