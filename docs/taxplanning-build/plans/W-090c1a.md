## Plan for W-090c1a — The person's own facts r.164(2) reads

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a form's condition can ask whether the person is ordinarily
resident, which RNOR is not, and how many house properties they own, both answered from the inputs every time a
condition context is built.

**Findings (each read this session):**
- F1: residence reaches the conditions as a boolean. `NodeContext.conditions()` collapses `Assessee.ResidentialStatus`
  to `resident = residence() != NON_RESIDENT` (`pipeline/NodeContext.java:343`), and `ConditionContext` holds only
  `boolean resident` (`rules/condition/ConditionContext.java:39`). RNOR is lost there.
- F2: there is a precedent for a status the inputs and the conditions share. `DisabilitySeverity` lives in
  `rules/condition`, `Individual.disability()` returns it, and `taxplanning` imports it
  (`taxplanning/profile/TaxpayerProfileRequest.java:4`). `ResidentialStatus` is nested in `input/Assessee.java:47` –
  `50`, and is named 127 times in 30 files, 4 of them in `taxplanning`.
- F3: every entered house property has a share above nil (`input/HouseProperty.java:55`), so under D-524 C2a every
  entry is a property owned. They are `EnteredFacts.houseProperties` (`input/EnteredFacts.java:137`).
- F4: a brought-forward loss is not an input fact. Whether a carried loss reaches this year is decided by the law's
  rows (the span, a return filed in time) inside `CarriedLossSetOff` (its class Javadoc). It is computed, so it belongs
  to W-090c1b. See C2.
- F5: `new ConditionContext(` is called in 3 main files (`NodeContext`, `MedicalTreatmentAbroadNode`,
  `HealthInsuranceDeduction`) and 4 test files (`ConditionTest` ×9, `ConditionContextTest` ×9, `RegimeLifecycleTest`,
  `TaxYear2025RulePackTest`). A new condition kind is listed in four places in `Condition.java` and has a tag and a
  parse arm in `ConditionJson` (W-090a1's F3).

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1a (recommended): move `ResidentialStatus` to `rules/condition`, beside `DisabilitySeverity`, and have the context
  carry it instead of `boolean resident`.**
  - `IsResident` holds for anything but `NON_RESIDENT`. The collapse moves from `NodeContext` into the condition, with
    its s.5(1)(c) reason.
  - The new `IsOrdinarilyResident` holds for `RESIDENT` only.
  - This is one type for one concept (CLAUDE.md §3). The 30 files change only their import or qualifier.
  - C1b: keep the boolean and add `boolean ordinarilyResident`. That is smaller, but two booleans allow
    "ordinarily resident non-resident".
- **C2a (recommended): `HasLossBroughtForward` moves to W-090c1b (F4).** The W-090c1a and W-090c1b rows change, with
  an erratum. C2b: build it here from the raw ledger, which would count a loss whose carry-forward span has expired.

**Files I will create** (under `backend/src/main/java/com/thiru/wealthlens/taxengine/rules/condition/` unless stated):
- `ResidentialStatus.java`: moved from `Assessee`, same three values. The Javadoc keeps the s.5(1)(c) note.
- `IsOrdinarilyResident.java`: holds for `RESIDENT`. Its reason is r.164(2)'s "resident other than not ordinarily
  resident".
- `HousePropertiesOwnedAtMost.java`: holds when the number owned is at most `n`, with `n` at least 0. Its reason is
  r.164(2)(b)'s "does not own more than two".
- Tests:
  - `IsOrdinarilyResidentTest`: `RESIDENT` holds; `RNOR` and `NON_RESIDENT` do not.
  - `HousePropertiesOwnedAtMostTest`: 2 owned at most 2 holds; 3 does not; 0 holds; unknown throws.
  - A `NodeContext` case: one 50% and one 100% property count 2, and RNOR reaches the context.
