## Plan for W-156b2 — health insurance's payment kinds by rule data

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** after W-156b1, `HealthInsuranceDeduction` still decides
by `HealthPaymentKind` which kinds may be paid in cash, which may be spread over years, which limit each kind fills,
and which kinds count as "paid for health insurance" (raising the senior limit and barring a senior's medical
spending). This item makes each a row reached by `health.kind` (D-345 H3 – H7).

**This plan raises C1 – C3. C3 changes two test assertions and asks your approval for them.**

### What I read (session 31, 2026-10-23)

- `node/HealthInsuranceDeduction.java` after W-156b1:
  - `record`: the cash test (`kind != PREVENTIVE_CHECKUP`), the spreading test (`kind != INSURANCE_PREMIUM`), and the
    `switch` on the kind that fills the insurance, check-up or medical limb;
  - `coversSenior` for a premium only (`case INSURANCE_PREMIUM`), with the comment that a scheme never raises the
    senior limit;
  - `requireNotInsuredSenior`'s `kind == INSURANCE_PREMIUM` filter.
- **The law, and where the repo holds its text:**
  - **s.126(9):** cash for a check-up only. `DED-HEALTH-CHECKUP`'s note says "Cash is allowed for a check-up
    (s.126(9)(a))". The 1961 counterpart, s.80D(2B), is quoted in `sources/2026-10-07-chapter-via.md` B12: "(i) any
    mode, including cash" for a check-up, "(ii) any mode other than cash in all other cases".
  - **Spreading a premium:** rulebook `DED-80D-009` (`01-rulebook.md:1087`, VERIFIED, s.126(8)(b), (10)(a), (10)(c)),
    "Multi-year lump-sum premium is spread". 1961 s.80D(4A), B13: "a deduction equal to the appropriate fraction".
  - **"Paid for health insurance":** s.126(7) "if no amount has been paid to effect or to keep in force the health
    insurance of such person", quoted in the bucket rows' notes. The senior substitution, s.126(8)(a), is "for a sum
    paid for health insurance"; the code comment cites it and D-145. A scheme contribution is not health insurance.
  - **The limbs:** s.126(2)(a)/(b) (insurance, scheme, check-up), (2)(c)/(d) (medical), (3) (the check-up limit), all
    quoted in the bucket rows' and `DED-HEALTH-CHECKUP`'s notes.
- `HealthInsuranceDeductionTest` (line numbers after W-156b1): `:243` asserts "s.126(9)", and `:211` "s.126(7)".
- `SEC-HEALTH-INSURANCE` lists the cash bar as `ByCondition PaidOtherThanInCash` and s.126(7) by rule. No row uses
  `PaidOtherThanInCash`.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the rows (all read `health.kind` only; each is the lone row of its own `EXACTLY_ONE` / `REGIME` set, tested
  by `RowReach`, as W-156a):**
  - **(a) recommended:**
    1. `DED-HEALTH-CASH-PERMITTED` — check-up (s.126(9)(a); 1961 s.80D(2B)(i)). Cash for a kind it does not reach is
       refused, as today.
    2. `DED-HEALTH-SPREAD` — premium (s.126(8)(b), (10); 1961 s.80D(4A)). Spreading for a kind it does not reach is
       refused, as today.
    3. `DED-HEALTH-INSURANCE-PAID` — premium (s.126(7), (8)(a); 1961 s.80D(4), (2)(c)–(d) proviso). It says which
       kinds raise the senior limit and which make a senior's medical spending ineligible: one concept, read twice.
    4. The limbs:
       - `DED-HEALTH-CHECKUP` gains `health.kind` = check-up;
       - a new `DED-HEALTH-INSURANCE-LIMB` reaches premium and scheme (s.126(2)(a), (b));
       - a new `DED-HEALTH-MEDICAL-LIMB` reaches medical (s.126(2)(c), (d)).

       Exactly one must reach a payment, else it is refused.

    Five new rows and sets, one row changed.
  - **(b)** a `RuleDefinition` mapping each kind to a limb and its flags. That needs a new permit and parsing for
    what conditions already say.
- **C2 — the node:**
  - `record` keeps a small `Limb` enum (where an amount accumulates: insurance, check-up, medical) chosen by which limb
    row reaches the kind. The `switch` is on that enum, not on the kind.
  - A premium's fraction applies to whatever the spreading row allows, so `relevantTaxYears` is read once.
  - `coversSenior` and `requireNotInsuredSenior` test `DED-HEALTH-INSURANCE-PAID`.
- **C3 — the refusals' words:**
  - **(a) recommended:** each refusal names its row, as W-155b and W-156a do.
    - `:243`'s "s.126(9)" → `DED-HEALTH-CASH-PERMITTED`.
    - `:211`'s "s.126(7)" → `DED-HEALTH-INSURANCE-PAID`.

    Both are assertions changed, not weakened, for approval.
  - **(b)** keep the section citations in the messages, which leaves the law's references in Java strings.
