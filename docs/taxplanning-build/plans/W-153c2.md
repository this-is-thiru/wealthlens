## Plan for W-153c2 — share deductions as reach rows (s.93)

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** every other-sources item is computed one way:
- its income line (W-153c1's row);
- then the share deduction whose row reaches its kind — half of interest on compensation, the entire commuted
  pension or gratuity, the family-pension share;
- then the expenses the expense rows reach (W-153b).

The kind `switch`, the five paths and the outright refusals go (D-321).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — `OS-DEEMED-DIVIDEND`, whose law the reach now carries:**
  - **(a) recommended: removed.**
    - Its two statements move into `OS-DIVIDEND-INTEREST`'s and `OS-OTHER-EXPENDITURE`'s `_note`s, as the reason
      neither reaches `DEEMED_DIVIDEND`: 1961 s.57 second proviso (F(No.2)A 2024 s.24(c), egazette 2024/256436 p.24),
      and S2 s.93(2) as substituted (FA 2026 s.44(b)). The words are copied from its `_note`.
    - Coverage lists those two rows for s.93 / s.57 already; the catalogue route goes to them.
    - A deemed dividend's refused expenses become nil lines on the rows they miss, as every other kind's do.
  - **(b)** kept as a "bar" row: a third role, "every expense against items this row reaches is refused, one nil line
    here". Today's deemed-dividend trace would stay, but a new role and a new set would exist for one row, while
    s.93(1)(f)'s "no other deduction" would be expressed differently.
- **C2 — the share deduction:**
  - **(a) recommended:** one set `RS-OS-SHARE` (`AT_MOST_ONE`, `REGIME_AND_CONDITIONS`) holding
    `OS-COMPENSATION-INTEREST`, `-COMMUTED-PENSION`, `-DEATH-GRATUITY`, `-FAMILY-PENSION` and
    `-FAMILY-PENSION-NEW-TAX-REGIME`.
    - Each reaches its kind by `othersources.kind`; family pension's two rows also keep their regimes.
    - The node applies the row that reaches the item, by its shape: `PercentOfBase` with the base from the cap, or
      `FamilyPensionShare`. That is dispatch on a shape type that only evaluates data, which the audit's criteria
      call not a finding.
    - The four one-row sets and `RS-OS-FAMILY-PENSION` go.
  - **(b)** `ALL_THAT_APPLY`, so several shares could stack on one item. No law today gives one item two shares, so
    `AT_MOST_ONE` refuses that loudly instead.
