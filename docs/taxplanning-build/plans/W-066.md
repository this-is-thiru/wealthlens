## Plan for W-066 — the proration bases

**What this item is for (one sentence):** give every rule that applies to part of a year one named, tested
way to measure that part (days, calendar months or completed months), so that no caller divides and the
choice sits visibly in data.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C4) before anything is built.**

### Findings

1. **A fraction is the wrong output for two of the three bases.** The month-based caps need a whole count to
   multiply by (`PerUnitPerPeriod`), and a fraction would bring back O-10's annual-divided-by-twelve. HRA
   needs a *salary for the period*, which is one division that must round exactly once.
2. **"Completed months" has two readings:**
   - (a) calendar months lying wholly within the window: 15 Apr–14 Jun gives 1 (May);
   - (b) whole months elapsed from the start: 15 Apr–14 Jun gives 2.
   Both give 0 for B2-20's 20 days. Which one a rule uses is that rule's question at authoring, where it is
   marked `NEEDS VERIFICATION` if unread. But the family has to define each variant precisely.
3. **`OccupationPeriods` is not a basis.** It is HRA's input, the windows the home was occupied, each measured
   by `Days`. Appendix E already calls it "adjacent".
4. **Nothing reads a basis from JSON yet.** `W-066b` adds `RuleRow.prorationBasis`, and caps read theirs when
   their first rule is authored (`W-069`: CEA/hostel). A JSON reader now would have no caller.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what a basis answers.**
  - **(a) recommended: two operations, and no public fraction.**
    - `int units(TaxYearId year, DateRange window)` — how many days or months of the window fall in the year.
      This is `PerUnitPerPeriod`'s count.
    - `Money apportion(Money forTheYear, TaxYearId year, DateRange window)` — the year's amount times units
      over units-in-year. It does the division once at `Money.CALC` and rounds once, to paise, in `Money`.
      This is the "no caller divides" rule: HRA's salary for the period and `W-066b`'s per-segment cap call
      it.
  - **(b)** `DR 15`'s `Fraction fractionOf`, with a new `Fraction` type and every caller multiplying. That is
    the division-per-caller the item exists to remove.
- **C2 — the three variants:**
  - `Days`: the window's inclusive days within the tax year, over the year's actual days (365 or 366).
  - `CalendarMonths`: each calendar month in which the window has at least one day counts as one, over 12.
  - `CompletedMonths`:
    - **(a) recommended:** calendar months lying *wholly* within the window, over 12. A "month" is then the
      same unit as `CalendarMonths`', and it matches B2-20.
    - **(b)** whole months elapsed from the window's start.
  - A window reaching outside the year is clipped to it. No overlap gives 0. A window with no end runs to the
    year's end.
- **C3 — `OccupationPeriods`:**
  - **(a) recommended:** not built here. HRA's occupied windows are `W-067`'s input, each measured by `Days`.
    Appendix E is corrected.
  - **(b)** A fourth variant with no declaration to build from.
- **C4 — the home:** `rules/temporal/proration/` (`ProrationBasis`, `Days`, `CalendarMonths`,
  `CompletedMonths`). It is pure rule vocabulary with no runner dependency, so both `W-066b` and the caps can
  use it. The `W-066b` row's `taxengine/temporal/` path is corrected to `rules/temporal/`.
