### Why this cannot be one item (§7)

The clauses need four different kinds of machinery:
- **Received in full, with s.93(1)(e)'s general expenditure:** (d), (h), (j) and the residue. That is a few
  `OtherSourceKind` values.
- **A formula:** (k), from three entered figures.
- **The business head's deductions:** (c) needs s.29(1)(e)'s due-date test; (f), (g) need s.28 and s.33 depreciation
  (block, rates, written-down value); s.95 needs s.38. All are `DEFERRED` / `BUILT_LATER`. Taxing (f)/(g) gross
  would overstate income, because depreciation is allowed whether claimed or not.
- **(l):** Schedule II Sl. 2's exclusion test, built nowhere (premium-to-sum-assured ratios by issue period, the
  ₹2,50,000 / ₹5,00,000 aggregate premium limits), plus S4 r.59 and its 1962 counterpart (to source).

Together that is well over ~300 lines and three sourcing rounds. The coverage close-out (catalogue entries for six
sections, SC-1) is a fourth piece.
