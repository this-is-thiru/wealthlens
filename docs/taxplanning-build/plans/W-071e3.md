## Plan for W-071e3 — cost-based benefits

**What this item is for (one sentence):** value the remaining prescribed benefits — holidays, credit-card and club
expenses, the use and the transfer of the employer's movable assets, and any other benefit at arm's-length cost — for
both tax years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C7). C2 is a reading where the two years' texts differ; nothing is coded
until you choose.**

### Findings

1. **Club spending: the 2026 Table joins what 1962 kept apart.** 1962: business use with records → nil (B); uniform
   health-club facilities → nil (C) — two exclusions. 2026: one exclusion needing business use, records **and**
   uniform health-club facilities. Read literally, a business club bill with records but no uniform health club is
   salary in TY 2026-27 and not in TY 2025-26.
2. **The excluded assets differ by year** — tablets and mobile phones join laptops and computers in TY 2026-27. A
   phone lent for use is 10% a year of its cost in TY 2025-26 and nil in TY 2026-27.
3. **"10% per annum"** of an asset used for part of the year: the Rules do not apportion; W-071c apportioned the
   car's wear and tear by months (D-113).
4. **Transfer wear and tear is by completed years**, reducing balance for two classes and straight line for the
   third; straight line past ten years would exceed cost.
5. **Six benefits, three input shapes:** an expense (holiday, card, club, other), an asset lent, an asset sold.

## As built — departures from the plan (for review)
- **The club rule by year is in data, as rows present or absent:** `PERQ-CLUB-BUSINESS` and `PERQ-CLUB-UNIFORM` exist
  for TY 2025-26 only, `PERQ-CLUB-BUSINESS-UNIFORM` for TY 2026-27 only; each is a nil (`Fixed` 0). The node applies
  whichever the year's pack carries. So 12 rows, not 8: the year's pack holds 11 (TY 2025-26) or 10 (TY 2026-27).
- **`MovableAssetKind` lives in `rules.definition`**, not `input`: `ExcludedAssets` lists it, and the rules vocabulary
  may not depend on the rest of the engine. `ExcludedAssets` is the seventh `RuleDefinition`; the definition switches
  gained a refusing case.
- **`Rate.leftAfter(base, periods)`** is new — the reducing balance, computed exactly and rounded once; straight line
  uses `Rate.ofShare`.
- **Transfer wear-and-tear rates are read off the rows' `PercentOfBase`** (the rate itself, not an amount), so the
  node never restates 50 / 20 / 10.
- **Wholly official records for an expense** reuse `OfficialUseRecord`; a higher official use (a vehicle's claim) is
  refused on an expense.
- **The catalogue text** says "in some years" rather than naming one (SC-2: no dates in text).
- **Locks:** law 2025-26.law.25 / 2026-27.law.32.
