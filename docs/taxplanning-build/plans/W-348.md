## Plan for W-348 — prod's 163 old-rebuild outcome rows: what the tax side needs, and how to supply it

> **Ruled** (D-812): C1a, C2a, C3a (owner, session 109, 2026-11-20). Found by W-334c5 row 2 (D-810). Nothing is changed by this item;
> W-348a, W-348b, W-348c and W-334a3 carry it out.

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** let the tax engine compute capital gains on prod's real sales, by giving their
rows the facts it needs — or deciding, per gap, that a user must supply or re-enter them.

**Facts (from the rehearsal, prod's copy):**
- All 163 `trade_outcomes` rows were written by the old startup rebuild (W-334c4a row 2: every `source_buy_lot_id` is `lot-…`), for 3 users.
- **Sale venue:** no row records `sell_exchange_name` (§3.4: 163). The engine decides the term from it (RS-CG-TERM, D-305), so every equity
  sale is refused for its term (§3.5 after W-349).
- **Segment:** no transaction on prod stores a segment (§3.4: `NSE/null 494`, `BSE/null 136`, `BMF/null 178`) — they predate W-292 — and no
  `user_charges` row exists to restore it (§2.14 found nothing). The STT side of a sale with nil charges needs `DELIVERY` or `INTRADAY`
  (§3.3), so it is refused too.
- **Charges without a breakdown:** 61 rows of 2 users carry broker charges and no breakdown; the capital-gains summary refuses such a user
  outright, "record those trades again" (§3.1, D-238 C2a). Nothing re-prices a stored trade (ADR-32).
- **Acquisition mode:** none recorded (§3.2: 163); read as `UNRECORDED`.

**The choices (C1 – C3):**
- **C1 — the venue and acquisition mode.** **C1a** rebuild each of the 3 users' rows from their transactions with today's code (§2.2, after
  backing up and deleting their rows; then §2.11): writes `sell_exchange_name` / `buy_exchange_name` from `transactions.exchange_name`, the
  acquisition mode, and shares buy charges correctly (D-798's ₹15.99 gone). Needs `m1.3` (W-343: same-day sales in id order) and a
  rehearsal. *C1b* a narrow correction that copies each row's sale exchange from its sale transaction (new code, one-time).
- **C2 — the segment.** No stored fact records it. **C2a** each affected user states it (in practice: "all my prod trades were delivery"),
  recorded as their declaration, and a scoped correction sets `segment` on their transactions with none — before C1a's rebuild, so the rows
  inherit it; a user who does not confirm stays refused. *C2b* leave it unknown: STT for those sales stays refused.
- **C3 — the 61 rows with charges and no breakdown.** **C3a** keep the refusal and tell the 2 users to record those trades again (the
  summary's own message), placed as an owner action per user; *C3b* a design item for the tax side to compute with an unbroken charge
  treated as not deductible, flagged to the user — a rule change, with its own design and rule data.

**Recommendation:** C1a + C2a + C3a: C1a fixes term, mode and the ₹15.99 with the standing rebuild rather than new one-time code; C2a keeps
the segment a fact the user supplies, never assumed; C3a needs no code and the users know their own trades.

**Then (placed on the ruling):** W-348a (C2a's declarations and correction), W-348b (C1a's per-user rebuild), both rehearsed on a fresh
copy with `m1.3` together with W-350; W-348c (C3a's owner actions).
