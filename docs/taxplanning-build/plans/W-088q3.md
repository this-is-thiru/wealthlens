### Design

1. **`SetOffNode.SetOffContext`**, a private record `(NodeContext ctx, OrderInUse order, Map<HeadCode, Money> income,
   CapitalGainsAbsorption parts, List<TraceLine> lines)`.
   - It replaces `Absorbing`, which held two of these five.
   - Its Javadoc says why it exists: the state one run's set-offs share and lower in place.
   - `evaluate` builds one, and the carried-loss calls take their four arguments from it.
2. **The three methods** take it in place of the five it holds, and read the temporal context from `ctx`:
   - `setOff(SetOffContext run, HeadCode lossHead, Money loss)`;
   - `absorb(SetOffContext run, HeadCode lossHead, Money allowed, RuleId row)`;
   - `absorbCapitalGains(SetOffContext run, HeadCode lossHead, Money loss, RuleId row)`.

   The bodies are unchanged apart from `run.` prefixes.
3. **`CapitalGainsNode`:**
   - `evaluate` builds `new CapitalGainsAbsorption(order, available)`.
   - `setOff(NodeContext ctx, CapitalGainsAbsorption parts, Money loss, boolean longTerm, List<TraceLine> lines)`
     calls `parts.absorb(loss, longTerm ? CapitalGainsPart::isLongTerm : part -> true, lines, …)`, with today's
     SET_OFF words. Then it writes the MEASURE line on what was not taken.
   - The row is read inside as `ctx.ruleIdOf(ROW)`, the same row `GoverningRow.of(ctx, ROW)` names.
   - The words name the declared order through a new accessor, `CapitalGainsAbsorption.order()`. Passing `order`
     down instead would make 6 parameters. The accessor has a caller here, unlike in W-088q2, where it was removed for
     having none.
   - The three `line(lines, row, …)` calls in `evaluate` become `ctx.traceLine(lines.size() + 1, row.id(), …)`, and
     `line` is deleted.
4. **Docs:** TS T6 `capitalgains` / `setoff` rows and a Changes row; WI "Built"; STATE D-266; CHECKPOINT.
