### The rule, stated once

Before the term, each bucket is tested against two sets, on facts it already carries (`capitalgains.asset` and a new
item fact `trade.transfer.segment`, the transfer side's segment, `UNRECORDED` when empty as the exchange is):

- **`RS-CG-NOT-COMPUTED`** (`AT_MOST_ONE`, conditions-only rows). A bucket a row reaches is income of another head
  that this engine does not compute, so it is refused, naming the row:
  - **`CG-SPECULATIVE`** — `capitalgains.asset = EQUITY` and `trade.transfer.segment = INTRADAY`. A contract settled
    otherwise than by delivery (S1; S3) is a speculative transaction, which is business income (Part D).
  - **`CG-DERIVATIVE`** — `trade.transfer.segment` `FUTURES` or `OPTIONS`. A specified derivative transaction is not
    speculative (S1(a), S2; S3 proviso (d)), so it is non-speculative business income.
- **`RS-CG-ELSEWHERE`** (`AT_MOST_ONE`, conditions-only). A bucket a row reaches is income another head charges, and
  it is entered there. It is left out with a nil trace line naming the row:
  - **`CG-DEPOSIT`** — `capitalgains.asset = FD`. What `portfolio` records on a deposit is its interest, which is
    income from other sources (S4, `OS-INTEREST`).

Every other bucket goes on to `RS-CG-TERM` as now. The adapter passes every non-nil bucket and reads no
classification.
