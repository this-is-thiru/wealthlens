## Plan for W-083 — the house election: which houses are own residences

**What this item is for (one sentence):** where more than two houses qualify for a nil annual value, or where
specifying one costs tax, price every choice of up to two houses by recomputation and take the cheapest. Today the
houses entered as own residences are taken as entered, and more than two are refused.

**This plan raises choices C1–C5. Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (session 21, 2026-10-18)

- **The row** (WI l.1144): *"`HouseElectionNode` | `DR 13` R-6 | which two houses is a choice, and optimisable"*. D-220
  adds *"the `HouseElection` variant of `Election` (W-082a), and `ElectionOutcome` as a `NodeValue` variant"*. TS

- **TY 2025-26:** `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE`'s note says the 1961 s.23(2), as substituted by the Finance Act, 2025 s.10, has

### Findings

1. **The specification is already an input,** and the houses that may be specified are already distinguishable.
   Every house that is not `LET` is either specified (`OWN_RESIDENCE`) or left out (`NOT_LET`). Under s.21(6) as both
   years now read (*"occupies it … or cannot actually occupy it due to any reason"*), every such house qualifies, the
   "other benefit" being an entry condition. So R-6's split of the input is not needed: the election only re-labels
   houses between `OWN_RESIDENCE` and `NOT_LET`.
2. **No node is needed, and none could price this inside the graph.** The house choice is read by
   `houseproperty.income`, early in the graph, and the in-run `Recomputation` cannot serve an early slot (W-082a
   finding 3). So an `elections.houses` node would have nothing to compute. The election belongs with the other two,
   resolved outside the graph. `ElectionOutcome` then still has no producer in the graph and stays a plain record.
3. **The choice includes fewer than two.** Specifying is optional (*"may, at his option, specify"*), and specifying a
   heavily mortgaged house can cost tax. So the choices are every set of at most two of the qualifying houses: 7 runs
   for three houses, 16 for five.
4. **One year cannot price everything.** A house left out can turn the head into a loss beyond the ₹2,00,000 set-off,
   and the rest is carried forward (W-081b). The carried loss's later value is not in this year's net payable, so the
   same limit as OQ-29 applies, declared on the line.
5. **Elections interact, and are resolved one at a time.** The regime changes what a house choice is worth (the new
   regime disallows own-residence interest and the set-off). Resolving houses within the regime being computed and
   the regime separately is not jointly optimal.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the model:**
  - **(a) recommended:** keep `HouseOccupancy`. A plain run computes the houses as entered. `HouseElection` (a new
    `Election` variant) has as candidates every set of at most two houses entered `OWN_RESIDENCE` or `NOT_LET`. The
    entered specification comes first (if within the limit), so a tie keeps what the assessee entered. Then larger
    sets before smaller, in entered order. `choosing` relabels those houses `OWN_RESIDENCE` and the other qualifying
    houses `NOT_LET`.
    - `stated` is empty, as for the regime (W-082b C2a): someone who has decided runs the plain pipeline.
    - Basis `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE`, node `houseproperty.income`.
    - The line reads *"own residences: properties [1, 2]"* (the node's own numbering) and says the carried loss's
      later value is not priced (finding 4).
  - **(b)** R-6's split (a usage fact plus an election flag), replacing `OWN_RESIDENCE` in about 20 places, for no
    choice the current input cannot express.
- **C2 — no node:**
  - **(a) recommended:** no `HouseElectionNode` and no `elections.houses` slot.
  - `ElectionOutcome` stays a plain record. Its `NodeValue` variant waits until a node produces one, and no backlog
    item now plans one (WI rule B2).
  - A WI erratum under W-083's row and Appendix B, and a TS change for `elections.houses`.
- **C3 — the >2 refusal:** it stays for a plain run (the assessee specified more than the Act allows). The election
  never offers more than two.
- **C4 — records:** D-230; WI errata (C2); TS `election/` and `houseproperty.income` rows.
- **C5 — finding 5:**
  - **(a) recommended:** place a row **W-084a**, *"resolve several elections together — the product of their choices,
    priced jointly — after `IncrementalRunner`"*, right after W-084. Joint pricing multiplies the runs, which W-084
    makes affordable.
  - **(b)** Drop it: each election stays resolved alone.

  ₹2,76,120 for {A, C}, and that ₹1,20,000 is carried forward on its line.
- The unit tier.
- **A tie case** in `HouseElectionPipelineTest`: three identical houses (letting value ₹3,00,000, no loan), entered
  {B, C}. Every pair costs the same, and each beats any smaller set, so {B, C} (as entered) is chosen.
- **Bite probe:** stop putting the entered specification first. The tie then goes to {A, B} (the first pair in entered
  order) and the tie case fails. The R-6 case does not, because its winner is unique.
