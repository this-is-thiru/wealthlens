## Plan for W-078e — coverage hygiene, and a build check that nothing is left without an item

**What this item is for (one sentence):** give each of the nine sections now `PLANNED` against a finished item an honest
classification, and add a build check that fails whenever a `PLANNED` section points at an item that is missing or
done. That way, what happened with W-078d cannot happen silently again.

**Reading for this plan found two more loose-end classes the D-159 audit missed. C1 splits them off, and C1 is the
first decision.**

### What I read

- **The row:** WI W-078e *(D-159)*: the nine sections — 2025 ss.4, 5, 11, 13, 155 / 1961 ss.4, 5, 14, 87 (W-046,
  W-049, W-060, W-067) — each re-pointed or reclassified with its reason, and a build check that every `PLANNED`
  `backlogItem` names an item in the backlog that is not done or retired.
- **History:** all nine were set by W-045o / W-045o2 (D-066, D-067) when the register was first built. None of the four
  items touched them. W-049b (D-077) marked the rebate's own sections (s.156 / 87A) `MODELLED` but not the framework
  sections s.155 / s.87. W-067 marked 1961 s.10 `MODELLED` but not 2025 s.11. No rule or catalogue entry cites any of
  the nine.
- **The sections, read** (Gazette 265620):
  - **s.4** (p.24): income-tax is charged at the rates a Central Act enacts, on the total income of the tax year.
  - **s.5** (p.25): the scope of total income by residence. A resident is taxed on income received, accruing or
    arising in or outside India (with the not-ordinarily-resident limb); a non-resident on income received or
    accruing in India. **s.6** (residence) is already `DEFERRED BUILT_LATER`.
  - **s.11** (p.36): income in Schedules II–VI is not included, on their conditions. Schedule III's built rows are
    exactly the rows 1961 s.10 is `MODELLED` by (D-099 and after).
  - **s.13** (p.36): the five heads. Built so far: Salaries and House property. Other Sources is W-079, Capital Gains
    W-088, Business `DEFERRED`.
  - **s.155** (p.186): rebates are deducted from income-tax computed before them, and s.156's never exceeds that tax.
    It is built in `RebateNode` with the `REBATE-*` rows.
- **The register:** `Coverage` (`MODELLED` needs rules and a manager; `PLANNED` needs an item;
  `DEFERRED(BUILT_FIRST)` = "in the first scope with no backlog item yet — **a gap for the owner to itemise**").
- **The precedent for a check that reads files outside `backend/`:** `CheckstyleBaselineTest`,
  `Path.of("..", "checkstyle-suppressions.xml")`.

### Findings

1. **The nine split into three kinds.**
   - **Built:** s.4 by the slab rows; s.11 by the Schedule III rows; s.155 / s.87 by the rebate rows. These can be
     `MODELLED` once each has a catalogue entry (SC-1).
   - **Not built and not in the first scope:** s.5 is the residence-based scope, like s.6.
   - **Still being built:** s.13 / s.14, the heads, last completed by W-088.
2. **Missed by D-159 — 42 sections in `DEFERRED BUILT_FIRST`.** These are in scope with no item at all. STATE has
   recorded them since W-045o as "for the owner to itemise", and they never were:
   - 2025: s.15, s.157, s.266, s.392, s.396, ss.403–410, ss.423–426, s.428, s.437.
   - 1961: s.15, s.89, s.140A, ss.192, 198, 199, ss.207–219, ss.234A–234D, s.234F, s.244A.

   They cover salary TDS, advance tax, self-assessment, the credits (`CreditsNode`, which "has no item id"), interest,
   the late-return fee, interest on refunds, arrears relief, and Salaries' own charging section.
3. **Missed by D-159 — a dangling "owed".** `ArchitectureTest` line 57: duplicate simple names (`DateRange`,
   `ExcelParser`) are "Owed: 03-WORK-ITEMS.md Appendix F", but Appendix F has no such row.
4. **What the check can see:** the STATE status board (`W-0nn X`, where `D` = done and `—` = retired or split). It is
   the one place that holds status (WI says statuses live in STATE, not WI). A `PLANNED` pointer must be on the board
   and be neither `D` nor `—`.
