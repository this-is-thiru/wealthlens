  - **B16 (80E, A0 pp.346–347):** an individual's interest *"out of his income chargeable to tax"* on a loan from a
    financial institution or approved charitable institution for his or a relative's higher education. No amount
    limit; *"the initial assessment year and seven assessment years immediately succeeding"*. The initial year is the
    one in whose previous year the assessee starts paying.

    exceed fifty thousand rupees"*. Sanctioned 1-4-2016 to 31-3-2017; loan at most 35 lakh; value at most 50 lakh; no
    house owned on sanction; no double deduction.

### Findings

1. **Every figure, window and condition is the same in both years**, so the definitions carry over unchanged.
2. **Same mechanics as TY 2026-27:**
   - s.131 refuses a loan s.130 covers, as 80EEA does for 80EE, and the node tries the earlier section first.
   - The span counts years the same way: the initial assessment year is the one after the tax year in which payment
     starts, and both ends shift alike.
   - The inputs already carry "paid" (80E) and "payable" (80EE–80EEB), as the sections do.
3. **After this item the only TY 2025-26 sections still refused are s.125, s.133 and s.134** (80CCH, 80G, 80GG). They
   are W-075d5's, and the existing rent and donation tests already assert the refusal naming W-075d5. The
   loan-refusal test from D-146 becomes a computation.
