## Plan for W-087b — `CapitalGainsAdapter`

**What this item is for (one sentence):** read a user's `CapitalGainsSummary` for a tax year and turn it into the
engine's `CapitalGainsInput`. Each bucket's gain is widened through `MoneyBridge`, and its `Term` and `RateBasisHint`
are decided by the special-rate provisions' conditions, which I read first (D-240).

**This plan raises choices C1–C6 and one open question (OQ-31). Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (session 21, 2026-10-18)

- **The row:** *"maps each bucket through `MoneyBridge` to `CapitalGainsInput.Bucket` — the (classification, asset
  type, sub-class) → (`Term`, `RateBasisHint`) mapping sourced first …; exposes `RateBasisHint` …; decides
  `SPECULATIVE`, `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS` and where `UNCLASSIFIED` is refused"*.
- **The law:** `sources/2026-10-18-capital-gains-basis.md`.
  - S2 ss.196, 197, 198 READ from egazette 265620.
  - 1961 ss.111A, 112, 112A READ from the thc copy, with every amendment since: FA (No. 2) 2019, FA 2021, FA 2023 and
    FA (No. 2) 2024 READ.
  - **The conditions are the same in both years:** equity share / equity-oriented fund unit / business-trust unit;
    STT on the sale (short-term); STT on acquisition and sale for a share, or on sale for a unit (long-term).
  - **The one thing not read** is the notification under 1961 s.112A(4) / S2 s.198(5): which acquisitions without
    STT still qualify.
- **What `portfolio` has:**
  - `HoldingPeriodPolicy` `EQUITY_LISTED` treats every `EQUITY` row as listed.
  - `TradeClassifier`: intraday equity is `SPECULATIVE`; futures and options are `UNCLASSIFIED`; mutual funds and
    bonds are `UNCLASSIFIED` until the instrument registry (D7) gives a sub-class.
  - `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS` is policy `FD_INTEREST`, fixed-deposit interest.
  - STT is a charge code (`STT`) in each row's buy and sell breakdown.
  - **The W-085 bucket does not carry it:** the STT facts are lost when rows are summed.
- **The engine side:** `CapitalGainsInput.Bucket(Money gain, Term term, RateBasisHint basis, Optional<String>
  portfolioRuleRef)`, plus `broughtForward` (W-088's ledger, D-237).
  - `RateBasisHint` is in `taxengine.rules.component`, not a named interface. D-025 put it there so the `portfolio`
    read-model could share it, but W-085 dropped that (D-237), so its only user is `CapitalGainsInput`.
- **X1.3:** a non-zero `UNCLASSIFIED` blocks with a 409. `SPECULATIVE` is business income that *"must surface"*. No
  `BlockingDataQualityException` exists (grep). `ControllerAdviser` maps `IllegalArgumentException` → 400. The sealed
  `TaxError` family is W-105's.

### Findings

1. **The rate basis needs two STT facts per bucket.** Without them, a short-term equity gain cannot be told from one
   the slab ladder charges, nor a long-term one from s.197 / s.112's. They are `portfolio` facts (a charge was
   levied), not tax, so they belong in the read-model's key.
2. **A long-term equity gain bought without STT** (bonus, rights, IPO allotment) qualifies only if the unread
   notification covers that acquisition. That needs an open question, OQ-31.
3. **Only `EQUITY` reaches a capital-gains classification today.** Every other asset is `UNCLASSIFIED` or
   `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS` upstream. A mapping for funds and bonds would be code no row can reach, so they are refused
   loudly instead.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the STT facts:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `CapitalGainsBucket` gains `boolean sttOnAcquisition, boolean sttOnTransfer`, part of its
    one-per-key rule.
    - `CapitalGainsSummaryService` sets them per row: the buy or sell breakdown holds `STT` above nil.
    - This modifies W-085's record, W-086's service, and both tests.
  - **(b)** a bucket per row instead of per key. That makes a summary as long as the trade list.
- **C2 — the mapping** (the conclusion of the sources file):

  | Classification | Asset | STT | → Term | → Basis |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `SHORT_TERM` | `EQUITY` | on transfer | SHORT | `SPECIAL_RATE_LISTED_EQUITY` (S2 s.196; 1961 s.111A) |
  | `SHORT_TERM` | `EQUITY` | none on transfer | SHORT | `SLAB` |
  | `LONG_TERM` | `EQUITY` | on acquisition and transfer | LONG | `SPECIAL_RATE_LISTED_EQUITY` (S2 s.198; 1961 s.112A) |
  | `LONG_TERM` | `EQUITY` | none on transfer | LONG | `SPECIAL_RATE_OTHER` (S2 s.197; 1961 s.112) |
  | `LONG_TERM` | `EQUITY` | on transfer only | — | **refused, naming OQ-31** |
  | `SHORT_TERM` / `LONG_TERM` | any other asset | — | — | refused: no mapping until the instrument registry classifies it |

  - **(a) recommended:** as the table shows, with **OQ-31** raised: which acquisitions without STT does the
    notification under 1961 s.112A(4) / S2 s.198(5) cover? I can give you a ChatGPT prompt for its text.
  - **(b)** hold the item until OQ-31 is sourced, and build the row then.
- **C3 — the other classifications:**
  - **(a) recommended:**
    - `UNCLASSIFIED` with any non-zero gain → refused, naming the asset and that the instrument registry or the
      derivatives head is missing (X1.3);
    - `SPECULATIVE` non-zero → refused: speculative business income is not computed here (the D1 user has none; ITR-3);
    - `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS` (fixed-deposit interest) → left out of capital gains, because it is other-sources income,
      entered there (`OtherSourceItem`). The Javadoc says so.
    - Zero-gain buckets of any kind are left out.
  - **(b)** as (a), but `SPECULATIVE` left out rather than refused. That keeps an intraday trader computable, and
    hides the income.
- **C4 — exposing `RateBasisHint`:**
  - **(a) recommended:** move it to `taxengine.input`, beside its one user. D-025's reason for `rules.component` is
    gone (D-237). That touches two test imports.
  - **(b)** a `component` named interface, which also exposes `ComponentDefinition` and the rest to `taxplanning`.
- **C5 — the adapter:** `taxplanning/portfolio/CapitalGainsAdapter` (`@Component @RequiredArgsConstructor`), with
  `inputFor(String email, TaxYearId year)`.
  - The year goes as `TLocalDate.financialYear(year.startsOn())`.
  - Buckets go in the summary's order; `portfolioRuleRef` is empty, and `broughtForward` is empty (W-088's ledger).
  - `asOf` and the reference come from the summary.
  - This is the first `taxplanning` → `portfolio` import, and the first caller of `fromTMoney`, so
    `only_the_adapter_converts` stops being vacuous.
- **C6 — records:** D-241; OQ-31; WI (the W-087b note; a W-088 erratum: `UNCLASSIFIED` is refused upstream, as a 400
  until W-105's `TaxError` gives it a 409); TS l.115 and `input/` row; the sources file.

  alternative.
