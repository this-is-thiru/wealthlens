## Plan for W-052 — `NetPayableNode`

**What this item is for (one sentence):** the amount payable before filing effects: the tax after rebate
plus the cess (later also the surcharge, less credits). It's the figure `round.netpayable` rounds and
`TaxComputation.netPayableBeforeFilingEffects` carries, and it completes Phase 5's chain from the rounded
total income to the rounded amount payable.

### What I read

- **The `W-052` row:**
  - Produces the node and test; *"In Phase 5 it **requires `tax.cess` only**; the `credits` edge is
    added by the Phase 9 item that builds `CreditsNode` (D-050)"*;
  - Verify: `-Dtest=NetPayableNodeTest`, reproducing `DR 08` **T1-N1** and **T1-N3**'s final figures.
  - Appendix D's `W-052` row: `TopologicalSort` rejects a required slot with no producer, hence no
    `credits` edge yet.
- **`DR 15` B3/B-table:** `tax.netpayable` reads *"cess, relief, credits"*, value `MoneyValue`, rules
  **"—"** (none). The pipeline graph is `… tax.cess → tax.netpayable → interest.and.fees →
  round.netpayable`.
- **`DR 23` A2:** `TaxComputation(… Money netPayableBeforeFilingEffects)`.
- **Rulebook `REBATE-ORDER-001`:** Form No. 130 row 17 *"Tax payable (13+15+16-14)"* is *"an arithmetic
  sum of already-computed rows"*.
- **`DR 08` totals:** T1-N1, T1-N2, T1-N3, T1-N4, T1-O2, T2-1.
- **Code:**
  - `CessNode` (`W-051`): `tax.cess` = **the cess amount alone**, a `MoneyValue`;
  - `RebateNode`: `tax.afterrebate` is a `TaxByBasis`;
  - `NodeResult.untraced`: *"A value that needs no line of its own, because it only re-shapes what upstream
    produced"*; `TraceLine` requires a `RuleId`;
  - `RuleJson`: a rule must carry a cap, relief, rounding or conditions;
  - `NodeIds.TAX_NETPAYABLE`; T6's `tax.netpayable` row (requires `tax.cess, credits`).

### Findings

1. **`tax.cess` alone can't give the amount payable.** Since `W-051` it's the cess, not the tax plus
   cess, so the node must also read `tax.afterrebate`. The row's *"requires `tax.cess` only"* was written
   when the cess slot's content wasn't settled.
2. **No law row exists for a sum, and none can load.** The design lists no rule for `tax.netpayable`,
   and the rulebook calls the Form 130 row *"an arithmetic sum"*. A row with no cap, relief, rounding or
   condition is refused. Yet every `TraceLine` must cite a rule. So the node can't emit a line without
   inventing a rule.
3. **The figure is still visible.** `round.netpayable`'s `ROUNDING` line takes it as its `in`, and
   `W-052b` projects it into `TaxComputation.netPayableBeforeFilingEffects`. Each part (tax, rebate,
   cess) already has its own line.
4. **Standing rules.** No section of either Act is modelled: the sum is arithmetic, and the charge is
   s.3(15)'s, already the cess row's. No catalogue entry and no rule, so no second year and no ID.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what it reads (finding 1):** requires **`tax.afterrebate` and `tax.cess`**.
  - The value is `afterRebate.asMoney() + cess`. The whole post-rebate tax is included, special-rate
    too.
  - `W-056` adds `tax.surcharge`; the Phase 9 `CreditsNode` item adds `credits` (subtracted).
  - Errata on the `W-052` row, T6, and `W-051`'s erratum on `W-056`, which now adds the surcharge here
    as well.
- **C2 — the trace (findings 2–3):**
  - **(a) recommended:** `NodeResult.untraced`. The node re-shapes upstream figures that already have
    lines, and the result appears as the rounding line's `in`. No rule is invented.
  - **(b)** A pack row "tax payable" citing Form No. 130 row 17. That needs a new kind of row that
    states nothing but a sum, or relaxing the must-carry rule D-075/D-078 just enforced. Not
    recommended.
  - When `credits` arrives, subtracting a credit is a step with its own rule (`TDS-CREDIT-001`), and
    that item traces it.
- **C3 — no pack change.** No row, lock or `RuleIds` change, and no coverage or catalogue change
  (finding 4).
