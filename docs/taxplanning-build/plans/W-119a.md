**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: what "proven" means.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** one new class, `StatutoryValidityTest`, thirteen whole-computation cases through
    `GoldenCaseRunner`. For a nil-line or capped row: the same facts with and without the forbidden claim give the
    **same total income**, and the claim's line carries its reason. For a refusal row: the computation refuses with
    the message. No hand figure is needed — the expected total is the run without the claim.
  - C1b: no new test; the table above is the proof, recorded in the decision row. W-119a is then the three links only.
- **C2: the four refusals (rows 4, 5, 8, 9).**
  - **C2a (recommended):** keep them. Each is two entered facts that cannot both be true (a house with rent entered
    as an own residence; three houses each called the chosen two; medical expenditure for a person entered as
    insured; interest left out of house property that is not what the loans carry). The engine cannot choose which
    fact is the mistake; a nil line would hide it. `DR 12` T12-9 and T12-10 say "blocked".
  - C2b: turn them into nil lines with a reason — four engine changes, each choosing a side for the person.
- **C3: what the two house-loan links become.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** `ByRule`, naming the section's own rule (`DED-HOUSE-LOAN`, `DED-HOUSE-LOAN-EARLIER`) — it
    is that rule's row that decides which loans the check compares (`HousePropertyInterest.java:104`) — with the
    description saying what is checked: the interest left out of house property must equal the interest entered here.
  - C3b: `ByRule` naming `HP-INTEREST`, the house-property side.
  The electric-vehicle link stays `NotModelled` either way, with its description and reason corrected to the business
  head.
