## Plan for W-075c6 — donations (s.133, 80G), TY 2026-27

**What this item is for (one sentence):** deduct, in the old regime, an individual's donations to the funds and
institutions s.133 names: in full or at half, some of them within 10% of adjusted gross total income. It also
settles how s.133 and s.134 are ordered, since each one's base contains the other.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C7); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### Findings

1. **The rulebook row's NEEDS VERIFICATION is cleared.** There are four groups: 100% (i)–(xxii); 100% within the
   limit (xxiii) (and (xxiv), which is for a company only); 50% (b)(i); 50% within the limit (b)(ii)–(vi). The limit is
   10% of adjusted gross total income, applied to the **sums** before the 100% or 50% (s.133(2); Form No. 114 note 5).
2. **s.133 and s.134 depend on each other.** s.133's adjusted gross total income is net of every other deduction,
   s.134 included. s.134's base is total income before s.134, which is net of s.133. But **s.133 depends on s.134
   only through the 10% limit**, and only when the limited sums exceed it. In every other case, working s.133 first
   and then s.134 on the remainder satisfies both sections' words exactly. The pair is genuinely circular only when
   **the limit binds and rent is claimed**.
3. **Mixing 100% and 50% sums under the limit** ((a)(xxiii) with (b)(ii)–(vi)): s.133(2) says the excess is
   *"ignored"* but not which sums lose it. With only one of the two kinds there is nothing to decide.
4. **s.133(6) is narrower than `DR 12` says.** It reaches (1)(b)(ii) only. `DR 12` is the claim-integrity design for
   a later item, so this needs recording now (erratum) rather than building a check on the wider reading.
5. **The ₹2,000 cash bar applies to each donation.** Over ₹2,000 in cash, that donation gets nil. ₹2,000 or less in
   cash counts.
6. **No part of gross total income is untaxed today**, so the (7)(a) reduction for such a portion is nil until a
   head produces one. The 2025 words exclude nothing else (special-rate capital gains, for instance, are not
   named). That matters once capital gains are built (Phase 9), so the row's note records it. The 1961 s.80G
   wording is not read here (W-075d).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the input:** `EnteredFacts.donations` — `List<Donation(DonationClause clause, Money amount, boolean
  paidInCash, boolean reportedByDonee)>`. The clauses, named for what the donee is:
  - `NAMED_FUND` — (1)(a)(i)–(xxii);
  - `FAMILY_PLANNING` — (a)(xxiii);
  - `DROUGHT_RELIEF_FUND` — (b)(i);
  - `CHARITABLE_INSTITUTION` — (b)(ii);
  - `PUBLIC_BODY` — (b)(iii)–(vi).

  (a)(xxiv) is a company's, so it has no clause. `reportedByDonee` is read only for `CHARITABLE_INSTITUTION`. The
  other per-fund conditions are conditions of entry: Clean Ganga for a resident only, CSR spend excluded, a CM fund's
  three conditions, approvals and notifications.
- **C2 — s.133 against s.134 (finding 2):**
  - **(a) recommended:** s.133 runs first, on adjusted gross total income — GTI less the other deductions, held
    within it — and s.134 runs after it. If the qualifying limit binds **and** a rent declaration is entered, the
    computation is refused, raising **OQ-23**. That is the one case where both words cannot be met in order.
  - **(b)** In that case, solve the pair together. Each one's limbs are linear, and the slopes (10% and 25%) keep the
    solution unique. The words would hold exactly, but it is about 50 more lines, and an exact solution no other tool
    may compute.
  - **(c)** Always s.134 first, ignoring s.133 in its base. Simpler, but it departs from s.134(5) even when nothing
    is circular.
- **C3 — mixed sums under a binding limit (finding 3):** **(a) recommended:** refused, raising **OQ-24** (the pattern
  of OQ-18 and OQ-20). **(b)** The 100% sums are counted first. This is the reading most favourable to the taxpayer,
  but the words do not say it.
- **C4 — the rows** (TY 2026-27 from 2026-04-01, `ExceptIn` NEW_TAX_REGIME, `SOURCE_CHECKED`):
  - `DED-DONATION-WHOLE` — `PercentOfBase` 100 AMOUNT_PAID (s.133(1)(a));
  - `DED-DONATION-HALF` — `PercentOfBase` 50 AMOUNT_PAID (s.133(1)(b));
  - `DED-DONATION-QUALIFYING` — `PercentOfBase` 10 ADJUSTED_GTI (s.133(2)), the most the limited sums may count;
  - `DED-DONATION-CASH` — a new definition, `CashPaymentLimit(Money atMost)` = 2000 (s.133(5)), so the figure is pack
    data as `PaidOtherThanInCash`'s Javadoc says.
- **C5 — the lines:**
  - a `MEASURE` line for the qualifying limit where it binds;
  - one `DEDUCTION` line per clause group: whole, whole-within-limit, half, half-within-limit;
  - a nil line for a donation the cash bar or s.133(6) stops, naming which;
  - a nil line naming the regime in the new regime.

  A HUF's donations and TY 2025-26 are refused, as in the earlier items.
- **C6 — `DR 12` erratum (finding 4):** an erratum below its s.133(6) line saying the provision reaches (1)(b)(ii)
