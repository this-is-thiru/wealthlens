## Plan for W-238e1 — liquidity of a suggested change, as pack data

**For:** a user reading a suggestion to move pay into the NPS, the PF or a superannuation fund. They should learn
when that money can come out, and on what terms, as the law states it for the year. Row `03-WORK-ITEMS.md` W-238e1
(W-238e C1a, D-493). Design `03-advisory.md` A2.6 (`liquidity`), C11.

### What I located (session 68): `sources/2026-11-08-fund-liquidity.md`

- **NPS:** all 13 Gazette copies (the 2015 Regulations and amendments N1 – N12) are on egazette and hashed. Two
  versions fall in each pack year: N11 (2025-26) and N12 (2026-27) both start on publication.
- **EPF:** the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 2026 (G.S.R. 525(E), issue of 29-06-2026) supersedes the 1952
  Scheme. Its para 46 covers partial withdrawals. **The 1952 Scheme has no Gazette copy I can reach** (F4). It governs
  all of 2025-26 and 1-4-2026 to 28-06-2026.
- **Superannuation:** no Gazette instrument states a fund's terms; its trust deed does (F6). The law gives the bound:
  the 2025 Act Sch. XI Part B para 3(b), and Rules 2026 rr.307 – 308. The 1961 / 1962 equivalents for 2025-26 are not
  located.
- No term has been read into the plan yet. Each is read from these copies at build and shown in the report.

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1: the first day of a version that starts "on publication"** (F1).
- (a) The date printed on the Gazette issue (N11 15-12-2025, N12 14-07-2026, E1 29-06-2026). That is the publication
  the Gazette records; the id carries the upload date.
- (b) The regulator's and footnote's date (16-12-2025, 20-07-2026).

**C2: the 1952 EPF Scheme** (F4).
- (a) The pack states EPF liquidity only from 29-06-2026. Before that, the fact says the terms are not stated, and
  why. Place a row for the 1952 terms, BLOCKED on a copy.
- (b) You name a consolidation of the 1952 Scheme trustworthy (e.g. the EPFO's or India Code's). I build both
  periods from it.
- (c) You supply Gazette copies.

**C3: superannuation, and an exempted PF trust** (F5, F6).
- (a) The fact states only the law's bound: annuity on retirement, incapacity or death, and commutation per r.308. It
  says the fund's own rules govern within that bound. For an RPF, it states the EPF Scheme's terms and says an exempted
  trust's rules may differ.
- (b) Leave superannuation without a liquidity fact.

**C4: the shape** (names checked at build).
- (a) A `liquidity` list on each fund row (`exec/funds/`) and `EMPLOYEE_PF` / `_NPS` / `_SUPERANNUATION` component
  row. Each entry carries the trigger (the event), what may be withdrawn as the instrument states it, its `from`/`to`
  inside the year, and its provenance (file, paragraph, sha256). The row is published on `FundChange`,
  `FollowingChange` and `ComponentFacts`. No Java label (CLAUDE.md §4).
- (b) One text summary per row and year: smaller, but it hides the mid-year change (F3).
