### Findings

1. **Both Acts aggregate across employers in exactly two places:** other gratuity (S2 s.19(2)(a); 1961 s.10(10)(iii)
   first proviso) and leave salary (S2 s.19(2)(f); 1961 s.10(10AA)(ii) proviso). Each says *"one or more than one
   employer in the same tax year"*. The four entries here limit *"such amount"* or *"compensation received"* with no
   such words. Read against the two places that aggregate, the plain reading is that each payment is limited on its
   own.
2. **Statutory gratuity** is per employer by its nature: the statute's amount is each employer's. Nothing in either Act
   adds it up across employers.
3. **Commutation:** each employer's pension has its own commuted value. The gratuity condition says *"the employee has
   received gratuity"* (S2) / *"receives any gratuity"* (1961) and names no employer. Read literally, a gratuity from
   any employer counts, which is the code as built.
4. **Voluntary retirement** is the one I would not settle on the text alone. Each amount is capped at ₹5,00,000, and
   only another year is barred. Two schemes in one year would each get up to ₹5,00,000. The text supports that, but a
   lifetime ₹5,00,000 is widely stated in secondary sources, and I have read no CBDT guidance or decision either way.
   Retrenchment's notified ₹5,00,000 is the same question in milder form: the text is per compensation, with no
   once-only rule.
