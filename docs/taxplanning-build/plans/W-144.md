## Plan for W-144 — JaCoCo and PIT gates for the engine

**For:** PRD §5 N10 and N11 are enforced by the build, not asserted: coverage of `taxengine` cannot quietly fall, and
the cap shapes and marginal reliefs keep a mutation score whose expected values were derived by hand (`DR 07` X5.5).

### What I read and measured (session 48)

- **X5.5** (`taxplanning-design/07-architecture.md:627`): JaCoCo `check` on `taxengine` **95 % line, 90 % branch**;
  on the rules vocabulary (`taxrules`, now `taxengine.rules`, D-048) 90 / 85; PIT on the `CapShape` implementations
  and the marginal-relief functions, **90 %**. PRD §5 N10, N11 say the same.
- **Today's gates** (root `pom.xml`): JaCoCo `check-charges-engine` (and the charges services) at `verify`; the
  `mutation` profile targets `brokercharges` and the legacy `taxplanning.engine` at 85 %. Nothing gates `taxengine`.
- **Measured** (full run, 4629 tests, `target/site/jacoco/jacoco.csv`):

| Scope | Line | Branch | X5.5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `taxengine` outside `rules` | **95.25 %** (279 missed) | **81.56 %** (533 of 2,890 missed) | 95 / 90 |
| `taxengine.rules` | **92.26 %** (340 missed) | **76.91 %** (560 of 2,425 missed) | 90 / 85 |

  Most missed branches: `input` 310 (65.9 %), `node` 116, `rules.definition` 108, `rules.json` 97, `pipeline` 60,
  `rules.condition` 58 — `input` is chiefly record guards (refusals of malformed inputs).
- **PIT measured** (scope `rules.cap.*`, `rules.relief.*`, tests `taxengine.*`, 6 min): **94.2 %** — 97 of 103 killed;
  2 survived (`SlabLadder`, `PercentOfBaseByPopulation`), 4 without coverage (`PercentOfBaseByPopulation`,
  `PercentOfBaseByLocation`, `AggregateWithCeiling`, `LeastOf`).
- **So:** lines and PIT meet X5.5 today; **branches do not, by ~1,100 branches.** The written figure and the enforced
  one cannot both stand (memory: when they differ, the owner decides which side moves).

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — the branch gap.** (a) **Gate lines at X5.5's figures (they pass) and branches at today's floor as a ratchet —
81 % and 76 % — that may only rise**, with a placed row to bring branches to 90 / 85 package by package, `input`
first; the build fails on any fall. (b) Close the gap here: ~1,100 branches of new tests before any gate, a large item
in place of a small one. (c) Lower X5.5's branch figures to today's and call it done.

**C2 — the mutation gate.** (a) A **separate profile** `mutation-taxengine` (scope as measured, threshold 90 %), and the
six remaining mutants killed or covered by tests with hand-derived values — so it stands at 100 % of what is reachable,
or each survivor is named as equivalent. Not in CI (6 minutes); run like the existing `mutation` profile. (b) Add the
scope to the existing `mutation` profile: its single threshold is 85 %, below N11's 90.

**C3 — how the JaCoCo rule aggregates.** (a) One `check` execution per scope with class `includes`/`excludes` and a
`BUNDLE` rule, so the scope is judged as a whole, as X5.5 states it. (b) `PACKAGE` rules: every package held to the
figure on its own — today 20 of the 27 `taxengine` packages are under 90 % branch, so it fails at once.
