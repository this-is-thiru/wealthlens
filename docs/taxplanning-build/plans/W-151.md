## Plan for W-151 — s.516 rounding as rule data

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** the rounding rows state the whole of s.516's method — the
multiple and the point from which a remainder rounds up — and the rounding step applies
whatever its row says, instead of pinning ₹10 in Java and carrying the method in a `Money` method named after the
section (audit P1, D-313).

**This plan raises C1 – C3. C1 reverses D-076's refusal; it needs your explicit yes.**

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what the row states:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `RuleRounding(toNearest, roundsUpFrom)`:
    - `toNearest` — the multiple (₹10), as today;
    - `roundsUpFrom` — the remainder from which the amount goes up to the next multiple (₹5: *"the last figure … is five
      or more"*); a whole number of rupees, above nil and at most `toNearest`.

    *"Ignoring any part of a rupee consisting of paise"* needs no field: with a whole-rupee `roundsUpFrom`, dropping the
    paise first can never move a remainder across it (a remainder of 4.99 and of 4 are both below 5; 5.40 and 5 both
    at or above), so the method gives the same result either way. The record requires whole rupees and its Javadoc
    says why. (A field for it was in the first draft of this plan and was removed as having no effect.)

    `RoundingStep` applies them for any step; the ₹10 pin goes. **This reverses D-076** ("the node refuses any other
    step"): `RoundingNodesTest:171–191` changes from "a ₹100 row is refused" to "a ₹100 row rounds to ₹100" — a
    deliberate behaviour change, not a weakened assertion.
  - **(b)** keep D-076: remove nothing, record P1 as accepted ("the Acts state one method"). The audit finding is then
    closed without a change.
- **C2 — a negative amount (a refund):**
  - **(a) recommended:** stays as today — rounded on its magnitude, sign restored — in Java, named in the Javadoc as
    ROUND-NEG-001's reading (STATUS UNCLEAR). The Acts do not state it; a field for an unstated rule would be a
    guess made data.
  - **(b)** a fourth field for it.
- **C3 — the `Money` method:** `roundToNearestTenPerSection516()` replaced by `roundToMultiple(Money step, Money
  upFrom)` — a general method no longer named after a section (recommended); or keep both.
