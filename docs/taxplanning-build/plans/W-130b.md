**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** explain the differences that come from Chapter VIII
deductions:
- a claim declared to the employer but not proven (T8-2, MINOR, tax due at filing);
- a deduction the user is entitled to but never declared (T8-3, MINOR, refund due);
- the totals that move because of either.

### Findings

1. **A node's last trace step is not always its value.** `ChapterEightNode` writes one trace line per section and adds a
   capping line only where s.122(2) binds; its value is the total (`ChapterEightNode.java:228` – `:244`). W-130a's
   comparison reads a node's last step, which is right for `round.totalincome`, `tax.surcharge` and `tax.cess` but
   would read one section's amount for `chapterviii`.
2. **Row 11's Deductible Amount is the certificate's Chapter VIII total.** Its label reads "Aggregate of deductible amount
   under Chapter VIII … [10(a) + … + 10(k)]", and its columns are Gross / Qualifying / Deductible (`FORM_130.json`).
3. **A claim difference moves the lines after it.** A difference at row 11 changes rows 12, 15 – 17 and what Part B should
   have deducted. In T8-2 those differences are consequences, and the design still expects MINOR. If they stood
   unexplained, D-755 C2a's rule would make the report MATERIAL.
4. **Per-section attribution is possible but heavier.** Rows 10(a) – (k) against the trace's lines by section need a
   mapping from each row to the rules it covers. T8-2 and T8-3 are each one section, so the aggregate decides both.
