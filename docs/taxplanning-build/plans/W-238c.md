## Plan for W-238c — the employer's NPS contribution, movable

**For:** A2.6's own example. A user whose employer offers NPS learns how much pay to move into the employer's NPS
contribution, what it saves, and that the money moved is not take-home. Row `03-WORK-ITEMS.md` W-238c (D-489
finding). Parent W-238 (closed, D-490).

### What I read (session 65)

- **The input model.** The contribution is not a component. `EmployerFundContribution(int employment,
  EmployerFund fund, Money amount)` (`taxengine/input/EmployerFundContribution.java`) sits in
  `EnteredFacts.employerContributions` (`input/EnteredFacts.java:124`). `EmployerFund` has
  `RECOGNISED_PROVIDENT_FUND`, `NOTIFIED_PENSION_SCHEME` and `APPROVED_SUPERANNUATION_FUND`. No pack component
  stands for it (`exec/components/` has `EMPLOYEE_NPS` and `EMPLOYEE_SUPERANNUATION`, the employee's own).
- **How the engine treats it.** These rows key on the fact `employer.fund`:
  - `SAL-INCLUDES-PENSION-CONTRIBUTION` puts an NPS contribution into salary (S2 s.16(k); 1961 s.17(1)(viii)), read
    by `GrossSalaryNode.pensionContributions` (`node/GrossSalaryNode.java:98-105`).
  - `DED-NPS-EMPLOYER-OTHER` deducts it at 10 % of `SALARY` in the old regime (s.124(1)(b)).
  - `DED-NPS-EMPLOYER-NEW-TAX-REGIME` deducts it at 14 % in the new regime (s.124(2)).
  - `DED-NPS-EMPLOYER-GOVT` deducts it at 14 % for a Government employer.
  - `EmployerContributionNode` adds the excess of RPF + NPS + superannuation over the aggregate limit as a
    perquisite (`node/EmployerContributionNode.java:35-48`, `PERQ-EMPLOYER-CONTRIBUTION`).
  - `BASIC` counts in `SAL-SALARY-FOR-EMPLOYER-PENSION` (`exec/components/BASIC.json`).
  - The design's ids `DED-NPS-EMP-001/-002` and `PF-AGGCAP-001` are the rulebook's. The pack ids are the ones above.
- **The optimiser.** `Allocation` is keyed by `ComponentCode` (`optimise/Allocation.java:18`).
  `AllocationPricing.over` refuses a code the pack does not define (`:62-68`) and applies an allocation to one
  employment's components. `takeHome` counts components only (`:110-122`), so a contribution is already not cash
  (C11, C3 binds). `AllocationSpace` refuses an empty `movable` (`api/AllocationSpace.java`).

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1: which funds may move.**
- **(a) Recommended: NPS and superannuation, named by `EmployerFund`.** The provident fund is refused with a message
  pointing at W-238d, because it follows basic (C8). Both are the employer's free choice within C5, and the engine
  already prices both. No fund-specific code: the 10 % / 14 % caps and the aggregate limit are pack rows, so they are
  measured, not written.
- (b) NPS only, the row's literal words. Superannuation would then need its own item later for the same code path.

**C2: how a fund move is published.**
- **(a) Recommended: a separate list, `Optimisation.fundChanges`,** of `FundChange(fund, current, suggested,
  savingAlone, cashImpact)`. This mirrors the input model, where components and employer contributions are separate.
  The `cashImpact` is −(suggested − current): the residual's cash goes into a fund, by the same take-home rule.
  `Change` and its pack `ComponentFacts` are unchanged.
- (b) Fold funds into `changes` under a made-up component code. That names something the pack does not define, and
  `ComponentFacts` would then be invented.
- The rules a fund feeds (A2.6's `ruleIds`) are not published here. Reading them would mean scanning every rule's
  conditions for `employer.fund`. They belong with W-238e's per-component pack facts: I extend that row's text rather
  than add a row.

**C3: the request.** **(a) Recommended:** `AllocationSpace` gains `Set<EmployerFund> movableFunds`. It refuses
nothing to move (no component and no fund) and a fund entered twice for the residual's employment. A fund not yet
entered starts at nil, in the residual's employment. `DraftOptimisationRequest` gains optional `movableFunds`, absent
meaning none. `TaxCalculator.optimise` keeps its signature.

### Shape (names checked at build)

`Allocation` keyed by a sealed `Movable` (`Movable.Component(ComponentCode)`, `Movable.Fund(EmployerFund)`), so the
greedy, saturation and exhaustive steps work on funds unchanged. `AllocationPricing.applied` writes a fund's amount
into `employerContributions` (replacing, or adding for that employment). Each method ≤ 60 lines and ≤ 7 parameters.

### Hand figures (TY 2026-27; slabs and cess as `TaxCalculatorOptimiseTest`'s Javadoc)

1. **NPS, new regime wins.** Basic 16,00,000, special 8,00,000, no rent, an `OTHER` employer, NPS movable.
   - Before: 24,00,000 − 75,000 = 23,25,000 → 2,81,250, + 4 % = **2,92,500** (new).
   - NPS saturates at 14 % × 16,00,000 = **2,24,000**. Above that, salary is unchanged (the contribution is salary)
     and no more is deducted.
   - After: 23,25,000 − 2,24,000 = 21,01,000 → 2,00,000 + 25 % × 1,01,000 = 2,25,250, + 4 % = **2,34,260** (new).
   - Old regime after: 10 % cap 1,60,000; 24,00,000 − 50,000 − 1,60,000 = 21,90,000 → 1,12,500 + 30 % × 11,90,000 =
     4,69,500, + 4 % = 4,88,280. So the new regime stays.
   - Saving **58,240**, cash impact **−2,24,000**, residual 5,76,000.
2. **The aggregate limit binds.** As case 1, plus 6,00,000 entered to a recognised provident fund, so the headroom
   under the limit is 1,50,000.
   - Above 1,50,000, each rupee more is deducted and also added back as a perquisite, so total income is flat.
   - Suggested NPS **1,50,000**. After: 23,25,000 − 1,50,000 = 21,75,000 → 2,00,000 + 43,750 = 2,43,750, + 4 % =
     **2,53,500**.
   - The limit is `PERQ-EMPLOYER-CONTRIBUTION`'s TY 2026-27 version, `AmountInExcess` of `CONTRIBUTIONS` over
     `Fixed 750000` (S2 s.17(1)(h)), read in the pack. The 12 % RPF charge is TY 2025-26 only
     (`EmployerContributionNode.java:44-47`), so 6,00,000 to the RPF adds nothing here.
3. **Superannuation (C1a).** Case 1 with superannuation movable instead. There is no deduction, and the contribution
   is not salary until the aggregate limit.
   - Salary falls rupee for rupee up to 7,50,000. Special allowance 8,00,000 funds it.
   - After: 16,50,000 − 75,000 = 15,75,000 → 20,000 + 40,000 + 15 % × 3,75,000 = 1,16,250, + 4 % = **1,20,900**
     (new). Cash impact **−7,50,000**.
   - Old regime after: 15,50,000 → 2,77,500, + 4 % = 2,88,600. The new regime stays.
4. **Refusals:** RPF movable (names W-238d), nothing movable, the fund entered twice for that employment.
