## Plan for W-088k — the term decided on the tax side

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** stop `portfolio` deciding whether a gain is short-term or
long-term (F2, D-249). Instead the read-model publishes the dates and asset facts, and the tax engine decides the term
from tax-year-versioned holding-period rule data, as CLAUDE.md §4 requires ("every legal date, boundary … is rule data
on the tax side").

**A design item, and mis-sized for one commit (§7).** It needs:
- the law sourced; none of it is in `sources/`;
- a read-model change in `portfolio`;
- a new rule set with an engine step.

So **this plan proposes a split (D1 – D3)**, as W-088j2 did. Nothing is built from it.

- **`portfolio` today:**
  - `holding/TradeClassifier.classify` decides segment first: `INTRADAY` equity → `SPECULATIVE`; `FUTURES` /

  - `HoldingPeriodResolver` applies 11 policies from `data/holding-periods/holding-period-policies.json`:
    - 12 months for listed equity, equity-oriented funds, listed bonds and SGBs;
    - 36 months, or 24 for a transfer on or after 23-7-2024, for other funds and unlisted bonds;
    - s.50AA's "always short-term";
    - FD → `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS`.

    The test is `acquisitionDate.plusMonths(n).isBefore(transferDate)` (`:115–116`). These are dates and periods of
    law held in `portfolio`, outside rule data.
  - Its callers write `trade_outcomes.capital_gains_type`: `TradeOutcomeRecorder`, `PortfolioService`,
    `TradeMatchingService` and `ProfitAndLossService`. `ProfitAndLossService` also uses it for `portfolio`'s own P&L
    reports.
- **The tax path:**
  - `CapitalGainsSummaryService` groups rows into buckets keyed on `classification` and the facts
    (`BucketKey`, `:117–122`). The key has `acquiredOn`, but **no transfer date**.
  - `CapitalGainsAdapter` (`:56–63`) maps `SHORT_TERM` / `LONG_TERM` to `Term`, drops `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS`, and
    refuses `UNCLASSIFIED` and `SPECULATIVE`.
  - Every `RS-CG-PART` row tests `capitalgains.term`.
- **The law, as far as reached here:** the 1961 Act, thc copy (fetched this session, 955 pp.; iLovePDF, 2025-12-17).
  - s.2(42A) has a base of *"thirty-six months"*. The provisos give *"twelve months"* for a listed security, a UTI or
    equity-oriented-fund unit, or a zero coupon bond, and *"twenty-four months"* for an unlisted share or immovable
    property.
  - Explanation 1(i) has rules for the period of holding:
    - (d) a rights share, from allotment;
    - (f) a bonus, *"allotted without any payment"*, from allotment;
    - (g) a demerger share, including the demerged company's holding period;
    - (b) s.49(1) acquisitions, including the previous owner's period.
  - **The copy predates the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024's change for transfers on or after 23-7-2024.** It has no
    "23rd day of July, 2024". That change, and S2's counterpart for TY 2026-27, are **NOT YET READ**.

### Decisions (recommended first)

- **D1 — where the term is decided:**
  - **(a) recommended:** in the engine, by a rule set `RS-CG-TERM`. Its rows hold:
    - the asset facts it applies to;
    - the transfer-date window (validity on the transfer date, as the law keys it);
    - the months; or "always short-term" (s.50AA).

    An engine step derives `Term` from `acquiredOn` and the transfer date before the part rows run, traced citing
    its row. `CapitalGainsAdapter` stops mapping `SHORT_TERM` / `LONG_TERM` to `Term`: both become a capital-gains
    bucket whose term the engine decides.
  - **(b)** keep `portfolio`'s classification and only move `holding-period-policies.json` into the rule pack,
    read by `portfolio`. This breaks the module boundary: `portfolio` may not depend on `taxengine`.
- **D2 — what `portfolio` keeps:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `TradeClassifier` and `HoldingPeriodService` stay for `portfolio`'s own P&L reports and
    `trade_outcomes.capital_gains_type`, which the tax side **stops reading for the term**.
    - It still reads the segment-decided classes (`SPECULATIVE`, `UNCLASSIFIED`, `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS`) to route or
      refuse.
    - `HoldingPeriodService`'s duplicate law is recorded for **W-088l** (the law-in-code audit) to decide.
    - Removing it from `portfolio`'s reports is a product change, outside this row.
  - **(b)** delete it now and have `portfolio`'s reports show no term. That is a product change, and I do not
    recommend it here.

### What I am NOT doing

- Any code, data or rule in this item.
- Segment-decided classes (speculative intraday, derivatives, FD interest). They are law too (s.43(5) and others),
  but outside this row; W-088l's audit lists them.
- Indexation and grandfathering (W-088g / others).
- Asset kinds the engine has no part row for. A term for a fund or a bond decides nothing until those parts exist,
  so W-088k3 builds the equity rows and says which others wait.

### Risks or ambiguities

1. **Two terms for one disposal.** Until W-088l decides, `portfolio`'s report and the tax engine may disagree on a
   boundary day if Q1's answer differs from `plusMonths(n).isBefore(transfer)`. The tax result follows the rule
   data, and W-088k3's trace says which row decided.
2. **Bucket count.** Adding the transfer date to the key splits buckets by sale date. That is more buckets, but the
   gains are the same sums. Each is tested.
3. **Size:** three rows. k1 is reading only, k2 is about 100 lines, and k3 about 250, each within §7.
