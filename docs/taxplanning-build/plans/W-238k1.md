  plus the addition, with no new pricing code.

  the follower, so the addition can over-fill the ceiling.

    takes.

- (b) As W-238k: entered → entered + addition (60,000 → 1,12,500). The reader then has to add the two changes up to

**Risks or ambiguities I noticed:**
- **The trim only lowers.** Where an answer lowers basic, the follower falls and the headroom grows, but the addition
  stays at its point at the pay as entered. That is an under-fill, so cash is kept, never wasted.
  - Raising it needs the floor check and a case where basic falls.
  - **Proposed: row W-238k3,** placed after W-238k2 unless you say otherwise or drop it.
- **The trim rests on the bill following total income** under the answer's regime. The addition reaches only Schedule
  XV, which enters the bill through total income, so it is not re-priced.
- **The basic candidate under the old regime** assumes the HRA limbs move by the rupee. If the exemption rounds per
  month, the bisection may find an extra point under 26L. The greedy still ends at 26L, since each step saves at the
  same rate a rupee.
- **The ratio is the user's fact** (A2.2 C8). An entered `EMPLOYEE_PF` that already holds a voluntary part follows
  basic at that ratio. The optimiser cannot tell the two apart, and that is the input's meaning, not this item's.
