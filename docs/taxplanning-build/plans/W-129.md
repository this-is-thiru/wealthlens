**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** given every Form No. 130 read for one tax year:
- say how the employers' certificates fit together (one Part C from the last employer, or one per employer);
- report a gap or overlap between the employers' periods;
- warn where a standard deduction granted by each employer, or a previous employer's salary left undeclared, will leave
  tax to pay at filing.

### What the design says (`06-algorithms.md`, G5.3)

- **Inputs:** Part A/B blocks `(tan, periodFrom, periodTo, quarterlyTdsRows)` and Part C annexures.
- **(a)** "assert no two (tan, period) ranges overlap"; gaps give `Notice(ATTENTION, "unemployed or undocumented
  period")`.
- **(b)** One Part C with several Part A/Bs is `LAST_EMPLOYER_CONSOLIDATED`: row 1(e) should be non-zero, else
  `Notice(ACTION_REQUIRED, …, [TDS-SAL-12B-001])`. As many Part Cs as Part A/Bs is `PER_EMPLOYER`: "check Σ row 4(b)
  ≤ one year's standard deduction # SAL-STDDED-003" and "check Σ row 2(b) is consistent with one year of HRA #
  SAL-HRA-003". Otherwise `MIXED`, and M4.2's verdict `CANNOT_COMPARE`.
- "`PER_EMPLOYER` is where the money is … the highest-value single check … for the 'Priya' persona."

### Findings

1. **Two of G5.3's citations do not say what the checks rely on.**
   - SAL-STDDED-003 (`01-rulebook.md:542`) is "capped at the salary itself, so it cannot create or enlarge a loss"; the
     one-year ceilings are SAL-STDDED-001/002 (`:540` – `:541`).
   - SAL-HRA-003 (`:564`) is the "relevant period" of occupation. A sum of 2(b) across certificates cannot test it
     without each period's rent and salary, which Form No. 130 does not print.
2. **Overlap is not always an error.** Two jobs at once is lawful. One TAN twice for the same period is a revised
   certificate, which `13-edge-cases.md` B9-75 handles: "the later one supersedes by `certificateNo` +
   `lastUpdatedOn`".
3. **No notice type exists for documents.** `taxengine.trace.Notice` is internal, and `ComputedTax.Notice` belongs to a
   computation (it has a `sequence`). A second class named `Notice` would break the "no duplicate simple names" rule
   (`ArchitectureTest`).
4. **Readings name rows by path.** W-128c's facts already mark row A and 4(b). Part A's TAN, period and row 1(e) have
   none (`FORM_130.json`).
5. **The law is at hand.** `TaxCalculator.salaryCertificateLaw` (W-128c) gives each regime's ceiling. `ParseAttempt`
   (`ParseAttempt.java:15`) carries the document id beside the fields.
