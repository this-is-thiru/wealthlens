## Plan for W-337j2 — the Mongo client removed

> Planned in session 126 (2026-11-28). Placed by D-1016 (C1a); after W-337j1 (D-1017). Probed first: risk 1 of `plans/W-337j.md`
> (the `@Field`s) settled by a throwaway test, run and deleted.

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** remove the MongoDB client from the build — its configuration, the
Mongo converter interfaces, the BSON helper, the `@Field`s, the dependencies, the profiles' cluster URIs and the test container —
so the app starts and every test runs with no MongoDB and no `MONGO_*`, and the architecture rules guard `@Entity` / `@Column` instead.
