**What this item is for (one sentence):** run every `DR 08` T3 case, the HRA exemption, end to end through the
shipped salary pipeline in the years and regimes the coverage map gives it. Each run must produce the figure DR 08
states, with the PROVISIONAL runs reported separately.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C4). Two of them are places where DR 08 and the engine, as approved in
D-099, disagree. Under R3 the golden figure wins unless you decide otherwise.**

### Findings

1. **T3-1, T3-2, T3-3 and T3-5 need no change to the engine.** Worked by hand from the pack, they give DR 08's
   figures.
2. **T3-4a conflicts with D-099.** On 1 Apr – 31 Oct 2026, 214 days of 365, the engine computes:
   - salary 13,20,000 × 214/365 = 7,73,917.81;
   - limb (b) 2,10,000 − 77,391.78 = **₹1,32,608.22**, not ₹1,33,000.

   DR 08 assumes months; the Rules name no unit. Days split a mid-month move exactly. Months can't:
   `CalendarMonths` counts a part month as a whole one, so both homes in the month of a move would count it.
3. **T3-4b conflicts with D-099's silence.** DR 08 requires a nil line with the reason. The engine gives no line.
4. **The harness must carry salary facts and an exemption expectation**: pay lines, whether the terms provide DA,
   tenancies, the expected exemption and the limb that bound.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — T3-4a, the unit of the relevant period:**
  - **(a) recommended: keep days (D-099) and record an erratum to DR 08 T3-4a.** DR 08 assumes 7/12; r.279(2)(a)
    names no unit; days are the only basis that splits a mid-month move.
    - The case is authored as 1 Apr – 31 Oct 2026, expecting **₹1,32,608.22**, worked by hand as in finding 2 and
      shown in the case file beside DR 08's ₹1,33,000.
    - A new **OQ-8** asks whether the period should be measured in the months salary is paid for.
    - Longer term, the exact answer is the taxpayer entering the HRA and salary actually due for each period,
      since those are "the actual amount … received in respect of the relevant period". Not in this item.
  - **(b)** Switch HRA to calendar months. That gives ₹1,33,000, but a mid-month move double-counts its month, and
    `ExemptionNodeTest`'s mid-year case changes.
  - **(c)** Leave T3-4a out until OQ-8 is answered.
- **C2 — T3-4b, the new regime:**
  - **(a) recommended: follow DR 08.**
    - Where a tenancy is entered and the HRA row does not apply in the regime, `ExemptionNode` gives **one nil
      line per tenancy**, `limitedBy` "not in the NEW_TAX_REGIME regime".
    - With no tenancy entered there is no line, since nothing was claimed.
    - `ExemptionNodeTest`'s silence test changes to assert the line. That is a behaviour change, made
      deliberately.
    - The same silence in LTA and the allowances is **proposed, not done**. T5-4 (nil) will ask it of LTA.
  - **(b)** Keep D-099's silence, with an erratum to DR 08 T3-4b.
- **C3 — the harness:** `GoldenCase` gains an optional `salary` block and optional expectations.
  - The `salary` block holds pay lines by component code, `termsIncludeDearnessAllowance`, and tenancies (from, to,
    location as entered for that year, rent paid).
  - The expectations are `salaryExemptions` and `limitedBy` alongside `payable`, each case stating what DR 08
    states.
  - The runner puts the salary block into `EnteredFacts`. A case with a salary block and no total income runs the
    shipped pipeline **up to `salary.income`**, with no stand-in.
  - A case whose location differs by year (Bengaluru/Pune: named in TY 2026-27, `ANY_OTHER_PLACE` in TY 2025-26)
    carries one file per year. So T3-2 and T3-3's TY 2025-26 half are their own files, as DR 08 numbers them.
- **C4 — the test class:** `T3HouseRentAllowanceTest`, like T1 and T2.
  - One parameterized test runs every case in every year it names, asserting the exemption and, where stated,
    the limb.
  - PROVISIONAL runs are logged and **counted in a separate assertion**: the TY 2025-26 runs, and only they. This
    is "report separately".
  - Three cross-case assertions:
    - T3-2 = T3-1, the metro list is not load-bearing there;
    - T3-3 TY 2026-27 − T3-3 TY 2025-26 = ₹80,000;
    - T3-5 ≠ ₹2,40,000.
  - Limb wording is asserted **as the pack names it** ("rent less one-tenth of salary"), with DR 08's "10%" noted
    in the case file.
