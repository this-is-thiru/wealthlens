- **What the Gazette says (C17 – C21):**
  - **ss.153, 154 and 127 carry no employer condition.**
  - **ss.154(2)(c) and 127(6)** require a copy of the medical authority's certificate **furnished along with the
    return**, in the prescribed form and manner. A certificate needing reassessment lapses after its year unless a new
    one is furnished (ss.154(2)(b), 127(7)).
  - **s.124(1)**: the deduction arises only *"if the employer makes any contribution"*.
  - **r.205(2) Sl. 4** lists "Deduction under Chapter VIII" among the claims an employee may give the employer.

**Draft content (Claude's, for your adoption; no digits; each legal statement read in the Gazette, C17 – C21):**
- **Savings interest:**
  - Add up the interest every savings account paid you in the year (banks, co-operative banks, the Post Office).
  - Enter it as income in your return, and the deduction is worked out from it.
  - Tell your employer only if you want less tax deducted from salary.
  - *Mistakes:* counting fixed-deposit interest; leaving the interest out of income because "it is exempt".
- **Senior's deposit interest:**
  - Add up the interest on every deposit, fixed deposits included, from banks, co-operative banks and the Post Office.
  - Enter it as income, and the deduction follows.
  - *Mistakes:* claiming it before the year you are a senior citizen; also claiming the savings-interest deduction.
- **Own disability:**
  - Get a certificate from a medical authority.
  - Furnish a copy with your return, in the form the Rules prescribe.
  - If the certificate says the disability is to be reassessed, get a new one before it expires.
  - *Evidence:* the certificate, closed by the copy furnished with the return.
  - *Mistakes:* using an expired certificate; a dependant also claiming for you.
- **Dependant's disability:**
  - Check the person is your spouse, child, parent, brother or sister, and depends on you.
  - Keep the bills for their treatment, training and care, or the scheme's receipts.
  - Get the medical authority's certificate and furnish a copy with your return.
  - *Mistakes:* claiming for a dependant who claims the own-disability deduction; using an expired certificate.
- **Employer's NPS:**
  - Check your payslip or Form 16 for your employer's contribution to your NPS account.
  - If there is none, there is nothing to claim.
  - If there is one and Form 16 does not show the deduction, claim it in your return.
  - *Mistake:* counting your own contribution here.
