**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** compare a year's confirmed certificates with the engine's
computation, line by line. Explain each difference with one reason from M4.2's closed set, and give one verdict:
`MATCHED`, `MINOR_DIFFERENCE`, `MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE` or `CANNOT_COMPARE`.

### What the design says

- **M4.2** (`05-domain-model.md:534`):
  - `ReconciliationReport(computationId, documentIds, lines, verdict)`;
  - `ReconciliationLine(ruleId or formRow, documentValue, computedValue, difference, explanation)`;
  - eight reasons: declared but unproven (TDS-SAL-12BB-001); entitled but never declared (TDS-SAL-12B-001); other-head
    income raising TDS (TDS-SAL-REDUCE-001); Part B is tax + surcharge + cess (DOC-F130-TAXSUM-001); only the last
    employer's Part C (DOC-F130-ANNEX-001); a different regime (row A); rounding to ₹10 (RATE-ROUND-002); capital gains
    cannot be in Form No. 130.
  - "`CANNOT_COMPARE` is a first-class verdict".
- **Golden tests T8-1 – T8-5** (`08-golden-tests.md:821` – `:900`):
  - **T8-1:** two employers, previous salary undeclared and the standard deduction twice → `MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE`
    ₹2,14,500.
  - **T8-2:** 10(a) ₹1,50,000 declared, ₹60,000 proven → `MINOR_DIFFERENCE` "with a warning that additional tax will be
    payable".
  - **T8-3:** ₹25,000 s.126 never declared → `MINOR_DIFFERENCE`, refund due.
  - **T8-4:** Part B 2,14,500 = rows 13 + 15 + 16 − 14 → `MATCHED`.
  - **T8-5:** row A against 4(b), a contradiction. W-128c already reports it.

### Findings

1. **No rule decides MINOR from MATERIAL.** T8-2 owes tax at filing yet is MINOR; T8-1 owes tax and is MATERIAL. What
   separates them in the tests is the reason's nature: the user's own declarations (T8-2, T8-3) against an employer's
   error across documents (T8-1).
2. **Nothing maps a certificate row to the computation.**
   - `ComputedTax` (`ComputedTax.java:31`) exposes `totalIncome`, `bill`, `credits`, `finalAmount` and a
     `trace` of `Step(node, rule, out…)` (`:106`).
   - Rows carry facts (W-128c, W-129) but no link to a node.
3. **The inputs exist only as records.** `DocumentConfirmation(attemptId, acceptedFields, rejectedFields)`
   (`DocumentConfirmation.java:15`) is built, but storage and the confirm endpoint are W-131. W-129 gives the assembly
   (mode, `MIXED`).
4. **Some reasons need the computation's inputs, not its result.** "Declared but unproven" needs the user's proven claims
   per section; "entitled but never declared" needs the claims the employer never saw. These are the claim catalogue's
   facts (W-117, W-121).
5. **Size.** A model, a mapping, five or more reasons and a verdict rule: more than one item (§7).

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — the split:**
  - **(a) W-130a:** the report model (`taxplanning.documents.reconcile`: `ReconciliationReport`,
    `ReconciliationLine`, `Verdict`, `ReconciliationReason`). It compares a single-employer certificate's totals with
    the computation: row 12 total taxable income, row 17 tax payable, Part B's tax deducted against the computation's
    salary TDS. It has the reasons it can decide from the certificate and the computation alone (TAXSUM, a different
    regime, rounding, capital gains), `CANNOT_COMPARE` for a `MIXED` assembly, and T8-4's golden case.
  - **W-130b:** the claim reasons per Chapter VIII line (T8-2, T8-3; needs the claim facts).
  - **W-130c:** the multi-employer reasons from W-129's assembly (T8-1).
  - Each child gets its own plan and probe.
  - (b) one item.
- **C2 — the verdict rule** (finding 1):
  - **(a)** Every reason carries a class, as data with the reason:
    - **EXPECTED** — the user's own declarations or choices, TAXSUM, rounding, capital gains;
    - **EMPLOYER_ERROR** — previous salary undeclared, the standard deduction granted twice.
  - The verdict is then:
    - `MATCHED` when every line is within ₹10;
    - `MINOR_DIFFERENCE` when every difference has an EXPECTED reason;
    - `MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE` when any has an EMPLOYER_ERROR reason or no reason at all;
    - `CANNOT_COMPARE` for a `MIXED` year.
  - This reproduces T8-1 – T8-4. It is Claude-drafted, needs your adoption, and gets an erratum adding the rule to M4.2.
  - (b) A rupee threshold. No source gives one.
- **C3 — linking rows to the computation** (finding 2):
  - **(a)** A `computedBy` on a certificate line, as data in `FORM_130.json`: the engine node whose output the row
    should equal, validated against the pack's node ids. A later form version carries its own.
  - (b) A mapping table in `taxplanning` code.
- **C4 — the document side:**
  - **(a)** The confirmed values (`DocumentConfirmation.acceptedFields`), never the raw reading (M4: "nothing is used
    until this exists"). W-130 takes them as arguments, and W-131 stores them.
  - (b) The raw reading, until W-131.
- **C5 — the ₹10 tolerance:**
  - **(a)** The rounding reason for any difference within ₹10 (RATE-ROUND-002; G5.2's ₹10), so it never fails a match.
  - (b) Exact.
