## Plan for W-079e — dividend income out of the surcharge's 25%/37% test

**What this item is for (one sentence):** make `SurchargeNode` test the 25% and 37% clauses on total income
*excluding* dividend income, as the Finance Act says. This lifts W-079b's interim refusal for every case where taking
the dividends out leaves that ladder unbitten. The 15% limit on the dividends' own tax is still refused, because it
needs a measure of that tax which no text gives (C1, finding 3).

**This plan raises choices (C1–C5); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### Findings

1. **Most dividend cases need no split at all.** Where the excluding income is at or under ₹2 crore, the 25%/37%
   clauses don't apply. The rate is then 10% or 15% on the whole tax, and the last clause's 15% limit cannot bind.
   That covers every case the interim guard refuses today except those where the income *without* dividends still
   exceeds ₹2 crore.
2. **Where the excluding income exceeds ₹2 crore and there are dividends,** the non-dividend tax bears 25% or 37%
   and the dividends' own tax bears at most 15%. That needs the tax *"computed in respect of that part of income"*.
3. **For slab-taxed dividends, no text I have read says how to measure that tax.** It could be the tax on total
   income less the tax on the excluding income (a top slice), or a pro-rata share, or something else. Neither the
   Finance Act nor `DR 06` / `DR 13` answers it. This is a new open question, **OQ-26**, not something to choose
   silently (R1, R6).
4. **Unit income is not "dividend income"** on the words: s.2(40) is a company's distribution, and the Act names
   unit income separately. The interim guard counted it as dividend, which was the cautious reading for a refusal but
   is not right for computing the tax.
5. **"Dividend income" is what total income includes:** received, less the TY 2025-26 interest the head allows
   against it. The year's 20% limit is shared with unit income, so where it binds *and* units also bore interest,
   the dividends' share of the allowance is not determined by any text. That is a second part of OQ-26.
6. **Marginal relief needs no new rule.** It is on total income, and in every case W-079e computes, the rate on both
   sides of the threshold is the same 15%, so W = U + V gives no relief. U is still recomputed at the threshold with
   the excluding income = threshold − dividend income (floored at nil), so the code reads the Act one way throughout.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — scope:** **(a) recommended:**
  - the 25%/37% ladder reads total income less dividend income;
  - a case where that ladder still bites with dividend income present is refused, naming OQ-26 and the item that
    builds the 15% limit (C4);
  - every other case is computed.

  **(b)** Also build the 15% limit here, on one of finding 3's measures. That needs OQ-26 answered first.
- **C2 — what counts as dividend (finding 4):** **(a) recommended:** `DIVIDEND` and `DEEMED_DIVIDEND`. `UNIT_INCOME`
  stays in the excluding income, so a unit-income case is no longer refused. **(b)** Count unit income as dividend
  too, as the interim guard did.
- **C3 — the amount (finding 5):** **(a) recommended:** dividends and deemed dividends received, less the interest the
  head allows against the dividend items. When the limit doesn't bind, that is the interest entered on them; when it

  split. When it binds above nil and unit items bore interest too, the case is refused, naming OQ-26. The allowance is computed once: `OtherSourcesNode`'s arithmetic moves to a
  package-private static method both nodes call, and its trace is unchanged. **(b)** The dividends as received,
  ignoring the deduction: simpler, but it takes out more than total income holds.
