### A. How the notice travels (recommended first)

- **A1 (recommended): a field in the body, without changing its shape.** The endpoint returns a new
  `taxplanning/salary/dto/KnownErrorsComputation` record:
  - `@JsonUnwrapped TaxComputationEntity computation`;
  - `String knownErrors`.

  Serialised, the JSON is the entity's own fields, unchanged, plus one top-level `knownErrors`
  field. Existing clients keep working: the current assertion on `newRegimeResult` is a live check
  of that. The entity (Mongo) is not touched.
- **A2:** an HTTP `Warning: 299` header. It changes no body, but a person looking at the response
  doesn't see it, and RFC 9111 has made `Warning` obsolete.
- **A3:** a wrapper `{computation, knownErrors}`. It's the most visible, but it breaks every client
  that reads `data.newRegimeResult`.

**The notice text** (a constant on the record; no figure typed):
> "Known errors: this calculator is being replaced and its results are known to be wrong in some
> cases — for example, it charges no surcharge at high incomes under the new regime. Do not rely on
> these figures."

It is sourced from brief §12 OQ-3.
