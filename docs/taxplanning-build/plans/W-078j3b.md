## Plan for W-078j3b — the TY 2025-26 opt-out vehicle by business income

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** let one year's election protocol name a different
vehicle and form for a class of taxpayer, so TY 2025-26's opt-out says Form No. 10-IEA by the s.139(1) due date to a
person with business income and "in the return" to one without, as Income-tax Rules, 1962 r.21AGA(1) prescribes.

### What I read

- **The row:** WI W-078j3b *(D-174)*. `plans/W-078j3.md` Revision 1. D-133 (elections versioned per year).
- **The law (already READ in W-078j3, `sources/2026-10-11-rules-2a-to-26c.md` G8, G9):** r.21AGA(1), inserted by
  G.S.R. 452(E), 21-6-2023 (egazette 2023/246719 p.6):
  - *"(a) in Form No. 10-IEA on or before the due date specified under sub-section (1) of section 139 for furnishing

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the shape.**
  - **(a) Recommended: routes on the protocol.** A new record `VehicleRoute(List<Condition> appliesWhen,
    ElectionVehicle vehicle, Optional<FormRef> form)`, and `ElectionProtocol` gains `List<VehicleRoute>
    vehicleWhen` (empty = everyone uses the protocol's own vehicle). It also gains `vehicleFor(ConditionContext)`:
    the first route whose conditions all hold wins, otherwise a route built from the protocol's own vehicle and
    form.
    - This is the same idiom as `bindingAppliesWhen`, with the same `HasIncomeUnderHead` condition, entirely in data.
    - JSON key `vehicleWhen`, optional (absent = empty). Only TY 2025-26 carries it, so TY 2026-27 compiles
      unchanged.
  - (b) Put the bound person's vehicle on `BindingSpec`. It is shorter, but it ties "who is locked in" (s.115BAC(6)
    proviso) to "how the option is made" (r.21AGA). The two coincide today only by accident of drafting.
  - (c) A new enum value such as `PRESCRIBED_FORM_IF_BUSINESS`. It hard-codes the condition in Java, against D-PURE
    and the data-driven design.
- **C2 — withdrawal (r.21AGA(2)).**
  - (a) Recommended: no separate withdrawal field. Only a person with business income can withdraw (the proviso to
    s.115BAC(6)), and their route already names Form No. 10-IEA, whose title covers *"exercise … or withdrawal"*.
    The form's `displayName` is that title, copied, and the provenance quotes r.21AGA(2).
  - (b) Add a `withdrawalVehicle`. It would be a field with no difference from the route today, which is
    speculative.
- **C3 — the provenance after the fix.** (a) Recommended: `FIRST_SCHEDULE_RATES` TY 2025-26 → `SOURCE_CHECKED`.
  s.115BAC(6) is read (FA 2023 s.52(C)(e), p.35), (1A) is read (F(No.2)A 2024 s.37; FA 2025 s.25), r.21AGA is read in
  the Gazette and agrees with the Department, and the interim FA 2024 is read. Nothing is left in its whatToCheck.
  (b) Leave it `UNVERIFIED` until a reviewer looks.
- **C4 — the design record.** (a) Recommended: TS T2 adds `VehicleRoute` to `regime/`, with a Changes entry (TS is
  edited in place, D-046). An erratum goes under DR 17 L3's `ElectionProtocol` record, stating that one year can
  need two vehicles (r.21AGA(1)) with the correction, verified by the Gazette page. (b) Only the TS change.

### Tests (B1: the item carries its own)

- `TaxYear2025RulePackTest`, the shipped TY 2025-26 opt-out:
  - for a taxpayer with business income, `vehicleFor` → `PRESCRIBED_FORM`, form `10-IEA`, and the protocol's
    deadline `DueDateUnder("139(1)")`;
  - for a salaried taxpayer → `IN_THE_RETURN`, no form.
- `RegimeLifecycleTest`, a nested group on the record (reusing `taxpayerWith`):
  - a route with no conditions is rejected;
  - a `PRESCRIBED_FORM` route without a form is rejected;
  - a form on an `IN_THE_RETURN` route is rejected;
  - with no routes, everyone gets the protocol's own vehicle;
  - the first matching route wins.
- Each new guard is proven by a deliberate violation, then reverted (CLAUDE.md §5).
