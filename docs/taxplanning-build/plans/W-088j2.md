## Plan for W-088j2 — establishing STT for a disposal with no breakdown

**What this item is for (one sentence):** choose how a disposal whose charge breakdown is missing gets an STT state the
law can use, so W-088j's `UNKNOWN` stops refusing most real equity gains. W-088j's runbook §3.3 expects that state to
cover most rows while the charge flags are off.

**A design item (its row: "the options chosen when taken, a design item first").** This plan chooses the approach and
proposes a split. Nothing is built from it. **It raises D1–D3 and one legal question, Q1.**

  - **Caveat:** `segment` defaults to `DELIVERY` for everything recorded before Chunk 10b (`AssetEntity:66`). For

### Q1 — is *"chargeable"* enough for s.198's *"paid"*?

s.198(1)(c)(i) asks whether STT *"has … been paid"* on the acquisition and on the transfer. On a recognised exchange
STT is collected on the transaction, so a chargeable exchange trade has, in the ordinary course, had it paid. That is
a reading, not a text I have read. j2a sources Chapter VII's collection provisions and puts the reading to you with
them:
- **(a)** chargeable means paid for s.198, with the reason quoted;
- **(b)** s.198 needs the breakdown or a declaration (B);
- **(c)** something else the text shows.

I will not build s.198's acceptance before that ruling.
