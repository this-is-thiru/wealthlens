### Build record — stopped under C2a (session 49)

**What was run.** A synthetic timeline for TY 2099-00 – 2101-02 (`SYN_DEFAULT` nil to 5,00,000 then 10 %; `SYN_CLASSIC`
nil to 3,00,000 then 20 %, `OPT_OUT`, `PRESCRIBED_FORM`; `SYN_FLAT` one band at 8 % from nil, `OPT_IN`, binding on later
years, in force to 2101-03-31; the standard deduction 50,000 to 30 June 2099 and 80,000 from 1 July,
`PRORATE_PER_SEGMENT` on `DAYS`, `ExceptIn(SYN_FLAT)`), locked and loaded through the production boot path
`ShippedRulePacks.compile`, validated by `PackValidator`, computed by the production `TaxCalculator`. Generator and
throwaway spike kept as `tools/w146_synthetic_pack.py` and `tools/zz_synthetic_spike_test.java.txt` (usage in the
generator's header); nothing of them is left in `backend/`. No `src/main` file was touched.

**What held, with data alone:** the timeline reads, compiles, locks and validates for all three years with three regimes;
a flat rate as a one-band `SlabLadder` (C3a) loads and charges; `SYN_FLAT` is in the 2099-00 and 2100-01 packs and
absent from 2101-02, where the comparison prices the other two (T17-11's data half); the mid-year proration gives
salary 10,00,000 a total income of **9,27,480** in 2099-00 — by hand 50,000 × 91/365 + 80,000 × 274/365 = 12,465.75 +
60,054.79 = 72,520.55; 10,00,000 − 72,520.55 = 9,27,479.45 → 9,27,480 — and **9,20,000** in 2100-01 (one version,
80,000). Those figures came with borrowed rows (F1's probe), so they show the mechanism, not a finished case.
