## Plan for W-073a — the aggregate employer contribution

**What this item is for (one sentence):** add to salary the part of the employer's contributions to a recognised
provident fund, the notified pension scheme and an approved superannuation fund that together exceed ₹7,50,000 in the
tax year, both years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C5); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-073a` row** (D-125) and `plans/W-073.md` (read this session): S2 s.17(1)(h), Gazette 265620 p.38 — *"aggregate
  amount of any contribution, in excess of ₹ 750000 in a tax year, made to the account of the assessee by the
  employer— (i) in a recognised provident fund; (ii) in the scheme referred to in section 124(1); and (iii) in an
  approved superannuation fund"*; 1961 s.17(2)(vii) as substituted by FA 2020 s.13, Gazette 218938 p.21 — *"(a) in a
  recognised provident fund; (b) in the scheme referred to in sub-section (1) of section 80CCD; and (c) in an approved
  superannuation fund, to the extent it exceeds seven lakh and fifty thousand rupees in a previous year"*.
- **`DR 01` R13** `PF-AGGCAP-001` and its worked example: 2,88,000 + 3,36,000 + 2,00,000 → **74,000**. `DED-NPS-EMP-*`:
  the employer's NPS contribution is also **deducted** under s.124(1)–(2) — a Chapter VIII matter (W-075/W-076), not
  netted against this charge (R13's note).
- **`02-TECH-SPEC`:** `employer.contrib.charges` — `EmployerContributionNode`, `MoneyValue`, an input to
  `salary.gross`.
- **Code:** `GrossSalaryNode` adds `salary.perquisites` on a second line (D-111); no pipeline node, NodeId or input for
  employer contributions exists; no pay line carries them.

### Findings

1. **"Aggregate … in a tax year"** is per assessee — every employer's contributions together (D-125 C2).
2. **The charge is a perquisite** (s.17(1)(h) sits in the definition of "perquisite"), salary in every regime, for every
   employee — no specified-employee test.
3. **The contributions themselves are not salary** otherwise: only the excess enters income. They are not pay lines,
   so they cannot come from the component partition.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — input:** `EnteredFacts.employerContributions`, each an **`EmployerFundContribution`**: the employment; the fund
  (`RECOGNISED_PROVIDENT_FUND`, `NOTIFIED_PENSION_SCHEME`, `APPROVED_SUPERANNUATION_FUND`); the amount the employer
  contributed in the tax year. Any other fund (an unrecognised PF) is not in the aggregate and has no value to enter
  here.
- **C2 — node:** **`EmployerContributionNode`** producing `employer.contrib.charges` — one trace line, the excess of the
  aggregate over the limit, or none where nothing is entered. `GrossSalaryNode` adds it on its own line, as it adds
  perquisites (the tech spec already lists it as an input).
- **C3 — row:** `PERQ-EMPLOYER-CONTRIBUTION`, `AllRegimes`, both years — `AmountInExcess` of `CONTRIBUTIONS` over
  `Fixed` 7,50,000. "In excess of" / "exceeds": exactly ₹7,50,000 gives nil.
- **C4 — pipeline:** both regimes gain `employer.contrib.charges` before `salary.gross`.
- **C5 — verification:** TY 2026-27 `SOURCE_CHECKED`; TY 2025-26 `UNVERIFIED` — the figure is read in the Gazette, but
  the interim Finance Act, 2024 is unread for s.17(2)(vii).
