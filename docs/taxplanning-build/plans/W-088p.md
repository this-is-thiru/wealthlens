- **The helpers share one shape.** Each fixes its node and step kind, and builds a line from:
  - a sequence;
  - a rule id;
  - that rule's governing version's provenance (`TemporallyApportionedCap.governingVersion(ctx.rule(id), temporal)`);
  - money in and out;
  - optionally a limit text, through `withLimitedBy`.
- **`NodeContext.forNode(node)`** (`pipeline/NodeContext.java:108`) knows the node but does not keep its `produces()`.
- **Checkstyle** (`checkstyle.xml:71–74`) caps `ParameterNumber` at 7. It counts methods and constructors, not record
  components. W-088v lowers it to 5 with a shrinking baseline.
- **`ConditionContext`** (10 components) was left to this item by W-088m2. It is a record: data, not a method, and
  outside the parameter rule.
- **W-088q** ("one carried-loss mechanism … heads absorbable, span, return condition") reshapes exactly the
  set-off / carried-loss methods above.

### Proposed split (C0)

| Row | What | Why separate |
|---|---|---|
| **W-088p1** | **Trace lines from the node's context.** `NodeContext` keeps its node's id, and `ctx.line(sequence, rule, kind, in, out)` (5 parameters) builds the line under that node, with the rule's governing-version provenance. The 13 `line(...)` helpers are deleted and their callers use `ctx.line(...)` (`.withLimitedBy(...)` as now). `TraceLine.of` stays the record's factory for lines that are not a rule's money step (for example `TaxByBasis` values), and is W-088v's baseline entry. Behaviour unchanged: every trace line identical. | One mechanism, about 15 files, provable by identical traces |
| **W-088p2** | **The other node and engine methods.** One named type each for `DonationDeduction.deduct`, `ExemptionNode.period`, `OtherSourcesNode.wholeDeducted`, `RetirementNode.payment` / `voluntaryRetirement` and `ElectionResolver.runChoosing`, designed in its plan from what each passes together. | Six small, unrelated designs |
| **→ W-088q** | **The set-off context** (the 7 set-off / carried-loss methods) moves into W-088q, which rebuilds those methods as one mechanism. | Typing them now would be rewritten by W-088q |

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C0 — split:**
  - **(a) recommended:** p1, p2, and the set-off context to W-088q, as above.
  - **(b)** one item: about 30 files across three unrelated designs.
- **C1 — the five validators and private constructors** (`NodeContext`'s private constructor, `requireDescribed`,
  `requireDeclarations`, `Instrument.assented`, `PackDiff.compare`):
  - **(a) recommended:** leave them to W-088v's baseline, each with its reason. They take a record's components to
    validate them, or are private constructors, and a wrapper type would add a second copy of the record.
  - **(b)** type each in p2.
- **C2 — `ConditionContext`'s 10 components:**
  - **(a) recommended:** no change. It is a record, the parameter rule is about methods, and W-088m2's note is closed
    here with that reason.
  - **(b)** group its person facts into a `PersonFacts` record in p2.
