**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** let a draft record, per kind of claim, what the person can
actually show for it — so the integrity panel (W-119c) and the checklist (W-120) have something true to read — without
that record ever changing the tax (W-119 C4a, C6a).

**Probe (2026-11-15; the Gazette read, code read, nothing run):**
- **r.205(2) is read** (`sources/2026-11-15-evidence-of-claims.md` E1 – E3; Gazette 271092 p.1687): four rows —
  house rent allowance (landlord's name, address, PAN above the rent threshold, and relationship); leave travel
  (evidence of expenditure); house-property interest (**lender's name, address and PAN**); Chapter VIII (evidence of
  investment or expenditure). Not searched: amendments after 20-3-2026 (E4); the TY 2025-26 counterpart's Sl. 2 – 4
  (E5).
- **Three things in W-119's C4a list need correcting against that reading:**
  - *"The lender's certificate held"* is not r.205's; it is a condition of the higher interest limit and **already an
    engine fact** (`taxengine/input/HousingLoan.java`, `lenderCertificateHeld`, read at
    `node/HousePropertyInterest.java:228`). Recording it again would let two answers disagree.
  - *Lender's particulars on file:* the profile's `Lender` has `name`, `pan`, `kind` and **no address**
    (`taxplanning/profile/Lender.java:32-39`); `Landlord` has all of name, address, PAN and relationship
    (`Landlord.java:33-44`).
  - *Months of rent receipted* is the product's record, not a requirement of the table (E3).
- **Where it lives:** as W-122a's plan found, a part of `DraftInputs` beside `ThirdParties`
  (`taxplanning/workspace/DraftInputs.java:23`) is stored encrypted, patched with `If-Match`, kept in revisions,
  defaulted when absent, and never handed to the engine (`assemble/EngineInputsAssembler.java:65-66`).
- **What the amounts attach to:** `Tenancy(occupied, location, ownedByAssessee, rentPaid)`, `Journey`,
  `QualifyingPayment(code, amount)` — lists in `EnteredFacts`, each item known only by its position.
- **Names:** `rules/catalogue/ClaimEvidence` exists (what a claim needs); this record is `EvidenceHeld` (W-119 C4a),
  a different simple name.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: rent.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** per tenancy, by its position in the entered list: the months of that tenancy for which a
    receipt is held (none above the months it runs). A patch that leaves the two lists of different lengths is
    refused in words, so removing a tenancy means removing its evidence in the same patch.
  - C1b: one count of receipted months for the year — cannot say which home when rents differ, which W-119c's second
    run needs.
- **C2: the lender's address.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** add `address` to the profile's `Lender`, encrypted as `Landlord`'s is; a lender stored
    before reads as no address, and "particulars on file" is then name, address and PAN all present — computed from
    the profile record a draft names, never typed as a yes/no.
  - C2b: record "particulars on file" as a yes/no on the draft — a second statement of what the profile already
    shows.
- **C3: the lender's certificate.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** not in `EvidenceHeld`; W-119c and W-120 read the engine fact that exists. This corrects
    C4a's list.
  - C3b: record it here as well.
- **C4: which Chapter VIII payments carry an evidenced amount in this item.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** qualifying payments, by their code (the largest family, and what `DR 12` T12-13 tests).
    Health payments, donations, loan interest and the rest are placed as **W-119b1**, after W-119c shows the shape
    working — a row is added at approval so nothing is left unplaced.
  - C4b: every Chapter VIII input now — six or more input families, each with its own identity question.
- **C5: leave travel.**
  - **C5a (recommended):** per journey, by position: the expenditure for which evidence is held, not above what the
    journey's travellers incurred.
  - C5b: one amount for the year.

**Roles:**
- **Architect:** evidence is what the person holds, the product's fact; it sits beside the entries and outside the
  engine, and where the profile or the engine already knows a thing it is read from there, not typed twice.
- **PM:** the person answers "what can you show?" once per claim; nothing they answer can raise their tax.
- **Senior engineer:** one record, one draft part, patch checks, one profile field.
- **Automation tester:** absent reads as none; each bound refused; revisions carry it; figures and fingerprint
  unmoved (shown red once); the address encrypted.
- **Manual tester:** enter a twelve-month tenancy, record eight months receipted, compute before and after and see
  the same tax; try thirteen and read the refusal.

**Risks or ambiguities I noticed:**
- Position as identity is fragile: reordering entries silently re-attaches evidence. The length check catches
  removal, not reordering; if drafts' lists are ever reordered by a client, entries need ids — a larger change,
  flagged, not done here.
- The profile's landlords are named by a draft (`ThirdParties.landlordIds`) but a tenancy does not say which landlord
  is its own; "particulars on file" for rent is therefore per draft, not per tenancy — W-119c's plan must say so.
- r.205's later amendments are not searched (E4); nothing in this item turns the table into rule data, so nothing
  ships resting on it — W-120 will, and must close E4 first.
