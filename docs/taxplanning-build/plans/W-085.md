## Plan for W-085 — `CapitalGainsSummary`, `CapitalGainsBucket`

**What this item is for (one sentence):** declare `portfolio`'s published capital-gains read-model. It holds the year's
**gross** realised amounts per classification and asset type, never a tax (`DR 13` R-10; `DR 15` F5; `DR 07` X1.3), so
that W-086 can publish it and the tax side can map it into the engine's `CapitalGainsInput`.

**This plan raises choices C1–C4. Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### Findings

1. **The rate basis cannot be decided from the P&L entity.** The tax rate differs by asset (listed equity and
   equity-oriented funds against everything else), and `ProfitAndLossEntity` sums the assets together. The read-model
   needs **asset type and sub-class per bucket**, which only `trade_outcomes` has. So W-086 builds it from there.
2. **`portfolio` cannot supply brought-forward losses, and should not.** A brought-forward balance is what earlier
   *returns* left after their own set-off (s.108(2), s.111). That is a tax figure, and the tax side owns it (s.111 →
   W-088), as the house-property loss is carried in the tax ledger (W-081b). F5's *"`portfolio` supplies … un-netted
   brought-forward balances"* is wrong about the data it names. That needs an erratum.
3. **F5's `ProfileId` and `TaxYearId` are not visible to `portfolio`.** X1.3's own sketch already uses `email`; the
   year is `portfolio`'s `financialYear` string.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the bucket:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `CapitalGainsBucket(CapitalGainsType classification, AssetType assetType,
    Optional<String> instrumentSubClass, double gainRupees)`.
    - The gross gain, which may be negative (a loss), is canonicalised by `TMoney.scale`; a non-finite amount is
      refused.
    - It carries no rate basis and no tax. The tax side maps (classification, asset type, sub-class) to a `Term` and a
      `RateBasisHint` (W-088).
    - The full five-way partition is kept (X1.3).
  - **(b)** R-10's literal bucket with a `RateBasisHint`. It cannot compile in `portfolio` (D-025).
  - R-10's `portfolioRuleRef` is left out: nothing fills it and nothing reads it.
- **C2 — brought-forward losses:**
  - **(a) recommended:** none in the read-model (finding 2). An erratum under `DR 15` F5 and one under `DR 07` X1.3
    (whose sketch has two brought-forward fields): the balances are the tax ledger's, owned by W-088 with S2 s.111.
  - **(b)** a `broughtForward` list for W-086 to fill. It has no source.
- **C3 — the summary:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `CapitalGainsSummary(String email, String financialYear, List<CapitalGainsBucket> buckets,
    Instant asOf, String portfolioComputationRef)`, records in `portfolio/dto/`.
    - It refuses nulls, a blank email or reference, and two buckets with the same (classification, asset type,
      sub-class).
    - Buckets are copied defensively.
  - `portfolioComputationRef` stays: `CapitalGainsInput` needs it for the pin (F4.1).

  - WI: the W-085 row note, and Appendix l.1354 → built;
