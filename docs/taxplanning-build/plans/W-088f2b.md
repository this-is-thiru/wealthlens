### The shape

**`ItemDateBefore(ItemFactId fact, LocalDate date)`** (`rules/condition`, the 16th permit):
- `holds` is `ctx.item().date(fact).isBefore(date)`, which is strict. *"before the 1st day of October, 2004"*
  excludes that day, and 1-10-2004 itself falls under (II).
- `describe()` returns *"{fact} is before {date}"*.
- Nulls are refused.

**`ItemFacts`** gains a third component, `Map<ItemFactId, LocalDate> dates` (copied defensively), and a
`date(fact)` lookup that **throws** for an absent fact, as `code` and `quantity` do. Six construction sites get
`Map.of()`:
- `NONE`;
- `of(…)`;
- `MotorCarPerquisite:178`;
- `CapitalGainsNode`, which gets its date instead;
- `NodeContextRuleSetTest` (×2);
- `ConditionTest` (×1).

**`ConditionJson`:** tag `ItemDateBefore` → `new ItemDateBefore(new ItemFactId(fact), PackJson.date(node, "date",
path))`.

**`CapitalGainsNode.factsOf`:** adds the date fact `capitalgains.acquired.on`, set to `BucketFacts.acquiredOn()`.

**Row `CG-PART-LONG-NOTIFIED`** in `RS-CG-PART`, definition `BucketPart(LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY)`, `AllRegimes`, with
two versions. Its condition, limbs in this order:
```
AllOf(
  ItemFactIs(capitalgains.term, LONG_TERM),
  ItemFactIs(capitalgains.asset, EQUITY),
  ItemFactIs(capitalgains.stt.transfer, PAID),
```
