## Plan for W-069 — the allowance exemptions

**What this item is for (one sentence):** exempt the salary allowances the Act exempts: duty allowances up
to what was actually spent (Sch. III Table Sl. 12), and the prescribed allowances up to their prescribed limits
(Sl. 13). This is done by regime, with the new regime's uncertain subset flagged where every figure that uses
it can be seen.

**This plan raises design decisions (C0–C6) and a sourcing gap before anything is built.**

### Findings

1. **TY 2025-26 amounts can't be sourced here.** R1 forbids guessing them, and the compendium's figures are
   known to have been amended since. This blocks the TY 2025-26 half of the Sl. 13 rows and the 2025-26
   new-regime subset, unless they're sourced first.
2. **"Seed from the section, flag visibly":** s.202(2)(a)(ii) says only a *prescribed* subset of Sl. 12 and 13
   survives in the new regime. r.280(3) prescribes one but addresses it to the opt-out. In the old regime Sl.
   12 and 13 apply in full anyway, so the subset only means something in the new regime. Seeding from the
   section means applying r.280(3)'s list in the **new** regime, and marking that version so any figure citing
   it shows as unverified.
3. **Transport for disabled employees needs more than a figure:**
   - "Metro Cities" is undefined in r.280;
   - "plus dearness allowance thereon" is unexplained;
   - it needs the employee's disability.
4. **Sl. 12 allowances need the amount actually spent**, and CEA/hostel need children and months. None is
   entered today.
5. **The armed-forces and area allowances (r.280(2) Sl. 1–6, 9, 11–15)** are defence and remote-area, and the
   rulebook puts them out of MVP scope.
