## Plan for W-078c — unrealised rent (and stock-in-trade, deferred)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** leave rent the owner cannot realise out of the actual rent before the annual
value is found (S2 s.21(4) with Rules 2026 r.21; 1961 s.23(1) Explanation with Rules 1962 r.4), in both years. Stock-in-trade
(s.21(5)) is recorded as deferred with the business head.

### What I read

- **The row:** WI W-078c: unrealised rent (S2 s.21(4); 1961 s.23(1) Explanation, r.4) after sourcing the Rule;
  stock-in-trade (s.21(5)) deferred with the business head *(D-155)*. "⚠ the Rule not read". **`DR 01` R16**
  `HP-AV-UNREAL-001`: "VERIFIED as to the section; the prescribed conditions are NEEDS VERIFICATION". `HP-AV-STOCK-001`.
- **Sourced and read this session (the ⚠ is cleared):**
  - **S2 s.21(4)** (Gazette 265620, p.47): "The rent which cannot be realised by the owner shall not be included in
    computing the actual rent received or receivable, subject to the rules as may be made in this behalf."
  - **Income-tax Rules, 2026 r.21** (G.S.R. 198(E), Gazette CG-DL-E-20032026-271092, p.1551; the Gazette the pack
    already cites 162 times): "For the purposes of section 21(4), the amount of rent which the owner cannot realise
    shall be equal to the amount of rent receivable by the assessee but not paid by a tenant of the assessee and so
    proved to be lost and irrecoverable where, — (a) the tenancy is bona fide; (b) the defaulting tenant has vacated,
    or steps have been taken to compel him to vacate the property; (c) the defaulting tenant is not in occupation of
    any other property of the assessee; and (d) the assessee has taken all reasonable steps to institute legal
    proceedings for the recovery of the unpaid rent or satisfies the Assessing Officer that legal proceedings would be
    futile."
  - **1961 s.23(1) Explanation** (thc p.146): "For the purposes of clause (b) or clause (c) of this sub-section, the
    amount of actual rent received or receivable by the owner shall not include, subject to such rules as may be made
    in this behalf, the amount of rent which the owner cannot realise." So it applies to the higher-of comparison and
    to the vacancy rule, as S2 s.21(4) does by excluding the rent from "actual rent" throughout.
  - **Income-tax Rules, 1962 r.4** (the Department of Revenue compendium on dor.gov.in, p.1.54; the copy
    `SAL-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE` cites, current to 2010-11): the same four conditions, "rent payable but not paid", and
    "would be useless". It was substituted by the IT (Eighth Amdt.) Rules, 2001, w.e.f. 1-4-2002 (footnote 82).
  - **S2 s.21(5):** stock-in-trade not let has a nil annual value for two years from the end of the financial year of
    the completion certificate ("nil up to" by FA 2026 s.37).
- **Code:**
  - `HouseProperty` has nine fields, and its record constructor is near checkstyle's complexity limit.
  - `HousePropertyNode.annualValue` takes `rentReceivable` straight into the higher-of and the vacancy test.
  - `arrearsReceived` already covers "unrealised rent realised subsequently" (S2 s.23; W-078a), so a rent left out
    now and recovered later is caught.
  - `HouseOccupancy` has no stock-in-trade value.

### Findings

1. **The Rule's conditions are facts only the assessee (or the Assessing Officer) can attest to.** These are a bona
   fide tenancy, the tenant vacated or steps taken, the tenant not in another of the assessee's properties, and all
   reasonable steps to sue or futility shown to the Assessing Officer. The engine can only record that they are met.
2. **The exclusion comes before the higher-of.** Excluding unrealised rent can bring the rent below the expected
   letting value, and then the expected value governs. Subtracting it afterwards would give a different and wrong
   answer. That makes a good bite probe.
3. **Stock-in-trade** is a business asset (D-155), and no input can enter it. There is nothing to refuse and nothing
   to build.
4. **Coverage:** after this item the head is complete except s.21(5). But ss.20–25 / 1961 ss.22–27 cannot become
   MODELLED without catalogue entries (SC-1), which is what you decided for W-078b. They currently point at W-078c,
   which would then be done.

### Numbers

None. r.21 / r.4 set conditions, not amounts; `PercentOfBase` 100 is the arithmetic (the whole of the unrealised
rent leaves the actual rent).

### How it will be verified (by hand, then the tests)

R16's let house: expected letting value ₹4,80,000, rent ₹5,40,000, local taxes ₹18,000. Both years.

1. **₹90,000 unrealised:** actual rent ₹4,50,000, below the expected ₹4,80,000, so the expected value governs. Annual
   value ₹4,62,000, less 30% (₹1,38,600) = **₹3,23,400**.
2. **₹30,000 unrealised:** rent ₹5,10,000. Annual value ₹4,92,000, so **₹3,44,400**.
3. **Vacant, no local taxes** (W-078a's vacancy house), rent ₹3,60,000 with ₹60,000 unrealised: rent ₹3,00,000
   (s.21(2)), so ₹3,00,000 − ₹90,000 = **₹2,10,000**.
4. **Unrealised more than the rent, or on a house not let:** refused.

Bite probes: unrealised rent ignored (case 1 gives ₹3,65,400); unrealised rent subtracted after the higher-of (case 1
gives ₹4,50,000 − ₹18,000 = ₹4,32,000, so ₹3,02,400).

Then `./mvnw clean test -pl backend -Punit`.
