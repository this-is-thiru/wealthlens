## Plan for W-064a — entertainment allowance

**What this item is for (one sentence):** deduct, for TY 2025-26 only, the entertainment allowance of an employee
paid by the Government, as 1961 s.16(ii) allows. The 2025 Act has no such deduction, so TY 2026-27 taxes the
allowance as ordinary salary.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6). One of them (C1) is an engine question the item cannot avoid: a
component referring to a rule that exists in only one year.**

### What I read

- **The `W-064a` row** (D-094, D-095): *"`SAL-ENTMT-001`, 1961 s.16(ii): `Employment` employer type (Government),
  component `ENTERTAINMENT_ALLOWANCE`, the 'salary (exclusive of any allowance, benefit or other perquisite)'
  measure, and `SalaryDeductionNode`'s three-limb least-of — TY 2025-26 only, old regime only (s.115BAC(2)); no
  2025-Act counterpart"*.
- **1961 s.16(ii)** (thc.nic.in, current to Act 7 of 2019, p.140; substituted by Act 14 of 2001 s.12, w.e.f.
  1-4-2002): *"a deduction in respect of any allowance in the nature of an entertainment allowance specifically
  granted by an employer to the assessee who is in receipt of a salary from the Government, a sum equal to one-fifth
  of his salary (exclusive of any allowance, benefit or other perquisite) or five thousand rupees, whichever is
  less"*.
  - That is **two** limits, one-fifth of that salary and ₹5,000, not three. The backlog row's "three-limb" is my
    wording from W-064, and the Act's words don't bear it out.
  - A text search of FA 2019, FA 2023, F(No.2)A 2024 and FA 2025 finds no amendment. FA 2020–2022 are not on hand.
- **New regime:** FA 2023 s.52's s.115BAC(2)(i) excludes *"clause (ii) or clause (iii) of section 16"* (p.34), so
  the deduction is old regime only.
- **TY 2026-27:** S2 has no entertainment-allowance deduction. The rulebook's `SAL-ENTMT-001` records a full-text
  search with zero hits, and s.19(1) Table Sl. 1–14 (read in W-066a) has no such entry. This is an **absence**
  (`ABSENT_IN_THIS_ACT`), as `SAL-LEAVE-TRAVEL-DISTANCE-RATE` models the reverse.
- **`DR 05` M3.2:** `employerType // GOVERNMENT | OTHER`.
- **Code:**
  - `Employment(components, termsIncludeDearnessAllowance)`, with 9 construction sites.
  - `SalaryBaseNode` measures the bases in its `BASES` list.
  - `SalaryDeductionNode` gives the tax on employment and the standard deduction.
  - **`PackValidator` I2** requires every rule a component's `feedsInto` or `salaryBaseFor` names to exist **in
    that year's pack**. A node's `rules()` may name a rule absent from a year: `ExemptionNode` already names the
    TY 2026-27-only distance rate.

### Findings

1. **The measure of salary is its own base.** "Salary (exclusive of any allowance, benefit or other perquisite)"
   excludes dearness allowance, which is an allowance, so it is not the HRA base.
   - On today's components it is `BASIC` alone.
   - Whether bonus and commission count is the same question as **OQ-5**, and they stay out until it is answered.
2. **I2 blocks both references for TY 2026-27.**
   - `BASIC` would name the TY 2025-26-only base row.
   - `ENTERTAINMENT_ALLOWANCE` would name the TY 2025-26-only deduction row.
   - The TY 2026-27 pack would be refused, although both rows correctly have no TY 2026-27 version.
3. **An entertainment allowance is still salary in TY 2026-27** (S2 s.16: "salary" includes allowances). Only the
   deduction goes.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — a component referring to a one-year rule (finding 2):**
  - **(a) recommended: the compiler projects each component into a year**, keeping only the `feedsInto` and
    `salaryBaseFor` references to rules that year's pack holds.
    - The timeline is checked separately: every reference must name a rule the timeline states in **some** year.
      That keeps I2's protection against a misspelt rule.
    - I2 on the compiled pack stays as it is.
    - In TY 2026-27, `ENTERTAINMENT_ALLOWANCE` feeds gross salary only, and `BASIC` measures no entertainment base.
  - **(b)** Give both rows a TY 2026-27 version that applies in no regime. That states law for a year whose Act
    has none, so a false row.
  - **(c)** Limit the component to TY 2025-26, with `RequireUserCorrection` after. That fixes the component but
    not `BASIC`'s reference.
- **C2 — the limits:**
  - **(a) recommended: the two the Act states** — least of one-fifth of the measure and ₹5,000. Deducted only where
    an entertainment allowance was paid (the words "in respect of any allowance … specifically granted").
  - **(b)** Also cap it at the allowance received. That is the common reading, but it is not in the words, so a
    number I would be adding. It differs only where the allowance is below both limits.
- **C3 — the input:**
  - **`EmployerType { GOVERNMENT, OTHER }`** on `Employment` (`DR 05` M3.2's own word), updating the 9 sites.
  - **One Government employment with an entertainment allowance.** Two or more are refused: the Act gives "the
    assessee" one sum, and whether it is per employment or in all is not stated.
- **C4 — rows:**
  - **`SAL-ENTERTAINMENT-ALLOWANCE`:** `LeastOf` of "one-fifth of salary" (`PercentOfBase` 20 of `SALARY`) and
    "limit" (`Fixed` 5000). `ExceptIn [NEW_TAX_REGIME]`. TY 2025-26 only. `provision2025` `ABSENT_IN_THIS_ACT`.
  - **`SAL-SALARY-FOR-ENTERTAINMENT`:** a `SalaryBase`, TY 2025-26 only, with `BASIC` in it outright.
  - One-fifth as 20% is exact, so no new shape is needed.
  - **Component `ENTERTAINMENT_ALLOWANCE`:** open-ended, feeding `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES` and the deduction row.
- **C5 — node:** `SalaryDeductionNode` adds the entertainment line after the standard deduction, in s.16's order
  (ia), (ii), (iii). `SalaryBaseNode` adds the base to `BASES`.
- **C6 — sourcing:**
  - `introducedBy`: FA 2023 s.52 (the regime rule), as `SAL-TAX-ON-EMPLOYMENT`'s TY 2025-26 version does.
  - Provenance: the thc.nic.in text, **`UNVERIFIED`**, with `whatToCheck` naming FA 2020–2022, as W-066a.

4. **Concept ids:** C4.

### What I am NOT doing

- The cap at the allowance received (C2b).
- Bonus and commission in the measure (OQ-5).
- Two Government employments (C3).

### How I will verify

- **Hand-worked tests:**
  - Government, basic 3,00,000, allowance 10,000: least of 60,000 and 5,000 → 5,000.
  - Basic 20,000, allowance 2,000: least of 4,000 and 5,000 → 4,000. Under C2b it would be 2,000.
  - DA 50,000 besides basic 20,000: still 4,000, because DA is excluded.
  - OTHER employer → no line.
  - New regime → no line.
  - TY 2026-27 → the allowance is in gross and nothing is deducted.
  - No allowance paid → no line.
- **Compiler:** TY 2026-27's compiled `ENTERTAINMENT_ALLOWANCE` and `BASIC` hold no reference to the absent rows. A
  misspelt reference is refused on the timeline.
- **Bite probes:** DA counted in the measure; the ₹5,000 limit ignored; TY 2026-27 still deducting.
- **Unit tier:** `./mvnw clean test -pl backend -Punit`.

## As built — departures from the plan (for review)
- **Rule ids:** `SAL-ENTMT-ALLOWANCE` and `SAL-SALARY-FOR-ENTMT`, not `SAL-ENTERTAINMENT-ALLOWANCE` / `SAL-SALARY-FOR-ENTERTAINMENT`: a `RuleId` segment is at most twelve characters and "ENTERTAINMENT" has thirteen; `ENTMT` is the rulebook's own (`SAL-ENTMT-001`). The component keeps `ENTERTAINMENT_ALLOWANCE`.
- **`EmployerKind`, not `EmployerType`:** `taxplanning.enums.EmployerType` (GOVT / PSU / PRIVATE) already holds that simple name, and `ArchitectureTest` forbids a second; the closed engine may not depend on the legacy one. Reconciling the two is proposed, not done.
- **One more test than planned:** the DA bite probe did not bite at first — node tests use fixture components and the ₹5,000 limit binds in the pipeline case — so a test on the shipped registry now holds DA out of the base.
