### Step 1 — characterization tests, on `HEAD`, before any cut

Each fixture gives the sell and the lot **different** values for every field the code takes from one side (for
example the sell's stock name "Sell Co", the lot's "Lot Co"), so a field read from the wrong side fails.

- **`TradeMatchingServiceTest`:**
  - `buildLotsFromBuys_whenBuysShareADayButNotAHolder_mergesOnlyTheSameKey`:
    - two buys, same day and key: 4 @ ₹100 (charges 2 / 0.5) and 6 @ ₹110 (3 / 0.5);
    - one buy that day for another holder, and one an earlier day.
    - Expected: three lots sorted by date; the merged lot's price (4 × 100 + 6 × 110) ÷ 10 = ₹106, quantity 10,
      charges 5 / 1, ids in order, `id` "lot-<code>-<date>", every identity field, `corporateActions` null,
      not derived.
    - Same-day lots are found by holder, not by position: their order comes from a `HashMap` and is not pinned.
  - `matchSellToLots_whenOneSellSpansTwoLots_writesEveryField`. Lots: L1 2024-01-10, 10 @ ₹100, broker 20, misc 5;
    L2 2024-03-01, 10 @ ₹120, broker 10, misc 0; L3 another broker; L4 another holder. Sell 2025-06-02, 15 @ ₹150,
    broker 30, misc 3. Worked:
    - **trade 1 (L1, 10):** buy broker 20 ÷ 10 × 10 = 20; buy misc 5; sell broker 30 ÷ 15 × 10 = 20; sell misc
      3 ÷ 15 × 10 = 2; total buy 1,000 + 20 + 5 = 1,025; total sell 1,500 − 20 − 2 = 1,478; net 453; percentage
      453 ÷ 1,025 × 100 = 44.1951…; holding days 509 (2024-01-10 → 2025-01-10 is 366, a leap year; then
      21 + 28 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 2 = 143);
    - **trade 2 (L2, 5):** buy broker 10 ÷ 10 × 5 = 5; buy misc 0; sell broker 30 ÷ 15 × 5 = 10; sell misc 1;
      total buy 600 + 5 = 605; total sell 750 − 10 − 1 = 739; net 134; percentage 22.1487…; holding days 458
      (2024-03-01 → 2025-03-01 is 365, then 31 + 30 + 31 + 1 = 93);
    - **after:** L1 0, L2 5, L3 and L4 untouched (10).
    - Every other field is read from its side (identity from the sell; buy price, date, lot id, derived flag,
      corporate actions and acquisition from the lot); the type is the stubbed `HoldingPeriodService` answer; the
      FY is in the form `:404` already pins.
- **`PortfolioServiceTest`:** `sellStock_v1_writesEveryFieldOfTheOutcome`, one lot through `sellStock`, the
  captured `TradeOutcomeContext` asserted field by field. The buy charges are what V1's caller passes, so the
  expected values follow `LotChargeAllocator`'s rule, worked in the test from the lot's figures.
- **`TradeOutcomeRecorderTest`:** `record_whenOneLot_writesEveryFieldOfTheRow`. One lot, the engine's sell
  breakup, and a recorded buy breakup; every field, the `TMoney.scale`d values worked to the paisa.
- Each new case must **pass on `HEAD`**. The double count (C2) is pinned by none: no new case sells a lot
  twice.
