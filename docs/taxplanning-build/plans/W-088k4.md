## Plan for W-088k4 — a bonus lot dated from its allotment

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a bonus share's holding period runs *"from the date of the
allotment"* (1961 Explanation 1(i)(f); 2025 s.2(101)(c)(C)(IV); D-303). So `portfolio` should record that date and
date the bonus lot by it, instead of the corporate action's ex-date. Then W-088k3's term counts from the law's date
and its trace stops caveating every bonus lot.

**Mis-sized for one commit (§7).** Recording the date for new actions and correcting lots already processed are
different kinds of work. The second is a one-time data operation with its own runbook entry, test and removal row
(CLAUDE.md §6). **This plan proposes a split (C1 – C3).**

### What I read (session 27, 2026-10-21)

- **The row,** D-304 C2, D-305, and `sources/2026-10-21-holding-period.md` ("The bonus-date gap").
- **`corporate/entity/CorporateActionEntity`:** `record_date`, `ex_date`, `date`, `ratio`. It has **no allotment
  date**.
  - `date` is accepted by `CorporateActionDto` but **never copied** to the entity (`getAsEntity`, `:42–58`). Nothing
    reads it.
  - The API collection's examples send `date` equal to `exDate` (`api-collection/Corporate Actions/Add - CA.yml`).
  - So it is not an allotment date, and I do not reuse it.
- **Entry:** `POST /corporate-action/add` (`CorporateActionController:30`); there is no general update endpoint (only
  `/update/priority`). Actions are processed later, by `PUT /corporate-action/perform` and the per-user perform.
- **Bonus processing** (`CorporateActionService.processBonusShares`, `:336–370`, and `processBonusSharesTxns`,
  `:372–395`):
  - the new lot (`AssetEntity`) and its `TransactionEntity` are both dated `corporateAction.getExDate()`
    (`:357`, `:388`);
  - the lot gets `corporateActionType = BONUS`;
  - V2 and the rebuild carry the lot's date into `trade_outcomes.buy_date`, which the summary publishes as
    `acquiredOn`.
- **The engine** (`node/CapitalGainsTerm`, D-305) adds *"; a bonus lot is dated at its ex-date, not its allotment
  (W-088k4)"* to **every** bonus bucket's trace, because it cannot tell an ex-dated lot from an allotment-dated one.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — where the allotment date lives:**
  - **(a) recommended:** a new optional `allotment_date` on `corporate_action` (entity, DTO and `getAsEntity`).
    - **Performing a `BONUS` action without it is refused,** naming the action. That is fail-fast: a lot is never
      dated by a guess.
    - The bonus lot and its transaction are dated by it.
    - Actions are performed after allotment in practice (performing is a separate, later call), so the date is
      known by then.
  - **(b)** reuse the unused `date` field. Its meaning is unstated and its examples are ex-dates, so this would
    give one field two meanings.
  - **(c)** keep the ex-date and leave the caveat in the engine permanently, closing this row.
- **C2 — lots already processed** (dated at the ex-date):
  - **(a) recommended: a separate row, W-088k4b.** It is a one-time, `SUPER_USER`, `dryRun`-by-default correction
    (D-280).
    - For each `BONUS` action that now has an `allotment_date`, it re-dates that action's bonus lots and
      transactions.
    - Then §2.2's per-user rebuild re-dates `trade_outcomes`.
    - It is idempotent (a second run changes nothing), with an integration test and a runbook §2 entry.
    - It gets a Phase 15 removal row, and a YouTrack child under WL-28.
    - The owner supplies each existing action's allotment date. The runbook gives a read-only list of the `BONUS`
      actions lacking one, and the command that sets one.
  - **(b)** no correction. Old lots keep the ex-date, and the engine caveat becomes *"a bonus lot recorded before
    W-088k4 may be dated at its ex-date"*. That is permanent.
- **C3 — the split:**

### What I am NOT doing

- Any code in this item (a split proposal; k4a's plan is written on approval).
- Removing `CorporateActionDto.date`. It is dead, but out of scope, and is noted for W-088l.
- Other corporate actions' dates. A demerger keeps the parent's date, which is the law's (Explanation 1(i)(g)).
- Rights issues (Explanation 1(i)(d), from allotment). `grep RIGHTS_ISSUANCE corporate/service/*.java` finds
  nothing: no processing creates a rights lot, so there is no rights date to correct today. A rights lot entered as
  a purchase carries its trade date, as entered.

### How I will verify

This item writes only docs: the split rows, D-306 and the board.

### Post-deployment actions (R15)

None here. k4a adds a read-only §3 entry; k4b adds the §2 correction.

### Risks or ambiguities

1. **The effect is small.** Allotment usually follows the record date by a working day or two, so only a sale within
   those days of the twelve-month anniversary changes term. C1(c) (close) is the cheap honest option, if you prefer
   it.
2. **Data burden.** Every bonus action needs its allotment date entered from the company's announcement. The refusal
   at perform time makes a missing one visible, not silent.
