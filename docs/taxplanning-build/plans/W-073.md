## Plan for W-073 — employer retirement contributions (`EmployerContributionNode`)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** charge to salary the parts of an employer's retirement contributions the Acts
treat as income — the aggregate over ₹7,50,000, the accretion on that excess, and the recognised-fund contributions
and interest beyond their limits — for both tax years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C4). C1 splits the item — its four charges are sourced very unevenly — and you
place the parts (R4).**

### What I read

- **The `W-073` row:** `EmployerContributionNode`, `DR 01` R13, *"four independent charges, not one"*. `02-TECH-SPEC`:
  node `employer.contrib.charges` (○ Phase 8), which `salary.gross` already lists as an input.
- **`DR 01` R13** (H-6 verdict): `PF-AGGCAP-001` the aggregate excess; `PF-AGGCAP-002` the accretion on it;
  `PF-RPF12-001` recognised-fund employer contributions over 12% of salary and interest over the notified rate;
  `PF-EEINT-001` interest on the **employee's own** contributions over ₹2,50,000 / ₹5,00,000. Its worked example
  (TY 2026-27): 2,88,000 + 3,36,000 + 2,00,000 = 8,24,000 → **74,000** a perquisite.
- **TY 2026-27, S2** (Gazette 265620): **s.17(1)(h)** *"aggregate amount of any contribution, in excess of ₹ 750000 in a
  tax year, made to the account of the assessee by the employer — (i) in a recognised provident fund; (ii) in the
  scheme referred to in section 124(1); and (iii) in an approved superannuation fund"* (p.38); **(i)** the annual
  accretion on the fund *"to the extent it relates to the contribution"* in (h), computed as prescribed (p.39);
  **Sch. XI Part A para 6** (p.550): *"The portion of the annual accretion in the tax year to the employee's balance in
  a recognised provident fund consisting of — (a) contributions made by the employer exceeding 12 % of the employee's
  salary; and (b) interest credited … at a rate exceeding such rate as fixed by the Central Government by
  notification, shall be deemed to have been received by the employee and included in his total income"*.

  I ÷ F(avg.); PC the year's employer contribution over the limit, PC1 and TP1 those of earlier years from 1-4-2020,
  capped at the opening balance (r.16(2)(b)). The definition of PC reads *"in excess of ₹ 750000 lakhs"* — a drafting
  slip; PC1's reads *"₹ 750000"*.
- **TY 2025-26, 1961 s.17(2)(vii)–(viia)** — **read in the Gazette** this session: Finance Act, 2020 s.13

  contribution made to the account of the assessee by the employer — (a) in a recognised provident fund; (b) in the
  scheme referred to in sub-section (1) of section 80CCD; and (c) in an approved superannuation fund, to the extent it
  exceeds **seven lakh and fifty thousand rupees** in a previous year"*; (viia) the accretion, *"computed in such manner
  as may be prescribed"*. The later Acts read in W-066a (FA 2021–2025, TLA 2025) do not touch (vii); the interim

- **Not read:** **Rules 1962 r.3B** (the TY 2025-26 accretion formula) — not in the consolidated rule 3 the owner

- **Code:** no node produces `employer.contrib.charges`; `GrossSalaryNode` adds `salary.perquisites` only (D-111).
  Employer contributions are not pay lines today (no PF / NPS / superannuation employer component).

### Findings

1. **The four charges are independent and unevenly sourced.** The aggregate excess (h) is fully read in both years.
   The accretion (i) is read for TY 2026-27 only. The 12% / excess-interest charge needs a notified rate never read. The
   employee's own-interest charge needs r.9D and is not an employer contribution at all.
2. **`PF-EEINT-001` is not salary.** It is interest on the employee's own contributions, taxed because its exemption
   (Sch. II Sl. 3–4) is withdrawn — income of the provident fund's interest, not of the employment. It belongs with the
   other-sources head.
3. **The accretion formula needs years of history** — earlier years' excess contributions and taxed accretions
   (PC1, TP1), and the fund's income and balances — facts from the employer's fund statements.
4. **r.16's "₹ 750000 lakhs"** is a slip; read as ₹7,50,000 like PC1 (the Act's own figure).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — split** (you place the parts):
  - **`W-073a` — the aggregate excess**, both years, **buildable now**: an input per employment — the employer's
    contributions in the year to a recognised PF, to NPS (s.124(1) / 80CCD(1)) and to an approved superannuation fund
    — and the node `employer.contrib.charges` producing the excess over ₹7,50,000 (all employments together, "in a
    tax year"), added to gross salary on a line of its own. Rows `PERQ-EMPLOYER-CONTRIBUTION` (`AmountInExcess` over
    `Fixed` 7,50,000).
  - **`W-073b` — the accretion on the excess**, both years: TY 2026-27 buildable (r.16); **TY 2025-26 needs Rules 1962
    r.3B** — sourcing.
  - **`W-073c` — recognised-fund contributions over 12% and interest over the notified rate**, both years — **needs the
    notified rate** (both years), the 1961 Sch. IV Part A r.6 text and Sch. XI's "salary" — sourcing.
  - **`PF-EEINT-001`** — moved to the other-sources work (finding 2), not W-073's; you name where.
  - **Recommended order:** 073a now; 073b and 073c once sourced.
- **C2 — the aggregate is across employments** (the Act's "in a tax year"), per assessee; one line.
- **C3 — r.16's slip** read as ₹7,50,000, recorded in the row's note (for W-073b).
- **C4 — verification (073a):** TY 2026-27 `SOURCE_CHECKED`; TY 2025-26 `UNVERIFIED` only for the unread interim
  Finance Act, 2024 (the figure itself read in the Gazette).
