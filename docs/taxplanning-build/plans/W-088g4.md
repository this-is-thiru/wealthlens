For TY 2025-26 the 1961 Act governs (s.50C; s.48 with s.55(1)(b); s.55(2)(b)). Those texts are **not re-read here**:
the copies the repo cites for 1961 (thc.nic.in, current to Act 7 of 2019) predate later amendments of s.50C, so each
build row sources its own from the Gazette.

**What the engine does today with a land bucket.** It takes `portfolio`'s consideration and cost as recorded
(`CapitalGainsAdapter.java:75-82`): no stamp-duty value is held anywhere, no improvement is recorded, and the cost of
a pre-2001 purchase is the price paid. So the figure is right when the stamp-duty value is within 110 % of the
price, nothing was spent improving the property, and it was bought on or after 1-4-2001 — and silently wrong
otherwise: too low in case 1, too high in cases 2 and 3.

**What the engine has to say so.** A `Notice` (`trace/Notice.java`) with a `Severity` — `INFO`, `ATTENTION` ("the
number stands"), `ACTION_REQUIRED` ("the number is provisional until the taxpayer does something"),
`UNVERIFIED_RULE` — attached to the trace line it is about as a `TraceAnnotation` (`NodeResult.java:11-13`), never a
free list of warnings (O-5). Two nodes use it today (`ExemptionNode:133`). No endpoint serves the engine yet (W-109),
so a notice is visible in the trace and, later, wherever W-109 renders it.

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — how the engine behaves until the three provisions are built.**

- **(a) Recommended: mark it, and place the three builds.** The figure stands; the land bucket's line carries a notice
  per provision that could apply to it, naming the section and what it would change. Why: refusing throws away a
  correct figure for the common sale (price at or above the stamp-duty value, no works, bought after 2001) to protect
  the uncommon one; a notice keeps the figure and tells the taxpayer exactly what to check. The three builds become
  their own rows (C3), so nothing is left unplaced.
- (b) Refuse a land bucket again until all three are built. Safe, and it returns land to where it was before
  W-088g2 — W-088g, g2 and g3's work becomes unreachable until three more items land.
- (c) Build them now, in order. Each needs a fact `portfolio` does not hold — the stamp-duty value (and the
  agreement-date value, and how part was paid), each improvement's cost and year (indexed separately for the cap's
  measure, s.72(8)(c)), the 1-4-2001 fair market value and stamp-duty value — so each is an entered fact, its own plan
  and its own Gazette sourcing; three items, not one. Until the last lands, (a) or (b) is still needed.

**C2 — under C1a, how strongly each notice speaks.**

- **(a) Recommended: by what the provision can do to the figure.** `ACTION_REQUIRED` for the stamp-duty comparison
  (row 1 can raise the tax, so the figure is provisional until the taxpayer confirms the value is within 110 % of the
  price); `ATTENTION` for improvement and the 2001 option (rows 2, 3 can only lower it, so the figure stands as a
  ceiling).
- (b) `ATTENTION` for all three — understates that the figure may be too low.
- (c) `ACTION_REQUIRED` for all three — overstates: a taxpayer who made no improvements has nothing to do.

**Where the notices' conditions live (not a choice — CLAUDE.md §4).** Which buckets a provision reaches is law: land
or building for rows 1 and 3, acquired before 1-4-2001 for row 3. So the conditions are rule data — a new set, one row
per unbuilt provision, its `provision1961` / `provision2025` naming the section, its conditions the asset kind and the
date — and the node attaches a notice for each row a land bucket meets. Building a provision later is deleting its
row. A Java `if` on the asset kind would be the law-in-code W-088l audits.

**C3 — the three build rows (under C1a or C1c).** Proposed, in this order, after W-088g5:

- **W-088g6** — the stamp-duty value as consideration (2025 s.78; 1961 s.50C): first, because it is the only one that
  can make the figure too low.
- **W-088g7** — the cost of improvement (2025 s.72(1)(b), s.90(1)(b), s.72(8)(c); 1961 s.48, s.55(1)(b)).
- **W-088g8** — the 1-4-2001 fair market value option and its stamp-duty cap (2025 s.90(9)(a)–(b), (10); 1961
  s.55(2)(b)).

(b) A different order, or fewer — the owner's to say.

### Risks or ambiguities

1. **A notice is not a correction.** Under C1a a sale below the stamp-duty value is still under-taxed until W-088g6;
   the notice makes that visible, it does not fix it. If the owner would rather never show a possibly-low figure,
   C1b is the safe choice.
2. **The 1961 citations are not yet re-read.** The 2025 sections are read above; the 1961 sub-clauses (s.50C as
   amended, s.55(1)(b), s.55(2)(b)) are read from the Gazette in Revision 1 before they go into the rows.
3. **"Within 110 %" is the 2025 text.** Whether the 1961 s.50C tolerance in TY 2025-26 is the same figure is part of
   that re-reading; the notice names the section, not the figure (SC-2), so it does not depend on it.
