**What the text settles, and what it does not.** The relief is triggered "in the case of transfer of" land or building
(rows 2, 4). But A and B are each "income-tax computed under sub-section (1)(b)", and (1)(b) is the tax on "such
long-term capital gains" — all of them (row 1); and s.72(2), which builds B's gain, substitutes indexed cost with no
limit to land (row 3). So the words support three readings, and the built one is a fourth:

- **Land alone** — the excess is the tax the amendment added on that transfer; A and B are measured on the land gain.
  Supported by the opening words; strains "computed under sub-section (1)(b)".
- **Literal, whole base** — A on all of s.197(1)'s gains, B on all of them *each with indexed cost* (row 3). Supported
  by A's and B's words; it lets a land sale lift indexation onto unrelated assets. For TY 2025-26 the 1961 proviso
  compares against the whole pre-2024 Act, which for a non-land asset is not simply 20 % with indexation (listed
  securities had the old s.112(1) 10 % proviso) — a computation the engine does not have.
- **As built (C2a)** — A on the whole part, B on the whole part with **only the land's** cost indexed. W-088g called it
  the literal reading; on row 3 it is not quite — the literal reading indexes every gain in B. It is the only one of
  the three the engine could express then (W-088g finding 1).

### What I will build (C1a, C2a, C3a — a revised plan for any other choice)

The rule data does not change: `RATE-CG-LONG-OTHER`'s `LeastOf` (12.5 % of `SPECIAL_RATE_GAINS`, 20 % of
`INDEXED_SPECIAL_RATE_GAINS`) is applied **once per qualifying land bucket** with that bucket's two measures, and **once
to the rest of the part** with the rest as both bases — where `LeastOf(12.5 % × r, 20 % × r)` is 12.5 % × r. No new cap
shape, no relock, no rule or coverage change.

1. **The head carries the land measures, not one indexed total.** `CapitalGainsHead.indexedParts` (a map of part →
   indexed total) becomes, per part, the list of qualifying buckets' `(gain, indexed gain)` pairs, built where
   `CapitalGainsNode` computes them (`:110-112`). The part's total stays as it is. Name: reuse the repository's word for
   the measure (`indexed`), checked by grep before naming.
2. **Every reduction of a part attributed pro rata (C3a).** Netting a losing bucket into its part (`CapitalGainsNode`),
   set-off (`CapitalGainsAbsorption`, which today lowers both maps by the same `taken`), and the shift
   (`TaxByBasisNode.basesOf`, `:123`, which today subtracts the shifted rupees from the indexed total) each lower the
   land pairs by their share of the part's gaining amounts, the indexed gain by the same rupees, floored at nil.
3. **`TaxByBasisNode`** applies the special-rate row per land pair and once to the remainder, summing to the part's tax,
   one trace line per application naming which.
4. **The review and D-429 corrected** (R2's sentence and figure): the built reading was approved, the finding is a
   re-decision, and the ₹2,82,264.96 figure is the land-alone reading's.

**The type.** A new public record `pipeline/IndexedGain(Money gain, Money indexedGain)` — one qualifying land bucket's

should rounding leave the scaled gains a paisa above `to`, the last absorbs it, so they never exceed the part. The
one method serves all three reductions (C3a). Names grepped: `IndexedGain`, `indexedGains` are unused.

**The head and the set-off value.** `CapitalGainsHead.indexedParts` and `HeadsAfterSetOff.indexedCapitalGains` (a part

that a part's listed gains do not exceed the part. The two-argument constructors stay (no indexed measure).

**Where the measures are made and lowered.**
- `CapitalGainsNode` — per bucket, a qualifying bucket with a gain adds `IndexedGain(chargeable, indexed floored at
  nil)` to its part (`measureOf`'s line moves to the bucket); once the part is netted, its list is `lowered` from

- `CapitalGainsAbsorption.absorb` and so `SetOffNode` / `CarriedLossSetOff` — the list `lowered` from `available` to

- `TaxByBasisNode` — `basesOf` gives way to applying the part's rate row once per `IndexedGain` (lowered from the

  application: a part with a single application keeps today's text (`PART; limb`), so the seven existing cases' line
  assertions hold; several read `PART, qualifying gain i of n; limb` and `PART, the rest; limb`.
