## Plan for W-237a — the means of making a request, published

**For:** a user can learn, without logging in, how to exercise each right the Act gives them, what identifies them, and
who answers questions about their data; and every response to such a request names that person (DPDP Rules r.14(1),
r.9; D-470).

### What I read (session 50; Gazette copy 267650, sha256 `eabc7d05…`; Act 248045, `4deb2398…`)

- **r.14(1):** the Fiduciary *"shall prominently publish on its website or app, or both, … (a) the details of the means
  using which a Data Principal may make a request for the exercise of such rights; and (b) the particulars, if any, such
  as the username or other identifier of such a Data Principal, which may be required to identify her under its terms of
  service"*. r.14(2): she makes the request *"using the means and furnishing the particulars required"*.
- **r.9:** publish *"on its website or app, and mention in every response to a communication for the exercise of the
  rights … the business contact information of … a person who is able to answer on behalf of the Data Fiduciary the
  questions of the Data Principal about the processing of her personal data"*.
- **The rights** (Act): s.11 a summary of the data and processing, and with whom it was shared; s.12 correction,
  completion, updating, erasure; s.13 grievance redressal; s.14 nominating another person.
- **What exists:** one self-service request — taxplanning profile erasure, `POST /tax/user/{email}/profiles/{id}/erasure`
  (W-235), whose `ErasureReceipt` already names a contact from `app.tax.privacy-contact` (`taxplanning/erasure/
  PrivacyContact.java`; env `TAX_PRIVACY_CONTACT`, **not yet given** — CHECKPOINT). `UserRecordsErasureService` (portfolio)
  is not a user's request. No public page or endpoint says any of this. The repo holds the API only; any website or app
  that shows it is outside the repo.
- **Identifier:** every account is keyed by its email (`/{resource}/user/{email}`; `AuthFilter` matches the token to it).

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — how it is published.** (a) **A public endpoint `GET /privacy/rights` (no login), returning a notice: for each
right, the means — the self-service endpoint where one exists, otherwise *"write to the contact from your registered
email"* — the identifier (the account's email), and the contact.** The rights, their sections and wording are
configuration (`app.privacy.*`), not code; a client renders it. (b) Documentation only (`api-collection`, a README) —
not "on its website or app".

**C2 — the contact, app-wide.** (a) **One key `app.privacy.contact` (env `PRIVACY_CONTACT`) in `shared`, read by the
notice and by taxplanning's `ErasureReceipt`; `app.tax.privacy-contact` / `TAX_PRIVACY_CONTACT` removed** — you have not
set it yet, so nothing deployed changes; the runbook's §0.5 line is updated. Startup still fails without it (W-235's
rule). (b) Keep the tax key and add a second — two places for one person.

**C3 — "every response".** (a) **A test that every response to a rights request names the contact** — today the erasure
receipt and the new notice; W-237b (grievances) and W-237d (account erasure) add theirs under the same test. (b) Rely on
each item remembering.
