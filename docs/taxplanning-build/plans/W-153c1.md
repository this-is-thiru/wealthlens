## Plan for W-153c1 — income rows by kind (s.92)

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** the row that charges an item of other-sources income is
chosen by the item's kind in data, from one set of the s.92 rows. Unit income moves to the residual s.92(1) row, the
statute's place for it. A kind no row charges is refused generically (D-321, C1a / C2a / C3a).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the income row for interest on compensation (s.92(2)(i)), needed so the refusal check knows the kind is
  charged:**
  - **(a) recommended:** a new row `OS-COMPENSATION-INTEREST-INCOME` in `RS-OS-INCOME`:
    - 100% of base `COMPENSATION_INTEREST`;
    - provisions s.92(2)(i) / s.56(2)(viii);
    - `introducedBy` and provenance copied from `OS-COMPENSATION-INTEREST` (it already cites F(No.2)A 2009 s.26 for
      s.56(2)(viii) and the 2025 Act s.92(2)(i)), `SOURCE_CHECKED` as that row is.

    The row's line appears from c2. In c1 it decides only that the kind is charged.
  - **(b)** widen `OS-INTEREST` to reach it, adding (2)(i) / (2)(viii) to its provisions. Fewer rows, but
    `OS-INTEREST`'s `introducedBy` names only (1), (2)(e) / (id), and s.153 reads `OS-INTEREST`.
- **C2 — the interim dispatch until c2:**
  - **(a) recommended:** the `switch` keeps its arms for the kinds whose path c2 removes:
    - interest on compensation, family pension, the two whole-deducted kinds;
    - and the dividend path for dividend, unit income and deemed dividend, whose outright refusals c2 replaces.

    Every other kind takes one path: the income line on its `RS-OS-INCOME` row, then `otherExpenditure`.
    `interest()` and `inFull()` become that path. The dividend path's income line is on the item's own income row,
    so unit income is on `OS-RESIDUAL`.
  - **(b)** do c2's deduction rows first. Their path removal needs the income rows, so the interim is the same, the
    other way round.
