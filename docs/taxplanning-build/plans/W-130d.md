**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** explain the difference that comes from other-head income:
- the employee told the employer more or less "income under any other head" (row 8) than the computation holds;
- that difference, and the lines it moves, carry an expected reason rather than reading as unexplained.

### What the law and design say

- **`01-rulebook.md:1474` (TDS-SAL-12B-001):** the employee "may, at his option" furnish other-head income (not a loss
  except the HP loss), and it "increase[s] or decrease[s] the tax to be deducted".
- **`01-rulebook.md:1476` (TDS-SAL-REDUCE-001):** "The tax deductible from salary shall not be reduced in any case
  except on account of (i) loss under 'Income from house property' and (ii) TDS/TCS … Other-head income may only
  increase the deduction."
- **M4.2's reason** (`05-domain-model.md:555`): "Other-head income the user declared, which could only raise TDS".
- **Form No. 130:** row 8 "Total amount of other income reported by the employee [7(a)-7(b)]".

### Findings

1. **No node holds "other heads".** The engine has `gti` and `salary.income` (`NodeIds.java`). Row 8's counterpart is
   their difference: gross total income less income under "Salaries".
2. **W-130b's direction reasons already carry to later lines.** A comparison with `whenHigher` / `whenLower` explains
   its own difference, and rows 9 – 17 and Part B inherit it (`Reconciliation.reasons`). Row 8 comes before row 11 in
   printed order, so once both differ, later lines take the first reason only.
3. **Capital gains sit in gross total income but cannot be in row 8** (M4.2's last reason). A computation with capital
   gains would make row 8 differ for that reason, not this one.

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — row 8's counterpart:**
  - **(a)** A `ComputedTax` figure `otherHeads`: the run's `gti` value less its `salary.income` value, less the
    capital gains charged (from `capitalGainsBuckets`), comparable as `ComparedTotal.OTHER_HEADS`.
  - (b) Compare rows 6 and 9 with `salary.income` and `gti`, and read row 8's difference off the two.
- **C2 — the reasons, by sign:**
  - **(a)** Both EXPECTED:
    - **`OTHER_INCOME_REPORTED`** (TDS-SAL-REDUCE-001): the certificate is above the computation, because the employee
      reported more, which "could only raise TDS".
    - **`OTHER_INCOME_NOT_REPORTED`** (TDS-SAL-12B-001): the certificate is below, because the employee did not report
      it, which is their option; tax is payable at filing.
  - (b) One reason for both directions.
- **C3 — several direction reasons above a line** (finding 2):
  - **(a)** A later line inherits every direction reason above it, in order. The reasons are now a list (W-130c), so both
    causes are named.
  - (b) Only the first, as today.

### Correction in build (owner, 2026-11-17)

The build's probe found that `gti` is gross total income after set-off (`GrossTotalIncomeNode`: `HeadsAfterSetOff.total()`).
So C1a's "gti less salary income less the capital gains charged" depends on set-off and on reading charged gains from the
buckets. The owner chose (AskUserQuestion) to use the two heads row 8 reports, before set-off: `otherHeads` = the
house-property head's total (signed, a loss negative) + the other-sources head (`HousePropertyHead.total()`,
`OtherSourcesHead.asMoney()`). Capital gains never enter it.
