## Plan for W-117a — the claim catalogue's frame, with HRA and 80G

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a checked, pack-shipped claim catalogue
(`content/claim-catalogue.json`), with its first two entries: HRA (channel E, survives a missing Form 16 entry) and
80G for a donee that must report (channel T, does not).

**Probe (2026-11-14; no code run):**
- **The pattern to follow:** `SectionCatalogue` (`rules/catalogue/`) is built from the timeline's content, checked at
  build by tests (SC-1 – SC-4), and read by nothing at run time yet. Its `{{RULE-ID.field}}` placeholder and
  `TYPED_FIGURE` check (digits, ₹, %, lakh) are reusable.
- **Deadlines (I26):**
  - The F30 calendar is `taxplanning`'s `DraftCalendar.Kind` (`RETURN_DUE`, `DEDUCTION_PAID_BY`, …), which the
    closed `taxengine` cannot import.
  - `rules/regime/DeadlineSpec` (`DueDateUnder`, `WithTheReturn`, `NoDeadline`) was declared for the catalogue too,
    but an employer's proof cut-off is neither statutory nor dated.
- **Chapter VIII Part (I24)** is data nowhere. The Part headings were read from the Gazette (`sources/2026-10-07-chapter-via.md`
  B2; DR 22 C2).
- **Rule data the pilots need already exists:** the landlord-PAN threshold (`SAL-HOUSE-RENT-LANDLORD-PAN`, a
  `ParticularsThreshold`, `exceeds`); s.133(6) unreported donations (`DED-DONATION-UNREPORTED`); the HRA rule's
  regimes.
- **Sources for the pilots:**
  - r.205 / r.26C (`sources/2026-10-09-landlord-pan.md`, Gazette G.S.R. 198(E));
  - s.133(6) (Gazette CG-DL-E-22082025-265620 p.169, read for D-142).
  - Still to read at build: s.392(4)(a)'s "at his option" (channel E, DR 22 C2), from the 2025 Act's Gazette copy
    (2025/265620). If it does not say so, HRA's "claimable in the return" verdict waits and you are told.

**The model** (`taxengine.rules.catalogue`, build-time like the section catalogue):
- `ClaimCatalogueEntry`:
  - `rule` (a pack rule id) and `section` (its `SEC-…` entry: name and summary, C2a);
  - `channel` (`E`, `R`, `R_PLUS`, `T`, `P`) and `chapterViiiPart` (optional; `B`, `C`, `D`, `E`);
  - `steps`; `askWhom` (who to ask, with what for); `evidence` (each with `closedBy`); `mistakes`;
  - `ifMissingFromForm16`: `CLAIMABLE_IN_RETURN`, `NOT_ALLOWABLE_UNLESS_REPORTED`, `NOTHING_TO_CLAIM` or
    `BLOCKED_UNTIL_CERTIFIED`;
  - `basis`: each tax year's instrument and section, as the section catalogue's provisions are written.
- `ClaimStep`: `order`, `instruction` (imperative), `actor` (`YOU`, `EMPLOYER`, `INSTITUTION`, `LENDER`, `LANDLORD`),
  `by` (C1), `produces` (optional).
- **Compiler checks** (each a refusal with a test):
  - every text has no figures (C4a), and placeholders name a real rule and field;
  - `rule` and `section` exist;
  - I24: Part `C` requires a step saying the late-return bar applies; a Part other than `C` may not say so;
  - I25: every evidence item has `closedBy`;
  - I26: every `by` is from C1's set;
  - the steps are numbered `1..n`.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: a step's deadline.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** a closed set: `TAX_YEAR_END`, `RETURN_DUE`, `EMPLOYER_PROOF_CUT_OFF` and `NONE`. The first
    two are dates the F30 calendar has (`DEDUCTION_PAID_BY`, `RETURN_DUE`), and a `taxplanning` test holds that
    mapping. The employer's cut-off is named, not dated ("by your employer's cut-off").
  - C1b: reuse `DeadlineSpec`, adding an employer case.
- **C2: where the Chapter VIII Part lives.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** on the catalogue entry, sourced in its `_sources`. It is a fact about where a section sits
    in the Act, used only by the catalogue.
  - C2b: on the rule's `provision2025`, as rule data. Every rule file changes and every lock moves.
- **C3: when the catalogue is checked.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** at build, by the compiler and tests, like the section catalogue. W-118 reads it at run time.
  - C3b: also at pack load now.

**Draft content (Claude's, for your adoption; no digits; placeholders filled from the rules):**
- **HRA** (`SAL-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE`, `SEC-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE`, channel E, `CLAIMABLE_IN_RETURN`):
  - **Steps:**
    1. "Keep a rent receipt for every month you pay rent." (YOU)
    2. "If your rent for the year is more than {{SAL-HOUSE-RENT-LANDLORD-PAN.exceeds}}, get your landlord's name,
       address and PAN." (LANDLORD, by `EMPLOYER_PROOF_CUT_OFF`)
    3. "Give the receipts and those details to your employer on the declaration form they give you." (YOU → EMPLOYER,
       by `EMPLOYER_PROOF_CUT_OFF`)
    4. "If you missed your employer's cut-off, claim it in your return instead." (YOU, by `RETURN_DUE`)
  - **Evidence:** rent receipts (closed by: a receipt for each month); landlord's particulars (closed by: name,
    address and PAN, where the rent is above that figure).
  - **Mistakes:** leaving dearness allowance out of the salary the limits are measured on; claiming for the whole year
    when you lived there for part of it; claiming for a home you own and live in.
- **80G, reported donee** (`DED-DONATION-UNREPORTED`, `SEC-…` found at build, channel T, Part B,
  `NOT_ALLOWABLE_UNLESS_REPORTED`):
  - **Steps:**
    1. "Ask the institution whether it reports your donation to the tax department." (YOU → INSTITUTION)
    2. "Get its certificate of the donation." (INSTITUTION)
    3. "Check that the donation appears in your annual information statement before you file." (YOU, by `RETURN_DUE`)
  - **Evidence:** the institution's certificate (closed by: the certificate it issues once it has reported the
    donation).
  - **Mistakes:** claiming a donation the institution has not reported; claiming a cash donation above the limit.
  - **Note:** this applies only to the donees s.133(6) names (D-142 C6); the other donees are W-117b's.
