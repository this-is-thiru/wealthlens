## Plan for W-075c2 — Chapter VIII: loan interest (80E, 80EE, 80EEA, 80EEB), TY 2026-27

**What this item is for (one sentence):** compute the deductions for interest on an education loan (s.129), a
first-home loan (s.130, s.131) and an electric-vehicle loan (s.132) for TY 2026-27, in the pack and
`ChapterEightNode`.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (Gazette CG-DL-E-22082025-265620, pp.165–167, this session)

- **s.129:** an individual, *"amount paid as interest during a tax year … on a loan taken by him from any financial
  institution or any approved charitable institution, if the — (a) loan taken is for the purpose of pursuing higher
  education of himself or his relative; and (b) payment is made out of his income chargeable to tax"*; (2) *"the
  initial tax year and seven tax years immediately succeeding the initial tax year, or until the interest … is fully
  paid … whichever is earlier"*; (3)(d) the initial tax year is *"the tax year in which the assessee starts paying the
  interest"*; (3)(e) a relative is the spouse, children, or a student the individual is guardian of. **No cap.**
- **s.130:** an individual, *"interest payable on loan taken by him from any financial institution for the purpose of
  acquisition of a residential house property"*, *"shall not exceed ₹ 50000"*; (3) sanctioned 1-4-2016 to 31-3-2017;
  loan sanctioned ≤ ₹35 lakh; *"value of residential house property"* ≤ ₹50 lakh; no residential house owned on the
  date of sanction; (4) the same interest not deductible under any other provision.
- **s.131:** *"an individual not eligible to claim deduction under section 130"*, the same interest up to ₹150000;
  (2) sanctioned 1-4-2019 to 31-3-2022; *"stamp duty value"* ≤ ₹45 lakh; no house owned on sanction; (3) the bar.
- **s.132:** an individual, interest payable on a loan *"for the purpose of purchase of an electric vehicle"*,
  sanctioned 1-4-2019 to 31-3-2023, *"shall not exceed ₹ 150000"*; (4) the bar.
- **Rulebook** `DED-80E-001`, `-002`, `DED-80EE-001`, `DED-80EEA-001`, `DED-80EEB-001`, `DED-80EX-001`.
- **Code:** a rule version is a cap or a definition, never both (W-075a2, D-135); a definition may carry its
  figures (`DisabledTransportLimit`, `PremiumShareBands`).

### Findings

1. **Each house / EV section is a limit plus eligibility figures** — a sanction window, a loan ceiling, a value
   ceiling — law data that one cap cannot carry; a definition carrying the limit as well holds them (D-135's pattern).
2. **s.129 has no cap but a span of years** — the initial year and seven after — also law data.
3. **s.130's "value of residential house property" is undefined**; s.131 says *"stamp duty value"*. They are entered
   as different facts.
4. **The bar on deducting the same interest twice** (s.130(4), 131(3), 132(4)) matters against house-property interest
   under s.22(1)(b) — `W-078`, not built.
5. **"Paid" (s.129) and "payable" (s.130–132)** — the entered amount is named for each.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — inputs:** `EnteredFacts.educationLoanInterest` — `EducationLoanInterest(Money interestPaid, TaxYearId
  initialTaxYear)`; `EnteredFacts.purposeLoanInterest` — `PurposeLoanInterest(LoanPurpose purpose, Money
  interestPayable, LocalDate sanctionedOn, Money amountSanctioned, Optional<Money> propertyValue, Optional<Money>
  stampDutyValue, boolean ownedResidentialHouseOnSanction)`, purposes `RESIDENTIAL_HOUSE`, `ELECTRIC_VEHICLE`. The
  institution, the relative and *"out of his income chargeable to tax"* are entry conditions, named in the notes.
- **C2 — s.129's span:** **(a) recommended:** a new definition **`TaxYearSpan(int succeedingYears)`** — 7 — on
  `DED-EDUCATION-LOAN` (a definition row, no cap); the node allows the whole interest where the tax year is within the
  initial year and the span, else a nil line naming the span. **(b)** The 7 in the node.
- **C3 — the house and EV rows:** a new definition **`SanctionedLoanLimit(Money limit, LocalDate sanctionedFrom,
  LocalDate sanctionedTo, Optional<Money> maxAmountSanctioned, Optional<Money> maxPropertyValue, Optional<Money>
  maxStampDutyValue, boolean requiresNoHouseOwned)`** on `DED-HOUSE-LOAN-EARLIER` (s.130: 50,000; 2016-17; 35 lakh;
  value 50 lakh), `DED-HOUSE-LOAN` (s.131: 1,50,000; 2019–2022; stamp duty 45 lakh) and `DED-ELECTRIC-VEHICLE-LOAN`
  (s.132: 1,50,000; 2019–2023). The node allows the lesser of the interest and the limit where every condition holds,
  else a nil line naming the first that fails.
- **C4 — s.130 before s.131:** a house loan that meets s.130 is taken under s.130 only (s.131(1) *"not eligible to
  claim deduction under section 130"*); s.131 is tried only where s.130 fails.
- **C5 — the bar:** recorded on each row and the `W-078` row — house-property interest must leave out interest
  deducted here; nothing to enforce until that head exists.
- **C6 — common:** all four rows `ExceptIn` NEW_TAX_REGIME, TY 2026-27, `SOURCE_CHECKED`; an individual only (a HUF
  refused); one definition switch case added where definitions are switched on (as D-121, D-135).
