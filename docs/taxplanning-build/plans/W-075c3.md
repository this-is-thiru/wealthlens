## Plan for W-075c3 — the Agniveer Corpus Fund, and the pension contributions that are salary (s.16(k)–(l), s.125)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** make the Central Government's Agniveer Corpus Fund contribution salary
and deduct it (with the Agniveer's own subscription) under s.125 — and, found while reading for it, make the
employer's pension-scheme contribution salary as s.16(k) says, which the engine does not do today.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6), two of them about code already built; nothing is coded until you
choose.**

### What I read

- **S2 s.16** (Gazette 265620, pp.37–38), this session: *"For the purposes of this Part, "salary" includes— … **(k)
  the contribution made by the Central Government or any other employer in any tax year, to the account of an
  employee under a pension scheme referred to in section 124; and (l) the contribution made by the Central Government
  in any tax year, to the Agniveer Corpus Fund account of an individual enrolled in the Agnipath Scheme referred to in
  section 125**"*.
- **S2 s.125** (p.161): (1) an individual enrolled in the Agnipath Scheme who subscribes to the Agniveer Corpus Fund
  on or after 1-11-2022, *"the whole of the amount paid or deposited in his account in the said Fund during the tax
  year"*; (2) *"Where the Central Government makes any contribution to the account of an assessee in the Fund … the
  whole of the amount so contributed"*; (3) definitions. s.202(2)(a)(xii) keeps (2), not (1), in the new regime.
- **S2 Schedule III Table Sl. 7** (p.501): a payment *from* the Fund — Nil (exempt); a later-year receipt.
- **`SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES`'s own note** already quotes s.16(k) and (l), and for TY 2025-26 1961 s.17(1)(viii)
  (*"pension-scheme contribution"*, thc.nic.in) and (ix) (*"the Agniveer Corpus Fund contribution, inserted by FA 2023
  s.10(i)"*).
- **Code:** `GrossSalaryNode` adds pay lines, perquisites and `employer.contrib.charges` (the s.17(1)(h) excess over
  ₹7,50,000, W-073a) — **not** the employer's NPS contribution itself; `EmployerFundContribution` carries it (fund
  `NOTIFIED_PENSION_SCHEME`); `ChapterEightNode` deducts it under s.124(1)–(2) (W-075a).

### Findings

1. **OQ-14 is answered, and was answerable earlier.** s.16(k) makes the employer's pension contribution salary — the
   repo's own `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES` note quoted it when I raised OQ-14 (W-074, D-128); I did not check. My error.
2. **The engine understates salary wherever an employer NPS contribution is entered** (since W-073a): gross salary
   leaves the contribution out, while W-075a deducts it under s.124(1). For T4-1 that is ₹3,36,000 of salary missing.
3. **s.16(k) and s.17(1)(h) overlap.** The s.17(1)(h) excess counts the same NPS contribution in its aggregate. Read
   literally both apply; whether the excess should leave out what s.16(k) already makes salary is not settled by the
   words.
4. **W-074a's T4 check is conditioned on OQ-14.** D-128 C4 asserts gross − pay = ₹74,000 *"without asserting where the
   NPS contribution lands (OQ-14)"*. With s.16(k) it becomes 74,000 + 3,36,000. DR 08's own T4-2 (*"the uncapped
   ₹96,000 … remains income"*) already assumes the contribution is income.
5. **The Agniveer pair nets to nil for the Government's contribution** in both regimes (s.16(l) in, s.125(2) out).
