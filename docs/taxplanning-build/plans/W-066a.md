## Plan for W-066a — retirement and termination

**What this item is for (one sentence):** take the retirement and termination payments out of income under
"Salaries" as the Acts allow. That means gratuity, commuted pension, leave encashment, retrenchment compensation
and voluntary retirement. Notice pay and other profits in lieu of salary stay fully taxable.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6), and recommends narrowing the item. Several of its limits come from
notifications or from other Acts that I have not read.**

### What I read

- **The `W-066a` row** (D-096): **creates** `RetirementNode` (`salary.retirement`) and the `RET-*` rules. It
  covers gratuity, leave encashment, commuted pension, VRS and retrenchment, with components `GRATUITY`,
  `LEAVE_ENCASHMENT`, `COMMUTED_PENSION`, `VRS_COMPENSATION` and `RETRENCHMENT_COMPENSATION`. It also covers 2025
  s.18 (`RET-NOTICE-001`), and adds `salary.retirement` as an input of `salary.income`.
- **TY 2026-27, S2 s.19(1) Table Sl. 3–14 and s.19(2)** (gazette pp.41–46, read in full):

  | Sl. | Payment | Deduction |
  |---|---|---|
  | 3, 4 | death-cum-retirement gratuity (s.19(2)(g)); defence retiring gratuity | entire amount |
  | 5 | gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 | as restricted by that Act's s.4(2) and (3) |
  | 6 | any other gratuity | least of: actual; the amount notified; half a month's salary × completed years |
  | 7 | commuted pension, government and similar schemes | entire amount |
  | 8 | commuted pension, any other employer | commuted value of ⅓ of the pension (gratuity also received), else ½ |
  | 9 | commuted pension from a Sch. VII Sl. 3 fund | entire amount |
  | 10 | retrenchment compensation | least of: received; ID Act s.25F(b); an amount notified, not below ₹50000 |
  | 11 | retrenchment under an approved scheme | compensation received |
  | 12 | VRS, for the employers in s.19(2)(h) | least of: received; ₹500000 |
  | 13 | leave encashment, Central or State Government | entire amount |
  | 14 | leave encashment, any other employee | least of four limbs, (c) notified |

  - **s.19(2)** adds three constraints:
    - **(a), (f):** the aggregates across employers and years, A − B, where A is notified.
    - **(b):** the salary for Sl. 6 and 14 (`SAL-SALARY-FOR-GRATUITY`, W-062).
    - **(e):** VRS is once only, with no deduction where s.157 relief was allowed on the amount.
  - **s.18(1):** profits in lieu of salary include compensation "at or in connection with the termination of his
    employment".
  - **s.202(2)(a):** it excludes s.19(1) Sl. 1 only (p.227), so **Sl. 3–14 apply in the new regime**.
- **TY 2025-26, 1961 s.10(10), (10A), (10AA), (10B), (10C)** (thc.nic.in text, current to Act 7 of 2019,
  pp.71–75):
  - The same entries as exemptions. One difference: s.10(10)(iii) also covers gratuity "received by his widow,
    children or dependants on his death".
  - **s.115BAC(2)(i)** as substituted by FA 2023 s.52 (pp.33–34) excludes s.10(5), (13A), (14), (17) and (32),
    **not** s.10(10)–(10C). So these apply in the new regime too.
  - **s.17(3)(i):** profits in lieu, in the same words as 2025 s.18(1)(a).
  - Text search of FA 2023, F(No.2)A 2024 and FA 2025 finds no amendment to these clauses. FA 2020–2022 are not on
    hand.
- **Notifications:** 2025 **s.536(2)(j)** keeps a notification issued under the 1961 Act in force "so far as it is
  not inconsistent", as if issued under the corresponding 2025 provision. So one notification can serve both years.
  **I have read none of the notifications.** Commonly quoted figures, all unread:
  - gratuity ₹20 lakh (S.O. 1213(E), 8-3-2019);
  - leave encashment ₹25 lakh (2023);
  - retrenchment ₹5 lakh.
- **Other Acts:** Sl. 5 needs the Payment of Gratuity Act s.4(2)–(3), and Sl. 10(b) needs the Industrial Disputes
  Act s.25F(b).
  - **I have read neither.** I also recall, **unverified**, that the labour codes came into force in late 2025 and

  - If so, TY 2025-26 changes mid-year, and the 2025 Act cites repealed Acts.
- **Code:**
  - `SalaryIncomeNode` requires gross, exemptions and deductions; its javadoc names `W-066a` as the next addition.
  - `LedgerSnapshot` already carries `vrsClaimedIn`, `gratuityAlreadyAllowed` and `leaveEncashmentAlreadyAllowed`.
  - `SAL-SALARY-FOR-GRATUITY` exists (both years).

### Findings

1. **Seven of the twelve entries need nothing outside the Acts:** Sl. 3, 4, 7, 9, 11 and 13 (entire amount) and
   Sl. 12 (₹500000, once).
   - Sl. 8 needs only the commuted value of the pension. That is an actuarial figure the Act leaves to the
     employer's tables, so the taxpayer states it.
2. **Five limits rest on notifications I have not read:** Sl. 6(b), 10(c) and 14(c), and the A of s.19(2)(a) and
   (f). Seeding them now would break R1.
3. **Two limits rest on other Acts, possibly repealed:** Sl. 5 and 10(b). This is an open question, not a number.
4. **Which entry a payment falls under is a fact about the employer and the scheme**, which the engine cannot
   infer. For example, gratuity under the CCS (Pension) Rules vs the Payment of Gratuity Act vs neither. The
   taxpayer states it, as on Form 16.
5. **Notice pay needs no rule of its own.** It is salary, being profits in lieu, and fully taxable. A component
   feeding `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES` is the whole of it.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — scope:**
  - **(a) recommended: narrow W-066a to findings 1 and 5**, and create two new items for you to place (R4):
    - **`W-066c`:** Sl. 6, 10 and 14 with the s.19(2)(a)/(f) aggregates, and their 1961 twins. Blocked until the
      three notifications are read for both years.
    - **`W-066d`:** Sl. 5 (Payment of Gratuity Act) and Sl. 10(b) (ID Act s.25F(b)). Blocked until those Acts, and
      whether the labour codes replaced them, are read.
    - Until then, a payment the taxpayer puts under Sl. 5, 6, 10 or 14 is **refused** with the reason, never
      treated as taxable in full. Refusing is honest; taxing it in full would be a plausible wrong figure.
  - **(b)** Everything in one item, blocked until all sourcing lands.
  - **(c)** Seed the commonly quoted figures, UNVERIFIED. Refused under R1: none of them has been read.
- **C2 — the input:** `RetirementPayment` in a new `EnteredFacts.retirementPayments`. Each entry has the
  component, the `RetirementEntry` it falls under, and two optional facts:
  - the commuted value of the whole pension, for Sl. 8;
  - s.157 relief already allowed, for Sl. 12.
  - **`RetirementEntry`** holds the Acts' categories, with both years' references:

    | Entry | Payment | 2026-27 | 2025-26 |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `GOVERNMENT_GRATUITY` | government gratuity | Sl. 3 | s.10(10)(i) |
    | `DEFENCE_RETIRING_GRATUITY` | defence retiring gratuity | Sl. 4 | s.10(10)(i) |
    | `GOVERNMENT_COMMUTATION` | government commutation | Sl. 7 | s.10(10A)(i) |
    | `OTHER_EMPLOYER_COMMUTATION` | other employer's commutation | Sl. 8 | (10A)(ii) |
    | `FUND_COMMUTATION` | commutation from a fund | Sl. 9 | (10A)(iii) |
    | `APPROVED_SCHEME_RETRENCHMENT` | retrenchment under an approved scheme | Sl. 11 | (10B) second proviso |
    | `VOLUNTARY_RETIREMENT` | voluntary retirement | Sl. 12 | (10C) |
    | `GOVERNMENT_LEAVE_ENCASHMENT` | government leave encashment | Sl. 13 | (10AA)(i) |

    W-066c/d add their constants. Whether gratuity was also received (Sl. 8) is read from the entries, not asked
    twice.
- **C3 — rows:** one per payment in C2, named for the concept (D-035), all `AllRegimes`, both years. The entire
  amounts are `PercentOfBase` 100 of the payment received. The two limits:
  - `RET-COMMUTED-PENSION-OTHER`: `PercentOfBase` of the stated commuted value — ⅓ when gratuity was received,
    else ½. These are two limbs chosen by the node, as the Act chooses them.
  - `RET-VOLUNTARY-RETIREMENT`: `LeastOf` received and `Fixed` ₹500000. It is refused where `vrsClaimedIn` names
    another year, or s.157 relief was allowed.
- **C4 — components:**
  - The five in `DR 05` M2, each feeding `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES` and its rows.
  - `NOTICE_PAY`, which feeds `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES` only. Its note cites s.18(1)(a) and s.17(3)(i).
- **C5 — node:** `RetirementNode` produces `salary.retirement` (`MoneyValue`), with one line per payment and its
  `limitedBy`. `salary.income` subtracts it, and every regime's pipeline gains the node.
  - **Line kind:** `DEDUCTION`, being s.19(1)'s word, although TY 2025-26 calls the same sums exemptions.
    Alternative: `EXEMPTION` for TY 2025-26. The figure is the same either way; only the trace wording differs.
- **C6 — sourcing:**
  - **TY 2026-27:** S2 (gazette 265620), `SOURCE_CHECKED`.
  - **TY 2025-26:** the thc.nic.in text, current to Act 7 of 2019, with FA 2023–2025 searched. Versions are
    `UNVERIFIED`, with `whatToCheck` naming FA 2020, 2021 and 2022, unless you or a delegated agent confirm none
    amends s.10(10)–(10C). As with W-062, this makes the TY 2025-26 goldens PROVISIONAL.
