### Q2 — `RATE-CESS-001`, 1961 side

FA 2026 (S3, `egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/271439.pdf`) **s.2(6)**, p.9: *"The amount of
income-tax as specified in sub-sections (1) to (5) and as increased by the applicable surcharge …
shall be further increased by an additional surcharge … to be called the "Health and Education Cess
on income-tax", calculated at the rate of four per cent. of such income-tax and surcharge …"*. It is
s.2 — the 1961-Act charge for AY 2026-27 (s.2(1), p.2) — because the next sub-section, (7), applies
"for the purposes of this section and Parts I-A and IV-A of the First Schedule". The rulebook's
"FA 2026 s.2(11)/(12)" is **wrong**; the 2025-Act side is s.3(15)/(16), p.29, as the rulebook says.
