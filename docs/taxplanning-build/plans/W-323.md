## Plan for W-323 — ITR-2's accrual tables as cells (TY 2025-26)

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** fill ITR-2's two "information about accrual/receipt"
tables by period, using the dates W-112a reads. These are Schedule CG item F (capital gains by rate category) and
Schedule OS item 10 (winnings and dividends). They are the break-up the form asks for "for allowing applicable relief
from section 234C" (W-112a C5a, D-661).

**Sources (Gazette, read this session):**
- ITR-2, Gazette CG-DL-E-30032026-271435 (sha256 `aef8d2f1956238ae`):
  - **CG F** (p.50): seven rows (STCG @ 20 %, @ 30 %, at applicable rates, at DTAA rates; LTCG @ 12.5 %, at DTAA rates;
    VDA @ 30 %). Each says "Enter value from item 3iii / 3iv / 3v / 3vi / 3vii / 3viii of schedule BFLA", or for VDA
    item 16 of Schedule SI. The five periods are up to 15/6, 16/6 – 15/9, 16/9 – 15/12, 16/12 – 15/3 and 16/3 – 31/3.
  - **OS 10** (pp.55 – 56): row 1 winnings (s.2(24)(ix)); 2 online games (s.115BBJ); **3a dividends at 1a(i)**;
    **3b dividends at 1a(iii)**; 4 – 9 non-resident and DTAA dividends; 10 s.89A income. The five periods are the same.
    Row 1a(ii), s.2(22)(e), has no row here, as s.425(6) excludes it from the relief.
- 2025 s.2(40) (Gazette 265620 p.10): **(e)** is a loan or advance to a shareholder; **(f)** is a payment on a buy-back
  of shares.

**What the engine has:**
- Each capital-gains bucket's outcome carries its part and gain (`CapitalGainsHead.bucketOutcomes`). Its transfer date
  is on the bucket's facts.
- Each other-source item carries its kind, received amount and, since W-112a, `receivedOn` (`OtherSourceItem`).
- Cells can already say "unavailable, because …" (`ScheduleMapper.Reading.unavailable`).
- `Figures.items` holds money only (`Map<String, Money>` per item), so it cannot carry a date.

**Finding: a W-093a placement defect.**
- `DEEMED_DIVIDEND` is the s.2(40)(f) / 2(22)(f) kind (`OtherSourceKind` Javadoc). W-112a treats it so: relieved,
  since only (e) is excluded.
- But W-093a's cell `OS_DIVIDEND_2_22_E` puts it on the **2(22)(e)** row, 1a(ii). 1a(iii), the (f) row, is left as
  ManualEntry, with guidance telling the person to "move any under 2(22)(f) here".
- My W-093a Q2a recommended that placement, and it reads the kind's definition backwards. OS 10 row 3b reads 1a(iii),
  so this item meets it head on.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: capital gains by period, given that CG F's rows are the BFLA figures, after set-off.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** each period holds the gains of that category's buckets transferred in it. This is filled
    only where the five periods add up to the category's BFLA figure (that is, no loss was set off against it).
    Otherwise every cell of the row is unavailable, with guidance to split the BFLA figure from the person's records.
    The form does not say how a set-off is spread across periods.
  - C1b: spread the set-off across periods (earliest first, or pro rata). That is a rule the form does not state.
- **C2: dividends by period.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** 3a and 3b hold the gross of the kinds their row of 1a holds, by `receivedOn`. Where any
    such item has no date, that row's cells are unavailable and say so.
  - C2b: put undated items in the first period. That would be a guess.
- **C3: rows the engine does not compute.** CG F rows 2, 4, 6, 7 and OS 10 rows 1, 2 and 4 – 10.
  - **C3a (recommended):** one ManualEntry per row, naming why, as W-093a did.
- **C4: the deemed-dividend placement.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** fix it here. `OS_DIVIDEND_2_22_F` (1a(iii)) reads `DEEMED_DIVIDEND`. 1a(ii) becomes a
    ManualEntry: a loan to a shareholder is not a kind the engine holds. `OS_DIVIDENDS` (1a) adds (f), not (e). I'll
    write an erratum on D-658's Q2a.
  - C4b: place a separate row; 3b stays ManualEntry until it is built.

**Design (C1a – C4a):**
- `Figures` gains `accruals`: by item kind, each item's amount with its date or none.
  - `TaxCalculator` fills it: each bucket's gain under its part's kind, with its transfer date; each other-source
    item's received amount under its `OtherSourceKind`, with `receivedOn`.
- Two sources on `FieldSource`:
  - `FromCapitalGainsAccrual(parts, period, settledBy)` — the gains of the parts in the period, checked against the
    set-off row and column the form names.
  - `FromOtherSourceAccrual(kinds, period)`.
  - A period is `{from, to}` as `MM-dd`, the Gazette's dates, held in the cell. It is form layout, not law.
- `ItrFormJson` reads both. `ItrScheduleReferences` / I21 check them like their siblings: kinds and parts known, the
  period inside the financial year.
- Cells: CG F rows 1, 3, 5 × 5 periods = 15; OS 10 rows 3a, 3b × 5 = 10; plus 2 ManualEntry rows for CG and 3 for OS.
  That is about 30 cell files on ITR-2's CG and OS schedules (TY 2025-26). C4a moves 3 cells.
- Relock (the forms layer) and the hand-listed cell tests.

**Worked case (by hand, for the route test, TY 2025-26, ITR-2):**
- **Inputs:**
  - Salary ₹5,00,000.
  - STCG s.111A: ₹50,000 sold 10 May 2025 and ₹30,000 sold 20 January 2026.
  - LTCG s.112A: ₹2,00,000 sold 1 August 2025.
  - Dividends: ₹10,000 received 5 September 2025 and ₹20,000 received 20 March 2026.
- **CG F:**
  - Row 1 (STCG 20 %): (i) 50,000, (iv) 30,000, others 0. The total 80,000 equals BFLA 3iii.
  - Row 5 (LTCG 12.5 %): (ii) 2,00,000.
  - Row 3 (applicable rates): all 0.
- **OS 10 row 3a:** (ii) 10,000, (v) 20,000. The total 30,000 equals OS 1a(i).
- **Variants:**
  - A ₹20,000 short-term loss (s.111A, sold 1 February 2026) is set off within row 1. BFLA 3iii becomes 60,000, so
    row 1 is unavailable.
  - A dividend with no date makes row 3a unavailable.
  - A deemed (f) dividend of ₹5,000 received 1 July 2025 puts 5,000 at 1a(iii) and at 3b (ii), and nil at 1a(ii).
- Each figure is read off the inputs. Which sale a gain belongs to, and its rate category, come from the engine's
  existing parts (no new law).
