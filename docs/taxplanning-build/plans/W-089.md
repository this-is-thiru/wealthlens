## Plan for W-089 — T13-1: the rebate with special-rate income

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** one golden computation that proves a ₹12,00,000 total
income made almost entirely of long-term capital gains pays its special-rate tax, because s.156(3) caps the new
regime's rebate at the tax at slab rates, which is nil — while a rebate taken against the whole tax would have wiped
the bill out (`DR 13` R-5, the "most dangerous interaction in the design").

**Files I will create:**
- `backend/src/test/java/com/thiru/wealthlens/taxengine/golden/T13RebateAndSpecialRateTest.java` — the T13-1 case,
  built from a real `Employment` and a real `CapitalGainsInput` bucket, run through the shipped
  `NEW_TAX_REGIME` pipeline to `round.netpayable`, with the shift, the special-rate line and the nil rebate asserted
  separately so a failure says which step moved.

**Files I will modify:**
- `backend/src/test/java/com/thiru/wealthlens/taxengine/golden/GoldenCaseRunner.java` — **one added method**: a run
  of the shipped pipeline from the heads to net payable on a given `EngineInputs`. Every existing method there either
  enters at total income (`:128-137`, a `GivenTotalIncome` stands in for the heads) or stops at one slot
  (`:151`, `:182`), and the node list `BUILT` (`:85-110`) is the one place the real nodes are named — so the test can
  reach `totalincome` without a second copy of that list drifting from it. Nothing existing changes.

  *This is the one file outside the item's "T13-1 test" wording, and it is test support, not a source change. If the
  owner prefers no touch there, the alternative is the node list duplicated in the test — worse, and I will not do it
  unasked.*

### Build (session 43) — what was built, and where it departed from this plan

**Built as approved.** One method on the test support, `GoldenCaseRunner:207-218` — the shipped pipeline's own
node list plus the real `TotalIncomeNode`, entered at the heads — and one test class,
`T13RebateAndSpecialRateTest`, whose single test builds the case's facts (basic pay ₹1,75,000; one long-term
listed-equity lot bought 1-4-2024 for ₹5,00,000 and sold on NSE 1-9-2026 for ₹16,00,000, STT paid both sides) and
asserts, in this order, the total income ₹12,00,000, the `RATE-CG-SHORTFALL-ORDER` measure line 11,00,000 → 8,00,000,
`TaxByBasis` nil at slab rates and 84,375 at `RATE_CG_LONG_LISTED_EQUITY`, the `REBATE-NEW-TAX-REGIME` line nil with a
reason naming the slab-rate tax, `tax.afterrebate` equal to `tax.bybasis` (the special-rate tax stands untouched),
cess 3,375 and `round.netpayable` 87,750. No engine, pack or rule-data file changed.

**The hand-derived figures held on the first run.** That is the only thing the first-run success proves — the figure
came from the pack rows, not from the run (rule R3).

**Departure — the deliberate violation failed differently from the plan's prediction.** The plan expected the
cap-bypassed `RebateNode` to pay nil and the golden to fail on 87,750. It failed **before** that assertion:
`java.lang.IllegalStateException: Rebates of 60000.00 under regime NEW_TAX_REGIME exceed the tax at slab rates 0.00;
the pack's rebate rows overlap` — `RebateNode:81-84`, the second guard, which refuses a rebate larger than the slab
tax. So the probe proved the case reaches the cap (and the node's overlap guard, which no test of this case had
exercised before), but it did not demonstrate a nil bill: a rebate in this engine comes off the slab-rate tax only,
so the shape cannot produce the design's nil-from-a-scalar-tax answer. One line, at `RebateNode:100`, reverted; the
re-run and the unit tier are green. Recorded rather than re-planned: the item is a test and the deviation changes no
figure or file.
