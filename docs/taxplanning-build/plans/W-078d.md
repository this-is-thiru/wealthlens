## Plan for W-078d — house-property catalogue entries

**What this item is for (one sentence):** write the section-catalogue entries that let the house-property sections
(2025 Act ss.20–25, 1961 ss.22–27 with 25A) be marked `MODELLED` (SC-1). Each entry explains in plain words, with no
figures, what the section does and which built rule decides it.

**This plan has decisions C1–C5; nothing is written until you choose. C2 changes what W-078d's own row says, and
why is below.**

### What I read

- **The row:** WI W-078d *(D-158)*: entries for S2 ss.20, 22–25 / 1961 ss.22, 24–27, then those sections `MODELLED`;
  s.21 / 1961 s.23 catalogued "but kept `PLANNED`" for stock-in-trade, deferred with the business head.
- **The precedent:** plans W-076a / W-076b (D-150, D-152), and `SEC-LEAVE-TRAVEL-CONCESSION.json` (a built section
  with one limb `NotModelled`).
- **The contract:**
  - `SectionCatalogue.verify`:
    - SC-1: a `MODELLED` section needs an entry citing it. `SectionRef.citedBy` reads the first section of a
      provision, so an entry covers one section per Act.
    - SC-2: no digit, ₹, %, braces, "Rs", "lakh" or "crore" in any text.
    - SC-3: every `ByRule` names a rule on the timeline.
  - `Coverage`:
    - `MODELLED` needs at least one rule and a manager.
    - `PLANNED` needs a backlog item. After this item, nothing in the backlog builds stock-in-trade: the business
      sections (2025 ss.26 onward) are `DEFERRED BUILT_LATER` and no row builds the business head.

  - 1961 (thc pp.145–148): s.22 *"Income from house property"*; s.23 *"Annual value how determined"*; s.24

    s.25A *"Special provision for arrears of rent and unrealised rent received subsequently"*; s.26 *"Property owned
    by co-owners"*; s.27 *"'Owner of house property', 'annual charge', etc., defined"*.
  - 2025 Act (Gazette 265620 pp.46–48): s.20 (charge); s.21 *"Determination of annual value"*; s.22 *"Deductions from
    income from house property"*; s.23 *"Arrears of rent and unrealised rent received subsequently"*; s.24 *"Property
    owned by co-owners"*; s.25 *"Interpretation"*.
- **The built rows** (W-078a–c, D-155–D-157): `HP-ANNUAL-VALUE`, `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE`, `HP-UNREALISED-RENT`,

### Findings

1. **Thirteen sections need seven entries.** Six pair across the Acts. **1961 s.25** (interest payable outside India)
   has no 2025 section of its own: it is s.22(6), so its entry cites 2025 s.22(6). s.22 is then cited twice, which the
   check allows.
2. **"Keep s.21 / 1961 s.23 PLANNED" cannot hold.** `PLANNED` must name a live item, and no item builds the business
   head. Leaving them `PLANNED` against W-078d would recreate the dangling pointer W-078e exists to catch. Every other
   part of s.21 is built (W-078a, W-078c). The precedent for a built section with one limb not built is `MODELLED`
   with a `NotModelled` disqualifier (`SEC-LEAVE-TRAVEL-CONCESSION`, the children limit).
3. **Co-owners (s.24 / 1961 s.26) and deemed owners (s.25 / s.27) have no row of their own.** The share is applied by
   the node to the head's rows, and a deemed owner enters the house as their own (W-078 C2, approved). The precedent
   for a section `MODELLED` by rows that do not cite it is 1961 s.80CCE by `DED-SCHEDULE-XV` (D-150).
4. **SC-2 forbids figures, rates included,** so "a fixed share of the annual value", not "thirty per cent".
