## Plan for W-079f — gifts: sums and property received without, or for inadequate, consideration

**What this item is for (one sentence):** bring into income from other sources, in both years, sums of money and
property a person receives without consideration, or (property only) for inadequate consideration, above the Acts'
thresholds, leaving out what the Acts exclude.

### Findings

1. **The two years differ in exclusions, not amounts.** ₹50,000 and 10% hold in both; the 1961 10% is FA 2020's. The
   1961 list has the two COVID-19 clauses, (XII) and (XIII), which the 2025 Act dropped. So they apply in TY 2025-26
   only, and (XIII)(B) limits the exclusion to ₹10,00,000.
2. **FA 2021's 20% cannot apply in TY 2025-26.** It reaches only property under s.43CA(1)'s second proviso, a window
   from 12-11-2020 to 30-6-2021 that I recall but have **not read**. I'll read s.43CA in step 1. If it is not
   time-limited, this becomes a re-plan (R13).
3. **Values are entered, not computed.** The stamp duty value is the authority's figure; s.92(4)(a) / the 1961
   proviso say which date's. Fair market value follows a prescribed method (r.57; 1962 r.11UA, the latter unread
   here). Both are entered as the person has them, as fund figures are (D-198).
4. **What is aggregated:**
   - sums: the year's total of non-excluded sums, "the total of which";
   - other property without consideration: the aggregate, "aggregate fair market value";
   - immovable property: each property, "the stamp duty value of which";
   - other property for consideration: each receipt, whose "aggregate fair market value of the property" reads as
     one transaction's.

   This last one is a reading, and I flag it.
5. **An excluded receipt is left out entirely,** and does not count toward a total.
