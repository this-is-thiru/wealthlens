### Findings (probe)

1. **The reading is already form-neutral.** `Form130Parser.java:66` – `:89` opens the PDF and `Matching` (`:96` – `:269`)
   reads any `CertificateLayout`. Only `reads()`, `id()`, `version()` and three messages name Form No. 130 (`:70`,
   `:112`). The probe's copy read Form 16's 93 lines and 3 tables: 87 fields, with values, sums inferred.
2. **The Total rows of Form 16's tables are not found.** `TableReading.java:166` matches the Total row by one word equal
   to the whole label. Form No. 130's is "Total"; Form 16's are "Total (Rs.)", "Total (Rs. )" and "Total ( Rs.)". All
   three tables read PARTIAL, and their data rows go unmapped.
3. **U+2010.** Four labels carry it (W-126e1). With the fold, nothing in Form No. 130 moved.
   - The fixture's font drops the glyph: it renders "VIA", so those four rows read "Not found".
   - An issuer prints an ASCII hyphen. So the fixture prints those labels with "-" (its `labels` override), and the fold is
     what lets them match.
4. **A Form No. 130 read as a Form 16 is PARTIAL, not FAILED.** Both forms open with the heading "PART A", the identity
   check at `Form130Parser.java:111`. Wrong-form uploads are caught only by the notes.
5. **The fixture is Form No. 130's.**
   - `Form130Fixture.java:38` prints "FORM NO. 130".
   - `PartBFixture` prints every table under "PART B".
   - Form 16's tables belong under "PART A". The probe's one-line change, the heading of the tables' own part, kept all 21
     Form No. 130 cases green.

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — sharing the reader:**
  - **(a)** Move the PDF opening and `Matching` into a package-private `CertificateReading` that takes the layout and the
    form's printed name. `Form130Parser` and a new `Form16Parser` keep only `reads()`, `id()`, `version()` and that
    name. `Form130Parser` stays at version 4, since its behaviour does not change.
  - (b) One parameterised parser bean per form, in `DocumentParsersConfig`.
- **C2 — Total rows with several words:**
  - **(a)** `TableReading` finds the Total row by its label's words in order, starting a line in the first column. It
    allows the label to wrap within its cell, as `LabelMatch` does for lines. "Total" alone still matches as today. A
    red-first case covers each of Form 16's three labels.
  - (b) Store "Total" alone in `FORM_16.json`. Rejected unless you prefer it: it is not what the form prints.
- **C3 — telling the two forms apart** (finding 4):
  - **(a) Not now.** A wrong-form reading stays PARTIAL with its notes, as the upload declares the form. The identity
    check is decided once a real Form 16 shows what it prints above Part A (C4); a placed row, **W-126e4**.
  - (b) Require the layout's title line now. That risks failing real certificates whose issuer prints it differently.
- **C4 — the drift probe** (D-740 C4a):
  - **(a)** If you give the path to your own TY 2025-26 Form 16, a local, uncommitted test reads it once. Only the outcome
    and the labels and tables not found are recorded in the source note: no figure, name, PAN or TAN.
  - (b) Skip it; W-126d covers a real certificate later.
- **C5 — the fixture:**
  - **(a)** Rename `Form130Fixture` → `CertificateFixture` and `PartBFixture` → `TableFixture`. The certificate's
    heading comes from the layout's first part; tables print under their own top part. Form No. 130's 21 cases keep their
    expectations; only the class names change in `Form130ParserTest`.
  - (b) Keep the names and add a parameter.
