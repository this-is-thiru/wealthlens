## Plan for W-088k2 — `portfolio` publishes each bucket's transfer date

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** add the date of transfer to the capital-gains read-model,
so W-088k3's engine step can count the holding period from `acquiredOn` to `transferredOn` itself (D-302 D1a), the
facts only, with no term computed in `portfolio`.

**This plan raises C1 and C2.**

### What I read (session 27, 2026-10-21)

  W-088k3", "The bonus-date gap").

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the listing fact W-088k3 needs for equity's twelve months** (s.2(42A) / s.2(101)(b)(i): *"security listed
  in a recognised stock exchange in India"*):
  - **(a) recommended: no new fact here.** `portfolio` publishes what it records:
    - the asset type `EQUITY`;
    - each side's exchange.

    Whether those facts show a listed security is a question of law for W-088k3's rows to state and cite (for
    example, a sale recorded on `NSE` / `BSE`, the recognised list of D-297). That keeps law out of `portfolio`.
  - **(b)** add an `isListed` fact to the read-model. `portfolio` has nothing to derive it from except the same
    exchange string, so it would be a derived flag (CLAUDE.md §4 forbids that).
- **C2 — the bonus-date gap** (Explanation 1(i)(f) / s.2(101)(c)(C)(IV) count *"from the date of the allotment"*;
  `portfolio` has the ex-date):
  - **(a) recommended:** place a row, **W-088k4**, after W-088k3. It records a bonus action's allotment date on
    `corporate_actions` and dates the lot by it, with a runbook entry for existing actions.
    - Until then W-088k3 uses `acquiredOn` as recorded, and its row's `_note` and trace say a bonus lot is dated at
      its ex-date.
    - Allotment usually follows the record date by days, so the effect is a sale within days of the boundary.
  - **(b)** no row. W-088k3 states the approximation and it stays.
  - **(c)** do it here. That is a corporate-action data change, outside this row's "transfer date".
