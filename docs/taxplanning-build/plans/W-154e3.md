## Plan for W-154e3 — the several-cars proviso by rule data

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** where an employment lets the employee use more than one of
its cars otherwise than wholly officially, one car is valued per month (employer running) and the rest at running and
wear and tear. That is today W-154e2's interim in Java (`severalCars`). This item makes it conditions on the path rows,
over two counted facts each vehicle publishes (D-330 C3a; V5; audit P4 `MotorCarPerquisite:150–151`, N1 the
several-cars proviso).

**This plan raises no new choice; it builds D-330 C3a.**

### What I read (session 30, 2026-10-23)

  entered" kept), D-332 (the interim), D-113 (the proviso read as "the first entered"), rulebook `PERQ-CAR-MULTI-001`
  ("one car is valued at the partly-official figure and every other car at the exclusively-private").
- **The code:** `MotorCarPerquisite.severalCars` (`:125–136`) and `carsNotWhollyOfficial` (`:206–216`): the
  employment's employer cars whose use is not wholly official, in entry order.
- **The rows:** `PERQ-CAR-PATH-PARTLY`, `-OWNRUN` and `-PRIVATE` (W-154e2), whose conditions are `AllOf`s of
  `ItemFactIs`. `ItemFactAtMost` (`fact`, `limit`) is used by the engine columns.
- **Tests:** `MotorCarPerquisiteTest:234–246`: two employer cars partly official → `PERQ-CAR-PARTLY-SMALL`,
  `PERQ-CAR-PRIVATE`, 2,60,000.

### The build

1. **Facts** (`MotorCarPerquisite.factsOf`), per D-330 C3a:
   - `vehicle.employer.cars.shared`: how many of this vehicle's employment's employer cars are entered with a use
     other than wholly official;
   - `vehicle.employer.cars.position`: this vehicle's place among them, from 1, or 0 if it is not one of them.

   Both are counts of the entered list. `carsNotWhollyOfficial` stays as the counter. Its owner / use test is the
   fact's definition, which C3a fixed, not a path choice.
2. **Pack:** both versions of three path rows. "Shared" means `Not(ItemFactAtMost shared 1)`; "alone" means
   `ItemFactAtMost shared 1`.
   - **`PERQ-CAR-PATH-PARTLY`:** owner `EMPLOYER_CAR` and `AnyOf` of:
     - (alone, use `PARTLY_OFFICIAL`, running `false`);
     - (shared, use not `WHOLLY_OFFICIAL`, `ItemFactAtMost position 1`) — the one car valued per month.
   - **`PERQ-CAR-PATH-OWNRUN`:** its existing conditions and alone.
   - **`PERQ-CAR-PATH-PRIVATE`:** owner `EMPLOYER_CAR` and `AnyOf` of:
     - (alone, use `WHOLLY_PRIVATE`);
     - (shared, use not `WHOLLY_OFFICIAL`, `Not(ItemFactAtMost position 1)`) — every other car.
   - The `_note`s cite r.15(3)(b) / r.3(2) proviso, `PERQ-CAR-MULTI-001` and D-113's "first entered" (the Rule does
     not say which car; position 1 is the reading).
   - Relock, one bump: law `.97` → `.98`, `.101` → `.102`. No rows added.
3. **`MotorCarPerquisite`:** `severalCars` and its computation test go; `valued` takes the path's computation as
   it is.

### Numbers I will use, and where each comes from

None new. The proviso's "one" and "the other car or cars" become `ItemFactAtMost` limits of 1 on counts. That 1 is
the proviso's own word "one", and it is held in the data. The amounts are the existing rows'.
