### Findings (probe)

1. **Every box heading is found by its own words.** All 13 were found: "Certificate No.", "Last updated on", both "Name and
   address …", "PAN of the Deductor", "TAN of the Deductor", "PAN of the Employee/specified senior citizen", "Employee
   Reference No./ … (If available)", "CIT (TDS)", "Assessment Year", "Period with the Employer", "From", "To". That
   includes the ones the grid wraps over two lines. Each value sat on the lines below its heading, inside that heading's
   column.
2. **The grid is four untitled tables of one row, with no Total.** W-126c2a's `CertificateTable` requires a title and a
   Total row (`CertificateTable.java:28` – `:30`), so the grid does not fit as is.
   - Its columns, the "Period with the Employer" group over From / To, and the midpoint column reach (`TableReading`)
     are what the grid needs.
   - Without a Total, a row needs an end: the next table's first heading or title.
3. **"CIT (TDS)" prints captions inside its box.** "Address", "City" and "Pin Code" sit on lines between the box heading
   and the values, interleaved with the From / To heading line.
4. **A value went missing.** "31-Mar-2026" under "To" did not appear in the extracted words. Unexplained, possibly the
   probe's HTML; the build checks it before anything else.
5. **Size.** A model change (optional title and total, with checks), the JSON reader, the reader's row end, four tables
   in `FORM_16.json` with a relock, and tests. More than one item (§7), as W-126c2 was.
