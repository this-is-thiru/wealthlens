## Plan for W-066f — other gratuity and other leave encashment from more than one employer

**What this item is for (one sentence):** let two employers' gratuities (or leave encashments) in one year each be
measured on that employer's own facts, with only the year's total capped at A − B, instead of being refused (W-066e).

**This plan raises choices C1–C5. Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (session 21, 2026-10-18)

- **The row** (WI, after W-066e, D-224): *"`RetirementPayment` names the employment that paid it (one payment per
  component per employment); each payment measured on its own employment's terms, separation or leave figures …; the
  year's total capped at A − B …; the split of the cap cannot change the tax (same head), so no election (D-223);
  lifts W-066e's refusal for these two entries"*.
- **The law, quoted in the rule notes** (each read for D-106):
  - `RET-GRATUITY-AGGREGATE` — S2 s.19(2)(a): *"if gratuity or gratuities was or were received from one or more than
    one employer in the same tax year (whether or not any gratuity or gratuities was or were received in any earlier
    tax year), the aggregate amount of deduction shall not exceed— A – B"*; 1961 s.10(10)(iii) first and second
    provisos, to the same effect.
  - `RET-LEAVE-ENCASHMENT-AGGREGATE` — S2 s.19(2)(f), the same for leave salary; 1961 s.10(10AA)(ii) provisos.
  - `RET-GRATUITY-OTHER` (Sl. 6) and `RET-LEAVE-ENCASHMENT-OTHER` (Sl. 14): each payment's least-of reads its own

    - `requireOneDay` compares the gratuity's separation with the leave's retirement across all payments;
    - `employmentOf` finds the first employment paying the component;
    - `capped` caps one line at A − B with B from `LedgerSnapshot`.
  - `ComponentSet.Line.employment` is the index into `EnteredFacts.employments`.

### Findings

1. **The payment must name its employment.** Two employers' `GRATUITY` lines share a component code, so nothing else
   can tell the payments apart.
2. **The aggregate cap is over the year's total.** Each payment takes its own least-of. Taken in entered order, each
   is limited to what A − B leaves after the payments before it, so the total is the lesser of the sum and A − B.
   Which payment absorbs the cut does not change the tax (one head, one total). It is declared on the line, as the
   set-off order is.
3. **`requireOneDay` must hold per employment.** Two employers end on two days. The rule's reason (*"one employment
   ends once"*) is per employment.
4. **The other four capped entries stay refused** (W-066g). Once payments are per employment, W-066e's test (*"one
   payment, component paid by two employments"*) can no longer occur. W-066g's case becomes *"the same entry paid
   by two employments"* as two payments, so the refusal is restated in that form.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the employment on a payment:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `RetirementPayment` gains `int employment`, the index into `EnteredFacts.employments` that
    `ComponentSet.Line` uses, required on every entry. Each factory takes it first. The node refuses an index with no
    employment, a component and employment entered twice, and a retirement line of an employment that no payment
    names. Every entry is then paid per employment; the in-full entries deduct the same as before when entered apart.
  - **(b)** `Optional<Integer>` on the two entries only. A second meaning of "absent", and two lookups.
- **C2 — the aggregate:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `capped` takes what this year's earlier payments of the same entry already allowed. Each
    line's limit is A − B less that, never below nil. A line it cuts cites the aggregate rule, *"notified limit less
    B allowed in earlier years and X allowed on this year's earlier payments, in the order entered (the split cannot
    change the tax; D-225)"*.
- **C3 — one day per employment:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `requireOneDay` compares the separation and retirement days within each employment only.
- **C4 — W-066e's refusal:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `OTHER_GRATUITY` and `OTHER_LEAVE_ENCASHMENT` leave the refused set. For the other four, the
    refusal becomes *"two payments of the same entry from different employments"*, naming W-066g. `gratuityReceived`
    is unchanged (W-066g's question).
- **C5 — records:** D-225; the TS `salary.retirement` row; the WI is unchanged (the row is this item).
