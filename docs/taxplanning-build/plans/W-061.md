## Plan for W-061 — gross salary, and what grounds it

**What this item is for (one sentence):** `salary.gross` becomes the total of the SALARIES head that the
partition (`W-060`) produced, cited to the Act's definition of salary. The first figure computed from
entered income is then explained in the trace, and the salary components have a rule to feed.

**This plan raises a design decision (C1) before anything is built.**

### What I read

- **The `W-061` row:** *"`GrossSalaryNode` — `DR 01` R7"*. There's no erratum. `DR 06` G1.1 step 2: *"Gross
  salary — `SAL-CHARGE-001`, `SAL-DEF-001` — salary due or paid, including a former employer"*. `DR 15`'s
  node table: `salary.gross` ← components, perquisites, charges; rules `SAL-CHARGE-001`, `SAL-DEF-001`.
- **Rulebook R7** (`01-rulebook.md:479-506`):
  - `SAL-DEF-001`: *"'Salary' includes: wages; annuity or pension; gratuity; fees or commission;
    perquisites; profits in lieu of salary; advance of salary; leave encashment; …"* — 1961 s.17(1),
    2025 Act **s.16**; *"VERIFIED (S2, s.16(a)–(l))"*, the 2025 Act only.
  - `SAL-CHARGE-001` (s.15, the charge on salary due, paid or arrears) is PLANNED for `W-065`.
- **The coverage register:**
  - 2025 Act **s.16 is `PLANNED` with `backlogItem: W-061`** — so W-061 models it (D-043 standing rules 1
    and 2: `MODELLED`, and a catalogue entry);
  - 1961 s.17 is `W-072`'s (perquisites share it), and s.15 in both Acts is `W-065`'s.
- **Code:**
  - A `RuleVersion` must carry a cap, a relief, a rounding or conditions (`W-049a` A3; `RuleJson`: *"a rule
    with no cap, relief, rounding or condition … would load, evaluate, and mean nothing"*).
  - The cap shapes (`Fixed`, `PercentOfBase`, `SlabLadder`, `LeastOf`, `AggregateWithCeiling`,
    `PerUnitPerPeriod`) are all **limits**. None is "the total of a head".
  - `TraceLine` requires a rule.
  - `ComponentSet.in(HeadCode.SALARIES)`.
  - `GoldenCaseRunner` leaves out `inputs.components` and `salary.gross`, as the T1 test asserts.

### Findings

1. **"Salary includes …" has no row shape.** It's an inclusion rule, not a limit, a relief, a rounding or
   a condition. The engine already carries its content: the registry puts each component under a head
   (`W-059`), and the partition sums nothing unknown (`W-060`). What a row would add is what only a row
   can give: provenance per year, validity, a line in the trace, and a rule for components to
   `feedsInto` (D-089).
2. **Without a row, gross salary is unexplained.** A node can't write a trace line without a rule, and the
   partition writes none, so the first figure built from entered income would appear in no line. That
   breaks P5, "every figure explained". `NetPayableNode` gets away with no line only because it sums
   figures that already have lines.
3. **The TY 2025-26 half** is 1961 s.17(1) (for AY 2026-27). The rulebook verifies the 2025 Act's s.16
   only, so the 2025-26 version must be read or authored `UNVERIFIED` with `whatToCheck` (standing
   rule 3).
4. **Scope held back:** perquisites (`W-071`…) and employer-contribution charges (`W-072`…) add to gross
   later; `SAL-CHARGE-001` (due, paid, arrears) is `W-065`'s.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what grounds gross salary.**
  - **(a) recommended: a rule row can carry a definition, split out as `W-061a` first.**
    - **W-061a:** `RuleVersion` gains an `Optional<RuleDefinition>`, a sealed family like `ReliefShape`
      (`W-048`/`W-049a`), whose first member is `HeadTotal(HeadCode head)` — "the amount is the total of
      this head's lines". It gets JSON, a loader and the at-most-one-of invariant; it holds no rows.
    - **W-061:** the row `SAL-DEF-001` (named by the Act's words per D-035 — proposed
      `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES`) with `HeadTotal(SALARIES)`:
      - 2026-27 on S2 s.16;
      - 2025-26 on 1961 s.17(1), read from the source this session or `UNVERIFIED` with `whatToCheck`.

      Then `GrossSalaryNode` sums the partition's SALARIES lines and emits an INCOME line citing it. Coverage
      moves s.16 (2025 Act) to `MODELLED`, and a catalogue entry is written. The node joins
      `GoldenCaseRunner`, whose dropped list empties; T1 enters no employment, so gross salary is 0 and no
      figure moves.
  - **(b)** No row. `GrossSalaryNode` is an untraced sum, s.16 moves to a later item, and salary components
    `feedsInto` the first rule that reads the gross (`SAL-STDDED-*`, `W-064`). It's smaller, but the first
    entered-income figure goes unexplained (finding 2), and "salary" gets its meaning only from registry
    heads, with no statutory citation anywhere.
  - **(c)** Author `SAL-DEF-001` as a conditions-only row so it loads. That's refused: it's the "meaningless
    rule" `RuleJson` exists to reject.
- **C2 — the node** (either way): `salary.gross` (`MoneyValue`) requires `inputs.components`; sums
  `in(SALARIES)` across employments; no age, regime or period logic.

### Numbers

None from law: s.16 is an inclusion list. The tests use fixture amounts (BASIC ₹6,00,000 + HRA ₹2,40,000,
two employments) and check that the sum is the SALARIES head only.
