## Plan for W-081b — carry-forward of the house-property loss

**What this item is for (one sentence):** use the house-property loss carried in from earlier years against this
year's house-property income, for up to eight years. In the new regime the part that came from a deduction the regime
disallows is destroyed (S2 s.110, s.202(2)(b)(i), (3); 1961 s.71B, s.115BAC(2)(ii)(a), (3)). Today
`LedgerSnapshot.housePropertyLossCarriedForward` exists but nothing reads it.

**This plan raises choices (C0–C6) and one open question (OQ-29), and proposes a split. Nothing is coded until you
choose.**

### Findings

1. **The ledger needs three totals per year of origin:** the loss that arose; how much of it was attributable to a
   deduction the new regime disallows; and how much has been absorbed or given full effect to since. That keeps
   M3.4's rule (the engine subtracts) and replaces the bare map.
2. **The Acts set no order**, either between this year's set-off and brought-forward loss, or among several years'
   losses. Two defaults are reasonable: this year's set-off first, then the oldest loss first (it expires first). A
   third choice matters only for a switch to the new regime: which part of a year's loss is used first, the
   attributable part or the rest. → **OQ-29** (C3).
3. **"The year's carried amount as a result"** has no consumer. The trace already carries it: W-081a's MEASURE line,
   plus the lines this item adds per year of origin. A `TaxComputation` field would be speculative (CLAUDE.md) until
   an item persists ledgers.
4. **The coverage close-out is a separate job.** It is 31 entries, three catalogue entries (SC-1 to SC-3), and
   classifying business and capital-gains sections. That is a W-079g3-sized content task.
