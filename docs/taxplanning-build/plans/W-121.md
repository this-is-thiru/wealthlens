**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** beside each claim's measured worth, say plainly when
making the claim would not change the amount finally payable, so a person can decide whether a small claim is worth
its paperwork (F40.4) — without ever hiding anything.

**Probe (2026-11-15; `DR 22` C6, the rounding rule, `ComputedTax` and W-118's plan read, no code run):**
- **What C6 asks** (`22-claim-catalogue.md:279-308`): three bands from two thresholds on the measured tax effect —
  under ₹5 "invisible after rounding", under ₹100 "below typical effort", else material; and "materiality never
  suppresses an item that increases income".
- **The ₹5 test is wrong at the edges.** s.516 rounds the amount payable to the nearest ₹10, five or more upward
  (`law/rules/RATE-ROUND-PAYABLE.json`, `rounding: toNearest 10, roundsUpFrom 5`). A claim worth ₹2 moves a payable
  of ₹10,006 to ₹10,004 — rounded, ₹10,010 to ₹10,000. A claim worth ₹8 moves ₹10,014 to ₹10,006 — rounded, no
  change. Whether a claim is invisible depends on where the bill sits, not on the claim's size.
- **The ₹100 has no source.** C6 marks it `INFERENCE`; nothing in the rulebook or PRD gives it.
- **What a run publishes:** `ComputedTax` (`taxengine/api/ComputedTax.java:29`) carries `bill` (unrounded) and
  `finalAmount` (what `RoundNetPayableNode` rounds). W-118 measures worth as the bill as it stands less the bill
  with the claim withheld — two runs, so both `finalAmount`s exist at no further cost.
- **Nothing is suppressed today:** W-118's playbook lists every claimed entry with its worth; it measures claims
  only, which lower tax. Income that raises tax is not in it (W-324).
- **Names:** no `Materiality` type exists (`grep -rn 'Materiality' backend/src/main/java`: nothing);
  `03-WORK-ITEMS.md:1828` reserves the name for this item.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: "invisible after rounding".**
  - **C1a (recommended):** measured — the final amount is the same with the claim and without it. No threshold;
    the rounding stays the pack's (`RATE-ROUND-PAYABLE`), so a change to s.516 changes the answer with no code.
    Erratum on C6's `classify`.
  - C1b: the design's test, worth under half the rounding multiple, the half read from the rule. Wrong in both
    directions at the edges, as the probe shows.
- **C2: "below typical effort".**
  - **C2a (recommended):** not built. The worth is shown in rupees and the person judges the effort; two outcomes
    only — changes the final amount, or does not. Erratum on C6.
  - C2b: a product setting you adopt (an amount in `app.tax.*`, stated as the product's and not the law's), giving
    the middle band. You would state the amount; I have no source for one.
- **C3: where it shows.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** on each claimed entry of W-118's playbook only: `changesFinalAmount` as a fact, and the
    final amount with and without. The words ("report it anyway…") are content, written in W-117's catalogue style.
  - C3b: also on F33's completeness items — a second surface, and those are things **not** yet entered.
- **C4: shape.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** publish the fact (the two final amounts and whether they differ), not a band label —
    "facts, not rules". No `MaterialityBand` enum; with C2a there are only two outcomes and a boolean fact says it.
  - C4b: the design's `Materiality(taxEffect, band)` with a two- or three-valued band.
