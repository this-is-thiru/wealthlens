### What I read (Gazette CG-DL-E-22082025-265620, this session)

- **Para 1(a)** (p.561): *"premium paid for a life insurance policy — (i) in the case of an individual, on life of such
  individual, spouse of the individual and any child of the individual … subject to paragraph 2"*.
- **Para 2** (pp.563–564): *"The deductions shall apply only to so much of any premium or other payment made on an
  insurance policy, other than a contract for a deferred annuity, — (a) as is up to 20 % of the actual capital sum
  assured, in respect of a policy issued on or before the 31st March, 2012; (b) as is up to 10 % … issued on or after
  the 1st April, 2012; (c) as is up to 15 % … if the policy is issued on or after the 1st April, 2013 and where such
  policy covers the life of, — (i) a person with a disability or severe disability as referred to in section 154; or
  (ii) a person suffering from a disease or ailment specified in the rules made under section 128"*; (2) *"actual
  capital sum assured"* — the minimum amount assured on the insured event, not counting premiums to be returned or
  bonus.
- **Para 1(c)** (p.561): *"sum deducted from salary payable by or on behalf of the Government … to the extent of 20 %
  of salary"*.
- **Para 1(j), (k)** (pp.561–562): *"contribution for participation in"* the Unit-linked Insurance Plan, 1971 / the
  unit-linked insurance plan of LIC Mutual Fund. **Para 1(l)** (p.562): *"sum paid to effect or to keep in force a
  contract for annuity plan of the Life Insurance Corporation or any other insurer notified …"*.
- **Para 6** (pp.567–568), Interpretation: defines "insurance" (includes certain maturity and minor's policies),
  "contribution" (not loan repayments), "Life Insurance Corporation" — **not "salary"**. S2 s.16's "salary" is *"for the
  purposes of this Part"* (the Salaries head); s.124(13)(b) and Schedule XI para 2(i) define it for themselves.
- **Para 4** (p.565): a life policy terminated within two years (or a ULIP within five) reverses the deductions
  already allowed — a later year's charge.
- **D-130 C1a** held (a), (c), (j), (k), (l); `DED-SCHEDULE-XV`'s sub-caps are one `CapShape` per contributor, applied
  to the contributor's total — a per-policy limit cannot be stated there.

### Findings

1. **Para 2 is per policy, by issue date and by whom the policy covers.** The limit needs each policy's premium, actual
   capital sum assured, issue date, and whether it covers a person within s.154 or the s.128 rules — facts no input
   carries. Summed premiums cannot be limited after the fact.
2. **The issue-date bands are law data** (two dates, three shares, one condition) — they belong in the pack, not the
   node; no existing rule shape states "a share chosen by a date window".
3. **(c)'s "salary" is undefined for Schedule XV.** Three statutory definitions exist, none for this Schedule.
4. **Whether para 2 reaches (j), (k), (l) is not settled by the words.** A ULIP *"contribution"* may be an *"other
   payment made on an insurance policy"*; an annuity plan may or may not be *"a contract for a deferred annuity"*,
   which para 2 excludes.
5. **Para 4's reversal** is a later year's income, not this year's deduction.

## Revision 1 — R13: C2a's rows cannot be loaded (2026-10-06)

**What happened.** Steps 1–2 are done and compile (`PolicyIssueWindow` with its JSON reader and switch cases;
`LifeInsurancePolicy` and its `EnteredFacts` field). Step 3's three rows were written as C2a said, each with a
`PercentOfBase` cap **and** a `PolicyIssueWindow` definition. The pack refuses them:

```
PackValidationException: law/rules/DED-PREMIUM-SHARE-DISABILITY.json (DED-PREMIUM-SHARE-DISABILITY).versions[0]:
A rule version has both a cap and a definition; a rule limits an amount, grants a relief, rounds an amount or defines
one, only one of them
```

**Finding.** A rule version is one thing: a cap, a relief, a rounding or a definition. A row that is both a share and a
window breaks that invariant, and C2a assumed it would not. A definition may carry figures itself —
`DisabledTransportLimit` carries rupees per month — which is C2's alternative (b).

**The work is on disk, uncommitted** (CHECKPOINT "Files touched"); nothing is reverted.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **R1 — the rows:**
  - **(a) recommended: C2(b), one definition row.** `DED-PREMIUM-SHARE` holds a `RuleDefinition`
    **`PremiumShareBands`** — a list of bands, each `{from?, to?, onlyCoveringDisabilityOrDisease, percent}` — the
    three bands of para 2 in one row (20% to 31-3-2012; 10% from 1-4-2012; 15% from 1-4-2013 covering only). The
    node picks the most specific band holding the policy and applies `min(premium, percent × sum assured)` through
    `Rate`. `PolicyIssueWindow` becomes the band's window type (not a definition of its own). One row, not three.
  - **(b)** Six rows: three caps (`PercentOfBase`) and three windows (`PolicyIssueWindow` definitions), paired by
    name — keeps "the share is a cap", doubles the rows, and pairs them by convention.
- **The on-disk work:** **(a) recommended:** keep and amend (the three row files become one; the window record is
  reused inside the bands). **(b)** Revert.
