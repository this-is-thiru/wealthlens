## Plan for W-078h — a notice channel for nodes, and the landlord-PAN notice

**What this item is for (one sentence):** let a node say something *about* a line it produced — the first case being
"give your employer the landlord's name, address and PAN, because the rent paid in the year exceeds ₹1,00,000" — without
that ever becoming a figure.

### What I read

- **The row:** WI W-078h *(D-159)*: `NodeResult` gains notices beside value and trace; the first notice is
  `SAL-HRA-006`, landlord PAN where rent paid in the year exceeds the S4 r.205(2) threshold; the TY 2025-26
  counterpart in the Rules 1962 is sourced in the item. "a notice, never a figure".
- **Origin:** Appendix F; D-099 (W-067 left it out because nodes had no notice channel).
- **Rulebook R9 `SAL-HRA-006`:** "Landlord PAN: name, address and PAN of the landlord(s) must be furnished to the
  employer where aggregate rent paid during the tax year exceeds ₹1,00,000, together with the relationship with the
  landlord if any".
  - 1961: r.2BB / Form 12BB. 2025: Rules 2026 r.205(2) Table Sl. 1, Form No. 124.
  - VERIFIED for TY 2026-27; TY 2025-26 NEEDS VERIFICATION.
  - The worked example (l.600): rent ₹3,60,000 > ₹1,00,000 → yes.
- **Read this session:** Gazette CG-DL-E-20032026-271092 (Income-tax Rules, 2026), p.1687, r.205.
  - (1): "The assessee shall furnish to the person responsible for making payment under section 392(1), the evidence
    or the particulars of the claims referred to in sub-rule (2) in Form No. 124".
  - (2) Table Sl. 1: "House rent allowance. — Name, address and Permanent Account Number of the landlord or
    landlords, where the aggregate rent paid during the tax year exceeds ₹ 100000 and relationship with the landlord,
    if any."
- **TY 2025-26 source, tried:** dor.gov.in `rules_files/IT Rules_1.pdf` opens (400 pages) but predates 2016. It has no
  r.26C and no Form No. 12BB (searched: "26C" hits only Form No. 26C of old r.37; "12BB" none). incometaxindia.gov.in
  answers 403 from here.
- **The code:**
  - `trace/Notice` (severity, message, relatedRules). Its contract: "**There is no separate warnings collection**
    (O-5). A notice hangs off the line that produced it".
  - `trace/TraceAnnotation(sequence, notices)` is the separate layer A8 built for exactly this ("everything said
    *about* a line"; keyed by the line's sequence). **Nothing produces one yet.**
  - `pipeline/NodeResult(value, trace)`: 15 main and 1 test construction sites.
  - `PipelineRunner` renumbers each node's lines into one global sequence (l.116, l.205, "TraceAnnotation, which
    addresses a line by sequence, could not tell ten of them apart") but its `Result` carries no annotations.
  - `ExemptionNode` builds HRA's lines per `Tenancy(occupied, location, ownedByAssessee, rentPaid)`.

### Findings

1. **"Notices beside value and trace" must not be a free list.** That is the separate warnings collection O-5
   forbids. The design already has the right shape: a node returns `TraceAnnotation`s addressing its own lines, and
   the runner renumbers them with the lines.
2. **The threshold is law, so it is a rule row.** It has a version per year and its provenance, and is cited by the
   notice. It is not a cap, relief or rounding on HRA, and no existing definition says "particulars are due where an
   amount exceeds X".
3. **`SAL-HRA-006` is a rulebook number, not a concept name** (D-035). The built HRA row is
   `SAL-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE`.
4. **TY 2025-26 cannot be read here yet.** r.26C and Form 12BB came by a 2016 amending notification whose Gazette
   number is not located.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the channel:**
  - **(a) Recommended.** `NodeResult` gains `List<TraceAnnotation> annotations`. Each addresses one of the node's own
    lines by its local sequence; the constructor refuses one that addresses no line. A two-argument constructor keeps
    every existing site unchanged.
  - `PipelineRunner` renumbers annotations together with the lines, and `Result` gains `annotations` (by global
    sequence).
  - Nothing else reads them yet; the report and API projections are later items.
  - **(b)** A free `List<Notice>` on `NodeResult`. Breaks O-5, so not recommended.
- **C2 — the rule:**
  - **(a) Recommended.** `SAL-HOUSE-RENT-LANDLORD-PAN`, the concept from r.205(2)'s own words, with the rulebook's
    `SAL-HRA-006` mapped in a WI erratum. A new definition `ParticularsThreshold(Money exceeds)` — "particulars are due
    where the amount exceeds this" — `AllRegimes`.
  - TY 2026-27 version: S4 r.205(2) Table Sl. 1, ₹1,00,000, `SOURCE_CHECKED`.
  - **(b)** Keep the id `SAL-HRA-006`.

- **C4 — the notice:**
  - Emitted by `ExemptionNode`, which declares the rule, only where HRA applies in the regime
    (`inRegime(...).applies()`). In the new regime there is no claim, so there are no particulars to give.
  - It is emitted where at least one tenancy is not owned and the **aggregate `rentPaid` of the year's tenancies
    exceeds** the threshold. ₹1,00,000 exactly gives no notice ("exceeds").
  - It is attached to the **first HRA line**, because the notice is about the year's aggregate, not one tenancy.
  - Severity `ACTION_REQUIRED` ("the number is provisional until the taxpayer does something").
  - The message gives no figure (the threshold is resolved from the rule, so the text stays law-free): "Give your
    employer the landlord's name, address and PAN, and your relationship with the landlord if any — rent paid in the
    year is over the limit for the particulars." `relatedRules` = [the rule, `SAL-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE`].
