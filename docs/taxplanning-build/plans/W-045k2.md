### A. The entries

**`SEC-INVESTMENT-DEDUCTION`**: 1961 `s.80C` (`DED-MAP-001`), 2025 `s.123` (`DED-80C-001`).
- **Summary:**
  - one ceiling covers many payments and investments, including the provident fund already deducted
    from salary, so the room left is often smaller than expected (`DED-MAP-021`, H-7);
  - the employee pension limb has its own inner cap (`DED-80C-NPS-CAP-001`);
  - the deduction is not available in the default regime (`DED-NEWREGIME-001`).
- **Routes:** one per item `DED-80C-ITEMS-001` quotes, **23** in all (counted from the row), each with its Schedule XV
  paragraph in `_sources` where the rulebook gives one (1(a), 1(f), 1(g), 1(q), 1(x), 1(y), para 3).
  Every route's condition is `NotModelled`, because `W-076` builds the rule.
- **Disqualifiers**, both `NotModelled`:
  - `NOT_ELIGIBLE`: in the default regime (`DED-NEWREGIME-001`, s.202(2)(a)(xii));
  - `CONDITION_FAILED`: tuition that is not for full-time education at an Indian institution, or that
    is a development fee or donation, or that is for more than the number of children the Schedule
    allows (`DED-80C-ITEMS-001`).

**`SEC-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE`**: 1961 `s.10(13A)`, 2025 `Sch. III Table Sl. 11` (`SAL-HRA-001`).
- **Summary:** part of the HRA is not taxed if you pay rent for a home you do not own. The exempt part
  is the least of three limits, so raising one alone often changes nothing (`SAL-HRA-001`, `DR 22` C5).
- **Route:** "Pay rent for a home you do not own, while your salary includes HRA", `NotModelled`
  (`W-067`).
- **Disqualifiers**, all `NotModelled`:
  - `NOT_ELIGIBLE`: the default regime (`SAL-HRA-005`, s.202(2)(a)(i));
  - `CONDITION_FAILED`: you own the home, or rent is not actually incurred (`SAL-HRA-002`);
  - `CONDITION_FAILED`: only the period you lived there counts (`SAL-HRA-003`).

**Register rows** (`coverage.json`), all `PLANNED`, `assesseeTypes [INDIVIDUAL]`, the only
`PersonType`:

### B. Findings

1. **Not every item is safe to write.** `DED-80C-ITEMS-001` says Schedule XV "includes, **among
   others**". It carries figures that SC-2 keeps out of text: a salary cap on the Government
   deferred annuity, five-year terms on two deposits, a three-year lock-in, and "any two children".
   It also sources **no** lock-in breach. So there is **no `LATER_REVERSAL` disqualifier**; the
   brief says "each instance sourced before it is written".
2. **What "catalogued" means for 1961 s.10.** s.10 holds dozens of exemptions, and the register
   works at section level (D-059 B1). Once the HRA entry cites `s.10(13A)`, all of s.10 counts as
   catalogued for SC-1. That is true only of one clause. It doesn't break anything today. `W-045o`,
   which classifies every section, should decide whether s.10 is registered by clause.
3. **Who can claim s.123.** The rulebook doesn't say whether an HUF can. `INDIVIDUAL` is all
   `PersonType` has anyway, so the register rows say only what the product plans for.

### C. Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — saying the list is incomplete.**
  - **(a) recommended:** a 24th route, "Other Schedule XV items not listed here", `NotModelled` with
    the reason "the rulebook quotes Schedule XV only in part; every clause of its first paragraph
    remains to be read". **No code change**: it uses the existing vocabulary, and a reader of the
    explorer sees the gap.
  - **(b):** a new entry field such as `routesWhatToCheck` (plan W-045k B5). It needs a code change.
- **C2 — counts and tenures in text.** SC-2's scan catches digits; the brief bans "rupee figure,
  rate or date".
  - **(a) recommended:** name the instrument, not its tenure or cap ("a tax-saving term deposit with
    a scheduled bank", "the Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme"), and say "the number of children the
    Schedule allows". Every figure then waits for `W-076`'s rule and a placeholder.
  - **(b):** allow counts spelled in words ("two children", "five-year"). The build can't enforce
    that, and it lets a figure into text that could drift.
- **C3 — 1961 s.10 in the register now**, as the row says, with finding 2 left to `W-045o`.
  - **Recommended: yes.**
  - The alternative is to leave s.10 out until `W-045o`.
- **C4 — 1961 provisions marked `PRESENT`.** `s.80C` and `s.10(13A)` are named in the rulebook's
  1961 columns without the explicit "*not opened*" marker it uses elsewhere (`REG-OPTOUT-SAL-001`).
  - **Recommended:** `PRESENT`, with the rulebook row as the source.
