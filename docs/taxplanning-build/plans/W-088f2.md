## Plan for W-088f2 — the capital-gains rate basis as rule data

**What this item is for (one sentence):** move the decision of which part of the capital-gains head a bucket falls in
out of Java and into tax-year-versioned rule rows. That covers D-241's STT mapping and S.O. 5054(E)'s waiver. The
engine then receives the bucket's facts instead of a `RateBasisHint` the adapter computed.

**This plan proposes a split (§7) and raises choices C1–C5.** No code is written until you approve.

### Sizing (§7) — proposed split

The whole row is the following:
- a new date predicate, with `ItemFacts` gaining dates;
- a new definition type and its JSON;
- a new rule set with five rows × two tax-year versions;
- `CapitalGainsInput.Bucket` reshaped and `RateBasisHint` deleted;
- `CapitalGainsNode`, the adapter and about six test classes rewritten;
- coverage, a relock and the docs.

That is well over ~300 lines of Java. I recommend splitting along the row's own two halves. Each half has a caller.

| Row | What | Behaviour change |
|---|---|---|
| **W-088f2a** | D-241's STT mapping as rule rows. The bucket carries facts, `RateBasisHint` is deleted, and the engine chooses the part from rule set `RS-CG-PART`. Equity only, as today: any other asset has no row and is refused. | None. Every case gives today's answer. A long-term equity bucket with no STT on the buy is still refused, now by the engine, naming the facts and W-088f2b. |
| **W-088f2b** | S.O. 5054(E)'s waiver as a row: a new `ItemDateBefore` predicate, `ItemFacts` dates, and the row `CG-PART-LONG-NOTIFIED` with its date window and its qualifying acquisition kinds. | A long-term equity gain with no STT on the buy reaches the listed-equity part when (I) the acquisition was before 1-10-2004, or (II) it was a qualifying corporate-action allotment. Anything else is still refused (C3). |

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the facts on the engine's bucket:**
  - **(a) recommended:** add a new record `CapitalGainsInput.BucketFacts` and reshape the bucket to
    `Bucket(gain, term, facts, portfolioRuleRef)`.
    - The record's fields: `assetKind`, `sttOnAcquisition`, `sttOnTransfer`, `acquisitionMode`,
      `corporateAction: Optional<String>` and `acquiredOn: LocalDate`.
    - `assetKind`, `acquisitionMode` and `corporateAction` are **strings carrying `portfolio`'s enum names**.
      `taxengine` may not see `portfolio`'s enums, and an open category passes through as itself.
    - The record keeps the constructor at 4 parameters, against Checkstyle's limit of 7.
    - `Term` stays on the bucket (it is W-088k's).
  - **(b)** flat fields on `Bucket` (9 parameters). Rejected: it breaks `ParameterNumber` 7.
- **C2 — the item facts the rows test** (the `ItemFactId` pattern is lower case and dotted):

  | Fact | Value |
  |---|---|
  | `capitalgains.term` | `SHORT_TERM` / `LONG_TERM` |
  | `capitalgains.asset` | e.g. `EQUITY` |
  | `capitalgains.stt.acquisition` | `PAID` / `NOT_PAID` |
  | `capitalgains.stt.transfer` | `PAID` / `NOT_PAID` |
  | `capitalgains.acquisition` | `PURCHASE` / `CORPORATE_ACTION` / `UNRECORDED` |
  | `capitalgains.corporate.action` | e.g. `BONUS`; carried only when there is one |

  - In f2b, `capitalgains.acquired.on` is added as a date.
  - **STT codes are `PAID` / `NOT_PAID`.** For now they are mapped from today's booleans, which means "no breakdown"
    still reads as `NOT_PAID`. That is F1, unchanged and owned by W-088j. W-088j adds a third code, `UNKNOWN`, which no
    row will accept, so that code is refused with no rule change.
  - **(a) recommended:** as listed.
  - **(b)** booleans as `true` / `false` codes. Rejected: W-088j would have to rename every row.
- **C3 — the qualifying acquisition kinds in f2b** (any number per tax year, in data):
  - **(a) recommended: `BONUS`, `DEMERGER`.** This is the sources' conclusion under row 26: allotted by the company
    under (b)(i), and for a demerger also (b)(ix), a s.47 mode. These are also the only two kinds `portfolio` creates
    lots for.
  - **(b)** also `RIGHTS_ISSUANCE`. Rights shares are a fresh issue, not "existing listed equity share[s]", so (b)
    does not catch them. The sources' conclusion does not name them, though, and no lot is ever created that way. I
    would record this reading in the sources first.
  - **Either way:**
    - a `PURCHASE` on or after 1-10-2004 with no STT stays **refused**. S.O. 5054(E) turns on whether the purchase was
      off-market (b), a preferential issue (a) or made while the company was delisted (c), and `portfolio` records
      none of those. The row asks no such fact, so no row applies.
    - `UNRECORDED` on or after 1-10-2004 is likewise refused.
- **C4 — a bucket that no row covers:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `RS-CG-PART` takes `AT_MOST_ONE` on the ground `REGIME_AND_CONDITIONS`.
    - `CapitalGainsNode` refuses an empty answer with an `IllegalArgumentException` that names the bucket's facts and
      the set. That is the same kind and content as the adapter's refusal today.
    - Several rows applying stays an `IllegalStateException`, because that is a pack defect.
  - **(b)** `EXACTLY_ONE`, with `ruleOf`'s generic `IllegalStateException`. Rejected: it reports the taxpayer's
    uncovered case as a pack defect, and a 500 once the adapter is wired.
- **C5 — where the part comes from:**
  - **(a) recommended:** a new definition record `rules/definition/BucketPart(CapitalGainsPart part)`, read from the
    chosen row.
    - The Java `switch` in `partOf` and `requireTerm` are deleted.
    - The two pairings no provision makes become "no row".
  - **(b)** derive the part from the row id. Rejected: ids are names, not data.

### W-088f2a — the rows

Each row has two versions: TY 2025-26 cites 1961 (with D-241's sources), TY 2026-27 cites S2. All are on
`AllRegimes`, in set `RS-CG-PART`. Every condition is an `AllOf` of `ItemFactIs`.

| Row | Conditions | `BucketPart.part` | Law (sources rows) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CG-PART-SHORT-LISTED` | term `SHORT_TERM`, asset `EQUITY`, stt.transfer `PAID` | `SHORT_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY` | S2 s.196(1); 1961 s.111A(1) (1, 4) |
| `CG-PART-SHORT-SLAB` | term `SHORT_TERM`, asset `EQUITY`, stt.transfer `NOT_PAID` | `SHORT_TERM_SLAB` | no special provision; charged with total income (1, 4) |
| `CG-PART-LONG-LISTED` | term `LONG_TERM`, asset `EQUITY`, stt.acquisition `PAID`, stt.transfer `PAID` | `LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY` | S2 s.198(1)(c)(i); 1961 s.112A(1)(iii)(a) (3, 4) |
| `CG-PART-LONG-OTHER` | term `LONG_TERM`, asset `EQUITY`, stt.transfer `NOT_PAID` | `LONG_TERM_OTHER` | S2 s.197(1); 1961 s.112 (2, 4) |

- Each segment of a row id is at most 12 characters.
- `CG-PART-LONG-OTHER` holds whatever STT the buy had: s.197 / s.112 has no asset condition (row 2).
- **Not covered, and so refused (C4):**
  - long-term equity with STT on the sale but none on the buy (f2b);
  - any asset except `EQUITY`. That is D-241's "only equity is mapped", now as data, so a new asset is a new row.

### Risks or ambiguities

1. **The split** (§7) is yours to place. I recommend f2a then f2b, both before W-088j, in place of W-088f2.
2. **C3** is a reading of S.O. 5054(E) for a case the notification does not name (rights). I take (a) unless you say
   otherwise.
3. **`AllOf` short-circuits** by contract (`rules/condition/AllOf.java:8–12`, `:35–41`), and its Javadoc says rules
   list their most certain limbs first. `capitalgains.corporate.action` is absent on a purchase, and `ItemFacts.code`
   throws for an absent fact. So f2b's row tests the mode before the corporate action, and a test proves that a
   purchase is refused rather than thrown on.
4. The refusal moves from the adapter (before the engine) to `CapitalGainsNode` (inside it). The adapter has no
   caller yet, so nothing user-visible changes. Once it is wired, the refusal arrives from the pipeline, still as an
   `IllegalArgumentException`.
5. **Names checked:** a repo-wide grep finds no `BucketPart`, `BucketFacts`, `ItemDateBefore`, `RS-CG-PART` or
   `CG-PART` in `main` or `test`.
