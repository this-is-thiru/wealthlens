## Plan for W-088b — special-rate tax on capital gains

**What this item is for (one sentence):** tax the three special-rate capital-gains parts that W-088a leaves on
`setoff` (S2 ss.196, 197, 198; 1961 ss.111A, 112, 112A) at their own rates in `tax.bybasis`, with the ₹1,25,000 of
s.198 / s.112A, the unused basic exemption shifted onto them for a resident individual or HUF, and Chapter VIII kept
off them. Then `gti`'s W-088a refusal can be removed.

**This plan raises choices C1–C6 and one open question (OQ-33). Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### OQ-33 (raised; a declared default meanwhile)

S2 s.198(3)(b) says the tax on the shifted balance is *"computed at the rate as referred to in sub-section (2)"*.
Read literally, that is 12.5% with no ₹1,25,000, because the exemption is in (2)(a), not a rate. The 1961 proviso
reduces the gains *"for the purposes of clause (i)"*, so the exemption still applies, and the Department's utility
does that for TY 2025-26.

- **Default (recommended):** TY 2026-27 follows the 1961 reading: shift first, then the ₹1,25,000.
- The `RATE-CG-LONG-LISTED-EQUITY` 2026-27 version's `_note` names OQ-33.
- **What turns on it, worked:** T13-1's facts (TY 2026-27, new regime, salary income ₹1,00,000, s.198 gains
  ₹11,00,000, resident):

  - gains after the shift = 8,00,000;
  - default: (8,00,000 − 1,25,000) × 12.5% = **84,375**;
  - literal: 8,00,000 × 12.5% = **1,00,000**.
