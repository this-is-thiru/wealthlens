**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** turn one stored computation into a PDF a person can keep
or hand over — made when asked for, from what was stored, with every line's sentence, figures and rule, a mark on any
rule that is not verified, and one disclaimer (F10).

**Probe (2026-11-15; code and pack read, nothing run):**
- **A stored line carries** `sequence`, `node`, `rule`, `kind`, `limitedBy`, `in`, `out`, `explanation`
  (`taxengine/api/ComputedTax.java:85-87`). So grouping by kind and the sentences need nothing new. **It carries no
  verification status.**
- **Status in the shipped pack:** 1,172 `verificationLevel` values say `SOURCE_CHECKED` and 2 say `UNVERIFIED`
  (`grep -rhoE '"verificationLevel": *"[A-Z_]+"' …/timeline/law/rules`). A4.2 wants a status "visible at the point
  of use" for what is not verified — which is rare, and must not be missed.
- **A stored computation knows which rule text it used:** `rule_checksums` — "the checksum, then, of each rule its
  trace read" (`taxplanning/compute/DraftComputation.java:52-53`, W-138) — and `TaxCalculator.ruleChecksums(year,
  ruleIds)` (`taxengine/api/TaxCalculator.java:399`) gives today's. Equal means the rule is as it was computed.
- **Reading by receipt already guards the copy:** `DraftComputationService.byReceipt`
  (`compute/DraftComputationService.java:141-145`) refuses another account, refuses a profile held for erasure, and
  records the read in the access log.
- **The year's instruments** are `GoverningInstruments(act, financeAct, rules)`
  (`taxengine/rules/registry/GoverningInstruments.java`); `TaxCalculator` has no read for them yet, and has
  `packVersions(year)` (`:387`) to compare with a computation's stored `law_and_exec`.
- **Content:** `content/explanations.json` holds keyed sentences (`otherLimits`, `reasons`, `terms`, `headroom`,
  `explanations`); `Explanations` is its record. `new Explanations(` appears at 6 sites under `backend/src`
  (main and test together); a new part touches each.
- **The PDF stack** is on the classpath (`flying-saucer-pdf`, `itextpdf`; root `pom.xml:26-27`), used by
  `taxplanning/document/DocumentGenerationService.java:39-48`. iText can read text back out of a PDF, so tests need
  no new library.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: verification status on a line.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** a new calculator read gives, for the year, each named rule's verification level. The
    report marks a line "not verified" only where its rule is not source-checked **and** its stored checksum equals
    today's. Where a rule's checksum differs, the line says the rule has changed since this computation, and no
    status is claimed for it. Nothing is added to stored results.
  - C1b: store the status on each line from now on; a computation stored before reads "not recorded". An engine
    result change (every golden moves) for two rules.
- **C2: when the pack has moved on since the computation.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** the report still renders — it is what was shown — and says on its first page that the
    rule data has been updated since, naming the stored and the current versions. Figures are never recomputed here.
  - C2b: refuse to render until the draft is recomputed — the person loses the record of what they were shown.
- **C3: the disclaimer's words** (draft for adoption; instruments and year are filled from data, no digit typed):
  > These figures are computed from {act}, {financeAct} and {rules} for tax year {taxYear}, on the information you
  > entered. They are an aid to planning, not tax advice, and not a filing. A figure marked "not verified" rests on a
  > rule that has not been confirmed against a primary source. Check anything material with a qualified
  > professional before acting on it.
  - **C3a (recommended):** adopt as drafted (A4.3's sentences, with the Acts as placeholders and "needs
    verification" reworded to match the mark).
  - C3b: your wording.
- **C4: the address.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** `GET /tax/user/{email}/computations/{receipt}/report`, `application/pdf`, beside the JSON
    read of the same receipt.
  - C4b: another path of your choosing.
