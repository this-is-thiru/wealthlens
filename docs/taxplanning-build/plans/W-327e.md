The other lines without a reason write only a limit name that the content already words, such as "salary" or
"allowance received" (W-116). They need no reason, and the pin must allow them.

Typical texts: "by 2025-06-15, not yet reached", "still owed by 2025-06-15", "advance tax", "not liable: a resident
individual of 61 with no business income", "total income exceeds 1200000.00", "marginal relief at 10000000.00",
"greater than RATE-SUR-FIRST-SCHEDULE at 15%", "LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY; slab". They carry dates, rule ids, rates and
parts.

`TaxByBasisNode:105` ("total income … less the capital gains at special rates …"), which is in the second group. So the
clause has nothing left to do beyond that site.
