## Plan for W-078b — interest on borrowed capital, under income from house property

**What this item is for (one sentence):** deduct, per property, the interest payable on capital borrowed for it and
the pre-construction instalments, with the cap for own residences, the new regime's disallowance and the bar on
interest already taken under ss.130–131, in both years.

**C1 and C2 below are the decisions that matter. C1 is a place where the statute and the design disagree.**

### Findings

1. **The literal TY 2026-27 text allows pre-construction interest on an own residence in the new regime, within the
   cap.** s.202(2)(a)(v) removes only s.22(1)(b). In 1961 the instalments sat inside s.24(b) and went with it. In
   S2 they are a separate clause, (1)(c), and FA 2026 s.38 put (c) under the cap without extending s.202(2)(a)(v).
   **`DR 06` G1.4** (`interestAllowed = 0` for a self-occupied house in the new regime, pre-construction included)
   and **`DR 01` R16's worked example** (new regime "income 0" on facts that include a ₹40,000 instalment) follow the
   1961 reading. I have read nothing (CBDT circular, FAQ) that says the omission is unintended. incometaxindia.gov.in
   returns 403 from here.
2. **A loss is the ordinary result for an own residence.** Its annual value is nil, so any interest makes a loss. If
   every loss is refused property by property, the cap rules can never produce a value: they can only be reached as
   a refusal. If the properties are netted within the head first (s.108(1); 1961 s.70(1), `SETOFF-INTRA-001`, which
   W-081 owns), the cap is testable whenever another property's income covers it.
3. **The cap is per assessee, not per property** (s.24(2): each co-owner is "individually entitled" to the s.21(6)
   relief). The loan figures must therefore be the assessee's own. Applying W-078a's share to a whole-property
   interest figure after the cap would halve a co-owner's ₹2,00,000.
4. **The cap has two groups and one ceiling.** In 1961 the provisos are explicit: aggregate ₹30,000 for houses
   outside the second proviso, two lakh within it, and two lakh for both together. S2 s.22(2) and (5) say the same
   more tersely. This is exactly `AggregateWithCeiling` (sub-cap first, then the ceiling).
5. **The higher limit's conditions differ by year:** 1961 adds "borrowed on or after 1-4-1999". The years and the
   date are numbers, so they belong in the row.
6. **Instalments are fixed by tax year, not counted** ("for the said tax year and for each of the four immediately
   succeeding tax years"). The tax year of acquisition decides them, so `LedgerSnapshot.preConstructionInstalmentsClaimed`
   is not needed.
7. **The ss.130–132 bar:**
   - s.130(4) and s.131(3) bar a deduction "under any other provisions" for interest already deducted under them.
   - The engine cannot tell whether two entered figures are the same interest unless the entry says so.
   - s.132 is an electric-vehicle loan, so it can never be house-property interest.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — new regime, own residence, TY 2026-27:**
  - **(a) Follow the text (recommended):** (b) is disallowed, while the (c) instalments are allowed within the
    s.22(2) cap. I would append errata under `DR 06` G1.4 and `DR 01` R16's new-regime example, citing s.202(2)(a)(v),
    FA 2026 ss.38 and 56. A row note would record that no guidance was read.
  - **(b) Follow the design:** both are disallowed, as in 1961. The row's `whatToCheck` would name the textual gap.

  TY 2025-26 is the same under either choice: both are disallowed.
- **C2 — losses:**
  - **(a) Net within the head, refuse only a negative head (recommended).** The head is the sum of the properties'
    incomes. The netting is cited as s.108(1) / 1961 s.70(1) in the trace and the javadoc, without a row (W-081 adds
    `SETOFF-INTRA-001`). A negative head is refused, naming s.109(1)(b) / W-081 for the old regime and s.202(2)(b)(ii)
    for the new.
  - **(b) Refuse any property below nil.** This is strictly the row's wording, but the own-residence cap becomes
    untestable except as a refusal.
  - **(c) Place W-081 before W-078b.**
- **C3 — input:** `HouseProperty` gains `acquiredOrConstructedOn` (`Optional<LocalDate>`) and
  `loans` (`List<HousingLoan>`). Each loan is the **assessee's own** interest, not shared (finding 3):
  `HousingLoan(LoanUse use, LocalDate borrowedOn, Money interestPayable, Money preConstructionInterest,
  Money interestDeductedUnderChapterEight, boolean lenderCertificateHeld, boolean payableOutsideIndiaUntaxed)`.
  - `LoanUse` is `ACQUISITION_OR_CONSTRUCTION` or `REPAIR_OR_RECONSTRUCTION`. Repairs get no instalments and only
    ₹30,000 on an own residence: the higher limit and (c) require acquisition or construction.
  - An acquisition loan needs `acquiredOrConstructedOn`, and a date after the tax year is refused (the house has no
    income yet; its interest is still pre-construction).
  - `preConstructionInterest` is the prior-period total. It is entered **as already reduced under s.22(3)**: an entry
    condition, stated in the javadoc.
- **C4 — the ss.130–131 bar:**
  - **(a) Recommended:** `interestDeductedUnderChapterEight` is taken off the loan's interest before the head. Where
    both housing loans and residential-house `PurposeLoanInterest` are entered, the sums must be **equal**, or the
    entry is refused. This catches the same interest entered twice.
  - **(b)** Only "at most".
  - **(c)** No check, javadoc only.
- **C5 — rows (concept ids, both years):**
  - `HP-INTEREST`: (b), and s.22(6) / 1961 s.25, for properties other than an own residence. `AllRegimes`.
  - `HP-PRE-CONSTRUCTION`: (c) and (3) / the 1961 Explanation, for properties other than an own residence.
    `TaxYearSpan(4)`. `AllRegimes`.
  - `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-INTEREST`: (b) on an s.21(6) property. `ExceptIn NEW_TAX_REGIME` in both years.
  - `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-PRE-CONSTRUCTION`: (c) on an s.21(6) property. `TaxYearSpan(4)`. TY 2025-26 is
    `ExceptIn NEW_TAX_REGIME`. TY 2026-27 is `AllRegimes` under C1(a), `ExceptIn` under C1(b).
  - `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-INTEREST-CAP`: s.22(2), (4), (5) with FA 2026 s.38 / the 1961 provisos. `AllRegimes`. Its
    `AggregateWithCeiling` has contributors `OWN_RESIDENCE_INTEREST_HIGHER_LIMIT` (sub-cap `Fixed` 200000) and
    `OWN_RESIDENCE_INTEREST_ANY_OTHER_CASE` (sub-cap `Fixed` 30000), with a ceiling of 200000. These are two new
    registered components (precedent: `MEDICAL_EXPENDITURE_FAMILY`). It also carries a **new definition**

    5 / none for TY 2026-27, 5 / 1999-04-01 for TY 2025-26.
  - Sources: Gazette 265620 and 271439 (TY 2026-27, `SOURCE_CHECKED`). For TY 2025-26: thc for s.24 and s.25

### Risks

- **Size:** about 300 lines of main code (the class, the record, the definition, the node change) plus five rows and
  two components. That is at §7's limit. If you want it smaller, the split would be: s.22(6) and the Chapter VIII
  bar move to a W-078b2.
- C1(a) departs from the design on a reading no guidance confirms. If a later CBDT text reads (v) as covering (c),
  the TY 2026-27 version of one row changes. It is data, not code.
- The C4(a) equality refuses one rare honest entry: a s.131 house entered without its loan beside a different
  mortgaged house. The message would say so.
- `HouseProperty` grows to nine fields. Its two new fields are the house's own, and the loan is its own type.

## Revision 1 (R13, session 14, 2026-10-08) — C5's cap row cannot carry a definition

**Found while coding step 2:** `RuleVersion` refuses a version that states more than one of a cap, a relief, a
rounding and a definition (`rules/temporal/RuleVersion.java`, `requireAtMostOneOf`). `SanctionedLoanLimit`'s javadoc
records the same constraint: "a rule version is a cap or a definition, never both". C5 put an `AggregateWithCeiling`
cap **and** the `HigherInterestLimitWindow` definition on `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-INTEREST-CAP`, so that row cannot load.

**Reverted:** step 1's files (`LoanUse`, `HousingLoan`, the `HouseProperty` change). Copies are kept in the session
scratchpad and will be restored unchanged on approval, because the revision does not touch them.

**C5′ — choices (recommended first):**
- **(a) One definition carrying the limits (recommended).** This follows the `SanctionedLoanLimit` precedent (D-139),
  which met exactly this constraint.
  - `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-INTEREST-CAP` carries `OwnResidenceInterestLimit(Money conditionsMet, Money anyOtherCase,
    Money aggregate, int completionYearsAfterBorrowingYear, Optional<LocalDate> borrowedOnOrAfter)`:
    - TY 2026-27: 200000 / 30000 / 200000 / 5 / none.
    - TY 2025-26: the same, with 1999-04-01.
  - The node does the two-group arithmetic: the any-other-case group is capped at its figure, then the total at the
    aggregate. This is the order `AggregateWithCeiling` documents.
  - **No new components.**
  - Coverage manager: `RuleDefinition`.
- **(b) Split it in two.** `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-INTEREST-CAP` keeps the `AggregateWithCeiling` cap with the two new
  components, and a sixth row, `HP-HIGHER-INTEREST-LIMIT`, carries `HigherInterestLimitWindow`. One more row and
  two registered components, in return for reusing the shape.

Everything else in the plan is unchanged.
