## Plan for W-194 — an audit record of the term the engine decides per bucket

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** since W-160c a trade no longer stores its short-term /
long-term label and the engine decides it per bucket as it computes, so this item asks whether that decision is
recorded, in a form a machine can read, with the computation that made it — the answer decides what the item that
first stores a computation has to carry.

### What I found (session 42, 2026-10-31)

**The decision is made per bucket, and today it is recorded only as a trace line.**

| Where | What it does | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| `CapitalGainsTerm.of` | picks the one `RS-CG-TERM` row the bucket's asset and transfer exchange meet, reads the months from `ShortTermHoldingPeriod`, and decides long-term only when `acquiredOn.plusMonths(months)` is before the transfer; a bucket no row covers is refused, its facts named | `taxengine/node/CapitalGainsTerm.java:44-66` |
| the same, the line it adds | a `MEASURE` line under the row's id, `in` = `out` = the gain, and `limitedBy` = *"Held A → B: more than / not more than N months, LONG_TERM / SHORT_TERM (calendar months, D-303)"* | `:58-64` |
| `CapitalGainsNode.evaluate` | the term is used only to pick the bucket's part; the node's value is `CapitalGainsHead(parts, shortTermLossLeft, longTermLossLeft)` — no bucket, no term | `CapitalGainsNode.java:95`, `:122-123`; `taxengine/pipeline/CapitalGainsHead.java` |
| a bucket the head does not take | one nil line naming the `RS-CG-ELSEWHERE` row, and **no term at all** | `CapitalGainsScope.java:48-68` |
| `TaxComputation` | carries the pack refs, the inputs fingerprint and the whole trace — *"the trace is the result, not a log of it"* — and nothing per bucket | `taxengine/result/TaxComputation.java:56-58`; `TraceLine.java:12` |

**What the line cannot say: which bucket.** `limitedBy` names the dates, the months and the term, but not the asset,
the segments or the venue; `in` and `out` are the gain. Two buckets on the same dates with the same gain — a listed
equity lot and a debt-fund lot, say — read identically. `TraceLine` deliberately carries no display label (A8,
`TraceLine.java:20-30`), so a consumer that wants the term as a value has only a display string to parse.

**No computation of this kind is stored today, so the record has nothing to travel with yet.** Measured by grep over
`backend/src/main/java`: `ComputationProjector.project` has no caller; `PipelineRunner.run` is called only by
`ComputationProjector` and `ElectionResolver`; `ElectionResolver` has no caller; `CapitalGainsAdapter.inputFor` has no
caller. The one stored computation document is the salary path's `tax_computations` (`TaxComputationEntity`), and its
`capital_gains_result` is an `Object` field written nowhere — its declaration
(`taxplanning/salary/entity/TaxComputationEntity.java:47-48`) is its only occurrence in the module.

**Where the stored computation will live, if the design is followed.** `11-filing-drafts.md:43` makes the
`Computation` immutable and pack-stamped; `:289` gives `tax_computations` a `draft_id` and an index by pack and
fingerprint; `:327` is the endpoint that pins the pack and the portfolio `asOf` and returns a `Computation`. E6
(`19-engineering-foundations.md:447-462`) makes the receipt resolve to exactly that object: its pack version and
checksum, its inputs fingerprint, its regime, its draft revision and its full trace. The item that builds the compute
endpoint is W-109 (`03-WORK-ITEMS.md:1419`), and it has no plan yet.

**Why an audit record at all.** R-8 (`13-edge-cases.md:173-186`): a retrospective amendment means the same inputs
legitimately give two answers under different packs, and pinning the pack is what makes a stored answer explainable.
A live re-derivation under a later pack can give a different term — and then nothing says what the earlier computation
decided. Today the term is nowhere but the trace of a computation nobody keeps.

**What is already settled, and not reopened here.** W-160c/D-365 removed the term from `trade_outcomes`, no writer
sets it, and runbook §2.5 unsets what is left. CLAUDE.md §4 keeps a law-derived label off a facts table, so the
record cannot go back on a trade. A stored computation is a different kind of object — the record of a decision made
under a pinned pack (E6, R-8) — and that is what may hold a term.

**W-160g is a live view, not this record.** It serves a holding's or a realised gain's term as `RS-CG-TERM` decides
now, over `portfolio`'s facts (`03-WORK-ITEMS.md:1420`), including holdings and gains in no stored computation. It
cannot stand in for the record, and the record does not outdate it.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what the record is:**
  - **(a) recommended: a typed outcome per bucket, carried by the capital-gains node and so by the stored
    computation.** One entry per taken bucket — the bucket's facts (asset, STT on each side, acquisition mode,
    acquired and transferred dates, both venues), its gain, the term, the `RS-CG-TERM` row it met and the months
    read; a bucket the head leaves out is recorded as left out, naming the row that took it. It stays a `NodeValue`,
    so it travels through the pipeline into `TaxComputation` beside the trace: no second store, no re-derivation, and
    the trace line stays as it is for the reader.
  - **(b) the trace alone.** The term is already in a line under the `RS-CG-TERM` row, with the dates, the months
    and the term, and a stored computation takes that line with it. No code at all. What it gives up: the line does
    not name its bucket, so two same-dated, same-gain buckets cannot be told apart, and the term survives only inside
    a display string.
  - **(c) store the term on `trade_outcomes` again.** Rejected: reverses D-365, puts a law label on a facts table
    (CLAUDE.md §4), and the tax side must not read it (D-305).
- **C2 — when it is built:**
  - **(a) recommended: with W-109**, the item that first stores a computation. Nothing stores one today (measured
    above), so a new type now would have no caller and no end-to-end test — speculative code (CLAUDE.md §2). On
    approval W-109's row gains the requirement, as a row edit (the form D-395 used to rewrite W-185); W-160g is left
    as the live view.
  - **(b) a separate row after W-109**, so the build is placed now and planned then.
  - **(c) build now.** Rejected for the reason in (a): a type no endpoint fills, no store keeps and no test can prove
    end to end.

### The five roles

- **Architect:** the record belongs to the computation — the one object that may carry a law-derived decision because
  it is pinned to a pack (E6, R-8) — while a facts table stays facts (§4); the node already produces the value the
  computation is projected from, so the record adds no second pipeline.
- **Product manager:** *"why was this gain long-term?"* becomes answerable per bucket, months after filing, with the
  row and the pack that decided it; today it is answerable only as prose in a trace nobody keeps.
- **Senior engineer:** the finding is measured, not assumed — the decision, the line it writes, what the node's value
  carries, that no computation is stored, that the adapter and `capital_gains_result` have no caller and no writer.
- **Automation tester:** nothing to test in this item; the guard lands with W-109 — a stored computation over a
  two-bucket input reports each bucket's term and the row it met, and a bucket the head leaves out is recorded as left
  out rather than missing.
- **Manual tester:** after W-109, compute a year holding one long-term and one short-term disposal and read both terms
  in the response with their rule ids; remove the field and watch the test fail.
