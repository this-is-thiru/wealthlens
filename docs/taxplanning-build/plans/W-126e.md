### Findings (sources: `sources/2026-11-17-form-16.md`, written this session)

1. **Read from the Gazette (text extraction, eye check still to do):**
   - **F16-1**, G.S.R. 379(E) of 22-05-2023 (egazette 2023/245989, pp.28 – 33), substitutes the whole of Form 16 *"with
     effect from 1st July 2023 … for the assessment year 2024-25 and subsequent assessment years"*.
   - **F16-2**, G.S.R. 639(E) of 15-10-2024 (egazette 2024/257917, p.6), replaces Annexure-I row 19 with rows 19 – 21
     (TDS and TCS per Form No. 12BAA, then "Net tax payable (17-18-19-20)").
2. **The window is not closed.** No source yet proves that nothing else amended Form 16 between F16-1 and the end of the
   1962 Rules. The Gazette footnote chain runs through every Rules-1962 amendment from G.S.R. 379(E) to March 2026, likely
   dozens of notifications; I have not counted them. Precedent: the owner accepted the Department's consolidated
   rule page to close a window (D-199, D-206), ruling each time.
3. **Shape differs from Form No. 130:**
   - Part A's header is a grid of headed boxes (PAN of the Deductor, TAN, Assessment Year, Period From/To), not labelled
     rows.
   - The quarterly, BIN and CIN tables are in **Part A**, not Part B.
   - Annexure-I keeps the 1961 Act's section words (s.17(1), s.10(13A), 80C …).
   - Annexure-II is headed "Annexure II", without "Part B".
   - Annexure-I's rows A, 1 – 21 and their printed sums, and Annexure-II's A, 1 – 17, fit W-126a/W-127b's line, column and
     sum model.
   - W-126c2a's `CertificateTable` fits the three tables, with titles and headings as printed.
4. **The code is already form-generic below the parser.**
   - `Form130Parser.java:68` takes its layout from `taxCalculator.certificate(year, new CertificateCode(reads().name()))`.
   - `DocumentType.java:9` already has `FORM_16`.
   - `DocumentParsers.java:35` refuses two parsers for one form.
   - The 2025-26 pack has a forms layer (`2025-26.lock.json:14`, `2025-26.forms.5`); `FORM_130.json` is versioned from
     2026-04-01.
5. **One printed sum is self-referential.** Annexure II row 17 prints "Net tax payable (16-17)". W-127b's validator refuses
   a target among its own terms (D-734).
