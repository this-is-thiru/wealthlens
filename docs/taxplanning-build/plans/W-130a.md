### Findings (probe)

1. **The computation exposes totals and a trace.**
   - Totals: `ComputedTax.totalIncome` (rounded), `bill` ("the tax, surcharge and cess before anything already
     paid"), `credits` and `finalAmount` (`ComputedTax.java:16` – `:23`).
   - The trace steps give each node's output by rule: `round.totalincome` (RATE-ROUND-TOTAL-INCOME, out 1,25,000),
     `tax.afterrebate` (REBATE-…), `tax.surcharge`, `tax.cess` (RATE-HEALTH-EDUCATION-CESS), `credits`
     (CREDIT-TAX-PAID).
2. **D-755 C3a's "engine node" fits some rows, not the one that matters most.**
   - One node: row 12 ↔ `round.totalincome`; row 15 ↔ `tax.surcharge`; row 16 ↔ `tax.cess`.
   - No single node: row 17 "Tax payable (13+15+16-14)" and Part B's total tax deducted. Their counterpart is
     `ComputedTax.bill`, a sum the engine reports as a total.
   - Part B against the bill is T8-1's and T8-4's comparison: what the employer deducted against what the year's tax is.
3. **`credits` is no counterpart for Part B.** It is "tax already paid", entered from the same certificate, so comparing
   them would compare the certificate with itself.
