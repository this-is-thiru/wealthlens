## Plan for W-090d — TY 2025-26's ITR-1 and ITR-2 from the Income-tax Rules, 1962 r.12

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** give TY 2025-26 its forms as data, in the same model
W-090c2 built for TY 2026-27. ITR-1 and ITR-2 serve individuals and HUFs (W-090 C3a) under 1962 r.12 as in force
for AY 2026-27, which r.164(14) of the 2026 Rules keeps live.

**Sources (all in `sources/2026-11-13-itr-forms-ty-2025-26.md`):**
- **Gazette, READ:** the forms of 30 March 2026 (2026/271423 ITR-1, 271435 ITR-2). The r.12 amendment chain:
  2026/271423, 2025/262765, 2023/243558 and 250835, 2022/234750, 2021/226336, 2020/215154 and 219667, 2019/201697,
  2018/184509, 2017/175133, 2016/168931.
- **Department copies under your rulings:** 2013 (34/2013) and 2012 (14/2012), READ as pre-October-2013 copies;
  2015 (49/2015), as a cross-check.
- **Not located, by your ruling:** 2010 – 2011. r.12(1)(a)'s income heads are taken from the Gazette ITR-1's header
  (271423 p.16) together with the chain above (D-row with this item).

**r.12(1) as in force for TY 2025-26, rebuilt from the chain:**
- **ITR-1, (a): an individual who is a resident other than not ordinarily resident** (2018), with income only under:
  - (i) "Salaries" or family pension;
  - (ii) "Income from house property", owning not more than **two** house properties (2026), with no loss brought
    forward or to be carried forward under the head (2018);
  - (iii) "Income from other sources", except lottery winnings or race horses, with no loss under the head (2013);
  - (iv) "Capital gains": only long-term capital gains under s.112A, up to ₹1,25,000, with no loss brought forward
    or to be carried forward (2025).
- **ITR-1's bars** (the proviso):
  - (I) assets outside India; (IA) signing authority outside India; (IB) income from a source outside India;
    (IC) income apportioned under s.5A (2018).
  - (ID) a s.57 deduction other than (iia); (IE) a director in any company; (IF) unlisted equity held; (IG) income
    taxed at source in another person's hands (2019).
  - (II) relief under s.90 / 90A or a deduction under s.91 (2013).
  - (III) agricultural income over ₹5,000 (2015).
  - (IV) total income over ₹50 lakh; (V) income taxable under s.115BBDA; (VI) income of the s.115BBE kind (2017).
  - (VII) TDS under s.194N; (VIII) tax deferred under s.191(2) or s.192(1C) (2021; 2020's joint-ownership items were
    withdrawn by its 12th Amendment).
- **ITR-2, (c):** an individual not under (a), or an HUF, with no income under "Profits or gains of business or
  profession" (2017, 2018).
- **Manner of furnishing,** r.12(3) Table s.no.1 (2017; item (b) 2019): digital signature, EVC or ITR-V; paper as
  well for an individual of 80 or more filing ITR-1. This is the same shape as TY 2026-27.

**How it maps onto what exists:** the ITR-1 and ITR-2 files each gain a **second version**, valid 2025-04-01 to
2026-03-31, with each bar's provenance pointing to its Gazette page.
- **Conditions:** the same condition types as TY 2026-27. The capital-gains part is `LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY`, s.112A
  for this year; I check its 2025-26 row at the build.
- **Declared codes** are reused for (I) – (IC), (IE) – (IG), (II), (III), (VII) and (VIII).
- **Computed:** (IV) is `Not IncomeAtMost 50,00,000`. (ID) is `DeductionAllowedUnder` the s.57 rows other than
  `OS-FAMILY-PENSION`; each row has a 2025-26 version.
- **New:** `ItrForms2025Test`, mirroring `ItrForms2026Test`.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: return kinds.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** `S139_1`, `S142_1_I`, `S148`. These are the 1961 counterparts of TY 2026-27's s.263(1),
    s.268(1)(a) and s.280. Belated and revised returns stay with OQ-40, as W-090c C3a ruled.
  - C1b: every provision r.12(1) names (also s.139(3), (4A) – (4F), s.153A).
- **C2: bar (V), income taxable under s.115BBDA.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** a new declared code `INCOME_TAXABLE_UNDER_S115BBDA`, written as the rule writes it.
    Whether any such income can arise in TY 2025-26 is the law's question, not the code's. It needs the declaration
    catalogue entry and the content row.
  - C2b: leave it out, with the reason in `_note`; that reason would need a sourced reading of s.115BBDA's reach.
- **C3: bar (VI), income of the s.115BBE kind.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** a new declared code `UNEXPLAINED_INCOME_S115BBE`.
  - C3b: reuse TY 2026-27's `SPECIAL_CASE_INCOME`. That needs an unread equivalence between s.115BBE and the 2025
    Act's Part A of Chapter XIII.
- **C4: the sourcing ruling as a D-row.** **C4a:** record your three rulings (Department copies before October 2013;
  the closing "Gazette form + read edits"; the 2015 cross-check) with this item.

**Files:** `rulepacks/timeline/forms/itr-forms/ITR-1.json`, `ITR-2.json`; the declaration catalogue and content
rows for the two codes (C2a, C3a); both lock files (relocked with `run_relock.sh`, one bump); `ItrForms2025Test`
(new); `STATE.md`, `CHECKPOINT.md`.

**Probe (limited, said plainly):** no test asserts that TY 2025-26 has no forms (grep of the 11 ItrForm and pack
test classes). The full probe, meaning the data, a relock, and the ItrForm, pack and coverage tests, runs at the
start of the build. Any test that moves stops the build for you.

**Not doing:** ITR-3 and ITR-4 (parked as W-090c3 for TY 2026-27; the same applies here); ITR-U; W-091 – W-094.

**Verify:** `ItrForms2025Test` red first (forms absent), then green, with a boundary case on each limit (₹1,25,000
and ₹1,25,001 LTCG; ₹5,000 and ₹5,001 agricultural income; ₹50 lakh and ₹50,00,001 total income). Then
`ItrForms2026Test`, the pack tests, `ItrFormCoverage` (I19, I20), and a probe dropping one bar so its test fails.
`validate`; the tiers run on the pipeline.

**Post-deployment actions:** none.

**Five roles:**
- **Architect:** law as versioned data; TY 2025-26 sits beside TY 2026-27 in the same files.
- **Product manager:** a TY 2025-26 filer is told which form fits, and why not.
- **Senior engineer:** no new condition types; two codes only if C2a / C3a.
- **Automation tester:** a boundary pair on every limit, and probes.
- **Manual tester:** none until W-091 serves form selection.
