### C1 — what `portfolio`'s reports show instead of a term (yours: D-302 D2a)

- **(a) recommended: facts only.**
  - `portfolio` stores and reports what happened: dates, days held, asset type, segment, venue, acquisition mode.
  - The P&L partitions realised amounts by those facts: asset type and segment, not term.
  - The holdings view gives each holding's acquisition date and days held, not a one-year split.
  - A term, or a head of income, as the law of a year has it, is the tax side's to say. A read endpoint in
    `taxplanning` over the engine's `RS-CG-TERM` is placed as a row next to W-109 (TS T8), so the product keeps a
    by-term view, served by the law.
  - The term-shaped responses (`shortTermCapitalGains`, `longTermCapitalGains`, `gainsByClassification`,
    `/assets/holding/{LONG_TERM|SHORT_TERM}`) change shape. That is a breaking API change for whatever reads them.
- **(b) a term written into `portfolio` by `taxplanning`.** It keeps today's shapes, but stores a derived legal
  label (§4) and needs a re-write whenever the law or a fact changes. I do not recommend it.
- **(c) keep `portfolio`'s term as it is,** recorded as an accepted exception in the audit file. W-160 then shrinks
  to P2 / P5 (the head of income), and the duplicate law stays.
