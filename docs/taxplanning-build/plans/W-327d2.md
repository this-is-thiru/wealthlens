That is **20 new codes**, one new fact kind (`DateFact`) and one new term map (`terms.sttState`).
The `CG_` prefix follows W-327d1's `HP_`.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: the charge's name.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** the reason names the limb only ("an expense of the sale", "part of the cost", "not deducted").
    The charge's code, side and amount are already in the line's charge table (`ComputedTax.Charge`).
  - C1b: a `terms.charge` map with a word for each code. Charge codes come from `brokercharges`' catalogue and include
    `MISC`, so the compiler cannot hold them to a declared list as it does parts.
- **C2: dates.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** a `DateFact(LocalDate)`, written as "2 June 2025" (day, month in words, year).
  - C2b: no dates. The term sentence then says only "more than {months} months", and the window has no ends.
- **C3: the STT state's word.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** content `terms.sttState`. The compiler refuses a state some `RS-STT-CHARGEABLE` row names with
    no word, and a word for a state no row names, as it does for parts (D-687).
  - C3b: a sentence that names no state ("no record shows the STT on the sale, so the year's rule decides it"). No term
    map, but loses what the rule decided.

**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption; no digits):**
- **Terms, STT state:** `CHARGEABLE` "chargeable to securities transaction tax".
- `CG_PART_LOSS`: "It is a loss in your {part}."
- `CG_PART_GAIN`: "It is your {part}."
- `CG_INDEXED_MEASURE_NIL`: "With its cost indexed, the cost is above what it sold for, so nothing is left to tax at the indexed rate."
- `CG_SHORT_TERM_LOSS_SET_OFF_AGAINST_PART`: "Your short-term capital loss is set against your {part}."
- `CG_LONG_TERM_LOSS_SET_OFF_AGAINST_PART`: "Your long-term capital loss is set against your {part}."
- `CG_SHORT_TERM_LOSS_CARRIED_FORWARD`: "The rest of your short-term capital loss is carried forward, to be set against capital gains in later years."
- `CG_LONG_TERM_LOSS_CARRIED_FORWARD`: "The rest of your long-term capital loss is carried forward, to be set against long-term capital gains in later years."
- `CG_CHARGE_TRANSFER_EXPENDITURE`: "This charge is an expense of the sale, so it is deducted from the gain."
- `CG_CHARGE_COST_OF_ACQUISITION`: "This charge is part of what the asset cost you, so it is deducted from the gain."
- `CG_CHARGE_NOT_DEDUCTED`: "The law does not let this charge be deducted from the gain."
- `CG_COST_INDEXED`: "Its cost of {cost} is indexed from {from} to {to}, to {indexed}."
- `CG_LEFT_TO_ANOTHER_HEAD`: "Another head of income taxes this gain, so it is left out here and entered there."
- `CG_STT_ON_BUY`: "No record shows the STT on the buy; under the year's rule it was {state}."
- `CG_STT_ON_SALE`: "No record shows the STT on the sale; under the year's rule it was {state}."
- `CG_HELD_LONG_TERM`: "Held from {bought} to {sold}, more than {months} months, so it is long-term."
- `CG_HELD_SHORT_TERM`: "Held from {bought} to {sold}, not more than {months} months, so it is short-term."
- `CG_REINVESTMENT_OUTSIDE_WINDOW`: "The new house, dated {house}, is outside the window from {from} to {to}, so nothing is exempt."
- `CG_REINVESTMENT_NO_GAIN`: "The sales this claim covers show no gain together, so nothing is exempt."
- `CG_REINVESTMENT_WHOLE`: "The new house, counted at {cost}, costs at least the {consideration} you received, so the whole gain is exempt."
- `CG_REINVESTMENT_SHARE`: "The new house, counted at {cost}, costs less than the {consideration} you received, so only that share of the gain is exempt."
