### What the code does when `engine-enabled` is false (read 2026-10-30)

- **`ChargeEngineSwitch.requireEnabled`** (`brokercharges/service/ChargeEngineSwitch.java:30 – 34`) throws
  `ServiceUnavailableException`, which `ControllerAdviser:47 – 50` maps to **503**. It has three callers, and so
  three endpoints:
  - `ChargeSimulationService.simulate` (`:63`), from **`POST /charges/simulate`** (`ChargeSimulationController:30, 36`);
  - `AmcChargeService.findDue` (`:63`), from **`GET /charge-accounts/due`** (`ChargeAccountController:68`);
  - `AmcChargeService.runCycle` (`:69`), from **`POST /charges/amc/impose`** (`ChargeAccountController:76`).
- **The trade path does not throw.** `ChargeRecordingGatewayImpl.record` (`:62`) returns `Optional.empty()` when
  `!engineEnabled() || !shadowRecording()`. Nothing is recorded, the trade saves, and `PortfolioService.applyComputedCostBasis`
  (`:401 – 405`) keeps the entered charge, because the computation is empty.
- **`ChargeReadinessAuditor.audit`** (`:39`) returns at once, so there is no startup readiness log.
- **"backfill" is gone.** `ChargeSimulationController`'s Javadoc (`:24 – 27`): *"This controller once also carried
  `POST /charges/backfill`, which repriced a user's whole history. It was removed with ADR-32"*. The one backfill
  endpoint left, `POST /profit-and-loss/v2-figures/backfill` (`ProfitAndLossV2BackfillController:34`), never reads
  the switch.

**Found beside it:**
- **`authoritative` needs `shadow-recording` too.** CLAUDE.md says *"`authoritative` makes the computed total the cost
  basis on a V2 buy"*. The computation comes only from the gateway, and the gateway returns empty unless
  `shadow-recording` is on (`:62`). With `shadow-recording: false, authoritative: true`, nothing is computed and the
  entered charge stays. All three flags ship `true`, so this only matters when an operator turns one off.
- **The same claim in `README.md:354`:** *"`false` → simulate, backfill and the AMC cycle answer **503**"*.
- `docs/charges-engine/decisions.md:566` says *"the four entry points … simulate, backfill, the AMC cycle"*. That is
  a decision record of the time, and history; it stays.

### The new CLAUDE.md sentence (C1a, shown here first, since CLAUDE.md is the owner's file)

Today:

> `engine-enabled` is a **master kill switch** — false makes simulate, backfill and the AMC cycle answer 503 and
> stops shadow recording. `shadow-recording` makes the trade path price and record every V2 trade with the result
> ignored. `authoritative` makes the computed total the cost basis on a V2 buy.

Proposed:

> `engine-enabled` is a **master kill switch** — false makes `POST /charges/simulate`, `GET /charge-accounts/due` and
> `POST /charges/amc/impose` answer 503 (`ChargeEngineSwitch`), and makes the trade path record nothing while the trade
> still saves with its entered charges. `shadow-recording` makes the trade path price and record every V2 trade with
> the result ignored. `authoritative` makes the computed total the cost basis on a V2 buy — only while
> `shadow-recording` is also on, since that is what computes it.
