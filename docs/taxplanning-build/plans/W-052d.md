### Findings

1. **TY 2025-26's election is the same in structure as TY 2026-27's** — default new regime; opt out in the return
   (no business income) or by the due date (business income, binding, one withdrawal) — **but under other
   provisions**: s.115BAC(6) and s.139(1), not s.202(4) and s.263(1).
2. **The vehicle for TY 2025-26 is not settled by the Act alone.** (6)(ii) says *"along with the return"*, but both
   limbs are *"in the manner as may be prescribed"*, and the prescribed manner (Rules 1962) is unread.
3. **A regime has no per-year shape.** One regime file serves both years; the year's election must be chosen at
   compile time or carried as versions.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the shape:**
  - **(a) recommended: versions on the regime.** A regime file's `election` becomes `elections`: a list of
    `{validity, protocol}` like rule versions; `RulePackCompiler` emits the year's one protocol into
    `law/regimes.json`, so `RegimeSpec`, `RegimeJson`'s reader of the compiled pack and every consumer are
    unchanged, and **TY 2026-27's compiled pack is byte-identical** (the row's Verify).
  - **(b)** An election rule per year (`REG-ELECTION-*` rows) that a regime names — a new kind of rule row, and a
    reader for it, for data that is not a computation.
- **C2 — TY 2025-26's protocols:**
  - `NEW_TAX_REGIME`: `DEFAULT_NO_ACTION`, no deadline; provenance 1961 s.115BAC(1A) as substituted by F(No.2)A 2024
    s.37, p.29.
  - `FIRST_SCHEDULE_RATES`: `OPT_OUT`, `IN_THE_RETURN` (the Act's *"along with the return"*), `DueDateUnder("139(1)")`,
    the same business-income binding; provenance FA 2023 s.52(C)(e), p.35.
  - Both **`UNVERIFIED`**, `whatToCheck`: Rules 1962 r.21AGA / Form 10-IEA (the prescribed manner — it may make the
    vehicle `PRESCRIBED_FORM` for the business limb) and the interim Finance Act, 2024.
- **C3 — validity:** TY 2025-26's versions `Between 2025-04-01 and 2026-03-31`; TY 2026-27's `from 2026-04-01`.
- **C4 — the regime's other 1961 citations** (`provision1961` s.115BAC(1A) / (6)) are already right; unchanged.
