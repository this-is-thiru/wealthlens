## Plan for W-071f — specified securities and sweat equity

**What this item is for (one sentence):** value shares or other specified securities an employer (current or former)
allots or transfers free or at a concession — the employee stock option or sweat equity — as their fair market value
on the day the option is exercised, less what the employee paid, both years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-071f` row** (D-110): S2 s.17(1)(d), s.17(4); S4 r.15(6)–(7); 1961 s.17(2)(vi), r.3(8). `DR 01`
  `PERQ-ESOP-001` (the value), `PERQ-ESOP-002` (an eligible start-up's deferral, s.392(3)); `TDS-SAL-ESOP-001`.
- **TY 2026-27, S2** (Gazette 265620, pp.38, 40, read in W-071b): **s.17(1)(d)** *"the value of any specified security
  or sweat equity shares allotted or transferred, directly or indirectly, by the current employer, or former employer,
  free of cost or at concessional rate to the assessee"* — for every employee; **s.17(4)(a)** fair market value by the
  prescribed method; **(e)** "option"; **(f)** "specified security"; **(g)** "sweat equity shares"; **(h)** *"the value
  … shall be the fair market value … on the date on which the option is exercised by the assessee, as reduced by the
  amount actually paid by, or recovered from, the assessee"*.
- **TY 2026-27, S4 r.15(6)–(7)** (Gazette 271092, (6) p.1547, (7) and (8)(l) p.1548, read here): an equity share —
  (a) listed: *"the average of the opening price and closing price of the share on that date"*; (b) listed on more than
  one exchange: on the one *"which records the highest volume of trading"*; (c) not traded that day: the closing price
  *"on a date closest to the date of exercising of the option and immediately preceding such date"* (on the
  highest-volume exchange where several); (d) unlisted: *"such value … as determined by a merchant banker on the
  specified date"*; (7) any other specified security: a merchant banker's value on the specified date. (8)(l)
  "specified date" — the exercise date, or a date *"not being a date which is more than one hundred and eighty days
  earlier"*.
- **TY 2025-26, 1961 s.17(2)(vi) and Explanation (a)–(e)** (thc.nic.in p.143, read in W-071c) — the same words with
  "the employer, or former employer"; **Rules 1962 r.3(8)–(9)** (consolidated rule 3, pp.4–5, V5) — the same methods,
  and 180 days.
- **S2 s.392(3)** (`PERQ-ESOP-002`, read in the design): an eligible start-up's employee may **defer the deduction or
  payment of tax** on this income — its timing, not its amount or the year it is income in.
- **Code:** `PerquisiteNode` and helpers; no share price data exists in the engine.

### Findings

1. **Every employee** — s.17(1)(d) / 1961 (vi) are not benefits or amenities under (c); no specified-employee test.
2. **The method needs prices the engine does not hold** — an exchange's opening and closing prices, or a merchant
   banker's report. They are facts of the day and of the share, entered as such.
3. **Which exchange** records the highest volume is itself a fact — the prices entered are that exchange's.
4. **The deferral changes when tax is paid, not the income** — the perquisite is still income of the year of
   allotment. The computation of income is unaffected; TDS and payment are later phases.
5. **The years' texts agree** — only the Rule numbers and "tax year" / "previous year" differ.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — input:** `EnteredFacts.shareAllotments`, each an **`EmployerShareAllotment`**: the employment (current or
  former — an entered employment, possibly with no pay lines); the date allotted or transferred (in the tax year);
  the exercise date; the number of shares or securities; the price paid per unit; and the fair-market-value basis —
  one of:
  - `ListedTraded(openingPrice, closingPrice)` — on the highest-volume exchange (r.15(6)(a)–(b));
  - `ListedNotTraded(closingPrice, onDate)` — the closest preceding date's close (r.15(6)(c)); `onDate` before the
    exercise date;
  - `MerchantBankerValue(valuePerUnit, specifiedDate)` — unlisted equity or another security (r.15(6)(d), (7));
    `specifiedDate` on or up to 180 days before the exercise date, else refused.
- **C2 — arithmetic:** fair market value per unit × units, less the price paid × units, floored at nil; the average of
  opening and closing is taken once over the whole, (open + close) × units ÷ 2, rounded once.
- **C3 — the deferral:** **not modelled here** (finding 4): no effect on the income computed; a note on the row and in
  `DR 01`; the TDS / payment phase carries it.
- **C4 — rows,** `AllRegimes`, both years:
  - `PERQ-SPECIFIED-SECURITY` — 100% of the value the node computes (the row carries the Act's and the Rule's words and
    provenance);
  - `PERQ-SPECIFIED-DATE` — the 180-day window for a merchant banker's specified date. It is days, not rupees, so no
    cap shape fits: **(a) recommended** a new `RuleDefinition` **`DayWindow(days)`**, so the figure is data like every
    other and a later change is a row version; **(b)** 180 written in the input's check — a figure in code, refused by
    R1's spirit.
- **C5 — who:** every employee; the employment may be a former employer's.
- **C6 — verification:** TY 2026-27 `SOURCE_CHECKED`; TY 2025-26 `UNVERIFIED` (the 1961 text via thc.nic.in, the Rule
  via the consolidation).

### How I will verify

- **Listed, traded:** 1,000 shares, open 510, close 490 → fair market value 500; price paid 100 → (500 − 100) × 1,000
  = **4,00,000**, both years.
- **Listed, not traded:** preceding close 480, 1,000 shares, paid 100 → **3,80,000**.
- **Unlisted:** a merchant banker's 250 on a date 90 days before exercise, 2,000 units, paid 10 → **4,80,000**; a
  report 181 days before → refused.
- **Paid more than the value** → **0**. **Former employer** → valued the same.
- **Allotted outside the tax year** → refused.
- **Bite probes:** the average taken on the closing price alone; the 180-day window made 181; the price paid not taken
  off.
