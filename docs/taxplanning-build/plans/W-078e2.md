### Findings

1. **They fall into five homes.**

   | Group | 2025 Act | 1961 Act | Home |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Interest and fees | ss.423–426, 428, 437 | 214–217, 234A–234D, 234F, 244A | **W-112** (exists) |
   | When salary is taxed, and the relief for arrears | s.15, s.157 | s.15, s.89 | new |
   | Tax already paid, credited | s.266, s.396, s.410 | 140A, 198, 199, 219 | new: `CreditsNode` |
   | Advance tax, what is due and when | ss.403–409 | 207–211, 218 | new |
   | Salary TDS, the employer's deduction | s.392 | 192 | new |

2. **s.15 is the "salary due, paid in advance, or arrears" rule.** That is exactly the timing the arrears relief
   depends on, so the two belong together. Today the engine takes the year's salary as entered.
3. **The order matters.** Credits and advance tax come before W-112 (interest is computed on what was not paid). All
   four new items come after the tax computation is complete (W-089) and before the ITR forms (W-090), which need
   credits.
4. **The duplicate-names debt is outside taxplanning apart from one class** (`taxengine`'s `DateRange`).
