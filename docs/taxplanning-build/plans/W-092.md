## Plan for W-092, W-093 (with T21-14, T21-15) — a form's schedules as data, filled from the trace (`DR 21` I5)

Part of batch W-091 – W-094 (`plans/W-091.md`, C1a: W-091 done, D-653).

**What this is for (one sentence, in my own words):** for the chosen form, lay out its income and tax cells as data. Each
cell says where its figure comes from in the computation, or says plainly that the person must enter it, so an
export is one fold over the definition, not a mapper per form.

**Sources:** `sources/2026-11-13-itr-schedules-ty-2025-26.md`: TY 2025-26 ITR-1 (Gazette 271423 pp.16 – 18) and ITR-2
(271435 pp.38 – 70), extracted and spot-read this session. TY 2026-27's forms under r.164 are not located, so this
item ships TY 2025-26 schedules only.

**Probe (throwaway, deleted):** Ravi and Anand ran through the full pipeline; each node's value and the trace's rules
per node were listed.
- **Scalar nodes:** most cells map to a node holding one amount — `salary.gross`, `salary.exemptions`,
  `salary.deductions`, `salary.income`, `houseproperty.income`, `othersources.income`, `gti`, `chapterviii`,
  `totalincome`, `round.totalincome`, `tax.surcharge`, `tax.cess`, `tax.netpayable`.
- **Regime-specific rules:** a sub-cell is a rule's figure in the trace. Schedule S 5a, the standard deduction, is
  `SAL-STANDARD-DEDUCTION-NEW-TAX-REGIME` in the new regime and another row in the old one. So DR 21's
  `FromRule(RuleId)` needs a **list** of rules, of which the run's regime fires one.
- **Structured capital-gains node:** `capitalgains.income` is a `CapitalGainsHead` (parts by `CapitalGainsPart`), and
  tax is a `TaxByBasis`. Cells such as Part B-TI 3a(ii) / 3b(i) ("8vi of item E of schedule CG") need a source that
  reads one **part**, which `FromNode` cannot express.
- **No per-employer figures:** the engine computes no per-employer figures. Schedule S repeats its rows "in case of
  more than one employer"; only the totals (S.2 – S.6) are computable.

**Finding against DR 21 I5 (T21-14):** the Gazette numbers the salary block differently in the two forms.
- ITR-1 is Part B **B1** i, ia – ic, ii, iii, iv, iva – ivc, v (271423 p.17).
- ITR-2 is **Schedule S** 1, 1a – 1f, 2, 3, 3a, 4, 5, 5a – 5c, 6 (271435 p.41).

"Schedule S defined once, referenced by both forms" therefore cannot hold for field paths. What can be shared is the
**cell**: its label and its source. See C2.

**Sizing (said plainly, D-622):** ITR-2's income and tax parts hold several hundred cells (Schedule CG alone is
pp.42 – 48). The whole form in one item is too big, so C1 bounds it.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: which schedules this item ships.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** the model, plus every cell of ITR-1 Parts B, C and D (pp.17 – 18). Then, for ITR-2: Schedule
    S, HP, OS items 1 – 3 and the totals, VI-A's totals, Part B-TI, and Part B-TTI up to the tax payable.
    - Every cell is either sourced or a `ManualEntry` with its reason ("per-employer gross: the engine computes
      totals only").
    - ITR-2's Schedules CG, CYLA, BFLA, CFL, SI and EI are placed as one row, **W-093a**, read from the same sources
      file.
  - C1b: all of ITR-2 in this item, CG included.
- **C2: sharing between forms.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** a **cell catalogue**: `salary.gross` labelled "Gross salary", from node `salary.gross`.
    Each form's schedule maps its own paths to catalogue cells (ITR-1 "B1.i" → cell `SALARY_GROSS`; ITR-2 "S.2" → the
    same). T21-14 then reads "the same cell gives identical values in both forms". The same cell is defined once and
    referenced, as DR 21 intends; only the paths are each form's.
  - C2b: one schedule per form, with a cell's source repeated in each form. Simpler, but a change to a source is made
    twice.
- **C3: new source kinds DR 21 lacks.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** `FromRules(List<RuleId>)`, the sum of the trace's figures for those rules, in place of
    `FromRule`; and `FromCapitalGainsPart(CapitalGainsPart)`, read from the computed return's facts.
  - DR 21's `FromProfile` is **not** built: no cell in C1a's scope needs it (the "no speculative code" standard).
- **C4: a cell whose source is absent from the run (T21-15).**
  - **C4a (recommended):** the export shows the cell as **unavailable** with its reason, never blank or zero. Examples:
    "rule SAL-STANDARD-DEDUCTION did not fire in this run", or a `ManualEntry`'s guidance.
