### Findings

1. **Far over §7.** Ten sections, each with its own inputs and conditions.
2. **Two depend on heads not built.** s.153 deducts interest the gross total income *includes* — Other Sources is
   `W-079`. s.134 needs total income before itself, so it must come last among the deductions, and its *"prescribed"*
   conditions (Rules 2026) are unread.
3. **Questions the words leave open, each for its part's plan:** s.127's *"up to ₹ 75000"* — a fixed amount (as 1961
   s.80DD was, unread here) or capped at what was spent; s.125(2) — whether the Government's contribution is income
   first (as OQ-14 for employer NPS).
4. **80DDB's senior limb is now read**, so it need not be held as D-129 C1a said.
