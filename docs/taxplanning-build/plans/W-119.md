## Plan for W-119 — claim integrity: design and split first

> Written ahead by the planning session (session 97, 2026-11-14) for a later build session. **Not approved.** It
> depends on no open choice of W-117b or W-118. Every path and name below was read on 2026-11-14 at `d44536db`; grep
> each again before relying on it.

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** for each claim in a draft, say how much the law allows on
the person's facts, how much of it their documents support, and what would close the gap (F23) — and make sure no
total ever includes a claim the law forbids on those facts (F24).

**Probe (2026-11-14; DR 12, the engine's inputs and the section catalogue read, no code run):**
- **DR 12 assumes three things the product does not have.**
  - **A declared amount.** C2's `D` is "what the draft currently claims", and F24 blocks `D` above the statutory
    maximum `S` (T12-2: HRA "declared at ₹3,00,000" against ₹2,28,000). The engine takes facts, never a claimed
    amount: `EnteredFacts` (`taxengine/input/EnteredFacts.java`) holds tenancies, journeys, payments and the like, and
    each rule's line is computed and capped from them. `DeclaredClaim` is named in that file's Javadoc (line 17) as a
    type no item ever produced.
  - **Evidence.** C2's `E` reads `ProofOfClaim` amounts. No such type exists; nothing in `taxplanning` or
    `taxengine/input` records a document held. The profile has landlords and lenders (`taxplanning/profile/Landlord`,
    `Lender`) and a draft names them (`DraftInputs.ThirdParties`).
  - **Third-party data.** None is imported (W-324's row says the same).
- **Most of F24 already exists as rule data.** The section catalogue's routes and disqualifiers carry 382 links to
  what enforces them; 332 name a rule or condition, 50 say `NotModelled` with a reason. C3.1's thirteen checks against

- **The engine answers a forbidden claim with a nil line and its reason** (`RegimeVerdict`, the explanations of
  W-327), not by refusing the computation. F24's "blocks the total" and C3.1's "errors, not warnings" ask for more.
- **T5-4's open variant (D-159):** "no expenditure evidence → exemption ₹0, citing `SAL-LTA-002` and
  `TDS-SAL-12BB-002`". DR 12 C2 says the opposite for the same situation: surface the gap, with what closes it.
  Schedule III Sl. 8 (Gazette 2025/265620 p.505, read for W-117b) conditions the exemption on the sum received and
  prescribed conditions — not on evidence the product holds.
- **r.205(2)'s evidence table** (the successor to Form 12BB; `TDS-SAL-12BB-002`): Sl. 1 rent — landlord's name,
  address and PAN above the threshold; Sl. 2 leave travel — evidence of expenditure; Sl. 3 house-loan interest —
  lender's name, address and PAN; Sl. 4 Chapter VIII — evidence of investment or expenditure. Sl. 1 is read
  (`sources/2026-10-09-landlord-pan.md` L1) and is rule data (`SAL-HOUSE-RENT-LANDLORD-PAN`); **Sl. 2 – 4 are quoted
  from the rulebook only and must be read in G.S.R. 198(E) before they become data.**
- **Names:** `rules/catalogue/ClaimEvidence` exists since W-117a (what a claim needs). A second `ClaimEvidence` for
  what a person holds would be a duplicate simple name, which `ArchitectureTest` refuses.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: the split.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** four items, each with its own plan, red test, review and commit:
    - **W-119a, statutory validity (F24):** one test per C3.1 row proving the pack refuses it; close row 9 (the same
      interest twice) and correct the three stale `NotModelled` links; nothing new for the person to enter.
    - **W-119b, evidence held:** the draft records what documents the person holds, for the four kinds r.205(2)
      names; Sl. 2 – 4 read in the Gazette first.
    - **W-119c, the integrity panel (F23):** an endpoint giving, per claim, the allowed amount, the evidenced amount
      and the gap, with what closes it.
    - **W-119d, T5-4's variant and DR 12's tests:** T12-1 to T12-14 as written or corrected by errata, the copy test
      (T12-14) included.
  - C1b: one item.
- **C2: the declared amount.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** there is none. What a draft claims is what its facts produce, already capped, so "claimed
    above the law" cannot be entered; `D` and `S` are the same figure, and F23 shows two amounts (allowed, evidenced)
    and one gap. Room under a ceiling stays F33's. Errata on DR 12 C2, T12-2 and F24's wording.
  - C2b: let a person type a claim amount per rule, so an overshoot can exist and be blocked. A second input surface
    that can disagree with the facts.
- **C3: a claim the law forbids on the facts.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** as today — a nil line with its reason in words, and the total excludes it. Nothing is
    blocked, because nothing unlawful is in the total. W-119a proves that row by row.
  - C3b: refuse the computation (400) until the facts are corrected.
- **C4: how evidence is recorded (W-119b).**
  - **C4a (recommended):** a `taxplanning` record on the draft, `EvidenceHeld`, by kind: months of rent receipted
    and whether the landlord's particulars are on file; leave-travel expenditure evidenced; the lender's certificate
    held and particulars on file; and, per qualifying payment code, the amount evidenced. The engine's inputs and
    fingerprint do not change.
  - C4b: evidence flags on the engine's input records — every record and the fingerprint change.
- **C5: how the evidenced amount is computed (W-119c).**
  - **C5a (recommended):** a second engine run on the facts cut down to what is evidenced (rent for receipted months
    only, payments at their evidenced amounts), as DR 12's worked example does. The gap is the claim's line as it
    stands less its line in that run.
  - C5b: arithmetic outside the engine per claim.
- **C6: T5-4's no-evidence variant.**
  - **C6a (recommended):** missing evidence never changes the computed tax; it shows as a gap with what closes it.
    Erratum on T5-4's variant.
  - C6b: an unevidenced leave-travel claim computes to nil, as T5-4 says.
