## Plan for W-280 — V1 records every trade as delivery

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a V1 buy is priced and recorded under the segment the trade
carries, so an intraday V1 buy draws the intraday rate card instead of the delivery one (PS-13).

**Findings (each read this session, 2026-11-11):**

1. **V1's buy context drops the segment.** `updateBrokerChargesAndProfitAndLoss` (`PortfolioService.java:710-715`)
   builds `ProfitLossContext` with the 13-argument constructor (`ProfitLossContext.java:33-39`), which fills in
   `TradeSegment.DELIVERY`; `AssetRequest` carries a `segment` (default `DELIVERY`) that V2's `buyContext` passes on.
2. **The segment reaches pricing and the record.** The context goes to `ProfitAndLossService.updateProfitAndLoss`,
   whose buy path calls the charge engine (shadow recording, on in every profile) and writes a `user_charges` row
   with `segment` and `schedule_code` (`UserChargeEntity` `@Field("segment")`, `@Field("schedule_code")`); the card is
   chosen by segment among other things (`ChargeScheduleResolver`, CLAUDE.md "Broker charges").
3. **The overload's other callers:** none. The two other `ProfitLossContext` constructions (`:481` V2 buy, `:771` V2
   sale) pass the segment; its Javadoc's premise ("every trade recorded before Chunk 10b was delivery") stopped
   being true once `AssetRequest` gained `segment`.
4. **The seed data has both cards:** `ZERODHA_EQ_INTRADAY_2025_04` and `ZERODHA_EQ_DELIVERY_2025_04`
   (`resources/data/charges/`), in force on 5 Jan 2026 (the 2026 cards start in March).
5. **Stored rows:** V1 intraday buys recorded before this carry `segment: DELIVERY` and a delivery card's charges;
   they cannot be told from real delivery buys by stored fields alone — the transaction row carries the segment the
   caller sent.

**Choices for the owner:**

- **C1a (recommended):** V1's buy passes `assetRequest.getSegment()`, and the 13-argument constructor, left with no
  caller, is removed with its Javadoc.
- **C2 — rows already recorded.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** runbook 3.15, read-only: count V1 buy transactions with `segment: INTRADAY` whose
    `user_charges` row says `DELIVERY` (a `$lookup` on `transaction_id`); a re-pricing would be its own row (ADR-32
    forbids re-driving the engine without one).
  - C2b: no check.

**Files I will modify:** `PortfolioService` (one argument), `ProfitLossContext` (overload removed); a test — a V1
intraday buy of INFY on Zerodha, NSE, 5 Jan 2026, with the seeded cards: its `user_charges` row says `INTRADAY` and
`ZERODHA_EQ_INTRADAY_2025_04` (before: `DELIVERY`, `ZERODHA_EQ_DELIVERY_2025_04`); runbook 3.15 (C2a), so WL-A-2 is
republished.

**Numbers I will use:** none — the test asserts the card and segment, not amounts (the charge figures are W-264b's).

(`ChargesIntegrationTest`, `ShadowRecordingIntegrationTest`, `ProfitAndLossIntegrationTest`); `./mvnw validate`.
