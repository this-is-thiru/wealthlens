## Plan for W-235 — erasure on request

**For:** a user who asks WealthLens to erase their tax data gets it erased, is told what (if anything) is kept, why,
and until when (`DR 14` O5; D-447 C6a; DPDP Act, 2023 s.12(3)).

### What I read (session 48)

- **`DR 14` O5** (`taxplanning-design/14-operational-design.md:126`), a `RECOMMENDATION`: on request, `DRAFT`/`READY`
  drafts and revisions erased; `ACTED_ON` drafts and computations **retained 8 years** "with a documented explanation";
  cross-year ledgers retained; the audit log retained, pseudonymised; landlord and lender data erased with the draft.
- **The law, from Gazette copies only** (`sources/2026-11-07-data-protection.md`): DPDP Act s.12(3) — erase on request
  *"unless retention … is necessary for the specified purpose or for compliance with any law"*; s.8(7) the same for
  withdrawal of consent, and the Fiduciary makes its processors erase. **Rules r.8(3)** — keep the personal data and the
  logs of any processing **at least one year from the processing**, *"even if X deletes her account"* (Illustration,
  Case 1), then erase. R.9: every response to a rights request names a contact. R.14(3): grievances answered within
  90 days. **These duties commence 18 months after 13-11-2025** (G.S.R. 843(E)(c); Rules r.1(4)) — about May 2027.
- **What I could not settle:** I found no law that binds *WealthLens* to keep a filed computation (the Income-tax
  record duties fall on the assessee). So `DR 14` O5's eight-year hold has no cited legal basis under s.12(3); it rests
  on "the specified purpose" — which a user asking to erase is ending. That is C1, the owner's.
- **The code:** an account (email) owns several `TaxpayerProfile`s (D-046 B2). Under a profile: `tax_family_members`,
  `tax_employers`, `tax_landlords`, `tax_lenders`, `tax_bank_accounts`, `tax_year_workspaces`, `tax_filing_drafts`,
  `tax_draft_revisions`, `tax_draft_computations`, `tax_regime_election_history`; `tax_access_log` keys by principal
  (email) and subject id (`protection/AccessRecord.java:23`). `jobs/DraftRemoval` deletes one draft with its revisions
  and computations. `RetentionPurge` runs monthly (`retention-purge-cron`, `application.yaml:27`). No erasure entry
  point exists; the legacy `DELETE /user/{email}/profile/{profileId}` (`TaxPlanningController.java:73`) is W-159's.
  No uploaded documents exist yet (W-131).

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — what an erasure keeps.** (a) **Everything under the profile is erased**, `ACTED_ON` drafts and the election
history included, after the one-year hold of C2. Before erasing, the response points the user to their own copy
(the draft's PDF/export, which carries no third-party data — W-135). `DR 14` O5 is amended to match. (b) `DR 14` O5 as
written: `ACTED_ON` drafts, computations and the election history kept 8 years with the reason shown. Needs the
owner's legal reading that this is "necessary for the specified purpose"; I cannot cite one.

**C2 — the r.8(3) hold.** (a) A request **stops all processing at once** and erases **one year after the request**
(the request is the last processing of the data). The profile gets `erasure_requested_at` (a fact, UTC); from then it
reads as unknown ("This account holds no such profile", as today), is never computed or swept, and its `pan_digest`
is dropped so the same PAN can be registered again. `RetentionPurge` erases it once `erasure_requested_at + hold` has
passed. The hold is config, `app.tax.retention.erasure-hold: P1Y` (with `archive-after` and `purge-archived-after`).
(b) Erase at once and ignore r.8(3) until it commences — erasure is irreversible, and the deploy date is not known.

**C3 — scope and entry point.** (a) One profile per request: `POST /tax/user/{email}/profiles/{profileId}/erasure`,
answering `202` with what is erased, when, and the contact (r.9). A family member's profile is erasable alone. An
account-wide erasure (portfolio, insurance, the login) is outside `taxplanning`: **W-237** (below). (b) Account-wide
from `taxplanning` alone: half an erasure, presented as whole.

**C4 — the access log.** (a) The profile's records (`subject_id` = the profile or one of its drafts/revisions) are
erased with it at the end of the hold; the hold is the r.6(1)(e) one-year log period. (b) Pseudonymise and keep 8
years (`DR 14` O4) — whether an HMAC of the email is still personal data (s.2(t)) is not settled.

**C5 — the r.9 contact.** (a) `app.tax.privacy-contact`, required at start, its value given by the owner (**…**);
returned in the erasure response. (b) Leave it to W-237.

### Build (C1a – C5a)
1. `TaxpayerProfile.erasureRequestedAt`; `TaxProfileService` refuses a held profile everywhere it resolves one.
2. `taxplanning/protection/ErasureRequest` (service): sets the fact, unsets `pan_digest`, in one transaction; answers
   `ErasureReceipt` (profile id, `eraseOn`, what is erased, contact).
3. `ProfileErasure` (service): deletes, in one transaction, every collection above for the profile (drafts through
   `DraftRemoval`), its access records, then the profile.
4. `RetentionPurge` gains a pass: profiles with `erasureRequestedAt + hold ≤ now` → `ProfileErasure`.
5. Controller endpoint (C3a); `StalenessSweep` and `AutoArchive` skip held profiles.
6. `DR 14` O5 amended (C1a, C4a); runbook §1.z (C5 key, and what to check after the first purge); WL-A-2;
   api-collection request; W-237 placed.

### Tests, by hand
- Request 2026-11-07T10:00Z, hold P1Y → `eraseOn` 2027-11-07T10:00Z. Purge at 2027-11-07T09:59Z → kept; at 10:00Z →
  every row with that `profile_id` gone, in all ten collections, and its access records; another profile on the same
  account untouched.
- After the request: `GET` profile → 400 "This account holds no such profile"; compute → the same; registering the
  same PAN on a new profile → 201 (digest freed). A second request → 400 (already held, not reset).
- Purge run twice → the second changes nothing.
- **Probes:** purge ignores the hold → the 09:59Z case fails; `pan_digest` kept → the re-register case fails (409/400).

### Build record

**Departures from the plan, each found while building:**
1. **Packages.** `taxplanning/erasure/` holds `ErasureService`, `ErasureReceipt`, `PrivacyContact` and the controller;
   the removal is `jobs/ProfileRemoval`, beside `DraftRemoval` which it reuses. In `protection/` the purge (`jobs`) and
   the removal would have depended on each other.
2. **`pan_digest`'s unique index is sparse.** Two held profiles both lack a digest, which a non-sparse unique index
   refuses. A start cannot change an existing index's options; runbook §0.5's drop (before the first deploy) recreates it.
3. **The receipt read was a back door.** `GET /tax/user/{email}/computations/{receipt}` checked the email only; it now
   goes through the draft's profile, so a held profile's receipts are refused too.
4. **The receipt cannot point to the user's copy:** from the request the profile reads as absent. It says what is
   erased, that nothing is kept after `eraseOn`, why it is held, and the contact. The client offering the PDF *before*
   the request is in W-237.
5. A re-registration answers **200**, not 201 (profile create has no `@ResponseStatus`).
6. `ProfileRemoval` removes the seven profile-scoped collections through `MongoTemplate` by `profile_id`, one list like
   `TaxProfileIndexInitializer`'s, rather than seven repositories.
7. The purge logs `Job retention-purge erased profile <id>` per profile.
