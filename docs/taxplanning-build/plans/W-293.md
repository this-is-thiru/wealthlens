## Plan for W-293 — every sale records its share of a lot's buy charges in the replay

**What this item is for:** the `assets` replay must record a sale's share of a lot's buy charges for a V2 sale as well
as a V1 one, because the live V2 path does. Otherwise a rebuilt V2-sold lot shows false drift, and §2.12's write
would zero its allocated charges (D-588).

**Finding (proved red, 2026-11-12):**
- `TradeOutcomeRecorder.rowFigures` (`:166 – 167`) calls `LotChargeAllocator.deductBroker` / `deductMisc` on each
  lot a V2 sale draws, and `sellStockV2` saves those lots (`PortfolioService:487`).
- Example: a V2 buy of 10 with ₹30 entered, then 4 sold. The stored lot has ₹12 allocated (₹30 × 4 / 10), and the
  replay reported it as differing.
- D-582's finding 1 said the opposite and was wrong. W-283a2's integration test missed it because its trades carried
  no charges.

**Approved (owner: "Fix now, simplify"):**
- `LotLedger.sell` allocates for every sale.
- The sale-flow inference loses its purpose and is removed: `saleFlowFromOutcomes`, `saleFlowUnknown`,
  `TradeOutcomeRepository.findByEmailAndSellChargeBreakupExists`, and `AssetsRebuild`'s outcome-row reads.

**Files:**
- Code: `LotLedger`, `AssetsRebuild`, `AssetsRebuildReport`, `TradeOutcomeRepository`.
- Tests: `LotLedgerTest` (the V2 case now expects ₹10 allocated), `AssetsRebuildTest`, `AssetsRebuildIntegrationTest`
  (the red case).
- Docs: `assets-rebuild.md`, runbook §2.12, the api-collection request; errata on D-582.

**Numbers:** ₹30 × 4 / 10 = ₹12 and ₹30 × 1 / 3 = ₹10, by hand.
