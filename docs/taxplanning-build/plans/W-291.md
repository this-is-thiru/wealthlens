## Plan for W-291 — a bonus lot and its transaction carry only what the bonus is

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** bonus shares cost nothing and were never submitted, so
the bonus lot and the bonus transaction must not carry the copied buy's charges, sales or submission identifiers
(D-584).

**Findings (read this session, 2026-11-12):**

1. **The bonus lot is a JSON copy of the first lot held.** `CorporateActionService:297 – 304` resets id, quantity,
   price, date, order times, type and buy ids. It keeps `broker_charges`, `misc_charges`, `allocated_buy_charges`,
   `allocated_buy_misc_charges`, `sell_transaction_ids` and the first lot's `corporate_actions`.
2. **Both flows' sales then charge the copied buy charges again.** A sale of the bonus lot takes its share of
   `broker_charges` through `LotChargeAllocator`: V1 in `allocateSale`, V2 in `TradeOutcomeRecorder.rowFigures`
   (`:166 – 167`, D-588). That share goes into the `trade_outcomes` row's buy charges and the P&L. It overstates cost
   and understates the gain, the direction that under-reports tax.
3. **The bonus transaction is a JSON copy of the first buy** (`:327 – 335`). It keeps `broker_charges`,
   `misc_charges`, `order_id`, `order_execution_time`, and also `idempotency_key`, `trade_fingerprint`, `submitted_at`
   and `source_temp_transaction_id`. The last are under **unique** sparse indexes (`TransactionEntity:126`, `:152`,
   created at startup). So if the first buy carried a client key, or came from a redrive, saving the bonus should
   fail with a duplicate key and the bonus cannot be performed. **Not yet run** — the build proves it red.
4. **The replay copies the same** (`LotLedger.allot`, W-283a2), so rebuilt equals live today. It changes with live.

**Choices for the owner:**

- **C1 — what a bonus carries.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** the lot gets nil charges and nil allocated charges, no sales, and in its action list only
    the bonus. The transaction gets nil charges, and no order id, order time, idempotency key, fingerprint, submission
    time or redrive link. `LotLedger.allot` does the same.
  - C1b: charges only (lot and transaction); the identifiers stay.
- **C2 — data already stored.**
  - **C2a (recommended):**
    - **§3.17 (read-only):** a count of bonus lots carrying charges or sales, and bonus transactions carrying charges
      or copied identifiers.
    - **§2.13, one-time, owner, `mongosh`:** zero the bonus transactions' charges and unset their copied identifiers.
      Backup first; safe to run twice.
    - Then, per affected user: §2.12 (lots), §2.2 (that user's `trade_outcomes`, if bonus-lot sales are among them),
      §2.11 (P&L).
    - A removal row and a tracker issue for §2.13.
  - C2b: the count only; the correction becomes its own row.

**Files I will modify:**
- `CorporateActionService` — `processBonusShares`, `processBonusSharesTxns`.
- `LotLedger.allot`.
- Runbook §3.17, §2.13 (C2a); a backlog row and YouTrack issue for §2.13's removal.
- `assets-rebuild.md` (bonus no longer copied as-is).
- Tests:
  - `CorporateActionIntegrationTest` (red before):
    - a holding lot carrying ₹20 and a sale gives a bonus lot with nil charges, no sales and one action, and a bonus
      transaction with nil charges;
    - a first buy with an idempotency key still lets the bonus be performed.
  - `LotLedgerTest` — the allot case now expects nil.
  - `AssetsRebuildIntegrationTest` — a bonus performed live is rebuilt with no difference.

**Numbers I will use:** none from law; ₹20 → ₹0 by the rule.

**Design sections I read:** D-584; D-307 (bonus dated by allotment); D-588.

**What I am NOT doing:** changing how many bonus shares are allotted; W-292.

**How I will verify:**
- The red tests first.
- Probe: keep the copied charges, and the charge case must fail.
- The unit and integration tiers, run from cleared report directories.

**Post-deployment actions (R15):** §3.17 (read-only), then §2.13 (once, with backup, safe to run twice, removal
ticket), then the per-user rebuilds §2.12 → §2.2 → §2.11, all as C2a lists.

**Five roles:**
- **Architect:** the bonus lot and transaction record the facts of an allotment (quantity, date, nil cost), not a
  copied buy.
- **Product manager:** gains on bonus shares stop being understated, and a bonus can be performed for users whose
  buys carried keys.
- **Senior engineer:** explicit resets on the two copies, mirrored in the replay.
- **Automation tester:** red cases for charges and keys, a probe, and the rebuild equality.
- **Manual tester:** on staging, perform a bonus for a holding bought with charges. The bonus lot shows ₹0, and a
  sale of it shows ₹0 buy charges in its outcome row.

**Risks or ambiguities:**
- §2.13 rewrites stored transactions. It runs only after a backup, and only on documents marked
  `corporate_action_type: "BONUS"`.
