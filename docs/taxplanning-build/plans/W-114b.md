## Plan for W-114b — the calendar's last three F30 rows: proof cut-off, revised return, regime election

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** settle the last three rows of `DR 20` F30's table. The
regime election gets an entry priced by the tax it saves; the other two have no measurable cost, so they are left
out with the reason recorded.

**Design sections I read:** W-114b's row (`03-WORK-ITEMS.md`, D-665); `20-product-gaps.md` F30 and its W-114 and
W-114a errata; `01-rulebook.md:66` (`REG-OPTOUT-FORM-001`), `:1475` (`TDS-SAL-12BB-001`), `:1671` (H-16);
`plans/W-114.md` and `plans/W-114a.md`.

**What the repo has (read this session):**

| Row | Data | Measurable cost of missing it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Regime election** | **In the pack:** `regimes/FIRST_SCHEDULE_RATES.json` `elections`: TY 2025-26 `OPT_OUT`, `IN_THE_RETURN`, deadline `DueDateUnder("139(1)")`; TY 2026-27 the same with `"263(1)"`, each sourced from a Gazette copy (FA 2023 s.52, r.21AGA; Rules 2026 r.136). `DeadlineSpec` (`rules/regime/DeadlineSpec.java`) has `DueDateUnder`, `WithTheReturn` and `NoDeadline`. **Nothing resolves a deadline to a date yet** (`grep -rn 'deadline()'` → none) | **Yes:** the default regime's bill less the opted-out regime's, from `compareRegimes` (`RegimeComparison.Priced(regime, bill)`) |
| **Employer proof cut-off** | The law sets **no date**. `TDS-SAL-12BB-001`: the employer *"shall obtain"* evidence in Form No. 124 (s.392(5)(b); Rules 2026 r.205(1)). The cut-off is each employer's payroll practice. No `payrollCalendar` exists | **No.** Missing it means more is deducted at source, and that comes back as a refund after filing (D-664's forecast shows it). No tax is lost |
| **Revised-return window** | No row. s.263(5) has not been read this session | **No.** A revision matters only where the furnished return is wrong, which a draft cannot know |

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: which rows get an entry.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** the regime election only. The proof cut-off and the revised-return window are left out,
    with errata on their two `DR 20` F30 lines and W-114b's row, as W-114a did for the LTA block (D-666 C4a). With
    that, F30's table is settled.
  - C1b: also the revised-return window, as a new rule row read from the 2025 Act's s.263(5) (Gazette 265620) and
    1961 s.139(5), with the amending Acts searched both ways, and a nil cost. That breaks F30.2.
  - C1c: also the proof cut-off, as a date the person enters on an employment. It is the employer's date, not law,
    and it still has no cost.
- **C2: the election's date.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** the engine resolves it. A new `TaxCalculator.regimeElection(EngineInputs)` returns, for
    each opt-out regime with an election in the year, `RegimeElectionDue(regime, deadline (Optional), the default's
    bill, its bill)`. `DueDateUnder` resolves to `FilingFacts.dueDate`, the one due date a draft holds and the one
    s.263(1) / s.139(1) name. `WithTheReturn` and `NoDeadline` have no date, so they get no entry. A future row
    naming another provision fails loudly rather than being read as the return's due date.
  - C2b: `taxplanning` uses the draft's due date directly. That ignores the pack's protocol, which is law in code.
- **C3: when the entry shows.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** only where the opted-out regime's bill is below the default's, and the draft has not
    already elected it when acted on. Cost of missing = the difference, in **tax** (as W-114a's deduction entry).
    The entry is `REGIME_ELECTION` on the due date, `rule` = the regime code, `amountToPay` empty.
  - C3b: always, with a nil cost where opting out saves nothing.
- **C4: business income.** The pack's protocol switches the vehicle to Form No. 10-IEA by the same due date, and the
  choice binds later years.
  - **C4a (recommended):** the same entry, with the vehicle (`IN_THE_RETURN` / `PRESCRIBED_FORM` and the form
    number) carried as a fact. Binding later years is not priced, and `notIncluded` says so.
  - C4b: no entry for anyone with business income.
