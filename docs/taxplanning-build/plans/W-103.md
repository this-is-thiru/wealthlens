### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — where filing facts live (row 4).**
- **(a) A draft's document becomes `{"entered": …, "filing": …}`,** bound to a small `DraftInputs` record holding `EnteredFacts` and `FilingFacts`. Patches and replay then cover credits too. **Patch paths gain `/entered`,** and D-441's tests move with them; nothing is stored anywhere real yet. The client states the due date, because no rule data holds it until W-112; compute refuses without it, naming it.
- (b) A separate `PUT /drafts/{id}/filing`, outside revisions: a computation could not be replayed from its revision.

**C2 — whose capital gains (row 5).**
- **(a) `TaxpayerProfile.holdsPortfolio`, at most one per account.** Compute reads portfolio gains only for that profile, and the response says whether they were included. A second holder is refused.
- (b) Gains on every profile: counts them twice. (c) Individual profiles only: two individuals in an account still double-count.

**C3 — scope.**
- **(a) Build W-103, W-104, W-107, W-109; park W-105 and W-106** behind the deploy gate, since a readable 400 already exists. **W-109a** (row 9's per-bucket outcome, D-410) comes **next**, not parked, as it is your standing requirement. The response carries a fixed `notIncluded` list naming row 6's ledgers and row 10's pin, so no user mistakes the figure for complete. Ledgers get **W-234**, gated before the deploy.
- (b) All of T9 and W-109a now: a much larger family before the first figure.

**C4 — exposing the engine (rows 1 – 3).**
- **(a) A small `taxengine.api` named interface** with `TaxCalculator` (`compute`, `compareRegimes`), hiding pipeline and registry; `ShippedNodes` moves the list into main, and `GoldenCaseRunner` reads it, so tests run what ships. `@NamedInterface("input")` on `ElectionState` and `DisabilitySeverity` only, and D-440's `DisabilityLevel` goes in favour of the engine's type.
- (b) Expose `pipeline` and `rules.regime` whole: `taxplanning` could reach into the runner.

### Build (C1a, C2a, C3a, C4a)

1. `taxengine.api`, `ShippedNodes`; the two annotations.
2. `DraftInputs`; document shape; `holdsPortfolio`.
3. **W-103 `EngineInputsAssembler`:** the only place `EngineInputs` is built: draft, profile → `Individual` or `Huf`, family → `FamilySnapshot`, election history → snapshot, adapter or none → gains, `LedgerSnapshot.empty()`, `asOf` from a clock here, never in the engine.
4. **W-104 `DraftComputationService`** (the legacy name is taken): runs, projects, stores in **`tax_draft_computations`** (row 7), with the revision, `InputsFingerprint` and pack versions.
5. **W-109:** `POST /drafts/{id}/compute` with a regime; `POST /drafts/{id}/compare-regimes`. Returns bill, credits, final payable or refund, trace, receipt, `notIncluded`, whether gains were included.
6. **W-107:** `GET /computations/{receipt}` resolves a receipt to its stored run.
