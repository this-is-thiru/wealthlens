### The rule, stated once

- **A gift** is charged under the one limb whose row reaches it (`GiftIncome.limbOf`, unchanged). An excluded
  receipt must be reached by a limb too, since an exclusion is of a receipt the limbs charge. A sum received for a
  consideration then reaches none and is refused in the rows' words.
- **Pre-construction interest** on a loan is deducted on the row of the pair that reaches the house **and the loan**
  (`house.occupancy`, `loan.use`). Interest entered on a loan no row reaches would be ignored, so it is refused.
  "Property 1, loan 1: no row of RS-HP-PRE-CONSTRUCTION or RS-HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-PRE-CONSTRUCTION takes
  pre-construction interest on capital borrowed for REPAIR_OR_RECONSTRUCTION in TY …; it would be ignored, so it is
  refused".
