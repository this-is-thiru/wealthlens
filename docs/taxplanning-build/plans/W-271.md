## Plan for W-271 — V1 sells lots bought after the sale date

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a V1 sale takes only lots held on its date, as V2's already
does, so a sale dated before a purchase cannot consume it (G-3).

**Findings (each read this session, 2026-11-11):**

1. **V1's sale reads every lot.** `PortfolioService.sellStock` (`:476`) uses
   `findByEmailAndStockCodeAndBrokerNameAndAccountHolderOrderByTransactionDate` (`PortfolioRepository.java:20`), its
   only caller. V2's `sellStockV2` uses `findEligibleHoldingsForSell` (`:23-27`: `transaction_date $lte` the sale,
   sorted ascending — FIFO among lots held on the sale date). Proven: W-264a scenario 8 — a V1 sale dated 5 Jan took
   a lot bought 5 Feb (200).
2. **Every V1 sale path is affected:** the route, the upload, and redrive of a V1 or pre-W-270 temporary (all reach
   `sellStock`).
3. **Past V1 sales that took a later lot are visible in stored data:** V1's sale writes a `trade_outcomes` row per lot
   with `buy_date` and `sell_date` (`TradeOutcomeEntity` `@Field("buy_date")`, `@Field("sell_date")`); a row with
   `buy_date` after `sell_date` is such a sale. How many exist is not known.

**Choices for the owner:**

- **C1 — the fix.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** `sellStock` uses `findEligibleHoldingsForSell`, as V2 does; the unfiltered finder, left
    with no caller, is removed. A V1 sale dated before every lot answers 400 "Stock not found with code: …", as V2's
    does (`validateTransaction`).
- **C2 — sales already recorded that took a later lot.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** a read-only runbook check (§3, 3.12) counts `trade_outcomes` rows whose `buy_date` is after
    their `sell_date`, per database; a non-zero count is reported, and a correction would be its own row.
  - C2b: no check.

**Files I will modify:** `PortfolioService.sellStock`, `PortfolioRepository` (finder removed); tests — W-264a scenario
8 as the first test, a V1 sale across lots held on its date leaves a later lot untouched, any existing test or mock
naming the removed finder; `post-deployment-runbook.md` 3.12 (C2a), so WL-A-2 is republished; CLAUDE.md's "Two live
buy/sell flows" paragraph (both sells now take lots dated on or before the sale).

**Numbers I will use:** none.

**What I am NOT doing:** the fractional comparison (W-274); V1's same-day merge (W-272, W-279); correcting past rows.

**How I will verify:** scenario 8 red then green; a V1 sale on 5 Mar of 15 over lots of 10 (5 Jan) and 10 (5 Feb)
with a third lot bought 5 Apr leaves the April lot at 10 and February's at 5; probe: the old finder back → scenario
8 fails; the affected classes; `./mvnw validate`.

**Post-deployment actions (R15):** runbook 3.12, read-only, owner, after the deploy; safe to repeat; nothing to undo:
`db.trade_outcomes.countDocuments({ $expr: { $gt: ["$buy_date", "$sell_date"] } })`.

**Five roles:**
- **Architect:** one rule for both flows — a sale takes what was held on its date.
- **Product manager:** a back-dated sale no longer takes shares bought later; past ones are counted, not silently
  rewritten.
- **Senior engineer:** reuse V2's query; delete the finder that only served the wrong behaviour.
- **Automation tester:** scenario 8 and the three-lot case; a probe.
- **Manual tester:** a V1 sale dated before your only purchase answers 400.

**Risks or ambiguities:** a client that relied on V1 accepting a back-dated sale against later lots now gets 400 —
that is the defect being fixed.
