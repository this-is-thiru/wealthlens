## Plan for W-066d — retirement limits set by other Acts

**What this item is for (one sentence):** deduct gratuity paid under the gratuity statute (Sl. 5) and a workman's
retrenchment compensation (Sl. 10) up to the limits the labour Acts set. The Payment of Gratuity Act and the
Industrial Disputes Act are read as the Codes that replaced them on 21-11-2025 (D-107).

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C5).**

### What I read

- **The `W-066d` row** (D-103, D-106, D-107): Sl. 5 (Payment of Gratuity Act s.4(2)–(3)) and Sl. 10 whole (least of
  received, ID Act s.25F(b), and the notified amount), with s.19(2)(c)–(d); 1961 s.10(10)(ii), (10B).
- **D-107 (OQ-9):** the references are read as the Code on Social Security s.53(2)–(3) and the Industrial Relations
  Code s.70(b). I recommended then that rows resting on that reading be marked `UNVERIFIED`, with the reasoning.
- **TY 2026-27, S2 s.19(1) Table:**
  - **Sl. 5** (p.41): *"Gratuity received under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 — Amount received, as restricted to
    the amount calculated as per the provisions of section 4(2) and (3) of the said Act"*.
  - **Sl. 10** (p.43): least of *"(a) compensation received; (b) amount calculated as per provisions of section
    25F(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947; (c) such amount, not being less than ₹50000, as may be notified"*.
  - **s.19(2)(c), (d)** (p.45): closure and certain transfers are deemed retrenchment, and "employer" and "workman"
    take the ID Act's meanings.
- **TY 2025-26, 1961 Act:**
  - **s.10(10)(ii)** (p.71): *"any gratuity received under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 …, to the extent it does
    not exceed an amount calculated in accordance with the provisions of sub-sections (2) and (3) of section 4 of
    that Act"*.
  - **s.10(10B)** (p.73): the same three limbs, the limit being *"such amount, not being less than fifty thousand
    rupees, as the Central Government may, by notification … specify"*.
- **The labour texts** (`sources/2026-09-27-retirement-limits.md`, all read):
  - **PGA s.4(2):** fifteen days' wages per completed year or part over six months, at the rate last drawn; monthly
    rate ÷ 26 × 15; seasonal seven days a season; piece-rated on a three-month average. **s.4(3):** the ceiling "as
    may be notified".
  - **CSS s.53(2):** the same, *"or such number of days as may be notified"*, plus fixed-term and deceased employees
    pro rata. **s.53(3):** the ceiling "as may be notified". Explanation 3 (p.45): the same ÷ 26 × 15.
  - **CSS s.2(88) "wages"** (pp.18–19): basic, DA and retaining allowance. But *"if payments made by the employer …
    under sub-clauses (a) to (i) exceeds one-half … the amount which exceeds such one-half … shall be deemed as
    remuneration and shall be accordingly added in wages"*. **So the Code's wage is not the PGA's:** allowances above
    half of remuneration are added back.
  - **IDA s.25F(b) and IRC s.70(b):** "fifteen days' average pay" per completed year or part over six months. IRC
    adds *"or average pay of such days as may be notified by the appropriate Government"*. "Average pay" is averaged
    over three calendar months, four weeks or twelve working days, depending on how the workman is paid.
  - **Repeal:** PGA repealed 21-11-2025 (S.O. 5319(E), read); IDA *"stood repealed"* from the same date (S.O. 465(E),
    read). CSS s.164(2)(a) deems notifications under a repealed Act made under the Code.
  - **Central rules of May 2026:** neither changes the fifteen days. **The States are not checked.**
- **The limits:**
  - **Gratuity ceiling ₹20 lakh:** S.O. 1420(E), 29-3-2018, under PGA s.4(3). **Not read**: the link 404s.
  - **Retrenchment ₹5,00,000:** read only in the DoR consolidation's footnote (Notification "10969"/1096, 25-6-1999).
- **Code:** `RetirementEntry`, `RetirementPayment`, `RetirementNode` and `NotifiedRetirementLimits`. The retirement
  refusal message currently names W-066d.

### Findings

1. **The statutory amount (limb "(b)") is a figure the employer must already compute**, to pay lawfully. Computing it
   here would need, by Act and by date:
   - wages as each Act defines them (PGA: basic + DA; the Code: basic + DA plus the add-back above one-half of
     remuneration);
   - the pay basis (monthly, daily, piece-rated, seasonal);
   - "average pay" over the right window;
   - and any notified days, which the States have not been checked for.
2. **The words are the same across the repeal, but the sums may not be.** After 21-11-2025 the Code's wage can be
   larger. A separation in TY 2025-26 falls on one side or the other.
3. **The gratuity ceiling isn't read**, but Sl. 5's limb is "the amount calculated as per s.4(2) **and (3)**". So the
   ceiling is part of that amount, and the employer's figure already includes it.
4. **Retrenchment's own limit (c) is the tax law's**, not labour law's, so it belongs in the pack: ₹5,00,000,
   `UNVERIFIED` on the DoR consolidation.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the statutory amount:**
  - **(a) recommended: the employer's figure.**
    - `STATUTORY_GRATUITY` carries *"the gratuity payable as calculated under PGA s.4(2)–(3), or for a separation on
      or after 21-11-2025 under CSS s.53(2)–(3)"*.
    - `RETRENCHMENT` carries *"the compensation calculated under IDA s.25F(b), or on or after 21-11-2025 IRC s.70(b)"*.
    - Each also carries the separation day, which says which Act the figure answers to, and appears in the trace.
    - This is consistent with leave encashment (D-106). The engine neither invents a conversion nor picks a wage
      definition.
  - **(b)** Compute it for monthly-rated employees (÷ 26 × 15 × years, part year over six months counted) from an
    entered last-drawn wage, and refuse other pay bases. Exact for the PGA wage; for a Code-era separation the
    taxpayer must still apply s.2(88)'s add-back.
- **C2 — rows**, all `AllRegimes` (s.202(2)(a) and s.115BAC(2)(i) exclude neither), both years:
  - **`RET-GRATUITY-STATUTORY`:** `LeastOf` of "gratuity received" and "amount under the gratuity statute" (the
    employer's figure). No ceiling is seeded: finding 3.
  - **`RET-RETRENCHMENT`:** `LeastOf` of "compensation received", "amount under the retrenchment statute" (the
    employer's figure) and "notified limit" (`Fixed` 500000).
  - Named for the concept, not a repealed Act, since each spans both Acts.
- **C3 — verification:**
  - **TY 2026-27:** `UNVERIFIED`. `whatToCheck` records the D-107 reading and asks for a CBDT word or an amendment
    naming the Codes. For retrenchment it also asks for the 1999 notification's Gazette copy.
  - **TY 2025-26:** `UNVERIFIED` for those reasons plus the three unread Acts.
  - Every figure using these rows shows as PROVISIONAL. That is honest while the law names repealed Acts.
- **C4 — inputs:** new entries `STATUTORY_GRATUITY` (Sl. 5; 1961 s.10(10)(ii)) and `RETRENCHMENT` (Sl. 10; 1961
  s.10(10B)). A payment under either carries **`StatutoryEntitlement(separatedOn, amountUnderStatute)`**. The
  retirement refusal message stops naming W-066d, since every Table entry will then be built.
- **C5 — out of scope:** checking a figure against the pay basis or wage definition (C1a); States' notified days; a
  workman's status or the deemed-retrenchment conditions of s.19(2)(c), which the entry asserts.
