- **s.93(1)(d)** (p.141): *"for income in the nature of family pension (a regular monthly amount payable by the
  employer to a family member of an employee upon the death of such employee),–– (i) an amount equal to one-third of

- **s.93(1)(g)** (p.142): *"for income in the nature of commutation of pension received from a fund as specified in

- **s.19(2)(g)** (p.46): the death-cum-retirement gratuity of Table Sl. 3 is one received *"under the revised pension

  Union civil and defence-civil posts, the all-India services, State civil services and local-authority employees.

  after the 1st August, 1996 or any other insurer under a pension scheme"*.

**Design and code:**
- `DR 01` R17 has `OS-FAMPEN-001` / `-002`, `OS-GRATUITY-DEATH-001` and `OS-COMMUT-001`.
- `OtherSourceKind` has no kind for any of the three, so today they can only be entered as `OTHER`, which is refused
  naming W-079g.
- A rule version is a cap **or** a definition, never both. *"One-third"* cannot be held exactly as a percentage;
  `CommutedPensionShare` carries such a share as a divisor and divides once with `Money.apportioned` (D-103).
- Rows keyed by regime are an `ExceptIn` / `OnlyIn NEW_TAX_REGIME` pair, as `DED-NPS-EMPLOYER-OTHER` /
  `-NEW-TAX-REGIME` are.

### Findings

1. **The rulebook's 1961 column is incomplete.** `OS-FAMPEN-001` marks ₹25,000 / ₹15,000 *NEEDS VERIFICATION*, and
   F(No.2)A 2024 s.24(b) now verifies it. `OS-GRATUITY-DEATH-001` and `OS-COMMUT-001` show "—" for 1961, yet
   s.10(10)(i) and s.10(10A)(iii) take the same amounts out of income. That is an exemption rather than a deduction,
   and it has the same effect.
2. **"Thirty-three and one-third per cent" (1961) and "one-third" (2025)** are the same fraction. I hold both as a
   divisor of 3, so neither year rounds a percentage.
3. **Only a Government death-cum-retirement gratuity is covered.** s.93(1)(h) reaches only the s.19(2)(g) gratuity, so
   a private employer's gratuity paid to the family has no clause here. It is residual s.92(1) income, which is
   W-079g's.
4. **The excluded family pensions** (Schedule III Sl. 15, 16; 1961 s.10(18)(ii), (19)) are not income, so they are not
   entered. The kind's Javadoc will say so.
5. **TY 2025-26's `introducedBy` for s.57(iia)** needs FA 1989 s.13 and FA 1997 s.20 (D-188). This session I scanned
   the Goa Series I reprints for 1988–92 and 2002–04, and found neither Act; only FA 1988, 1990 and 1991 are there. My
   memory says FA 1997 is not in the Goa archive at all.
