## Plan for W-327a — a plain "why": the mechanism, and the regime's reasons

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** the engine publishes why a line was not applied in a regime
as a fact (a reason code and the regime), and the content turns that fact into words. "disallowed in the
NEW_TAX_REGIME regime: Income-tax Act, 2025 s.202(2)(a) …" then reads "It is not allowed under the default regime."

**Probe (run before this plan, then removed; `git diff` shows `TraceLine`, `PipelineRunner` and `TraceLineTest`
unchanged):**
- **Sites:** the W-327 log (388,387 `limitedBy` writes) re-read for this item.
  - **The regime's reason:** 1,274 writes in the run, all "disallowed in the NEW_TAX_REGIME regime". A `grep` finds **15 sites** that write `whyNot()` alone; the run reached 13 of them (not `LoanInterestDeduction:111` or `PensionDeduction:154`). Three more mix it with node text, so they stay with their families: `HousePropertyInterest:178` and `CarriedLossSetOff:189` prefix it, and `RentPaidDeduction:85` returns it among the deduction's other bars (written at `:66`).
- **Moving tests:** I gave `TraceLine` a ninth component and set it on every regime and cap line, then ran the taxengine unit tier (4,175 tests). **Exactly one test moved:** `TraceLineTest.aLineCarriesNoCommentaryOfItsOwn:165`, which pins the record's eight components. No equality assertion in any node test moved.

**Findings that change the W-327 plan:**
- **Regime names carry citations.** The regime and disallowance-set `displayName`s are legal titles ("New tax regime for individuals … (s.202(1))", "Income-tax Act, 2025 s.202(2)(a) computes …"). C3a keeps citations out of the sentence, so C2a's "reuse the `displayName`" cannot apply to regimes, and their words go in the content `terms` map. The content already says "the default regime" and "the old regime" (`content/explanations.json`), so those are the words.
- **Cap results are a review of their own.** The 9 cap shapes name their reason through 65 distinct base, component and period codes (`SALARY`, `PAYMENT_RECEIVED`, `BENEFIT_VALUE`, …), and each would need a drafted word. 13 sites pass a cap result through unchanged.
- **"Not met" and "excluded by" belong with their sites.** "Not met" is 2 sites, but it renders 36 condition types' `describe()` (`RebateNode:94`, `DisabilityDeduction:97`). "Excluded by" is 3 perquisite sites, and each names the excluding row itself, whose `what` (W-325a) already says it is not taxed.

**Choices for the owner:**

  - **C1a (recommended):** W-327a is the mechanism plus the regime's two reasons (the 15 sites). Place **W-327a1**, the cap results and their 65 words, next on the board. "Not met" moves to its sites' families (`RebateNode` → W-327e, `DisabilityDeduction` → W-327c), and "excluded by" moves to W-327b.
  - C1b: keep the cap results, "not met" and "excluded by" in this item, as D-677 placed them. That is one review of the mechanism, 65 drafted words and 36 condition types.
- **C2: where the reason sits.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** a ninth `TraceLine` component, `Optional<Reason> reason`, and amend the A8 pin with its reason. The reason is a computed fact, like `limitedBy`, and not commentary: no content is read to build it, so I27 still holds (a content edit changes the explanation and never the trace).
  - C2b: carry it in `TraceAnnotation`. That layer holds what is said *about* a line and is derived from content, which a node-computed fact is not.

**The design (C1a, C2a):**
- **`rules/id/ReasonCode`**, with the codes' existing grammar `^[A-Z][A-Z0-9_]{1,63}$` (`ComponentCode:16`).
- **`rules/reason/Reason(ReasonCode code, Map<String, ReasonFact> facts)`.** `ReasonFact` is sealed, with one kind in this item, `RegimeFact(RegimeCode)`; later items add the kinds they need. `rules/reason/ReasonCodes` lists the codes the engine emits. It lives in `rules` because W-327a1's `CapResult` (`rules/cap`) must carry one, and rules may not depend on `trace` (`ArchitectureTest:330`).
- **`RegimeVerdict.reason()`:** `Disallowed` gives `REGIME_DISALLOWS {regime}`, `ExcludedByRow` gives `REGIME_EXCLUDES_ROW {regime}`, and `Applies` refuses, as `whyNot()` does. Each of the 15 sites adds `.withReason(verdict.reason())`; its `limitedBy` is unchanged.
- **`TraceLine.withReason`;** `PipelineRunner.renumbered` carries the reason.
- **`content/explanations.json`** gains `reasons` (`{code, why}`) and `terms.regime` (`{CODE: word}`). `ExplanationJson` reads both. `Explanations` holds them, with a `why` checked by `requireProse` (no figure). The compiler refuses a code `ReasonCodes` does not list, a listed code with no `why`, and a regime of the timeline with no term. It copies `reasons`, and the terms of the year's regimes, into each year.
- **The renderer** names the limit when `byLimit` does (as today); else fills the reason's `why`, using the term for the regime; else prints `limitedBy` as written. A `why` naming a fact the reason lacks falls back to `limitedBy`, as a `what` falls back to the rule id (W-116).
- **Both locks:** relocked, one bump.

**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption):**
- `REGIME_DISALLOWS`: "It is not allowed under {regime}."
- `REGIME_EXCLUDES_ROW`: "This rule does not apply under {regime}."
- Terms: `NEW_TAX_REGIME` "the default regime"; `FIRST_SCHEDULE_RATES` "the old regime".
- Example: "₹0 of the interest on your loan for higher education is deducted. It is not allowed under the default regime."
