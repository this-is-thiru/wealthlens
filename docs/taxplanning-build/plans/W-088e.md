  item yet — a gap for the owner to itemise"*. `NOT_COMPUTATIONAL` is used for definitional sections too (S2 / 1961
  ss.2, 3). `OUT_OF_SCOPE` needs a reason and a decision (precedents: 1961 s.71A, ss.80CCA / 80CCB, *"spent"*).
- **The 57 sections, each read from its official copy** (S2 Gazette 265620 pp.36, 104–137; 1961 thc pp.~240–300):
  - **S2 s.13 / 1961 s.14:** the five heads of income.
  - **s.67 / s.45:** the charge — gains are income of the year of transfer.
  - **ss.68–81 / ss.46–51:** how the gain is computed:
    - liquidation (s.68 / s.46); buy-back (s.69 / s.46A);
    - transactions that are not transfers (s.70 / s.47), and withdrawing that exemption (s.71 / s.47A);
    - the mode of computation (s.72 / s.48); cost in certain modes (s.73 / s.49);
    - depreciable assets (s.74 / s.50; s.75 / s.50A); the s.50AA deemed short-term assets (s.76);
    - slump sale (s.77 / s.50B); stamp-duty value (s.78 / s.50C); unquoted shares (s.79 / s.50CA);
    - fair market value where consideration is unascertainable (s.80 / s.50D); advance money (s.81 / s.51).
  - **ss.82–89 / ss.54–54H:** the reinvestment exemptions.
    - S2 s.86 / 1961 s.54F reach *"any long-term capital asset, not being a residential house"*. That includes the
      equity gains `portfolio` sends, reinvested in a house.
    - 1961 ss.54E (*"before the 1st day of April, 1992"*), 54EA and 54EB (*"before the 1st day of April, 2000"*)
      and 54ED (*"before the 1st day of April, 2006"*) apply only to old transfers. 54EE applies to units *"issued
      before the 1st day of April, 2019"*, with a three-year holding condition. All are spent by TY 2025-26, and none
      has a 2025-Act counterpart.
    - 1961 s.54GB: transfers up to 31-3-2022 for an eligible start-up (FA 2021 s.22 changed the proviso's 2021 to
      2022). Its five-year holding condition can still bite in TY 2025-26.
  - **s.90 / s.55:** cost of improvement / acquisition.
  - **s.91 / s.55A:** reference to a Valuation Officer (procedure).
- **The code:**
  - `CapitalGainsNode` builds the head from `portfolio`'s buckets (W-088a).
  - The `RATE-CG-*` rows tax it (W-088b); `SETOFF-*` set it off (W-088a, W-088d).
  - The adapter maps equity only (D-241).
  - No node computes a gain's amount: R-10 makes that `portfolio`'s.

    brief), so (b) would mislabel them.
