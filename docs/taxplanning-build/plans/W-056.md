## Plan for W-056 — the surcharge node, with its marginal relief

**What this item is for (one sentence):** `tax.surcharge` charges the Finance Act's surcharge on the tax
after rebate, at the greater of the two ladders' rates, less the `W = U + V` relief. Cess and net
payable then include it, and every Phase 6 row is evaluated by a node (`NOT_YET_EVALUATED` empty).

### Findings

1. **U needs re-entry the engine doesn't have.** G3.2 and `UnrelievedTax` both put it on the caller,
   but a node's context can read only declared values and can't run other nodes. Pricing U inside the
   node would mean choosing the slab ladder again (`TaxByBasisNode`'s age-ladder selection, duplicated)
   and assuming the rebate at C is nil. That is true today but it's an inference (`REBATE-ORDER-001`),
   and it breaks as soon as special-rate income exists at C.
2. **The two buckets have nothing to split yet.** Both halves of `RATE-SUR-SPLIT-001` need income the
   graph doesn't carry:
   - the **excluding** income: dividends, which `W-079` brings, and special gains, which `W-088`
     brings;
   - the **special-rate tax**, which is always empty today.

   Dividends are taxed at slab rates, yet the Act still puts them in the 15%-limited part. So the split
   isn't simply "slab versus special" either, and deciding how to attribute tax to them is unsourced.
   Implementing it now would be arithmetic with no source for either input.
3. **The rate is chosen, not marginal.** One rate, the band the income falls in, applies to the whole
   tax. The two ladders compose as the greater of their rates (D-084). At exactly 50L, 1Cr, 2Cr or 5Cr
   the income "does not exceed" the threshold, so it stays in the lower band (`upperInclusive`).
4. **Surcharge is charged on the tax after rebate** (`REBATE-ORDER-001`). The two never co-apply, so the
   choice changes no figure, but reading `tax.afterrebate` keeps the pipeline in the rulebook's order.
5. **Standing rules (D-043):**
   1. coverage: none — Finance Act provisions, not Act sections;
   2. catalogue: none;
   3. both years: no new rows; the node reads the spanning rows, and every test runs in both years;
   4. concept IDs: none new.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — how U is priced (finding 1).**
  - **(a) recommended: re-enter the pipeline, as a separate item `W-056a` committed first.**
    - `NodeContext.recomputed(NodeId target, NodeId pinned, NodeValue value)` returns what `target`
      would be if `pinned` had been `value`.
    - The runner supplies it by re-running only the nodes between `pinned` and `target`, in the same
      order and through the same scoped contexts. It uses a fresh value store, which avoids the
      write-once check, and it discards the sub-run's trace.
    - A node may ask only for a `target` and `pinned` it declared in `requires()`, so a hidden
      dependency is still a test failure.
    - `NodeHarness` gains a stub for it. The runner's own tests prove the sub-run matches a fresh run at
      that income.
    - `SurchargeNode` then prices U as `recomputed(tax.afterrebate, round.totalincome, C)` plus the
      unrelieved surcharge on it. The rebate, the age ladders and (later) the special rates all bear on
      U, with none of them duplicated.
  - **(b)** `SurchargeNode` prices U itself: the regime's ladder at C, rebate taken as nil. It's
    smaller, but it duplicates `TaxByBasisNode`'s ladder selection and rests on finding 1's inference.
- **C2 — `RATE-SUR-SPLIT-001` and the excluding income (finding 2).**
  - **(a) recommended: move both to where their income arrives.**
    - W-056 takes the excluding income as the total income. That's exact while the graph carries no
      dividend or special-rate income.
    - It **refuses loudly** (an `IllegalStateException` naming `W-088`) if `tax.afterrebate` carries
      special-rate tax, the same pattern as `RebateNode`'s `W-089` refusal.
    - `RATE-SUR-SPLIT-001` and T2-6 move to `W-088`.
    - Appendix F gains two lines: `W-079` must feed dividends into the excluding income, and `W-088`
      must add the split, T2-6 and the special gains.
  - **(b)** Author the 15% limit rows and the two-bucket arithmetic now, unit-tested on a hand-built
    `TaxByBasis`. The excluding income would still have no source, so only half of the clause would be
    real.
- **C3 — the graph.**
  - A new node `tax.surcharge` (`MoneyValue`): it requires `tax.afterrebate` and `round.totalincome`,
    and declares the six Phase 6 rows.
  - `exec/pipelines/tax.surcharge.json`; `NodeIds` regenerated.
  - Both regimes' `nodeRefs` gain `tax.surcharge` after `tax.afterrebate`.
  - `CessNode` requires it, and its base becomes tax after rebate plus surcharge (FA 2026 s.3(15):
    "such income-tax and surcharge").
  - `NetPayableNode` adds it; `GoldenCaseRunner` registers the node.
  - Locks: law (regimes) 2025-26 `law.5`, 2026-27 `law.14`, plus an exec bump for the pipeline file.
- **C4 — the trace (P5).**
  - One `TAX` line for the surcharge, citing the ladder whose rate won, with `limitedBy` naming the
    other ladder when it lost.
  - One `RELIEF` line from the regime's `-CEILING` row, a nil one included, with its reason (as
    `RebateNode` does).
  - Rows for the other regime leave no line.
  - Where both ladders give the same rate, the line cites the total-income ladder: clause (i)/(ii)
    comes first in the Act.
- **C5 — `SlabLadder.bandFor(Money income)` → `Optional<Band>`**, the band with
  `lowerExclusive < income ≤ upperInclusive` (empty at 0). The row's "adds a band lookup"; no new field
  (D-050 as amended by D-085).
- **C6 — `NOT_YET_EVALUATED`:** deleted. The I3 test then asserts that every shipped rule is evaluated
  by a node, with no exception list. That's what "empties the set" means once the set has no members.
