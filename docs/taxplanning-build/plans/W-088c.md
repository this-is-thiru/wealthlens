## Plan for W-088c — the surcharge split

**What this item is for (one sentence):** surcharge the tax on dividend income and on the special-rate capital gains
at no more than 15%, take those gains out of the 25%/37% test as well as the dividends, and lift both `SurchargeNode`
refusals. Then a return with special-rate gains or large dividends gets a surcharge figure.

**OQ-30 is answered (D-245, the documented law):** unit income is not dividend income, so D-200 C2a stands.
**This plan raises choices C1–C5. Nothing is coded until you approve.**

### What I read (session 21, 2026-10-18)

- **The row:** *"`RATE-SUR-SPLIT-001` (the 15% limit on the tax on dividends and the special gains), special gains
  out of the `-EXCLUDING` ladders, the dividend slice pro-rata (D-229 C1), both `SurchargeNode` refusals lifted,
  `DR 08` T2-6"*.
- **The law, read this session from the Gazette (FA 2026, egazette 2026/271439, pypdf):**
  - **TY 2025-26, new regime:** s.2(4)(b) Table Sl. 10 (p.7). The 25% clause (iii) is on total income *"excluding
    dividend income or capital gains under the provisions of sections 111A, 112 and 112A"*. Then (v): *"where the
    total income includes any dividend income or capital gains under the provisions of sections 111A, 112 and 112A
    of the said Act, the rate of surcharge on the income-tax in respect of that part of income shall not exceed
    fifteen per cent. and the provisions of clause (i) or (ii), as the case may be, shall apply accordingly"*.
  - **TY 2026-27, new regime:** s.3(4)(b) Table Sl. 10 (p.14). The same words, with ss.196, 197 and 198.
  - **Old regime:** First Schedule Part I-A Para F Table 1 Sl. 1 (vi) (p.83) and Part I-B (vi) (p.87). The same
    limit, *"on the amount of income-tax computed in respect of that part of income"*.
  - **So the limited tax bears the 10% / 15% clause that applies, never more than 15%. The rest bears the greater of
    the two ladders.**
- **How "that part"'s tax is measured:**
  - **Special-rate gains:** the special-rate tax itself, `TaxByBasis.atSpecialRates` (W-088b).
  - **Slab-taxed dividends:** D-229 C1 ruled the pro-rata share, *"the slab tax × dividend income ÷ aggregate
    income"*. ITR-2 (Gazette 271435 p.68) defines *"15 Aggregate income (12-13+14)"*: total income less income at
    special rates. The utility's `E332` is *"0.25×(tax − … − V309) + 0.15×V309"*, with `V309` the 15%-limited taxes
    (the dividends' share `V300` and the special-rate taxes).
- **The code:**
  - `SurchargeNode` chooses one rate, the greater of two ladders, and applies it to the whole of `tax.afterrebate`.
  - It refuses any special-rate tax. It also refuses dividends whose excluding income still reaches the 25% band
    (OQ-26, now answered).
  - Its excluding income is total income less dividend income (D-200). Marginal relief comes from re-entering
    `tax.afterrebate` at the threshold (`ThresholdLadderCeiling`; new regime 50L / 1Cr / 2Cr, old regime adds 5Cr).
  - `SurchargeNodeTest` holds five refusal cases (lines 174, 210, 289, 335, 389) that this item turns into computed
    figures.
- **DR 08 T2-6** (old regime, TI ₹2,50,00,000 of which ₹50,00,000 is s.198 gains): *"the surcharge rate selected is
  15%, and the trace records clause (v) as the reason"*.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the rows:**
  - **(a) recommended:** two law rows, both years, one per regime, each a `PercentOfBase` of 15 on a base
    `LIMITED_PART_TAX`: the ceiling on the surcharge of the limited tax.
    - `RATE-SUR-NEW-TAX-REGIME-LIMIT` (s.2(4)(b) / s.3(4)(b) Sl. 10 (v));
    - `RATE-SUR-FIRST-SCHEDULE-LIMIT` (Part I-A / I-B (vi)).

    `SurchargeRows` gains a fourth row, and a relock follows. These are the rulebook's `RATE-SUR-SPLIT-001`.
  - **(b)** one row for both regimes. The existing surcharge rows are kept one set per regime, and (a) keeps that
    pattern.
- **C2 — the excluding income:** total income less the dividend income (as now) **less the special-rate parts that
  `setoff` holds** (the gains included in total income, before W-088b's shift), floored at nil. `SurchargeNode` gains
  `setoff` in `requires()`.
- **C3 — the split:**
  - limited tax = special-rate tax + dividend slice;
  - dividend slice = slab tax after rebate × dividend income ÷ (total income − special-rate parts);
  - surcharge = chosen rate × (tax − limited) + min(total-income-ladder rate × limited, 15% × limited).

  Applying "clause (i) or (ii)" means the limited part takes the total-income ladder's 10% or 15%. With today's
  ladders the 15% ceiling never binds beyond that, but the row says it, so a later ladder change cannot exceed it.
  Then marginal relief as now: U recomputed at the threshold, with `setoff` and the dividends held, plus the split
  surcharge there. **Recommended.**
  - The rebate is nil wherever surcharge arises (the rebate ends at ₹12,00,000), and `RebateNode` still refuses a
    rebate that meets special-rate tax (W-089). So "slab tax after rebate" equals the utility's slab tax in every case
    this computes.
- **C4 — the trace:**
  - No limited part: the two lines exactly as today.
  - With one: TAX (the chosen ladder, on the tax less the limited part), TAX (`…-LIMIT`, on the limited part, naming
    its dividend and special-rate shares and "clause (i) or (ii)"), RELIEF.
- **C5 — the refusals:** both go, and their five tests become computations with hand-worked figures. Each test
  comment shows its working, as before (R3 is not engaged: the tested behaviour changes by design, and the plan
  names each case).
