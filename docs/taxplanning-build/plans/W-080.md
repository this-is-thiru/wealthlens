## Revision 1 — T6-3 in TY 2026-27 (R13, stopped mid-build)

**What happened.** The first run of `T6HousePropertyAndOtherSourcesTest` gave 8 runs: 7 passed and 1 errored. The
error was T6-3 in TY 2026-27:

```
java.lang.IllegalArgumentException: Income from house property is a loss of 40000.00; setting it off against other
heads or carrying it forward (S2 ss.109, 110; 1961 ss.71, 71B) — or, in the new tax regime, refusing to
(S2 s.202(2)(b)(ii)) — waits on W-081
```

T6-1 (both years), T6-3 (TY 2025-26), T6-6 (both) and T6-7 (both) passed.

**Why — the engine is right, and my plan was wrong.**
- D-156 C1(a) (W-078b) settled this from the text. In TY 2026-27 the new regime removes only s.22(1)(b) for an own
  residence (S2 s.202(2)(a)(v), Gazette 265620 p.227). The Finance Act, 2026 did not extend that to s.22(1)(c)
  (its s.56 omits only s.202(2)(a)(iii), Gazette 271439 p.46). So the pre-construction instalment, ₹40,000, is
  allowed, and the house comes to **−₹40,000**.
- In TY 2025-26, 1961 s.115BAC(2)(i) removes the whole of s.24(b), so ₹0 holds.
- `DIS-NEW-TAX-REGIME-NO-EXEMPTION`'s TY 2026-27 version holds `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-INTEREST` only. The
  `HP-OWN-RESIDENCE-PRE-CONSTRUCTION` row's `_note` says the same.
- The erratum is already under `DR 01` R16's new-regime example (`01-rulebook.md` ll.1296–1300: *"in TY 2026-27 …
  the house's income is **−₹40,000**, a loss the new regime may not set off against another head (s.202(2)(b)(ii));
  the ₹0 holds for TY 2025-26"*). It is not under `DR 08` T6-3, which reproduces R16. I didn't check for it while
  planning; finding 2's *"T6-3 … 0 … in both years"* was wrong.

**Revised choices (recommended first):**
- **R1 — T6-3:**
  - **(a) recommended:** T6-3 runs in **TY 2025-26 only** (₹0, both own-residence rows nil). Its TY 2026-27 half
    (−₹40,000, which in the new regime is neither set off nor carried forward) is placed on **W-081** with T6-2,
    T6-4 and T6-5.
  - **(b)** As (a), and also assert that the TY 2026-27 run is refused naming the ₹40,000 loss. That pins D-156's
    figure until W-081 replaces the refusal; W-081 would then change that assertion.
- **R2 — the `DR 08` erratum under T6-3** (added to C4): *"In TY 2026-27 the expected result is −₹40,000, as
  `DR 01` R16's erratum (D-156 C1(a)): the new regime disallows s.22(1)(b) only, so the ₹40,000 pre-construction
  instalment is allowed; the loss may not be set off against another head (s.202(2)(b)(ii)). ₹0 holds for
  TY 2025-26. The TY 2026-27 half runs with W-081."* The matrix's T6-3 in the 2026-27 NEW column is marked to match.
- Everything else in the plan is unchanged.

**Files:** as planned. `T6-3.json`'s `taxYears` becomes `["2025-26"]`, and its `_source` cites D-156.
