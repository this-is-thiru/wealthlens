## Plan for W-264d — gap audit: P&L, reports and analytics

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** drive the P&L read and the four analytics endpoints end to
end through the HTTP API, with every expected value worked by hand first. Each gap I find is reported and placed as
a row (W-264, D-530). This is the last of the four audits.

**What is in scope (read this session, 2026-11-13):**
- `GET /portfolio/user/{email}/profit-and-loss?financialYear=` (`PortfolioController:54 – 58`, served by
  `ProfitAndLossService:239 – 245`).
- `/analytics/user/{email}/` `portfolio-summary`, `asset-allocation`, `performance-metrics` and `xirr`
  (`AnalyticsController`, `AnalyticsService` 278 lines).
- The test routes `/request` and `/request2` (`PortfolioController:101 – 113`, "for testing purpose only").
- **Reports:** portfolio has no PDF or Excel report of its own. Held assets' Excel is tax planning
  (`/tax/user/{email}/…/held-assets/excel`), and the tax-planning PDF is tax planning, so neither is here. **Not in
  scope:** the charge hierarchy's figures, already audited by W-264b and settled by W-309.

**Hypotheses read from the code** (each is a scenario; none is claimed until a run shows it):
- **H1. XIRR counts held trades.** It reads every transaction (`AnalyticsService:199`), including those held
  `TEMPORARY`.
- **H2. XIRR quietly drops open positions with no price.** A holding missing from `currentPrices` is left out while
  `includesOpenPositions` says true (`:211 – 222`).
- **H3. Two prices for one stock give a 500.** `Collectors.toMap` throws `IllegalStateException` (`:211`).
- **H4. A wrong year looks empty.** The P&L stores `2025-2026` (`FinancialYear:13`). A request for `2025-26`, the
  form tax planning uses, finds nothing and answers 200 with an empty P&L (`ProfitAndLossService:241 – 244`).
  `FinancialYear.parse` already refuses a bad label with a clear message (`:39 – 41`).
- **H5. "No losses" reads as a ratio of 0.** With wins and no losses, `winLossRatio` is 0.0 (`:137`).
- **H6. The trade count includes held trades.** The summary's `totalTrades` counts every transaction (`:48, 60`).
- **H7. Test routes ship.** `/request` echoes a body, and `/request2` runs query filters over assets.
