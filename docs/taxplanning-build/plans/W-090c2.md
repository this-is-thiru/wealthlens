## Plan for W-090c2 — TY 2026-27's ITR-1 and ITR-2 as form rows

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** ship r.164's first two forms for TY 2026-27 as pack rows,
built only from conditions that already exist, and switch on the two checks that keep a year's forms a single cascade:
I19 (a form for everyone) and I20 (never two at once). It also extends I27 so the form rows provably change no figure.

**Findings (each read this session):**
- F1: the law. Gazette 271092 (Income-tax Rules, 2026), read from the egazette text copy, pp.1662 – 1664:
  - r.164(1) covers returns "under section 263(1) or section 268(1)(a) or section 280, relating to the tax year
    commencing on the 1st April, 2026", and (14) keeps earlier years on their own forms. So each row is valid for
    2026-04-01 – 2027-03-31 only, as the declarations are (D-520 C3a).
  - r.164(2): SAHAJ (ITR-1) is for "an individual, who is a resident other than not ordinarily resident" whose total
    income includes income under these heads:
    - (a) salaries or family pension;
    - (b) house property, where the assessee "does not own more than two house property and does not have any brought
      forward loss or loss to be carried forward under the head";
    - (c) other sources, "except winnings from lottery or income from race horses and does not have any loss under
      the head";
    - (d) capital gains, where there are "only long-term capital gains under section 198 not exceeding ₹ 125000" and
      no loss brought forward or to be carried forward.
  - r.164(3)(a) – (n): fourteen bars.
  - r.164(4): ITR-2 is for an individual "not being an individual to whom sub-rule (2) applies", or an HUF, "where the
    total income does not include income under the head business or profession".
  - r.164(12) Table:
    - Sl. 3 (anyone other than Sl. 1, 2 and 4): digital signature, EVC, or electronic data then ITR-V.
    - Sl. 4 (an individual "of the age of eighty years or more at any time during the tax year" filing ITR-1 or
      ITR-4): the same three manners, plus "paper form".
    - Sl. 2 (accounts audited under s.63): s.63(1) audits "every person, carrying on the business or profession"
      (Gazette 265620, the 2025 Act, p.97). Neither form serves a person with business income, so Sl. 2 never reaches
      them.
- F2: every condition the rows need already exists. Each bar maps as follows:
  - The 13 declarations of W-090a2 cover (a) – (d), (f) – (j) and (l) – (n). (i) is two of them: s.159 relief and
    s.160 deduction. (j) is `AGRICULTURAL_INCOME`, an amount. Each declaration's `_note` names its clause
    (`exec/declarations/*.json`).
  - (e), s.93 other than (1)(d), is `DeductionAllowedUnder` naming six rows:
    - `OS-BANKER-COMMISSION` (1)(a);
    - `OS-OTHER-EXPENDITURE` (1)(e);
    - `OS-COMPENSATION-INTEREST` (1)(f);
    - `OS-COMMUTED-PENSION` (1)(g);
    - `OS-DEATH-GRATUITY` (1)(h);
    - `OS-DIVIDEND-INTEREST` (s.93(2)).

    The two family-pension rows are (1)(d) and are left out. `OS-COMPENSATION-INTEREST-INCOME` charges income and
    deducts nothing. No row cites s.93(1)(b) or (c).
  - (k), total income above ₹50 lakh, is `Not(IncomeAtMost(round.totalincome, 5000000))`.
  - (2)(d)'s "section 198" gain is the part `LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY` (`CG-PART-LONG-LISTED`: "s.198(1)";
    `RATE-CG-LONG-LISTED-EQUITY`: s.198(2)(a)).
  - The age reaches a condition as the completed years on the tax year's last day, which `NodeContext.conditions()`
    documents as what "at any time during the tax year" asks.
- F3: lottery winnings cannot be entered: `OtherSourceKind.WINNINGS` is refused, its rate not built
  (`input/OtherSourceKind.java:94`). No input carries race-horse income. So (2)(c)'s exception has no fact to read. It
  will need a bar the day either one is computed.
- F4: the checks.
  - `PackValidator` runs `ItrFormReferences` (`validate/PackValidator.java:95`), and the build validates every shipped
    pack (`RulePackLoadTest`), which the lock then pins.
  - Nothing checks I19 or I20 yet.
  - I5 counts only the person types some regime serves (`PackValidator.java:184`). No regime serves an HUF
    (`PersonType` Javadoc).
- F5: the layers.
  - The forms layer compiles to `forms/itr-forms.json` (`RulePackCompiler.java:95`) and is left out of the compute
    cache key (`RulePackLayer.affectsComputation`).
  - `ContentPerturbationTest` is I27 for `content/`. Its Javadoc says W-090c extends it to `forms/`.
  - The 2026-27 lock holds `2026-27.forms.2`, which this item bumps to 3.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1a (recommended): I19 counts the person types a regime of the year serves, times the return kinds the year's
  forms name.** This is I5's rule (D-062).
  - Today that is INDIVIDUAL times the three kinds, which ITR-1 and ITR-2 cover. ITR-2 still lists HUF, because
    r.164(4) does.
  - A year with no forms names no kind, so TY 2025-26 still loads until W-090d. W-091 must refuse to pick a form in
    a year that has none.
  - C1b: every year must have forms, which fails TY 2025-26 until W-090d is built.
- **C2a (recommended): leave (2)(c)'s winnings and race-horse exception unwritten, and place a row for it.**
  - No computable return can hold either income (F3). The ITR-1 row's `_note` quotes the exception and names the
    row.
  - The new row is **W-261**: ITR-1's bar for lottery winnings and race-horse income, written when either is
    computed. It is parked, in `PARKED-ROWS.md`.
  - C2b: two new declarations now. That means questions about income the engine refuses anyway.
- **C3a (recommended): (2)(b)'s two-property limit holds whether or not the house-property income is nil.** Every
  property owned is a house-property figure, even a nil one (D-524 C2a), so owning a third is income "under the head"
  that (b) bars.
  - C3b: apply the limit only where house-property income is non-nil, a narrowing the text does not state.
