**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** since W-258, an ITR-2 return for TY 2025-26 with net agricultural
income in the rates must show the form's own split — tax at normal rates on the aggregate income (B-TTI 2a), the rebate
on agricultural income (2c), and Part B-TI rows 13 – 15 — instead of today's figures, which are wrong in 2a and absent in 2c.

- **The cells today** (`forms/itr-forms/ITR-2.json`, TY 2025-26 version only; TY 2026-27's forms are not located, so it places

  - B-TI is placed only to row 12 (`:965 – 1055`); Schedule EI is not placed (the schedules are S, HP, CG, OS, CYLA, BFLA, CFL,
    VI-A, PART-B-TI, PART-B-TTI).

  (`:58 – 65`), and a cell whose rules did not apply is **unavailable** (`ScheduleMapper.java:306 – 317`); a `SumOf` with any

- **Probe** (applied, run, reverted; tree clean): `TAX_AT_NORMAL_RATES` as `SumOf(TAX_ON_TOTAL_INCOME, −TAX_AT_SPECIAL_RATES,

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — 2a and 2c:**
  - **(a)** `REBATE_AGRICULTURAL` = `FromRules` over the four `RATE-AGRICULTURAL-*` rows, kind `RELIEF`, with a new
    `FromRules` field `noneApplied` = `NIL` | `UNAVAILABLE` (JSON `"noneApplied": "NIL"`; absent means `UNAVAILABLE`, so
    every existing cell is unchanged): no row applying is a computed fact — no integration — not a missing figure.
    `TAX_AT_NORMAL_RATES` = `SumOf(TAX_ON_TOTAL_INCOME, −TAX_AT_SPECIAL_RATES, REBATE_AGRICULTURAL)` = (Z + S) − S + Y = X.
  - (b) A new `FromTaxBasis` basis `AGRICULTURAL_REBATE`, with Y carried on `ScheduleMapper.Figures` from the run. Reads by
    rule set rather than by listing rows, but adds a figure to a 10-field record and a basis to the enum.
- **C2 — B-TI 13 – 15:**
  - **(a)** Place all three. 13 `SPECIAL_RATE_INCOME_IN_TOTAL` = `SumOf(SPECIAL_RATE_INCOME)` (B-TI 10's figure: Chapter VI-A
    may not reach special-rate gains, B-TI 11 "limited to (9-10)", so what is in 9 is what is in 12). 14
    `NET_AGRICULTURAL_INCOME_FOR_RATE` = a new source `FromDeclaration(NET_AGRICULTURAL_INCOME)` (Figures gains the answers;
    unavailable when unanswered; I21 checks the code is in the year's catalogue). 15 `AGGREGATE_INCOME` = `SumOf(TOTAL_INCOME,
    −SPECIAL_RATE_INCOME_IN_TOTAL, NET_AGRICULTURAL_INCOME_FOR_RATE)` — the form's arithmetic.
    - **A reading:** 15 is marked "[applicable if (12-13) exceeds maximum amount not chargeable]"; where it is not applicable
      the cell still shows the sum, while 2a (from the engine) is the tax on 12 − 13 alone. The form gives no other figure for
      that case.
  - (b) Place 13 and 14 only; 15 not placed.
  - (c) None now; a row placed for them.
- **C3 — Schedule EI:**
  - **(a)** Place item 2 only: i, ii, iii `ManualEntry` (the person computes them; the engine holds only the net), iv =
    `NET_AGRICULTURAL_INCOME_FOR_RATE` (the same cell as B-TI 14 — B-TI 14 points at EI, and its "3" reads like a misprint for
    2(iv): this placement follows the evident intent, a reading). Rows 1, 3 – 5 (other exempt income) are not computed and
    not placed.
  - (b) Not now; a row placed.
