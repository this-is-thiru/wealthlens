## Plan for W-172 — backfill the V2 sales' profit, brokerage and misc

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** every V2 sale made before the deploy left its P&L year
with its purchase and sale amounts only; this one-time, idempotent operation adds each such sale's profit, brokerage
and misc — from the trade-outcome row the recorder wrote for it — into the P&L split, so "total realised profit"
counts them (D-372 C2a; R15).

**Medium: one operation (service, controller, report), an integration test, R15 entry, Phase 15 row, YouTrack child.
It changes figures users see, for past V2 years.**

### What I read (session 35, 2026-10-25)

- W-172's row; `plans/W-169.md`; `plans/W-160d3.md` and its operation (`ProfitAndLossSplitReshape`,
  `StoredReport`); `TradeOutcomeRecorder` (`record`, `buildRow`, `buySide`, `sellSide`);
  `PortfolioService.toTradeOutcomeContext` / `v1Figures` / `v1BuySide` (`:700–765`); `TradeOutcomeEntity`
  (`AuditableEntity`, `audit_metadata`).
- **How to tell a V2 sale's row from a V1 one:**
  - The recorder (V2) always sets `buyChargeBreakup` and `sellChargeBreakup` — maps, empty when nothing was priced.
  - V1 (`toTradeOutcomeContext`) sets neither.
  - The trade-outcome rebuild (runbook §2.2) writes rows "with no charge breakdown" (§2.2's own words).
  - So **a row carrying `sell_charge_breakup`, even an empty one, is the recorder's**. Whether an empty map is
    actually stored, rather than dropped, is something the build verifies first (step 1, below). If it is dropped,
    I stop (R13).
- **What such a row holds:** `buyBrokerCharges`, `buyMiscCharges`, `sellBrokerCharges`, `sellMiscCharges`,
  `netProfit`. These are the figures W-169 now folds for new sales. So the backfill adds brokerage = buy + sell
  broker, misc = buy + sell misc, and profit = `netProfit`, the same mapping as `recordSale`.
- **Which rows are before the deploy:** sales after it are recorded in full by W-169. A row's sell date is no guide,
  because a sale can be entered late. Its `audit_metadata.createdAt` says when it was written.
- **A V2 sale made before the recorder existed** has no row at all. The P&L cannot say which of its amounts are
  V2's, so such a sale cannot be found and keeps purchase and sell only.
- **Correction to `plans/W-160d3.md`:**
  - That plan said rebuilding V1 figures from `trade_outcomes` would use "a different formula". It does not.
    `v1Figures` uses the same buy-charge allocation and per-unit sell charge as the V1 P&L. The differences are
    rounding (V1's half-month profit and misc use `+`, not `TMoney.add`) and the rebuilt rows of §2.2 (W-088u7's
    double-counted buy charges).
  - W-160d3's own result is unaffected, since it moved the stored figures unchanged. I record it here because it
    bears on C1.

### The operation (C1a – C4a)

For each `trade_outcomes` row that carries `sell_charge_breakup`, has an `audit_metadata.createdAt` before
`recordedBefore` (an instant the owner passes: the deploy), and is not already counted:

1. **Where it goes:**
   - the P&L document of its email and the financial year of its `sell_date`;
   - the side its `account_type` gives (`SELF` → `realised_profits`, else `out_sourced_realised_profits`);
   - the split entry of its asset type and its segment (`sell_segment`, else `segment`, else `DELIVERY`);
   - the month and half of its `sell_date`.
2. **Placed** when that split entry exists. Its brokerage, misc and profit are added at the year, the month and the
   half with `TMoney.add`, and the row's id is added to the document's `pnl_backfilled_outcome_ids` in the same
   update. The update is conditioned on the id not being there yet, so a row is never counted twice (C2a).
3. **Not placed** when the document, side or split entry does not exist, for example a side W-160d3 could not place,
   or when the row has no asset type. Such a row is listed with its reason, and nothing is written for it.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what to backfill from:**
  - **(a) recommended:** the recorder's rows, adding only the three missing figures. Purchase and sale amounts, and
    every V1 figure, stay as they are.
  - **(b)** rebuild every split from all `trade_outcomes` rows. Simpler to reason about, but it rewrites V1 figures
    too (rounding, and §2.2's rebuilt rows).
- **C2 — never counting a row twice:**
  - **(a) recommended:** an id set on the P&L document (`pnl_backfilled_outcome_ids`), written in the same update as
    the figures, so each document's update is atomic. Unset by the Phase 15 removal (W-173).
  - **(b)** a marker on each trade-outcome row. That is a second write in another collection, not atomic with the
    figures without Mongo transactions.
- **C3 — "before the deploy":**
  - **(a) recommended:** `recordedBefore` is required and passed by the owner (the deploy instant). Rows written
    after it are W-169's and are skipped.
  - **(b)** every recorder row. That would double-count every sale made after the deploy.
- **C4 — rows with no split entry to go to:**
  - **(a) recommended:** listed and skipped, like W-160d3's unplaced sides. The runbook asks for the list to be
    brought back with §2.6's.
  - **(b)** create the split entry with just the three figures. It would then show profit with no purchase or sale.
