### Findings

1. **The law is in the pack, not reachable from `taxplanning`.**
   - `SAL-STANDARD-DEDUCTION-NEW-TAX-REGIME.json` applies `OnlyIn` `NEW_TAX_REGIME`, from 2026-04-01, as `LeastOf`
     "statutory amount" `Fixed` 75000 and "salary". `SAL-STANDARD-DEDUCTION-ANY-OTHER-CASE.json` is its twin.
   - The cess rate is `RATE-HEALTH-EDUCATION-CESS.json`.
   - `TaxCalculator` has no accessor for either (its public methods, `TaxCalculator.java:90` – `:570`, grep), but has
     `defaultRegime(TaxYearId)` (`:434`).
2. **Row A asks about the default regime.** "Whether opting out of taxation under section 202(1)?" (Annexure-I and -II
   row A). s.202(1) is the new regime, the default (`01-rulebook.md:64`, REG-DEFAULT-001). So "No" means the year's
   default regime and "Yes" means any other.
3. **The deduction may be less than the ceiling:** "or the salary, whichever is less" (`LeastOf`). A 4(b) below the
   regime's ceiling may be right.
4. **The rows sit at different paths in the two annexures** (Annexure-I 4(b), 13 – 16; Annexure-II 3(b), 11 – 14). A
   check that names paths in Java would put the form's layout in code.
5. **A fixture the checks reach.** `Form130ParserTest.read_whenASpecifiedBankIssuesAnnexureII_readsItsRowsAndInfersWhatItsSumsFix`
   prints Annexure-II row A "No" (the new regime) with 3(b) = 50,000, which is the other regime's ceiling. No other
   fixture prints 4(b), 3(b), 16 or 14 (grep of `backend/src/test`).
