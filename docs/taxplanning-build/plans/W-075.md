### Findings

1. **Mis-sized (§7).** About 38 rows, each with two versions, plus the Schedule XV components G2.2 needs — far over
   one item. A natural cut follows G2: the s.123 family with NPS (G2.2), 80D (G2.3), and the rest.
2. **TY 2025-26 cannot be authored (R1, R2).** R15 gives no 1961 figure for any row; the design's 1961 column names
   sections only. The 1961 text as amended up to AY 2026-27 is unread here, and the reachable consolidation stops
   at 2019. The TY 2025-26 versions need a sourcing round first.
3. **TY 2026-27 can be authored and read.** Every figure is in R15, cited to S2 (Gazette 265620), which opens here;
   I will read each section's page myself before marking a version `SOURCE_CHECKED`, as for every row so far.
4. **s.123 needs component codes the design does not name.** `AggregateWithCeiling` takes component codes; the
   design names `EMPLOYEE_PF` and `EMPLOYEE_NPS` and nothing for the other ~22 Schedule XV items (LIC premium,
   tuition fees, housing principal …). Naming them is a concept-id decision (D-043), not something to invent.
5. **The s.124 rows touch OQ-14.** `DED-NPS-EMP-001`…`-003` can be authored as stated, but whether the employer's
   NPS contribution is salary first (OQ-14) decides how `W-076` uses them, not the rows' figures.
