**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** let a person get a first, honestly labelled tax figure
from very little, and see it tighten as they answer five groups of questions, instead of facing every field at once
(F34).

**Probe (2026-11-15; `DR 20` F33 – F34, the PRD and the draft, compute and profile code read, no code run):**
- **What F34 asks** (`20-product-gaps.md:207-228`; PRD F34.1 – F34.3): five steps — (1) gross CTC, city, age;
  (2) basic / HRA split and rent; (3) employer PF and NPS; (4) investments and insurance; (5) other income and
  property — a valid computation after step 1, every step re-prices and narrows a stated confidence band, a coarse
  estimate is labelled coarse, and F33 says what the next step is worth.
- **What exists.** A draft is created empty (`POST …/workspaces/{taxYear}/drafts`, `DraftController.java:51`), edited by
  JSON Patch (`:68`), computed (`DraftComputationController.java:41`), compared across regimes (`:47`) and measured
  for completeness (`:62`, W-115): `DraftCompleteness` ranks what the draft lacks by a second engine run each.
- **What does not exist** (grepped `backend/src/main/java` for `coarse`, `confidenceBand`, `onboarding`: nothing):
  - **Steps.** Nothing records which groups of questions a person has answered. Facts alone cannot say it: "no
    investments" and "not asked yet" are the same empty list.
  - **A band.** `DraftComputationView` (`compute/DraftComputationView.java:15`) carries the tax and `notIncluded`, no
    range. `DraftCompleteness.topWorth` adds worths that overlap ("entering all of them need not save the sum"), so
    it is not a band's width.
  - **The upper side.** Only items that lower the tax are measured; income the draft leaves out waits on department
    data no module imports (W-324, `03-WORK-ITEMS.md:1596`).
  - **CTC.** The engine takes pay by component (`taxengine/input/SalaryComponent`), each resolved against the year's
    registry. No input means "cost to company", and nothing splits one into basic and HRA.
- **City and age in step 1.** Age comes from the profile; city decides nothing until a home is rented (the HRA
  location is the tenancy's). Step 1 as designed asks for a fact step 2 is the first to use.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: the split.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** three items.
    - **W-122a, the steps:** the five steps as product vocabulary; the draft records which a person has answered;
      a read gives, per step, answered or not, and for the next unanswered step what completeness says it is worth.
    - **W-122b, the band and the label:** every computation of a draft says whether it is coarse and gives the band
      (C3); the PRD's F34.2 "narrows" proven by a test that walks the five steps.
    - **W-122c, the first figure:** one call that takes step 1's answers, creates the draft and returns both
      regimes' figures (F34.1), labelled coarse.
  - C1b: one item.
- **C2: what step 1 enters, with no pay split known.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** the year's gross pay as one fully taxable pay component, and nothing assumed. The figure is
    the tax if none of it were exempt — a true upper figure for salary, labelled coarse. City moves to step 2 with the
    rent (erratum on F34's step 1).
  - C2b: assume a split of CTC into basic, HRA and employer contributions. Every such percentage is an invention —
    no source fixes it — and a person's first figure would rest on it.
- **C3: what the band is.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** measured, not estimated. The upper end is the tax as the draft stands. The lower end is
    one more engine run with every item completeness measures filled to its room at once (so overlaps are counted
    once). The band says in words that income not yet entered is outside it, until W-324. Answering a step removes
    its items from the run, so the band can only narrow or stay.
  - C3b: no band until W-324 gives both sides; W-122b carries the label only.
  - C3c: a band from `topWorth` — wrong by construction, the worths overlap.
- **C4: how "answered" is known.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** stored on the draft as a `taxplanning` record (which steps are answered), set by the
    person, never inferred — like W-119's `EvidenceHeld`, outside the engine's inputs and fingerprint. A draft stored
    before it reads as no step answered.
  - C4b: inferred from which facts are present; "nothing to enter" then never counts as answered and the label never
    clears for a person with a simple year.
- **C5: when a figure stops being coarse.**
  - **C5a (recommended):** when all five steps are answered. Until then every computation view of the draft carries
    the label and the steps still open.
  - C5b: when the band is narrower than a stated amount — a threshold with no source.

**Roles:**
- **Architect:** steps and "answered" are the product's knowledge about a conversation, not law and not engine
  input; the band is the engine asked one more question, as completeness already asks.
- **PM:** the honest first figure is "tax on your whole pay, before anything you can claim", and each step shows how
  far it can still fall.
- **Senior engineer:** W-122a a stored-shape change and a read; W-122b one more run per computation view — its plan
  measures the cost against the N1 budget before choosing where the run happens; W-122c one endpoint over existing
  services.
- **Automation tester:** a five-step walk on one persona, the band's two ends derived by hand from that persona's
  golden figures; the label present until step five and absent after.
- **Manual tester:** enter only gross pay, read both regimes and the label; answer each step with "nothing" and watch
  the label clear without the figure moving.

**Risks or ambiguities I noticed:**

  first (employment, employer and residence facts may be required).

- F33's acceptance says completeness "drives the onboarding order"; C1a keeps the five steps in F34's fixed order and
  uses completeness only to price the next one. If you want the order itself to follow worth, say so at C1.
