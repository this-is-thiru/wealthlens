## Plan for W-153d — dividend income for the surcharge by rule data

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** which items are "dividend income" for the surcharge's 15%
limit is stated by the surcharge-limit rows themselves, through `othersources.kind`. Today it is
`SurchargeNode.DIVIDEND_KINDS` (`:100`), with the dividends' part of the year's interest allowance picked by kind at
`DividendInterestAllowance:78`. This is the last kind test of the W-153 family (D-318, K4).

**This plan raises C1 – C2.**

### What I read (session 29, 2026-10-22)

- The WI row (W-153d), D-318, D-200 (C2a: dividend income = dividend and deemed dividend, not unit income), D-229,
  D-234 (the dividends' share of the allowed interest, bounded by each side's claim), and D-245 (**OQ-30 answered**:
  *"dividend income"* in FA 2026 Para F is S2 s.2(40)'s, without unit income).
- **The law, already in the rows:**
  - `RATE-SUR-FIRST-SCHEDULE-LIMIT` and `RATE-SUR-NEW-TAX-REGIME-LIMIT` (set `RS-RATE-SUR-LIMIT`, `REGIME` ground,
    chosen by regime, one version each from 2025-04-01, "Spans TY 2025-26 and TY 2026-27: the same words") quote FA
    2026 First Schedule Part I-A / I-B Para F Table 1 Sl. 1 (vi) and s.2(4)(b) / s.3(4)(b) Table Sl. 10 (v):
    *"where the total income includes any dividend income or capital gains under the provisions of sections 111A, 112
    and 112A …, the rate of surcharge … shall not exceed 15 per cent."*
  - Their `_note`s already say *"dividend income excludes unit income (S2 s.2(40); D-200 C2a, D-245)"*.
- **"Dividend":**
  - S2 s.2(40) (Gazette pp.10–11, read in W-153c, `sources/2026-10-22-unit-income.md` U3): (a) – (f), (f) being *"any
    payment by a company on purchase of its own shares"*, the deemed dividend;
  - 1961 s.2(22) (thc p.39, U5), with (f) inserted by F(No.2)A 2024 (`OS-DEEMED-DIVIDEND`'s former note, now
    `OS-DIVIDEND-INTEREST`'s).

  So dividend income is `DIVIDEND` and `DEEMED_DIVIDEND` in both years. Unit income is not dividend income.
- **The code:**
  - `SurchargeNode.dividendIncome` (`:205–215`) sums the `DIVIDEND_KINDS` items, less `interestAllowedOnDividends`;
  - that method (`:222–232`) bounds the allowed interest by `DividendInterest.receivedOnDividends` /
    `claimedOnDividends`, which `DividendInterestAllowance.of` (`:78`) fills from the items whose kind is `DIVIDEND`;
  - `OtherSourcesNode` also calls `.of` (through `allowed`) and never reads those two fields.
- `RS-RATE-SUR-LIMIT`'s two rows have no conditions. Its ground is `REGIME`, so selection never tests conditions
  (`NodeContext.appliesHere`), and an item condition there changes no selection.
- **Tests:** `SurchargeNodeTest` has the unit-income (`:411`) and deemed-dividend (`:423`) cases and the D-234
  attribution cases (`:462–500`).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — where "dividend income" is stated:**
  - **(a) recommended: on the limit rows**, the provision that uses the words. Each version gains
    `AnyOf(ItemFactIs othersources.kind DIVIDEND, … DEEMED_DIVIDEND)`.
    - `SurchargeNode` takes as dividend income the items its regime's limit row reaches (`OtherSourcesNode.reaches`,
      item conditions only, as since W-153a).
    - A Finance Act that changes which income the 15% reaches is then a new version of that row.
  - **(b)** reuse `OS-DIVIDEND`'s reach (the s.92(2)(a) row). There is no pack change, but the surcharge's "dividend
    income" would be tied to the head's charging row, and `SurchargeNode` would have to declare `RS-OS-INCOME`.
  - **(c)** a new row of its own. That gives the same as (a), with one more row and set.
- **C2 — the dividends' part of the allowance (`:78`):**
  - **(a) recommended:** `SurchargeNode` computes `receivedOnDividends` / `claimedOnDividends` itself, over the items
    both `OS-DIVIDEND-INTEREST` and its limit row reach.
    - `DividendInterest` keeps `base`, `claimed`, `limit`, `untaxedAbroad`.
    - `interestAllowedOnDividends` takes the two sums. D-234's formula is unchanged.

    `DividendInterestAllowance` then holds no kind test, and `OtherSourcesNode` (which does not declare the
    surcharge's sets) never needs the limit row.
  - **(b)** keep them in `DividendInterest`, with `.of` taking the limit row. `OtherSourcesNode` would then have to
    declare `RS-RATE-SUR-LIMIT`.
