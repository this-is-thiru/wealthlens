## Plan for W-066c — retirement limits set by notification

**What this item is for (one sentence):** deduct gratuity from employers outside the government schemes, and
leave encashment for employees who are not government employees, up to the limits the Acts and the Central
Government's notifications set, including the caps across employers and years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6). It recommends moving retrenchment (Sl. 10) to W-066d, because one of
its three limbs is the Industrial Disputes Act's, which OQ-9 blocks.**

### Findings

1. **The ten-month average needs its own input.** The year's pay lines can't give the ten months before a June
   retirement: most of them fall in the previous tax year.
2. **Leave limb (a) is an amount the employer computes.** Both Acts limit the leave *period* (thirty days per year),
   and the 1961 Act also caps it at ten months. Neither says how many days make a month, and the common "÷ 30" is
   not in either Act. So limb (a) is best entered as the employer's figure for the capped leave, not computed.
3. **Each notification states which events it covers.**
   - Gratuity: events **on or after 29-3-2018**.
   - Leave: from **1-4-2023** by deeming, *"in relation to employees … who retire"*.
   - An earlier event would need an earlier notification (S.O. 141(E) for gratuity), which hasn't been read.
4. **Retrenchment can't be built here.** Its limb (b) is IDA s.25F(b), repealed on 21-11-2025. That is OQ-9, the
   same question as W-066d.
5. **Gratuity paid to a widow or dependants** (1961 only) is not the employee's salary, so it has no place in this
   salary pipeline.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — scope:**
  - **(a) recommended: Sl. 6 and Sl. 14 with their aggregates, both years.** Retrenchment (Sl. 10) moves wholly to
    `W-066d`: all three of its limbs are built together once OQ-9 is answered. The row text is updated to say so.
  - **(b)** Build Sl. 10 now on limbs (a) and (c) only. That drops a limb the Act states, so the figure would be
    wrong.
- **C2 — the input:** new entries `OTHER_GRATUITY` (Sl. 6; 1961 s.10(10)(iii)) and `OTHER_LEAVE_ENCASHMENT`
  (Sl. 14; s.10(10AA)(ii)). A payment under either carries a **`Separation`**:
  - the date service began;
  - the date of the event (retirement, incapacity, death or termination);
  - the **pay lines of each of the ten months** immediately preceding the event's month.
  - The engine counts **completed years** from the two dates, and averages the ten months' salary by
    `SAL-SALARY-FOR-GRATUITY`'s own inclusions, so DA counts only where the terms provide. That keeps the salary
    definition in one place (D-092) rather than asking the taxpayer to apply it.
  - Gratuity and leave from the same separation must carry the same `Separation`, and are refused if they differ.
  - A leave payment also carries **`leaveSalaryAtCredit`**: the cash equivalent of the earned leave at credit, with
    entitlement capped at thirty days per year of actual service, as the employer computed it (finding 2).
- **C3 — rows**, all `AllRegimes`, both years:
  - **`RET-GRATUITY-OTHER`:** `LeastOf` of "gratuity received", "notified limit" (`Fixed` 2000000), and "half a
    month's salary for each completed year" (`PercentOfBase` 50 of the ten-month average × completed years, which
    the node forms).
  - **`RET-LEAVE-ENCASHMENT-OTHER`:** `LeastOf` of "payment received", "leave salary at credit", "ten months'
    average salary" (`PercentOfBase` 1000 of the average) and "notified limit" (`Fixed` 2500000).
  - **`RET-GRATUITY-AGGREGATE` and `RET-LEAVE-ENCASHMENT-AGGREGATE`** (s.19(2)(a), (f); the 1961 provisos): A − B
    as `AmountInExcess` of the notified limit over the ledger's amount allowed before. The node caps this year's
    total of each at it, with one line per aggregate that binds.
  - **Provenance:** TY 2026-27 is S2 plus the notification, `SOURCE_CHECKED`. TY 2025-26 is the thc.nic.in 1961
    text plus the notification, **`UNVERIFIED`**, with `whatToCheck` naming the three unread Acts (F(No.2)A 2019,
    TLA 2019, interim FA 2024).
- **C4 — event dates:** a gratuity event before 29-3-2018, or a leave retirement before 1-4-2023, is **refused**,
  naming the notification. Its limit would be an earlier notification that hasn't been read.
- **C5 — node:** `RetirementNode` gains the two entries and the aggregate step, and no longer refuses
  `GRATUITY` / `LEAVE_ENCASHMENT` paid under them. The refusal stays for retrenchment, gratuity under the Payment
  of Gratuity Act, and anything else unbuilt, naming `W-066d`.
- **C6 — out of scope:** gratuity received by a widow or dependants (1961 s.10(10)(iii)), as finding 5 says.

## As built — departures from the plan (for review)
- **Leave's ten-month average is the employer's figure** (asked after approval): Sl. 14(b) and 1961 s.10(10AA)(ii) average the ten months "immediately preceding his retirement", a period ending on the day, which calendar months do not give. So `LeaveAtRetirement` carries it beside the leave salary at credit, and `Separation` is gratuity's alone. The "same separation" check compares the gratuity's event day with the leave's retirement day.
- **The aggregates state A as a `Fixed` amount and the node subtracts B**, rather than `AmountInExcess`: that shape subtracts a shape from a base, and A is data while B is the ledger's. Each notified amount therefore sits in two rows, and a test holds them equal in both years.
- **The notifications' coverage dates are read from the rows**: each version's `introducedBy.effectiveFrom` (29-3-2018; 1-4-2023) is the day from which the event must fall, so no date is in code.
- **Limb (a) is named by each year's Act** (owner's review question, "apply the W-066c limb fix"): TY 2026-27's Sl. 14(a) is "the cash equivalent of the leave salary" — what the employer pays; the 1961 clause has no such limb and values the leave at credit "on the basis of the average salary". So the TY 2025-26 version names it "leave at credit valued at the ten-month average salary", `LeaveAtRetirement` states what the employer's figure must be in each year (and each year's "salary"), and a test holds the TY 2025-26 trace to the 1961 name. Only that version and its checksum moved (`2025-26.law.16`, still uncommitted).
- **The node's new logic is in `NotifiedRetirementLimits`**, beside `RetirementNode`, as `AllowanceExemption` sits beside `ExemptionNode`.
