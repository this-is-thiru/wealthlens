## Plan for W-090c1b — The computed facts a return form reads: the split

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** the computation publishes the facts r.164(2) and (3)(e)
read about the year's return, so a form's conditions can test them. These are each head's income or loss, the
capital gains by part, the deductions allowed, and the losses brought forward and carried forward. This plan sizes the
item and proposes a split, because the facts come from two different places.

**Findings (each read this session):**
- F1: the result. `ComputationProjector.project` builds `TaxComputation` from a run's anchor slots
  (`result/ComputationProjector.java:43` – `52`): `round.totalincome`, `tax.bybasis` and `tax.netpayable`, plus the
  whole trace. `TaxCalculator:66` calls it. Nothing stores the engine's `TaxComputation`:
  - `taxplanning`'s `TaxComputationEntity` belongs to the older engine (`RestructuringEngine.java:19`).
  - So adding fields changes no stored shape.
- F2: three groups of facts already exist in the run.
  - Each head's income or loss of the year: the nodes `salary.income`, `houseproperty.income` and
    `othersources.income` hold it, and `capitalgains.income` holds a `CapitalGainsHead`, whose `total()` is the
    capital-gains figure (`node/SetOffNode.java:100` – `106`). A negative value is the year's loss under the head.
  - The capital gains by part: `CapitalGainsHead.parts()`. The parts are pack data (CG-PARTS: `SHORT_TERM_SLAB`,
    `SHORT_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY`, `LONG_TERM_OTHER`, `LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY`). `LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY` is the part
    `RATE-CG-LONG-LISTED-EQUITY` taxes, at nil up to ₹1,25,000 and then 12.5% (D-244), which is the s.198 gain.
  - The deductions allowed: each rule's step is a `TraceLine` naming its `RuleId`, and the trace is already on
    `TaxComputation`. The s.93 deductions are separate rule sets: `RS_OS_BANKER_COMMISSION`,
    `RS_OS_OTHER_EXPENDITURE`, `RS_OS_DIVIDEND_INTEREST` and `RS_OS_SHARE` (`OtherSourcesNode`,
    `DividendInterestAllowance`).
- F3: the losses are not values. `SetOffNode` publishes `HeadsAfterSetOff(total, capitalGains, indexedGains)`
  (`pipeline/HeadsAfterSetOff.java:19`).
  - What is left to carry forward is only a MEASURE trace line, with text (`SetOffNode.java:161` – `165`).
  - What `CarriedLossSetOff` brings in, and what of it lasts, is also only trace lines (its class Javadoc).
  - Deriving either from trace text would be parsing prose.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1a (recommended): split W-090c1b into two.**
  - **W-090c1b1, the year's facts, read from what the run already holds.**
    - `TaxComputation` gains a `ReturnFacts` record:
      - each head's income or loss of the year;
      - the capital gains by part;
      - the rule sets that allowed a non-nil deduction, read from the trace's `RuleId`s through the pack's rule sets.
    - Conditions: `HasLossUnderHead(head)`, `CapitalGainsOnlyInParts(parts)`, `CapitalGainsPartAtMost(part, limit)`
      and `DeductionAllowedUnder(sets)`.
    - No node changes.
  - **W-090c1b2, the losses across years.**
    - `SetOffNode` and `CarriedLossSetOff` publish, as values, each head's loss brought forward into the year (within
      its span) and each head's loss left to carry forward.
    - `ReturnFacts` gains them, with `HasLossBroughtForward(head)` and `HasLossCarriedForward(head)`.
    - A node's value changes, so its plan names every reader of `HeadsAfterSetOff`.
  - C1b: one item, touching the projector, the result, two nodes and six conditions.
- **C2a (recommended): the s.93 bar names rule sets in the form's data.** r.164(3)(e)'s "deduction under section 93,
  other than … (1)(d)" becomes `DeductionAllowedUnder` with the sets W-090c2 lists, each checked against its rows'
  provisions when the form is written. The fact is "this set allowed a deduction", never "barred from ITR-1".
  - C2b: the code reads each row's provision text for "93" and "(1)(d)". That is law parsed in Java (CLAUDE.md §4).
- **C3a (recommended): the facts live on `TaxComputation`, as `ReturnFacts`.** W-091 builds a form's context from
  them, and W-092's schedules read the same figures.
  - C3b: W-091 recomputes them from the run. The result would then not say what a form was chosen on.
