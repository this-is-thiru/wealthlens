  2018/190360, Part II s.3(ii), No. 3875, 4 pages. Its metadata are Ghostscript, 3-10-2018: a Government Press
  upload, not a capture. **READ** in full. In force from AY 2019-20 (para 2). It *"notifies the transactions of
  acquisition of equity share entered into"*:
  - **(I)** before 1-10-2004; or
  - **(II)** on or after 1-10-2004 and *"not chargeable to securities transaction tax"*, other than:
    - **(a)** a preferential issue of existing listed shares in a company whose shares are not frequently traded.

      a preferential issue outside SEBI ICDR Chapter VII.

      when made under the SCRA:
      - (i) *"through an issue of share by a company other than the issue referred to in clause (a)"*;
      - (ii) by banks, reconstruction or securitisation companies, or PFIs;
      - (iii) court or regulator approved;
      - (iv) under an ESOP / ESPS;
      - (v) by a non-resident under FDI;
      - (vi) under the SEBI takeover regulations;
      - (vii) from the Government;
      - (viii) by an investment fund, VCF or QIB;
      - (ix) by a s.47, s.50B or s.45(3) / (4) mode, if the previous owner did not acquire them by an (a)–(c) mode.
    - **(c)** shares acquired while the company was delisted.
