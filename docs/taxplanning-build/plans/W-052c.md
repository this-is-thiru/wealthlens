### Findings

1. **Every Phase 5 figure is unchanged in 2025-26, but for six of the ten rules the governing instrument
   differs.** Each version carries one `introducedBy` and one provenance. A version spanning both years
   would name one instrument for both, which is untrue for those six (new-regime slab, three rebates,
   two roundings). The three ladders and the cess come from **one** instrument in both years (FA 2026:
   Part I-A / I-B; ss.2(6) / 3(15)).
2. **Assessment-year dates.** The 1961-side amendments speak in assessment years: "with effect from the
   1st April, 2026" means AY 2026-27, whose previous year is TY 2025-26.
3. **The regimes have no per-year versions, and their election data is the 2025 Act's.** Under the 1961
   Act the option is s.115BAC(6) with the deadline s.139(1). No Phase 5 node reads election data, but the
   2025-26 pack would carry the 2025-Act election mislabelled as governing that year.
4. **The 2025-26 instruments are partly unread.** No gazette reference was read for the 1961 Act, and
   the **Income-tax Rules, 1962** were not read at all.
5. **The "registry entry" is the lock's `instruments` block.** No production registry exists (D-054
   UNOWNED); tests build `TaxYearRegistry` from the provider.
6. **Standing rules.** The 1961 sections are already `MODELLED` (s.115BAC, s.87A, ss.288A/288B) and
   catalogued; the First Schedule and FA s.2 aren't in the register. Nothing new to register.

## Built — where the build departs from or adds to the plan (for review)

- **B1 — the roundings' 2025-26 `introducedBy` names the 1961 Act itself** (assented 1961-09-13, in force
  1962-04-01, both read). `AmendmentRef.notifiedOn` is a required date, and the amending Acts' own dates
  weren't read: s.288A was inserted by Act 13 of 1966 and its wording substituted by Act 19 of 1968;
  s.288B was substituted by Act 29 of 2006. Those amendments are **in the citation text**, from the
  1961 copy's footnotes (p.822). No unread date is entered.
- **B2 — the 1962 Rules' `inForceFrom`** is the **Act's** commencement (1962-04-01). `Instrument`
  requires a date, and the Rules' notification wasn't read. The entry is `UNVERIFIED` and its note says
  so. FA 2019's `gazetteRef` is "not read — copy is not the gazette original".
- **B3 — an earlier error, corrected.** The three ladders' `provision1961` said *"Income-tax Act, 1961
  First Schedule Part I Paragraph A"* (since `W-046b`), but the 1961 Act doesn't state these rates; FA
  2026's First Schedule Part I-A does. C1's spanning edit replaced it with FA 2026 Part I-A.
- **B4 — the bite probe is on the data, with the lock re-pinned.** The 2025-26 pack holds only 2025-26
  versions, so no code-side probe can make a year read the wrong version. So:
  - `RATE-ROUND-TOTAL-INCOME`'s 2025-26 `provision1961` was pointed at the 2025 Act, and the 2025-26
    lock re-pinned to its checksum for the probe;
  - only case [8] of the provision test failed, on its assertion;
  - both files were restored byte for byte.
- **B5 — six existing tests assumed the new-regime slab rule has one version** (`versions.get(0)` =
  the 2026-27 version). Each keeps its intent:
  - `RulePackCompilerTest` ×2 and `PackDiffTest` ×2 now edit the last version (the 2026-27 one);
  - `CoverageRegisterTest` drafts every version;
  - `TimelineReaderTest`'s gap case now opens its gap at 2024-25 (the 2025-26 version closed the old
    one) and expects three versions.
- **B6 — the test reads the instruments from the 2025-26 lock**, the year's registry entry (finding 5),
  so the lock is their single record.
