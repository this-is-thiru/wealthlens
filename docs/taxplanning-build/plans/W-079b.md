## Plan for W-079b — income from other sources, the head: interest and dividends

**What this item is for (one sentence):** compute income from other sources for interest, dividends, deemed dividends
and mutual-fund unit income, with each year's deductions, and add it to gross total income in both regimes. Every
other kind of receipt is refused, naming the row that will carry it.

### What I read (all quoted with page, `sources/2026-10-16-other-sources.md` will hold the quotes)

**TY 2026-27, Income-tax Act, 2025 (egazette 2025/265620):**
- s.92(1) (p.137): the residual charge, "Income of every kind … if it is not chargeable … under any of the heads
  specified in section 13(a) to (d)".
- s.92(2) (pp.138–139): (a) "any dividend"; (b) winnings; (c) employee contributions received; (d) keyman; (e)
  "interest on securities"; (f), (g) letting machinery; (h) forfeited advances; (i) "interest received on compensation";
  (j) termination compensation; (k) business-trust sums; (l) life-policy sums; (m) gifts.
- s.93(1) (pp.141–142): (a) banker's commission; (d) family pension; (e) "any other expenditure (not being in the
  nature of capital expenditure) laid out or expended wholly and exclusively for making or earning such income"; **(f)
  "for income of the nature referred to in section 92(2)(i), an amount equal to 50% of such income and no other
  deduction"**; (g) commutation; (h) death gratuity.
- s.93(2) as enacted (p.142): (a) deemed dividend s.2(40)(f) no deduction; (b) other dividends / units, interest
  expense only, "limited to 20%".
- s.94(1) (p.142): personal expenses; interest, or salary, payable outside India without tax paid or deducted. s.94(4)
  winnings, no deduction.

**FA 2026 (egazette 2026/271439), s.44 (p.43), in force 1-4-2026 by s.1(2)(a) (p.1):** (a) s.93(1)(a) becomes
"for interest on securities" only; (b) s.93(2) becomes "in respect of any dividend income or income from units of a
Mutual Fund … or … a specified company …, no deduction shall be allowed".

**TY 2025-26, Income-tax Act, 1961:**
- thc.nic.in, current to Act 7 of 2019. s.57 (p.310): (i) banker's commission "in the case of dividends, other than
  dividends referred to in section 115-O, or interest on securities"; (iii) other expenditure; **(iv) 50% of
  s.56(2)(viii) interest** (ins. Act 33 of 2009). s.58(1)(a) (p.310): personal expenses, and interest or salary
  payable outside India untaxed. s.58(4) (p.311): winnings.
- **FA 2020 s.30 (egazette 2020/218938, p.25), w.e.f. 1-4-2021:** (a) "dividends" for the 115-O words in clause (i);
  (b) the proviso: "no deduction shall be allowed from the dividend income, or income in respect of units of a Mutual
  Fund … other than deduction on account of interest expense, and … such deduction shall not exceed twenty per cent.
  of the dividend income … included in the total income for that year, without deduction under this section".
- **F(No.2)A 2024 s.24 (egazette 2024/256436, p.24):** (a) in clause (i), "other than that referred in sub-clause (f)
  of clause (22) of section 2" (w.e.f. 1-10-2024); (c) "no deduction shall be allowed in case of dividend income of
  the nature referred to in sub-clause (f) of clause (22) of section 2" (w.e.f. 1-10-2024). (b) is family pension, so
  it belongs to W-079c.
- **The rest of the chain**, searched for "In section 56/57/58 of the Income-tax Act": s.56(2) is amended by F(No.2)A

  s.40 amends the 2025 Act's s.58, which is unrelated.)

### Found while reading (for your decision)

1. **The rulebook has no row for interest on compensation** (1961 s.56(2)(viii) / s.57(iv); 2025 Act s.92(2)(i) /
   s.93(1)(f): a flat 50% deduction).
2. **The rulebook's OS-COLL-001 says TY 2025-26 allows banker's commission on dividends.** It does not: since 1-4-2021
   the s.57 proviso allows nothing but interest expense against dividends. So banker's commission is allowed only on
   interest on securities, in both years.
3. **s.92(2) has eight more clauses** — (c), (d), (f), (g), (h), (j), (k), (l) — that no row covers.
