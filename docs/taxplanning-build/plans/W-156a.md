donation counts whole or half, whether it falls within the qualifying limit (a `switch`, `:139–145`;
`DonationClause.isWhole()`, G10), and whether the donee-report bar applies (`:154`). This item makes the rows say

  (`NodeContext.chosen`, `rows.size() <= 1`). So each row keeps its set, and `RowReach` tests its item conditions,
  which ignore the regime.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the share and the limit:**
  - **(a) recommended:** item conditions on the existing rows, tested with `RowReach`.
    - `DED-DONATION-WHOLE`: `donation.clause` any of `NAMED_FUND`, `FAMILY_PLANNING`.
    - `DED-DONATION-HALF`: any of `DROUGHT_RELIEF_FUND`, `CHARITABLE_INSTITUTION`, `PUBLIC_BODY`.
    - `DED-DONATION-QUALIFYING`: any of `FAMILY_PLANNING`, `CHARITABLE_INSTITUTION`, `PUBLIC_BODY`.

    limited where the qualifying row reaches it. The `switch` and `DonationClause.isWhole()` go. The two share sets
    stay named in Java, as the node's declared sets (G7).
  - **(b)** one merged `RS-DED-DONATION-SHARE` set chosen on conditions. It fails in the new regime (above) unless
    `NodeContext` gains a regime-free choice, which is a wider change.
- **C2 — the donee-report bar:**
  - **(a) recommended:** a conditions-only row `DED-DONATION-UNREPORTED`, the lone row of a new set
    `RS-DED-DONATION-REPORT`, on `donation.clause = CHARITABLE_INSTITUTION` and `donation.reported.by.donee = false`.
    Provisions: s.133(6) and s.80G(5D) Explanation 2A, from the two quotes above. Tried after the cash bar, as today.
  - **(b)** a condition on `DED-DONATION-HALF` itself. That row would then stop reaching an unreported donation's
    share, and the donation would be refused as having no share rather than barred.

      "s.133(6)".
    - The group lines read "without limit" / "within the qualifying limit". Whole or half is the line's row and its
      cap, not a word chosen in Java. `:197–202`'s four expected words change to match.

    Both are assertions changed, not weakened, shown here for approval.
  - **(b)** keep today's words, through a map from share row to "the whole" / "half" in Java. That puts the share
    back into strings.
