### Files I will create

- `backend/src/main/resources/rulepacks/timeline/forms/certificates/FORM_130.json` — one version, validity from
  2026-04-01 (the Rules, 2026 govern TY 2026-27 on, `01-rulebook.md` YR-ACT-004), its instrument and provenance (Gazette
  id, pages, URL, sha256, `SOURCE_CHECKED`), its parts (`partA`, `partC.annexureI`) with their printed headings, and
  its fields: path, part, printed label, page, value kind (`AMOUNT`, `YES_NO`, `TEXT`, or `NONE` for a group row such as
  "1. Gross Salary"), the printed column headings where a row has several (rows 10(a) – (k)), and `repeating` for 2(d) and
  10(j). TY 2025-26 gets no version (Form 16 is W-126e).
- `taxengine/rules/id/CertificateCode.java` — the form's code, `^[A-Z][A-Z0-9_]{1,15}$` (`FORM_130`, the same name as
  `taxplanning`'s `DocumentType`, matched by name since `taxengine` cannot see it).
- `taxengine/api/CertificateLayout.java`, `CertificatePart.java`, `CertificateField.java`,
  `CertificateValueKind.java` — what `TaxCalculator` returns (the `api` named interface, as `ClaimPlaybook` is).
  A field path is dot-separated (`partC.annexureI.row2b`, `^[a-z][A-Za-z0-9]*(\.[a-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9]*)*$`), unique within
  the form, and names a part that exists.
- `taxengine/rules/json/CertificateJson.java` — reads a version, refusing an unknown property, a malformed path, a field
  in no part, a duplicate path, a blank label, and `columns` on a non-`AMOUNT` field.
- Tests: `CertificateJsonTest` (each refusal, red first), `Form130LayoutTest` (the shipped TY 2026-27 layout: every
  Part A field and Annexure-I row present with the label in the source note, checked against a list typed from the
  Gazette in the test; TY 2025-26 has none).
