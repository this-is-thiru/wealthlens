## Plan for W-090c1b1 — The year's facts a return form reads

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** every computation carries the facts r.164(2)(c), (d) and
(3)(e) ask about the year's return, so that a form's conditions can test them. Those facts are each head's income or
loss, the capital gains by part, and the rows that allowed a deduction. All three are read from what the run already
holds, with no node changed.

**Findings (each read this session):**
- F1: `RS-OS-SHARE` mixes clauses (`law/rules/*.json`, `provision2025`):
  - `OS-FAMILY-PENSION` and `OS-FAMILY-PENSION-NEW-TAX-REGIME` are s.93(1)(d), the deduction r.164(3)(e) exempts from
    its bar.
  - `OS-COMPENSATION-INTEREST` (s.93(1)(f)), `OS-COMMUTED-PENSION` ((1)(g)) and `OS-DEATH-GRATUITY` ((1)(h)) are not.
  - So the fact and its condition name **rows** (`RuleId`), not sets (D-526 C2a's "or rows").
  - The other s.93 rows are each alone in their set: `OS-BANKER-COMMISSION` (s.93(1)(a)), `OS-OTHER-EXPENDITURE`
    ((1)(e)) and `OS-DIVIDEND-INTEREST` (s.93(2)).
- F2: a deduction's trace line records claimed in and allowed out. In `OtherSourcesLines`, `deduction` writes
  `DEDUCTION` with `in` claimed and `out` allowed, and `nil` writes `out` 0 (`OtherSourcesLines.java:55` – `61`,
  `:87` – `89`). The family-pension share (`OtherSourcesNode.java:152`) and `DividendInterestAllowance.java:40` do the
  same.
  - The trace is the result, and "ITR figures are projections" of it (`trace/TraceLine.java` class Javadoc).
  - 48 lines in `node/` write `DEDUCTION`. I verified the convention only for the s.93 producers. See Risks.
- F3: each head's figure of the year sits in a node:
  - `salary.income`, `houseproperty.income` and `othersources.income` (`MoneyValue`);
  - `capitalgains.income`, a `CapitalGainsHead` whose `parts()` are the gains by part after the head's own netting,
    and whose `total()` is their sum (`pipeline/CapitalGainsHead.java:75` – `77`).

  `SetOffNode` reads exactly these four (`SetOffNode.java:100` – `106`). The engine computes no business income, so
  that head is absent.
- F4: `ComputationProjector.project(run, inputs, id)` is the one place a run becomes a `TaxComputation`
  (`result/ComputationProjector.java:43`). `new TaxComputation(` is called in 2 files. `new ConditionContext(` is
  called 28 times, mostly through the 4-argument constructor, which leaves new facts unknown.

**Files I will create** (under `backend/src/main/java/com/thiru/wealthlens/taxengine/` unless stated):
- `rules/condition/ReturnFacts.java`, the year's facts a return form reads, as a record:
  - `incomeByHead` (`Map<HeadCode, Money>`, negative for a loss);
  - `capitalGainsByPart` (`Map<CapitalGainsPart, Money>`);
  - `deductionsAllowed` (`Set<RuleId>`, the rows whose `DEDUCTION` line allowed more than nil).

  `NONE` holds nothing and every read throws. A head that was not computed throws when read: "business income is not
  computed", never "no loss".
- Conditions in `rules/condition/`:
  - `HasLossUnderHead(head)`: the head's figure of the year is below nil.
  - `CapitalGainsOnlyInParts(parts)`: every part with a non-nil gain is in `parts`. No capital gains at all holds.
  - `CapitalGainsPartAtMost(part, limit)`: that part's gain is at most `limit`.
  - `DeductionAllowedUnder(rows)`: any of `rows` allowed a deduction.
- `result/ReturnFactsProjection.java`: builds `ReturnFacts` from a run. It reads the head nodes of F3 and the trace's
  `DEDUCTION` lines, in one place, so the projector stays small.
- `rules/validate/DeductionRowReferences.java`, run at build time: every row a `DeductionAllowedUnder` names, in a
  rule, a condition set or a form, is a rule of the year's pack.
- Tests, with values worked out by hand:
  - `ReturnFactsTest`
  - `HasLossUnderHeadTest`: house property −₹20,000 holds; nil does not; business throws.
  - `CapitalGainsOnlyInPartsTest`: only `LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY` holds; adding `SHORT_TERM_SLAB` ₹1 fails; none
    holds.
  - `CapitalGainsPartAtMostTest`: ₹1,25,000 at a limit of ₹1,25,000 holds; ₹1,25,001 fails.
  - `DeductionAllowedUnderTest`
  - `ReturnFactsProjectionTest`: a computed run with a banker's commission of ₹2,000 lists `OS-BANKER-COMMISSION`;
    a family pension lists `OS-FAMILY-PENSION` only; a refused deduction (nil line) is not listed.
  - `DeductionRowReferencesTest`

**Risks or ambiguities:**
- I have not audited all 48 `DEDUCTION` producers for "out is the amount allowed". `deductionsAllowed` is read only
  through rows a form names, and W-090c2 names only s.93 rows (verified in F2). The Javadoc will say the fact holds
  for rows whose lines follow the convention, and `DeductionRowReferences` cannot check that. A broader audit is a
  placed row if you want one.

  capital-gains parts after the head's own netting (F3). W-090c2 writes that reading on the form rows.
