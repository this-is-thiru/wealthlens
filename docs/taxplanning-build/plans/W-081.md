## Plan for W-081 — set-off

**What this item is for (one sentence):** let a head's loss reduce income under the other heads as the Acts allow,
carry what is left forward, and destroy it where the new regime says so. Today a house-property or other-sources
loss is refused outright, so DR 08 T6-2, T6-4, T6-5 and T6-3's TY 2026-27 half can't run (D-211).

**This plan raises choices (C1–C6) and proposes a split (§7). Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### Findings

1. **Current-year set-off needs only the heads that exist.** Salaries, house property and other sources are
   built; business and capital gains are not (W-085/W-088). So s.109(1)(a) and s.109(2) have nothing to bite on yet.
2. **The order is undecided in law** (s.109(1)(b) names none). With today's heads it matters only through
   dividends: the surcharge's excluding ladder takes total income less dividend income (D-200), so absorbing the
   house-property loss against other sources rather than salary can change the surcharge.
3. **Carry-forward needs a ledger the snapshot can't express.** It needs, per year of origin, the loss arisen, the
   amount absorbed since, and whether it came from a deduction the new regime disallows (s.202(2)(b)(i)). It also
   needs the year's newly unabsorbed loss returned for the next year's ledger. That is a model change (13 call
   sites) plus a result field.
4. **T6-2 needs income under another head to set the loss against.** DR 08 states none, so a fixture must supply
   one (an inference).
5. **Size:** current-year set-off (node, two rows, a disallowance set, the pipeline, two refusals lifted, four
   golden cases) is about 300 lines of main code and data. Carry-forward (ledger redesign, s.110 / s.71B, the eight
   years, s.202(2)(b)(i) and (3), the result field, `preConstructionInstalmentsClaimed`) is as much again. **The
   item is mis-sized.**
