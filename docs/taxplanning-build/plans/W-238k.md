### What I found

- **Four codes are employee contributions.** `EMPLOYEE_NPS`, `EMPLOYEE_PF`, `EMPLOYEE_SUPERANNUATION` and
  `GOVERNMENT_DEFERRED_ANNUITY` are the pack's `EMPLOYEE_CONTRIBUTION` rows. All are `CASH_OUTFLOW`. The 51 rows
  split 19 `SALARY`, 27 `QUALIFYING_PAYMENT`, 4 `EMPLOYEE_CONTRIBUTION` and 1 `STATUTORY_DEDUCTION`.
- **Take-home already counts them.** It subtracts each `CASH_OUTFLOW` component (`AllocationPricing.java:288`), so
  the cash floor (C3) binds a contribution with no new rule.
- **The search would miss own-NPS.** Own NPS takes only what the 10 % sub-cap left (`ScheduleXvDeduction.java:120`,
  `subCapResidue`; T5-1; D-130 C3 "`EMPLOYEE_NPS` residue", owner-approved).
  - Wherever the ₹1,50,000 ceiling binds before the sub-cap does, total income is flat between them and then falls
    again. That is the case with basic above 15L, or with a PF contribution entered.
  - `Saturation` bisects on "the next rupee stops lowering total income" (`Saturation.java:29-55`). It would stop at
    the flat stretch and never reach own NPS.

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1: which codes.**
- **(a) Recommended.** A new set, `contributions`. Each code must be an `EMPLOYEE_CONTRIBUTION` row in the year's
  pack, read from the row, with no code list in Java.
  - `QUALIFYING_PAYMENT` rows stay refused.
  - W-238g's message for such a code in `movable` adds "name it under contributions".
- (b) Accept any `CASH_OUTFLOW` row. That brings in A3's investments without A3's ranking.

**C2: what the choice moves.**
- **(a) Recommended. An addition on top of what is entered, never a cut.** A cut only removes a deduction. Adding on
  top keeps the entered statutory share whole.
  - `EMPLOYEE_PF` named where it follows a movable `BASIC` is refused. **W-238k1** is proposed for that case.
- (b) Any amount, the entered amount one candidate. That could cut a statutory share the optimiser cannot see.

**C3: a contribution's saturation point.**
- **(a) Recommended.** The least addition whose total income equals the total income at the ceiling. The ceiling is
  the cash in hand before tax.
  - This is sound wherever total income never rises with the contribution. A guard refuses (`IllegalStateException`)
    where it does, at the cost of one more run.
  - Pay and funds keep today's bisection.
- (b) Today's bisection. NPS would then stop at the ceiling, short of own NPS (case 1).

**C4: the answer's shape.**
- **(a) Recommended.** `contributionChanges`, a list of the existing `Change`: facts (kind, cash nature, rules fed,
  liquidity), current, suggested, saving alone, and a cash impact of minus the addition.

  - Checkstyle's `ParameterNumber` does not catch this, since `Optimisation` builds at 12 today. CLAUDE.md §4's
    "more than 7 means a missing type" is broken already. **W-238k2** is proposed to group the lists.
- (b) Group the four lists now. That is wider: every optimiser test asserts `changes()`.

**C5: HR support.**
- **(a) Recommended.** No likelihood on a contribution change. It is the employee's own declaration.
- (b) A value per code in `hr-support.json`, set by you.
