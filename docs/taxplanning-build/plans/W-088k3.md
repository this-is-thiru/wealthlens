## Plan for W-088k3 — the engine decides the term

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** the engine stops taking short-term / long-term from
`portfolio` and decides it per bucket from rule set `RS-CG-TERM` (the holding period of D-303's sources, both years)
and the bucket's dates, traced. `CapitalGainsAdapter` stops mapping `portfolio`'s classification to a term (D-302 D1a).

**This plan raises C1 – C3.**

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what shows a *"security listed in a recognised stock exchange in India"*** (1961 s.2(42A) first proviso;
  2025 s.2(101)(b)(i)) for the twelve months:
  - **(a) recommended:** the **sale** is recorded on `NSE` or `BSE`, the recognised list of D-297.
    - Listing is tested at transfer ("immediately preceding the date of its transfer"), and an exchange sale shows
      the share traded there that day.
    - A reading, named in the row's `_note`: SCRA also allows securities *"permitted to trade"* that are not listed;
      the reading treats an exchange sale as listed.
    - An equity bucket whose sale venue is unrecorded, or another string, matches no twelve-month row and is
      **refused**, naming its facts. The remedy for rows from before W-088j2b is §2.2's rebuild, as for STT.
  - **(b)** either side recorded on `NSE` / `BSE`. A buy on an exchange shows listing at purchase, not at transfer.
  - **(c)** every `EQUITY` bucket is treated as listed, as `portfolio`'s `EQUITY_LISTED` policy does. No record
    supports it; it is the app's assumption.
- **C2 — the 24-month row** (unlisted shares and every other asset):
  - **(a) recommended: not shipped now.**
    - Nothing `portfolio` records shows an equity share to be unlisted, so for equity the row would only catch
      "listing unknown". That would silently give 24 months to listed shares whose venue is unrecorded: a wrong
      answer instead of a refusal.
    - Funds and bonds have no part rows yet, so a term for them decides nothing.
    - It ships with the first fact or part that needs it. The exclusion goes in `RS-CG-TERM`'s set `_note`
      (s.2(42A) / s.2(101)(a), sources rows 3, 7).
  - **(b)** ship it for `assetKind` other than `EQUITY` only. It is dead until fund or bond parts exist.
  - The **s.50AA / s.76** "always short-term" row is likewise not shipped (no fund or bond parts); the `_note` says
    so.
- **C3 — the input:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `CapitalGainsInput.Bucket` **drops `term`**, and `BucketFacts` gains `LocalDate
    transferredOn`.
    - The engine cannot be handed a term it should derive, and a field the engine ignores would be dead weight
      (CLAUDE.md §2).
    - `Term`'s Javadoc is rewritten: the engine derives it, for s.108(2) and the parts.
  - **(b)** keep `term` as an optional override. Nothing would set it.

### The build (C1(a), C2(a), C3(a))

1. **Definition:** `rules/definition/ShortTermHoldingPeriod(int months)`. It means *"short-term if held for not more
   than `months` immediately preceding the transfer"*. `months` is positive (otherwise refused). It is added to
   `RuleDefinition`'s permits, and `DefinitionJson` reads `{"type": "ShortTermHoldingPeriod", "months": 12}`.
2. **Rule set `RS-CG-TERM`** (`AT_MOST_ONE`, ground `REGIME_AND_CONDITIONS`), `_note` citing C2's exclusions. It has
   one row, `CG-TERM-LISTED-SECURITY`, with two versions (TY 2025-26 and TY 2026-27):
   - Conditions: `capitalgains.asset` `EQUITY`, and `trade.transfer.exchange` `NSE` or `BSE`.
   - Definition: `ShortTermHoldingPeriod` 12.
   - Provisions:
     - TY 2025-26: 1961 s.2(42A) first proviso, as amended by FA (No. 2) 2024 s.3(b) (sources rows 1–3);
     - TY 2026-27: 2025 s.2(101)(b)(i) (row 7).
   - `introducedBy`: TY 2025-26 FA (No. 2) 2024 s.3(b) (egazette 2024/256436, w.e.f. 23-7-2024, `SOURCE_CHECKED`);
     TY 2026-27 the 2025 Act (egazette 2025/265620).
   - The `_note` holds:
     - C1's reading;
     - D-303's boundary;
     - that a bonus lot is dated at its ex-date until W-088k4 (Explanation 1(i)(f) / s.2(101)(c)(C)(IV));
     - that a demerged lot keeps the demerged company's period, as recorded (Explanation 1(i)(g) / (B)(III)).
3. **The step,** `node/CapitalGainsTerm.of(ctx, bucket, lines)`, package-private like `CapitalGainsStt`:
   - It asks `optionalRuleOf(RS_CG_TERM, facts)`, with facts `capitalgains.asset` and `trade.transfer.exchange` (an
     absent exchange given as `UNRECORDED`).
   - **No row:** refused, naming the bucket's facts and *"no row of RS-CG-TERM"*.
   - **A row:**
     - long-term if `acquiredOn.plusMonths(months).isBefore(transferredOn)`, else short-term (D-303);
     - a `MEASURE` trace line citing the row: *"Held 3-4-2023 → 10-6-2025: more than 12 months, long-term
       (calendar months, D-303)"*, adding *"; a bonus lot is dated at its ex-date (W-088k4)"* when the acquisition
       is a `BONUS`.
4. **`CapitalGainsNode`:**
   - declares `RS-CG-TERM`;
   - its bucket loop derives the term first, then STT, then the part;
   - `factsOf` and the refusal take the derived term.

   `CapitalGainsStt.of` takes the term as a parameter for its D-294 sentence. Every method stays ≤ 60 lines.
5. **`CapitalGainsInput`:** `Bucket(gain, facts, portfolioRuleRef)`; `BucketFacts.transferredOn` (9 components),
   null-checked.
6. **`CapitalGainsAdapter`:**
   - `SHORT_TERM` and `LONG_TERM` both → `engineBucket(bucket)`, carrying `transferredOn`;
   - `portfolio`'s term is no longer passed;
   - the other three cases are unchanged.
7. **Relock,** one bump: law 2025-26.83 → .84, 2026-27.87 → .88; rule count +1 each.
