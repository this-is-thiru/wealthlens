## Plan for W-289 — hold a trade for an earlier quarter's unperformed corporate action

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a trade must not be applied to lots that a split, bonus
or demerger recorded before it has not yet reshaped. Today only an action in the trade's own quarter holds it
(D-579).

**Findings (read this session, 2026-11-12):**

1. **The hold looks back only to the trade's quarter start.** `filterOutTransaction` (`TemporaryTransactionService:46`)
   holds a trade when `anyCorporateActionToPerform` finds an unperformed split, bonus or demerger. It searches record
   dates from the trade's quarter start to its date (`:76 – 79`, `getQuarterStart` `:120`).
2. **So a trade in a later quarter is not held.** Take a split recorded 20 Mar that is unperformed, and a sale dated
   5 Apr. The sale is applied at once, to pre-split units. Holding 10, selling 4: live gives (10 − 4) × 2 = 12;
   correct is 10 × 2 − 4 = 16 (`assets-rebuild.md`). **Not yet run** — the build's first step proves it red.
3. **Callers:** both flows (`PortfolioService:92`, `:132`); the redrive's still-held check (`:310`, through the
   boolean overload `:68`); and `CorporateActionController:75 – 79`, which answers whether anything is pending.
4. **An action reaches only lots dated before its record date, per broker** (`PortfolioRepository:29`,
   `processStockSplit` per broker). An old action on a stock the user did not hold then reshapes nothing.
5. **Correcting users already affected** needs no new tool: §2.12 (`assets`) after the action is performed, then
   §2.11 (P&L). `trade_outcomes` (§2.2) only if its rows are wrong.

**Choices for the owner:**

- **C1 — which earlier actions hold a trade.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** an unperformed split, bonus or demerger on the stock, recorded on or before the trade's
    date in **any** quarter. The user must also hold, with that broker, a lot dated before the record date — the
    lots the action would reach. The same-quarter rule stays as it is.
  - C1b: any unperformed one from any quarter, whether or not the user held the stock then. Such trades wait until
    the owner performs that quarter, which marks it performed even with nothing held.
  - C1c: refuse the trade (400, naming the action) instead of holding it.
- **C2 — users already affected.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** a read-only runbook count (§3.16): sales dated after an action's record date, in a later
    quarter, of a stock the seller held before it, where that action was performed after the sale was submitted. The
    entry points each one to §2.12 then §2.11.
  - C2b: no count; the owner runs the §2.12 dry run per user.

**Files I will modify:**
- `TemporaryTransactionService` — `anyCorporateActionToPerform` adds the earlier-quarter search (C1a). It reads lots
  through `PortfolioRepository.findByEmailAndStockCodeAndTransactionDateBefore`, filtered by broker.
- Runbook §3.16 (C2a).
- `assets-rebuild.md` — the gap is closed.
- Tests:
  - `PortfolioGapAuditIntegrationTest` or a new class:
    - **red before:** buy 10 on 5 Jan 2026; an unperformed 2:1 split recorded 20 Mar; a sale of 4 on 5 Apr is held
      as temporary. After performing and redriving, 16 are held.
    - A trade with no held lots before the old action is not held.
    - A trade after a performed one is not held.
  - `TemporaryTransactionServiceTest` for the branch.

**Numbers I will use:** none from law; 12 against 16 by hand above.

**Design sections I read:** `assets-rebuild.md` §"A live gap"; D-579; W-213 (D-438) for the split.

**What I am NOT doing:** performing actions automatically; changing the same-quarter rule; correcting stored data.

**How I will verify:**

- Then the change; probe: drop the lot condition, and the "no held lots" case must fail.
- The unit and integration tiers, run from cleared report directories.

**Post-deployment actions (R15):** runbook §3.16, a read-only count (C2a). Any user it finds is corrected through
§2.12 and §2.11, both standing.

**Five roles:**
- **Architect:** the hold reads lots — `portfolio` reading its own repository — and corporate actions, as today. No
  new seam.
- **Product manager:** a user who trades a stock across a quarter boundary before the owner performs the action sees
  the trade wait (temporary) instead of applied wrongly. The message is the existing one.
- **Senior engineer:** one condition added where the hold is decided; the overloads share it.
- **Automation tester:** red before, the probe, and a case for each side of the condition.
- **Manual tester:** on staging, a split recorded last quarter left unperformed, then a sale this quarter. Expect
  "stored as temporary"; perform, redrive, and the holdings show the split quantity less the sale.

**Risks or ambiguities:**
- After deploy, trades of users with old unperformed actions on stocks they hold will wait until the owner performs
  those quarters. That is the intent, but it may surprise. §3.16's count shows how many before they appear.
