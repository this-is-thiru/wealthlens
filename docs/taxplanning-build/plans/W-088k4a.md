## Plan for W-088k4a — the allotment date on a bonus action

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** `corporate_action` records a bonus's allotment date, and
performing a bonus dates the new lot and its transaction by it, so a bonus share's holding period starts where the
law starts it (Explanation 1(i)(f); s.2(101)(c)(C)(IV)). A bonus missing the date is refused, never guessed (D-306).

**This plan raises C1 and C2.**

### What I read (session 27, 2026-10-21)

- **Background:** D-306, `plans/W-088k4.md`, and `sources/2026-10-21-holding-period.md` ("The bonus-date gap").
- **`corporate/entity/CorporateActionEntity`** (`@Field` names in snake_case) and **`corporate/dto/
  CorporateActionDto`.**
  - The DTO has `@JsonFormat` dates.
  - `getAsEntity` (`:42–58`) copies the fields, but not `date`.
- **`CorporateActionService`:**
  - `performPendingCorporateAction` (`:200–227`) dispatches `BONUS` to `processBonusShares(email, action, broker)`
    (`:314–334`). That method finds the holdings up to the record date, then calls the four-argument
    `processBonusShares` (`:336–370`) per broker.
  - The four-argument method dates the bonus `AssetEntity` at `getExDate()` (`:357`), and `processBonusSharesTxns`
    (`:372–395`) dates the bonus `TransactionEntity` likewise (`:388`).
  - `updateLastlyPerformedCorporateAction(…, getExDate(), …)` (`:332`) records when the action was performed, which
    drives duplicate detection. **It is not the lot's date, and it stays.**
- **Tests:** `integration/CorporateActionIntegrationTest`.
  - `performPendingCorporateActions_singleBroker_success` (`:709–754`) and `_allBrokers_success` (`:759–`) perform a
    `BONUS` with record and ex-dates only. Under the refusal they need an allotment date in their data.
  - **No test asserts a bonus lot's date today.**
- **The engine:** `node/CapitalGainsTerm` appends *"; a bonus lot is dated at its ex-date, not its allotment
  (W-088k4)"*, and `CapitalGainsNodeTest.evaluate_whenABonusLotIsHeld_tracesTheExDateApproximation` asserts it.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — where the refusal sits:**
  - **(a) recommended:** in `processBonusShares(email, action, broker)`, before any holding is read: *"The bonus on
    RELIANCE (record date 2024-01-15) has no allotment date; a bonus share's holding period runs from its allotment
    (Income-tax Act, 1961 Explanation 1(i)(f) to s.2(42A); Income-tax Act, 2025 s.2(101)(c)(C)(IV)) — add it before
    performing"*. It is an `IllegalArgumentException`, so 400.
  - **(b)** at `POST /corporate-action/add`. An action is often entered when announced, before allotment, so this
    would block entry.
- **C2 — the engine's caveat until W-088k4b's correction is logged:**
  - **(a) recommended:** *"; a bonus lot processed before W-088k4a may be dated at its ex-date (W-088k4b)"*. The
    engine still cannot tell the two apart, so the caveat stays on every bonus bucket, worded for what remains true.
  - **(b)** remove it now. That would be untrue for old lots until k4b runs.

### The build

1. **`CorporateActionEntity`:** `@Field("allotment_date") private LocalDate allotmentDate;`, with a one-line comment
   on why: the date a bonus share's holding period runs from.
2. **`CorporateActionDto`:** an `allotmentDate` field (`@JsonFormat` like its siblings), copied in `getAsEntity`.
3. **`CorporateActionService`:**
   - `processBonusShares(email, action, broker)` refuses a null `allotmentDate` (C1a);
   - the four-argument method dates the lot at `getAllotmentDate()`;
   - `processBonusSharesTxns` dates the transaction at it.
4. **`node/CapitalGainsTerm`:** the caveat's text (C2a).
5. **The sources file:** the gap section gains *"From W-088k4a, `corporate_action.allotment_date`; lots processed
   earlier keep the ex-date until W-088k4b"*.
6. **Runbook §3.6:** a read-only list of `BONUS` actions without `allotment_date`:
   `db.corporate_action.find({ type: "BONUS", allotment_date: { $exists: false } }, { stock_code: 1, record_date: 1,
   ex_date: 1 })`. Performing any of them is refused until the date is entered, and W-088k4b corrects their lots. WL-A-2
   is republished.
