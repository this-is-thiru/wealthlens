**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** check a Form No. 130 across its parts:
- the employer's own deduction in Part B against the net tax payable in Part C (D-750 C3a: row 21, within ₹10);
- Part A's "Period with the employer" against the tax year it certifies.

### Findings

1. **Part A's period is text today.** `FORM_130.json` holds `partA.employer.period` (item 8, "Period with the
   employer", kind TEXT). The form prints "(dd/mm/yyyy) to (dd/mm/yyyy)" in its value cell
   (`sources/2026-11-16-form-130.md:17`). `CertificateValueKind` has AMOUNT, YES_NO, TEXT and NONE
   (`CertificateValueKind.java`), and `ExtractedValue` has `MoneyRead`, `FlagRead` and `TextRead`
   (`ExtractedValue.java:9`, `:17`, `:21`). `TaxYearId` has `startsOn()`, `endsOn()` and `contains(LocalDate)`
   (`TaxYearId.java:47` – `:56`).
2. **The Part B ↔ Part C relation is not law.** It is the design's validation (G5.2, corrected by D-750 C3a): Part B's
   "Amount of tax deducted" Total (`partB.summary.total.taxDeducted`) ≈ row 21 "Net tax payable (17-18-19-20)". The
   specified bank's Annexure-II prints the same shape: row 17 "Net tax payable (15-16)".
3. **A difference here is not a misread.** An employer can deduct more or less than the year's liability (a late
   declaration, a mid-year change), so the two figures can both be printed correctly and still differ. G5.2 lists the
   check with the arithmetic ones, but its meaning differs.
4. **Where the reading reaches.** `ExtractedValue` is matched in `CertificateReading`, `Inference`, `Contradictions`,
   `CellReading` and `TableReading` (grep). The `kind` strings are switched in `CertificateReading.read` and
   `TableReading.emit`.
