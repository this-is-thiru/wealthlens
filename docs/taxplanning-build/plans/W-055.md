### Findings

1. **The shape is done; the parameters are not.** `W-048` built and tested the formula. What's missing is
   rows that give it the Act's thresholds, per regime and year, and a test that the shipped rows relieve
   correctly.
2. **Four statements, two parameterisations.** The two first-schedule tables are identical
   (50L/1Cr/2Cr/5Cr), and the two new-regime tables are identical (50L/1Cr/2Cr, **no 5Cr** — H-1). So,
   as `W-054` did, each regime is **one row with one version spanning both years**, citing s.2/Part I-A
   as `provision1961` and s.3/Part I-B as `provision2025`. "Four parameterisations" in the row becomes
   two rows × two years; nothing is lost.
3. **The checkpoint's question: restate the thresholds, or derive them from the ladders?** They
   coincide in number but not in meaning. Table 2 tests **total income** against all its thresholds,
   while `W-054` put 2Cr and 5Cr on the **excluding** ladder (D-084). Deriving the relief thresholds
   from band lower bounds would take two of them from a ladder on a different base, and it would be
   correct only by coincidence. Also, `ThresholdLadderCeiling` refuses an empty threshold list, and its
   Javadoc makes thresholds its parameter ("the thresholds are the parameter, this is the shape").
4. **No slab-rate limit.** Table 2's W bounds "income-tax and surcharge". There are no words limiting
   it to slab-rate tax (unlike s.156(3)), so `limitedToSlabRateTax: false`, stated.
5. **Standing rules (D-043).**
   1. Coverage: none — these are Finance Act provisions, not Act sections (as `W-054`).
   2. Catalogue: none, for the same reason.
   3. Both years: finding 2, one spanning version, both sourced this session.
   4. Concept IDs: C2.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — where the thresholds live (finding 3).**
  - **(a) recommended:** the relief rows **restate** Table 2's column C. `W-056` reads them from the
    relief row, not from band lower bounds. An erratum on the `W-056` row replaces "the relief threshold
    is the band's lower bound" with this. `SurchargeReliefTest` adds a **drift guard**: each regime's
    thresholds equal the non-zero band lower bounds of its two `W-054` ladders, taken together. Then a
    future Finance Act that moves one and not the other fails the build instead of passing silently.
  - **(b)** `W-056` derives them from the ladders, as D-050 planned. That means no relief rows (or a
    `ThresholdLadderCeiling` taught to accept no thresholds), and the relief would be tested against
    the excluding base for 2Cr/5Cr, which is the coincidence in finding 3.
- **C2 — IDs, named by the Act's words (D-035; W-045d2's precedent: "the Act does not say
  'marginal'"):** `RATE-SUR-FIRST-SCHEDULE-CEILING` and `RATE-SUR-NEW-TAX-REGIME-CEILING`. The Act calls
  W "the total amount beyond which the total amount payable … shall not exceed". The rulebook gets a
  rename line mapping `RATE-SUR-MR-001` → both.
  - Regimes: `OnlyIn` their regime; no conditions (as `W-054`).
  - Provisions: FA 2026 **Part I-A Para F Table 2 Sl. 1** / **s.2(5) Table Sl. 6** (`provision1961`);
    FA 2026 **Part I-B Para F Table 2 Sl. 1** / **s.3(5) Table Sl. 6** (`provision2025`).
- **C3 — I3:** the two rows join `NOT_YET_EVALUATED` under D-084's existing exception, with no new
  decision. `W-056`'s Verify already empties the set.
- **C4 — the lock:** `2025-26` → `law.4`, `2026-27` → `law.13`; `RuleIds` regenerated; the inventory
  tests (14 → 16 rules).
