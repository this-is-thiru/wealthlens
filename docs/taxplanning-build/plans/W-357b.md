### Decisions (for approval with this plan)

- **C1 — the split.** **C1a (recommended):** one item per module, **W-357b1 – W-357b10**, in this order: `finance`, `insurance`, `helper`,
  `auth`, `corporate`, `brokercharges` (replacing `docs/charges-engine/`), `portfolio` (replacing `docs/trade-ledger/`), `shared`,
  `taxengine`, `taxplanning`. Smallest first, so the format is settled on a small one. **Each item re-homes the plan and doc citations in its
  own module's tests**, then deletes any kept plan or review nothing cites any more. **C1b:** the ten docs first, then all re-homing in one
  last item.
- **C2 — the template** (`docs/modules/<module>.md`). **C2a (recommended):** these sections, in order, each claim citing `path:line` read at
  the time:
  1. **Purpose:** why the module exists, in two or three sentences.
  2. **Boundaries:** its `allowedDependencies` and who depends on it.
  3. **Routes:** zone, method, path, request type, answer and refusals, with their statuses and messages.
  4. **Stored data:** collections, fields, indexes, encrypted fields; "none" said plainly.
  5. **Behaviour:** the rules and formulas, each with one worked example by hand.
  6. **Configuration:** properties and their shipped values.
  7. **Tests:** which classes cover what.
  8. **Known gaps:** each one a placed backlog row.
  9. **Not covered here:** what another doc owns.

  **C2b:** a lighter doc (purpose, routes, data) and no worked examples.
- **C3 — what a doc finds wrong.** **C3a (recommended):** written under "Known gaps" and **placed as a row**, never fixed inside a doc item (a
  doc item changes no code). **C3b:** fixed in the doc item.
