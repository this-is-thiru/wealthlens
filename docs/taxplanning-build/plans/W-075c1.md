## Plan for W-075c1 — Chapter VIII: disability and treatment (80DD, 80DDB, 80U), TY 2026-27

**What this item is for (one sentence):** compute s.154 (a disabled assessee), s.127 (a disabled dependant) and s.128
(treatment of a specified disease) for TY 2026-27, in the pack and `ChapterEightNode`.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (Gazette CG-DL-E-22082025-265620, this session)

- **s.154** (pp.185–186): *"An individual, being resident in India, who is certified by a medical authority, at any
  time during the tax year, as a person with disability or person with severe disability, shall be allowed a
  deduction of ₹ 75000 or ₹ 125000, respectively"*; the certificate furnished with the return (2); meanings from s.127
  (3).
- **s.127** (pp.162–164): a resident individual or HUF, *"a deduction up to ₹ 75000 … if during that year he has —
  (a) incurred expenditure for the medical treatment (including nursing), training and rehabilitation of a
  dependant, being a person with disability; or (b) paid or deposited any amount under a scheme … for the maintenance
  of a dependant"* (with (2)'s scheme conditions); *"₹ 125000"* for severe disability (3); certificate (6)–(7); (8)
  *"The dependant … shall not include a person who has claimed deduction under section 154"*; (9)(b) "dependant" —
  for an individual, *"the spouse, children, parents, brothers and sisters … dependant wholly or mainly on such
  individual … for his support and maintenance"*; (9)(g) severe — 80% or more.
- **s.128** (pp.164–165): a resident assessee, *"the amount actually paid during the tax year or a sum of ₹ 40000,
  whichever is less … for the medical treatment of such disease or ailment as may be prescribed — (a) for himself or a
  dependant"*; a prescribed specialist's prescription (2); *"reduced by any amount received under an insurance … or
  reimbursed by an employer"* (3); **(4)** *"If the amount actually paid is in respect of the assessee or his dependant
  … and who is senior citizen, the amount of deduction … shall be substituted with "₹ 100000" for "₹40000""*; (5)(a)
  "dependant" as s.127(9).
- **Code:** `Individual.disability` (`DisabilitySeverity` DISABILITY / SEVERE_DISABILITY) and residence;
  `FamilySnapshot.Member` relationship, dependant, disability, `residentInIndia` (W-075b); conditions `IsResident`,
  `IsCertifiedDisabled(minimum)` (reads the assessee only), `AgeAtLeast(years, INSURED_PERSON)`.

### Findings

1. **s.154 needs no new input**: the assessee's own certified severity and residence decide it.
2. **s.127's "up to ₹ 75000"** reads as a ceiling on the amount spent or deposited; whether it is instead a fixed
   amount whenever something is spent (the 1961 s.80DD position, unread here) is not settled by the words.
3. **s.127 does not say whether two disabled dependants give one deduction or two.**
4. **s.128(4) does not say which limit applies when treatment is paid for a senior and a non-senior in one year.**
5. **"Prescribed" diseases and specialists** (s.128(1)–(2), Rules 2026) are entry conditions; the Rules are not read
   here.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — s.154 (80U):** rows `DED-DISABILITY` (`Fixed` 75,000; conditions `IsResident`, `IsCertifiedDisabled
  DISABILITY`) and `DED-DISABILITY-SEVERE` (`Fixed` 125,000; `IsResident`, `IsCertifiedDisabled SEVERE_DISABILITY`);
  the node takes the severe row where its conditions hold, else the other where they hold. No input.
- **C2 — s.127's "up to" (80DD):** **(a) recommended:** the lesser of the amount spent or deposited and the limit
  (the words' ceiling), raising **OQ-19**. **(b)** The full limit whenever anything is spent.
- **C3 — more than one disabled dependant:** **(a) recommended:** refused (OQ-19). **(b)** One deduction across all,
  the severe limit if any is severe.
- **C4 — s.127 input and rows:** `EnteredFacts.disabledDependantSupport` — `DisabledDependantSupport(int
  familyMember, Money spentOrDeposited, boolean dependantClaimsSection154)`; the member must be a dependant spouse,
  child, parent or sibling with a certified disability; (8) refuses a dependant who claims s.154. Rows
  `DED-DISABLED-DEPENDANT` (`Fixed` 75,000) and `-SEVERE` (125,000), picked by the member's severity.
- **C5 — s.128 (80DDB) input and rows:** `EnteredFacts.specifiedDiseaseTreatment` — `SpecifiedDiseaseTreatment(boolean
  forAssessee, Optional<Integer> familyMember, Money paid, Money reimbursed)`; a dependant as s.127(9). Rows
  `DED-SPECIFIED-DISEASE` (`Fixed` 40,000) and `-SENIOR` (100,000; `AgeAtLeast 60 INSURED_PERSON`, residence as in
  W-075b); the deduction is the lesser of (paid − reimbursed) and the row. Mixed senior and non-senior treatment in
  one year: **(a) recommended:** refused, raising **OQ-20**; **(b)** the senior limit over all of it.
- **C6 — common:** all six rows `ExceptIn` NEW_TAX_REGIME (s.202(2)(a)(xii) keeps none of them), TY 2026-27,
  `SOURCE_CHECKED`; an assessee not resident in India gets none (s.127(1), 128(1), 154(1)); a HUF refused (as W-075b);
  the certificate and prescription are entry conditions, named in the rows' notes; new-regime nil lines where
  something is entered.
