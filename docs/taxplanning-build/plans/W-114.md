## Plan for W-114 — the tax calendar (F30)

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** list a draft's dated actions for the year. Each entry is
built from the person's own facts and carries what missing it costs, measured by computing the draft twice
(`D-MARGINAL`). A past entry shows what actually happened. It is served at
`GET /tax/user/{email}/drafts/{draftId}/calendar`.

**Design sections I read:** `01-PRD.md:201` (F30.1 – F30.4); `20-product-gaps.md` F30 (lines 68 – 100);
`23-architecture-consolidation.md` A6 and A10 (the calendar row); `00-SUMMARY.md:360` (`D-MARGINAL`: every saving is
`compute(A) − compute(B)`); `02-TECH-SPEC.md:367` (the route).

**What the probe shows:** measured as of today, December costs nothing, because its interest has not accrued. The
cost has to be measured as of the due date. Measured that way, missing December costs 7,934 − 2,670 = **₹5,264**,
filing a day late costs 14,688 − 7,934 = **₹6,754**, and missing the carve-out after a January sale costs
7,934 − 877 = **₹7,057**.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: which entries.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** three kinds, the ones with data and a measurable cost. These are each advance-tax
    instalment, the s.425(4) paid-by day where a relieved income arose after an instalment date (F30.3), and the
    return's due date. The rest go to placed rows. **W-114a** covers the 80C / 80D year end and the LTA block end:
    the data exists, and the cost is the tax on the unused headroom, which needs the optimiser's pricing. **W-114b**
    covers the employer proof cut-off, the revised-return window and the regime election, which have no data yet.
  - C1b: every row of F30's table now. That needs the missing data first, and is too large for one item.
- **C2: how a cost is measured.** Both runs use the draft as it stands, as of the later of the return's due date and
  today, with the return assumed furnished on the due date unless the draft states when.
  - **C2a (recommended):**
    - **Instalment:** the run with that instalment's `stillOwed` paid on its `dueOn`, against the run without it.
      The entry reads "pay ₹X by then; missing it costs ₹Y".
    - **Carve-out:** the run with the advance tax still owed at the last instalment paid on the paid-by day.
    - **Due date:** the run furnished the day after the due date.
    - **The cost is the change in interest and fees only,** never in tax: the money paid is not a cost.
  - C2b: pay only the instalment's own share (`dueByThen` less the previous one), not the catch-up. This gives a
    smaller figure, but it is not the action that stops the interest.
- **C3: past entries (F30.4).** An entry dated before today shows what happened: an instalment's `dueByThen` and
  `paidByThen`, and the return's `furnishedOn` with the interest and fees actually charged. It keeps the measured
  cost of having missed it.
  - **C3a (recommended):** as above.
  - C3b: past entries carry no cost.
- **C4: the carve-out's date.** It lives only inside the engine.
  - **C4a (recommended):** expose it. `ComputedTax.AdvanceTax` gains `Optional<LocalDate> reliefPaidBy`: the
    rule's day in the year, present only where a relieved income arose after an instalment date. `DefermentRelief`
    already decides this per date. The change is to the taxengine API (`InterestAndFees` carries it to
    `ComputedTax`), so it needs C4a's approval.
  - C4b: leave F30.3 to a placed row.
- **C5: storage.**
  - **C5a (recommended):** compute on every request and store nothing, as W-113 does (D-664 C2a). The cost is up to
    seven runs per view (one base run, four instalments, the carve-out, the due date). An instalment already paid
    in full, or a carve-out that does not apply, skips its run.
  - C5b: materialise in `tax_projections` (A10).
- **C6: regime.** The draft's elected regime, else the year's default, as in W-113 (D-664 C3a).

**Risks or ambiguities:**
- **The assumed filing date.** Measuring as of the due date assumes the return is furnished on time. The response
  states `measuredAsOf` so that assumption is visible.
- **The carve-out cost overlaps.** It includes s.424 interest that any payment by 31 March would avoid
  (`ADV-MAR31-001`), so it can overlap the March instalment's cost. The entries are separate actions, not a sum,
  and the response says so.
