## Plan for W-117 — the claim catalogue: design and split first

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a pack-shipped catalogue that tells a person, for each
claim the engine computes, how to make it: the channel, the steps and who does each, the evidence, and whether it
survives being missing from Form 16. It is DR 22's F40 content, which W-118's endpoint will serve.

**Probe (no code run; DR 22, the content layer and the rule data, read 2026-11-14):**
- **DR 22 asks for more than the row says.** C4's entry has a plain name, what it is, the channel (E / R / R+ / T / P),
  ordered steps (each with an actor, a deadline and what it produces), whom to ask, evidence (each with what closes
  it), amounts, common mistakes, the "missing from Form 16" verdict and materiality. C8 adds four invariants:
  - **I23:** every deduction or exemption rule has an entry.
  - **I24:** the channel is consistent with the Chapter VIII Part; Part C carries the s.122(5) late-return warning.
  - **I25:** every evidence item names what closes it.
  - **I26:** every dated step resolves on the tax calendar (F30).
  C8 also lists tests T22-1 to T22-12.
- **I23 against the row.** The row says 12 entries, but about 74 of the 244 rules are deductions or exemptions (counted by rule-id prefix, so approximate). Full
  I23 means roughly 74 entries.
- **What exists already:**
  - The section catalogue (`content/sections/`, 66 entries, `SectionCatalogue`) gives each section a `plainName` and a
    `summary` in plain words. That is DR 22's `plainName` and `whatItIs` again.
  - It also has a figure-free, placeholder pattern (`{{RULE-ID.field}}`, `TYPED_FIGURE` refuses digits, ₹, % and
    lakh).
  - `DeadlineSpec` is already shared for the catalogue (`rules/regime/DeadlineSpec.java`).
  - The landlord-PAN threshold is rule data (`SAL-HOUSE-RENT-LANDLORD-PAN`).
- **DR 22's entries are not content-ready as written.** They hold figures (₹1,50,000; ₹1,00,000; 10%), form numbers
  ("Form No. 124") and design-era rule ids (`SAL-HRA-001`, `DED-80C-001`) where the pack has others
  (`SAL-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE`). Each entry must be re-keyed and have its figures moved to placeholders.
- **Sourcing.** The legal bases DR 22 marks VERIFIED cite sections and rules. Under the Gazette-only rule, each
  entry's legal statements must trace to a rule's Gazette provenance already in the pack, or be read from a Gazette
  copy when the entry is built. Steps are DR 22's `RECOMMENDATION`s and are marked so.
- **Materiality** (C6) needs a computed tax effect, so it is not content.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: the split.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** four items, each with its own plan, sourcing, red test, review and commit:
    - **W-117a, the frame:** the entry model and its JSON, loading, the compiler's checks (I24 – I26; placeholders;
      no figures), and two pilot entries that differ most: HRA (channel E) and 80G (channel T).
    - **W-117b, channel E:** 80C, own NPS, 80D, home-loan interest, leave travel, professional tax.
    - **W-117c, channels R, R+ and P:** 80TTA / 80TTB, 80U / 80DD, employer NPS, the regime election.
    - **W-117d, I23:** the coverage check (C3).
  - C1b: one item.
- **C2: "what it is".**
  - **C2a (recommended):** the entry points to its section entry (`SEC-…`), whose `plainName` and `summary` already
    say it, so there is one source of those words.
  - C2b: the entry repeats its own `plainName` and `whatItIs`, as DR 22 C4 writes.
- **C3: I23's scope.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** every deduction or exemption rule either has an entry or is listed in content as "not yet
    catalogued", and a test fails on a rule that is neither. No silent gap, and the list only shrinks.
  - C3b: all about 74 entries before I23 holds.
  - C3c: no I23 for now.
- **C4: figures and form numbers.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** no digits in the catalogue, as SC-2 requires. Figures come in by `{{RULE-ID.field}}` from
    rule data, as the section catalogue does, and forms are named in words ("the declaration your employer gives you
    for this").
  - C4b: a form-number placeholder from a new forms register (more data to build first).
- **C5: materiality.**
  - **C5a (recommended):** moved to W-118. It is computed per computation, not content.
  - C5b: in W-117a.

**What I am NOT doing here:** any code or content. On approval, this places W-117a – d on the board, and W-117 closes
by the split. W-118 (the endpoint) and W-120 (the documentation checklist) stay after it.

**Post-deployment actions (R15):** none.

**Roles:**
- **Architect:** the catalogue is pack content beside the rules (DR 22 C4), checked by the compiler. It reuses the
  section catalogue's words and placeholders instead of adding a second vocabulary.
- **PM:** F40 is MVP (DR 22 C8: 1.00). The two pilots prove the channels that differ most: E survives a missing Form 16
  entry, T does not.
- **Senior engineer:** the frame once, then entries as data. Most of the work is sourcing, not code.
- **Automation tester:** I24 – I26 as compiler refusals, each with a red check; T22-1 – 3, 10 and 11 against the
  pilots and batches.
- **Manual tester:** read each entry as the person it is for, and check every step is something they can do.

**Risks:**
- Content-heavy items have needed sourcing rounds. Each batch plan lists the Gazette copies it reads before any entry
  is written.
- DR 22's figures and design ids must not leak in; the compiler's no-digits check enforces that.
