## Plan for W-093a — ITR-2's remaining schedules, and the per-item rows, as cells (TY 2025-26)

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** finish ITR-2 as cells. That covers Schedules CG, CYLA, BFLA,
CFL, SI and EI, plus the rows moved here by W-092's Q10: HP per property, OS 1 – 3 by kind, and ITR-1 C3(a)(i), (ii)
(D-654, D-655).

**Sources:** `sources/2026-11-13-itr-schedules-ty-2025-26.md` (Gazette 271435 pp.42 – 48 CG, 55 – 57 CFL, CYLA and
BFLA, 61 SI, 62 EI; 271423 p.18 C3(a)).

**Probe findings (read, not run; nothing changed):**
1. **No line names its item.** `TraceLine` carries node, rule, kind, in, out and limit, but no item.
   `HousePropertyNode` emits each property's lines in a loop (`HousePropertyNode.java:82`), so the trace cannot say
   which property a figure belongs to. The same holds for other sources' items.
2. **Item keys on lines are wide.** Putting an item key on every line touches 83 `traceLine(...)` calls and 73
   `TraceLine` constructions (57 main, 16 test).
3. **Capital gains already keep items.** `capitalgains.income` holds `bucketOutcomes` (`CapitalGainsHead.java:27`): per
   bucket, its facts, gain, charges and term. Sale consideration and cost are the bucket's inputs, not outputs.
4. **Schedules hold fixed paths only.** HP repeats "for each property", CG by asset and term, and Schedule S by employer.
   No schedule can repeat a group of cells per item today.

**Sizing (said plainly, D-622):** CG alone is seven Gazette pages of cells (A1 – A8, B1 – B12, C, D, E and the
set-off matrix). With repeating groups and a per-item source, this is the largest item of the batch. C1 and C2 decide
whether it stays one item.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: where per-item figures come from.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** node values carry the per-item breakdown. `houseproperty.income` gains each property's

  - C1b: an item key on every `TraceLine` (about 156 call sites), with `FromRules` per item.
  - C1c: every per-item row is ManualEntry for now; this item fills only the totals and set-off rows, from figures

- **C2: rows that repeat per item.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** a schedule may hold a **group** that repeats once per item of a kind (house property,
    other-source item, capital-gains bucket, employer). Its paths carry the item's number (`HP.1[2].a`), and its cells
    read `FromItems`.
  - C2b: fixed paths only; per-item rows are ManualEntry (goes with C1c).
- **C3: Schedule CG's depth.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** fill the sections the engine computes — A (short-term: s.111A equity, at applicable rate)
    and B (long-term: s.112A and s.112), each bucket's sale consideration, cost, expenses and gain, and E (the

  - C3b: every row of CG as cells, most ManualEntry.
- **C4: set-off schedules.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** CYLA and BFLA read the `setoff` node (`HeadsAfterSetOff`: totals and `LossesAcrossYears`).
    CFL reads its losses to carry forward by head and year. Each row is checked by a persona with a house-property loss
    and a brought-forward capital loss; there is none today, and W-092's B-TI 6 and 8 wait on the same cases.

**Build decisions (recorded as made):**
- **C1a, how:** a structured value on the same node, not a sibling node. `NodeValue` is sealed with `asMoney()`, so
  `HousePropertyHead` returns the head's total and only the two casts move (`SetOffNode:106`,
  `ReturnFactsProjection.money`), plus `TaxCalculator.computedAmounts`. That avoids a law-layer change. Done for house
  property; other sources next.
- **HP 1h for own residences:** the s.24(b) limit is applied to own residences together. The amount allowed is
  apportioned by each house's interest before the limit, and the last house takes the rounding remainder (owner:
  "Apportion"), so the properties' 1k add up to the head to the paisa.
- **HP 1a / 1b:** the rent compared with the lettable value is net of rent not realised (1961 s.23(1) Explanation).
  Where the rent is the measure, the unrealised rent is shown at 1b and added back at 1a; otherwise 1b is nil.
  1a − 1b − 1c is the annual value either way.
- **OS by kind, not per item:** Schedule OS prints its rows by kind (dividends, savings interest, deposits, family
  pension …), so `othersources.income` became an `OtherSourcesHead`: the total plus gross by kind. Item kinds use their
  names, plus `PROVIDENT_FUND_INTEREST`, `GIFTS`, `BUSINESS_TRUST` and `LIFE_POLICY`. OS needs no repeating group.
  Its deductions u/s 57 are read from their rows' lines (`FromRules`). Done.
- **Step 2 (C2a) built:** `ScheduleGroup` (item kind, path, fields) on `ItrSchedule.groups`. Paths print as
  `HP.1[n].e`, n from 1. `FromItemFigure(figure)` reads the group's item, and I21 refuses it outside a group.
  `FromOtherSourceKinds(kinds)` adds the gross by kind. The calculator hands the mapper each house property's figures
  as `HOUSE_PROPERTY` items. In `TaxCalculator`, the defensive `instanceof` checks became direct casts: those nodes
  always produce these values, and "nothing silent" rules out a default.
- **Coverage, measured on taxengine tests alone:** rules 0.8624 / 0.9508. Outside rules, 0.9000 / 0.9639 against
  0.90 / 0.95, at the floor; CI's tax-planning tests add to it. Step 4's loss personas cover `TaxCalculator:159` (a head
  with a loss).
- **Step 4's persona found a W-092 defect, now fixed.** Anand under the TY 2025-26 old regime, with an own residence
  (₹2,50,000 interest, capped at ₹2,00,000) and a ₹30,000 short-term loss carried from TY 2023-24: ITR-2 is chosen,
  and every B-TI line matches the hand figures (5,50,000 / 0 / 1,04,000 / 6,54,000 / 2,00,000 / 4,54,000, GTI 4,54,000,
  VI-A 90,000, total income 3,64,000). But `SETOFF_CURRENT_YEAR` (B-TI 6) read ₹4,00,000: a set-off row writes a MEASURE
  line for the amount allowed and a SET_OFF line for the amount taken, and `FromRules` added both. `FromRules` now takes
  an optional `kind`, and B-TI 6 and 8 read SET_OFF lines only. This corrects D-655's "not proven by a case".
