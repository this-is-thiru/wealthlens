## Plan for W-178 — equity futures and options

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** an equity futures or options trade at any of the three
brokers should be priced from the broker's published charges and the law's own rates and dates, not recorded as a
₹0 gap.

**Source:** `03-WORK-ITEMS.md` W-178; `reviews/2026-10-28-charge-coverage.md` G4; D-383.

**The engine:**
- `TradeSegment.FUTURES` / `OPTIONS` exist, but nothing else in the code names them.
- Turnover is price × quantity, so an option's premium × quantity is its premium turnover, which is the base the law
  uses for options.
- `ChargeEvent` has `BUY` and `SELL` but **no exercise or assignment event**. STT on an exercised option (0.125 %,
  then 0.15 %, on intrinsic value) and SEBI's notional-value fee on exercise **cannot be priced** (see NOT doing).

**A defect found in shipped cards (C3):**
- Every **2026-03 cash card** charges NSE's combined 0.0030699 % **and** a separate IPFT 0.0001 % line, so IPFT is
  counted twice from 1-3-2026: ₹10 per crore, or ₹0.10 on a ₹1 lakh trade, plus GST on it.
- The seven cards: `ZERODHA_EQ_DELIVERY_2026_03`, `_2026_06`, `ZERODHA_EQ_INTRADAY_2026_03`,

### What I am NOT doing

- **An option's exercise or assignment** (STT 0.125 % / 0.15 %, SEBI's notional fee): there is no event for it. This
  is a gap, noted on the options cards. Adding an event is its own item if you want one, and I would propose it, not
  build it.
- BSE F&O (W-179 S1). Currency and commodity derivatives, which the app has no segment for. The 2026-03 cash cards'
  double count (W-185, C3a).
- How a futures or options position is held, sold or valued in the portfolio. This item prices charges only.

   shows paise for F&O stamp duty, that is a convention question for all cards, not this item's.
