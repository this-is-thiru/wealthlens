## Plan for W-045o2 — the coverage register for the Income-tax Act, 1961 as in force for AY 2026-27 (D-066)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** the 1961 Act still governs TY 2025-26 (D-041). Its sections
should be in the register the way the 2025 Act's now are: every one listed from a source, and every
one classified by a rule you approved.

### What I read

- **The `W-045o2` row:** the list is reconstructed from the thc.nic.in copy plus amending Acts since;
  omitted sections are not registered; classification uses an owner-approved chapter table. Verify:
  the count equals the list, the list is continuous, nothing is unclassified, counts are reported.
- **D-038, D-066**, and `W-045o`'s method and code (`content/acts/`, `CoverageRegister`'s Act check).
- **Probe of the thc.nic.in copy** (`Central Governmental Acts/Income Tax Act 1961_.pdf`, 955 pp.):
  - **it is current to the Finance Act, 2019 (Act 7 of 2019)**: every 2019 footnote cites Act 7, and
    none cites a later Act;
  - its arrangement of sections has about **860 entries, about 125 of them "[Omitted.]"**, in about
    **51 chapters** (lettered ones included: VI-A, XII-B…XII-H, XIV-B, XX-A…);
  - the in-force base is therefore about 735 sections as of February 2019.

### Findings

1. **The amendment chain is the real work.** Every Act since Act 7 of 2019 that inserted or omitted a
   section of the 1961 Act must be found and read, with the date each change took effect. The
   **known candidates**:
   - already downloaded in `W-045n`: Finance (No. 2) Act 2019, Finance Acts 2020–2025, Finance
     (No. 2) Act 2024;
   - **not yet fetched**: the Taxation Laws (Amendment) Act 2019 (the company-rate sections), the
     Taxation and Other Laws (Relaxation and Amendment of Certain Provisions) Act 2020, the
     Taxation Laws (Amendment) Act 2021, and the Jan Vishwas Act 2023;
   - **possibly more**: a 2025 "Taxation Laws" Bill that amends the 1961 Act turned up in
     `W-045o`'s search, and I haven't checked whether it was enacted.

   India Code's amendment history, which would give the complete list, is unreachable (403/504). So
   the list of amending Acts is itself **a finding to source**. I'll search for every Act of
   2019–2026 that amends "the Income-tax Act, 1961", and record how I searched, so a gap is visible
   rather than silent.
2. **"In force for AY 2026-27" needs dates.** A section inserted *with effect from* a date after
   1-4-2026 doesn't count; one omitted with effect from a date on or before 1-4-2026 doesn't either.
   Each change carries its own date in the amending Act.
3. **Two approved rules conflict.** B3 (the list must be continuous) and B4 (omitted sections aren't
   registered) can't both hold: ss.18–21 are omitted, so a list without them has a gap.
4. **Lettered sections** (80C, 115BAC, 10AA…) are most of the Act. Continuity can only be checked for
   plain numbers. A lettered section is checked by being present in the arrangement or in an
   insertion I read, not by sequence.

### A. Choices (recommended first)

- **A1 — findings 3 and 4.**
  - **(a) recommended:** the Act list carries **every** section number in the arrangement plus
    insertions, **with omitted ones marked `"omitted": true`** and the date and source of the
    omission. The list stays continuous, and the register must equal the list minus the omitted
    sections. That's a small code change in `CoverageRegister`, about 15 lines.
  - **(b)** Drop the continuity check for the 1961 Act.
- **A2 — the shape of the item.**
  - **(a) recommended:** one item with **two stops**:
    1. after the reconstructed list (you see the chain and the effective dates);
    2. after the chapter table (as in `W-045o`).
  - **(b)** Split it into `W-045o2a` (the list) and `W-045o2b` (classification).
- **A3 — the chapter table** uses the same classes and tiers as `W-045o`, and **the three existing
  1961 rows are kept**: 115BAC `MODELLED`; 80C and 10 `PLANNED`. Where a 1961 section has an obvious
  2025-Act counterpart that `W-045o` classified, I'll propose the same classification and cite the
  counterpart's number only when the rulebook's `DED-MAP-*` or R-tables state it. **No section
  mapping is invented.**

`./mvnw test -pl backend -Dtest=CoverageRegisterTest`, then the unit tier.

**Size:** about 15 lines of code, a tool, and about 750 register lines. **The research is the risk**, not

### Risks or ambiguities

- **Completeness of the amendment chain** (finding 1). If an amending Act is missed, the list is wrong
  in a way no check can catch. The mitigation is a logged search. It is not a guarantee.
- **The thc.nic.in copy isn't a gazette original** and its currency comes from its own footnotes.
- **This is a lot of classification** (about 750 sections). The chapter table makes it about 51 rows
  plus exceptions.

## Step 2 — the reconstructed list and its chain, for review

**Result:** 951 entries (plain numbers continuous 1–298), **131 omitted, 820 in force for AY 2026-27**,
in 52 chapters (the 2019 base's 51, plus Chapter XIX-AA inserted by FA 2021). It's built by
`tools/build_act_1961.py`, and each change since 2019 is an entry in its `CHAIN` table with the
amending Act and page. The built JSON is parked outside the repo until step 4, as in `W-045o`.

**How the chain was found.** Two independent candidate sources:
1. a scan of every amending Act read (F(No.2)A 2019, TLA 2019, FA 2020, TOLA 2020, FA 2021,
   TLA 2021 (**Act 34 of 2021**, not 45 as I had it), FA 2022, FA 2023, FA 2024, F(No.2)A 2024,
   FA 2025, FA 2026) for "shall be inserted"/"shall be omitted";
2. a diff of the 2019 base against a **consolidation** "as amended by Finance Act, 2025" (ClearTax,
   a commercial copy, used **only to find candidates, never as a source**).

Every change in the list was then **confirmed in the amending Act's own text**. Candidates that
turned out to be parse artefacts are excluded: Second and Fourth Schedule *rules* 8A, 19A, 68A, 68B,
76, 77, and sections the consolidation parse missed (80TTA, 115TA, 115VA, 115WA, 271FAA). FA 2026
s.26's substitution of ss.276B–276D keeps all six numbers (276CCC was split across a page header).
The rebuilt Chapter XIV-B (F(No.2)A 2024) keeps the base's numbers.

**Changes applied** (details in `CHAIN`):
- **67 insertions in force** (counted from the built list; 245-OA included; 206AB/206CCA, inserted then omitted, are counted under omissions). Examples:
  - 115BAA/BAB (TLA 2019); 115BAC/BAD (FA 2020); 115BAE, 115BBJ (FA 2023);
  - 194K, 194-O … 194T; 245MA in the new Chapter XIX-AA;
  - 44BBD and 285BAA from 1-4-2026 (FA 2025);
  - 234-I (FA 2026, deemed from 1-3-2026); 292BA/292BC (FA 2026, deemed from 1-10-2019 and
    1-4-2021).
  - Seven re-use a number omitted before 2019: 80M, 89A, 130, 144B, 194K, 231, 245M.
- **Omitted since 2019** (confirmed): 88 (FA 2023), 158BI (FA 2025), 194F (F(No.2)A 2024),
  203AA (FA 2020), 271BB (FA 2025), and 206AB and 206CCA (inserted by FA 2021, omitted by FA 2025).

### A finding against the committed `W-045o`

**FA 2026 also amends the Income-tax Act, 2025.** Its s.81 inserts **s.354A**, ss.100 and 102
**omit ss.443 and 447**, and it substitutes other sections under the same numbers (pp.42–64). The
2025-Act list `W-045o` committed is the Act **as enacted**. For TY 2026-27 it needs FA 2026 applied.
The same `omitted` mechanism (A1a) will support that. I propose a small follow-up item rather than
widening this one.
