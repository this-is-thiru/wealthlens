## Plan for W-064 — the cash-salary components, and entertainment allowance

**What this item is for (one sentence):** ship the pay lines a salaried person actually enters (basic, DA, HRA,
bonus and the rest), so that gross salary and the salary bases compute real figures, and settle the last
rulebook R8 row, `SAL-ENTMT-001` (entertainment allowance).

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C4) before anything is built.**

- **Rulebook R8 `SAL-ENTMT-001`:** no counterpart in the 2025 Act (confirmed: zero hits for "entertainment
  allowance" in S2 and Finance Act 2026). TY 2025-26 is 1961 s.16(ii) (thc p.140): *"a deduction in respect of
  any allowance in the nature of an entertainment allowance specifically granted by an employer to the assessee
  who is in receipt of a salary from the Government, a sum equal to one-fifth of his salary (exclusive of any
  allowance, benefit or other perquisite) or five thousand rupees, whichever is less"*. It is excluded in the
  new regime by s.115BAC(2) (FA 2023 s.52: *"clause (ii) or clause (iii) of section 16"*).
- **What the code requires:**
  - A component must `feedsInto` a rule (or be `informationalOnly`).
  - D-093: gross counts only lines feeding `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES`.
  - D-092: `salaryBaseFor` entries carry `onlyWhereTermsProvide`.
  - `Employment` has no employer type.
- **The W-062 finding (D-092):** the Department of Revenue compendium annotates r.2A: *"Commission based on
  sales paid in addition to salary as part of the contract of employment will be 'salary' — Gestetner
  Duplicators v. CIT [1979] 117 ITR 1 (SC)"*.
  - For bonus, the annotations conflict: *"salary will not include bonus — CIT v. B. Ghosal (Ker.)"* against
    *"'Salary' will include bonus after the passing of the Payment of Bonus Act — India Radiators (Mad.)"*.

### Findings

1. **Seven pay codes are read by a built rule now, and five are not.**
   - `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES` reads every cash-salary code. So under D-089 all twelve could ship.
   - But the five allowance-specific codes exist only to reach their `SAL-ALW-*` exemptions (`W-069`). Shipped
     now, they would be plain taxable salary, indistinguishable from `SPECIAL_ALLOWANCE`.
2. **`HRA` and `LTA` will be taxed in full in the old regime until `W-067` / `W-068`** add their exemptions.
   That is the correct figure *without* the exemption, and no production path runs the engine yet (D-054,
   unowned). I'm noting it, not deciding it.
3. **What counts in the salary bases is only settled for basic and DA.**
   - The statutory text for all four bases is "includes DA if the terms provide, but excludes all other
     allowances and perquisites". Pay that is neither an allowance nor a perquisite (commission, bonus) is not
     excluded by the words.
   - Case law cited in the compendium includes contractual commission (Gestetner, SC) and splits on bonus.
   - The rulebook's H-4 shorthand is "basic + DA". This is interpretive, and it moves HRA, gratuity and both
     pension limits.
4. **`SAL-ENTMT-001` needs things the engine doesn't have:**
   - an employer type (Government), which is a new `Employment` field;
   - an `ENTERTAINMENT_ALLOWANCE` component;
   - its own salary measure, "salary (exclusive of any allowance, benefit or other perquisite)", which is a
     fifth base;
   - and a three-way `LeastOf` extension of `SalaryDeductionNode`.
   It applies only in TY 2025-26, only in the old regime, and only to Government employees. That is a node
   change, not a law row.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — which components ship.**
  - **(a) recommended: the seven core cash-salary codes** `BASIC`, `DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE`, `HRA`, `LTA`,
    `SPECIAL_ALLOWANCE`, `BONUS` and `COMMISSION`, both years. Each is `(SALARIES, SALARY, CASH, TAXABLE)` and
    each `feedsInto` `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES`.
  - The five allowance-specific codes arrive with `W-069`'s exemptions, which amends D-089's "cash-salary
    codes" for them.
  - **(b)** All twelve now.
- **C2 — the salary bases.**
  - **(a) recommended: basic and DA only for now.** `BASIC` is in all four bases outright; `DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE`
    is in all four with `onlyWhereTermsProvide: true`. Commission and bonus are in none.
  - The interpretive question (contractual commission under Gestetner; bonus with split authority) is recorded
    as open question **OQ-5**. Answering it later is a data change: a registry entry, not code.
  - **(b)** Also put `COMMISSION` in the HRA base now, on Gestetner and the compendium annotation. I haven't
    read the judgment itself, and the annotation is about HRA only.
- **C3 — `SAL-ENTMT-001`.**
  - **(a) recommended: split it out as a new item** (`W-064a`, placed by you), with its own plan: the employer
    type on `Employment`, the component, the "salary exclusive of allowances" measure, and the node extension.
  - Until then, the TY 2025-26 figure for a Government employee in the old regime omits a deduction of at most
    ₹5,000 (s.16(ii)). This is recorded in the `SEC-DEDUCTIONS-FROM-SALARY` catalogue entry as a `NotModelled`
    route.
  - **(b)** Build it in `W-064`, which doubles the item.
- **C4 — each component's source:**
  - The head and "salary" come from S2 s.16 (and 1961 s.17(1)), clause by clause: wages; fees or commission;
    profits in lieu of or in addition to salary for bonus and special allowance; allowances are salary.
  - `CASH` means paid in money.
  - Each component's note names its clause. `kind` and `treatment` follow `DR 05` M2.

### Numbers

None. Components carry no amounts, and the tests use fixture amounts added up by hand.

### Standing rules (D-043)

1. **Coverage:** no section status moves. 2025 s.16 is already `MODELLED` (`W-061`). Components are not
   sections.
2. **Catalogue:** `SEC-DEDUCTIONS-FROM-SALARY` gains the `NotModelled` entertainment-allowance route (C3a).
3. **Both years:** each component's lifecycle is live from 2025-04-01 and open-ended, with provenance from S2
   s.16 and 1961 s.17(1).
4. **Concept ids:** component codes as `DR 05` M2 names them (`DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE`, not `DA`).

- `SAL-ENTMT-001` (C3a).
- Any exemption.
- `SAL-CHARGE-*` (`W-065`).
