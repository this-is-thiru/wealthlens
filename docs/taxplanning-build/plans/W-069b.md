## Plan for W-069b — transport allowance for a disabled employee

**What this item is for (one sentence):** exempt the commuting allowance paid to an employee who is blind, deaf
and dumb, or orthopaedically handicapped. It is the one prescribed allowance that survives the new regime, and it
is exempt up to the Rules' monthly limit.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C5) before anything is built, and settles how to proceed on OQ-7.**

### What I read

- **The `W-069b` row:** *"S4 r.280(2) Table Sl. 10 / r.2BB(2) Table; the one Sl. 13 allowance that survives the
  new regime (r.280(3)) — needs OQ-7 answered, the employee's disability, and TY 2025-26 sourcing"*.
- **OQ-7:** what "Metro Cities" is, and what "plus dearness allowance thereon" means.
- **Sources, as read:**
  - **TY 2025-26, Rules 1962 r.2BB(2) Table Sl. 11** (Income Tax Department consolidation, p.5): *"Transport
    allowance granted to an employee, who is blind [or deaf and dumb] or orthopaedically handicapped with
    disability of lower extremities, to meet his expenditure for the purpose of commuting between the place of his
    residence and the place of his duty — Whole of India — [Rs. 3,200 per month]"*.
    - It **survives s.115BAC** by r.2BB(3) (G.S.R. 452(E), read): *"… and at serial no. 11 of the Table"*.
    - The amount and the words "or deaf and dumb" are bracketed as later substitutions. The other agent reported
      S.O. 1002(E) of 13-4-2015 (₹1,600 → ₹3,200) and S.O. 2604(E) of 23-9-2015 ("or deaf and dumb"); I have not
      read those notifications.
  - **TY 2026-27, Rules 2026 r.280(2) Table Sl. 10** (pp.1782–1783): *"Transport allowance granted to an
    employee, who is blind or deaf and dumb or orthopaedically handicapped with disability of lower or upper
    extremities, to meet his expenditure for the purpose of commuting between the place of his residence and the
    place of his duty — Whole of India — i) Metro Cities: ₹15000 plus dearness allowance thereon per month. (ii)
    Other Cities: ₹8000 dearness allowance thereon per month."*
    - It survives the new regime by r.280(3), with that rule's opt-out wording (D-101).
  - **"Metro Cities" in S4:** not defined in r.280. The one other use is a housing-project form elsewhere in the
    Rules, *"Metropolitan cities [Chennai, Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai]"*. r.279's HRA table lists eight cities but
    doesn't call them metro cities.
- **Code:**
  - `Individual.disability` is an `Optional<DisabilitySeverity>` (80U's severity), not the Rules' categories.
  - `AllowanceUse` carries an amount, or children and months.
  - `AllowanceExemption` finds the rule by the component's `feedsInto`.

### Findings

1. **TY 2025-26 is clear:** ₹3,200 per month; blind, deaf and dumb, or orthopaedically handicapped with a
   disability of the **lower** extremities; any regime.
2. **TY 2026-27 is not:**
   - "Metro Cities" has no definition in r.280. Taking r.279's eight, or the form's four, would be inventing law.
   - "₹15000 plus dearness allowance thereon" could be DA calculated on the ₹15,000, or the employee's DA. The
     "other cities" row reads "₹8000 dearness allowance thereon", apparently missing "plus".
   - The upper extremities are newly included.
3. **The qualifying conditions are the Rules' own categories** (blind; deaf and dumb; orthopaedic, lower or upper),
   and differ by year. 80U's severity doesn't express them.
4. **Months matter:** the limit is per month.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — OQ-7:**
  - **(a) recommended: the taxpayer states the facts the Rules leave undefined; the engine doesn't define them.**
    - For TY 2026-27, the claim carries `metroCity` (whether the employer treats the place as a metro city) and
      `dearnessAllowanceThereon` (the monthly amount the employer pays as DA on this allowance).
    - The limit is ₹15,000 or ₹8,000 plus that amount per month.
    - The TY 2026-27 version is `UNVERIFIED`, with `whatToCheck` naming both undefined terms and r.280(3). So
      every figure using it shows as PROVISIONAL.
  - **(b)** Adopt r.279's eight cities (or the form's four) and read the DA as nil. That fills a gap with an
    invented list: refused under R1.
  - **(c)** Ship TY 2025-26 only, and refuse a TY 2026-27 claim until OQ-7 is answered.
- **C2 — the input:** a `DisabledTransportClaim` on `EnteredFacts`, with:
  - `condition` ∈ {`BLIND`, `DEAF_AND_DUMB`, `ORTHOPAEDIC_LOWER_EXTREMITIES`, `ORTHOPAEDIC_UPPER_EXTREMITIES`},
    the Rules' categories;
  - `monthsCovered` (1–12);
  - `metroCity` (Optional, TY 2026-27);
  - `dearnessAllowanceThereon` (Optional, per month, TY 2026-27).

  It sits apart from `AllowanceUse`, whose two shapes it doesn't fit.
- **C3 — the row:** `SAL-ALW-TRANSPORT-DISABLED` (D-035), `AllRegimes` both years, with a new `RuleDefinition`
  member `DisabledTransportLimit`:
  - **TY 2025-26:** conditions {blind, deaf and dumb, orthopaedic lower}, ₹3,200 per month.
  - **TY 2026-27:** conditions {all four}, metro ₹15,000 and other ₹8,000, plus DA thereon.

  The node takes the least of the allowance paid and the limit × months. A condition not listed for the year
  (upper extremities in TY 2025-26) gives nil, with the reason.
- **C4 — component:** `TRANSPORT_ALLOWANCE_DISABLED`, feeding gross salary and the row.
- **C5 — sourcing:**
  - **TY 2025-26:** `introducedBy` G.S.R. 452(E) (the latest change to r.2BB read, as W-069); provenance the
    Department's consolidation, `SOURCE_CHECKED`. The two 2015 notifications are named in the note as reported,
    not read.
  - **TY 2026-27:** S4, with provenance `UNVERIFIED` (C1a).
