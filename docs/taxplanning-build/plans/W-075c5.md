## Plan for W-075c5 — deposit interest (s.153; 1961 ss.80TTA, 80TTB)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** deduct, in the old regime, the interest on deposits with a bank, a
co-operative bank or a Post Office: up to ₹10,000 of savings-account interest for an individual who is not a senior
citizen, or for a HUF, and up to ₹50,000 of interest on any such deposit for a senior citizen.

**This plan raises choices (C1–C5); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### Findings

1. **Both years, not TY 2026-27 only.** The row's title says TY 2026-27, but W-075d / d0 / d5 hand 80TTA/80TTB to this
   item ("80TTA/80TTB wait for W-075c5"), and the coverage register points the 1961 sections here. The 1961 text is
   read (above). So I propose building both years, with an erratum on the WI row (C5).
2. **The input cannot tell a savings account from a time deposit, or a bank from a company.** `OTHER_INTEREST` mixes
   them. s.153 turns on both: the deposit-taker (bank, co-operative bank, Post Office, or anyone else) and the account
   (savings, or any other).
3. **Gross or net? The words leave this open.** The deduction is of *"the interest"* the gross total income
   *"includes"*. The rule that makes it the net figure (2025 s.122(9); 1961 s.80AB) is written for Part C only, and
   s.153 / 80TTA / 80TTB are in Part D / CA. This matters only when an expense is entered against a deposit's
   interest, which `OtherSourcesNode` deducts as other expenditure.
4. **Order.** s.133's adjusted gross total income is net of every other Chapter VIII deduction, s.153 included, and
   s.134 is worked on what is left. So s.153 has to run before s.133, after the sections already there.
5. **A non-resident individual of 60 or over is not a senior citizen** (s.2(100)). So s.153(1)(a) applies to them: the
   ₹10,000 on savings interest only. The law has no residence bar on (1)(a), and the HUF gets (1)(a) as well.
6. **A firm's or an AOP's deposit (s.153(3), (4))** is not the assessee's own interest, so there is nothing to enter.
   The kinds' Javadoc will say so, rather than adding a flag nothing reads.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the input (finding 2):** **(a) recommended:** split `OtherSourceKind.OTHER_INTEREST` into three. This is
  only a change to the enum, because the kind is already "what its deductions turn on":
  - `SAVINGS_DEPOSIT_INTEREST`: a savings-account deposit, not a time deposit, with a bank, a co-operative bank or a
    Post Office.
  - `DEPOSIT_INTEREST`: any other deposit with those three, time deposits included.
  - `OTHER_INTEREST`: everything else, e.g. a company deposit or a loan. Its Javadoc loses "deposits, savings".

  `OtherSourcesNode` treats all three as interest, exactly as today. **(b)** `DR 15` D-16's bank-account entity
  (`AccountKind`, refund nomination), with interest linked to an account. That is broader than this item, and nothing
  needs the refund half yet.
- **C2 — gross or net (finding 3):** **(a) recommended:** refuse a deposit-interest item that has an expense entered,
  when s.153 would apply to it. Raise **OQ-25**. This refuses rather than guesses (D-142). **(b)** Work on the net
  interest the head includes. **(c)** Work on the gross interest.
- **C3 — the rows:** two rows, each with a TY 2025-26 and a TY 2026-27 version, `AllRegimes`, added to
  `DIS-NEW-TAX-REGIME-NO-EXEMPTION` in both years (the D-166 pattern):

    1961 s.80TTA(1).
  - `DED-DEPOSIT-INTEREST-SENIOR`: `LeastOf` [100% of `INTEREST`, `Fixed` 50000], `AgeAtLeast` 60. 2025 s.153(1)(b),
