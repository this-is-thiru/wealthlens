## Plan for W-045e — the three status axes (D-036)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** split the one `status` field into the three questions it
answers today — *what does the law say about this version*, *how sure are we we copied it right*,
and *is it released to computations* — so that only released versions can ever reach a rule pack.

Package note: the row says `taxrules/registry/`; since `W-045b2` that is
`taxengine/rules/registry/` (below, `rules/` = `backend/src/main/java/com/thiru/wealthlens/taxengine/rules/`).

### What exists today (read this session)

- `rules/registry/VerificationStatus` — `VERIFIED`, `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`, `STATUS_UNCLEAR`; used by
  `Provenance`, `Instrument`, `GoverningInstruments`, `AmendmentRef`, `json/ValueJson`, the javadoc
  of `trace/Severity`, and 15 test files.
- `rules/registry/DraftOrFinal` — `DRAFT`, `FINAL`, `UNKNOWN`; a field of `Provenance` only.
- **Values actually used** (`git grep` over every pack JSON and all Java): `VERIFIED` ×74,
  `NEEDS_VERIFICATION` ×1 (broken pack 2091, the I1 test), `FINAL` everywhere. **`STATUS_UNCLEAR`,
  `DRAFT` and `UNKNOWN` are used nowhere.**
- `Provenance` enforces **I1** in its constructor (not `VERIFIED` ⇒ `whatToCheck` required).
- `Validity.PendingNotification(interimRule, interimDescription, basis)` **already** models "pending
  notification". Its constructor requires the interim rule, and `PackValidator` checks that the rule
  exists in the pack (**I16**, `02-TECH-SPEC.md:230`, ✅ `W-029`).
- `RuleRow.versionAt` picks the version whose `validity().contains(date)`.
- The **withdrawal gate** is `ArchitectureTest.withdrawn_abstraction_names_do_not_appear_in_source`.
  It forbids one name today (`ComputationStep`) anywhere in `src/**/*.java`.
- `02-TECH-SPEC.md:72` already plans exactly this replacement (○ `W-045e`).

### The change

| Axis (brief §5.3) | New type in `rules/registry/` | Carried by | Replaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal status | `LegalStatus` — `BILL` `ENACTED` `NOTIFIED` `PENDING_NOTIFICATION` `IN_FORCE` `WITHDRAWN` `STRUCK_DOWN` | `RuleVersion.legalStatus` | `DraftOrFinal` (a draft source is a `BILL`) |
| Verification | `VerificationLevel` — `UNVERIFIED` `SOURCE_CHECKED` `PROFESSIONALLY_REVIEWED` | `Provenance.verificationLevel`, `Instrument`, `AmendmentRef` | `VerificationStatus` |
| Publication | `PublicationStatus` — `DRAFT` `IN_REVIEW` `PUBLISHED` `SUPERSEDED` | `RuleVersion.publicationStatus` | — (new) |

Verification belongs on `Provenance` because it describes the reading of a source, and that is also
where `whatToCheck` lives. Legal status and publication describe the version itself, so they go on
`RuleVersion`.

**Old → new values** (no data is lost: the three unused values have nothing to migrate):
`VERIFIED` → `SOURCE_CHECKED`; `NEEDS_VERIFICATION` → `UNVERIFIED`; `STATUS_UNCLEAR` → dropped (its
meaning is `UNVERIFIED` plus a `whatToCheck` that states the ambiguity); `DraftOrFinal` → dropped
(every row is `FINAL`, and the legal status carries the difference from now on).

**Rules enforced:**
1. **I1, unchanged in meaning:** `UNVERIFIED` ⇒ `whatToCheck` required (`Provenance` constructor).
   The user-visible flag becomes `verificationLevel == UNVERIFIED`. `PROFESSIONALLY_REVIEWED` counts
   as checked.
2. **Only `PUBLISHED` enters a pack:** the `RulePack` constructor refuses a rule version whose
   `publicationStatus` is not `PUBLISHED`, naming the rule and the status. The constructor is the one
   place every assembler passes through, so no loader can get around it.
3. **`PENDING_NOTIFICATION` requires its interim rule:** `RuleVersion` requires `legalStatus ==
   PENDING_NOTIFICATION` **if and only if** `validity` is `Validity.PendingNotification`. That type
   already requires the interim rule, and I16 already requires the rule to exist in the pack.
4. `VerificationStatus` and `DraftOrFinal` are added to the withdrawal gate.

**Legal status of the two shipped rows:** `IN_FORCE`. Source: each row's `introducedBy.effectiveFrom`
is `2026-04-01` (`law/rates.json`), which is before today (2026-09-24). **Publication:** `PUBLISHED`.

### Choices for you

- **C1 — pending notification is stated twice.** `Validity.PendingNotification` already says it, and
  now `LegalStatus.PENDING_NOTIFICATION` will too. The two could disagree. I recommend **(a)**:
  keep both and require them to agree (rule 3). (b) would drop the enum value, but the brief lists
  it.
- **C2 — a non-`PUBLISHED` version in a pack is refused, not filtered.** I recommend **refuse**: the
  pack is already compiled output, and a draft in it means the build of that pack is wrong. Dropping
  it quietly would break the "nothing silent" standard. Filtering drafts out of the timeline is
  `W-045g`'s compiler.
- **C3 — legal status does not yet change which version applies on a date.** The brief says it
  gates this, but I cannot source what `WITHDRAWN` and `STRUCK_DOWN` mean for dates: retrospective
  or not? Today `validity` decides the date and legal status is checked only by rule 3. I recommend
  **leave `versionAt` unchanged** and have `W-045n`, the research item, source the date meaning of
  each value. Say if you want it here instead.
- **C4 — `SUPERSEDED`.** My reading: a *transcription* replaced by a corrected one, **not** an older
  version of the law. An older law is still `PUBLISHED`, because an earlier year's pack needs it. The
  javadoc will say so.
- **C5 — sizing (§7).** New code, estimated: 3 enums with javadoc ~75 lines, the `RuleVersion` and
  `RulePack` checks ~35, JSON reading ~10, the gate ~10, `RuleStatusTest` ~120. **About 250 lines, so
  no split.** On top of that come mechanical edits (below).
