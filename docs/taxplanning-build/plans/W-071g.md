## Plan for W-071g — tax paid by the employer on a non-monetary perquisite

**What this item is for (one sentence):** make sure tax an employer pays, at its option, on an employee's non-monetary
perquisite is not counted as the employee's income — and settle what, if anything, the engine must compute for it.

**This plan finds nothing to compute and raises one reading of the 2025 text (C2); nothing is coded until you
choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-071g` row** (D-110): S2 Sch. III Table Sl. 10; 1961 s.10(10CC). `DR 01` **`PERQ-TAXPAID-001`** — *"Where
  the employer pays the tax on a non-monetary perquisite at its own option, that tax is not included in the
  employee's income"* (VERIFIED, S2 Sch. III Sl. 10); `TDS-SAL-PERQ-001` (the employer paying that tax, at the average
  rate, is a TDS matter) and `TDS-SAL-PERQ-002` (*"Tax so paid … is excluded from the employee's income"*).
- **1961 s.10(10CC)** (thc.nic.in p.75, read here): not included in total income — *"in the case of an employee, being
  an individual deriving income in the nature of a perquisite, not provided for by way of monetary payment, within the
  meaning of clause (2) of section 17, **the tax on such income actually paid by his employer**, at the option of the
  employer, on behalf of such employee"*.
- **2025 Act Sch. III Table Sl. 10** (Gazette 265620, p.506, read here) — Schedule III: *"the income mentioned in column
  B … shall not be included, subject to the conditions mentioned in column D"* (p.504). Sl. 10: **column B** *"Income in
  the nature of a perquisite."*; **C** *"An employee, being an individual."*; **D** *"(a) Such perquisite is not
  provided for by way of monetary payment, within the meaning of section 17(1); and (b) the tax on such income actually
  paid by his employer, at the option of the employer, on behalf of such employee."*
- **Code:** no salary component, input or node carries tax an employer pays for the employee into income
  (`exec/components/*.json`: no such component); perquisites are valued, never grossed up for tax.

### Findings

1. **1961: the tax paid is what is left out.** The engine never adds it, so the exclusion already holds for TY 2025-26
   by construction — there is no figure to compute.
2. **2025, read literally, leaves out the perquisite itself.** Column B names "Income in the nature of a perquisite";
   the tax paid appears only in the conditions. Read that way, a non-monetary perquisite on which the employer pays
   the tax is not income at all in TY 2026-27 — a far wider exclusion than 1961's. The design (`PERQ-TAXPAID-001`,
   `TDS-SAL-PERQ-002`) reads it as 1961 does: the tax is excluded, the perquisite stays.
3. **The employer's paying the tax is TDS** (`TDS-SAL-PERQ-001`), a later phase — not the income computation.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what to build:**
  - **(a) recommended: no engine change.** The exclusion holds by construction (finding 1). Close the item with a
    test that proves it stays so — a pipeline case where the employer is stated to pay the perquisite's tax is not
    possible (no such input), so instead **record it** in the catalogue: the "Tax your employer pays on a benefit"
    route becomes a `NotModelled` with the reason *"never counted as your income; the employer's payment is part of
    tax deduction at source"*. Coverage is kept per section, not per clause — 1961 s.10 is already `MODELLED` by its
    other clauses and 2025 s.11 (Schedule III) `PLANNED` under W-067 — so it does not change.
  - **(b)** An input "the employer pays the tax on this employment's non-monetary perquisites" and a nil trace line —
    a fact with no effect on any figure (standard 2: no speculative code).
- **C2 — the 2025 reading (finding 2):**
  - **(a) recommended:** follow the design — the tax is excluded, the perquisite stays, both years; record the literal
    reading as proposed **OQ-13**. If OQ-13 is answered the literal way, a later item adds the input and an exclusion
    of the perquisite in TY 2026-27.
  - **(b)** Build the literal reading now: a stated fact per employment, and in TY 2026-27 its non-monetary perquisites
    left out of total income.
