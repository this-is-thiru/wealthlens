## Plan for W-079g1 — other sources, the clauses received in full

**What this item is for (one sentence):** compute, in both years, five more kinds of income from other sources:
- Keyman policy sums;
- forfeited advances;
- compensation on termination of employment, where not salary;
- a business trust's specified sums;
- the residual s.92(1) / s.56(1) income.

Each bears the general expenditure deduction. The business-head clauses, (c), (f) and (g), are refused by name
(D-202 C2a), and life-policy sums are refused, naming W-079g2.

**This plan raises choices (C1–C5); nothing is coded until you choose.**

- **s.92(2)** (pp.138–139):
  - (d) *"any sum received under a Keyman insurance policy, as defined in Schedule II (Note 1) including the bonus
    allocated on such policy, if such income is not chargeable … under the head "Profits and gains of business or

  - (h) *"any sum of money received as an advance or otherwise during negotiations for the transfer of a capital asset,
    if–– (i) such sum is forfeited; and (ii) the negotiations do not result in transfer of such capital asset"*;
  - (j) *"any compensation or other payment, due to or received by any person, by whatever name called, in connection
    with the termination of his employment, or the modification of its terms and conditions"*;
  - (k) *"any specified sum received by a unit holder from a business trust during the tax year with respect to a unit
    held by him at any time during such tax year … specified sum = A-B-C (which shall be deemed to be zero, if the sum
    of B and C is greater than A)"*. A is the aggregate distributed on the unit, in this year or earlier ones, that is

- **s.18(1)** (pp.40–41), "profits in lieu of salary":
  - (a) compensation *"from his employer or former employer"* at or in connection with termination or modification;
  - (b) amounts from a person before joining or after leaving employment *"with that person"*;
  - (c)(iii) Keyman policy sums.

  All of these are salary. `SEC-PROFITS-IN-LIEU-OF-SALARY` already routes employer compensation through
  `RETRENCHMENT_COMPENSATION`, `VRS_COMPENSATION` and `NOTICE_PAY`, and records Keyman as salary "not modelled".

### Findings

1. **(d) and (j) reach only what salary doesn't.** Employer compensation on termination, and Keyman sums received as
   an employee, are profits in lieu of salary (s.18(1)(a), (c)(iii); 1961 s.17(3)(i), (iii)). So (j) is compensation
   from someone other than the employer or former employer, and (d) is Keyman sums that are neither salary nor
   business. The kinds' Javadoc says so. The engine can't tell who paid, so the person's choice of kind is their
   claim, as with gift grounds (D-201).
2. **(k) needs three figures no input has.** A business trust reports A (Rules 2026 forms, pp.2060–2063). B is the
   issue price; C is the person's own history.
3. **"Deemed to be zero, if the sum of B and C is greater than A"** — so equal gives A − B − C = 0 too. Never
   negative.
4. **The residue is a catch-all.** Choosing `OTHER` is the person's claim that the income belongs nowhere else.
5. **TY 2025-26 `introducedBy` (D-188):**
   - FA 2018 s.23 and FA 2023 s.32 are read.
   - Act 25 of 2014 is on egazette (2014/160514).
   - Act 33 of 1996 is not on egazette. The Goa reprints are where earlier Acts were found (D-196 / D-197); I'll scan
     them.
   - s.56(1) as enacted has the same unfound 1961 Gazette as `OS-INTEREST` and `OS-OTHER-EXPENDITURE`, which are
     `UNVERIFIED` for that reason.
