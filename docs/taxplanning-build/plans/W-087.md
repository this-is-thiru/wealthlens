## Plan for W-087 — `MoneyBridge` (and the `CapitalGainsAdapter`, D-238 C5a)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** the one seam where `portfolio`'s canonical `double` rupees become the
engine's exact `Money` (`DR 19` E1), and, per D-238 C5a, the adapter that reads `CapitalGainsSummaryService` and maps
its buckets into `CapitalGainsInput`.

**This plan proposes a split (§7) and raises choices C1–C4. Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (session 21, 2026-10-18)

- **The row:** *"`MoneyBridge` | `DR 19` E1 | `toTMoney` throws — one-way seam"*. **D-238 C5a** adds
  *"`CapitalGainsAdapter` in `taxplanning.portfolio` — calls `CapitalGainsSummaryService.summaryFor` and maps the
  buckets to `CapitalGainsInput` through the bridge"*.
- **`DR 19` E1, the seam:** `MoneyBridge.fromTMoney(double) = Money.ofExact(BigDecimal.valueOf(canonicalRupees))`,
  *"lossless in this direction because TMoney guarantees the double is already canonical to paise"*.
  `toTMoney(Money)` throws `UnsupportedOperationException`.
- **`ArchitectureTest`:**
  - `only_the_adapter_converts`: no class outside `..taxplanning.portfolio..` calls a method named `fromTMoney`;
  - `taxengine_does_not_use_TMoney`.
- **`Money`:** the constructor applies `setScale(2, HALF_UP)`, so `ofExact` rounds silently. A non-canonical
  `145.37999999999997` would become 145.38 without complaint. That is the guarantee E1 relies on, unchecked at the
  seam.
- **`CapitalGainsInput.Bucket(Money gain, Term term, RateBasisHint basis, Optional<String> portfolioRuleRef)`.** Every
  bucket needs a term and a rate basis. `RateBasisHint` is `SPECIAL_RATE_LISTED_EQUITY`, `SPECIAL_RATE_OTHER` or
  `SLAB`.
- **`RateBasisHint` lives in `taxengine.rules.component`.** That is not a named interface: `taxengine` exposes `input`,
  `result`, `money` and `id`. `taxplanning` therefore cannot construct a `Bucket` without a modulith violation.
  D-048: *"A package is exposed by the item that first needs it from outside."*
- **The rulebook has no capital-gains rate group.** ss.111A / 112 / 112A (1961) and ss.196 / 197 / 198 (2025) appear
  only inside the surcharge tables (`01-rulebook.md` l.309–346). No row states which asset, holding and sub-class
  fall on which basis, or the conditions (STT paid, equity-oriented fund, s.50AA specified fund). START-HERE R1 / R2
  bar me from writing that mapping from memory.
- **The summary (W-085/W-086)** also carries classifications the input has no slot for:
  - `SPECULATIVE` is business income, which X1.3 says *"must surface it, not hide it"*;
  - `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS` is another head;
  - `UNCLASSIFIED` is X1.3's blocking state (409; W-088's row).

### Findings

1. **The bridge is small and fully specified,** except that it must **refuse** a non-canonical or non-finite
   `double`, not let `Money` round it. Otherwise the "lossless" claim is unchecked at the one place it is made.
2. **The adapter is not.** It needs, in its own plan:
   - **(a)** a sourced mapping from (classification, asset type, sub-class) to (`Term`, `RateBasisHint`): the 2025
     Act ss.196–198 and the 1961 Act ss.111A, 112, 112A read first, including the STT condition and the fund tests;
   - **(b)** a decision on exposing `RateBasisHint` (a `component` named interface, or moving the enum to
     `taxengine.input`);
   - **(c)** what becomes of `SPECULATIVE` and `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS` amounts, and where `UNCLASSIFIED` is refused
     (adapter or W-088).

   That is a sourcing round plus three design choices, too much to settle inside this plan.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C0 — the split:**
  - **(a) recommended:**
    - **W-087** = `MoneyBridge` only, as the row first said;
    - **W-087b** = `CapitalGainsAdapter`, placed after W-087 and before W-088, with D-238 C5a moving to it. Its plan
      comes with a source-first round on ss.196–198 / 111A, 112, 112A, and it decides finding 2's (b) and (c).
  - **(b)** keep both in W-087 and write the adapter's plan as a Revision 1 after sourcing.
- **C1 — `fromTMoney(double canonicalRupees)`:**
  - **(a) recommended:** refuse a non-finite amount, and one whose `BigDecimal.valueOf` has more than two decimal
    places after stripping trailing zeros, naming the amount and TL-8. Otherwise `Money.ofExact(...)`, which then has
    nothing to round.
  - **(b)** E1 as written: rounding left to `Money`.
- **C2 — `toTMoney`:**
  - **(a) recommended:** build it as the row and E1 say: it throws `UnsupportedOperationException` with E1's reason.
    It is the named statement of the one-way seam, and a test pins it.
  - **(b)** omit it (CLAUDE.md standard 2: no method without a caller), and record the one-way rule in the class
    Javadoc and a D-row.
- **C3 — where:** `taxplanning/portfolio/MoneyBridge.java` (a `final` class, static methods, private constructor),
  the package `only_the_adapter_converts` names. Its first caller is W-087b. Until then the ArchUnit rule stays
  vacuous, as its own Javadoc expects.
- **C4 — records:** D-240; WI (W-087 note, W-087b row, D-238 C5a moved); TS l.115.
