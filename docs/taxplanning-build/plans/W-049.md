### Findings

1. **The ⚠ "reads `atSlabRates` ONLY" is too strong.** s.156(2)(b) is on *"the income-tax payable on
   such total income"* (the whole tax), and s.156(3) then **caps** a s.156(2) deduction at slab-rate
   tax. G3.1 and R-5 say the same: compute on the tax, cap at `atSlabRates`. And **s.156(1) has no such
   cap** in the text.
2. **The s.156(1) interaction is unsourced.** How s.156(1) meets special-rate income (e.g. capital
   gains taxed at special rates in the other regime) is not in anything I've read. It can't arise
   before `W-088` wires capital gains. It is the kind of thing `W-089` ("rebate with special-rate
   income") exists to settle.
3. **The spec table is incomplete.** It lists `tax.afterrebate` as requiring only `tax.bybasis`; it
   also needs `round.totalincome`, because the ceilings test total income.
4. **Wiring a relief** into `RuleVersion` touches all 13 construction sites (mechanical) and adds a
   relief reader to `ShapeJson`.

### A. Choices (recommended first)

## Built — where the build departs from or adds to the plan (for review)

- **B1 — conditions are `[IsResident, HasPersonType [INDIVIDUAL]]`, not `[IsResident]` alone.** s.156(1) says
  "an individual resident in India", and s.156(2) says "a resident individual assessee". A resident HUF would satisfy
  `IsResident` alone. Neither regime serves a HUF today (`appliesTo: [INDIVIDUAL]`), so no figure changes.
- **B2 — the reason goes in `TraceLine.limitedBy`.** `TraceLine` has no reason field, and `NodeResult` carries no
  annotations. `limitedBy` ("why the output was smaller than it might have been") is the only text slot. Each
  `RELIEF` line carries one of three reasons:
  - the shape's reason;
  - "not met: <condition>";
  - "limited to the tax at slab rates, <amount>".
- **B3 — the s.156(3) limit isn't named by section in the node.** The flag is generic data. The reason says "limited
  to the tax at slab rates", and the row's `_note` cites s.156(3).
- **B4 — the W-089 refusal narrowed.** An unlimited rebate meeting special-rate tax is refused only when the rebate
  on the whole tax is **positive**. Above the ceiling the answer is nil on any reading, so an old-regime assessee with
  capital gains above ₹5,00,000 isn't refused.
- **B5 — more cases copied from `DR 08`:**
  - T1-N2 (10.00; rebate 59,991.50);
  - T1-N4, both sides of the crossover (70,580.00; 70,588.50);
  - T1-O1 (0).
- **B6 — sources re-read this session:**
