### Findings

1. **The 1961 figures equal the rows':**
   - 25,000 for (a) and (b) (B11; Act 20 of 2015 s.19);
   - 50,000 medical for an uninsured senior, and 50,000 for each pair (B11; Act 13 of 2018 s.26);
   - check-ups 5,000 within (B12, (2A));
   - cash only for a check-up (B12, (2B));
   - the senior's 50,000 (B13, (4));
   - a lump sum by its fraction (B13, (4A));
   - "senior citizen" — resident, sixty at any time in the previous year (B13).

   The rows carry over unchanged.
2. **Defect 1 (both years): a scheme payment for a parent is counted.** `bucketOf` sends a parent's payment to the
   parents' bucket whatever its kind, so a CGHS or notified-scheme contribution for a parent counts up to 25,000.
   Neither s.126(2)(b) nor 80D(2)(b) allows it. The parents' row notes also say *"insurance, a notified health scheme
   and preventive check-ups"* — wrong for (b).
3. **Defect 2 (both years): a scheme payment triggers the senior's ₹50,000.** `coversSenior` is set by a scheme
   contribution as well as a premium. s.126(8) and 80D(4) substitute 50,000 only for a sum paid for the *health
   insurance* of a senior. The node's own (7) check already reads "health insurance" as premiums only, so the two
   readings disagree within one class.
4. **The guard** (D-144) refuses health payments for TY 2025-26 naming W-075d2. Once the rows exist that test changes
   to a computation, and the next refusal (W-075d3) needs its own test.
