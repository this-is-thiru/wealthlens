**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: a deferment date with relief** (one reason per line).
  - **C1a (recommended):** four combined codes, so the sentence says both the state and the relief:
    `DEFERMENT_PAID_ENOUGH_RELIEVED`, `DEFERMENT_SHORT_RELIEVED` `{date, relieved, paidBy}`;
    `DEFERMENT_PAID_ENOUGH_NOT_RELIEVED`, `DEFERMENT_SHORT_NOT_RELIEVED` `{date, tax, paidBy, paid}`.
    `DefermentRelief.AtDate` then carries these figures as facts, not only text.
  - C1b: the relief's reason alone (two codes), and the state is read from the interest figure.
- **C2: "granted" or "as if granted" on refund interest.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** not in the sentence. The sentence's "to {to}" is true either way, and `limitedBy` keeps the
    word.
  - C2b: twice the refund codes, one pair for each.

That is **23 new codes** with C1a and C2a.

**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption; no digits):**
- `ADVANCE_TAX_NOT_LIABLE_BY_AGE`: "You are not liable to advance tax: you are resident, aged {age}, with no business income."
- `ADVANCE_TAX_NOT_LIABLE_BELOW`: "You are not liable to advance tax: what is due after tax at source is below {threshold}."
- `ADVANCE_TAX_LIABLE`: "You are liable to advance tax on the bill less the tax deducted or collected at source."
- `ADVANCE_TAX_STILL_OWED`: "This is what is still owed of the instalments due by {date}."
- `CREDIT_TAX_AT_SOURCE`: "Tax deducted or collected at source counts as paid."
- `CREDIT_ADVANCE_TAX`: "Advance tax you paid counts as paid."
- `CREDIT_SELF_ASSESSMENT_TAX`: "Self-assessment tax you paid counts as paid."
- `INTEREST_NOT_LIABLE_TO_ADVANCE_TAX`: "You are not liable to advance tax, so no interest is due on it."
- `DEFERMENT_NOT_YET_REACHED`: "The instalment date {date} has not come yet."
- `DEFERMENT_PAID_ENOUGH`: "By {date} you had paid enough, so no interest is due for it."
- `DEFERMENT_SHORT`: "By {date} you had paid less than the share due, so interest is due on the shortfall."
- `DEFERMENT_PAID_ENOUGH_RELIEVED`: "By {date} you had paid enough. The {relieved} of tax on income that arose after that date is left out, as it was paid by {paidBy}."
- `DEFERMENT_SHORT_RELIEVED`: "By {date} you had paid less than the share due. The {relieved} of tax on income that arose after that date is left out, as it was paid by {paidBy}."
- `DEFERMENT_PAID_ENOUGH_NOT_RELIEVED`: "By {date} you had paid enough. The {tax} of tax on income that arose after that date is not left out: only {paid} of it was paid by {paidBy}."
- `DEFERMENT_SHORT_NOT_RELIEVED`: "By {date} you had paid less than the share due. The {tax} of tax on income that arose after that date is not left out: only {paid} of it was paid by {paidBy}."
- `FEE_RETURN_FURNISHED_IN_TIME`: "The return is furnished by its due date, {date}, so no fee is due."
- `FEE_RETURN_FURNISHED_LATE`: "The return is furnished after its due date, {date}, so the late fee is due."
- `INTEREST_FOR_MONTHS`: "Interest runs for {months} months, from {from} to {to}."
- `INTEREST_NOT_YET_DUE`: "No interest yet: it runs only from {from}."
- `SHORTFALL_ADVANCE_TAX_PAID_ENOUGH`: "The advance tax you paid reaches the share of the assessed tax the law asks for, so no interest is due."
- `REFUND_BELOW_SHARE`: "A refund of {refund} is less than {share} of the tax of {tax}, so it carries no interest."
- `REFUND_INTEREST_ON_RETURN_PAYMENT`: "Interest is added for {months} months, from {from} to {to}, on the part of the refund that returns the tax you paid on {paidOn}."
- `REFUND_INTEREST_ON_PREPAID_TAX`: "Interest is added for {months} months, from {from} to {to}, on the part of the refund that returns tax at source and advance tax."
