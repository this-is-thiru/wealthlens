## Plan for W-045j — the coverage register (D-038)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** a register of the Acts' sections, each classified, checked by
the build, so that "which sections do we model" is a number the build reports and a rule cannot cite
a section nobody has classified.

### What I read

- **Brief §7.1:** `timeline/content/coverage.json` lists every section of both Acts, and each carries
  exactly one classification. `MODELLED` carries its rule ids and manager; `PLANNED` the assessee
  types and a backlog item; `NOT_COMPUTATIONAL` nothing more; `OUT_OF_SCOPE` a reason and who decided.
  **CI fails** on an unclassified section, on a `MODELLED` section with no published rule, and on a
  rule citing a section the register does not know.
- **`W-045o`** (research, no code): the register is completed to **every section of both Acts**,
  from their tables of contents.

### Finding: the "105 sections" cannot be seeded as stated

`grep -oE "\bs\.[0-9]+[A-Z]*" 01-rulebook.md | sort -u` gives **116**, not 105, and the list is not
one Act's. It mixes:
- **1961-Act sections** (`s.80C`, `s.115BAC`, `s.87A`, …);
- **2025-Act sections** that reuse the same numbers for different law (`s.10`, `s.16` and `s.19` mean
  different things in each Act);
- **Finance Act sections** (`FA 2026 s.2`, `s.3`, `s.56`).

A true count needs each rulebook row read for which Act it cites. Each section then needs a sourced
classification, and for `PLANNED` a backlog item, about 110 decisions. That is `W-045o`'s kind of
work, and `W-045o` already covers every section, a superset of the rulebook's.

### A. Proposal

**This item builds the register and its three checks,** seeded with **only the sections the shipped
timeline cites**, so every check runs against real data. **`W-045o` fills the rest,** including every
section the rulebook cites. I'll record that as an erratum on the `W-045j` row.

What the timeline cites (rule and regime provisions, read from the files):

|---|---|---|---|
| Income-tax Act, 2025 | 202 | `RATE-SLAB-NEW-TAX-REGIME` (`s.202(1) Table`); regimes `NEW_TAX_REGIME` (`s.202(1)`), `FIRST_SCHEDULE_RATES` (`s.202(4)`) | `MODELLED`: rules `RATE-SLAB-NEW-TAX-REGIME`, manager `SlabLadder` |

so it adds no entry (B1).

### B. Choices (all recommended)

- **B1 — what counts as citing a section.** A provision whose instrument is one of the two Income-tax
  Acts, whose presence is `PRESENT`, and whose reference starts `s.<number><letters>`, e.g.
  `s.115BAC(1A)(iii)` → `115BAC`. A section is **(Act, number)**. Schedules, Rules and Finance Act
  provisions are not in this register; schedule coverage is `W-045o`'s to decide.
- **B2 — rules and regimes both cite.** Both carry `provision1961`/`provision2025`, and a regime
  resting on an unclassified section is the same gap.
- **B3 — the register is not compiled into year packs.** It describes the whole timeline, not a year.
  `TimelineReader` learns the one file `content/coverage.json` and carries it verbatim (optional,
  since test timelines have none); the compiler ignores it. **No lock changes.**
- **B4 — where the check runs.** `CoverageRegister.verify(Timeline)` in `taxengine/rules/coverage/`
  (coverage → timeline; no cycle), and the build runs it through `CoverageRegisterTest` against the
  shipped timeline. That test is the CI gate the brief asks for.
- **B5 — "published rule".** A `MODELLED` entry's rules must each be in the timeline with at least one
  `PUBLISHED` version.

### Types and files

- **Create:**
  - `taxengine/rules/coverage/`:
    - `SectionRef(Act, String section)`, with `Act` = `INCOME_TAX_ACT_1961`/`INCOME_TAX_ACT_2025`,
      parsed from a provision;
    - `Coverage`, sealed, one per classification with the fields the brief requires: `Modelled(rules,
      manager)`, `Planned(assesseeTypes, backlogItem)`, `NotComputational()`, `OutOfScope(reason,
      decidedBy)`;
    - `CoverageRegister` (`read`, `verify`).
  - `rulepacks/timeline/content/coverage.json`: the 2 entries above.
  - `CoverageRegisterTest`.
- **Modify:** `TimelineReader` and `Timeline` (the one content file, carried verbatim).
- **Docs:** `02-TECH-SPEC.md` T2 + Changes; `03-WORK-ITEMS.md` erratum (the seeding moves to
  `W-045o`); `STATE.md`; `CHECKPOINT.md`

**Numbers:** none.

### How I will verify

`./mvnw test -pl backend -Dtest=CoverageRegisterTest,TimelineReaderTest`, then the unit tier.
Each refusal starts from a copy of the shipped timeline:
- **Passes:** the shipped timeline and register.
- **Fails, naming the problem:**
  - a rule citing `s.999` (unregistered);
  - a `MODELLED` entry naming a rule with only `DRAFT` versions;
  - a `MODELLED` entry naming a rule absent from the timeline;
  - an entry with no classification, and one with two;
  - a `PLANNED` entry missing its backlog item.
- **Not a citation:** a `First Schedule` reference, and an `ABSENT_IN_THIS_ACT` provision.
