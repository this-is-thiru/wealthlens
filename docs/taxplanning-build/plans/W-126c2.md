**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** read Part B of a Form No. 130 — each quarter's amount paid,
tax deducted and deposited, and each deposit's challan or book-adjustment details — so W-128 can check Part B's tax
against Part C (G5.2) and W-129 can assemble several employers' Parts A and B (`DOC-F130-MULTI-001`).

### What the Gazette and the design say

- Part B, pp.2274 – 2275, is three tables. Every heading, column and note was read by eye and recorded in
  `sources/2026-11-16-form-130.md` ("Part B … read for W-126c2"):
  - (i) the summary: Quarter (s) · Receipt Numbers … · Amount paid/credited · Rate of deduction of tax · Amount of tax
    deducted · Amount of tax deposited/ adjusted. Its "Total" row merges the Amount paid/credited and Rate cells.
  - (ii) "I. … through book adjustment": Sl. No. · Tax deposited (refer Note 5) · four columns under the group "Book
    Identification Number (BIN)".
  - (iii) "II. … through challan": Sl. No. · Tax deposited (refer Note 5) · four columns under the group "Challan
    Identification Number (CIN)".
- Each table's data rows are blank on the form. The issuer writes one row per quarter or per deposit, so **a data row
  has no printed label**.
- `DR 06` G5.1 (`06-algorithms.md:482`): L1 finds parts by heading and rows by statutory label — "structural first,
  positional never". G5.2 (`:509`) needs Part B's total tax deducted.
- Notes 3 – 5 (p.2275): government deductors fill item I, others item II; Part B's tax deducted is tax + surcharge + cess.

### Findings

1. **The label-only parser cannot read Part B.** `Form130Parser` matches each layout line by its label
   (`Form130Parser.java:112`, `:161`), and a data row has none.
2. **Splitting cells at gaps fails** (probe). Gaps over `LabelMatch.COLUMN_GAP` (12 pt) are not reliable:
   - **Adjacent headings run together.** In two narrow header cells, "Form No. 137" and "Form No. 137" were printed 8 pt
     apart and read as one cell.
   - **An empty cell shifts the row.** Q2's blank Rate cell moved every later value one column left when counted by
     order.
   - **A merged cell lands under the wrong column.** The Total row's merged cell printed its amount under "Rate", by
     position.
3. **Rebuilding each heading from its words works** (probe, by hand on the word stream):
   - **Method:** take a column heading's words in order. A continuation is the next word on the same line, or a word on
     a following line whose box overlaps the heading's words so far. Rebuilt this way, every heading in the probe —
     single-line, wrapped, the BIN group and its sub-columns — came out whole and kept apart from its neighbours.
   - **Examples:** "Amount of tax deducted" is lines 2 – 3 at x 366 – 423; "paid" plus "/credited" joins as LabelMatch
     already joins a broken word.
   - **Values:** each value's centre then falls inside exactly one heading's span, except the Total row's merged cell,
     which the layout has to declare.
4. **The current matcher cannot find centred headings.** `LabelMatch.take` refuses a continuation line that starts
   more than 12 pt right of the label's first word (`LabelMatch.java:116`). The Gazette centres Part B's table titles
   and column headings, so `LabelMatch` alone cannot find them.
5. **Size.** A table type in the layout (its records, JSON reader, validator and accessor, plus the data and relock)
   and a table reader in the parser (heading spans, rows, the merged Total cell, L2 by column) come to well over 300
   lines (§7).

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — the split** (finding 5):
  - **(a) W-126c2a — Part B's tables as layout data:**
    - `tables` on the certificate, with their records, JSON reader, validator and `CertificateLayout` accessor.
    - The three tables in `FORM_130.json` from the source note, plus a `partB` part; the relock and layout tests.
    - The parser is untouched.
  - **(a) W-126c2b — the table reader:**
    - Find each title and column heading as in finding 3.
    - Put each value under the heading whose span holds its centre; read the Total row's declared cells; read each
      cell by its column's kind (L2).
    - Fixtures laid out as the Gazette lays Part B out.
  - (b) one item.
- **C2 — the table in the layout:**
  - **(a) A table records:**
    - `path`, e.g. `partB.summary`, `partB.bookAdjustment`, `partB.challan`, and its printed `title` lines.
    - `columns`, each with its printed `heading`, a `key` (camel case, since "paid/credited" cannot be a path
      word), its `kind` and an optional `group` (the BIN or CIN heading).
    - `total`: the printed label "Total" and its cells, a merged cell naming every column it spans.
    - The Gazette `page`.
  - **(a) Read cells** become `partB.summary.row1.amountPaid` … in printed order, and `partB.summary.total.taxDeducted`.
  - **(a) The validator refuses:**
    - a key twice;
    - a merged cell naming a column the table lacks;
    - a total cell for a column the table lacks;
    - a group heading with one column.
  - (b) one `CertificateField` per column with `repeating`. It is at its 7-component limit
    (`CertificateField.java:16`), and a field is found by a label that a data row does not have.
- **C3 — which column a value is read under** (findings 2, 3):
  - **(a) The heading whose printed span holds the value's centre**, the span found by the heading's own words, so
    nothing depends on fixed page coordinates. A value under no heading, or straddling two, is left unread, named in
    the notes, and makes the reading `PARTIAL`.
  - (b) by the order of gap-separated cells. The probe shows this breaks on an empty cell.
- **C4 — the rate column:**
  - **(a) `TEXT`, kept as printed.** No percentage kind exists, and G5.2 does not use the rate.
  - (b) a new `PERCENT` kind and value — more types for a figure nothing reads yet.
