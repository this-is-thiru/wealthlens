**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: the condition texts** ("not met: …" and the loan's five sanction checks):
  - **C1a (recommended):** each takes one general code, and the specific condition stays in `limitedBy`, as D-681 C1a decided for salary.
  - C1b: a code per check (window, amount, value, stamp duty, house owned), with a date fact kind added. That gives finer sentences, at five codes and a new kind.

**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption):**
- `DEDUCTIONS_WITHIN_INCOME`: "Deductions cannot be more than the income they are taken from."
- `GROSS_TOTAL_LESS_SPECIAL_GAINS`: "Gross total income here leaves out the {gains} of capital gains taxed at special rates."
- `DEPOSIT_INTEREST_SAVINGS_ONLY`: "Only savings-account interest is deducted for you; interest on time deposits is deducted only for a senior citizen."
- `CONDITION_NOT_MET`: "Nothing is deducted: a condition of this deduction is not met."
- `DISABILITY_CERTIFIED`: "It is the amount the law sets for a certified disability of this degree."
- `DEPENDANT_NOTHING_SPENT`: "Nothing is deducted: nothing was spent on or deposited for the dependant's care."
- `NOT_RESIDENT`: "Nothing is deducted: this deduction is only for a resident."
- `DONATION_QUALIFYING_LIMIT`: "The qualifying limit is a share of your adjusted gross total income of {adjusted}."
- `DONATION_CASH_LIMIT`: "Nothing is deducted: it was paid in cash above {limit}."
- `DONATION_UNREPORTED`: "Nothing is deducted: the donee has not reported this donation."
- `DONATION_WITHOUT_LIMIT`: "These donations are deducted without a qualifying limit."
- `DONATION_WITHIN_QUALIFYING_LIMIT`: "These donations are deducted within the qualifying limit."
- `HEALTH_PAID_IN_CASH`: "Nothing is deducted: it was paid in cash, which the rules do not allow for this payment."
- `HEALTH_CHECKUP_SPLIT`: "It is split as you claimed: {family} for your family and {parents} for your parents."
- `EDUCATION_LOAN_OUTSIDE_YEARS`: "Nothing is deducted: interest counts only for the initial tax year {initial} and the {years} years after it."
- `ALL_DEDUCTED`: "All of it is deducted."
- `LOAN_SANCTION_CONDITION_NOT_MET`: "Nothing is deducted: the loan does not meet a condition on its sanction or the house."
- `DEDUCTED_IN_FULL`: "All of it is deducted: it is within the limit of {limit}."
- `DEDUCTION_LIMITED`: "It is limited to {limit}."
- `RENT_OWNS_HOME_HERE`: "Nothing is deducted: you own a home where you live or work."
- `RENT_OWNS_HOME_ELSEWHERE`: "Nothing is deducted: you own and live in a home elsewhere."
- `RENT_RECEIVES_HRA`: "Nothing is deducted: you receive house rent allowance, which is exempted instead."
- `PREMIUM_SHARE_OF_SUM_ASSURED`: "The premium counts only up to {share} of the sum assured of {sumAssured}."
