### Findings

1. **"Research, no code" can't meet its own Verify.** "The build reports the count by
   classification" and "the count equals each Act's" both need code: a count on `CoverageRegister`
   and assertions against each Act's reference count. It's small, about 40 lines, but it is code.
2. **Brief §9 has sections that no classification fits.** D-033 says the product is *designed for*
   every assessee and income head. Some things are only *built later*: business income, firms,
   companies, non-residents, clubbing, alternative minimum tax. Brief §9 says "out of scope" means
   **not built yet**, not **not designed for**. But:
   - `PLANNED` requires a backlog item, and those have none;
   - `OUT_OF_SCOPE` means "deliberately excluded", which would contradict D-033.

   Classifying them either way would mislabel hundreds of sections.
3. **The 1961 Act, as in force for TY 2025-26, isn't available as one document.** It has to be
   reconstructed:
   - the thc.nic.in base (to 2019);
   - plus every section inserted or omitted by the Finance Acts of 2019 (No. 2) to 2025, which
     `W-045n` already downloaded;
   - plus the non-Finance amending Acts of that period, not yet fetched: the Taxation Laws
     (Amendment) Acts 2019 and 2021, and the Taxation and Other Laws (Relaxation…) Act 2020.

   That's real research with its own risk of omission.
4. **Scale.** The 2025 Act has several hundred sections, and the 1961 Act has many hundreds more,
   counting lettered insertions. Classifying section by section is well over a thousand judgements.
   It needs a **rule the owner approves**, not per-section invention.
5. **Omitted 1961 sections** ("[Omitted]" in the arrangement): count them as sections or not?
