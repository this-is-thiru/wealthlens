**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** let a draft remember which of F34's five groups of
questions the person has answered — including "nothing to enter" — and tell them, for the groups still open, what
answering is measured to be worth.

**Probe (2026-11-15; code read, nothing run):**
- **Where a draft keeps what the engine never reads.** `DraftInputs` (`taxplanning/workspace/DraftInputs.java:23`) has
  four parts; the fourth, `ThirdParties`, is the precedent (W-239a): stored in the draft's encrypted document, changed
  by the same `PATCH …/drafts/{draftId}` with `If-Match`, kept in every revision, defaulted in the compact constructor
  so a draft stored before it reads as none (`:25-27`), and not passed to the engine — the assembler hands over
  `entered`, `brought` and `filing` only (`assemble/EngineInputsAssembler.java:65-66`).
- **What is measured today.** `DraftCompletenessService.items` (`compute/DraftCompletenessService.java:60-73`) has two
  sources: `priceDeductionRoom` (Chapter VIII rooms — F34's step 4) and `priceRentRoom` (rent — step 2). **Steps 1,
  3 and 5 have no measured worth**: pay itself, employer funds, and other income and property (income left out waits
  on W-324; house-loan interest, leave travel and education-loan interest have no bound, `:33-35`).
- **Names.** No type named `Onboarding…` exists (`grep -rli onboarding backend/src/main/java`: nothing).
- `new DraftInputs(` has one caller (`DraftInputs.blank`, `:30`); no test constructs one.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: where "answered" is kept.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** a fifth part of `DraftInputs`, as `ThirdParties` is: changed by the existing patch at
    `/onboarding/answered`, so it has the draft's revisions, conflict handling and encryption for nothing new.
  - C1b: a field of its own on the stored draft with its own write endpoint — outside revisions, and a second way to
    change a draft.
- **C2: the five names** (product vocabulary, F34's order; adopt or reword):
  `PAY`, `PAY_STRUCTURE_AND_RENT`, `EMPLOYER_FUNDS`, `INVESTMENTS_AND_INSURANCE`, `OTHER_INCOME_AND_PROPERTY`.
- **C3: what the read says a step is worth.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** each open step lists the completeness items that belong to it, each with its own measured
    worth, never added (they overlap). Step 2 carries the rent item, step 4 the deduction rooms; steps 1, 3 and 5
    say in words that nothing measures them yet. An answered step lists nothing — the person has said there is no
    more to enter.
  - C3b: one summed figure per step — wrong where items overlap.
- **C4: order.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** any step may be marked answered or open again at any time; "next" is the first open one
    in F34's order. F34: "a user may stop at any step".
  - C4b: steps must be answered in order; a patch out of order is refused.

  `/completeness` returns — its existing tests stay untouched and green.
- Own NPS sits with the deduction rooms (step 4) though F34 names NPS in step 3 for the **employer's** contribution;
  the read follows the measurement's source, and says nothing for step 3.
