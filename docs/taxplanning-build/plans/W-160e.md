## Plan for W-160e — holdings by facts

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** `portfolio` still splits holdings into short-term and
long-term at one year before today (`PortfolioService.getAssets`, `GET …/assets/holding/{SHORT_TERM|LONG_TERM}` and
its Excel), the audit's P7. This item removes that split and gives each holding the facts instead: when each part was
bought and how many days it has been held (D-362 C1a; T1 P7; an API change).

**Small: about 80 main lines removed, 15 added; five integration tests and two API-collection requests removed; unit
tests added. No stored data.**

### What I read (session 35, 2026-10-25)

- W-160e's row; `plans/W-160.md` (P7, C1a: "the holdings view gives each holding's acquisition date and days held, not
  a one-year split").
- `PortfolioController:123–134` (both endpoints); `PortfolioService:810–862` (`getAssets`, `getLongTermHeldAssets`,
  `getShortTermHeldAssets`) and `:898–905` (`downloadTermAssets`); `HoldingType` (`SHORT_TERM`, `LONG_TERM`);
  `TLocalDate.lastYearSameDateInString` (`:25`).
- **The one-year test:** every asset type is split at `transaction_date` one year before today, which is the
  listed-equity rule applied to everything.
- **What a holdings row already carries:** `combineAllDetailsOfEntities` (`:540–577`) gives every grouped row
  (`/stocks/all`, `/mfs`, `/stock/{stockCode}/all`, the date-range and search views) its `transactionQuantities`:
  each buy date and the quantity bought on it. `EntityExportService:48` exports them as their own sheet. So the
  acquisition dates are already served everywhere; days held is not.
- **Readers of what goes** (grep):
  - main: only the controller and service above;
  - `HoldingType` and `lastYearSameDateInString`: no other caller;
  - tests: `PortfolioIntegrationTest`, three `getAssets_*` cases (`:788–840`) and two `downloadTermAssets_*` cases
    (`:984–1020`);
  - `api-collection/Portfolio/Long - Short term.yml` and `… - Download.yml`.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the replacement:**
  - **(a) recommended:** every holdings row gains `daysHeld`, beside `transactionQuantities`: each buy date and the
    days from it to today (`TLocalDate.today()`, in IST). It is filled wherever `transactionQuantities` is, so every
    holdings view carries it. Then the two term endpoints go, with `getAssets`, its two helpers,
    `downloadTermAssets`, `HoldingType` and `lastYearSameDateInString`. No new endpoint: `/stocks/all` is the holdings
    view, now with days held.
  - **(b)** remove the term endpoints only. A client works days held out from the dates `transactionQuantities`
    already gives.
  - **(c)** a new `GET …/assets/holding-days` (and its Excel) beside `/stocks/all`. A second view of the same rows.
- **C2 — the Excel:**
  - **(a) recommended:** no new export. The holdings export (`EntityExportService` `assets`) already writes each buy
    date and quantity. Days held changes every day, so a sheet would be stale the next day.
  - **(b)** a "days held" column on that sheet.
