## Plan for W-067 — the HRA exemption

**What this item is for (one sentence):** exempt the part of house rent allowance the Act exempts, in the old
regime only: per occupied period, the least of the allowance received, rent paid less one-tenth of salary,
and a location's percentage of salary. Then take it off income under "Salaries".

**This plan raises design decisions (C0–C7) before anything is built.**

### What I read

- **The `W-067` row:** *"**creates** `ExemptionNode` — HRA — `DR 06` G1.3 — **per period**; metro % per period;
  `limitedBy` names the limb"*.
- **D-096:** `salary.income` gains `salary.exemptions` here.
- **D-097:** `OccupationPeriods` is this item's input, each measured by `Days`.
- **Rulebook R9:**
  - `SAL-HRA-001`: the least of (a) the allowance received for the relevant period, (b) rent paid less 10% of
    salary, (c) the location's percentage of salary.
  - `SAL-HRA-002`: not owned, and rent actually incurred.
  - `SAL-HRA-003`: the relevant period is the period occupied.
  - `SAL-HRA-004`: the salary base (W-062).
  - `SAL-HRA-005`: disallowed in the new regime (s.202(2)(a)(i)).
  - `SAL-HRA-006`: landlord PAN over ₹1,00,000.
  - H-2: the eight-city list is Rules 2026's; TY 2025-26's is shorter and must be read in r.2A.
  - The worked example (TY 2026-27, Bengaluru, basic 12,00,000 + DA 1,20,000, HRA 6,00,000, rent 3,60,000)
    gives **₹2,28,000**, with limb (b) binding.
- **`DR 13` R-11:** *"The three limbs are computed per period and summed"*, with *"one line per period with its
  own `limitedBy`, and a summary line"*.
- **Sources, as read:**
  - **S2 Sch. III Table Sl. 11 (p.506):** *"(a) Such allowance is specifically granted to meet expenditure
    actually incurred on payment of rent … in respect of residential accommodation occupied by the assessee;
    (b) … to such extent as may be prescribed having regard to the area or place in which such accommodation is
    situated …; (c) the residential accommodation … is not owned by him; and (d) the assessee has actually
    incurred expenditure on payment of rent"*.
  - **1961 s.10(13A) with its Explanation (thc p.73):** the same, with *"(a) the residential accommodation … is
    owned by him; or (b) the assessee has not actually incurred expenditure on payment of rent"* excluded.
  - **S4 r.279(1)–(2) (p.1776), for TY 2026-27:**
    - (a) the allowance received for the relevant period;
    - (b) *"the amount by which the expenditure actually incurred … in payment of rent … exceeds one-tenth of
      the amount of salary due … for the relevant period"*;
    - (c) *"in case of an assessee employed in the location mentioned in column B … such percentage of salary …
      for the relevant period"*: Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad and Bengaluru
      **50%**; any other place **40%**.
    - r.279(2)(a) defines the relevant period as occupation.
  - **Rules 1962 r.2A, for TY 2025-26** (DoR compendium pp.347–348, current to 2010–11; W-062):
    - (b) *"exceeds one-tenth of the amount of salary due … in respect of the relevant period"*;
    - (c)(i) *"where such accommodation is situate at Bombay, Calcutta, Delhi or Madras, one-half"*;
      (c)(ii) *"any other place, two-fifth"*.
- **Code:**
  - The cap shapes are `Fixed`, `PercentOfBase`, `SlabLadder`, `LeastOf`, `AggregateWithCeiling` and
    `PerUnitPerPeriod`. None subtracts, and none picks a percentage by location.
  - `CapContext` holds bases, contributions and counts, and no location.
  - `EnteredFacts` holds employments only; an `Employment` has no period.
  - `NodeResult` is (value, trace) and has no notices.
  - `SalaryBases.of(SAL-SALARY-FOR-HRA, employment)` exists; `Days.apportion` exists.
  - `SEC-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE` exists, with `NotModelled` routes. Coverage: 1961 s.10 is `PLANNED → W-067`.

### Findings

1. **Limb (b) is a subtraction** ("the amount by which rent exceeds one-tenth of salary"), and the one-tenth is
   law. No shape expresses it. D-NOFORMULA allows adding a typed shape.
2. **Limb (c) picks a percentage by location**, and the list differs by year: four cities in r.2A, eight in
   r.279. That is data, and no shape reads a location.
3. **The two years may key limb (c) differently.**
   - r.2A: where the accommodation *"is situate"*.
   - r.279(1)(c): an assessee *"employed in the location"*, under a column headed *"Location of residential
     accommodation"*.
   - For someone who lives in one city and works in another, the TY 2026-27 answer may differ. This is
     interpretive.
4. **Per-period inputs don't exist.** Nothing records occupied periods, their location, ownership or the rent
   for them. HRA and the salary base are entered for the whole year, per employment.
5. **Employments have no period (D-090).** With two employers in one year, an annual HRA can't be matched to
   the months each paid it. One employment is sound; two is not, until employment periods exist.
6. **The landlord-PAN duty (`SAL-HRA-006`) is a notice, not a figure.** No node can emit notices today.
7. **This is several items' worth:** inputs, two shapes, rows, the node, and wiring into `salary.income`.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C0 — split.**
  - **(a) recommended: two items.**
    - **`W-067a`:** the tenancy input, the two cap shapes and the `CapContext` location, with no row or node.
    - **`W-067`:** the rows, `ExemptionNode`, `salary.income` wiring, coverage and catalogue.
    - Each is reviewable alone.
  - **(b)** One item of about eight steps.
- **C1 — the input.**
  - **(a) recommended:** `EnteredFacts.tenancies`, a `List<Tenancy>`, with
    `Tenancy(DateRange occupied, String location, boolean ownedByAssessee, Money rentPaid)`.
    - `rentPaid` is for that window.
    - `location` is a code from the year's table (`MUMBAI` … `BENGALURU`) or the explicit `ANY_OTHER_PLACE`.
      Anything else is refused, naming the year's list, so a typo can't fall silently into 40%.
    - This is what `DR 13` R-11 needs, and the fingerprint covers it.
- **C2 — limb (b):** a seventh shape, `AmountInExcess(BaseId amount, CapShape threshold)`, giving
  max(0, amount − threshold). The row reads it as "rent in excess of 10% of `SALARY`".
- **C3 — limb (c):** an eighth shape, `PercentOfBaseByLocation(BaseId base, List<Band(Set<String> locations,
  Rate percent)>, Rate otherwise)`. It reads the location from a new `CapContext.locations`
  (`Map<BaseId, String>`, keyed `LOCATION`). A location missing from the context fails, as bases do.
- **C4 — the row:** `SAL-HOUSE-RENT-ALLOWANCE` (D-035, the Act's words), `LeastOf`, with limbs:
  - "allowance received" — `PercentOfBase 100% HRA_RECEIVED`;
  - "rent less one-tenth of salary" — `AmountInExcess(RENT_PAID, PercentOfBase 10% SALARY)`;
  - "percentage of salary" — `PercentOfBaseByLocation`.

  Regimes `ExceptIn [NEW_TAX_REGIME]` (s.202(2)(a)(i) / 1961 s.115BAC(2)).
  - **TY 2025-26 (r.2A):** MUMBAI, KOLKATA, DELHI, CHENNAI at 50%; others 40%. The note records that r.2A's
    Bombay, Calcutta and Madras are those cities. `UNVERIFIED`, since r.2A after 2011 is unsearched.
  - **TY 2026-27 (r.279):** the eight cities at 50%; others 40%. `SOURCE_CHECKED`.
- **C5 — finding 3:** use the tenancy's location for both years, per R-11 and r.279's own column heading.
  - Record r.279(1)(c)'s "employed in the location" as open question **OQ-6** for the reviewer, in the row's
    note.
  - The alternative, the place of employment for TY 2026-27, needs an employment location that doesn't exist.
- **C6 — the node** (`W-067` under C0a), `ExemptionNode` (`salary.exemptions`). It requires `salary.gross`,
  `salary.base` and `inputs.components`. For each tenancy, within the year, that is rented and not owned:
  - HRA for the period = the HRA lines × `Days.apportion`;
  - salary for the period = `SAL-SALARY-FOR-HRA` × `Days.apportion`;
  - then the row's `LeastOf`, and one EXEMPTION line per period with its own `limitedBy`.

  Around that:
  - An owned or rent-free tenancy gets a nil line naming `SAL-HRA-002`.
  - In the new regime: no line, and nil.
  - With **more than one employment**: refused, naming finding 5, until employment periods exist.
  - `SalaryIncomeNode` subtracts `salary.exemptions`.
- **C7 — landlord PAN (`SAL-HRA-006`):** not built. It is owed to the first item that gives nodes a notice
  channel, and recorded in Appendix F.
