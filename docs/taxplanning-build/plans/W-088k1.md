   - row 1's text, with FA (No. 2) 2024 s.3(b) applied (READ, both documents);
   - the Acts of 2025 – 2026 searched two ways (by name, and by "clause (42A)") for any later change, per the
     act-chain method;
   - the result: the amended text, reconstructed and quoted.
2. **Explanation 1(i)** (1961) and **s.2(101)(c)** (2025): only the clauses `portfolio`'s facts reach:
   - (b) s.49(1) previous owner (gift and inheritance: `portfolio` records none, so this is noted only);
   - (d) rights, from allotment;
   - (f) bonus, from allotment;
   - (g) demerger, including the demerged company's period.
   Each with its 2025 counterpart.
3. **s.50AA** (1961, as substituted by FA (No. 2) 2024) and its 2025 counterpart. That is "always short-term" for a
   Specified Mutual Fund acquired on or after 1-4-2023, a Market Linked Debenture, and an unlisted bond or debenture.
   It is recorded for the rows to come; the engine has no part for funds or bonds yet (D-302).
4. **Q1, the boundary day.** Both Acts say *"held … for not more than twelve months immediately preceding the date of
   its transfer"*. For a share bought 10-4-2024:
   - **(a) the plain calendar reading:** held twelve months on 10-4-2025. Sold that day it is **short-term** ("not
     more than twelve months"); sold 11-4-2025 it is long-term. This is what `portfolio` computes today
     (`plusMonths(12).isBefore(transfer)`, `HoldingPeriodResolver:115–116`).
   - **(b)** the period counted excluding the day of acquisition (General Clauses Act, 1897 s.9, *"from"*). Under this
     reading the same sale on 10-4-2025 is still short-term, and on 11-4-2025 long-term, so (a) and (b) agree. **I
     will check whether any date differs** (month ends: bought 31-1, sold 28/29-2 of the next year +1) and show you the
     cases.
   - **(c)** something the Department's material shows. Its ITR utility counts the holding period, and its schedule's
     formula may be readable as before (session 21: ITR-2 utility AY 2026-27).

   I put Q1 to you with the text and the worked dates, with AskUserQuestion, and record the answer in D-303.
5. **Status per row:** READ, REPORTED or NOT YET, as in the other sources files. The 1961 thc copy is a consolidation
   (a print), the 2024 and 2025 Acts are Gazette copies.
