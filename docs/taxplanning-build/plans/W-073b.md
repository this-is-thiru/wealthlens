## Plan for W-073b — the accretion on the excess employer contribution

**What this item is for (one sentence):** add to salary the interest, dividend or similar accretion on the part of the
employer's retirement contributions already charged as excess over ₹7,50,000, by the prescribed formula, both years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C5); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-073b` row** (D-125); `DR 01` `PF-AGGCAP-002`.
- **TY 2026-27:** S2 s.17(1)(i) (Gazette 265620 p.39) — *"the annual accretion by way of interest, dividend or any other
  amount of similar nature during the tax year to the balance at the credit of the fund or scheme referred to in clause
  (h), computed in such manner, as may be prescribed (to the extent it relates to the contribution referred to in the
  said clause in any tax year)"*; **S4 r.16** (Gazette 271092 pp.1548–1549, READ): **TP = (PC/2) × R + (PC1 + TP1) × R**;
  PC the year's employer contribution over the limit; PC1, TP1 the earlier years' (from 1-4-2020) excess contributions
  and taxed accretions; R = I ÷ F(avg); I the income accrued in the year; F(avg) = (opening + closing balance) ÷ 2;
  r.16(2)(b): where TP1 + PC1 exceed the opening balance, the excess is ignored. PC's definition reads "₹ 750000 lakhs"
  — a slip, read as ₹7,50,000 (D-125 C3).
- **TY 2025-26:** 1961 s.17(2)(viia) (FA 2020 s.13, Gazette 218938 p.21, READ); **Rules 1962 r.3B**, G.S.R. 155(E),
  5-3-2021, in force 1-4-2021 — the same formula, "Rs. 7.5 lakh", the Note capping TP1 + PC1 at the opening balance
  (READ in a third-party-hosted Gazette scan — `sources/…-perquisite-thresholds.md` X1).
- **Code:** `EmployerContributionNode` (W-073a) charges PC's year's excess via `PERQ-EMPLOYER-CONTRIBUTION`.

### Findings

1. **PC is W-073a's own figure** — the year's aggregate excess the node already computes; the formula reuses it.
2. **PC1, TP1, I and the balances are history and fund facts** — earlier years' excess contributions and taxed
   accretions, the fund's income for the year, its opening and closing balances — entered, not derived.
3. **The formula's "specified fund or scheme"** is the three funds together: I and the balances are the aggregate of
   the employee's accounts in them.
4. **R is a ratio of money to money**, applied to money twice — no existing shape states it; it needs one rounding.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — input:** `EnteredFacts.retirementFundHistory` (one per assessee, optional): PC1 (earlier years' excess
  contributions since 1-4-2020), TP1 (earlier years' taxed accretions), I (income accrued this year in the three funds),
  the opening and closing balances.
- **C2 — node:** `EmployerContributionNode` also computes TP (its PC from W-073a's excess) and adds it to
  `employer.contrib.charges` on a second trace line, citing `PERQ-EMPLOYER-ACCRETION`. Where no history is entered and
  PC is nil, TP is nil; where PC is positive and no fund facts are entered, the accretion is **refused** (it cannot be
  zero by default).
- **C3 — the cap:** PC1 + TP1 capped at the opening balance before multiplying (r.16(2)(b); r.3B Note).
- **C4 — arithmetic:** TP = (PC ÷ 2 + capped(PC1 + TP1)) × I ÷ F(avg), computed exactly and rounded once; F(avg) nil
  with PC or PC1 + TP1 positive is refused. A row `PERQ-EMPLOYER-ACCRETION` (`AllRegimes`, both years) carries the
  Rule's words; the halving and the ratio are the Rule's arithmetic in the node, cited on the line.
- **C5 — verification:** TY 2026-27 `SOURCE_CHECKED`; TY 2025-26 `UNVERIFIED` (r.3B via a third-party-hosted Gazette
  scan; the interim Finance Act, 2024 unread).

- **Nil PC and no history** → **0**; **PC positive, no fund facts** → refused.
