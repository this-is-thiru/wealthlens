### What I found

- **Only `BASIC` leads today.** `Following` has one `leader` (`Following.java:23`). `moved`, `ceiling` and
  `cashImpact` test `equals(following.leader())` (`Allocation.java:43`, `:58`; `AllocationPricing.java:412`). DA in
  the base is held as `fixed`.
- **Where DA could lead, it is refused.** `AllocationPricing.java:167-171` refuses DA as movable wherever a share
  follows and DA is in the base: stated true, or unstated with DA counting for retirement.
- **Where DA moves alone, nothing follows.** When `BASIC` is not movable, nothing follows (`:172-174`). That is right
  only while DA cannot lead.
- **DA at nil, movable.** "In the base" needs DA entered above nil (`:165-166`), and W-238j C3a refuses a stated base with
  no DA entered (`:216-218`). So DA at nil that counts for retirement would move freely with no follower, once the C2a
  refusal is lifted. C3 below handles it.
- **DA is taxed as basic is here.** In the case below, DA and basic are taxed the same: both feed only
  `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES`, there is no HRA, NPS or accommodation, and no RPF-excess row in TY 2026-27. So moving DA
  gives W-238j case 1's bill.

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1: the shape of what leads.**
- **(a) Recommended.** `Following(SortedMap<Movable.Pay, Money> leaders, Money fixed, followers)`.
  - The leaders are the movable parts of the base: `BASIC` when movable, and DA when movable and in the base.
  - `fixed` is the part that does not move: basic when not movable, and DA when in the base but not movable.
  - Each follower is entered × (Σ leaders now + fixed) ÷ (Σ leaders entered + fixed).
  - Each leader's ceiling is its amount + residual × base ÷ (base + funded), the same for either leader, since a rupee
    of either is a rupee of the base.
  - With only `BASIC` leading this reduces to W-238j's formula, so its cases stay green unchanged.
- (b) A list of `Following`s, one per leader. That would make two ratios for one fund, which is wrong.

**C2: what a following change says it follows.**
- **(a) Recommended.** `FollowingChange.follows` becomes `List<String>`: every leader, in code order.
  - This changes the JSON shape. W-238d's `"follows": "BASIC"` was pushed (at 212543d3). api-collection holds no
    response example, and no client is known to read the field.
  - W-238k2 regroups this result anyway.
- (b) Keep a `String`, naming the leaders joined, such as "BASIC, DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE". That is text a reader must
  parse.

**C3: DA movable, entered at nil, where a share follows.**
- **(a) Recommended. Refuse it where DA could be in the base:** DA counts for retirement, with the base unstated. W-238j
  C3a already refuses a stated base with no DA entered. The message: "DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE is not entered, so whether
  the provident fund follows it cannot be told".
- (b) Let a stated base decide even at nil. That lifts W-238j C3a for a movable DA. It is wider than the row, and an
  employer that pays no DA is rare.

**C4: refusals that change wording.**
- **(a) Recommended.** Each reworded refusal keeps its old words and adds the new case:
  - `requireHeldShareEntered`: "… but BASIC is not movable, nor dearness allowance in the provident fund's base …";
  - `requireBaseFactUsed`: "BASIC is not movable, nor dearness allowance …";
  - the "base unstated" refusal (`:181`) names whichever leader would move.
  - The existing assertions at `TaxCalculatorOptimiseTest.java:634` and `:720` keep their substrings, so none is
    weakened.
