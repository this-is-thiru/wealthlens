**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** reconcile a year with several employers' certificates:
- what they deducted between them against the year's bill;
- each certificate correct on its own, the shortfall visible only across them;
- the shortfall attributed to the employers' errors, so T8-1 reads MATERIAL at ₹2,14,500.

### What the design says

- **T8-1** (`08-golden-tests.md:821`): A (₹9,00,000) and B (₹12,00,000) each issue Part C with the ₹75,000 standard
  deduction and a full rebate, so each deducts ₹0. The year's liability is ₹2,14,500. "Expected: a MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE
  verdict of ₹2,14,500, attributed to 'previous employer's salary not declared' (TDS-SAL-12B-001) and 'standard deduction
  granted twice' (SAL-STDDED-003)." W-129's erratum corrected that citation to SAL-STDDED-001/002.
- **M4.2's reasons** include "Only the last employer issued Part C, so it already includes the previous employer's
  figures" (DOC-F130-ANNEX-001, DOC-F130-MULTI-001).

### Findings

1. **The comparison that shows T8-1 is the year's total deducted against the bill.** Each employer's rows 12 and 17
   cover only its own salary in `PER_EMPLOYER` mode, so comparing them with a whole-year computation would show
   differences that are no one's error.
2. **T8-1 attributes one difference to two reasons**, but W-130a's `ReconciliationLine` carries one optional reason.
3. **W-129's assembly gives half the evidence.** The ACTION_REQUIRED notice for a standard deduction granted by each
   employer (SAL-STDDED-001/002) exists. The notice for a nil 1(e) exists only for `LAST_EMPLOYER_CONSOLIDATED`. In
   `PER_EMPLOYER` mode, a later employer whose 1(e) is nil did not hear of the earlier salary: T8-1's "previous
   employer's salary not declared".

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — the lines for several employers:**
  - **(a) `PER_EMPLOYER`:** one line, Part B's total tax deducted summed over the certificates against the bill.
  - **`LAST_EMPLOYER_CONSOLIDATED`:** the consolidating Part C's rows compared as one employer's (W-130a/b), and Part B
    summed over every certificate against the bill. The certificates without Part C still deducted tax.
  - (b) Every comparison summed over certificates.
- **C2 — several reasons on one line** (finding 2):
  - **(a)** `ReconciliationLine.reasons` becomes a list. W-130a/b's single reason becomes a list of one. Their tests move
    from `reason()` to `reasons()`, an expected and listed mover.
  - (b) Keep one reason per line, and put a report-level list of causes beside the lines.
- **C3 — the employer-error reasons:**
  - **(a)** Both EMPLOYER_ERROR:
    - `STANDARD_DEDUCTION_TWICE` (SAL-STDDED-001/002), from W-129's notice;
    - `PREVIOUS_SALARY_UNDECLARED` (TDS-SAL-12B-001), when the consolidating Part C's 1(e) is nil (W-129's notice), or in
      `PER_EMPLOYER` mode when any employer after the first, by period, prints 1(e) nil.
  - Both attach to the summed Part B line when it falls short of the bill.
  - (b) Only the reasons W-129's notices already give.
- **C4 — M4.2's "only the last employer issued Part C":**
  - **(a)** EXPECTED (DOC-F130-ANNEX-001). It explains a consolidating certificate's own Part B falling short of its row 17
    when the earlier employers deducted the rest: the summed line then agrees, so no difference needs it. Not built until
    a line needs it. Recorded in the plan; no row.
  - (b) Build it now on the consolidating certificate's own Part B line.
