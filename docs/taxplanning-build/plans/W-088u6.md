## Plan for W-088u6 — the rebuild's double-counted buy charge

**What this item is for (one sentence):** the startup `trade_outcomes` rebuild charges each lot's buy charges once
across every sell that consumes it, through the same `LotChargeAllocator` V1 and V2 already use (B-7, B-8), instead
of re-dividing the whole charge by the remaining quantity on each sell (D-275).

**This is a behaviour change, on purpose:** a lot sold across more than one sell gets smaller buy charges, and so a
lower cost and a higher gain, on every sell after its first; and the rebuild's buy charges are scaled to the paisa,
as V1's and V2's already are.

### What I read (session 25, 2026-10-20, at `3958bb0e`)

- **The defect** (D-275):
  - `TradeMatchingService.figures:224–225` computes `(lot.getBrokerCharges() / lot.getQuantity()) * matchedQty`,
    with `lot.getQuantity()` the *remaining* quantity, and the same for misc charges.
  - `matchSellToLots:155` reduces only the quantity. `brokerCharges` / `miscCharges` are never reduced, and no
    record is kept of what was already charged.
  - The rebuild (`TransactionBasedTradeOutcomeMigration:104–113`) feeds every sell, in date order, through the same
    lots.
  - Worked: a lot of 10 with ₹10 broker charge, sold 5 then 5: 10 ÷ 10 × 5 = ₹5, then 10 ÷ 5 × 5 = ₹10, so ₹15
    against ₹10 paid. B-8's example (a 3-unit lot carrying ₹10, sold one unit at a time) gives 3.33…, 5.00, 10.00:
    ₹18.33.
- **The fix already exists for V1 and V2:** `LotChargeAllocator` (`portfolio/service`). Its Javadoc names this
  exact bug (B-7, B-8) and says "**One implementation on purpose.** V1 and V2 both allocate, and a second copy is
  how two of them eventually disagree".
  - Its rule, `shareOf(paid, alreadyDeducted, sellQuantity, remainingQuantity)`, takes
    `TMoney.scale((paid − alreadyDeducted) × sellQuantity ÷ remainingQuantity)`, so the last sell takes the
    remainder exactly.
  - It records the running total on the lot (`AssetEntity.allocatedBuyCharges` / `allocatedBuyMiscCharges`).
  - Its public methods take an `AssetEntity`. The rebuild's lots are `TradeMatchingService.BuyLot`, which has no
    allocated fields.
- **Rounding:** `TMoney.scale` is `HALF_UP` to the paisa (`TMoney:34–35`). Today the rebuild's figures are
  unscaled.
- **When the rebuild runs:** at startup, only when `trade_outcomes` is empty
  (`shared/config/TradeOutcomeMigrationRunner:46`). Rows it already wrote are not rewritten by this fix. They are
  recognisable: their `sourceBuyLotId` is the synthetic `lot-<code>-<date>` (`TradeMatchingService.lotOf`), which
  V1 and V2 never write (they write the asset's id). That decides C2.
- **Tests that pass through the changed lines:** `TradeMatchingServiceTest` (14). Every case sells a lot once,
  except `matchSellToLots_whenSoldEitherSideOfTheYearEnd_writesTheYearAsPortfolioDoes`, which sells twice with
  charges 0. And W-088u5's `matchSellToLots_whenOneSellSpansTwoLots_writesEveryField`: L1 whole (scale(20 × 10 ÷ 10)
  = 20), L2 half (scale(10 × 5 ÷ 10) = 5), so its expected values hold unchanged.

### Design

1. **`TradeMatchingService.BuyLot`** gains `allocatedBrokerCharges` and `allocatedMiscCharges` (`double`). They
   start at 0, a builder primitive's default, and mean what `AssetEntity`'s two fields mean: what earlier sells
   have already charged.
2. **`LotChargeAllocator`** gains `deductBroker(BuyLot lot, double sellQuantity, double remainingQuantity)` and
   `deductMisc(BuyLot …)`. They are overloads over its existing private `shareOf`, so the rule stays one
   implementation, and each records its share with `TMoney.add`, as the `AssetEntity` overloads do. The class
   Javadoc's "V1 and V2 both allocate" becomes "V1, V2 and the `trade_outcomes` rebuild".
3. **`matchSellToLots`** deducts once per match, in the loop, before the lot's quantity is reduced:
   `LotChargeAllocator.deductBroker(lot, matchedQty, lotRemainingQty)` and `deductMisc`, the way V1 deducts once per
   sell (`PortfolioService:607–608`). It hands both shares to `matchedTrade` → `figures`, which no longer computes
   the buy charges (`:224–225` removed). Its total-buy expression stays
   `(lot.getBuyPrice() * matchedQty) + buyBrokerCharges + buyMiscCharges`.
4. **Docs:** WI "Built"; STATE D-276; CHECKPOINT. No TS row.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — how to fix it:**
  - **(a) recommended:** through `LotChargeAllocator`, as above. One rule for all three writers, as its Javadoc
    requires; the rebuild's buy charges become paisa-scaled like V1's and V2's.
  - **(b)** a local fix: reduce `lot.brokerCharges` / `miscCharges` by each share inside the matcher. Unscaled, but
    a second copy of the rule, the thing the allocator's Javadoc says leads to disagreement.
- **C2 — rows an earlier rebuild already wrote:**
  - **(a) recommended:** place a row to find them (`sourceBuyLotId` starting `lot-`) and correct them. Correcting
    stored money is a data change with its own plan and backup, not part of a code fix. Its place is yours.
  - **(b)** nothing: rely on no environment having run the rebuild over lots sold more than once. I cannot verify
    that from here.
