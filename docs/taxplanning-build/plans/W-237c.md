## Plan for W-237c — breach intimation

**For:** when the owner learns of a personal data breach, each affected user is told what r.7(1) requires through
their account and their registered email, the Board is told on time, and the app keeps the record of who was told
and when, which the Board's report must contain (Act s.8(6); Rules r.7; D-470).

### What I read (session 51; Gazette copies re-checked: Act `4deb2398…`, Rules `eabc7d05…`)

- **Act s.2(u):** a breach is *"any unauthorised processing of personal data or accidental disclosure, acquisition,
  sharing, use, alteration, destruction or loss of access to personal data, that compromises the confidentiality,
  integrity or availability of personal data"*. **s.8(5):** reasonable safeguards to prevent one. **s.8(6):** intimate
  *"the Board and each affected Data Principal … in such form and manner as may be prescribed"* (248045 p.7).
- **Rules r.7(1)** (267650 p.26): *"to the best of its knowledge"*, *"in a concise, clear and plain manner and without
  delay, through her user account or any mode of communication registered by her"*: (a) description, nature, extent,
  timing; (b) consequences relevant to her; (c) the mitigation done or under way; (d) the safety measures she may take;
  (e) a contact who answers her queries.
- **r.7(2):** to the Board, (a) *"without delay"*: description, nature, extent, timing, location, likely impact;
  (b) *"within seventy-two hours of becoming aware … or within such longer period as the Board may allow on a request
  made in writing"*: (i) updated detail, (ii) the facts and reasons, (iii) mitigation, (iv) findings on who caused it,
  (v) remedial measures against recurrence, (vi) *"a report regarding the intimations given to affected Data
  Principals"*.
- **Not in these copies:** the channel by which the Board takes an intimation (portal, address, form). r.7 prescribes
  content and timing only. I have not looked elsewhere and will not guess (Risk 1).
- **What exists:** a user's only registered mode of communication is the account's email (`auth/entity/UserDetail`:
  `email`, `password`, `roles`). The app cannot send mail (no mail dependency). `shared` may not depend on `auth`
  (`shared/package-info.java:3`; `auth` depends on `shared`), so `shared/privacy` cannot list users itself.
  Detection itself (logs, r.6(1)(c), (e)) is W-237e.

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — how each user is told.** (a) **Both modes r.7(1) names:** a SUPER_USER posts a breach notice (r.7(1)(a)–(d) as
fields; (e) is the privacy contact, added by the app); each affected user reads it at
`GET /privacy/breach-notices/user/{email}`, and the app records when each user first read it. The owner also sends the
same text by email: a SUPER_USER read returns the notice text and the affected users' emails, ready to paste into a mail
client. (b) As (a), with the app sending the email itself: a mail dependency, SMTP credentials as a new secret, and
bounces to handle. (c) A runbook procedure only, and no record in the app.

**C2 — who is affected.** (a) **The notice names its scope: a list of emails, or `EVERY_REGISTERED_USER`.** "Every user"
is resolved through a `RegisteredUsers` interface in `shared/privacy`, implemented in `auth` (a decided seam: the module
direction forbids the other way round). The resolved list is stored on the notice, so the record says exactly who was told.
(b) Only explicit lists; for "everyone" the owner exports the emails by `mongosh` and pastes them.

**C3 — the Board, and the 72 hours.** (a) **The notice records the facts: `became_aware_at` (entered by the owner),
`board_first_intimated_at` and `board_report_sent_at` (each recorded by a SUPER_USER call when done), and any
extension the Board allowed (`board_report_due_at` moved, with the Board's reference).** The due time is
`became_aware_at` + `app.privacy.breach.board-report-hours` (72, configuration with its source, like W-237b's 90 days).
A SUPER_USER report read returns r.7(2)(b)(vi): per user, when posted, when read, and when emailed (recorded by the owner).
No "late" flag is stored. (b) Track nothing in the app: the runbook procedure alone.

**C4 — the procedure.** (a) **A runbook section "On a personal data breach": the steps in order, with clocks — record
awareness, tell the Board (channel per Risk 1), post the notice, email users, the 72-hour report with (i)–(vi), each
call and a read-only check.** It is not a deploy action. It sits in §5 (standing operations) and is renamed so §5 is no
longer the charge engine only.
