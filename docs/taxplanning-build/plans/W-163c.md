## Plan for W-163c — each statutory rate cited to the law

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** every rate on the four statutory cards, which are STT,
stamp duty, the SEBI fee and GST, is traced to the law that sets it, and recorded with its source. The cards then
say where they came from and when that was checked, like the broker cards.

**Source:** `plans/W-163.md` (D-380, C3a); W-163b (D-382, C3a: the cards carry no `sourceUrl` / `verifiedOn` until
this item).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what a statutory card's `sourceUrl` and `verifiedOn` hold:**
  - **(a) recommended:**
    - `sourceUrl`: the official URL of the **instrument that sets most of that card's rates** (Chapter VII of the
      Finance (No. 2) Act, 2004 for the equity and fund cards; the CGST rate notification for the account card).
    - **Each rule's `notes`:** its own instrument, provision and the row of a new sources record,
      `sources/2026-10-28-statutory-charge-rates.md`.
    - `verifiedOn`: the date the law was read.
    - **The tests:** `everyCardSays…` covers every card again, and the pin test goes.
  - **(b)** `sourceUrl` points to the repository's sources record rather than a primary document. That is one place
    to look, but it is not `https://`, so the test would need a statutory exception.
- **C2 — what counts as cited:**
  - **(a) recommended: READ wherever an official copy is reachable.** REPORTED, from a relay such as a ChatGPT share
    or a commercial consolidation, is used only where no official copy can be reached, with your ruling for each
    rate, as D-294.
  - **(b)** REPORTED is enough for every rate.

## Step 1 progress (session 37) — readings so far

- **STT Sl. 5:** Finance Act 2013 s.135(v), thc copy (an itatonline print), p.45, READ: *"against Sl. No. 5, under
  column (3) relating to rate, for the figures "0.25", the figures "0.001" shall be substituted"*, with effect from
  1 June 2013. Sl. 2A is a sale on a recognised exchange (p.45). The fund card prices a redemption, which is a sale
  to the Mutual Fund (s.97(13)(b), record row 1a), so Sl. 5 applies, at 0.001 %. **Agrees.**
- **Stamp duty:**
  - **The rates:** Finance Act 2019 (No. 7 of 2019), egazette **2019/198304**, p.15, READ. Schedule I **Article 56A**
    (the plan said 57A): *"(a) issue of security other than debenture; 0.005% (b) transfer of security other than
    debenture on delivery basis; 0.015% (c) transfer of security other than debenture on non-delivery basis;
    0.003%"*. "securities" (s.2(23A), p.10) includes SCRA s.2(h) securities, and SCRA s.2(h)(id) is *"units or any
    other such instrument issued to the investors under any mutual fund scheme"* (thc consolidated print, p.7,
    image read by eye). **All three rates agree.**
  - **In force:** Part I comes in "on such date as the Central Government may … appoint" (s.11). S.O. 1226(E) of
    30-3-2020, egazette **2020/218957**, READ, substitutes *"the 1st day of July 2020"* for "the 1st day of April
    2020" in S.O. 115(E), which amended S.O. 4419(E) of 10-12-2019. No later deferral was searched for after April
    2020.
  - **No later change to Article 56A:** Finance Act 2020 Part I (ss.142 – 144, thc copy pp.60 – 61, READ) adds a
    s.9A IFSC proviso and s.73B. FA 2021 s.126 (s.8G) and FA 2023 s.166 (Article 47) touch the Stamp Act but not 56A.
    FA 2022, FA 2024, F(No. 2)A 2024, FA 2025 and FA 2026 (egazette copies) do not name the Stamp Act.
