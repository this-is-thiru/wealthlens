**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a person whose net agricultural income is more than ₹5,000
pays tax on their total income at the rate that income *plus* the agricultural income reaches. The engine ignores
agricultural income today, so it understates the tax. This item makes it compute the law's two-ladder formula from
rule data, for both shipped years.

### What the law, the form and the code say

- **TY 2025-26: Finance Act, 2026 (4 of 2026) s.2(2)**, Gazette CG-DL-E-31032026-271439, pp.2 – 3:
  - (a) "Where an assessee as specified in column B of the Table below, has, in the previous year, any net agricultural
    income exceeding five thousand rupees, in addition to the total income, and the total income exceeds the maximum
    amount not chargeable to income-tax as specified in column C … the net agricultural income shall be taken into
    account, only for the purpose of charging income-tax in respect of the total income."
  - The Table's column C: Sl. 1 every other individual, HUF, AOP/BOI, artificial juridical person, Rs. 2,50,000.
    Sl. 2 a resident aged sixty to under eighty, Rs. 3,00,000. Sl. 3 a resident aged eighty or more, Rs. 5,00,000.
    Sl. 4 an "assessee whose income is chargeable to tax under section 115BAC(1A)", Rs. 4,00,000.
  - (b) "Zo = Xo – Yo". Xo is the tax on the Aggregate Income "at the rates specified in Paragraph A of Part I-A of the
    First Schedule or sub-section (1A) of section 115BAC … as if such AIo were the total income". Yo is the tax on "the
    net agricultural income increased by a sum as specified in column C", at the same rates. "Aggregate Income (AIo) =
    Total income + Net agricultural income."
- **TY 2026-27: Finance Act, 2026 s.3(2)**, pp.9 – 10. Same text for "the tax year", with "₹ 5000", column C
  ₹ 250000 / 300000 / 500000, and Sl. 4 for an "assessee whose income is chargeable to tax under section 202"
  at ₹ 400000. The formula is "Zn = Xn – Yn", at Paragraph A of Part I-B or s.202.
- **Net agricultural income is defined by s.2(7)(b), p.9, and s.3(18)(c), p.29:** "the total amount of agricultural income,
  from whatever source derived … computed in accordance with the rules contained in Part IV-A [IV-B] of the First
  Schedule".
  - Part IV-A starts at p.101; Part IV-B at p.104.
  - Rules 1 – 3 compute each kind of agricultural income as if under other sources, business or house property.
  - Rule 4 sets the share of tea, rubber and coffee income that is agricultural: 60%, 65%, 60% or 75%.
  - Rule 6 sets losses off within the year. Rule 7 deducts the State's agricultural income-tax.
  - Rule 8 (p.102) sets off losses of the previous eight years. Rule 8(3) allows this only for a loss the Assessing
    Officer determined.
- **Advance tax: s.3(14), pp.27 – 29.** The same formula applies "in computing the 'advance tax' payable".
  `AdvanceTaxNode` reads `tax.netpayable` (`AdvanceTaxNode.java:53`, `:63`), so advance tax follows with no change.
- **The Department's form (ITR-2 for TY 2025-26)**, Gazette CG-DL-E-30032026-271435, transcribed in
  `sources/2026-11-13-itr-schedules-ty-2025-26.md`:
  - Part B-TI (lines 2519 – 2522): "13 Income which is included in 12 and chargeable to tax at special rates", "14 Net
    agricultural income/ any other income for rate purpose", "15 Aggregate income (12-13+14) [applicable if (12-13)
    exceeds maximum amount not chargeable to tax]".
  - Part B-TTI (lines 2534 – 2540): "2a Tax at normal rates on 15 of Part B-TI", "2c Rebate on agricultural income",
    "2d Tax Payable on Total Income (2a + 2b - 2c)". Then "3 Rebate under section 87A" and the surcharge come after 2d.
  - Schedule EI (lines 2327 – 2331): "i Gross Agricultural receipts", "ii Expenditure incurred on agriculture", "iii
    Unabsorbed agricultural loss of previous eight assessment years", "iv Net Agricultural income for the year
    (i – ii – iii) (enter nil if loss)".
  - (Row 14 points to "3 of Schedule EI", which is *other* exempt income. It reads like a misprint for 2(iv). Nothing
    here relies on it.)
- **The engine today:**
  - `TaxByBasisNode.java:99` and `:105` apply the ladder to total income, or to total income less the special-rate
    gains. Nothing reads agricultural income.
  - `RebateNode.java:56`, `:67` read `tax.bybasis`.
  - The surcharge's marginal relief recomputes `tax.afterrebate` at the threshold income
    (`SurchargeNode.java:143`, `:314 – 316`).
  - `AGRICULTURAL_INCOME.json:3 – 4` asks the amount for the ITR bar only: "The engine does not bring agricultural
    income into the rates (W-258)".
  - An unanswered amount throws when a condition reads it (`Declarations.java:64`).

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — the law's figures as rule data:**
  - **(a)** A new rule set `RS-RATE-AGRICULTURAL` (`AT_MOST_ONE`, ground `REGIME_AND_CONDITIONS`) with four rows:
    `RATE-AGRICULTURAL-FIRST-SCHEDULE`, `-FIRST-SCHEDULE-AGE-60TO79`, `-FIRST-SCHEDULE-AGE-80` (regime
    `FIRST_SCHEDULE_RATES`) and `RATE-AGRICULTURAL-NEW-TAX-REGIME`.
    - Each row's conditions are `DeclaredAmountAbove(NET_AGRICULTURAL_INCOME, 5000)` plus its RS-RATE-SLAB twin's
      conditions, copied exactly.
    - Its cap is `Fixed`, set to column C.
    - One version from 2025-04-01, open-ended like the slab rows (`RATE-SLAB-FIRST-SCHEDULE.json`, validity from
      2025-04-01). `provision1961` is Finance Act, 2026 s.2(2); `provision2025` is s.3(2).
    - The ₹5,000 and each column C are data, so a Finance Act that moves either is a new version.
  - (b) The ₹5,000 in a row, but column C taken from the ladder's nil band, as `shiftShortfall` does
    (`TaxByBasisNode.java:112`). One row fewer per regime, but it ties the Table's column C to the slab ladder, which
    the Act states separately.
- **C2 — the input:**
  - **(a)** A new amount question `NET_AGRICULTURAL_INCOME`: "What was your net agricultural income for the tax year —
    gross agricultural receipts less agricultural expenditure and unabsorbed agricultural losses of the previous eight
    years, nil if a loss?" (drafted by Claude, for you to adopt).
    - It is valid 2025-04-01 – 2027-03-31, like `AGRICULTURAL_INCOME` (D-748 scope).
    - The bar's `AGRICULTURAL_INCOME` stays as it is. The r.164 bar says "agricultural income"; the Finance Act says
      "net agricultural income", computed under Part IV. Those are two terms.
    - Part IV's own computations (rules 1 – 8) are the person's to make, as the form's Schedule EI asks.
  - (b) Reuse `AGRICULTURAL_INCOME` for both, reworded to "net". One question, but the bar and the rates would then
    read the same number for two different legal terms.
- **C3 — the question left unanswered:**
  - **(a)** No integration, and a `WARNING` notice on the `tax.bybasis` line: the tax assumes no net agricultural
    income above ₹5,000 until the question is answered. This is W-090a's rule that an unanswered question never blocks
    the tax figure (`plans/W-090a.md`, Five roles, product manager), kept loud rather than silent.
  - (b) Refuse the computation until it is answered. Every draft would need the answer before its first figure.
- **C4 — what "total income" means in the test and in AIn:**
  - **(a)** Total income less the income charged at special rates, as ITR-2 Part B-TI rows 13 – 15 do (Gazette 271435).
  - (b) The Act's words read literally.
  - Why (a): under (b), a person whose slab income is under column C, but whose special-rate gains lift total income
    over it, gets Xn below Yn, a negative tax. So the literal reading cannot be meant once special-rate income exists.
    This is a reading: the Department's form, a Gazette text, makes it explicit.
- **C5 — the two marginal reliefs:**
  - **(a)** Both read the integrated tax.
    - The surcharge relief's U is recomputed at the threshold with the same net agricultural income
      (`SurchargeNode.java:314 – 316` calls the same node).
    - s.156(2)(b)'s excess relief reads the tax after integration.
    - Both follow s.3(2): integration *is* the charge "in respect of the total income", and B-TTI applies 87A and the
      surcharge after 2d.
  - (b) Leave integration out of both comparisons.
  - This is a reading. No Gazette text read today decides it beyond s.3(2)'s words and the form's order.
- **C6 — the return's cells:**
  - **(a)** Split. W-258 (this plan) builds the engine, the data, the question and the explanations. **W-258a** is placed
    next on the board for the ITR-2 cells: B-TTI 2a as Xn and 2c as Yn, B-TI 14 / 15, and Schedule EI 2(iv).
    - `REBATE_AGRICULTURAL.json:5 – 6` is a manual entry whose guidance says agricultural income "is not computed
      into tax". That becomes untrue with W-258, and W-258a corrects it.
    - `TAX_AT_NORMAL_RATES` reads `tax.bybasis` at slab rates, which would show Zn and not 2a's Xn until W-258a.
  - (b) All in one item: `TaxByBasis` gains Xn and Yn now, and the cells follow.
