## Plan for W-088d — capital-loss carry-forward

**What this item is for (one sentence):** set off a capital loss carried in from earlier years against this year's
capital gains, for up to eight years, with a long-term loss used only against long-term gains (S2 s.111; 1961 s.74).
It also carries this year's unabsorbed loss forward in the trace, as W-081b did for house property.

**This plan raises choices C1–C6. Nothing is coded until you approve.**

### What I read (session 21, 2026-10-18)

- **The row (D-242):** *"S2 s.111 / 1961 s.74 (`SETOFF-CF-CG-001`) — eight tax years, a long-term loss only
  against long-term gains — the ledger as W-081b built it for house property"*.
- **The law, read this session:**
  - **S2 s.111** (Gazette 265620 p.148):
    - (1)(a) a capital-gains loss not set off under s.108 *"shall be carried forward to the following tax year"*;
      (i) a short-term loss is set off *"only against the income under the head 'Capital gains' … in respect of any
      other capital asset"*; (ii) a long-term loss *"only against … any other long-term capital asset"*;
    - (1)(b) what is left carries again;
    - (2) *"No loss shall be carried forward under this section for more than eight tax years immediately
      succeeding the tax year for which the loss was first computed."*
  - **S2 s.121** (p.158): *"no loss which has not been determined in pursuance of a return filed under section
    263(1), shall be carried forward and set off under section 111(1)"*.
  - **S2 s.536(2)(n)** (p.493): a capital loss *"referred to in section 74 of the repealed Income-tax Act, brought
    forward from the tax year beginning before the 1st April, 2026"* is set off *"in accordance with the manner
    provided in the repealed Income-tax Act"* against capital gains under the 2025 Act, *"upto eight financial years
    immediately succeeding the financial year in which such loss was first computed"*. That is the same asymmetry
    and the same window, so a TY 2025-26 loss carries into TY 2026-27 unchanged.
  - **1961 s.74(1), (2)** (thc p.325), sub-section (1) as substituted by **FA 2002 s.29** w.e.f. 1-4-2003. The
    same Goa reprint as row 15 (Series I No. 36, 4-12-2003, PDF p.17); its OCR text reads *"In section 74 of the
    Income-tax Act, with effect from the 1st day of April, 2003, (a) for sub-section (1), the following sub-section
    shall be substituted"*. The text is (a) short-term against any capital gains, (b) long-term against gains *"not
    being a short-term capital asset"*, and (2) eight assessment years.
  - **1961 s.80** (p.327): the same return condition, *"in accordance with the provisions of sub-section (3) of
    section 139"*.
  - **Amendments 2019–2026:** none to s.74 or s.80. I searched the 12 Finance / Taxation Laws Acts' egazette text
    (earlier sessions' copies) for "section 74"; the only hits are CGST s.74.
  - **The new regime:** s.202(2)(b)(i) / s.115BAC(2)(ii)(a) bar only a carried loss *"attributable to"* a deduction
    the regime disallows. A capital loss is not, so it carries in both regimes.

### Findings

1. **What was absorbed in earlier years is the tax side's history, not the portfolio's.** Earlier returns set the
   loss against gains `portfolio` may not hold, or under another regime. So the ledger is the source (as W-081b), and
   `CapitalGainsInput.broughtForward` (an amount with no absorption) can only be wrong or unused.
2. **The return condition applies to capital losses** (unlike house property, s.110). The engine cannot see old
   returns, so the ledger entry must say it.
3. **No Act orders several years' losses, or a year's two terms.** W-081b's declared default (oldest year first)
   and W-088a's (a long-term loss first, then short-term, each taking the parts in `SETOFF-WITHIN-CAPITAL-GAINS`'s
   order) fit.

### Cases (worked by hand)

- **A** (TY 2026-27, old regime). This year: short-term listed gain 1,00,000; long-term listed 3,00,000.
  - Ledger:
    - TY 2023-24: long-term loss 2,00,000 with 50,000 absorbed, and short-term 30,000;
    - TY 2024-25: short-term 40,000.
  - Steps:
    1. 2023-24 long-term: 1,50,000 off the long-term listed part, leaving 1,50,000;
    2. 2023-24 short-term: 30,000 off the short-term listed part (the slab part is nil), leaving 70,000;
    3. 2024-25 short-term: 40,000 off short-term listed, leaving 30,000.
  - Result: short-term listed 30,000; long-term listed 1,50,000; the total falls by 2,20,000. Nothing still carried.
- **B (the window):** TY 2026-27. A 2018-19 loss of 10,000 is used (2026-27 is the eighth year after it); a 2017-18
  loss of 10,000 is shown expired.
- **C (the asymmetry):** a long-term loss of 1,00,000 brought in, and only a short-term listed gain of 50,000. Nothing
  is absorbed; *"still carried forward 1,00,000"*.
- **D:** case A's TY 2024-25 entry with the flag false. The 40,000 is not set off; short-term listed is 70,000; the
  line names s.121.
- **E:** case A in TY 2025-26 (1961 citations), with the same figures.
- **F:** a new-regime year: the same as A (no disallowed part).
