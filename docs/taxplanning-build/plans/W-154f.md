## Plan for W-154f — asset transfer wear and tear by rule data

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** how a transferred asset's wear and tear is worked out —
on the reducing balance for computers and cars, as a share of the cost for each completed year for the rest — is today
an `if` on `TransferredAssetKind.OTHER` in `AssetPerquisite`, with the rate read out of the row's cap. This item
makes the method and the rate one definition on each `RS-PERQ-ASSET-TRANSFER` row (D-325 Q10; audit P4
`AssetPerquisite:85`, `:92`; N1 the asset method by kind).

**This plan raises C1.**

### What I read (session 30, 2026-10-23)

- **The code:** `node/AssetPerquisite.java` whole (110 lines).
  - `transferred` (`:82–94`): the row by `asset.kind` (W-088m2); `OTHER` → cost less `rate.ofShare(cost, years,
    1)`, floored at nil; else `rate.leftAfter(cost, years)`. The words (`:90–93`) are "… at <rate> of the cost" or
    "… on the reducing balance".
  - `rateOf` (`:97–103`) reads the cap's `PercentOfBase` percent and throws otherwise. It is a cap used as a rate
    holder.
- **The rows:** `PERQ-ASSET-TRANSFER-CAR` (20%), `-ELECTRONIC` (50%), `-OTHER` (10%), both years, all
  `SOURCE_CHECKED`, each a `PercentOfBase` of `ASSET_COST` with an `asset.kind` condition. Their notes quote the
  method: "by reducing balance method" (car, electronic), "of the cost of the asset for each completed year" (other).

  - `PackJson.rate`.

- **Tests:** `ExpenseAndAssetPerquisiteTest:298–330`: car (both years), laptop, other (3 years; 12 years → nil).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — where the method lives:**
  - **(a) recommended: a definition replacing the cap.** `WearAndTear(Rate rate, WearAndTearMethod method)` in
    `rules/definition`, with the enum `WearAndTearMethod { REDUCING_BALANCE, SHARE_OF_COST_EACH_YEAR }`. Each
    row's cap becomes `{"type": "WearAndTear", "percent": "20", "method": "REDUCING_BALANCE"}` and so on, with the
    percentages copied from today's caps. `rateOf`'s cap-as-rate-holder goes. The node switches on the method, the
    arithmetic shape the row names (as `VehiclePath`, D-332).
  - **(b)** keep the caps and add the method elsewhere. A version cannot hold a cap and a definition, so the method
    would need a second row per kind.
