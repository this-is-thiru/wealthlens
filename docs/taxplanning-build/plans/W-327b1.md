**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption):**
- `ALLOWANCE_DISABILITY_NOT_LISTED`: "Nothing is exempt: your disability is not among those this year's rules list for this allowance."
- `WITHIN_LIMIT`: "All of it is exempt: it is within the limit of {limit}."
- `ALLOWANCE_MONTHLY_LIMIT`: "It is limited to {limit}, the monthly limit for the months covered."
- `HRA_NOT_RENTED`: "Nothing is exempt for this home: it is your own, or no rent was paid."
- `LTC_JOURNEYS_IN_BLOCK`: "Nothing is exempt for this journey: the rules exempt only {journeys} journeys in each block of {years} calendar years."
- `LTC_ASSISTANCE_RECEIVED`: "It is limited to the travel assistance you received."
- `LTC_CHILD_BEYOND_LIMIT`: "Nothing is exempt for this child: the concession covers only {children} surviving children."
- `MEDICAL_ABROAD_EXCLUDED`: "{excluded} of it is not taxed."
- `MEDICAL_TRAVEL_CONDITION_NOT_MET`: "None of it is excluded: a condition for excluding the travel is not met."
- `RETIREMENT_ALLOWED_BEFORE`: "Nothing is deducted: a deduction under this entry was already allowed in {year}."
- `RETIREMENT_BARRED`: "Nothing is deducted: the rules bar a deduction under this entry this year."
- `COMMUTED_PENSION_LIMIT`: "It is limited to {limit}, the share of the pension's commuted value the law exempts."
- `COMMUTED_PENSION_LIMIT_WITH_GRATUITY`: "It is limited to {limit}, the smaller share of the pension's commuted value the law exempts when you also received gratuity."
- `RETIREMENT_CEILING_USED`: "The ceiling across all your employers and years is reduced by the {before} allowed in earlier years."
- `RETIREMENT_CEILING_USED_THIS_YEAR`: "The ceiling across all your employers and years is reduced by the {before} allowed in earlier years and the {thisYear} allowed on this year's earlier payments."
- `SALARY_INCLUDES_PERQUISITES`: "This is the value of your perquisites, worked out on their own lines."
- `SALARY_INCLUDES_EMPLOYER_EXCESS`: "This is your employer's retirement contributions above the limit, worked out on their own lines."
- `SALARY_INCLUDES_PENSION_CONTRIBUTION`: "This is your employer's contribution to your pension-scheme account."
- `SALARY_INCLUDES_AGNIVEER`: "This is the Central Government's contribution to your Agniveer Corpus Fund account."
- `CAP_APPORTIONED`: "The law changed during the year, so each part of the year has its own limit for its share of the year."
- `EMPLOYER_ACCRETION_SHARE`: "It is the share of the fund's income this year that is earned on contributions above the limit, this year's and earlier years' taxed ones."
