### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — where a plot is entered.**

- **(a) Recommended: refuse it on the broker trade paths now, and place a property entry of its own.** One guard in
  `validateAssetRequest` (V1, V2) and its twin in `AssetRequestParser` (the upload), as the Gold Bond's maturity is
  guarded (D-386): a `LAND_OR_BUILDING` trade gets 400 naming why. A new row, **W-088g9**, builds a property entry —
  acquisition and transfer with their expenses carried by code (so D-375's rows can classify them), and the facts
  W-088g6 – g8 need (the stamp-duty value, improvements, the 1-4-2001 value), writing the `trade_outcomes` row the
  summary reads. Why: on the broker path a plot gets a broker, exchange and segment that mean nothing (row 1), V2
  zeroes what the buyer entered (row 3), and no purchase expense can reach the gain (row 6) — every land figure would
  be overstated, silently. Refusing costs no user anything today (row 7), and the engine's land work stays tested
  (row 8).
- (b) Keep a plot on the broker path and give it coded expenses — a request field for coded charges on the trade, read
  by the charge path when no card applies. Broker, exchange and segment still mean nothing for it, and it changes the
  charge path for every asset to serve one.
- (c) Keep it as is and pin today's behaviour with tests, as the row literally reads. Not recommended: the tests
  would prove a land buy's entered charges are zeroed and its expenses lost.

**C2 — the zeroing of entered charges on V2 for any asset with no card (row 3).** It is wider than land: a Gold Bond,
bond or FD bought on V2 with `authoritative` on has its entered charge replaced by nil for the same reason, and
`applyComputedCostBasis`'s Javadoc says the opposite should happen.

- **(a) Recommended: place it as its own row, W-223** — a charge-path decision (should `NO_SCHEDULE` count as
  "absent" for the cost basis?) with its own tests and a runbook entry for lots already written; not built here.
- (b) Fix it in this item. Not recommended: it changes every no-card asset's cost basis, which is not this item's
  scope (CLAUDE.md §2).
