## Plan for W-122b — the band and the coarse label

> **Approved as recommended — C1a – C5a, draft content adopted** (owner, session 101, 2026-11-16). Every path below was read on 2026-11-16 at `d06c8062`; grep each
> again before relying on it. Probe: code read, plus one throwaway timing test (run once, deleted).

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** every computation of a draft says whether it is coarse,
meaning not all five onboarding steps are answered. While it is coarse, it gives a band: the tax as the draft stands,
down to the tax with every room completeness measures filled at once. The view says in words that income not yet entered
is outside the band (PRD F34.2, F34.3; `DR 20` F34; W-122 C1a, C3a, C5a, D-701).

**Probe (2026-11-16):**
- **The view.** `DraftComputationView` (`compute/DraftComputationView.java:15`) is built in one place,
  `DraftComputationService.compute` (`:74`). It is **stored** as JSON (`:84`) and read back by receipt (`:179`) and by
  `:163`. A band on the view is therefore stored data, and every view stored before must still read.
- **The steps.** `OnboardingStep` has five values (`workspace/OnboardingStep.java:8`). The draft records which are
  answered (`owned.inputs().onboarding().answered()`, `DraftOnboardingService.java`). Completeness items belong to two
  steps (W-122a C3a): the rent room to `PAY_STRUCTURE_AND_RENT`, and the Chapter VIII rooms to
  `INVESTMENTS_AND_INSURANCE`. The other three steps have no measured item.
- **The fills are private.** Each room's filled inputs are built inside `DeductionRoomPricing` (Schedule XV, health for
  self, health for parents, own NPS; `optimise/DeductionRoomPricing.java:86`) and `RentRoomPricing`
  (`optimise/RentRoomPricing.java:79`). Each is run alone and then discarded. Nothing applies them together.
- **The bill.** Both measure the bill at `NodeIds.TAX_NETPAYABLE` (`DeductionRoomPricing.java:234`).
- **N1, measured.** One run each way, 500 iterations after 200 warm-up, on basic ₹12,00,000 with HRA ₹4,00,000
  (M-series laptop, `nice`):

  | Regime | One compute p50 / p99 | The band's extra work (rooms priced + one run) p50 / p99 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Old (`FIRST_SCHEDULE_RATES`) | 0.64 / 1.26 ms | 3.72 / 6.29 ms |
  | New (`NEW_TAX_REGIME`) | 0.56 / 0.92 ms | 3.04 / 4.04 ms |

  A compute with its band is under 8 ms at p99, against N1's 50 ms (`01-PRD.md:282`). The band can be worked out
  inside the compute call.
- **"Only narrows" is not true in general (finding for C4).** W-122 C3a says answering a step removes its items, so
  the band can only narrow or stay. The lower end does only rise. But the upper end rises when income is entered, and a
  room's worth grows with the marginal rate. So the band can widen when step 5's income is entered while step 2 or
  step 4 is still open. On F34's own order the earlier steps are answered first and the claim holds. Out of order it
  does not.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: where the band is worked out.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** in `compute`, on the view: `coarse` (a boolean) and `band` (empty when not coarse), stored
    with the receipt. The band is what was true when it was computed, at the N1 cost measured above.
    - A view stored before W-122b reads with `coarse` and `band` absent: "not stated", never "final". A red-first test
      reads an old stored JSON.
  - C1b: a separate read. That breaks F34's "every step's computation is labelled".
- **C2: the band's figure.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** the bill, `tax.netpayable`, which is what completeness's worths are measured on, so the two
    agree.
  - C2b: the amount finally payable, after credits, interest and fees, rounded. A claim's worth is not measured there.
- **C3: how the fills are applied together.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** the engine keeps the one definition of a fill.
    - Each pricing class gains a method returning its fill as filled `EnteredFacts`; the pricing it does today uses
      the same method, so the behaviour is unchanged.
    - `TaxCalculator.billWithRoomsFilled(inputs, Set<RoomFamily>)` applies the chosen families together and runs once.
      `RoomFamily` is `RENT` or `DEDUCTIONS`, a new `api` type.
    - `taxplanning` maps an open step to its family (step 2 → `RENT`, step 4 → `DEDUCTIONS`), the same mapping as
      `DraftOnboardingService.open`.
  - C3b: rebuild the fills in `taxplanning`. That would be a second definition, which would drift.
- **C4: "only narrows".**
  - **C4a (recommended):**
    - Correct W-122 C3a's claim by erratum (in `20-product-gaps.md` F34 and D-701's row): the band narrows on F34's
      order; entering income with step 2 or 4 still open can widen it.
    - The view says so in `notIncluded`.
    - The test walks the five steps in order on one persona and asserts each band is inside the one before.
  - C4b: hold the band to the previous one. That invents a figure.
- **C5: which outputs carry the label.**
  - **C5a (recommended):** the computation view only (compute and by receipt). W-122c's first figure labels both
    regimes on its own call (D-701). The PDF report and compare-regimes are placed as **W-122b1** (the label on the
    report and on the regime comparison), after W-122c.
  - C5b: all three now.

**Hand figures (the walk; TY 2026-27, old regime, born 1985 so under 60, no family, no HRA; `NodeHarness.plainTaxpayer`):**
- **Step 1 answered.** The only fact is gross pay of ₹12,00,000 as basic.
  - Upper end: 12,00,000 − 50,000 = 11,50,000 taxable. Tax is 1,12,500 + 30% × 1,50,000 = 1,57,500, plus cess
    6,300, giving **₹1,63,800**.
  - Lower end: steps 2 and 4 are open, so every room is filled. The rooms are Schedule XV 1,50,000, health for self
    25,000, own NPS 50,000 and rent paid 60,000. The rent figure is ₹5,000 × 12; the other limbs do not bind at the
    filling rent, and 25% of total income is above it. They total 2,85,000, so taxable is 11,50,000 − 2,85,000 =
    8,65,000. Tax is 12,500 + 20% × 3,65,000 = 85,500, plus cess 3,420, giving **₹88,920**.
- **Step 2 answered, no rent entered.** The rent room leaves the run. Taxable is 11,50,000 − 2,25,000 = 9,25,000.
  Tax is 12,500 + 20% × 4,25,000 = 97,500, plus cess 3,900, giving a lower end of **₹1,01,400**. The upper end is
  unchanged at **₹1,63,800**.
- **Step 3 answered, nothing entered.** No change: **₹1,01,400 to ₹1,63,800**.
- **Step 4 answered, Schedule XV ₹1,50,000 entered.** Taxable is 10,00,000. Tax is 1,12,500, plus cess 4,500,
  giving **₹1,17,000**. No family is open, so both ends are ₹1,17,000.
- **Step 5 answered, nothing entered.** `coarse` is false and there is no band.
- **Each band is inside the one before:** 88,920 ≤ 1,01,400 ≤ 1,17,000 and 1,17,000 ≤ 1,63,800.
- **Edge cases:**
  - A draft with no steps answered is coarse.
  - A new-regime draft whose regime allows no room has a band of zero width, still coarse.
  - An old stored view reads with `coarse` and `band` absent.
- **Red first:** the walk's containment check fails against a probe that skips removing step 2's family. Then the
  probe is reverted.
- The figures above assume `tax.netpayable` is tax plus cess at this income (no rebate, no surcharge). The build's first
  run confirms that before anything is asserted.
