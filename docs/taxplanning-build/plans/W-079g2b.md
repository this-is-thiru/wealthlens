## Plan for W-079g2b — several capped life policies

**What this item is for (one sentence):** decide, in both years, whether a capped life policy's sums are excluded
when the person holds more than one policy of its class:
- unit linked policies issued on or after 1-2-2021;
- other policies issued on or after 1-4-2023.

The rule is that only *"those"* whose premiums together stay within ₹2,50,000 / ₹5,00,000 are excluded, as the
Board's guidelines apply it. W-079g2 refuses these cases today.

**This plan raises choices (C1–C5); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (this session, 2026-10-17)

- **S2 Schedule II Sl. 2 col. C (a)** (Gazette 265620 p.499): *"aggregate of premium for all such policies (in any of
  the tax years during the term of all of such policies) is ≤ ₹ 2,50,000"* (ULIPs from 1-2-2021) / *"≤ ₹ 5,00,000"*
  (others from 1-4-2023). The Sl. 2 Note: the Board *"may issue guidelines …, which shall be binding on the
  income-tax authorities and the assessee"*.
- **1961 s.10(10D)**:
  - fifth proviso (FA 2021 s.6(c), 2021/226208 p.17): *"if the premium is payable, by a person, for more than one
    unit linked insurance policies, issued on or after the 1st day of February, 2021, the provisions of this clause
    shall apply only with respect to those unit linked insurance policies, where the aggregate amount of premium does
    not exceed the amount referred to in fourth proviso in any of the previous year during the term of any of those
    policies"*;
  - seventh proviso: the same for other policies at ₹5,00,000 (FA 2023 s.5(d), 2023/244830 p.16);
  - the Board's guidelines proviso.
- **S2 s.536(2)(j)** (p.492): any *"circular, direction, instruction … issued under any provision of the repealed
  Income-tax Act shall, so far as it is not inconsistent with the corresponding provisions of this Act, be deemed to
  have been … issued under the corresponding provision of this Act and shall continue in force"*. So the 1961
  guidelines govern TY 2026-27 too.
- **The guidelines themselves are not read.** The owner's share (`sources/2026-10-17-life-policies.md` C1) reports
  them, REPORTED only:
  - Circular No. 2/2022 (19-1-2022, ULIPs, eleven examples) and No. 15/2023 (16-8-2023, other policies, thirteen
    examples);
  - the exemption goes to policies the assessee chooses whose premiums together stay within the limit in every year
    their premium terms overlap;
  - a policy whose earlier receipt was claimed exempt stays in the chosen set;
  - premiums in years that don't overlap don't add (No. 15/2023 Ex. 8);
  - GST on the premium is not counted, and a pure term policy is not counted;
  - the examples prefer the more beneficial choice.

  Every incometaxindia.gov.in copy answers 403 here.

### Findings

1. **The law leaves the choice to the assessee, within one test.** The chosen set's aggregate premium must stay
   within the limit in each year any of them is payable. So the engine should check the person's choice against
   that test, not make the choice itself.
2. **The test needs each policy's premium by year,** not just its highest premium. An annual premium and the first
   and last tax years it is payable cover the circulars' examples as reported. A policy whose premium varies by year
   would need a schedule; I'd refuse it rather than approximate.
3. **Earlier claims bind.** A policy of the class whose earlier receipt was claimed exempt counts in the set, whether
   or not it pays out this year.
4. **ULIPs and other policies are separate pools** (separate limits; No. 15/2023 Ex. 13 as reported).

## Revision 1 (2026-10-17, R13) — after reading the circulars

Both circulars are read in full (`sources/2026-10-17-capped-policies.md` A2, A3). They confirm the set test, and that
earlier claims bind, but they differ from the approved C2a and C3a in two places.

**What the circulars say:**
- **The aggregate counts two kinds of policy.** It counts **old** policies, whose earlier consideration was claimed
  exempt, and **this year's receipts**. A policy merely held, with nothing received, is not counted until its year
  comes (2/2022 Ex. 7: X exempt in 2031-32 although A, B, C were held).
- **The test year is a premium year.** "The term" is the years premium is payable, and a surrender ends it (2/2022
  Ex. 10). Years that don't overlap don't add (15/2023 Ex. 8).
- **The exemption goes to a subset.** Where this year's receipts with the old ones exceed the limit, only *"such"*
  policies within it are exempt (para 4.1(iv), 4.2(iv); 4.2–4.3 in 15/2023). The examples take the set *"more
  beneficial to the assessee"*, without saying how benefit is measured. A single receipt that breaches with the old
  ones is simply not exempt (4.2(ii)).
- **GST is out of the premium** (15/2023 para 5).
- **A term policy is outside the caps,** and its premium isn't counted (para 6).

**Revised choices (recommended first):**
- **C2′ — the input:**
  - `LifePolicyReceipt.heldWithOthersOfItsClass` becomes `claimsExemption`, plus `premiumFromYear` /
    `premiumToYear`, the tax years its annual premium (`highestAnnualPremium`) is payable;
  - a new `EnteredFacts.claimedLifePolicies` lists the **old** policies, `ClaimedPolicy(issuedOn, unitLinked,
    annualPremium, premiumFromYear, premiumToYear)`, those whose earlier consideration was claimed exempt;
  - premiums are entered without GST.

  Held policies with nothing received are not entered.
- **C3′ — the check, per class (ULIPs and other policies apart):**
  - the set is the class's old policies plus this year's receipts that claim. A receipt whose own premium already
    exceeds the limit can never be exempt (W-079g2's single-policy test), so it is not excluded and is left out of the
    set (2/2022 Ex. 4: A exempt, B taxable);
  - if, in any premium year, their annual premiums exceed the limit, then:
    - a **single** claiming receipt is **not excluded** (4.2(ii)): charged under r.59, or refused as a ULIP (W-088);
    - with **several** claiming receipts, the choice of subset is the person's, so the engine refuses, naming the
      year and the aggregate and asking for a claimed set within the limit. It doesn't pick, because the circulars
      never define "more beneficial".
  - A receipt that doesn't claim is charged as before.

  **(b)** Pick the subset with the most excluded consideration. That's my metric, not the circulars'.
- **C4, C5 unchanged.** `OS-LIFE-POLICY-PREMIUM`'s notes and provenance cite both circulars and s.536(2)(j); D-207.
