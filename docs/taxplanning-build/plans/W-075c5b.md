### Findings

1. **The net per item is already defined** by the head's own arithmetic: received, through `OS-INTEREST`, less what
   `OS-OTHER-EXPENDITURE` allows. A deposit's banker's commission is nil, so it does not reduce the net.
2. **An item's net can be below nil** (expenditure above the interest). The head then includes that negative amount,
   and gross total income includes the sum. So the figure s.153 works on is the sum of the reached items' nets,
   floored at nil.
