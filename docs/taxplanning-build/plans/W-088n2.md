### Design

1. **The shape:** `rules/cap/PercentOfBaseByPopulation(BaseId base, BaseId population, List<Band> bands, Rate
   otherwise)`, with `Band(int exceeding, Rate percent)`.
   - `apply` reads the population as a count and takes the band with the highest `exceeding` the population is
     strictly greater than ("exceeding"), else `otherwise`.
   - The limit text: `10% of SALARY for a population of 5000000, exceeding 4000000`, or
     `5% of SALARY for a population of 1000000, exceeding no band`.
   - It refuses: no bands; a repeated threshold; a negative threshold; a population below zero.
   - JSON: `{"type": "PercentOfBaseByPopulation", "base": "SALARY", "population": "POPULATION", "bands":
     [{"exceeding": 4000000, "percent": "10"}, {"exceeding": 1500000, "percent": "7.5"}], "otherwise": "5"}`.
2. **The input:** `EmployerAccommodation.cityPopulation` (`Optional<Integer>`) replaces `band`.
   - It is required exactly for owned accommodation, and must be positive.
   - The Javadoc says it is the population as the Table's census counts it (2011 in both years), not today's.
   - `PopulationBand` is deleted.
3. **The node:** `PerquisiteNode` passes the population as the count `POPULATION`; `LOCATION` goes.
4. **The pack:** `PERQ-ACCOM-OWNED`'s two versions take the new cap; their `_note` names the input as the population.
   Relocked once.
