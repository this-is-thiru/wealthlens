**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** after a Form No. 130 is read, check it against itself and
the law: its printed sums, its tables against their totals, Part B against Part C, Part A's period against the year,
and the standard deduction and cess against the pack. Name every contradiction rather than picking a side.

### What the design says (`06-algorithms.md:491` – `:519`)

- **Arithmetic identities** "from DOC-F130-ROWS-001": 1(d), 2(f), 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 17, 21.
- **Across parts:** "Part B total tax deducted ≈ row 21 + row 19 + row 20 … tolerance ₹10" (`:509`), and "Part A
  period is inside the tax year" (`:510`).
- **Against the pack:** row 4(b) ∈ {SAL-STDDED-001, -002}; row 4(b) matches row A's regime; row 16 ≈ 4% × (13 + 15 − 14).
- **"any failure ⇒ lower the confidence of every cell in the failing group, and surface the contradiction to the user —
  never pick a side silently"** (`:517` – `:518`).

### Findings

1. **The arithmetic is already law data.** W-127b put the sums the form prints into `FORM_130.json` (`identities`:
   Annexure-I 2(f), 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 17, 21; Annexure-II 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 15, 17). `Inference` fills a blank cell
   from them, and leaves "a sum whose cells are all read and disagree … for W-128" (`Inference.java:17` – `:18`).
   - G5.2's **1(d)** has no printed formula. The form prints "Total" alone, and W-127b declared only the sums the form
     prints.
2. **G5.2's Part B identity looks wrong, as W-126c noted (D-735).**
   - Row 21 is printed "Net tax payable (17-18-19-20)".
   - Rows 19 and 20 are tax deducted and collected "as per Form No. 122 submitted under provisions of section 392(4)":
     tax others deducted, which the employee reports.
   - So the employer's own deduction in Part B approximates **row 21**, not 21 + 19 + 20, which counts 19 and 20 twice.
   - It holds only for one employer issuing both Part B and Part C. Several employers are W-129 (`DOC-F130-MULTI-001`,
     `-ANNEX-001`).
3. **The tables' totals are unchecked.** W-126c2b reads each Part B table's rows and its Total row; nothing compares
   them.
4. **Part A's period is text.** It prints "(dd/mm/yyyy) to (dd/mm/yyyy)" in the value column (`sources/2026-11-16-form-130.md`),
   read today as words, not dates.
5. **The checks against the pack need the law from `taxengine`.** The standard deduction's two amounts (`01-rulebook.md:540`
   – `:541`: ₹75,000 new regime, ₹50,000 otherwise, each "or the salary, whichever is less") and the cess rate live in
   the pack. `taxplanning` reaches them only through `TaxCalculator`, which has no accessor for them today (to be
   confirmed by grep in that child's plan).
6. **No confidence is set for a contradicted cell.** G5.1's bands are L1 0.95+, L2 0.85 – 0.95, L3 0.70 – 0.85, L4 none
   (`06-algorithms.md:482` – `:485`). G5.2 says only "lower".
7. **Size.** Three kinds of check with different inputs: more than one item (§7).

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — the split:**
  - **(a) W-128a** — the form's own arithmetic. Each printed sum whose cells are all read is checked; a mismatch is a
    contradiction. Each table's Total amount is checked against the sum of its rows.
  - **W-128b** — across parts: Part B against Part C (per C3), and Part A's period read as two dates and checked
    against the tax year.
  - **W-128c** — against the pack: row 4(b) against the year's standard deduction amounts and row A's regime; row 16
    against the cess rate.
  - Each child gets its own plan and probe.
  - (b) one item.
- **C2 — what a contradiction does** (finding 6):
  - **(a)** A note naming the check, the printed figure and the figure the check gives ("Contradiction: …"). The reading
    becomes PARTIAL. Every cell of the failing check drops to **0.50** confidence. That number is Claude-drafted (no
    design source), so it needs your adoption. It sits below L3's 0.70 floor so that a contradicted cell is never trusted
    above an inferred one.
  - (b) A note only, confidence unchanged.
  - (c) Another value you name.
- **C3 — the Part B identity** (finding 2):
  - **(a)** An erratum on `06-algorithms.md:509`: Part B's total tax deducted ≈ **row 21**, for one employer issuing both
    parts. W-129 decides the multi-employer case.
  - (b) Keep G5.2 as written.
- **C4 — the tolerance:** ₹10 for every approximate check (`06-algorithms.md:509`); exact equality for printed sums
  (C2a).
  - **(a)** As above.
  - (b) Exact everywhere.
- **C5 — G5.2's 1(d):**
  - **(a)** Not checked: the form prints no formula there (finding 1), and W-127b's rule is printed sums only.
  - (b) Declare 1(d) = 1(a) + 1(b) + 1(c) as a sum. That would be an inference about law, not printed law.
