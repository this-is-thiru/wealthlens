### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — which Fyers cards carry the line:**
  - **(a) recommended: both windows, futures and options — four cards.** The page states one rate with no
    effective date, and W-178's C4a already named this shape (`plans/W-178.md:86-91`, `:100-101`): the same

    conflict was a **dated** revision; here there is no date to respect, and (b) silently undercharges
    2025-04 → 2026-02-28. Not recommended.
- **C2 — where `CLEARING` stands in `RS-CG-CHARGE`:**
  - **(a) recommended: beside `EXCHANGE_TXN`, in both rules and both years.** A clearing charge is levied on the
    trade to settle it: cost on a purchase (1961 s.48(ii) / 2025 s.72(1)(b)), transfer expenditure on a sale
    (s.48(i) / s.72(1)(a)), exactly as D-375 C2a ruled for `EXCHANGE_TXN` (`STATE.md:495`). It takes the nine
    codes to ten. Without it `ChargeCatalogueCoverageTest` fails on every new code and the engine refuses the
    computation — the item exists partly to avoid that.
  - **(b) `NOT_DEDUCTED` beside `MISC`.** Would satisfy the coverage test and be wrong on the law the rows
    already cite: nothing distinguishes a clearing charge from an exchange charge on the same note.
- **C3 — golden cases:**
  - **(a) recommended: change the two existing Fyers F&O cases and add two.** The four rate/window combinations
    each get a case: futures 2026-04 (existing, changed), options 2025-06 (existing, changed), futures 2025-06
    (new), options 2026-04 (new). Otherwise the 2025-04 futures rate and the 2026-03 options rate are pinned by
    nothing.
  - **(b) the two existing cases only.** Smaller diff, and half the rates unpinned.
- **C4 — a zero `CLEARING` line on Fyers delivery and intraday:** **no rule.** The page prints "0", its GST line
  names clearing but 0 × 18 % is 0, and a zero-rate rule would put a `CLEARING: 0.00` line on every Fyers cash
  trade (changing four golden key sets) to say nothing. Not a choice worth offering; recorded here as a decision.
