## Plan for W-169 — the V2 P&L records profit, brokerage and misc

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a V2 sale writes only its purchase and sale amounts into the
P&L, never its profit, brokerage or misc, so every V2 year shows zero realised profit and "total realised profit"
counts V1 sales only; this item makes a V2 sale record all five figures, taken from the trade-outcome rows that
already compute them (D-366 C4a).

**Medium: the V2 sell flow reordered, a new P&L entry for a sale, about 15 tests re-pointed. It changes figures users
see, for sales made after the deploy.**

### What I read (session 35, 2026-10-25)

- W-169's row; `plans/W-160d.md` (the finding); `ProfitAndLossService` (whole, after W-160d2);
  `PortfolioService:633–674` (the V2 sell); `TradeOutcomeRecorder` (`record`, `allocate`, `buildRow`, `rowFigures`);
  `LotChargeAllocator` (its javadoc: B-8, "a lot that is asked twice is charged twice"); the tests that call the V2
  sell (`ProfitAndLossServiceTest`, 10 `SELL` contexts; `PortfolioServiceTest:449`, `:562`, `:590`;
  `ShadowRecordingIntegrationTest:99`).
- **Today's order in a V2 sale:**
  1. the charge engine prices it (`chargeRecordingGateway.record`);
  2. the P&L is updated from `BuyContext`s (quantity, date, price, asset type): purchase and sell only;
  3. `TradeOutcomeRecorder` writes one row per lot. Per lot it computes the buy charge share
     (`LotChargeAllocator.deductBroker`, which **deducts it from the lot**, so it can be asked once only), the misc,
     the sell charges allocated by code so they sum exactly (B-6), and `netProfit`.
- **The definitions agree.** V1's P&L profit is (sale − purchase) − brokerage − misc. A recorder row's `netProfit` is
  `totalSellValue − totalBuyValue`, where `totalBuyValue` = price × qty + buy charges + buy misc and `totalSellValue` =
  price × qty − sell charges − sell misc. That is the same quantity. So the recorder's rows already hold all five
  figures V1 writes, computed once.
- **Who calls a V2 sale into the P&L:** only `PortfolioService:668`. The two-argument overload is also reached for a
  sale by `ShadowRecordingIntegrationTest:99` and by `ProfitAndLossServiceTest`. In `main` it is reached for buys only
  (`:632`).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — where a V2 sale's figures come from:**
  - **(a) recommended: the recorder's rows.**
    - The recorder runs before the P&L and returns the rows it saved.
    - The P&L folds each row into the split under its asset type and segment: purchase = buy price × qty, sell = sell
      price × qty, brokerage = buy + sell broker charges, misc = buy + sell misc, profit = `netProfit`.
    - One computation feeds both records, so `trade_outcomes` and the P&L cannot disagree.
    - The engine still prices first, as the recorder needs the sell charge.
  - **(b)** the P&L computes them itself, from `BuyContext`s widened with each lot's buy charges and the engine's
    total. That is two computations of figures that must agree, and the lot's buy charge would have to be taken out
    of `LotChargeAllocator` before the recorder asks for it (B-8).
- **C2 — V2 sales made before the deploy:** this item changes only sales made after it. Every V2 sale on the
  databases now was made before it, and its year will still show no profit, brokerage or misc. W-160d3's reshape
  moved those figures as they were.
  - **(a) recommended: place a row, W-172, directly after W-169.** It would backfill those years' three figures from
    `trade_outcomes`, as a one-time R15 operation run at the deploy after §2.6. Its own plan settles how to tell a
    V2-written year from a V1 one.
  - **(b)** do the backfill in this item. It doubles the item.
  - **(c)** no backfill. Earlier V2 years stay without profit.
- **C3 — the sale overload:**
  - **(a) recommended:** `updateProfitAndLoss` handles buys only, and a sale goes through a new
    `recordSale(userMail, profitLossContext, computation, rows)`. A sale sent to `updateProfitAndLoss` is refused
    (`IllegalArgumentException`) rather than recorded without its figures. Nothing in `main` sends one.
  - **(b)** keep a sale accepted without rows, recording purchase and sell only. The defect would stay reachable.
