### The rule: what each state means

| A row's breakdown (buy or sell, separately) | State |
|---|---|
| present, `STT` above nil | `PAID` |
| present (non-empty), no `STT` or `STT` nil | `NOT_PAID` |
| absent or empty | `UNKNOWN` |

- The charge engine writes a line per charge it levied, so a breakdown it wrote without STT is a record of none
  charged.
- No breakdown at all is no record, whatever the row's `broker_charges` say.
- A bucket's key keeps the state, so `PAID`, `NOT_PAID` and `UNKNOWN` rows never merge.

### How `UNKNOWN` flows: refused by the rows, not by Java

The engine passes the state through as the item-fact code. **No row accepts `UNKNOWN` on the sale.** A row ignores
the buy unless it names it. So:

| Term | STT buy | STT sale | Part | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| short | any | `UNKNOWN` | **refused** | s.196(1)(b) / s.111A(1)(b) turn on the sale |
| short | `UNKNOWN` | `PAID` | SHORT_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY | the buy is not a condition of s.196 |
| short | `UNKNOWN` | `NOT_PAID` | SHORT_TERM_SLAB | likewise |
| long | `UNKNOWN` | `NOT_PAID` | LONG_TERM_OTHER | s.197 has no STT condition; s.198 already fails on the sale |
| long | `UNKNOWN` | `PAID` | **refused**, or see C2 | s.198(1)(c)(i) turns on the buy |
| long | any | `UNKNOWN` | **refused** | |

The WI row says *"`CapitalGainsAdapter` refuses"*. Since D-290 the adapter maps no STT and the rows decide, so the
refusal is the engine's: an `IllegalArgumentException` that names the facts. That follows D-290's design. I record it
as the row's wording superseded, not a change of intent.

    PAID)`, in both versions, with a relock. The waiver's own facts then settle it whatever the buy's evidence:
    - Clause (I): an acquisition before 1-10-2004 is notified, and no STT existed then.
    - Clause (II): a bonus or demerger allotment is not chargeable to STT.

      answers it.

  - **(b)** no entry. Rejected: refusing every disposal without a breakdown is a decided standing state, which is

  - **(a) recommended: a new row, W-088j2:** a way to establish STT for a disposal with no breakdown, such as the

  - **(b)** no row: `UNKNOWN` stays refused until trades are recorded again.

### Risks or ambiguities

1. **The effect may be most of today's data.** With the charge flags `false`, few rows have breakdowns, so most
   equity disposals may become refusals where they were silent slab or "other" charges. That is the row's intent
   ("nothing silent"), but it is a user-visible change once the adapter is wired. §3.3 sizes it, and C4 places the
   remedy.
2. **The WI row's "`CapitalGainsAdapter` refuses"** is met by the engine's refusal after D-290 (see above). If you
   want the adapter to refuse first as well, that would duplicate a rule in Java, which I recommend against.
3. **The rule in "The rule" table** (a breakdown present without STT = `NOT_PAID`) trusts the charge engine's
   breakdown as complete. A breakdown written by a user's manual misc charge carries no code (D-238), so it does not
   enter a breakdown map and cannot fake one.
