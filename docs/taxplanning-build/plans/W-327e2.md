## Plan for W-327e2 — a plain "why" for tax by basis, rebate and surcharge

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** each tax-by-basis, rebate and surcharge line publishes its
"why" as a reason with facts, and the relief shapes give their reasons the way the cap shapes do (W-327a1).

**Probe (the W-327e probe log, plus a read of each site and shape; 2026-11-14):**
- **Every site read:** `TaxByBasisNode:105`, `:149`, `:205`; `RebateNode:79`; `SurchargeNode:147`, `:151`, `:157`.
- **The relief shapes write the rebate and marginal-relief text.** `RebateNode:79` and `SurchargeNode:157` write
  `ReliefResult.reason()`, a string, from `ThresholdLadderCeiling`, `FlatWithCeiling`, `ExcessOverThreshold` and
  `NoRelief` (`rules/relief/`). `RebateNode.grant` adds two of its own: "not met: " + a condition's `describe()`
  (`:94`), and "limited to the tax at slab rates, …".
- **Correction to W-327e (D-689 erratum):** `TaxByBasisNode:149` writes the special-rate row's `cap.limitedBy()`. For the
  `SlabLadder` rows (`RATE-CG-SHORT-LISTED-EQUITY`, `RATE-CG-LONG-LISTED-EQUITY`) that is the ladder's name, "slab".
  That is the place D-679 C3a meant. No other `SlabLadder` row's text reaches `limitedBy`: the slab and surcharge
  ladders are read for their value or rate only (`TaxByBasisNode:96`, `SurchargeNode.bandRate`).
- **No test moves** except `ReliefShapeTest`: `limitedBy` stays as it is. `ReliefShapeTest:130` reads
  `result.reason()` and `:136` constructs a `ReliefResult`, so they follow the rename below.

**The shape change:** `ReliefResult(Money amount, String reason)` becomes
`ReliefResult(Money amount, String limitedBy, Reason reason)`, the same words as `CapResult`. One word per concept:
the audit text is `limitedBy` and the facts are `reason`. Every shape has a reason, so it is not optional.

That is **15 new codes** (164 in all).

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: the reason on a special-rate line (`:149`), where the row is a `SlabLadder`.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** the line's reason names the part (`CG_TAXED_AT_SPECIAL_RATE {part}`), and `SlabLadder`
    gets no reason of its own. `:149` is the only place its text shows, and this closes D-679 C3a.
  - C1b: `SlabLadder`'s `CapResult` gains a reason ("taxed at the rates of the year's bands"), and `:149` takes the
    cap's reason where there is one. That covers the ladder in general, but loses the part from the line, and the
    `LeastOf` row (`RATE-CG-LONG-OTHER`) still needs the part reason.
- **C2: the "not met" rebate.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** a new `RELIEF_CONDITION_NOT_MET`: "Nothing is given: a condition of this relief is not
    met." The existing `CONDITION_NOT_MET` says "Nothing is deducted …", which is wrong for a rebate.
  - C2b: reword `CONDITION_NOT_MET` so that it fits both deductions and reliefs.

**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption; no digits):**
- `RELIEF_ABOVE_NO_THRESHOLD`: "Your total income is above none of the thresholds this relief starts at, so it gives nothing."
- `RELIEF_MARGINAL_AT_THRESHOLD`: "Marginal relief: the tax, with surcharge, may exceed the tax at {threshold} by no more than your income exceeds {threshold}."
- `RELIEF_INCOME_ABOVE_CEILING`: "Your total income is above {ceiling}, so this rebate gives nothing."
- `RELIEF_LESSER_OF_TAX_AND_MAXIMUM`: "It is the tax itself, up to {maximum}."
- `RELIEF_INCOME_NOT_ABOVE_THRESHOLD`: "Your total income is not above {threshold}, so this relief gives nothing."
- `RELIEF_TAX_ABOVE_EXCESS`: "It is the tax above the {excess} by which your income exceeds {threshold}, so the tax left is no more than that excess."
- `RELIEF_NONE`: "No relief applies."
- `RELIEF_CONDITION_NOT_MET`: "Nothing is given: a condition of this relief is not met."
- `REBATE_LIMITED_TO_SLAB_TAX`: "It is held to the tax at slab rates, {tax}."
- `SURCHARGE_ON_TOTAL_INCOME`: "Surcharge is at {rate}, the rate on your total income, as it is greater than the {other} on your income without dividends and special-rate gains."
- `SURCHARGE_ON_INCOME_EXCLUDING`: "Surcharge is at {rate}, the rate on your income without dividends and special-rate gains, as it is greater than the {other} on your total income."
- `SURCHARGE_LIMITED_PART`: "On the tax for dividends ({dividends}) and for special-rate gains ({special}), surcharge is held to the limit the law sets."
- `TAX_AT_SLAB_RATES_ON_REST`: "Slab rates apply to your total income of {income}, less the {special} of capital gains taxed at their own rates."
- `CG_TAXED_AT_SPECIAL_RATE`: "Your {part} is taxed at its own rate, not at your slab rates."
- `CG_SHORTFALL_TAKEN_FROM_PART`: "Your other income leaves {shortfall} of the basic exemption unused, so {taken} of your {part} is not taxed."
