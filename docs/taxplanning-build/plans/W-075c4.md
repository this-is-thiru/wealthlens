## Plan for W-075c4 — rent paid without HRA (s.134, 80GG), TY 2026-27

**What this item is for (one sentence):** deduct, in the old regime, the rent an individual pays for their own home
when no house rent allowance is received, worked on total income before this deduction, last among the Chapter VIII
deductions built so far.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read (this session, 2026-10-07)

- **S2 s.134** (Gazette CG-DL-E-22082025-265620, p.170), heading *"Deductions in respect of rents paid"*:
  - (1) *"there shall be deducted any expenditure incurred by him towards payment of rent (by whatever name called)
    in respect of any furnished or unfurnished accommodation occupied by him for the purposes of his own residence"*;
  - (2) *"allowable on payment of such rent exceeding 10% of his total income, subject to a maximum of ₹ 5000 per
    month, or 25% of total income for tax year, whichever is less"*;
  - (3) *"such other conditions or limitations having regard to the area or place in which such accommodation is
    situated and other relevant consideration, as may be prescribed"*;
  - (4) none where *"(a) any residential accommodation is— (i) owned by the assessee or by his spouse or minor child
    or, where such assessee is a member of a Hindu undivided family, by such family at the place where he ordinarily
    resides or performs duties of his office or employment or carries on his business or profession; or (ii) owned by
    the assessee at any other place, being accommodation in the occupation of the assessee, the value of which is to
    be determined under section 21(6) or (7)(a); or (b) the assessee has any income falling in Schedule III (Table:
    Sl. No. 11)"*;
  - (5) *"10% of his total income"* and *"25% of his total income"* are of *"the total income of the assessee before
    allowing deduction for any expenditure under this section"*.

### Findings

1. **The item's ⚠ is cleared.** The only condition is r.65's Form 31 declaration, and no figure comes from the Rules.
2. **"Total income before this deduction" means GTI less the other Chapter VIII deductions.** So s.134 has to run
   after them, inside `ChapterEightNode`. Its base is GTI less those deductions, the deductions first held within GTI
   (s.122(2)), and it is not rounded. Once s.134 is added the total stays within GTI, because limb (c) is 25% of a
   base that is never negative.
3. **"₹5000 per month" — months of what?** The section does not say. Form 31 asks for the *"Period of Stay (in
   Months)"*, so the limit grows with the months of stay. Two things are still open: how a part month counts, and
   whether that figure is entered or derived from the tenancies' dates. `PerUnitPerPeriod`'s Javadoc says a count
   *"arrives here already resolved"* upstream, as the car's and the children's-allowance months are entered today.
4. **The bars need facts the engine does not have yet.** A spouse's, a minor child's or the HUF's house at the place,
   and a self-occupied house elsewhere, are not entered anywhere. House property is `W-078`. `Tenancy.ownedByAssessee`
   covers only the home being occupied.
5. **The HRA bar (s.134(4)(b))** is *"any income falling in"* Sl. 11, i.e. receiving the allowance at all, even where
   its exemption works out to nil. That is what the words say, and the rulebook reads it the same way.
6. **This is a problem for W-075c6, not this item.** s.133's qualifying limit is on *"adjusted gross total income"*,
   which is GTI less every other Chapter VIII deduction, s.134 included. s.134's base is total income before s.134,
   which includes s.133. Each base waits on the other. W-075c6's plan has to settle the order.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the input:** **(a) recommended:** `EnteredFacts.rentDeclaration` —
  `Optional<RentDeclaration(int monthsOfStay, boolean ownsHomeWhereResidesOrWorks, boolean ownsOccupiedHomeElsewhere)>`,
  which is Form 31. It is also the claim: with no declaration there is no s.134 line. The rent is the sum of the
  tenancies' `rentPaid` where `ownedByAssessee` is false. The first boolean covers the assessee, spouse, minor child
  and HUF (s.134(4)(a)(i)); the second covers s.134(4)(a)(ii). When W-078 lands, house property can supply the second.
  **(b)** Read everything from the tenancies, with no declaration. The bars in finding 4 would then have no input.
- **C2 — the months (finding 3):** **(a) recommended:** `monthsOfStay` is entered, as Form 31 item 4 asks. It must
  be 1–12 and no more than the calendar months the tenancies' occupied days touch in the tax year; otherwise it is
  refused. **(b)** Derive it as the calendar months touched. That quietly decides the part-month question, which the
  shape's Javadoc says belongs upstream. Either way, raise **OQ-22** to confirm what a part month counts as.
- **C3 — the row:** `DED-RENT-PAID` (the section heading's words; segments ≤ 12 characters), TY 2026-27 from
  2026-04-01, `ExceptIn` NEW_TAX_REGIME, `SOURCE_CHECKED`. The cap is `LeastOf`:
  - "rent less 10% of total income": `AmountInExcess` RENT_PAID over `PercentOfBase` 10 TOTAL_INCOME;
  - "₹5000 per month": `PerUnitPerPeriod.perPeriod` 5000 per MONTH;
  - "25% of total income": `PercentOfBase` 25 TOTAL_INCOME.

  The provenance is s.134 p.170, r.65 p.1581 and s.202(2)(a) p.227. The 1961 counterpart, s.80GG, is not
  verified (W-075d).
- **C4 — the bars:** checked in the node, in s.134(4)'s order. Where the claim is barred, or the regime is new, the
  node writes a nil `DEDUCTION` line whose `limitedBy` names the first bar that applies. That is the same pattern as
  W-075c2's failed conditions. The HRA bar applies when any `HRA` pay line is greater than nil (finding 5).
- **C5 — where it is built:** a `RentPaidDeduction` class (like `AgniveerDeduction`). `ChapterEightNode` calls it
  last, giving it the base from finding 2. A rent declaration entered by a HUF is refused, because s.134 speaks of the
  assessee's *"own residence"* and the HUF appears only in the ownership bar. A rent declaration entered for TY
  2025-26 is refused as now (W-075d).
- **C6 — records:** update the rulebook `DED-80GG-001` source column: r.65 and Form 31 read, with no prescribed area
  limit (an erratum-style addition, as W-075c3 did). Clear the ⚠ in WI W-075c4 with an erratum. Update the TS
  `chapterviii` row and STATE (D-141, OQ-22).
