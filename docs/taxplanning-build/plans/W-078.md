**What this item is for (one sentence):** compute the head "Income from house property" per property — annual value,
the 30% allowance, interest on borrowed capital, arrears — for both years, and carry it into gross total income.

**This item is mis-sized (§7) — C1 proposes a split for you to place. C2–C7 are the decisions for the first part.

### Findings

1. **About 20 rules, two years each, a new input family, a new `NodeValue`, and an interaction with set-off (W-081)
   and the house election (W-083)** — well over §7's one-session, ~300-line item.
2. **The four occupancy-driven rules split cleanly** into the annual-value side (nil for an own residence, the two-house
   limit) and the interest side (the cap, the new regime's disallowance, pre-construction inside the cap).
3. **A loss arises only on the interest side** (annual value less 30% is never below nil unless local taxes exceed the
   rent). The first part can therefore stand without set-off.
4. **The house election (W-083) is an optimiser**, choosing which two houses; s.21(7)(a) says the assessee specifies
   them. Until W-083, the assessee's specification is entered.
5. **Unrealised rent (s.21(4)) needs a Rule not read** (r.4 / its 2026 successor) — sourcing.

### How W-078a will be verified

By hand, both years, from `DR 01` R16's worked example: let out at ₹45,000 × 12 = ₹5,40,000, local taxes ₹18,000 →
annual value ₹5,22,000, 30% ₹1,56,600, **income ₹3,65,400** (before interest, W-078b); self-occupied → **₹0**; a
`NOT_LET` house with expected value ₹3,00,000 → ₹2,10,000; vacancy with rent below the expected value → the rent;
a 50% share → half; arrears ₹1,00,000 → ₹70,000; a third own-residence house → refused. Gross total income rises by
the head. Bite probes: the higher-of taken as the rent alone; local taxes not deducted; the share ignored.
Unit tier: `./mvnw clean test -pl backend -Punit`.
