- **Found while reading, and not placed anywhere:** the **consent notice**. Act s.5(1) (248045 p.4): *"Every request
  made to a Data Principal under section 6 for consent shall be accompanied or preceded by a notice"*. The notice must
  give the personal data and purpose, the manner of exercising rights under s.6(4) and s.13, and the manner of
  complaining to the Board. s.5(2) asks for the same notice to people whose consent was given before commencement,
  *"as soon as it is reasonably practicable"*. Rules r.3 (267650 p.24) adds three requirements:
  - (a) it must be understandable on its own;
  - (b) an *"itemised description of such personal data"* and the specified purpose;
  - (c) *"the particular communication link for accessing the website or app"* and the means to withdraw consent, to
    exercise rights, and to complain to the Board.

  `POST /auth/register` gives no notice and records no consent. A grep of `03-WORK-ITEMS.md`, `sources/2026-11-07-
  data-protection.md` and `plans/W-237*.md` finds no s.5 and no r.3 (the sources table's row 5 covers s.6(4) – (6) only).
