## Plan for W-048 — the `ReliefShape` sealed family

**What this item is for (one sentence):** the Acts' reliefs (the two rebates, rebate marginal relief,
and surcharge marginal relief) become a closed family of shapes, each applied to a context and
explaining itself, so a new relief formula is a new class and never an edit to a pipeline step
(`DR 10` E5, D-ONEIMPL).

### What I read

- **The `W-048` row:** Produces `ReliefShape`, `FlatWithCeiling`, `ExcessOverThreshold`,
  `ThresholdLadderCeiling`, `NoRelief`, `ReliefResult`, `ReliefContext`. Its Appendix D note says
  `ReliefResult` and `ReliefContext` are named only in E5's signature, and `W-049` owns the
  `REBATE-NEW-001` row.
- **`DR 10` E5:** the sealed interface, `ReliefResult apply(ReliefContext ctx)` *"value + the reason,
  for the trace"*, and the four statements mapped to the shapes.
- **`DR 06` G3:** G3.1 rebate marginal relief pseudo-code; G3.2 surcharge marginal relief, *"W = U +
  V … U computed by re-entering the tax computation at exactly the threshold with relief disabled"*.
- **Code:** `CapShape` (the model for a closed shape family), `CapResult`, `ShapeJson`,
  `RuleVersion` (holds one `CapShape cap`), and `NEW_TAX_REGIME.json`'s `_pendingRules` note.
- **S2 s.156** (read in `W-046`'s session): (1) *"100% of income-tax payable or ₹ 12500, whichever is
  less … if such total income does not exceed ₹ 500000"*; (2)(a) *"does not exceed twelve lakh rupees,
  100% of the income-tax payable or ₹ 60000, whichever is less"*; (2)(b) *"an amount equal to the
  amount by which the income-tax payable on such total income is in excess of the amount by which
  the total income exceeds twelve lakh rupees"*.

### Findings

1. **The row has no Verify.** I propose `./mvnw test -pl backend -Dtest=ReliefShapeTest`.
2. **No item wires a relief into a rule row.** `RuleVersion` holds a `CapShape`, and `ShapeJson` reads
   only caps. `W-049` authors `REBATE-NEW-001` and needs both, but its Produces lists neither.
3. **The row says `taxrules/relief/`.** The package is `taxengine/rules/relief/`, beside
   `rules/cap/`, as with `coverage/` and `catalogue/`.

### A. Choices (recommended first)

- **A1 — one result for every relief.** `ReliefResult(Money amount, String reason)`: the amount **to
  take off** the tax (a rebate, or the surcharge given back), never negative, with the reason for the
  trace. `NoRelief` gives zero with its reason. The capping that composes reliefs (`REBATE-NEW-MR-002`,
  `REBATE-CAP-001`) is the node's job in `W-049`, not a shape's.
- **A2 — what `ReliefContext` carries.**
  - `(Money totalIncome, Money taxBeforeRelief, Optional<UnrelievedTax> unrelievedAt)`, where
    `UnrelievedTax` is a one-method function: the tax and surcharge on a given income with relief
    disabled.
  - It exists only because `ThresholdLadderCeiling` needs `U` at the threshold. G3.2 says that means
    re-entering the computation, which only the caller can do.
  - It's `Optional`. The rebate shapes don't use it, and `ThresholdLadderCeiling` given none is
    **refused**, not zeroed.
- **A3 — each shape's parameters are data:**
  - **`FlatWithCeiling(Money maximum, Money incomeCeiling)`:** if total income ≤ ceiling,
    min(tax, maximum); otherwise zero. Covers s.156(1) and s.156(2)(a).
  - **`ExcessOverThreshold(Money threshold)`:** if total income > threshold, max(0, tax − (income −
    threshold)); otherwise zero. Covers s.156(2)(b).
  - **`ThresholdLadderCeiling(List<Money> thresholds)`:** take the highest threshold the income
    exceeds. `U` = `unrelievedAt(threshold)`, `V` = income − threshold. The relief is max(0, tax
    before relief − (U + V)). Below the lowest threshold it's zero. Four ladders make four
    parameterisations (G3.2); the ladders themselves are `W-054`'s.
- **A4 — finding 2.** Write an erratum on `W-049`'s row adding to its Produces: *"`RuleVersion` carries
  a relief; `ShapeJson` reads it"*, so its author decides the wiring with its first relief row.
  Building the wiring here would be speculative, since no relief row exists yet.
