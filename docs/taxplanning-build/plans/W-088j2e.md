## Plan for W-088j2e — Sl. 1 applies only to a buy by purchase

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** stop `STT-PURCHASE-DELIVERY` (Finance (No. 2) Act, 2004
s.98 Table Sl. 1, *"**Purchase** of an equity share in a company, where (a) … entered into in a recognised stock
exchange; and (b) … settled by the actual delivery"*) from deriving `CHARGEABLE` for a lot that was not bought. Today a
bonus or demerger lot is derived `CHARGEABLE`, and its trace cites D-294 where S.O. 5054(E) is the law that settles it.

**This plan raises C1 and C2.**

### What I read (session 27, 2026-10-21)

- **The row** (`03-WORK-ITEMS.md`, W-088j2e), D-299 C3, and `plans/W-088j2d.md` C3.
- **`STT-PURCHASE-DELIVERY.json`** (2 versions). Its conditions are `stt.side` `ACQUISITION`, `capitalgains.asset`
  `EQUITY`, `trade.exchange` `NSE` or `BSE`, and `trade.segment` `DELIVERY`. Nothing tests how the lot was acquired.
- **`node/CapitalGainsStt.codeOf`.** The facts it gives the set are side, asset, exchange and segment only.
  `CapitalGainsNode.factsOf` (`:154–164`) gives the part rows `capitalgains.acquisition` as well, from
  `BucketFacts.acquisitionMode()`. So the fact id exists, and `CG-PART-LONG-NOTIFIED` already tests it.
- **How a lot gets a mode.**
  - `Acquisition.of` (`portfolio/dto/Acquisition.java:33–37`) gives `PURCHASE` when the lot has no corporate-action
    type, and `CORPORATE_ACTION` otherwise.
  - Only two places set a lot's type: bonus (`CorporateActionService:359`) and demerger (`:581`).
  - So a purchase that is later split keeps `PURCHASE`, and so does the demerging company's own retained lot.
  - `UNRECORDED` (`AcquisitionMode`) comes from two places:
    - rows written before W-088f;
    - the rebuild merging buys whose acquisitions differ (`Acquisition.common`, `TradeMatchingService:87`).

    The rebuild writes the mode (`:280`, `:325`). A row that has a venue (after W-088j2b) therefore also has a mode,
    unless its merged buys differed.
- **`CapitalGainsNodeTest`.**
  - The chargeable-venue cases are `:334–358`. Case 9 there is `UNRECORDED`, acquired 2003-06-01, both sides
    `UNKNOWN@NSE/DELIVERY`, giving `LONG_TERM_LISTED_EQUITY`.
  - The trace tests are `:376–405`.
  - The part rows are not traced (`CapitalGainsNode` traces `ROW` lines only), so a bonus lot's change shows only as
    the absence of the `STT-PURCHASE-DELIVERY` line.
- **`sources/2026-10-20-securities-transaction-tax.md`.** It has no commencement date for Chapter VII: no
  "commence", and no "1st day of October, 2004" for Chapter VII itself. The only 1-10-2004 in the rules is
  S.O. 5054(E)'s clause (I), in `CG-PART-LONG-NOTIFIED`.

### Choices (recommended first)

  - **(a) recommended:** add the limb `capitalgains.acquisition` = `PURCHASE`.
    - `CapitalGainsStt` adds that fact for the acquisition side.
    - An `UNRECORDED` mode is not known to be a purchase, so that side stays `UNKNOWN`.
  - **(b)** `Not(capitalgains.acquisition = CORPORATE_ACTION)`. This also lets `UNRECORDED` through, which presumes a
    fact the record lacks.
- **C2 — a purchase before Chapter VII commenced** (the second half of the row):
  - **(a) recommended: not in this item.** Place **W-088j2f**, *"source Chapter VII's commencement and add the date
    limb to `STT-PURCHASE-DELIVERY`"*, next, before W-088k.
    - The date is not in `sources/`, and I will not put a date in rule data from memory (R1).
    - Meanwhile a pre-2004 purchase on `NSE` still gets the right part: `-LONG-LISTED` and `-LONG-NOTIFIED` give
      the same one. Only the trace's law is wrong.
  - **(b)** source it in this item first (a ChatGPT prompt or an egazette scan for the commencement notification),
    then build both limbs. This holds this item on a source.
  - **(c)** drop it, and record in the row's `_note` that a pre-2004 purchase is traced under Sl. 1. No figure
    changes.
