### Findings

1. **T5-1 states the premium, not the policy.** The share rule needs the sum assured and issue date; `DR 08` gives
   neither. Entered so the share does not bind, the expected ₹2,00,000 stands (as W-074a entered T4's fund history).
2. **T5-2 is about a wrong implementation.** Its "Expected" is the deduction being the *same* (₹1,50,000) while the
   aggregate differs; there is no headroom report (A3) to assert. What the engine shows is the trace: the s.123 line's
   aggregate is **₹3,10,000** (PF counted), not ₹1,90,000.
3. **T5-4's dates cannot be used as written for TY 2026-27.** "One journey already claimed in calendar 2027" cannot be a
   prior year's claim, and a journey in calendar 2026 would meet the carry-over rule. Read as: one journey already
   claimed in the 2026–2029 block (the ledger), this one on 10-2-2027. TY 2025-26 needs its own dates (block 2022–2025:
   one claimed, this one on 10-10-2025). The air fare is "NEEDS VERIFICATION" in `DR 08`; the engine takes the fare as
   the taxpayer states it, so a fare above ₹68,000 is entered and the amount incurred binds.
4. **Two T5-4 variants meet unbuilt or silent behaviour:**
   - **New regime** — `DR 08` wants "exemption ₹0 with a stated reason"; LTA says nothing (finding above). W-070
     proposed this exact change for LTA ("T5-4 (nil) will ask it of LTA").
   - **No expenditure evidence** — the engine has no evidence input; Form 12BB (`TDS-SAL-12BB-002`) is TDS work, not
     built.

  expectations gain optional `chapterEightDeduction` and `scheduleXvAggregate` (the s.123 line's `in`). A case expecting
  a deduction runs the regime's pipeline **to `chapterviii`**; the others still stop at `salary.income`.
- **C2 — T5-1:** both years, old regime: basic ₹10,00,000; PF ₹1,20,000 and NPS ₹1,50,000 as pay lines; tuition
  ₹60,000; LIC premium ₹30,000 on a policy entered as sum assured ₹10,00,000 issued 1-6-2020 (share 10% = ₹1,00,000,
  not binding). **Expected ₹2,00,000** (1,50,000 + 50,000).
- **C3 — T5-2:** the same facts; **expected aggregate ₹3,10,000 on the s.123 line, deduction ₹1,50,000 on it**, ₹2,00,000
  in all — the trace assertion `DR 08` asks for.
- **C4 — T5-3:** both years, old regime; basic ₹4,00,000, NPS ₹1,50,000. **Expected ₹90,000.**
- **C5 — T5-4:** LTA ₹1,20,000 received; a flight, ₹68,000 incurred, fare entered ₹80,000. **Expected exemption
