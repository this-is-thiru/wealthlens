- **No law is involved.**

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — how it is measured.** (a) A JUnit class tagged `benchmark`, excluded from the default, `unit` and `integration`
runs and run by a new `-Pbenchmark` profile: 500 warm-up runs, then 2,000 timed, p50 / p99 / max printed, and the test
fails over budget. Machine-dependent, so never in the default build. (b) JMH: a new third-party dependency for three
numbers. (c) In the default build: a slow CI runner would fail builds that changed nothing.

**C2 — N3.** (a) Record N3 as **unmeasurable**: place a row for the G6 optimiser (with N3 as its acceptance), and
measure `resolveTogether` here as the largest multi-compute operation that exists, labelled as such, not as N3. W-143a
is then decided on N1, N2 and the joint pricing. (b) Hold W-143 until the optimiser exists.

**C3 — W-143a.** (a) If N1, N2 and the joint pricing are inside budget, close W-143a with a D-row giving the numbers
(its own condition). If one misses, stop and report. (b) Leave W-143a open whatever the numbers.
