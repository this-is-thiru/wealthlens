## Plan for W-075b — Chapter VIII rows: 80D (health insurance and medical expenditure), TY 2026-27

**What this item is for (one sentence):** put s.126 — health insurance, preventive check-ups and medical expenditure
for the family and for parents, with their four interacting limits — into the pack and `ChapterEightNode` for TY
2026-27.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C7); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read

- **S2 s.126** (Gazette 265620, pp.161–162), this session:
  - (1) an individual or HUF, *"payment of which is made by any mode as specified in sub-section (9), out of his
    income chargeable to tax"*;
  - (2)(a) health insurance, CGHS or notified-scheme contributions, or preventive check-up, of *"the assessee or his
    family … up to ₹25000 in aggregate"*; (b) the same for *"the parent or parents … up to ₹25000 in aggregate"*;
    (c) medical expenditure on *"the assessee or any member of his family, up to ₹50000"*; (d) on *"any parent … up to
    ₹50000"*;
  - (3) preventive check-up within (2)(a) or (b) *"up to ₹ 5000 in aggregate"*;
  - (4) not over ₹50000 for (a)+(c), nor for (b)+(d);
  - (5)–(6) a HUF's own limits; (7) medical expenditure on a senior citizen only *"if no amount has been paid to effect
    or to keep in force the health insurance of such person"*; (8)(a) a senior citizen's ₹25000 becomes ₹50000;
    (8)(b) a lump sum for more than a year spread by the *"appropriate fraction"*; (9) cash only for a preventive
    check-up; (10)(a)–(c) 1 / the number of relevant tax years; *"family" means the spouse and dependant children*;
    (11) an approved insurer's scheme.
- **S2 s.2(100)** (p.20): *"senior citizen" means an individual resident in India who is of the age of sixty years or
  more at any time during the relevant tax year*.
- **`DR 01` R15** `DED-80D-001`…`-011`; **`DR 06` G2.3** — two `AggregateWithCeiling`s, the ₹5,000 cross-cutting
  sub-limit *"applied BEFORE either aggregate … then allocated back to the two buckets in the ratio claimed"*, the
  senior substitution, the exclusivity, the cash rule, the lump-sum fraction.
- **Code:** `FamilySnapshot.Member` (relationship, date of birth, dependant, disability …) — built for `DED-80D-007/8`
  (`15-review-findings.md` F2), but with **no residence**; `Individual` (the assessee) has one. `ChapterEightNode` and
  `DED-SCHEDULE-XV`'s pattern (W-075a, D-132).

### Findings

1. **These are per-person rules.** (7) and (8)(a) turn on *who* a payment covers; one entered total cannot carry them.
2. **"Senior citizen" needs residence**, and a family member carries none — a parent's status cannot be derived.
3. **A family floater** covering several people (one a senior) — whether (8)(a)'s *"such person is a senior citizen"*
   lifts the whole premium's limit is not settled by the words.
4. **G2.3's "allocated back … in the ratio claimed"** for the ₹5,000 check-up limit is the design's inference; s.126(3)
   says only *"up to ₹ 5000 in aggregate"*.
5. **The HUF limits (5)–(6)** are another assessee's; the engine computes for an individual.
6. **Size:** inputs, one model field, 5 rows, 4 components and the node's 80D part — at §7's limit.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — input:** `EnteredFacts.healthPayments` — `HealthPayment(HealthPaymentKind kind, List<PersonRef> covers,
  Money amount, boolean paidInCash, int relevantTaxYears)`; kinds `INSURANCE_PREMIUM`, `GOVERNMENT_HEALTH_SCHEME`,
  `PREVENTIVE_CHECKUP`, `MEDICAL_EXPENDITURE`; `PersonRef` = the assessee or a `FamilySnapshot` member by position.
  Spouse and *dependant* children are "family", parents are parents; anyone else, or one payment spanning both
  buckets, is refused.
- **C2 — senior status:** **(a) recommended:** `FamilySnapshot.Member` gains `boolean residentInIndia`; senior =
  resident and sixty at any time in the year (s.2(100)), computed from the date of birth as `Individual` does.
  **(b)** Each payment carries an entered `coversSeniorCitizen` flag.
- **C3 — a floater with one senior:** **(a) recommended:** refused, raising **OQ-18**. **(b)** The ₹50,000 limit if
  any person covered is a senior.
- **C4 — the ₹5,000 check-up limit:** **(a) recommended:** as G2.3 — applied first, then allocated to the two buckets
  in the ratio claimed (marked `INFERENCE` in the row's note). **(b)** Allocated to the self/family bucket first.
- **C5 — rows** (TY 2026-27, `ExceptIn` NEW_TAX_REGIME, `SOURCE_CHECKED`): `DED-HEALTH-FAMILY` and
  `DED-HEALTH-PARENTS` — `AggregateWithCeiling` (insurance + scheme + check-up sub-capped 25,000; medical expenditure
  sub-capped 50,000; ceiling 50,000); `-FAMILY-SENIOR` / `-PARENTS-SENIOR` the same with the 50,000 sub-cap
  (s.126(8)(a)); `DED-HEALTH-CHECKUP` — `Fixed` 5,000 (s.126(3)). Components `HEALTH_INSURANCE_FAMILY`,
  `MEDICAL_EXPENDITURE_FAMILY`, `HEALTH_INSURANCE_PARENTS`, `MEDICAL_EXPENDITURE_PARENTS` (`QUALIFYING_PAYMENT`,
  entered only through `HealthPayment`). The node picks the senior row where a covered person is a senior.
- **C6 — the conditions:** cash other than for a check-up refused (s.126(9)); a lump sum takes `amount ÷
  relevantTaxYears` (s.126(8)(b), (10)); medical expenditure on a senior with any premium paid for that person
  refused (s.126(7)); a HUF assessee refused (s.126(5)–(6) not modelled). (11)'s approved insurer is an entry
  condition, stated in the component's note.
- **C7 — split (§7):** **(a) recommended:** keep one item; if you prefer, **C2's model field** goes first as its own
  small item.
