### Findings (what the words show)

1. **For OQ-17, the Schedule answers more than D-134 thought:**
   - **(a) alone says "subject to paragraph 2"**; (b)–(z) do not. Para 2 speaks of "any premium or other payment made
     on an insurance policy", which is (a)'s subject.
   - **(b) excludes "the annuity plan referred to in clause (l)" from "a deferred annuity contract"** — the Schedule
     itself treats (l) as a kind of deferred annuity contract, which para 2 excludes by name.
2. **For OQ-16, the same Schedule shows how it says "the narrow salary" when it means it:** (y) spells out "salary,
   including dearness allowance, if the terms … but excluding all other allowances and perquisites". (c) says only
   "salary", and qualifies it as the salary *"payable by or on behalf of the Government"* from which the sum is deducted.
3. **(c) is a deduction from pay** (like employee PF), so it is a pay line of a Government employment; (j), (k), (l) are
   payments, like tuition.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — OQ-16, which "salary" bounds (c):**
  - **(a) recommended:** the salary that Government employment pays in money under "Salaries" — its pay lines under
    the head (basic, DA, allowances), not perquisites — because (c) measures against the "salary payable" the sum is
    deducted from, and the Schedule uses the narrow form only where it spells it out (finding 2). Per employment.
  - **(b)** The narrow base — basic and DA where the terms provide (`SAL-SALARY-FOR-EMPLOYEE-PENSION`'s words), as (y).
  - **(c)** Gross salary under s.16, perquisites included.
- **C2 — OQ-17, para 2's reach:**
  - **(a) recommended:** para 2 reaches **(a) only** — the one clause "subject to paragraph 2" — so (j), (k) and (l)
    count in full; (l) is in any case "a contract for a deferred annuity" by (b)'s own words (finding 1).
  - **(b)** Para 2 reaches (j) and (k) too, with each plan's sum assured entered; (l) outside.
- **C3 — components:** `GOVERNMENT_DEFERRED_ANNUITY` — a pay line (`EMPLOYEE_CONTRIBUTION`, head SALARIES, `DEDUCTIBLE`,
