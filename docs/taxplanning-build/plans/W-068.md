## Plan for W-068 — the leave travel concession exemption

**What this item is for (one sentence):** exempt leave travel assistance only as far as the Rules allow, in
the old regime only. Each journey is exempt up to the lesser of what was spent and the fare the Rules admit
for its mode. There are at most two journeys per four-calendar-year block, with the carried-over journey
counted separately, and the total never exceeds the assistance received.

**This plan raises design decisions (C0–C7) before anything is built.**

### What I read

- **The `W-068` row:** *"**modifies** `ExemptionNode` — LTA — `DR 01` R10 — **calendar** blocks; four independent
  caps"*.
- **Rulebook R10:**
  - `SAL-LTA-001`/`-002`: the value of travel concession, capped at the expense actually incurred.
  - `-003`: air (NEEDS VERIFICATION).
  - `-004`: rail-connected, the AC first-class rail fare.
  - `-005`: not rail-connected, first-class or deluxe fare, else ₹30/km.
  - `-BLOCK-001`: two journeys per block of four **calendar** years from 2022.
  - `-CARRY-001`: the carry-over.
  - `-CHILD-001`: at most two surviving children.
  - `-NEW-001`: not in the new regime.
  - H-8 names the four independent caps: actual expense, fare class, two journeys per block, and the regime.
- **`DR 13` R-12:** blocks are 2022–2025 and 2026–2029, and **each shipped tax year straddles a block boundary**,
  so one tax year can hold journeys from two blocks, *"each with its own two-journey allowance and its own
  carry-over state"*.
- **Sources, as read:**

  - **S4 r.278 (pp.1775–1776), for TY 2026-27:** *"the amount actually incurred … subject to"*:
    - (i) **air** — *"not exceed the fare admissible for the class of travel to which the employee is
      entitled, by the shortest route"*;
    - (ii) **rail-connected, other than air** — *"the air-conditioned first-class rail fare by the shortest
      route"*;
    - (iii) **not rail-connected** — (A) with a recognised public transport system, *"the 1st class or deluxe
      class fare"*; (B) with none, *"₹30 per kilometre, for the distance of the journey by the shortest
      route"*.
    - (2) two journeys *"in a block of four calendar years commencing from the calendar year 2022"*.
    - (3)–(4) the carry-over, *"first availed of … during first calendar year of the immediately succeeding
      block"*, not counted against the two.
    - (5)–(6) not more than two surviving children, except children born before 1-10-1998 and multiple births
      after one child.
  - **Rules 1962 r.2B (DoR compendium pp.348–350), for TY 2025-26:**
    - (1)(i) air — *"air economy fare of the national carrier by the shortest route"*;
    - (ii) *"air-conditioned first class rail fare"*;
    - (iii)(A) first class or deluxe fare;
    - **(iii)(B) no transport — "an amount equivalent to the air-conditioned first class rail fare, for the

### Findings

1. **The rulebook's air limb is the 1962 text.** `SAL-LTA-003` ("economy fare of the national carrier") is r.2B's
   wording. For TY 2026-27, r.278(1)(i) admits *the fare for the class the employee is entitled to*. The
   2025-26 and 2026-27 versions differ here, and in (iii)(B): an AC first-class rail fare equivalent, against
   ₹30/km.
2. **The fares are facts the engine can't know.** What is "the AC first-class rail fare by the shortest route"
   or "the class the employee is entitled to" is a figure the taxpayer supplies with the journey. The rule's job
   is to say *which* fare applies to the mode, and to take the lesser of that and the expense. Only r.278's
   ₹30/km is a figure the Rules themselves state.
3. **The block state is one block's, and a year spans two** (R-12). A journey in February 2026 counts in block
   2022–25, and one in May 2026 in block 2026–29. `LedgerSnapshot`'s single counter can't express that.
4. **The children limit (`-CHILD-001`) restricts which family members' fares count.** Without a per-person fare
   on each journey it can't be applied. Ignoring it over-allows for a family with three or more children born
   after 30-9-1998.
5. **The total is bounded by the assistance received** ("the value of travel concession or assistance
   received"), as well as per journey by the expense.
6. **This is as large as HRA was:** a journey input, a per-block ledger, rows for both years and the node.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C0 — split.**
  - **(a) recommended:**
    - **`W-068a`:** the journey input, the per-block ledger (replacing `LedgerSnapshot`'s two LTA fields) and
      the fingerprint;
    - **`W-068`:** the rows, the node, and coverage and catalogue.
  - **(b)** One item.
- **C1 — the journey input:** `EnteredFacts.leaveTravel`, a `List<Journey>`, each with:
  - the date started (which gives its block);
  - `mode` ∈ {`AIR`, `RAIL_CONNECTED`, `NOT_RAIL_CONNECTED_WITH_PUBLIC_TRANSPORT`,
    `NOT_RAIL_CONNECTED_WITHOUT_PUBLIC_TRANSPORT`}, the four clauses of r.278(1) / r.2B(1), a closed statutory
    classification;
  - `amountIncurred`;
  - `fareAdmissible` (the fare the Rules name for its mode, as the taxpayer states it; empty only for 2026's
    no-transport case);
  - `distanceKm` (for that case).
- **C2 — the per-block ledger:** replace the two LTA fields with `Map<Integer, LeaveTravelBlock>`, keyed by the
  block's first calendar year, each holding journeys already claimed in earlier tax years and whether the
  carry-over is still available. This is the R-12 fix.
- **C3 — the rows (D-035 names):**
  - `SAL-LEAVE-TRAVEL-CONCESSION`: `LeastOf` "amount actually incurred" / "fare admissible", both years,
    `ExceptIn [NEW_TAX_REGIME]`. The fare is the entered base for the modes whose fare the Rules name by
    class.
  - `SAL-LEAVE-TRAVEL-DISTANCE-RATE`: TY 2026-27 only, `PerUnitPerPeriod ₹30 per KM`, read for the no-transport
    mode. TY 2025-26 has no such figure: the entered rail-fare equivalent stands.
  - `SAL-LEAVE-TRAVEL-JOURNEYS`: two per block, with the carry-over as r.278(2)–(4) / r.2B(2)–(3) state. This
    needs a count shape; I propose `Fixed` read as a count, or a small `JourneysPerBlock` shape, decided at
    `W-068` with evidence.
- **C4 — the node:**
  - Journeys in date order.
  - Each journey is assigned to its block; a journey beyond the block's allowance (after the ledger's earlier
    claims) gets a nil line limited by "journeys in block".
  - A first-calendar-year carry-over journey is uncounted where the previous block was unused.
  - Per journey: the least of the expense and the admissible fare.
  - Total: the lesser of the sum and the `LTA` received.
  - One EXEMPTION line per journey with `limitedBy`. New regime: nil. Two or more employments: refused, as for
    HRA.
- **C5 — the children limit:**
  - **(a) recommended:** not modelled here. The catalogue gets a `NotModelled` disqualifier, and Appendix F
    notes it is owed with per-person fares.
  - **(b)** Take a per-person fare on each journey now.
- **C6 — the rulebook's `SAL-LTA-003`:** an erratum recording r.278(1)(i)'s real wording (finding 1).
- **C7 — sourcing:** TY 2026-27 is `SOURCE_CHECKED` (S4). TY 2025-26 is `UNVERIFIED`, as with W-062/W-067: the
  compendium is current only to 2010–11. Its `introducedBy` is the IT (First Amdt.) Rules, 1998 (clause (1)),
  and **its notification number and date are not in the compendium**, so that version cites the text and flags
  the gap with `whatToCheck`, or I stop to source it at `W-068`.
