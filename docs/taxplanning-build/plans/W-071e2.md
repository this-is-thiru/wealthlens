## Plan for W-071e2 — loans

**What this item is for (one sentence):** value an interest-free or concessional loan from the employer as the
interest the State Bank of India would have charged, less the interest paid, with each year's nil for petty and
medical loans.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6). C2 and C3 are readings of the Rule's words; nothing is coded until you
choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-071e2` row** (D-116). `DR 01` `PERQ-LOAN-2026` (was **NEEDS VERIFICATION** — "not located").
- **TY 2026-27, S4 r.15(5) Table IV Sl. 1** (Gazette 271092, p.1546, read here), for every employee (D-116 C2):
  - *"Benefit from the provision of interest-free or concessional loan for any purpose made available to the employee
    or any member of his household during the relevant tax year by the employer or any person on his behalf"*;
  - value: *"the sum equal to the interest computed at the annual rate charged by the State Bank of India … as on the
    1st day of the relevant tax year in respect of loans by the bank for the same purpose, using the maximum
    outstanding monthly balance as reduced by the interest, if any, actually paid by him or any such member of his
    household"*;
  - D: *"(a) No value would be charged if such loans are made available for medical treatment in respect of diseases
    specified in rule 18 or where the amount of loans is not exceeding **₹2,00,000** in the aggregate; and (b) where
    the benefit relates to the loans made available for medical treatment referred to in clause (a), the exemption so
    provided shall not apply to so much of the loan as has been reimbursed to the employee under any medical
    insurance scheme."*
  - **r.15(8)(e):** *"'maximum outstanding monthly balance' means the aggregate outstanding balance for each loan as on
    the last day of each month"*.
  - **r.18** (p.1549): the hospital-approval rule whose list the medical nil points to.
- **TY 2025-26, Rules 1962 r.3(7)(i)** (consolidated rule 3, p.3, V4): the same valuation *"as on the 1st day of the

- **Code:** `PerquisiteNode` and its helpers; `D-116 C3` — the SBI rate is entered, not seeded (it is the Bank's

### Findings

1. **The Rule states an annual rate on monthly balances but not the arithmetic between them.** The defined balance
   is a month-end figure; the natural reading is one month's interest per month-end balance — rate ÷ 12 × balance,
   summed over the months. Daily accrual would need balances the definition does not supply.
2. **"The amount of loans … in the aggregate" for the petty nil** is not defined: the amounts lent, or the balance
   outstanding. With a loan partly repaid the two differ. Neither text says whether an employer's loans are added
   across employers.
3. **The medical nil and the insurance proviso interact:** only "so much of the loan as has been reimbursed" loses
   the nil, from when it was reimbursed.
4. **The figures differ by year:** ₹2,00,000 / twenty thousand rupees; rule 18 / rule 3A for the diseases.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — input:** `EnteredFacts.loans`, each an **`EmployerLoan`**: the employment; the amount lent; the SBI rate for
  the same purpose on 1 April (a percentage, entered — D-116 C3); the month-end balances for the months in the year
  it was outstanding (up to 12); the interest paid in the year; whether it is for treatment of a disease the year's
  rule lists (a stated fact — the engine does not hold the list); and, for such a loan, the amount reimbursed under
  medical insurance and the month it was reimbursed.
- **C2 — the arithmetic (finding 1):**
  - **(a) recommended:** Σ over the months of (month-end balance × rate ÷ 12), rounded once, less the interest paid,
    floored at nil.
  - **(b)** Daily accrual — needs balances the Rule does not define.
- **C3 — the petty nil (finding 2):**
  - **(a) recommended:** the **amounts lent** by one employer, added up ("the amount of loans"), compared **not
    exceeding** the limit — nil for every loan of that employer when within it. Per employer, as D-117 C4a read
    "from the employer".
  - **(b)** The highest aggregate month-end balance.
- **C4 — medical (finding 3):** a loan for a listed disease is nil, except that from the month after reimbursement
  the valued balance is the lesser of that month's balance and the amount reimbursed.
- **C5 — rows**, `AllRegimes`, both years:
  - `PERQ-LOAN` — the valuation: 100% of the interest the node computes at the entered rate (the row carries the
    Rule's words and provenance, not a figure);
  - `PERQ-LOAN-PETTY` — a `Fixed` limit (₹2,00,000 / twenty thousand rupees), compared "not exceeding" (inclusive);
  - the medical nil is a nil line citing `PERQ-LOAN`, naming rule 18 / rule 3A.
- **C6 — verification:** TY 2026-27 `SOURCE_CHECKED`; TY 2025-26 `UNVERIFIED` (consolidated rule 3).

### How I will verify

- **A ₹5,00,000 loan outstanding all year, SBI rate 9%, ₹10,000 interest paid:** 12 × 5,00,000 × 9% ÷ 12 = 45,000
  − 10,000 = **35,000**, both years.
- **Repaid monthly** — month-end balances 2,90,000 down to 1,80,000 by 10,000: sum 28,20,000 × 9% ÷ 12 = **21,150**.
- **Petty:** lent 2,00,000 in TY 2026-27 → **0**; 2,00,001 → valued; lent 20,000 in TY 2025-26 → **0**, 25,000 →
  valued.
- **Medical:** ₹4,00,000 for a listed disease → **0**; with 1,00,000 reimbursed in month 6 and balances above it →
  1,00,000 × 9% ÷ 12 × 6 months = **4,500**.
- **Refused:** more than 12 balances; a reimbursement on a loan not stated as medical; a negative rate.
- **Bite probes:** ÷ 12 dropped; "not exceeding" made strict; the two years' petty limits swapped.

## As built — departures from the plan (for review)
- **`Rate.ofShare(base, part, whole)`** is new: an annual rate for a share of the year, multiplied and divided once
  and rounded once — `Rate.of` then a division would round twice (a test shows 0.62 at 9% for a month: 0.00 once,
  0.01 twice).
- **The insurance reimbursement's month is a position** in the month-end balances (0-based): the months after it are
  valued on the lesser of the balance and the amount.
- **A medical loan with a reimbursed part is still reduced by all the interest paid** — the Rule reduces "the
  interest … computed" by "the interest, if any, actually paid", without apportioning it. **Reviewer: confirm.**
- **`EnteredFacts`' constructor** was folded to one `copied` helper for its lists (Checkstyle's complexity limit,
  crossed by the twelfth field); every list keeps its message, `disabledTransport` its own.
- **Catalogue:** "Loans and other benefits" split into "Loans from your employer" (`ByRule`) and "Holidays, cards,
  clubs and assets" (`NotModelled`, W-071e3).
