### W-313 — a demerger reads its share ratio backwards (G-2)
- **The fix:** the ratio is read **new:held**, as the market writes it (ITC "1:10"). For each lot, the new lot's
  quantity = lot × new ÷ held, and its price = lot price × the new stock's cost share × held ÷ new. So quantity × price
  = the lot's cost × the share. The main lot keeps its quantity and takes its own cost share, as today.
  `DemergedStockContext` carries `newShares` and `heldShares`. `DemergerContext` loses its unused main-side ratio.
- **Refused at entry:** a ratio that is not two whole numbers above nil gives 400 "A demerger's ratio is new shares to
  shares held, two whole numbers above nil such as 1:10, but it was 1.5:10". Today it is refused only for not having
  two parts, and "1.5:10" would fail at perform with Java's number text.
- **W-214's TODO** ("come to this for uneven ratios") is what this fixes. The marker goes, and W-214's parked row
  keeps only its other TODO (`AssetEntity`).
- **Red tests:** report §4 scenario 6 (ITC: 100 ITC Hotels @ ₹540.40 = ₹54,040; today 10,000), and the entry refusal.
  **Probe:** multiply by `newShares` only, and scenario 6 fails.

### W-312 — a bonus pools every holder at a broker into the first lot's holder (G-1)
- **The fix:** the broker's lots are grouped by account holder (account type and holder), as a depository credits
  each demat account. Each holder gets their own entitlement, rounded down on its own, one bonus lot copied from
  **their** first lot, and one bonus transaction copied from **their** first buy. Only their transactions get the
  action noted. `processBonusSharesTxns` becomes private; only the class calls it.
- **Red tests:** report §4 scenario 1 (Self 20, Mother 12; today Self 26, Mother 6), and per-holder rounding (Self 3
  + Mother 3 at 1:2 gives 4 and 4; pooled it would be 1.5 + 1.5 → 3 to Self). **Probe:** group by broker only, and
  both fail.

  plan.
