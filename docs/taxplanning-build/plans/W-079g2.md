## Plan for W-079g2 — other sources, life-policy sums

**What this item is for (one sentence):** decide, in both years, whether a sum received under a life policy is
excluded from total income (S2 Schedule II Sl. 2; 1961 s.10(10D)). Where it is not excluded and the policy is not
unit-linked, charge the part beyond the premiums paid under other sources (S2 s.92(2)(l) with S4 r.59; 1961
s.56(2)(xiii) with its Rules 1962 rule).

**This plan raises choices (C1–C6); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### Findings

1. **Both years apply the same test to one policy:**
   - received on death: excluded;
   - issued before 1-4-2003: excluded, since no condition applies;
   - otherwise, the ratio against the band for its issue date;
   - for a ULIP issued from 1-2-2021, or another policy issued from 1-4-2023, the highest annual premium against
     ₹2,50,000 / ₹5,00,000.

   **The IFSC carve-out differs:** the 2025 Act reaches only IFSC policies issued on or after 1-4-2025; the 1961
   eighth proviso, as FA 2025 substituted it, has no date.
2. **Several capped policies need a choice the Acts leave to guidelines.** When a person holds more than one policy
   of a capped class, the exemption covers *"those"* policies whose aggregate premium stays within the limit. Which
   ones is the person's choice, under the Board's guidelines, which I have not read; they are on incometaxindia.gov.in,
   which answers 403 here. A single capped policy needs no guideline.
3. **A ULIP's non-exempt sum is capital gains, not other sources** (S2 s.67(5); 1961 s.45(1B), inserted by FA 2021
   s.16), which is W-088's.
4. **r.59's figure can't go below nil,** because s.92(2)(l) charges only *"the sum exceeding"* the premiums. A
   receipt below the premiums gives nil, not a loss.
5. **The engine can't hold premium history across years,** so B or D (premium paid, not deducted, not already used)
   is entered.
