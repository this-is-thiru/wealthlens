**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: where `notALine` is read** (D-672 C2a put it in the content file).
  - **C1a (recommended):** `ExplanationJson.notALine` reads `{rule, reason}` entries. It refuses a duplicate or a blank reason. `RulePackCompiler` refuses a listed rule the timeline does not hold, or one that is both explained and listed, as it already does for explanations. It copies **none** of the list into a year's pack, because nothing at run time reads it. `ExplanationCompilationTest` pins, for each shipped year, that every rule of the pack is either explained or listed.
  - C1b: the list lives only in the test. Then it is not next to the sentences it completes.
- **C2: charge lines whose `out` is nil** (STT and MISC are `NOT_DEDUCTED` today):
  - **C2a (recommended):** one sentence names both figures: "Of the {in} … , {out} is deducted in working out the gain." The row's limb stays the data, and nothing in the wording claims a charge is never deductible.
  - C2b: "… is not deducted". That states today's limb in the content.

**Draft sentences** (Claude's wording, for your adoption):
- **The four charges** (C2a). For example, `CG-CHARGE-COST`: "Of the {in} charged on the purchase, {out} is deducted in working out the gain." `-TRANSFER` says "on the sale", `-STT` says "of securities transaction tax", and `-MISC` says "of other charges".
- **`CG-DEPOSIT`:** "This gain of {in} is left out of capital gains: a deposit's earnings are entered as interest under income from other sources."
- **`CG-EXEMPT-RESIDENTIAL-HOUSE`:** "Of the {in} gain, the part put into a residential house is not taxed, leaving {out}."
- **The two `CG-TERM-*` rows:** "This line decides whether the {in} gain is short-term or long-term, from how long the asset was held."
- **The three `STT-*` rows:** "This line reads whether securities transaction tax was chargeable on the purchase (sale), which decides how the gain is taxed."
- **`CG-INDEXED-COST-LAND-BUILDING`:** "With its cost indexed for inflation, the {in} gain is {out}."
- **`SETOFF-WITHIN-CAPITAL-GAINS`:** "This line totals your capital gains and losses of each kind and sets a loss against gains in the order shown."
- **The three retirement rows and the car:** worded on their explained siblings, for example "Of the {in} commuted pension you received, {out} is not taxed."
