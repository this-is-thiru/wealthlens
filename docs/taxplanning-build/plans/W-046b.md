## Plan for W-046b — the old-regime age ladders and ladder selection (D-071)

**What this item is for (one sentence):** a resident aged 60 or 80 at any time during the tax year
should be taxed on the First Schedule's senior ladders when they opt out of the default regime, with

### Findings — B2 and B3 sourced

- **B3 (RNOR counts as resident): sourced.** S2 s.5(1) scopes the income of *"a person, who is a
  resident"*, and in (c) continues *"but when such person is 'not ordinarily resident' in India under
  section 6(13) …"*. So the Act treats a not-ordinarily-resident person **within** "resident".
  RNOR → `resident = true`; NON_RESIDENT → `false`.
- **B2 (age at any time during the tax year): sourced, with one unsourced edge.**
  - The test is statutory (s.2(100); the First Schedule's own words). The highest age reached during a
    year is the age on its **last day**. So age = completed years from the date of birth to
    `TemporalContext.taxYearEnd()`, and a person who turns 60 on any day of the year qualifies for
    the whole year.
  - **The edge:** someone born on **1 April** reaches the anniversary one day *after* the year ends,
    so by a calendar reckoning they are 59. A legal doctrine may say a person attains an age on the day
    *before* the anniversary, but **I only know that from memory and can't source it**. I'll use the
    calendar reckoning, pin it in a test named for the edge, and record it for the **OQ-1** reviewer.

### C. Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — selection (D-071 B1).** `TaxByBasisNode` also declares the two age ladders. For the running
  regime:
  - it takes every declared ladder **other than** `rateTable` whose in-force version `appliesIn` this
    regime and has **non-empty** conditions that **all** hold;
  - **one** such ladder replaces `rateTable`;
  - **none** means `rateTable` applies;
  - **more than one** is refused, naming both.

  Nothing in the node names an age. The ages live in the rules' conditions.
- **C2 — where the `ConditionContext` is built.** A new `NodeContext.conditions()` builds it from
  `EngineInputs`:
  - person type = `assessee.type()`;
  - resident per B3;
  - ages = {SELF → age per B2} for an `Individual`, and **empty** for an `Huf`, which has no birth
    date;
  - disabilities = {SELF → severity} where certified.

  It lives in `pipeline/`, because `rules/` may not depend on the runner (`ArchitectureTest`). It's
  **not in the row's Produces**; it's listed here for approval.
- **C3 — the ladders' conditions, as data:**
  - `…-AGE-60TO79`: `[IsResident, AgeAtLeast(60, SELF), Not(AgeAtLeast(80, SELF))]`;
  - `…-AGE-80`: `[IsResident, AgeAtLeast(80, SELF)]`.

  Both are `OnlyIn FIRST_SCHEDULE_RATES`.
- **C4 — years.**
  - **(a) recommended:** author the **2026-27** versions now (Part I-B). The 2025-26 versions (Part
    I-A, *"previous year"*) come in `W-052c`, beside the base ladder's own 2025-26 version, which

  - **(b)** Author both years now, as the row says.
