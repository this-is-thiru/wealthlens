## Plan for W-153c — the charging row and path per kind by rule data (model choice and split proposal)

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** which row charges each kind of other-sources income, and
how the item is then computed, stops being `OtherSourcesNode`'s `switch` and maps. A new kind, or a law that moves a
kind, then becomes new data (D-318, K1, K1r).

**This plan raises one design choice (C1) that decides the shape of everything after it, and proposes a split (C2).
Nothing is built from it.** It is over §7 whole: about 300 lines of main code, 15 rows touched or added and about 200
lines of tests.

    - s.92(2) clauses (a) – (m) (pp.138–139): (a) "any dividend", (e) interest on securities, (i) interest on

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — the model:**
  - **(a) recommended — the Act's own structure: one income row per item (s.92), and deduction rows that reach it
    (s.93).**
    - **Income:** one set `RS-OS-INCOME` (`AT_MOST_ONE`, `REGIME_AND_CONDITIONS`) holds the rows that charge an item:
      - `OS-INTEREST`: the four interest kinds;
      - `OS-DIVIDEND`: dividend, deemed dividend;
      - `OS-COMPENSATION-INTEREST-INCOME` (new, s.92(2)(i)), or `OS-INTEREST` widened to clause (i) (chosen in its
        plan);
      - `OS-KEYMAN-POLICY`, `OS-FORFEITED-ADVANCE`, `OS-TERMINATION-COMPENSATION`;
      - `OS-RESIDUAL` (s.92(1)): other, **unit income**, family pension, commuted fund pension, death gratuity.

      Each row reaches its kinds by `othersources.kind`, and its base is read from its own cap, so `KindRow` and the
      two maps go. A kind no row charges is refused, naming the kind and the set ("no row of RS-OS-INCOME charges
      WINNINGS in TY 2026-27; the coverage register defers it"). That replaces `requireBuiltKinds`' two named
      messages.
    - **Deductions:** `OS-COMPENSATION-INTEREST` (half), `OS-COMMUTED-PENSION`, `OS-DEATH-GRATUITY` (the entire
      amount), `OS-FAMILY-PENSION` / `-NEW-TAX-REGIME` (the share) and `OS-DEEMED-DIVIDEND` (nil) become deduction
      rows reaching their kinds by `othersources.kind`. The node applies each that reaches the item, after its income
      line.
    - **Expenses:** "no other deduction" (s.93(1)(f)) and "only interest expense" (s.93(2)) are already the expense
      rows' reach (W-153b). An expense no row reaches is a nil line on the row it misses, with W-153b's words. The
      outright refusals go.
    - **What no longer lives in Java:**
      - the `switch`, the five paths, `KindRow`, `IN_FULL`, `WHOLE_DEDUCTED`, `BUSINESS_HEAD_KINDS`;
      - the refusal's choice of row (`OS-DIVIDEND-INTEREST` / `OS-DEEMED-DIVIDEND` / the item's own row).

      What remains is the order of lines and the year's dividend limit (which is a year-level computation, not a
      kind choice).
    - **The cost — an exception to D-318 C3a (row ids unchanged), yours to grant:**
      - every number is unchanged;
      - but the five kinds with a share deduction get **two lines (income, then deduction) instead of one**;
      - unit income's income line moves from `OS-DIVIDEND` to `OS-RESIDUAL` (a correction, from the statute above);
      - refused expenses move from one combined nil line on the path's row to one nil line per row missed
        (commission, interest, expenditure);
      - the in-full kinds' trace words become the kind code ("item n: KEYMAN_POLICY"), as interest already is.

      Each change is shown by the dump in its sub-item.
  - **(b) the WI row's literal reading — a path definition chosen by kind:**
    - a classification set `RS-OS-KIND` with rows carrying a definition `OtherSourceTreatment(path, words)`, where
      the path is one of `INCOME`, `DIVIDEND_LIMITED`, `SHARE_DEDUCTED`, `PENSION_SHARE`;
    - the charging rows are chosen by kind in one set.

    Every trace line stays as it is (C3a holds). But the paths stay Java, and with them "no other deduction", "the
    entire amount", "only interest expense": which path a kind takes becomes data, what the path does does not. The
    path names would be the law's categories in an enum, which is what §4 forbids. Unit income's citation would also
    stay wrong unless (b) adds a row for it.
  - **(c)** close W-153c after only the unit-income correction, and place (a) or (b) later.
