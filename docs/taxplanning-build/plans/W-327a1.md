## Plan for W-327a1 — a plain "why" for cap results

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** when a cap result reaches a trace line with no limit sentence,
the line ends in the cap shape's engine text ("CONTRIBUTIONS in excess of fixed", "actual amounts", "100% of
PAYMENT_RECEIVED"). This item makes the shape publish a reason, as W-327a did for the regime, and the content word it.

**Probe (run before this plan, then removed; `git diff` shows `rules/cap` unchanged):**
- **What reaches a user.** I re-read the W-327 log at the 13 sites that pass a cap result through unchanged. Most of their lines already read in words: they are least-of limbs that `byLimit` names (HRA, standard deduction, allowances, retirement, set-off). Only these arrive as engine text:

  | "CONTRIBUTIONS in excess of fixed" | `AmountInExcess` | `EmployerContributionNode:111` (1,453) |

- **Moving tests:** none. I gave `CapResult` a third component and set it in every shape but `LeastOf`, then ran the taxengine unit tier: 4,186 tests, 0 failures.
- **Where the reason travels.** Each of the 13 sites holds a `CapResult` or an `AggregateOutcome` (`rules/cap`), which builds a `CapResult` through `asCapResult`. There are 12 `new CapResult(` calls, all in `rules/cap`, plus 2 in `CapShapeTest`.
- **Words for bases.** If the sentences named the base, 38 base codes in caps outside a least-of would need a word (36 `PercentOfBase` bases, plus `CONTRIBUTION` and `CONTRIBUTIONS`), and most of those caps sit at sites that write their own text.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: what the sentences name.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** the four shapes that reach a user publish a reason whose facts are figures only: money and a share. Their sentences name no base, so no base word is drafted now. Once a family item wants a sentence that names a base, it adds a base fact and its words.
  - C1b: the reasons also carry the base, and the content words all 38 bases in this item.
- **C2: the date-split cap** (`TemporallyApportionedCap`, standard deduction):
  - **C2a (recommended):** it publishes no reason, so its line keeps the dated text. Its words go to W-327b (salary), whose sites it reaches, as a recorded addition to that row.
  - C2b: word it here, with a reason that lists each part's dates and reason.
- **C3: the other four shapes** (`PerUnitPerPeriod`, `PercentOfBaseByLocation`, `PercentOfBaseByPopulation`, `SlabLadder`):
  - **C3a (recommended):** they publish no reason yet. They reach lines only inside composite texts at family sites (meals, accommodation, tax by slab), so W-327b and W-327e give them one where they word those sites.
  - C3b: give all eight shapes a reason now.

**The design (C1a, C2a, C3a):**
- **Facts and codes.** `ReasonFact` gains `MoneyFact(Money)` and `ShareFact(Rate)`. `ReasonCodes` gains:
  - `CAP_FIXED {amount}`
  - `CAP_SHARE {share}`
  - `CAP_IN_EXCESS {threshold}` (the threshold's figure, from `threshold.apply(ctx).value()`)
  - `CAP_ACTUAL_AMOUNTS {}`
  - `CAP_AGGREGATE_CEILING {ceiling}`
- **`CapResult(Money value, String limitedBy, Optional<Reason> reason)`.** The two-argument form stays for `LeastOf` (its limb is worded) and for the date-split result.
- **The shapes:** `Fixed`, `PercentOfBase` and `AmountInExcess` pass their reason. `AggregateWithCeiling` passes its reason into `AggregateOutcome`, which carries it to `asCapResult`.
- **`TraceLine.withCapResult(CapResult)`** sets `limitedBy` as today, plus the reason when there is one. The 13 sites change `.withLimitedBy(x.limitedBy())` to `.withCapResult(x)`, or `.withCapResult(outcome.asCapResult())` for the three `AggregateOutcome` sites.
- **The renderer** fills a money fact through `Money.inIndianNotation` and a share through `Rate.asPercentLabel`. The compiler already refuses an unworded code (W-327a).
- **Both locks:** relocked, one bump.

**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption):**
- `CAP_FIXED`: "It is a fixed amount of {amount}."
- `CAP_SHARE`: "It is {share} of the amount it applies to."
- `CAP_IN_EXCESS`: "Only the part above {threshold} counts."
- `CAP_ACTUAL_AMOUNTS`: "Your amounts together are within the overall ceiling, so all of them count."
- `CAP_AGGREGATE_CEILING`: "It is limited by the overall ceiling of {ceiling} on these amounts together."
- **Examples:**
  - "₹50,000 of your employer's contributions to your provident, pension and superannuation funds is taxed as a perquisite. Only the part above ₹7,50,000 counts."
  - "₹1,50,000 is deducted for your savings and payments under Schedule XV. It is limited by the overall ceiling of ₹1,50,000 on these amounts together."
