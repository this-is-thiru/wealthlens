## Plan for the batch W-318 – W-322 — XIRR, held trades, the P&L year, a duplicate price, the win/loss ratio

**What this batch is for:** the five gaps from the W-264d audit that you ruled into Phase 10 as one batch. Each keeps
its own red test, probe, D-row, board status and commit.

**Probe run (all five applied with their tests, then stashed as "W-318..W-322 probe"; the tree is unchanged):** 9
classes: the audit class, `Analytics*`, `ProfitAndLoss*`, `PortfolioIntegrationTest`, `PortfolioQueryServiceTest`,
`XirrCalculatorTest` and `ArchitectureTest`. **Three existing tests move (C4)**; everything else passes. The six new

`null` stock code. It now uses a plain set, sorted only for the message.

### W-318 — XIRR treats an unpriced holding as worth nothing (G-2)
- **The fix:** a holding with no price in `currentPrices` is **left out whole**, its trades as well as its value, and
  named: "Left out for want of a current price: INFY. Give currentPrices for them to include them." With no prices
  at all, every open holding is left out the same way. Today their cost stays in and their value is dropped, so the
  answer is a loss that isn't real.
- A stock no longer held keeps its trades. A price for a stock not held is ignored.
- **Red test:** report scenario 11: TCS + INFY, TCS priced → 2 cash flows, a positive XIRR, INFY named. Today:
  −27.4 %, no message. **Probe:** put the old "drop value only" back, and the test fails.

### W-319 — analytics count trades held `TEMPORARY` (G-1)
- **The fix:** the summary and XIRR read recorded trades only (`status != TEMPORARY`). `FAILED` is never set
  anywhere, so `TEMPORARY` is the only status that isn't a recorded trade.
- **Red tests:** scenarios 6 and 10 (`totalTrades` 2; 2 cash flows, 10 %). **Probe:** remove the filter, and both
  fail.

### W-320 — a P&L year written differently reads as empty (G-4)
- **The fix:** `getProfitAndLoss` reads the year through `FinancialYear.parse` (`FinancialYear:39 – 41`). `2025-26`
  gives 400 "A financial year is written as portfolio writes it, such as 2025-2026, but was 2025-26". A year with
  nothing traded is still 200, empty.
- **Red test:** scenario 5. **Probe:** remove the parse, and the test fails.

### W-321 — two XIRR prices for one stock answer 500 (G-3)
- **The fix:** 400 "currentPrices names TCS more than once; give one price per stock".
- **Red test:** scenario 12 (500 today). **Probe:** put `toMap` back, and the test fails.

### W-322 — wins with no losses show a win/loss ratio of 0.0 (G-5)
- **The fix:** no losses → no ratio (`null`), with nothing traded as well.
- **Red test:** scenario 8. **Probe:** put 0.0 back, and the test fails.

### Choices

  - **C1a (recommended):** left out whole and named, as above.
  - C1b: refuse the request until every holding has a price. That's stricter, but it makes the no-prices call (the
    common one) useless.
- **C2 (W-320):** **C2a (recommended):** refuse a year not written `2025-2026`, naming that form, as `FinancialYear`
  already does. C2b: also accept `2025-26`.
- **C3 (W-322):** **C3a (recommended):** `null`, so there is no number where none exists.
- **C4: the three expected values that move.** **C4a (recommended):** each changes by the rule this batch adopts,

  - `AnalyticsServiceTest:326` expects ratio 0.0 with nothing traded. It will expect `null`.
  - `AnalyticsServiceTest:469` expects "Open positions excluded". It will expect "Left out for want of a current
