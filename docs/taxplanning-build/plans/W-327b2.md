## Plan for W-327b2 — a plain "why" for perquisites

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** each of the 22 perquisite sites W-327b listed publishes its
"why" as a reason with facts, and the content words it, as W-327b1 did for the rest of salary.

**Probe (no code run):**
- **No test compares whole lines.** A search finds no test that compares whole trace lines (`isEqualTo(TraceLine…)`, `containsExactly(TraceLine…)` or a hand-built `TraceLine` in the node or golden tests). That agrees with W-327a's probe, where a reason on every regime and cap line moved only the field pin. So adding a reason at these sites moves no test.
- **Every site read.** I read each site's code for its facts (below), and read the sentence each line already has.

  - C2b: give each shape a reason anyway. None would reach a line today.

**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption):**
- `EXCLUDED_BY_RULE`: "So nothing is added to your salary for it."
- `ASSET_USE_COST_SHARE`: "It is a yearly share of the asset's cost for the {months} months you used it, less the {recovered} you paid back."
- `ASSET_USE_RENT`: "It is the rent your employer paid for it, less the {recovered} you paid back."
- `ASSET_TRANSFER_WRITTEN_DOWN`: "The asset is valued at {written} after {years} completed years of wear and tear, less the {price} you paid for it."
- `LESS_RECOVERED`: "It is the amount less the {recovered} you paid back."
- `GIFTS_BELOW_LIMIT`: "Gifts of {given} from this employer are below {limit}, so none of it is taxed."
- `GIFTS_REACH_LIMIT`: "Gifts of {given} from this employer reach {limit}, so all of it is taxed."
- `EDUCATION_WITHIN_LIMIT`: "The cost of {cost} a month is within {limit} a month per child, so it is not taxed."
- `EDUCATION_ABOVE_LIMIT`: "The cost of {cost} a month exceeds {limit} a month per child, so the whole for {months} months is taxed, less the {recovered} you paid back."
- `LOANS_WITHIN_PETTY_LIMIT`: "Loans of {lent} from this employer do not exceed {limit}, so nothing is taxed for them."
- `LOAN_FOR_LISTED_DISEASE`: "The loan is for treating a disease the rules list, so it is not taxed."
- `LOAN_INTEREST_AT_BANK_RATE`: "Interest at {rate} a year on the month-end balances comes to {interest}, less the {paid} you paid."
- `MEALS_IN_FULL`: "All of it is taxed: no limit reaches food provided this way this year."
- `CAR_PER_MONTH`: "It is the set amount for each of the {months} months."
- `CAR_PER_MONTH_WITH_CHAUFFEUR`: "It is the set amount for each of the {months} months, plus the amount for a chauffeur."
- `CAR_RUNNING_AND_WEAR`: "It is the running cost plus {wear} of wear and tear for {months} months, less the {charged} you were charged."
- `ACCOMMODATION_LESS_RENT`: "The accommodation is valued at {value}, less the {rent} rent you paid."
- `ACCOMMODATION_CONTINUED_CAP`: "It is capped at {cap}, the first year's value raised by the cost inflation index."
- `FURNITURE_COST_SHARE`: "It is a yearly share of the furniture's cost, less the {charges} you paid."
- `FURNITURE_HIRE`: "It is the hire charges, less the {charges} you paid."
- `NOT_SPECIFIED_EMPLOYEE`: "These benefits are not taxed for you: your salary paid in money, {salary}, does not exceed {threshold}, and you are not a director or a holder of a substantial interest."
- `SECURITY_VALUE`: "{units} units at a fair market value of {value} in all, less the {paid} you paid."
