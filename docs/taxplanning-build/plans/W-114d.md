**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** correct the engine so the assessee's own NPS
contribution reaches s.124(3) before s.123, as the Act allows; the defect under-deducts and makes the optimiser ask

**Reading:** the Act bars only the *same rupee* under both s.123 and s.124(3). It sets no order between them, and the
assessee claims. The engine claims under s.123 first and passes s.124(3) only what para 1(y)'s 10 % sub-cap left
(`ScheduleXvDeduction.lines` → `PensionDeduction.ownPension(residue)`; `01-rulebook.md`'s worked example under
`DED-NPS-SELF-001`, marked `INFERENCE`). So own NPS within the sub-cap gets no s.124(3) deduction when s.123's
ceiling is full, even though claiming it under s.124(3) is lawful and deducts more. Claiming s.124(3) first never
deducts less: every rupee there is deducted, while a rupee under s.123 may meet a full ceiling. A minor's deposit has
no other home, so it takes s.124's ceiling first.

**Probe (run and reverted before this plan):** `PensionDeduction.ownClaim` = min(own NPS, s.124 ceiling less the
minor's deposit), taken off `EMPLOYEE_NPS` before s.123.
