## Plan for W-112a — the s.425(4) / s.234C relief from deferment interest

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** stop charging deferment interest at an instalment date on
the tax of capital gains or dividends that had not yet arisen by that date, where that tax was paid later in the year,
as s.425(4) (2025) and s.234C's first proviso (1961) say. Until now `INT-DEFERMENT` charges it and only files an
`ATTENTION` notice (`InterestAndFeeNode:206 – 208`).

**Law, read this session (2026-11-13):**

| Text | What it says | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 s.425(4) | No interest under (1) or (3) on a shortfall where *"(a) the shortfall is on account of underestimation of, or failure to estimate"* (i) capital gains, (ii) s.2(49)(n) income, (iii) business income *"accruing or arising for the first time"*, (iv) dividend income, *"and (b) the assessee has paid in full, the tax payable on the said income had such income been part of total income, in any of the remaining instalments of advance tax, if any, or by the 31st day of March of the tax year"* | Gazette 265620 p.448 (sha256 `7acfb16fd7f3db8b`, matches the recorded copy) |

**What the engine has today:**
- A capital-gains bucket carries its `transferredOn` (`CapitalGainsInput:145`).
- A dividend (`OtherSourceKind.DIVIDEND`) carries **no date** (`OtherSourceItem` has `kind`, `received` and expenses only).
- The business head is not modelled, and winnings are refused by name (`OtherSourceKind` Javadoc). So (ii) and (iii)
  cannot arise in a computation.
- `TaxCalculator.compute` runs the pipeline as one pure call, `PipelineRunner.run(REGISTRY, pack, spec, inputs)`
  (`TaxCalculator` `compute`). A second run on fewer inputs is the same call. **Not yet probed** (see sizing).

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: what the relief measures at a date.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** the tax on carve-out income that **arose after** the date. At each date, the tax due on
    the returned income is taken *without* the carve-out income that arose after it: its tax is the tax due with it
    less the tax due without it ("had such income been part of total income"). The Gazette forms ask for exactly
    this break-up by period, "for allowing applicable relief from section 234C". The 15 March date leaves out
    income from 16 – 31 March.
  - C1b: any underestimation, whenever the income arose. The engine cannot know what the assessee estimated, so this
    is not computable. Not recommended.
- **C2: condition (b), "paid in full … in any of the remaining instalments … or by 31 March".**
  - **C2a (recommended):** relief at a date holds where the advance tax paid **after** that date and by 31 March is
    at least the tax left out at that date. Otherwise there is no relief at that date, and the trace line says why.
  - C2b: relief holds only where the advance tax paid by 31 March covers the whole tax due on the returned income.
    This is stricter, and denies relief to someone who paid the gains' tax but was short elsewhere.
- **C3: dividends have no date.**
  - **C3a (recommended):** `OtherSourceItem` gains `receivedOn` (`Optional<LocalDate>`), read only for this relief.
    A dividend without it gets no relief, and a notice names it. Stored drafts read as empty. The draft API docs and
    api-collection gain the field. The deemed dividend kind counts; s.2(40)(e) / s.2(22)(e) do not, as (6) says.
  - C3b: capital gains only. The notice stays, narrowed to dividends.
- **C4: where the law's list lives.** Facts, not rules: the relieved kinds and the 31 March day are tax-year data.
  - **C4a (recommended):** both live on `INT-DEFERMENT`'s `DefermentInterest` in each version: `relievedIncomes` with
    the four kinds as the Act names them, and `relievedIfPaidBy` = `03-31`. The provision references gain s.425(4),
    (6) and the 1961 proviso. Only capital gains and dividends can reach a computation. Winnings and first-time
    business income are named in `notIncluded` until their heads exist.
  - C4b: a new rule set `INT-DEFER-CARVEOUT`, as DR 15 C1 sketched (`INT-DEFER-CARVEOUT-001`). That adds a new id,
    a catalogue entry, a coverage row and its own section, for a sub-section of the same provision.
- **C5: the Gazette forms' accrual tables.** Schedule CG F and Schedule OS item 10 are not laid out as cells.
  - **C5a (recommended):** place a row after W-112a to lay them out from the figures this item computes.
  - C5b: lay them out in this item (larger).

**Worked example (by hand, for the golden test; the fixture is built to give these taxes):**
- Tax due on the returned income: ₹1,00,000. Without a long-term gain sold on 10 January: ₹74,000, so the tax left out
  at 15 June, 15 September and 15 December is ₹26,000. At 15 March the gain has arisen, so nothing is left out.
- Paid ₹11,100 by 15 June, ₹33,300 by 15 September, ₹55,500 by 15 December and ₹1,00,000 by 15 March.
- **With relief (C1a, C2a):** 15 June: 15 % of 74,000 = 11,100, so short 0. 15 September: 45 % = 33,300, short 0.
  15 December: 75 % = 55,500, short 0. 15 March: 100 % of 1,00,000, short 0. Interest **₹0**. Condition (b) holds:
  paid after 15 December = 44,500 ≥ 26,000.
- **Today's figure:** short 3,900 / 11,700 / 19,500 at 3 % = 117 + 351 + 585 = **₹1,053**.
- **(b) fails:** nothing paid after 15 December (cumulative stays 55,500). There is no relief at any date, so ₹1,053,
  plus 15 March 1 % of 44,500 = 445: **₹1,498**.
- Each base is a multiple of ₹100, so `INT-CALCULATION`'s rounding moves nothing.

**Risks or ambiguities:**
- C1 and C2 are readings of the Act, not stated by it. The forms support C1a; C2 is a judgement, which is why it is
  yours.
- Leaving out an income can change the slab tax on the rest, which is why the plan uses the with-and-without
  difference, as (b)'s "had such income been part of total income" reads.
