## Plan for W-241a — Persona gains through the portfolio

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** Ravi's and Meera's listed-equity sales enter the demo the way
a real user's do, as V2 buy and sell trades priced by the seeded cards. Their bills then show the s.196 / s.198 parts,
and Meera's shows the surcharge and its relief again.

**Findings (each read this session):**
- F1 — **No card prices a 2024 trade.** The earliest delivery cards start 2025-04-01 (`data/charges/zerodha-equity-delivery-2025-04-01.json`,
  `statutory-equity-delivery-2025-04-01.json`). With no card, the engine records nothing (`ChargeEngine.java:80` – `85`).
  A side with no breakdown reads as STT `UNKNOWN` (`CapitalGainsSummaryService.java:158` – `162`; `SttEvidence.java`), and
  s.198 turns on STT having been paid. Persona lots bought on 2024-04-01 (`Fixtures.java:48`) therefore cannot be
  bought through the API with STT on record. What the tax side does with `UNKNOWN` I have not traced. → **C1**.
- F2 — Every charge but STT is deductible: `CG-CHARGE-COST` (acquisition) and `CG-CHARGE-TRANSFER` (sale) list BROKERAGE,
  EXCHANGE_TXN, SEBI_FEE, IPFT, STAMP_DUTY, DP and GST. `CG-CHARGE-STT` lists STT as `NOT_DEDUCTED` (rule files
  under `rulepacks/timeline/law/rules/`, versions from 2026-04-01).
- F3 — Card mechanics: rates are percent (`TurnoverChargeCalculator.java:45`, `movePointLeft(2)`). Each line is rounded
  as its card says. GST (order 100) is 18 % of the BROKERAGE, EXCHANGE_TXN, SEBI_FEE, IPFT and DP lines already
  computed (`DerivedChargeCalculator.java`). DP is ₹13.50 per scrip per day to 2026-06-18 and ₹13 from 2026-06-19
  (`dedupeScope PER_SCRIP_PER_DAY`), so Ravi's two lots use two stock codes.
- F4 — Total income is rounded to ₹10 first (`RoundTotalIncomeNode.java:13` – `16`). The slab part is that rounded figure
  less the special-rate gains (`TaxByBasisNode.java:100`). Money keeps paise, rounded half-up (`Money.java:26`, `:42`).
- F5 — Only one profile per account may hold the portfolio (`TaxProfileService.java:84`). Ravi and Meera each need
  their own account, so the collection's one `{{EMAIL}}` / `{{JWT_TOKEN}}` (`opencollection.yml:9`, `:19`, `:23`) means
  logging in as each in turn.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1a (recommended)** — the demo buys the long lots on **2025-04-01**, the first day a card prices. They are still held
  long-term (17 months to 2026-09-01). Sale dates, prices and the short lot are the persona's.
