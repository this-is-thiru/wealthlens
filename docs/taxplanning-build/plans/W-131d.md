**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** let a person upload a Form No. 130, see what was read, confirm it,
and see it reconciled with their draft:
- behind a switch that stays off until W-131e and W-131f exist;
- refusing a certificate that is not theirs (B9-73);
- telling them in the notice that this is stored.

### Findings

1. **Two separable pieces:**
   - the document flow: upload, parse view, confirm, switch, PAN check, notice;
   - the reconciliation read, which joins confirmed certificates to a draft's computation.

   The second depends on the first's stored confirmations, and on nothing else new.
2. **The PAN check needs a fact,** like the TAN's, so the check finds the line from data.
3. **Which uploads to accept** can be read from the layout data (a year and form with a layout) rather than a year written
   in code. D-748's "TY 2026-27 on" then follows from the packs.
4. **The notice changes its version with any word.** Adding documents asks new registrations to accept the new notice;
   existing accounts are not asked again.

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — the split:**
  - **(a)** Two items, each with its own plan and probe:
    - **W-131d1:** the three T8 endpoints; the switch; the PAN check; what an upload accepts; the parse run started after an
      upload (W-131c); the notice; the api-collection; the runbook.
    - **W-131d2:** `GET /drafts/{draftId}/reconciliation`, which reconciles the draft's profile-and-year confirmed
      certificates with its computation (W-130), behind the same switch.
  - (b) One item.
- **C2 — the switch:**
  - **(a)** `app.tax.documents.enabled`: **false** in the three profile yamls and true in the integration-test yaml. Off,
    every documents endpoint answers 503 at its entry, as the charge engine's switch does, and nothing is written. Turning it
    on is a runbook step gated on W-131e and W-131f.
  - (b) No switch.
- **C3 — the PAN check** (B9-73):
  - **(a)** A fact `EMPLOYEE_PAN` on `partA.employee.pan` (relock).
    - Confirming refuses a certificate whose PAN differs from the profile's, or has none. The PAN compared is the one the
      person accepted, or, where they accepted none, the one read.
    - The parse view says whether the PAN matches, without printing either PAN in full.
  - (b) The check at upload, before parsing. That is not possible: the PAN is known only once read.
- **C4 — what an upload accepts** (finding 3):
  - **(a)** A document type some parser reads, and a tax year whose pack has that form's layout
    (`TaxCalculator.certificate` present). Anything else is a 400 naming the reason.
  - (b) A year written in code.
- **C5 — the notice** (finding 4):
  - **(a)** In W-131d1: the `tax-planning` part adds "salary certificates you upload (Form No. 130), what was read from
    them and what you confirmed". This is a new notice version, from the deploy carrying it.
  - (b) Only when the switch turns on.
