## Plan for W-076a — Chapter VIII catalogue entries, part 1

**What this item is for (one sentence):** write the section-catalogue entries that let 2025 ss.122, 124, 125, 126 and
1961 80A, 80CCC, 80CCD, 80CCE, 80CCH, 80D be marked `MODELLED` (SC-1, D-149). Each entry explains in plain words, with
no figures, what the deduction is and which built rule decides it.

**This plan has decisions C1–C4; nothing is written until you choose.**

### What I read

- **The catalogue contract:**
  - `SectionCatalogueEntry` — key `SEC-…`, `provision1961`, `provision2025`, `plainName`, `summary`, at least one
    route, disqualifiers.
  - `SectionCatalogue.verify`:
    - **SC-1** — a `MODELLED` section needs an entry citing it. `SectionRef.citedBy` takes **the first** section of a
      provision's reference, so an entry covers one section per Act.
    - **SC-2** — no digit, ₹, %, braces, "Rs", "lakh" or "crore" in any text, a `NotModelled` reason included. So
      clause numbers, OQ ids and dates cannot appear.
    - **SC-3** — every `ByRule` names a rule on the timeline.
  - `DisqualifierKind`: NOT_ELIGIBLE, CONDITION_FAILED, PROCESS_MISSED, THIRD_PARTY_MISSING, LATER_REVERSAL.
  - Existing entries (`SEC-INVESTMENT-DEDUCTION`, `SEC-REBATE-FOR-CERTAIN-INDIVIDUALS`): a `_note`, and a `_sources`
    line saying where each text comes from.
- **The rows' own notes** (W-075a–d5), and these headings:
  - 2025 Chapter VIII *"DEDUCTIONS TO BE MADE IN COMPUTING TOTAL INCOME"*, s.122 under *"A.—General"*;
  - s.124 *"Deduction in respect of employer and assessee contribution to pension scheme of Central Government"*;
  - s.125 *"Deduction in respect of contribution to Agnipath Scheme"*;
  - s.126 *"Deduction in respect of health insurance premia"*;
  - 1961 80CCC *"Deduction in respect of contribution to certain pension funds"*, 80CCE *"Limit on deductions under
    section 80C, section 80CCC and 80CCD"* (A0 pp.338, 340).

### Findings

1. **Ten sections need six entries.** Four pair across the Acts:
   - s.122 / 80A;
   - s.124 / 80CCD;
   - s.125 / 80CCH;
   - s.126 / 80D.

   **80CCC and 80CCE are 1961-only.** The 2025 Act folds both into s.123 (Schedule XV (x) and the s.123 ceiling), so
   each needs its own entry whose `provision2025` is s.123 — the only way, since `citedBy` reads one section.
2. **What cannot be said with digits is said in words:** "the first"/"the second" employer kind, "a share of salary",
   "the senior citizen's higher limit". The texts name rules through routes, not figures.
