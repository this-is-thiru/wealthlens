## Plan for W-112b — interest on a refund, both ways

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** show the interest the Government pays on a refund
(2025 s.437; 1961 s.244A), and record why the interest charged on an excess refund (s.426 / s.234D) and 1961
ss.214 – 217 are not computed (D-446).

**What the engine has:** `INT-CALCULATION` (r.269; TY 2025-26 `UNVERIFIED`, since r.119A has not been read) and
`months(from, to, wholeMonths)` in `InterestAndFeeNode` (used by s.423). `FilingFacts` holds the due date, furnishing
date, advance-tax and self-assessment payments with their dates, and tax at source. No draft holds a refund's grant
date. `InterestAndFees.Kind` has `INTEREST` and `FEE`, both owed by the person.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: what is computed.**
  - **C1a (recommended):** s.437(1) Sl. 1, 2 and (2) / s.244A(1)(a), (aa) and proviso, on the refund the
    computation shows (tax paid less the bill). The tax "as determined" for the 10 % test is the bill as computed,
    since a draft is the return as it will be furnished.
  - C1b: state it in `notIncluded` only and close the row.
- **C2: until when.**
  - **C2a (recommended):** to a grant date the person enters (`FilingFacts.refundGrantedOn`, `Optional`). Without
    one, to the as-of date, "as if granted today", as D-446 C2a does for interest owed. The line says which.
  - C2b: the as-of date only.
- **C3: which payment a refund comes out of.** Sl. 1 and Sl. 2 run from different dates, and the Act does not say
  which money is returned first.
  - **C3a (recommended):** out of self-assessment tax first, later payments first, and the rest out of TDS / TCS /
    advance tax. This never overstates the interest, and a notice says the order is an assumption.
  - C3b: TDS / advance tax first (larger interest).
  - C3c: pro rata.
- **C4: where it is shown.**
  - **C4a (recommended):** a separate figure, `InterestAndFees.Kind.RECEIVABLE`, kept out of the amount payable
    (`round.netpayable` adds only what is owed). Interest received is income of the year it is received in, and a
    notice says so.
  - C4b: netted against interest owed.
- **C5: coverage.**
  - **C5a (recommended):** 2025 s.437 and 1961 s.244A become `MODELLED` (catalogue `SEC-INTEREST-REFUND`).
    2025 s.426 and 1961 s.234D become `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, since a regular assessment cannot be entered in a draft
    (D-446's words for s.407). 1961 ss.214 – 217 become `OUT_OF_SCOPE` as "spent: s.214(3), not from assessment
    year 1989-90 (thc p.680)".

**Rule data (facts, not rules):** a new rule `INT-REFUND` (set `RS-INT-REFUND`) in both versions, definition
`RefundInterest(perMonth 0.5 %, noInterestBelowSharePercent 10)`. The periods' anchors (the due date, furnishing,
payment, grant) are the section's structure, as `INT-LATE-RETURN`'s are. Before naming, I'll check the id grammar
(`rules/id` PATTERN).

**Worked case (by hand, TY 2026-27, T13-1's bill ₹87,750 from `plans/W-089.md`):**
- **Base case:** tax at source ₹1,00,000, so the refund is ₹12,250 (at least ₹8,775, 10 % of 87,750). Furnished
  15-7-2027, by the due date of 31-7-2027, and granted 20-9-2027. Interest runs from 1-4-2027 to 20-9-2027: 5 months
  and 19 days, so 6 months. Base ₹12,200, times 0.5 % times 6 = **₹366**.
- **Late return:** furnished 10-8-2027. From 10-8 to 20-9 is 1 month and 10 days, so 2 months: **₹122**.
- **Under 10 %:** tax at source ₹95,750, so the refund is ₹8,000, below ₹8,775: **nil**.
- **No grant date, as-of 14-11-2027:** 1-4 to 14-11 is 7 months and 13 days, so 8 months: **₹488**, labelled as if
  granted that day.
- **Self-assessment (C3a):** TDS ₹80,000, self-assessment ₹20,000 paid 1-7-2027, furnished 15-7, granted 20-9.
  - The refund is ₹12,250. It comes first out of the ₹20,000 of self-assessment tax: Sl. 2 runs from 15-7 (the
    later of furnishing and payment) to 20-9, 2 months 5 days, so 3 months. ₹12,200 × 0.5 % × 3 = **₹183**.
  - Nothing comes out of TDS.
