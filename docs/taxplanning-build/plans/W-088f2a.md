### The shape

**`CapitalGainsInput`** (`taxengine.input`):
- `Bucket(Money gain, Term term, BucketFacts facts, Optional<String> portfolioRuleRef)` — `basis` replaced by `facts`;
  nulls refused as today.
- New nested record **`BucketFacts(String assetKind, boolean sttOnAcquisition, boolean sttOnTransfer,
  String acquisitionMode, Optional<String> corporateAction, LocalDate acquiredOn)`**. Blank or null refused;
  `corporateAction` present exactly when `acquisitionMode` is `CORPORATE_ACTION`, mirroring `portfolio`'s
  `Acquisition`, so a fact the rows test cannot be half-present. Strings are `portfolio`'s enum names, passed through.
- `grossGainOn(RateBasisHint)` deleted (no caller in `main`).
- **`RateBasisHint.java` deleted.**

**`BucketPart`** (`taxengine.rules.definition`, new): `record BucketPart(CapitalGainsPart part) implements
RuleDefinition` — the part the row assigns. Added to `RuleDefinition`'s permits and parsed in `DefinitionJson` as
`{"type": "BucketPart", "part": "…"}`.

**`CapitalGainsNode`:**
- `ruleSets()` adds `RS_CG_PART`.
- `partOf(ctx, bucket)`: `ctx.optionalRuleOf(RS_CG_PART, factsOf(bucket))` → the row's `BucketPart.part()`
  (`RuleDefinitions.require`); empty → `IllegalArgumentException`: *"Capital gains of {gain} ({term}, {facts}) fall
  in no row of RS-CG-PART for {tax year}; …"*, every fact named.
- `factsOf(bucket)`: `ItemFacts` with codes `capitalgains.term`, `capitalgains.asset`, `capitalgains.stt.acquisition`,
  `capitalgains.stt.transfer` (`PAID` / `NOT_PAID`), `capitalgains.acquisition`, and `capitalgains.corporate.action`
  only when present. `ItemFactId` constants in the node, as `TaxByBasisNode` does. The date is not an item fact until
  f2b (no row reads it); the refusal message names it.
- The `switch` in `partOf` and `requireTerm` deleted.

**`CapitalGainsAdapter`:**
- `engineBucket(bucket, term)` builds `BucketFacts` from `assetType().name()`, the two STT booleans,
  `acquisition().mode().name()`, `acquisition().corporateAction().map(Enum::name)`, `acquiredOn()`.
- `shortTermBasis`, `longTermBasis`, `requireMapped` deleted. The head-level handling stays: nil and
  `NOT_CAPITAL_GAINS` left out, `UNCLASSIFIED` and `SPECULATIVE` refused (D-241 C3a). Javadoc rewritten: the rate
  basis is the rule data's.

**`rule-sets.json`:** `{"id": "RS-CG-PART", "_note": "The part of the capital-gains head a bucket falls in, chosen
by its facts (S2 ss.196–198; 1961 ss.111A, 112, 112A; D-289); a bucket no row covers is refused",
"selection": "AT_MOST_ONE", "ground": "REGIME_AND_CONDITIONS"}`. `RuleSetIds` regenerated by
`GeneratedConstantsTest`.
