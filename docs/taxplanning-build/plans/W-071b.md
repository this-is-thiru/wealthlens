## Plan for W-071b — specified-employee and medical-travel thresholds

**What this item is for (one sentence):** put the two prescribed perquisite thresholds — the "specified employee"
salary amount and the medical-travel gross total income — into the pack for both tax years, each with the rule that
reads it.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C5). C1 changes what the item builds; nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read

### Findings

1. **Neither threshold has a reader today.** The specified-employee amount only decides whether a *benefit or amenity*
   (s.17(1)(c)) is a perquisite — the services, utilities, education and car items (W-071c/d), all blocked. The
   medical-travel amount only decides the travel limb of a *medical-treatment-abroad* perquisite, which nothing
   models. A threshold row with no rule reading it breaks I3 (every rule evaluated) and is speculative code
   (standard 2).
2. **The medical-travel test reads gross total income "before including therein the said expenditure".** The value
   decides a salary perquisite, but gross total income is computed from every head, after salary. In a DAG that is a
   cycle unless the travel perquisite is valued after the other income is known.
3. **"Income under the head "Salaries" by way of monetary payment" is a new measure** — not gross salary, not the
   accommodation salary. Whether it is before or after the s.19 deductions (standard deduction, professional tax) the
   words do not say ("income under the head" suggests after). This matters only when C1(b)'s specified employee is
   built.
4. **Both TY 2025-26 amounts are now READ**, and equal TY 2026-27's.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — what W-071b builds:**
  - **(a) recommended: medical treatment abroad, whole.** The perquisite of s.17(1)(f) / 1961 s.17(2)(iv) and its
    exclusion (s.17(2)(f), (3); 1961 proviso (vi)), with the r.19 / r.3D threshold as the rule that reads it. The
    specified-employee amount (r.17 / r.3C) moves to its first reader — W-071d (services, utilities) or W-071c (car),
    whichever you place first — with the measure of finding 3.
  - **(b)** Both thresholds now, the specified-employee status as its own traced determination with no consumer.
    Refused by standard 2 unless you accept it as a decided seam.
  - **(c)** Rows only: fails I3.
- **C2 — the gross-total-income test (only under C1a):**
  - **(a) recommended:** a new node `salary.medicaltravel` after `salary.income`, taking the "before" figure as the
    income already computed (every head, no travel). If it exceeds the threshold, the travel becomes a perquisite
    line added to the salary head's income. Today that figure is `salary.income`; when another head lands, the node
    must follow it (a note on the node and in DR 06). The standard deduction is taken before the travel is added —
    exact while salary alone exceeds ₹75,000/₹50,000; flagged for review when other heads land.
  - **(b)** The taxpayer states the gross total income before the travel. No cycle, but an entered figure the
    engine can compute.
  - **(c)** Refuse a travel claim where the test decides the answer. Leaves the limb unbuilt.
- **C3 — input (C1a):** `EnteredFacts.medicalTreatmentAbroad`, each a `MedicalTreatmentAbroad`: the employment that
  pays; the treatment and stay cost; the travel cost; the amount RBI permitted for treatment and stay (the taxpayer
  states it — RBI sets no fixed figure the Act names). Patient, family member and one attendant are one claim; a
  second attendant's cost is refused, since s.17(2)(f)(iii) excludes "one attendant".
- **C4 — rows**, all `AllRegimes`, both years:
  - `PERQ-MEDABROAD-STAY`: treatment and stay excluded up to the RBI-permitted amount; the excess is a perquisite.
  - `PERQ-MEDABROAD-TRAVEL`: travel excluded where gross total income before it ≤ ₹8,00,000; otherwise wholly a
    perquisite. The threshold is a `Fixed` amount compared "does not exceed", as the rebate compares its ceiling.
- **C5 — verification:**
  - **TY 2026-27:** `SOURCE_CHECKED` (s.17(2)(f), (3) and r.19 read in the Gazette).
  - **TY 2025-26:** the amount is READ (r.3D), but 1961 proviso (vi)'s text is read only in the thc.nic.in copy
    (current to Act 7 of 2019) plus FA 2025 s.9. The three Acts after it (F(No.2)A 2019, TLA 2019, interim FA 2024)
    are unread, as for the other TY 2025-26 salary rows, so the version is `UNVERIFIED`, its `whatToCheck` naming
    those three.

### How I will verify

- **Hand-worked tests, each year:**
  - **Treatment and stay 6,00,000, RBI permitted 5,00,000:** perquisite **1,00,000**.
  - **Travel 1,50,000, salary income 7,50,000 (no other head):** 7,50,000 ≤ 8,00,000 → excluded, perquisite **0**.
  - **Travel 1,50,000, salary income 8,00,001:** exceeds → perquisite **1,50,000**; salary income **9,50,001**.
  - **Boundary, exactly 8,00,000:** "does not exceed" → excluded.
  - **Refused:** a second attendant.
- **End to end:** total income rises by the taxable travel in both regimes.
- **Bite probes:** threshold compared "less than" instead of "does not exceed"; travel added before the test; the RBI
  cap ignored.

## Revision 1 — C2 (2026-09-28, before coding)

**What I found:** C2a said the taxable travel is "added to the salary head's income" by a node after `salary.income`,
and that "total income rises". Reading the wiring to build it:
- **No node produces `totalincome`.** `GoldenCaseRunner` (`backend/src/test/.../golden/GoldenCaseRunner.java:54–56`)
  stands in for it: *"the heads between gross salary and it are not built yet"*. The salary golden runs stop at
  `salary.income` (`:117–119`).
- So a node **after** `salary.income` has no reader: the travel would be valued and traced but reach no income
  figure — inert, and the plan's end-to-end check could not be written.

**Revised C2 (recommended first):**
- **(a) recommended — income before the travel, then income.** The arithmetic `SalaryIncomeNode` does today (gross
  less exemptions, retirement and s.19 deductions) moves unchanged to a new node **`salary.incomebeforetravel`**.
  A new **`salary.medicaltravel`** reads it as "gross total income, as computed before including therein the said
  expenditure" (the only head built) and values `PERQ-MEDABROAD-STAY` / `-TRAVEL`. **`salary.income`** becomes
  their sum, the travel and the stay excess on lines of their own. Pipeline: `… salary.deductions,
  salary.incomebeforetravel, salary.medicaltravel, salary.income, totalincome …`. When another head lands, the
  test must read the gross total income of every head, not the salary head alone — a note on the node, in DR 06,
  and in `whatToCheck`.
  - The treatment-and-stay excess does not depend on income, so it could sit in `PerquisiteNode`; it stays with the
    travel so one claim is valued in one place.
  - End-to-end check becomes: **income under "Salaries" rises** by the taxable amount, both regimes.
- **(b)** The taxpayer states the gross total income before the travel (the old C2b). No new node, an entered
  figure the engine can compute.
- **(c)** A node after `salary.income` read only by the future `totalincome` (the approved C2a as written): inert
  today. Refused.

Nothing is coded. **W-071b is back to EXPLAINED until you approve Revision 1.**

## As built — departures from the plan (for review)
- **The rows value the exclusion, not the perquisite**, as the exemption rows do. `PERQ-MEDABROAD-STAY` is `LeastOf`
  (the spending; the Reserve Bank's permitted amount); `PERQ-MEDABROAD-TRAVEL` is 100% of the travel under an
  `IncomeAtMost` condition on `salary.incomebeforetravel` at ₹8,00,000 (inclusive, "does not exceed"). The node
  takes the exclusion off the spending. Same figures as the plan's.
- **The condition reads a node value the context does not usually carry:** `ctx.conditions()` holds person facts
  only, so the node builds the `ConditionContext` with `salary.incomebeforetravel` added. First use of `IncomeAtMost`
  by a node.
- **`MedicalTreatmentAbroad.attendants`** carries the count, so a second attendant can be refused (C3).
- **The medical perquisite reaches income under "Salaries", not gross salary.** Revision 1 adds it at
  `salary.income`, so `salary.gross` (the form's gross-salary row) leaves it out. **Reviewer: confirm, or name the
  item that moves it into gross salary** once a later step can sit between the two without a cycle.
- **`SalaryIncomeNode` became `SalaryIncomeBeforeTravelNode`** (same arithmetic and refusal, new node id); its tests
  moved with it (`SalaryIncomeBeforeTravelNodeTest`). The new `SalaryIncomeNode` is the sum.
- **TY 2025-26 text:** 1961 proviso (vi) covers what the employer spends; clause (vii) covers what it reimburses
  "subject to the conditions specified in or under that clause" (thc.nic.in pp.144–145) — both cited. `-STAY`'s
  `introducedBy` is FA 2025 s.9, the last amendment of proviso (vi), with its citation saying it changed only
  condition (B). Both TY 2025-26 versions `UNVERIFIED`, `whatToCheck` naming the three unread Acts.
- **Locks:** law 2025-26.law.20 / 2026-27.law.27, exec 2025-26.exec.16 / 2026-27.exec.17; content unchanged (the
  compiler fixes it).
