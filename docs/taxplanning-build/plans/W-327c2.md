## Plan for W-327c2 — a plain "why" for other sources

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** every other-sources line publishes its "why" as a reason with
facts, and the content words it. The lines are written through `OtherSourcesLines.add` and `nil`, which take a reason beside
their audit text, and each of the 16 callers passes one.

**Probe (no code run):**
- **No test compares whole lines.** No test compares whole trace lines (searched for W-327b2).
- **Every caller read.** I read `OtherSourcesLines` and all 16 callers, and grouped the log's 549 lines by text shape. Most are "item N: KIND" (75 + 55 + 31 + 23 + 23 + … lines), and the line's `what` already names the income.
- **One signature change.** `add(rule, kind, in, out, why)` becomes `add(…, why, reason)` (6 parameters, within the limit of 7), and `nil(rule, amount, why)` becomes `nil(…, why, reason)`. `deduction(…)` keeps its signature and chooses its own reason (abroad-untaxed, or this item's). **Choices for the owner:**
- **C1: the "item N: KIND" lines** (most of the 549):
  - **C1a (recommended):** one short confirmation, `OS_FOR_THIS_ITEM`, since the line's `what` already names the income and its figure.
  - C1b: a word for each income kind (16 kinds) in a content `terms.otherSourceKind` map. That repeats what each row's `what` already says.

**Draft wording (Claude's, for your adoption):**
- `OS_FOR_THIS_ITEM`: "This line is for one item you entered."
- `OS_EXPENSE_NOT_DEDUCTED`: "Nothing is deducted: this expense is not allowed against this kind of income."
- `OS_UNTAXED_ABROAD`: "Nothing is deducted: it is payable outside India without tax deducted."
- `BUSINESS_TRUST_SPECIFIED_SUM`: "It is the {distributed} distributed, less the {issued} issue price and the {earlier} charged in earlier years, never below nil."
- `PF_CONTRIBUTION_ABOVE_THRESHOLD`: "Your contribution above the threshold goes into a taxable contribution account."
- `PF_TAXABLE_ACCOUNT_INTEREST`: "It is the interest earned in that taxable contribution account."
- `GIFT_EXCLUDED`: "This gift is excluded, so it is not taxed."
- `GIFT_COVID_DEATH_LIMIT`: "Sums received on a death related to COVID-19 are excluded up to the limit."
- `GIFT_ON_ITS_OWN`: "This gift is measured on its own against the threshold the law sets."
- `GIFTS_FOR_THE_YEAR`: "It is the year's total of these gifts, measured against the threshold the law sets."
- `LIFE_POLICY_EXCLUDED`: "The sums received under this policy are excluded from income."
- `LIFE_POLICY_ABOVE_PREMIUMS`: "Only the sum above the {premiums} of premium not already deducted or counted is income."
- `DIVIDEND_INTEREST_LIMITED`: "Interest expense is deducted against your dividend and unit income of {income}, up to {limit}."
