## Plan for W-090 — ITR forms as data: the split

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** each tax year's return forms, and who must use which, held as
versioned rule data, so W-091 can choose the form from a computed return. This plan sizes the item and proposes a split,
as W-238 and W-240 were split (D-488, D-507). Each part then gets its own plan.

**Findings (each read this session):**
- F1 — The law, TY 2026-27: Income-tax Rules, 2026 r.164 (Gazette 271092, pp.1662 – 1663, read from the Gazette copy).
  - (2) ITR-1 is for a resident (not RNOR) individual whose income is under salaries or family pension, house property
    (at most two, no loss), other sources (no lottery or race-horse income, no loss), or long-term capital gains under
    s.198 up to ₹1,25,000 with no loss.
  - (3) ITR-1 is barred by any of (a) – (n): assets or signing authority outside India, income from outside India, s.10
    apportionment, a s.93 deduction other than 93(1)(d), director in a company, unlisted equity held, TDS assessable in
    another's hands, s.159 / s.160 relief, agricultural income over ₹5,000, total income over ₹50 lakh, s.393(3) TDS,
    s.391(2) / 392(3) deferment, and Part A of Chapter XIII income.
  - (4) ITR-2 is for an individual "not being an individual to whom sub-rule (2) applies", or an HUF, with no business
    income.
  - (5) – (6) ITR-4 is for presumptive business income under s.58, with its own bars (a) – (o).
  - (7) ITR-3 is for business income otherwise. (8) – (10) ITR-5, 6 and 7 are for firms, companies and trusts.
  - (12) the manner of furnishing is in a Table I have not yet read; nor have I read r.165 (ITR-UN).
- F2 — The engine's person types are `INDIVIDUAL` and `HUF` only (`taxengine/rules/condition/PersonType.java:18` – `19`).
  ITR-5 to ITR-7 serve none of them.
- F3 — Most ITR-1 bars read facts the engine does not hold. `Condition` has `HasIncomeUnderHead`, `IncomeAtLeast`,
  `IncomeAtMost`, `IsResident`, `AgeAtLeast` and the rest of `rules/condition/`, but nothing for assets or signing
  authority abroad, foreign-source income, director, unlisted equity, agricultural income, s.159 / 160 relief, TDS in
  another's hands, ESOP deferment or Chapter XIII Part A. The design's own sketch (`DR 21` I3) names eleven condition
  types; only `HasIncomeUnderHead` and `IncomeAtLeast` exist. Each missing fact is an input the person answers, so each
  is an `EnteredFacts` / `Assessee` field plus a draft binding.
- F4 — What already exists: `ItrFormCode` (`rules/id/ItrFormCode.java`), with no caller in main code (grep; only `IdentifierTest:126` – `138` uses it). `law/` has `rules`,
  `regimes`, `disallowances` and condition sets (W-244), versioned per year; there is no `forms/`.
- F5 — TY 2025-26 forms are under the 1962 Rules r.12 as amended for that year (r.164(14) keeps them live). I have not
  located its Gazette copy.
- F6 — Schedules (`ItrSchedule`, `ScheduleField`, `FieldSource`) are W-092's (`02-TECH-SPEC.md:80`), so `ItrFormSpec`
  carries no `schedules` field until W-092 needs one (no field without a caller).
- F7 — I19 ("at least one form per (PersonType, ReturnKind)") and I20 ("no two forms simultaneously eligible") are checks
  at pack load (`02-TECH-SPEC.md:239` – `240`). I19 cannot be switched on until forms ship.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1a (recommended) — split W-090 into five, in this order** (each before the one deploy):
  - **W-090a** — the facts the bars read. Each one is an input the person answers, as a fact rather than a label:
    assets outside India, signing authority outside India, income from a source outside India, director in a company,
    unlisted equity held in the year, agricultural income (amount), relief under s.159 / s.160, TDS assessable in
    another's hands, s.10 apportionment, s.391(2) / 392(3) deferment, Part A Chapter XIII income. Each is an
    `EnteredFacts` field, a draft binding and a `Condition` type that reads it. This is the largest part, and I will
    likely split it again when I plan it.
  - **W-090b** — the model: `ItrFormSpec` (code, display name, cascade order, applies-to, return kinds, positive
    conditions, bars, excludes, furnishing, validity, provenance), `ReturnKind`, `FurnishingOption`; read from
    `law/itr-forms/<CODE>.json` with per-year versions, compiled into the year's pack; proven on a synthetic pack.
  - **W-090c** — TY 2026-27's forms from r.164: ITR-1, ITR-2, ITR-3 and ITR-4 for `INDIVIDUAL` and `HUF`, with the cascade
    and bars as data, the r.164(12) furnishing table (to be read first), and I19 and I20 switched on.
  - **W-090d** — TY 2025-26's forms from the 1962 Rules r.12 as amended for that year; the Gazette copy is located first.
  - **W-090e** — ITR-UN (r.165, updated return): parked until updated returns are computed (the engine computes an
    original or belated return only); it comes back then, or is waived at the deploy gate.
- C1b — build W-090 as one item: dozens of files across `taxengine.input`, `taxplanning`, `rules` and two years of law.
- **C2a** — forms are one file per form under `law/itr-forms/`, versioned like the regimes, rather than the row's single
  `forms/itr-forms.json` (which predates the timeline packs). I add an erratum for the row's file name.
- **C3a** — only `INDIVIDUAL` and `HUF` forms (F2); ITR-5 to ITR-7 arrive when a person type that files them does.
- **C4a** — ITR-V is a manner of furnishing in r.164(12) (verification after an electronic upload), not a form to select,
  so it is a `FurnishingOption` value, not an `ItrFormSpec`. `DR 21`'s "nine forms" gets an erratum.

  person type, but a person with business income is refused elsewhere first.
