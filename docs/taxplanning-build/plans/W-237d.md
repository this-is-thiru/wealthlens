## Plan for W-237d — account-wide erasure

**For:** a user can ask for their whole account to be erased, and once r.8(3)'s year has passed WealthLens erases
everything it holds about them in every module, not only the tax profiles W-235 reaches (Act s.8(7)(a), s.12(3);
Rules r.8(3); D-470 C2a).

### What I read (session 52)

- **Law:** `sources/2026-11-07-data-protection.md` rows 6 (s.8(7): erase *"unless retention is necessary for compliance
  with any law"*), 8 (s.12(3): erase on request, same exception), 12 (r.8(3): keep data and logs *"a minimum period of one
  year from the date of such processing"*, then erase; Case 1: kept *"even if X deletes her account"*). Nothing new read:
  no new figure enters this item.
- **W-235's pattern:** `taxplanning/erasure/ErasureService.request` holds a profile (`erasure_requested_at`); a held
  profile reads as absent; `jobs/RetentionPurge.eraseHeldProfiles` hands each profile past `app.tax.retention.erasure-hold`
  (P1Y, `application.yaml:77` and both profile yamls) to `jobs/ProfileRemoval`.
- **Per-user collections outside W-235's reach** (each keyed by `email`): `user_details` (`auth/entity/UserDetail`, email
  is the id), `assets`, `transactions`, `trade_outcomes`, `profit_and_loss` (`portfolio/entity/*`), `user_charges`,
  `charge_accounts` (`brokercharges/entity/*`), `lastly_performed_corporate_action` (`corporate`), `insurances` (with
  `agent_email`, a third party's data held in the user's record), `salary_profiles`, `tax_computations` (the older
  `taxplanning/salary` flow, keyed by email, not by profile — W-235 does not reach them), `privacy_grievances`,
  `privacy_breach_intimations`, and the user's email inside `privacy_breach_notices.affected`.
- **Not personal:** `corporate_action`, the charge catalogue, schedules, instruments, the tax policy collections, the
  Gazette collections. **`finance` stores nothing** (no `@Document` in the module): the row's "finance" has nothing to erase.
- **What blocks a simple build:** (1) the only scheduler is `taxplanning/jobs/TaxJobSchedule` (leases, dead letter);
  (2) `shared` may depend only on `portfolio`, so `shared/privacy` cannot call `brokercharges`, `insurance`,
  `corporate`, `auth` or `taxplanning`; (3) `AuthService.validateToken` checks expiry only — a token stays good for up to
  30 minutes whatever happens to the account; (4) `AmcChargeService` bills every due `charge_account` with no user in the
  request (`AmcChargeService.java:73`), so billing would process a held account.

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — how the modules take part.** (a) **One seam, as W-237c's `RegisteredUsers`:** `shared/privacy` declares
`PersonalDataHolder` (`hold(email, at)`, `erase(email)`), and each module that stores a person's data implements it in its
own package — portfolio, brokercharges, corporate, insurance, taxplanning, auth, and `shared/privacy` itself. The account
erasure service is given every implementation; the module direction is untouched (each depends on `shared` already).
(b) Spring Modulith events (`AccountErasureDue`) with a listener per module: no event publication registry is configured
(only `spring-modulith-starter-core`), so a failed listener is lost, not retried — not safe for an erasure.

**C2 — where the request and its clock live.** (a) **`privacy_erasure_requests` in `shared/privacy`: email, requested at
(UTC), erased at (absent until done) — facts.** `POST /privacy/erasure/user/{email}` (202, a receipt with the hold end and
the contact, under W-237a's r.9 guard and listed in `GET /privacy/rights`); `GET` the same path reads the receipt.
(b) A field on `UserDetail`: but the login is itself erased at the end, taking the record that the erasure was done.

**C3 — what "held" stops, at once.** (a) **Everything the person could start, and every job that would touch them:**
login refused; `AuthFilter` refuses a held account's token (one read per request, by id); registering the same email
refused until erased; AMC billing skips held accounts' charge accounts; each of the person's tax profiles is held through
W-235's `holdForErasure`, so the tax side behaves exactly as W-235 built. A breach notice to "every registered user" still
includes a held account: its data is still stored and can still be breached. (b) As (a) without the per-request token
check: a held account keeps working for up to 30 minutes.

**C4 — the year, and who runs the erasure.** (a) **One key, `app.privacy.erasure-hold` (P1Y, r.8(3)), read by both W-235
and this item; `app.tax.retention.erasure-hold` removed from the three yamls.** A new `TaxJob` `account-erasure` on its own
cron (`app.tax.jobs.account-erasure-cron`, daily like `retention-purge`) calls the privacy service: it reuses leases and the
dead letter; the job name says what runs it, the work lives in `shared/privacy`. Order: every holder except auth, then auth
last, then `erased_at` set — a run cut off part-way leaves the request open and the next run finishes it (each `erase` is a
delete by email: a second run changes nothing). (b) Two keys with the same value.

**C5 — a law that requires keeping.** (a) **Nothing is kept**, as W-235 decided for the tax side
(`sources/…` "not settled": the record-keeping duties of the Income-tax Act and the market rules fall on the user and the
broker, not on a tracker). Your word is needed here: is there a law you know of that binds WealthLens to keep a trade or
P&L record? (b) Keep a named class, with the law cited — only with a cited provision.

**C6 — "once the purpose is no longer served".** (a) **Request only, now; purpose-lapse erasure placed as a row (W-249),
waiting for your rule for when an account's purpose lapses** (an inactivity period is a number I cannot source; r.8(1)'s
Third Schedule does not cover WealthLens, row 11). (b) You give the period now and it is built here as data.

**C7 — the breach records.** (a) **Erase the person's intimations and take the email out of each notice's `affected` list;
the notice itself (text, Board steps) stays** — it is the owner's record to the Board, about the breach, not the person.

### Roles
- **Architect:** one seam, each module erasing its own data; the request outlives the login it erases.
- **Product:** one request erases everything; the receipt says when and whom to ask.
- **Senior engineer:** idempotent deletes, auth last, `Clock`, one key for the year.
- **Automation tester:** the guard over every per-user collection; dates by hand; three probes.
- **Manual tester:** on staging, a test account with a trade, insurance and a tax profile: request, try to log in, run
  the job with the clock moved, check each collection with a read-only query.

**Risks:** (1) The guard relies on the `email` field name; a collection keyed otherwise (e.g. `agent_email` only) would
slip it — I list the exceptions in the test with a reason each. (2) Holding stops AMC billing for the year; the broker's
own charge still accrues outside the app. (3) The per-request read in `AuthFilter` adds one lookup per call.
**Size:** large: ~8 shared classes, 6 holders, 3 changed services, 1 job, config, 1 integration class + guard. If you
prefer, C3's auth changes can split off.
