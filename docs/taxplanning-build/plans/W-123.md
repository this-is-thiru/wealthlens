**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** give a person a document of one computation they can keep
or hand to someone — every figure with the rule behind it and how far that rule is verified, nothing in it that the
computation did not produce (F10) — and make it the copy a person takes before their data is erased (D-477).

**Probe (2026-11-15; the design, the stored computation, the jobs and the legacy generator read, no code run):**
- **What the design asks.** F10 (`04-product-spec.md:59`): rendered from the trace, every figure with rule ids and
  status, `A4.3`'s disclaimer, no unreachable section (O-20). G7.3 (`06-algorithms.md:649`): grouped by `StepKind`.
  `DR 19` E8 (`19-engineering-foundations.md:498`): a `GeneratePdf` job "on `ComputationCompleted`", idempotent on
  the computation id, "cached by receipt".

  report producible for every profile until an erasure request holds it, refused once held; the erasure request's
  api-collection entry and `ErasureController`'s Javadoc point to it first (`api-collection/Tax Profile/Request
  Erasure of a Profile.yml:26` already says so).
- **What exists.**
  - A stored computation (`taxplanning/compute/DraftComputation.java:24`): `receipt`, `draft_id`, `revision`,
    `result_json`, `law_and_exec`. Its `ComputedTax` trace steps carry `in`, `out` and `explanation` — "the line in

  - **A job that runs on an event.** `TaxJob` (`taxplanning/jobs/TaxJob.java`) is `name`, `timeout`, `run`; every job
    built is scheduled and leased to one instance. E8's "on …" trigger was never built.
  - **A place to keep a PDF.** A cached file would be a new store of a person's whole tax position: to encrypt,
    retain, purge with its draft and erase with the profile.
- **The disclaimer as designed names the law in fixed words** (`03-advisory.md:361-367`: "the Income-tax Act 1961 and
  the Finance Act 2026 for tax year 2025-26 …"). The year's instruments are data in each lock
  (`rulepacks/2026-27.lock.json`, `instruments`, with `citation`).

    - **W-123b, the copy before erasure:** the report for every computation of a profile until an erasure request
