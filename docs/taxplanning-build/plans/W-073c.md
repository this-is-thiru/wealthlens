## Plan for W-073c — the recognised provident fund's own charges: contributions over 12% and interest over the notified rate

**What this item is for (one sentence):** add to salary the parts of the annual accretion in a recognised provident
fund that the Act deems received — the employer's contribution beyond 12% of salary (TY 2025-26 only) and interest
credited at a rate above the notified 9.5% (both years).

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6), and one finding changes the design: the 12% limb no longer exists for
TY 2026-27. Nothing is coded until you choose.**

### Findings

1. **The 12% limb is TY 2025-26 only.** FA 2026 removed it from para 6 on the day the 2025 Act commenced, so it was
   never in force under the 2025 Act.
2. **The design and a golden are now wrong for TY 2026-27.** `DR 01` `PF-RPF12-001` and `SAL-DEF-RPF-001` state the
   12% for TY 2026-27; `DR 08` T4-3 expects ₹30,000 in both years (`DR 08`'s year-by-regime table, line 897, runs
   T4-3 in 2025-26 OLD and 2026-27 OLD). For TY 2026-27 the law gives **0**. W-074b runs T4-3; it would fail on R3 unless the
   expectation is corrected by an erratum.
3. **The 12% is per employment.** Each employer's contribution to its fund against that employer's salary: r.6 speaks
   of "the employer" and "the salary of the employee" in one fund. Pooling across employments gives a different answer
   (test below).
4. **Both limbs are one threshold shape:** an amount *in excess of* a percentage of a base —
   `AmountInExcess(CONTRIBUTION, PercentOfBase(12, SALARY))` and `AmountInExcess(INTEREST, PercentOfBase(9.5,
   BALANCE))`, where BALANCE is what the interest was allowed on (interest credited ÷ the rate allowed). The 12 and the
   9.5 stay in the pack, not in the node.
5. **The design says the 12% charge stacks on the aggregate** (`DR 01` R13: *"Separately and additionally"*; H-6: *"a
   third, independent charge"*). In TY 2025-26, when one employer's RPF contribution is both above 12% and pushes the
   aggregate above ₹7,50,000, the same rupees are charged twice. The Act's words do not exclude it and I found no
   provision that does; I follow the design (risk 1).

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — rows** (rule ids within the 12-character segment limit):
  - `PERQ-RPF-CONTRIBUTION` — **one version, TY 2025-26** (`Between 2025-04-01 … 2026-03-31`), `AllRegimes`,
    provision1961 *Sch. IV Pt A r.6(a)* PRESENT, provision2025 **`ABSENT_IN_THIS_ACT`** (para 6 as substituted by FA
    2026 s.127), cap `AmountInExcess(CONTRIBUTION, PercentOfBase(12, SALARY))`. Not in the 2026-27 pack.
  - `PERQ-RPF-INTEREST` — **both years**, `AllRegimes`, cap `AmountInExcess(INTEREST, PercentOfBase(9.5, BALANCE))`;
    TY 2026-27 provision2025 *Sch. XI Pt A para 6 as substituted by the Finance Act, 2026 s.127(a)(iii)*.
  - `SAL-SALARY-FOR-RPF` — the base (`SalaryBase`, `WHERE_TERMS_PROVIDE`), **TY 2025-26 only** like
    `SAL-SALARY-FOR-ENTMT`; `BASIC` counts outright, `DEARNESS_ALLOWANCE` only where the terms provide (as for the
    pension base).
  - **Alternative:** one row `PERQ-RPF-CHARGES` with both limbs — rejected: the limbs have different years, bases and
    sources, and one row cannot have a version that holds only one limb.
- **C2 — input for the interest limb:** a new `RecognisedFundInterest(int employment, Money interestCredited, Rate
  rateAllowed)`, a list on `EnteredFacts` (empty where none) — the figures on the fund's annual statement. The
  contribution limb reuses `EmployerFundContribution` (fund `RECOGNISED_PROVIDENT_FUND`); nothing new.
- **C3 — node:** `EmployerContributionNode` adds both, each on its own trace line (one per employment with a figure),
  and now **requires `salary.base`** for TY 2025-26's 12%. An RPF contribution where the employment's salary in the base
  is nil is charged in full (12% of nil is nil — a fact, not a missing figure). Interest credited with a rate allowed
  of nil is **refused** (BALANCE undefined). Where the year's pack has no `PERQ-RPF-CONTRIBUTION`, the contribution limb
  is not computed.
- **C4 — rounding:** BALANCE = interest ÷ rate is rounded to paise, then 9.5% of it rounded once more — two roundings,
  at most a paisa apart from the exact figure. **Alternative:** a dedicated shape for "interest at a rate above a
  notified rate" that does one division — more code for a paisa; not recommended.
- **C5 — verification:** `PERQ-RPF-CONTRIBUTION` and `SAL-SALARY-FOR-RPF` `UNVERIFIED` — the thc.nic.in copy is not a
  Gazette text and FA 1997 s.58 ("twelve") is unread in the Gazette; the 2019–2025 amending Acts searched (above) and
  named as checked. `PERQ-RPF-INTEREST` `UNVERIFIED` both years — the 9.5% rests on a third-party mirror (Y1); TY
  2026-27's para 6 itself is SOURCE_CHECKED in `introducedBy` (Gazette 271439 p.71).
- **C6 — errata (R7, additions only):** below `DR 01` `PF-RPF12-001` and `SAL-DEF-RPF-001`, and below `DR 08` T4-3:
  *the 12% limb was removed from Sch. XI Pt A para 6 by the Finance Act, 2026 s.127(a)(iii) from 1-4-2026 — T4-3's
  ₹30,000 holds for TY 2025-26; TY 2026-27 is **0***. Verified by Gazette 271439 pp.70–71. An addendum under R13 for
  the build, as W-073a/b did. T4-3 itself is still W-074b's.
