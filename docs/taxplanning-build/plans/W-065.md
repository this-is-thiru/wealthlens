## Plan for W-065 — income under "Salaries"

**What this item is for (one sentence):** `salary.income` becomes the head's income: gross salary less the
deductions from salary (and, once they exist, less the exemptions and the retirement deductions). This is
the figure the return reports as income chargeable under "Salaries".

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C5) and one backlog gap before anything is built.**

### Findings

1. **Two of the four inputs don't exist.**
   - `salary.exemptions` arrives with `W-067` (HRA), which creates `ExemptionNode`.
   - `salary.retirement` (`RetirementNode`) belongs to **no backlog item**. The tech spec says only "Phase 7",
     and the Phase 7 table has none. No item owns `RET-*` (gratuity, leave encashment, commutation, VRS,
     retrenchment) or 2025 s.18 either.
   - My W-062/W-063 docs said "the retirement items build them". No such item exists, and I should have
     checked.
2. **`MoneyValue` or `HeadResult`.**
   - `HeadResult` is `DR 15`'s value for a head that set-off reads (house property, other sources and capital
     gains in Part D all have it).
   - Salary has no loss to carry and no rate hint, and nothing reads `salary.income` until set-off (Phase 9).
3. **No rule states "income under Salaries"**; `DR 06` gives it "—". Its parts are all already traced: gross is
   INCOME, and the two deductions are DEDUCTION lines.
4. **s.15 is the charge, and it constrains what is entered, not what is computed.**
   - Due or paid in the year, arrears, a former employer, advance salary taxed once, a partner's remuneration
     not salary: these decide what goes into `SalaryComponent.annualAmount` ("the amount for the whole tax year
     under this employment", D-090).
   - The engine has nothing to compute from them until arrears relief (`REL-ARREARS-001`, "Later") or an
     advance-salary ledger exists.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — inputs now.**
  - **(a) recommended:** `salary.income` requires `salary.gross` and `salary.deductions` only.
  - `W-067` adds `salary.exemptions` and the retirement item adds `salary.retirement`, each in its own item
    (the D-079 pattern). The tech spec row is annotated to say so.
- **C2 — value type.**
  - **(a) recommended:** `MoneyValue`, as T6 says. `HeadResult` arrives with the first head set-off needs
    (house property, Phase 9). Appendix E is corrected.
  - **(b)** `HeadResult` now, with no reader until Phase 9.
- **C3 — explanation.**
  - **(a) recommended: untraced, as `NetPayableNode` is.** It is gross less the deductions, every part already
    has its line, and no rule states the difference.
  - **(b)** A row `SAL-INCOME-UNDER-SALARIES` (s.15 with s.19(1)), with a new `RuleDefinition` member for "a
    head's income", and one INCOME line. That is a new shape to explain a subtraction.
- **C4 — coverage for s.15.**
  - **(a) recommended:** 2025 s.15 and 1961 s.15 stay `PLANNED`, reassigned to the item that models arrears or
    advance salary (`REL-ARREARS-001`). `SalaryComponent`'s Javadoc states the s.15 contract for what an entry
    means (due or paid in the year, former employer included, partner's remuneration excluded), since that is
    where s.15 bites today.
  - **(b)** Mark s.15 `MODELLED` on `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES`. That overstates it: nothing checks due/paid/arrears.
- **C5 — negative income.** Professional tax is deducted in full, so a tiny salary with a larger tax paid would
  go below zero.
  - **(a) recommended:** refuse it. Throw, naming the figures. The Act gives no loss under "Salaries", and
    silently flooring at zero would hide a data error.
  - **(b)** Floor at zero with a notice.
