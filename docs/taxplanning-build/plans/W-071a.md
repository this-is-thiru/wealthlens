## Plan for W-071a — the perquisite machinery and residential accommodation

**What this item is for (one sentence):** value residential accommodation an employer provides (government
licence, owned, leased, hotel, furnished), and add it to gross salary through a new perquisite node, for both tax
years.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C7). One of them (C5) needs sourcing I don't have: the Cost Inflation Index.**

### What I read

- **The `W-071a` row** (D-110): **creates** `PerquisiteNode` (every output `NON_CASH`), perquisite components counting
  in gross salary, the facts shape; accommodation per S4 r.15(2) Table I and sub-rules and r.15(8)'s "salary"; Rules

  - **(f)** Deputation.

### Findings

1. **The value depends on a new salary measure.** It is broader than the HRA base: allowances, bonus and commission
   count, but *exempt* allowances don't. So it depends on the exemptions, and PerquisiteNode must run **after**
   `salary.exemptions` and before `salary.gross`.
2. **The DA test differs from HRA's.** HRA counts DA "if the terms of employment so provide"; this counts it only if
   it "enters into the computation of superannuation or retirement benefits". It's a different fact about the
   employment.
3. **The population bands are the 2011 census's.** The Rules name no cities, so the taxpayer states the band (as
   OQ-7 did for metro cities).
4. **The CII cap needs index values that haven't been read** (the notifications under s.48 Explanation (v) for
   2023-24 onward, and under 2025 s.72(8)(a) for TY 2026-27). Without them the cap can't be applied, and ignoring
   it would overstate the perquisite.
5. **TY 2025-26's cap covers only owned accommodation**, by the 2023 text. TY 2026-27's covers owned or leased.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — input:** `EnteredFacts.accommodations`, each an **`EmployerAccommodation`**:
  - the employment it comes with;
  - the provider (`GOVERNMENT`, `EMPLOYER_OWNED`, `EMPLOYER_LEASED`, `HOTEL`);
  - the period occupied (`DateRange`);
  - the city's 2011-census band (`ABOVE_FORTY_LAKH`, `FIFTEEN_TO_FORTY_LAKH`, `OTHER_AREA`), for owned only;
  - the employer's cost for the period (the licence fee, lease rental or hotel charges);
  - the rent the employee paid;
  - optionally furniture (its cost or the hire charges, and the employee's charges);
  - the first tax year it was provided.

  Site accommodation, transfer retention (the 90 days) and deputation are **refused** with the reason, as not
  built.
- **C2 — the salary measure:** a new base row `SAL-SALARY-FOR-ACCOMMODATION` (both years).
  - It counts, from registry inclusions: `BASIC`, `BONUS`, `COMMISSION`, `SPECIAL_ALLOWANCE`, `HRA`, `LTA`,
    `CONVEYANCE_ALLOWANCE`, `UNIFORM_ALLOWANCE`, `CHILDREN_EDUCATION_ALLOWANCE`, `HOSTEL_ALLOWANCE`,
    `TRANSPORT_ALLOWANCE_DISABLED` and `ENTERTAINMENT_ALLOWANCE`.
  - It counts DA only where a new `Employment.dearnessAllowanceCountsForRetirement` says so.
  - It leaves out the lump sums, and subtracts the exemptions the `salary.exemptions` lines allowed against those
    components.
  - It is apportioned to the period occupied by days, as HRA is (OQ-8 applies alike).
- **C3 — node:** `PerquisiteNode` produces `salary.perquisites` (NON_CASH), after `salary.exemptions`.
  `GrossSalaryNode` adds it. One `PERQUISITE` line per accommodation, naming the limb.
- **C4 — rows**, all `AllRegimes`, both years:
  - `PERQ-ACCOMMODATION-GOVERNMENT`: the licence fee less rent.
  - `PERQ-ACCOMMODATION-OWNED`: `PercentOfBaseByLocation` 10 / 7.5 / 5 of the salary by band, less rent.
  - `PERQ-ACCOMMODATION-LEASED`: `LeastOf` lease rental and 10% of salary, less rent.
  - `PERQ-ACCOMMODATION-HOTEL`: `LeastOf` charges and 24% of salary, less rent.
  - `PERQ-ACCOMMODATION-FURNITURE`: 10% a year of the cost, or the hire charges, less charges, apportioned to the
    period.
  - The two-year difference in which accommodations the cap covers (finding 5) is in the data.
- **C5 — the CII cap:**
  - **(a) recommended:** source the CII notifications (for 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26 and TY 2026-27) and apply the
    cap from rows. Until they are read, an accommodation **first provided before this tax year is refused**, naming
    the missing index. A first year needs no cap.
  - **(b)** The employer states the capped amount.
  - **(c)** Ignore the cap: refused. It would overstate the perquisite.
- **C6 — verification:**
  - **TY 2026-27:** `SOURCE_CHECKED`.
  - **TY 2025-26:** `UNVERIFIED`. The Table is read in the Gazette, but its later amendments (2023–2025) and the
    Explanation's "salary" since 2011 aren't.
- **C7 — pipeline:** every regime's pipeline gains `salary.perquisites` between `salary.exemptions` and
  `salary.gross`. DR 06 G1.1 lists perquisites at step 3; the order of computation must follow the salary measure's
  dependency, and G1.1's form-row order is unaffected.

## As built — departures from the plan (for review)
- **Rule ids use the rulebook's `ACCOM`**: `SAL-SALARY-FOR-ACCOM` and `PERQ-ACCOM-GOVERNMENT`, `-OWNED`, `-LEASED`, `-HOTEL`, `-FURNITURE` — a `RuleId` segment is at most twelve characters and "ACCOMMODATION" has thirteen (as `ENTMT`, D-105).
- **The "other areas" band is `ANY_OTHER_PLACE`**, the name every location table's otherwise-place already has (HRA, D-099), not `OTHER_AREA` — one word per concept; its comment quotes "other areas".
- **`SalaryBase` now states its dearness-allowance test** (`WHERE_TERMS_PROVIDE` / `WHERE_IT_COUNTS_FOR_RETIREMENT`), required in the JSON: the five existing salary-base rows gained `"WHERE_TERMS_PROVIDE"`, so their checksums moved; no figure changed.
- **The exempt allowances are taken out in `PerquisiteNode`**, as the exemptions' total, not in `SalaryBaseNode`: the exemptions read the HRA base, so measuring this base after them in `SalaryBaseNode` would be a cycle. Every exemption built is of an allowance the base counts, so the total is exact today; a test holds the subtraction.
- **"As reduced by the rent" is applied by the node** for every Table I row, and the furniture's hire charges and the employee's charges likewise: a cap shape subtracts a shape from a base, not an entered amount from a limb.
- **Continued accommodation refused for owned and leased in both years.** TY 2025-26's cap covers owned only, so a continued *leased* TY 2025-26 home is refused although no cap would apply — safe, and lifted with the index row.
- **The non-cash nature is stated, not typed**: `PerquisiteNode`'s contract says its value is never take-home; no value type carries a cash flag yet.
- **`PackJsonWellFormedTest` checked any cap's top-level `bands` as slab bands**; the owned row is the first location cap at the top level, so the check now reads slab ladders only. The test's intent is unchanged.
- **The pipeline order** is base → exemptions → perquisites → gross in both regimes.
