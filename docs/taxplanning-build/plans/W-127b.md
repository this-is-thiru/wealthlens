and where exactly one cell of such a sum is unknown, work that cell out from the others (`DR 06` G5.1 L3), never

### Choices for the owner (recommended first)

- **C1 — where the sums live:**
  - **(a) a list of identities on the certificate** (`CertificateSpec.identities`, `identities` in the JSON), each a
    target cell, signed terms (+1 or −1) and its Gazette page; a cell is a line's path and, for a multi-column line, its
    column heading. The validator refuses a cell naming no declared `AMOUNT` line, a column the line does not print, a
    multi-column line with no column, a sign other than ±1, a cell twice in one identity, and a target among its own terms.
    W-128 checks the same list.
  - (b) on each target line as a `sum` — one more component on `CertificateField`, which is at the 7-component limit.
- **C2 — which cells L3 may fill:**
  - **(a) only a cell with nothing printed in it, or a line not found;** a cell printing something L2 could not read keeps
    its text and stays unread — inferring over printed text would hide what the certificate says.
  - (b) also a cell L2 could not read.
- **C3 — saying what was inferred:**
  - **(a) a note per inferred cell** — "Inferred partC.annexureI.row3 from the sum printed at row 3" — beside the field's
    0.80 confidence and empty text; the reading's outcome is unchanged (a line not found stays `PARTIAL`).
  - (b) no note: the confidence and the empty text say it.

### Files I will create (C1a, C2a, C3a)

- `taxengine/rules/filing/CertificateIdentity.java` — record: `target` (`CertificateCell`), `terms` (`List<Term>`),
  `page`; `CertificateCell` (path, optional column) and `Term` (cell, sign) as its own small records.
- `taxplanning/documents/parser/Inference.java` — L3: the fields' read amounts by cell path; repeatedly, any identity
  with exactly one fillable cell unknown is solved for it (target = Σ sign × term; a term = sign × (target − Σ the
  others)) until nothing more can be filled; a contradiction is left for W-128.
- Tests: `CertificateJsonTest` gains the identity refusals; `Form130LayoutTest` the ten sums typed from the Gazette with
  their pages; `InferenceTest` (a target filled, a term filled with each sign, a chain where one fill enables the next,
  two unknowns left alone, an unread cell left alone, a column cell); `Form130ParserTest` one end-to-end case.
