### Findings

1. **The row names the wrong rows.** In the rulebook, `RATE-SLAB-AGE-001` and `-002` are **statements
   of law, not ladders**:
   - AGE-001: the higher exemption is for a **resident**, and age is tested *"at any time during the
     tax year"*;
   - AGE-002: the new regime has **no age variants**.

   The age **ladders** are R3's `RATE-SLAB-OLD-2025-B/-C`, renamed by `W-045d` to
   **`RATE-SLAB-FIRST-SCHEDULE-AGE-60TO79`** and **`-AGE-80`**. So the rows to author are those two,
   with AGE-001 as their conditions and AGE-002 as a test.
2. **Choosing a ladder by age is new design.** A regime names **one** `rateTable`. The age ladders are
   separate rules whose versions carry `conditions` (resident; age ≥ 60, < 80; age ≥ 80). Nothing yet
   decides how a node chooses among them.
3. **Nothing turns `EngineInputs` into a `ConditionContext`.** `AgeAtLeast`'s javadoc says the "at any
   time during the tax year" test *"is resolved before it reaches here, by whoever fills the
   context"*. Nobody fills it yet. Doing it means:
   - a date of birth becomes the age reached by the end of the tax year;
   - `Assessee.ResidentialStatus` collapses to the context's boolean `resident`, where RNOR's
     treatment is a legal-reading question;
   - it happens in a type the row doesn't list.
4. **Authoring any new rule changes the law layer.** That means a lock bump (`2026-27.law.5` →
   `law.6`) and regenerated `RuleIds`.

### A. Proposed split (recommended)

- **`W-046` (this item), the node as the design specifies it:**
  - `TaxByBasisNode`: `produces = TAX_BYBASIS`; `requires = {ROUND_TOTALINCOME}`;
    `rules = {RATE_SLAB_NEW_TAX_REGIME, RATE_SLAB_FIRST_SCHEDULE}`.
  - `evaluate`: read the rounded TI; take the regime's `rateTable` row; `versionAt(temporal)`; apply
    its `SlabLadder` to `CapContext.of(ladder.base(), ti)`; return
    `TaxByBasis.atSlabRatesOnly(tax)` with one `StepKind.TAX` line carrying the version's provenance.
  - **No pack, lock or `RuleIds` change.** Both ladders are already in the pack.
- **New `W-046b` (before `W-053`), the age bands:**
  - the two ladders with their conditions, both tax years;
  - how the node selects a ladder (B1 below);
  - a `ConditionContext` built from `EngineInputs` (finding 3);
  - the lock bump.

  Each of its decisions gets its own plan and review.

The alternative is to keep all of it here, which about doubles the item and puts findings 2 and 3 in
this plan.

### B. Decisions I will bring to `W-046b` (listed now so you can object early)

- **B1:** the regime keeps `rateTable` as the ladder that applies when no conditional ladder does. A
  conditional ladder whose conditions hold replaces it, and **more than one holding is an error**, not
  a pick.
- **B2:** age for AGE-001 = completed years on the **last day of the tax year**, which is what "at any
  time during the tax year" means for the highest age reached. It's derived from the date of birth
  and `TemporalContext`, and needs a sourced reading.
- **B3:** RNOR → `resident = true`, to be sourced from the Act before it's written.
