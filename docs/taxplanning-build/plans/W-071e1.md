## Plan for W-071e1 — meals and gifts

**What this item is for (one sentence):** value free food and non-alcoholic beverages and gifts, vouchers or tokens an
employer gives, for both tax years, with each year's own limits.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C6); nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-071e1` row** (D-116). `DR 01` `PERQ-MEAL-2026`, `PERQ-GIFT-2026`, `PERQ-GIFT-BOUNDARY-001` (*"At exactly
  ₹15,000 the **whole** amount is a perquisite, not the excess"*).
- **TY 2026-27, S4 r.15(5) Table IV** (Gazette 271092, p.1546, read here), for every employee (s.17(1)(e); D-116 C2):
  - **Sl. 3**, free food and non-alcoholic beverages: *"the amount of expenditure incurred by such employer as reduced
    by the amount, if any, paid or recovered from the employee"*. Column D: *"This provision shall not apply to (a)
    free food and non-alcoholic beverages provided by such employer during working hours at office or business
    premises or through paid vouchers usable only at eating joints, to the extent the value thereof in either case
    does not exceed ₹200 per meal; or (b) tea or snacks provided during working hours; or (c) free food and
    non-alcoholic beverages during working hours provided in a remote area or an off-shore installation."*
  - **Sl. 4**, *"any gift, or voucher, or token (in lieu of gift) received by the employee or by member of his
    household on ceremonial occasions or otherwise from the employer"*: *"the sum equal to the amount of such gift"*;
    *"'nil', if the value of such gift, voucher or token … is below ₹ 15,000 in aggregate during the tax year"*.
  - **No new-regime restriction on vouchers**: S4 and S2 searched in full for "voucher", "free food" and
    "non-alcoholic" — the only hits are Table IV and unrelated TDS forms (READ, text search).
- **TY 2025-26, Rules 1962 r.3(7)(iii)–(iv)** (consolidated rule 3, pp.3–4, V4):
  - (iii) the same valuation; first proviso *"to the extent the value thereof in either case does not exceed **fifty
    rupees per meal**"*, tea or snacks, remote area or off-shore installation;
  - second proviso (G.S.R. 452(E), 21-6-2023, **read in the Gazette**, 246719): *"the provisions of the first proviso
    in respect of free food and non-alcoholic beverage provided by the employer through paid voucher shall not apply
    to an employee … who has exercised an option under sub-section (5) of section 115BAC or whose income is chargeable
    to tax under sub-section (1A) of section 115BAC"* — the new regime;
  - (iv) *"Provided that where the value of such gift, voucher or token … is below **five thousand rupees** in the
    aggregate during the previous year, the value of perquisite shall be taken as 'nil'"*.
- **Code:** `PerquisiteNode` (accommodation, vehicles). `RegimeApplicability` has `ExceptIn`, as
  `SAL-TAX-ON-EMPLOYMENT` uses. `PerUnitPerPeriod` and `AmountInExcess` exist.

### Findings

1. **Meals are exempt "to the extent" of the limit per meal** — only the excess over ₹200 (or fifty rupees) per meal
   is salary, per meal, so meals of different values cannot average each other out.
2. **The voucher bar is TY 2025-26 only and regime-dependent** — a row that does not apply in the new regime says it
   in data (`ExceptIn`), as the tax on employment does.
3. **Gifts are all-or-nothing:** below the limit nil; at or above it the whole aggregate (`PERQ-GIFT-BOUNDARY-001`).
4. **"From the employer" … "in aggregate during the tax year"** — with two employers, whether each employer's gifts
   are tested separately or all together is not stated.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — meal input:** `EnteredFacts.meals`, each an **`EmployerMeals`**: the employment; the setting
  (`AT_WORK`, `PAID_VOUCHER`, `TEA_OR_SNACKS_AT_WORK`, `AT_WORK_REMOTE_OR_OFFSHORE`, `OTHERWISE`); the number of
  meals; the employer's cost per meal; the employee's payment per meal. One entry per meal value, so a year with two
  meal prices is two entries.
- **C2 — meal rows**, `AllRegimes` unless stated, both years:
  - `PERQ-MEAL` — Sl. 3's valuation: 100% of the value (cost less payment), for `OTHERWISE`;
  - `PERQ-MEAL-AT-WORK` — `AmountInExcess` of the value over `PerUnitPerPeriod` ₹200 / fifty rupees per meal;
  - `PERQ-MEAL-VOUCHER` — the same shape; its **TY 2025-26 version `ExceptIn` `NEW_TAX_REGIME`**. Where it does not
    apply, vouchers are valued whole under `PERQ-MEAL` (finding 2);
  - tea or snacks and remote-area food: a nil line citing `PERQ-MEAL`, naming the exclusion.
- **C3 — gift input:** `EnteredFacts.gifts`, each an **`EmployerGift`**: the employment and the amount (a gift,
  voucher or token, to the employee or a household member).
- **C4 — the gift test (finding 4):**
  - **(a) recommended:** aggregate **per employer** ("from the employer") — nil if below the limit, else the whole.
  - **(b)** aggregate across all employers.
- **C5 — gift row:** `PERQ-GIFT`, a `Fixed` limit per year (₹15,000 / five thousand rupees) compared **strictly
  below**, as `PERQ-SPECIFIED-EMPLOYEE` compares strictly above; the node values the whole when the test fails.
- **C6 — verification:** TY 2026-27 `SOURCE_CHECKED`; TY 2025-26 `UNVERIFIED` (the first proviso and (iv) rest on the
  consolidation), `PERQ-MEAL-VOUCHER`'s bar citing the Gazette-read G.S.R. 452(E).
