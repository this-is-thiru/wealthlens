## Plan for W-075d3 — Chapter VIII, TY 2025-26: 80DD, 80DDB, 80U

**What this item is for (one sentence):** give the six rows W-075c1 built their TY 2025-26 versions under 1961
ss.80DD, 80DDB and 80U, so disability and specified-disease claims compute for TY 2025-26. 1961 s.80DD allows a fixed
sum, not "up to" one.

**This plan has decisions C1–C4; nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read

- **The record** `sources/2026-10-07-chapter-via.md`:
  - **B14 (80DD, A0 pp.343–344):** a resident individual or HUF who has incurred expenditure for a disabled
    dependant's treatment, training and rehabilitation, or paid or deposited under an approved scheme, *"shall … be
    allowed a deduction of a sum of seventy-five thousand rupees"*; *"one hundred and twenty-five thousand rupees"*
    for severe disability (Act 20 of 2015 s.20, w.e.f. 1-4-2016). **M9** (FA 2022 s.21) changes only the scheme's
    payout conditions.
  - **B15 (80DDB, pp.345–346):** *"the amount actually paid or a sum of forty thousand rupees, whichever is less"*,
    *"one hundred thousand rupees"* for a senior citizen (Act 13 of 2018 s.27), reduced by insurance or employer
    reimbursement.
  - **B18 (80U, pp.425–426):** a resident individual certified at any time in the year, *"a deduction of a sum of
    seventy-five thousand rupees"*, 1,25,000 severe.
  - No Act after 2019 amends 80DDB or 80U (W-075d0's scan).
- **The six rows:**
  - `DED-DISABILITY`, `-SEVERE` — `Fixed` 75,000 / 1,25,000, conditions `IsResident`, `IsCertifiedDisabled`;
  - `DED-DISABLED-DEPENDANT`, `-SEVERE` — `Fixed` 75,000 / 1,25,000;
  - `DED-SPECIFIED-DISEASE`, `-SENIOR` — `Fixed` 40,000 / 1,00,000, the senior one with `AgeAtLeast 60`.

  All TY 2026-27 only. They have no components.
- **`DisabilityDeduction.claimed`** computes `Money.min(entered, limit)` for both s.127 and s.128 — the "lesser of"
  lives in the node, the rows hold only the limit. `DisabledDependantSupport` accepts an amount of nil.

### Findings

1. **80U and 80DDB carry over unchanged:** fixed 75,000 / 1,25,000; the lesser of net paid and 40,000 / 1,00,000.
2. **80DD does not.** 1961 s.80DD gives *"a deduction of a sum of"* 75,000 once its condition holds (expenditure
   incurred, or an amount paid or deposited) — a fixed sum, as 80U is. The 2025 Act's s.127 says *"a deduction up to
   ₹ 75000"*, which W-075c1 built as the lesser of the amount spent and 75,000 (D-138 C2a). OQ-19 asks which reading
   s.127 bears. **The 1961 wording is evidence for OQ-19, not an answer to it,** so TY 2026-27 is left as built.
3. **The node cannot hold both readings while the "lesser of" is its own code.** With the 1961 versions it would cap
   80DD at the amount spent in TY 2025-26 — wrong under the 1961 words.
4. **Nothing spent.** Under a fixed sum, an entry of nil must give nothing: the condition is to have *"incurred any
   expenditure … or paid or deposited any amount"*. Today nil gives nil only because of the `min`.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — where "the lesser of" lives:**
  - **(a) recommended:** move it into the rows. The TY 2026-27 s.127 and s.128 rows become `LeastOf` of *"the amount
    claimed"* (`PercentOfBase` 100 of `AMOUNT_CLAIMED`) and the limit (`Fixed`) — the same results, now stated in
    data.
  - The TY 2025-26 80DD rows are a plain `Fixed` 75,000 / 1,25,000; the TY 2025-26 80DDB rows are `LeastOf`, as
    1961 says.
  - `claimed` applies the row's cap to the amount and takes its `limitedBy`.
  - **(b)** Keep the `min` in code and branch on the tax year — an engine fork on law the data should carry.
- **C2 — nothing spent (finding 4):** in both years, an 80DD entry of nil gives a nil line, *"nothing spent or
  deposited"*, before the cap.
- **C3 — the versions:** TY 2025-26 versions of all six rows — the same conditions and regimes, 1961 provenance (A0
  pages and their footnoted Acts), `SOURCE_CHECKED`, the D-144 residual. The 2026 notes' "unread (W-075d)" are
  corrected, and OQ-19's STATE row gets the 1961 wording as evidence.
- **C4 — records:**
