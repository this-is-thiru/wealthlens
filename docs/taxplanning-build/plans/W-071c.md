## Plan for W-071c — motor car and other conveyances, and the specified employee

**What this item is for (one sentence):** value the use of a car (or other conveyance) an employer provides or pays
for, per calendar month, for both tax years — and decide, with the ₹4,00,000 test, which employees an employer's
car is a perquisite for at all.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C8). Three of them (C1, C3, C5) are readings of the law no text settles;
nothing is coded until you choose.**

### What I read

- **The `W-071c` row** (D-110, D-112): S4 r.15(3) Table II; Rules 1962 r.3(2); the specified employee — S2 s.17(1)(c),
  S4 r.17 (₹4,00,000); 1961 s.17(2)(iii), r.3C (₹4,00,000). `DR 01` `PERQ-CAR-*`, `PERQ-SPECEMP-001`.
- **TY 2026-27, S4 r.15(3)** (Gazette 271092, pp.1542–1543, read here). Table II, **"per calendar month"**; column C
  is *"cubic capacity of engine does not exceed 1.6 litres **or the motor car is an electric vehicle**"*, column D
  *"exceeds 1.6 litres"*:
  - **Sl. 1**, owned or hired by the employer — (a) wholly official: no value *"if the documents specified in
    sub-rule (3)(c) are maintained"*; (b) wholly private, employer meets running: actual running and maintenance
    incl. chauffeur, *"increased by the amount representing normal wear and tear … and as reduced by any amount
    charged"*; (c)(i) partly private, employer meets running: **₹5000 / ₹7000 (plus ₹3000 if a chauffeur)**;

  - **Sl. 2**, the employee's car, employer meets running — (a) wholly official: no value with documents;
    (b) partly: *"the actual amount of expenditure … as reduced by the amount specified in Sl. No. (1)(c)(i)"*,

  - **Sl. 3**, the employee's other automotive conveyance — (a) wholly official: no value (the text cites
    "sub-rule (2)(c)", evidently (3)(c)); (b) partly: actual expenditure less **₹3000**.
  - **(3)(b)** several employer cars not wholly official: one at Sl. (1)(c)(i), the others at (1)(b).
  - **(3)(c)** a higher official share, on journey records and an employer certificate.
  - **(3)(d)** wear and tear **10% a year of the actual cost**.
- **TY 2025-26, Rules 1962 r.3(2)** (the Department's consolidated rule 3 — `sources/…-perquisite-thresholds.md` V2;
  a consolidation, no currency date): the same structure, **no electric-vehicle wording**; (1)(c)(i) **Rs. 1,800 /
  Rs. 2,400 (plus Rs. 900 chauffeur)**; (1)(c)(ii) **Rs. 600 / Rs. 900 (plus Rs. 900)**; Sl. 3(ii) less **Rs. 900**;
  the several-cars proviso; clause (B); wear and tear 10%.
- **The specified employee:** S2 s.17(1)(c) (Gazette 265620 p.38): *"(i) by a company to an employee, who is a

### Findings

1. **Neither Rule says whom the car is a perquisite for.** It values "use of motor car … by an employer". The Act
   decides that: a car the employer owns or hires is a "benefit or amenity" — s.17(1)(c), so a perquisite only for
   a director, a substantial-interest holder of a company employer, or an employee over ₹4,00,000. The employer
   meeting the running of the **employee's own** car pays the employee's obligation — s.17(1)(f) (1961
   s.17(2)(iv)) — a perquisite for everyone. That split is a reading (`INFERENCE`), not a text.
2. **The ₹4,00,000 measure is "income under the head Salaries by way of monetary payment"** — after the s.19
   deductions ("income under the head"), without non-monetary perquisites. The perquisite node runs before gross
   salary and the deductions, so the measure cannot be the pipeline's own income figure.
3. **TY 2025-26 has no electric-vehicle column** and an electric car has no cubic capacity.
4. **"Per calendar month"** — a car for part of a month: the Rules don't say whether that month counts.
5. **Wholly official without the documents** — the Table gives "no value, if the documents … are maintained" and
   says nothing for when they are not.
6. **The amounts differ by year** (₹1,800 → ₹5,000 etc.), and H-2 already flags this exact trap. Rows by year.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — who (finding 1):**
  - **(a) recommended:** Sl. 1 (employer's car) is valued only for a **specified employee** (s.17(1)(c)); Sl. 2
    and 3 (employee's own vehicle, employer pays) for everyone (s.17(1)(f)). Recorded as a proposed **OQ-10**,
    with a note on each row.
  - **(b)** Value every car for everyone. Overstates tax for employees at or under ₹4,00,000.
- **C2 — the specified-employee facts:** `Employment` gains `isDirector`, `hasSubstantialInterest` and
  **`employerIsCompany`** — limb (i) reads *"by a company"*. A new flag rather than a `COMPANY` value on
  `EmployerKind`, whose one reader (1961 s.16(ii)) asks only Government or not.
- **C3 — the monetary-salary measure (finding 2):**
  - **(a) recommended:** a new node **`salary.monetaryincome`** before `salary.perquisites`: the pay lines under the
    head (no perquisites), less the exemptions, less the retirement deductions, less the s.19 deductions applied
    to that pay (professional tax; the standard deduction capped at that pay). `salary.retirement` moves before
    `salary.perquisites` (it reads only components and ledgers). The standard deduction is read from its own rows,
    so a figure is never restated. The test: `Not(IncomeAtMost(salary.monetaryincome, 400000))` — "exceeds".
    Summed across employments ("from one or more employers").
  - **(b)** Before the s.19 deductions (pay less exemptions). Simpler; makes more people specified between
    ₹4,00,000 and ₹4,75,000.
  - **(c)** The taxpayer states whether they are a specified employee.
- **C4 — input:** `EnteredFacts.vehicles`, each an **`EmployerVehicle`**: the employment; whose it is
  (`EMPLOYER_CAR` owned or hired / `EMPLOYEE_CAR` / `EMPLOYEE_OTHER_CONVEYANCE`); the use (`WHOLLY_OFFICIAL` /
  `WHOLLY_PRIVATE` / `PARTLY_OFFICIAL`); who meets private running for a partly-official employer car; the engine
  (`UP_TO_1600_CC_OR_ELECTRIC` / `ABOVE_1600_CC`, C5); a chauffeur; **calendar months** provided (C6); the
  employer's running and maintenance expenditure; the car's actual cost (wear and tear); the amount charged; and
  the (3)(c) claim — records kept, certificate given, the higher official amount. Several employer cars not wholly
  official are valued per (3)(b) by the node, the first entered at (1)(c)(i).
- **C5 — an electric car in TY 2025-26 (finding 3):**
  - **(a) recommended:** refused for TY 2025-26 with the reason, until a source says how r.3(2) reads one.
  - **(b)** Treat it as ≤1.6 litres, as the 2026 Rules do — carries a 2026 rule back a year (the H-2 trap).
- **C6 — part months (finding 4):** the taxpayer enters the number of calendar months the car was provided;
  the engine does not apportion inside a month. A month count outside 0–12 is refused.
- **C7 — wholly official without records (finding 5):** refused, naming the documents, rather than valued on a
  row the Table does not state.
- **C8 — rows**, `AllRegimes`, one version per year, ids final at build (segments ≤ 12 characters):
  - `PERQ-CAR-PARTLY-SMALL` / `-PARTLY-LARGE` — Sl. (1)(c)(i), `PerUnitPerPeriod` per month (1,800 / 2,400;
    5,000 / 7,000);
  - `PERQ-CAR-OWNRUN-SMALL` / `-OWNRUN-LARGE` — (1)(c)(ii) (600 / 900; 2,000 / 3,000);
  - `PERQ-CAR-CHAUFFEUR` — plus per month (900; 3,000);
  - `PERQ-CAR-PRIVATE` — (1)(b) wear and tear, 10% of cost (the running is entered);
  - `PERQ-CAR-EMPLOYEE-SMALL` / `-LARGE` — Sl. 2(b), `AmountInExcess` of the expenditure over the (1)(c)(i) figure;
  - `PERQ-CAR-CONVEYANCE` — Sl. 3(b), expenditure over 900 / 3,000 per month;
  - `PERQ-SPECEMP` — the threshold, as the condition on the Sl. 1 rows (C1a, C3a);
  - TY 2025-26 `UNVERIFIED` (V1 consolidation; the three unread Acts for s.17(2)); TY 2026-27 `SOURCE_CHECKED`.

### Risks or ambiguities

- C1 and C3 are readings, to be recorded as OQ-10 if you agree; the CBDT's salary-TDS circulars likely speak to C1
  but none is read (incometaxindia).
- Sl. 2(b) deducts "the amount specified in Sl. No. (1)(c)(i)": whether that includes the chauffeur add-on when the
  employer also pays a chauffeur is not stated. Recommended: the base figure, plus the add-on only where the
  entered expenditure includes a chauffeur's pay.
- The TY 2025-26 figures rest on a Departmental consolidation with no "as amended up to" date.
- This is a large item (≈10 rows, a node, an input, a measure node). **Alternative:** split the specified
  employee (C1–C3) into its own item placed before the car — but it would then have no reader until the car lands
  (the D-112 C1 objection). Recommended: keep together.

## As built — departures from the plan (for review)
- **C2 as one fact, not three:** `Employment.companyEmployer` is an `Optional<CompanyEmployer>` (director,
  substantial interest), present only where the employer is a company — "director of a non-company" cannot be
  entered. Same test as the plan.
- **C5 through data, not a year in code:** `CarEngine` has `ELECTRIC`, and two rows exist for TY 2026-27 only —
  `PERQ-CAR-PARTLY-ELECTRIC` (₹5,000) and `PERQ-CAR-OWNRUN-ELECTRIC` (₹2,000), column C's figures. TY 2025-26's pack
  has no electric row, so an electric car is refused there (C5a), as `SAL-ENTMT-ALLOWANCE` is absent from TY 2026-27.
- **Rows, 11 (not ≈10):** `PERQ-SPECIFIED-EMPLOYEE` (a `Fixed` ₹4,00,000 compared strictly, "exceeds" — not a row
  condition, since director and substantial interest are not conditions the pack can state); `PERQ-CAR-PARTLY-SMALL`,
  `-LARGE`, `-ELECTRIC`; `-OWNRUN-SMALL`, `-LARGE`, `-ELECTRIC`; `-CHAUFFEUR`; `-PRIVATE` (the wear and tear;
  running added and charge taken off by the node); **one** `PERQ-CAR-EMPLOYEE` for Sl. 2(b) (not SMALL/LARGE: the
  engine only picks the (1)(c)(i) amount handed in as `TABLE_OFFICIAL_USE`); `PERQ-CAR-CONVEYANCE`. The last two are
  `LeastOf` two `AmountInExcess` limbs — the Table's amount, or a higher official use claimed on records.
- **Wear and tear is apportioned to the months provided** (cost × months / 12, then 10%), because Table II is "per
  calendar month". The plan's hand figure (12 months) is unchanged. **Reviewer: confirm.**
- **An amount charged to the employee is read only by Sl. 1(b)** — the only row that says "as reduced by any amount
  charged". Entered against any other row, it is refused rather than ignored.
- **Sl. 2(b)'s fixed reduction is taken without the (3)(c) records.** TY 2026-27's text adds "if the conditions
  mentioned in sub-rule (3)(c) are fulfilled" to Sl. 2(b); read with (3)(c) — which is about claiming *more* than the
  Table — as governing the higher claim. **Reviewer: confirm.**
- **An employee's own car or conveyance used wholly privately** is refused (the Table has no such row), as is a
  chauffeur for a conveyance.
- **The several-cars proviso applies only where two or more** of one employer's cars are used otherwise than wholly
  officially (as `PERQ-CAR-MULTI-001` reads it); with one car, its own row.
- **A wholly official vehicle with records has no trace line** (nil, nothing to explain); a non-specified employee's
  employer car has a nil line citing `PERQ-SPECIFIED-EMPLOYEE` and the figure tested.
- **Shared code, not restated:** `GrossSalaryNode.pay` and `SalaryDeductionNode.on` / `RULES` are package-private
  helpers the monetary-income node reuses; `MotorCarPerquisite` is a package-private helper of `PerquisiteNode`.
- **`salary.retirement` moved before `salary.perquisites`** in both regimes' pipelines (it reads only components and
  ledgers). `GeneratedConstantsTest`'s shipped-rule list moved to a constant (Checkstyle's 60-line method limit).
- **Locks:** law 2025-26.law.21 / 2026-27.law.28, exec 2025-26.exec.17 / 2026-27.exec.18.
