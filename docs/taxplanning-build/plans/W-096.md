### Tests, by hand

`Pan`: `ABCDE1234F` accepted, masked `ABCXXXXX4F` (F1's substring rule: first three, then `XXXXX`, then from index 8); refused: lowercase, nine and eleven characters, digit in the letters. A duplicate PAN → 400, its message masked. Two profiles under one account → both stored. A second user reading the first's profile → refused, as `/tax/user/{email}` scopes it (checked against how `AuthFilter` treats another user's email at build). A duplicate `{profile_id, tan}` → 400. A log capture on a refused PAN holds no full PAN. **Bite checks:** the unique index off; the owner check off; the mask off. Each tier once.
