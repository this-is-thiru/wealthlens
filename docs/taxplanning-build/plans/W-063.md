## Plan for W-063 — the deductions from salary: tax on employment and the standard deduction

**What this item is for (one sentence):** `salary.deductions` becomes what s.19(1) lets a salaried taxpayer
subtract under the head: the tax on employment (professional tax), in full, but never in the new regime; and
the standard deduction, which is ₹75,000 or the salary, whichever is less, in the new regime, and ₹50,000 or
the salary, whichever is less, in any other case.

**This plan raises design decisions (C1–C4) before anything is built.**

### Findings

1. **Professional tax would be counted in gross salary.** As a pay line it has to sit under a head, and s.19
   puts it under SALARIES. `GrossSalaryNode` sums the whole head, so the first shipped deduction component
   would *raise* gross salary by the tax paid.
   - The registry already carries the statutory link D-089 intended: salary components `feedsInto`
     `SAL-SALARY-INCLUDES`; professional tax would feed its own rule.
2. **The row assignments collide.**
   - D-089 gives the standard-deduction rows to `W-064` and professional tax to `W-063`, but the node computes
     both.
   - Built alone, the node would be half a node, and `W-064` (a law-rows item) would have to reopen its code.
3. **"Entire amount" has no row shape.** A tax-on-employment row that isn't a cap, a relief, a rounding or a
   condition would be refused (`RuleJson`). But it *is* a total: the lines that feed it.
4. **The new-regime rule differs in kind for the two deductions.**
   - The standard deduction is regime-keyed *in s.19 itself* ("where income-tax is computed under section
     202(1)"), so each version is regime-scoped.
   - Professional tax is removed only by s.202(2)(a)(iv), a disallowance. The design's mechanism for that
     (`DIS-S202-2A`) needs a basis row and run-time application, and neither exists.
5. **"The salary" in Sl. 2** is gross salary (s.16's definition, `salary.gross`), supplied to the cap as a
   base. That is what makes limb (b) bind for a part-year joiner.

### Choices (recommended first)

- **C1 — keep professional tax out of gross salary.**
  - **(a) recommended: a head total counts only the head's lines that feed the defining rule.**
    - `HeadTotal` keeps its shape, but its meaning becomes "the total of the head's lines whose component
      `feedsInto` this rule".
    - `GrossSalaryNode` filters the partition's SALARIES lines by the registry.
    - This modifies `W-061`'s node and `HeadTotal`'s contract. No shipped figure moves, because no component
      ships yet.
  - **(b)** Exclude by `TaxTreatment.DEDUCTIBLE`. It's smaller, but it reads an attribute rather than the
    declared statutory link, and a future non-income line under SALARIES that isn't `DEDUCTIBLE` would leak
    in.
- **C2 — scope.**
  - **(a) recommended: W-063 authors both Sl. 1 and Sl. 2 rows, and the whole node.** `W-064` keeps the
    cash-salary components and the entertainment-allowance row. The `SAL-STDDED-*` assignment moves from
    `W-064` to here, which amends D-089.
  - **(b)** W-063 does professional tax only, and `W-064` adds the standard deduction to the node. D-089 is
    kept, but the node is built in two passes.
- **C3 — the rows** (D-035 names; none found in `RuleIds`):

  | Row | Shape | Regimes |
  |---|---|---|
  | `SAL-TAX-ON-EMPLOYMENT` | `definition: HeadTotal(SALARIES)` (C1a makes it "the SALARIES lines feeding this rule", i.e. professional tax) | `ExceptIn [NEW_TAX_REGIME]`, citing s.202(2)(a)(iv) |
  | `SAL-STANDARD-DEDUCTION-NEW-TAX-REGIME` | `LeastOf`: "statutory amount" `Fixed ₹75,000`, "salary" `PercentOfBase 100%` of base `SALARY` | `OnlyIn [NEW_TAX_REGIME]` |
  | `SAL-STANDARD-DEDUCTION-ANY-OTHER-CASE` | the same with ₹50,000 | `ExceptIn [NEW_TAX_REGIME]` |

  - `SAL-STDDED-003` becomes the `LeastOf`'s second limb and gets no row of its own (`DR 05` erratum W-013).
  - Component `PROFESSIONAL_TAX`, both years: head SALARIES, kind `STATUTORY_DEDUCTION`, `CASH_OUTFLOW`
    ("sum paid by the assessee"), `DEDUCTIBLE`, `feedsInto` `SAL-TAX-ON-EMPLOYMENT`.
- **C4 — professional tax in the new regime.**
  - **(a) recommended:** the row's `ExceptIn [NEW_TAX_REGIME]` carries it. The node evaluates only the rows
    that apply in the regime, so in the new regime professional tax contributes nothing and leaves no line.
    `DIS-S202-2A` (and the "disallowed because s.202(2)(a)(iv)" explanation) is recorded as owed in Appendix F,
    to land with the first item that applies disallowances. `NEW_TAX_REGIME`'s pending note is updated to say
    so.
  - **(b)** Build `DIS-S202-2A` now: a basis row with some shape, plus run-time disallowance application in
    the pipeline. That is a large, cross-cutting change for one line.

**The node** (either way): `salary.deductions` (`MoneyValue`) requires `salary.gross` and `inputs.components`.
- It evaluates, in order, the tax-on-employment row and then the regime's standard-deduction row, each only
  where it applies.
- Each gives one DEDUCTION line citing its row. The standard deduction's line names the limb that bound.
- The value is their sum.

### BLOCKED at step 2 (R6) — the TY 2025-26 new-regime standard deduction

  deduction is ₹50,000, not the rulebook's ₹75,000.**
