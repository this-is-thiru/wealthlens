## Plan for W-090a2 — Declarations, the draft

**What this item is for (one sentence, in my own words):** a person can see the questions TY 2026-27's return asks and
the engine cannot compute, answer them in their draft (`/filing/declarations`), and have an answer the year does not ask,
or one of the wrong kind, refused when they save it, not only later when they compute.

**Findings (each read this session, Gazette copies fetched 2026-11-09):**
- F1, the clauses. r.164(3) for ITR-1 and r.164(6) for ITR-4 (Gazette 271092 pp.1662 – 1663). r.164(1) limits the
  rule to "the tax year commencing on the 1st April, 2026".
- F2, the three clauses D-517 left unread, all read in the 2025 Act (Gazette 265620). Each is a fact the engine cannot
  compute, so each becomes a row and none moves to W-090c:
  - (l) s.393(3) Table Sl. 5 (p.409): tax deducted at 2% by a bank, co-operative bank or post office on cash paid
    out of a person's accounts above ₹1 crore (₹3 crore for a co-operative society). No engine input carries it.
  - (m) s.391(2) and s.392(3): payment or deduction of tax on s.17(1)(d) income (a specified security or sweat equity
    share) from an eligible start-up employer, deferred to s.289(3). `EmployerShareAllotment` has no start-up or
    deferment fact (grep `start-up|defer` in it finds nothing).
  - (n) Chapter XIII Part A, ss.190 – 195 (pp.214 – 219):
    - s.190: relief on income on which no tax is payable.
    - s.191: the provident-fund accumulated balance.
    - s.192: block assessment.
    - s.193: Global Depository Receipts.
    - s.194 Table: winnings, patent royalty, carbon credits, virtual digital assets, online games, life insurance
      profits.
    - s.195: ss.102 – 106 income.
    - The engine refuses winnings (`OtherSourceKind.java:94` – `95`), and no rule in the pack names any of these
      incomes (grep `lottery|winnings|"194` in `law/rules` finds none).
- F3, two clauses that are not single facts:
  - (3)(i) is "relief of tax under section 159 or deduction of tax under section 160". These are two different facts:
    - s.159 is relief under a double-taxation agreement (p.187).
    - s.160 is the deduction for tax paid in a country with no agreement (p.189).
  - (6)(l) reads "deduction of tax under clause (d) of sub-section (1) of the said". s.160(1) has no clause (d), so the
    clause cannot be read as printed. That is W-090c's problem; the facts here stay the same.
  - (3)(m) and (6)(n) word the same deferment differently: "deferred" against "payable or deductible under". Both
    describe one fact.
- F4, the draft:
  - The draft stores `DraftInputs` as JSON, read strictly (`DraftInputsMapper.java:24` – `40`).
  - A patch is applied, then bound (`DraftService.java:130` – `131`). Nothing validates it against the pack today: an
    unknown component code is refused only at compute.
  - `DraftInputs.Filing` builds the engine's `FilingFacts` (`DraftInputs.java:51` – `59`) and passes `Map.of()` for
    declarations (W-090a1).
  - A sealed engine type is named in JSON through a mixin, as `FairMarketValueBasis` is (`DraftInputsMapper.java:45` –
    `52`).
  - The draft knows its year (`FilingDraft.taxYearStart`, `:36`).
- F5, the boundary. `taxplanning` sees only `taxengine`'s named interfaces. `TaxCalculator` already serves pack
  facts by year (`defaultRegime`, `packVersions`, `TaxCalculator.java:117`, `:145`). The declaration catalogue is not
  served yet.
- F6, a defect in W-090a1's run-time refusal. It prints the answer itself (`"is answered " + answer`, in
  `NodeContext.declarationsOf`), so an amount reaches the message. `DR 14` O1.1 (`DraftInputsMapper.java:19` – `20`)
  says a refusal names where, never the value. This item fixes it by naming the answer's kind instead.

**Choices for the owner:**
- **C1a (recommended): refuse on save.**
  - A patch whose `/filing/declarations` names a question the draft's year does not ask, or answers it in the wrong
    kind, is refused with 400, naming the question.
  - The check is the engine's own: W-090a1's check moves from `NodeContext` into one `pipeline` class, and
    `TaxCalculator.requireAsked(year, answers)` exposes it. One rule, two callers, no copy.
  - C1b: refuse only at compute, as component codes are today.
- **C2a (recommended): a read endpoint for the questions.**
  - `GET /tax/user/{email}/declarations/{taxYear}` returns each question: code, question, answer kind, source.
  - A person cannot answer questions they cannot see, and the codes are pack data, not something to hard-code in a
    client.
  - C2b: no endpoint; the api-collection lists the codes.
- **C3a (recommended): each row is valid only for 2026-04-01 – 2027-03-31,** the year r.164(1) names. TY 2025-26's
  questions come from 1962 r.12 (W-090d). A later year's Rules extend a row only once read. C3b: open-ended from
  2026-04-01.

- Automation tester, with expected values worked out by hand:

  `GET` the draft to see it back. A code such as `NOT_ASKED` gives 400. Edge cases: a 2025 draft (no questions), an
  empty map, and a negative amount (400).
