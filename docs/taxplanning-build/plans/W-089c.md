## Plan for family B, second part — W-089c (advance tax) and W-112 (interest and the late-return fee)

**For:** today a user who paid too little advance tax, or files late, sees a balance with no interest and no fee, and nothing
says so: `DraftComputationService.NOT_INCLUDED` (`:26-30`) names neither, so the figure is **too low, silently**. This family
adds what the user owes for advance tax, by instalment, and the interest and fee on the return. Sources:
`sources/2026-11-06-advance-tax-interest.md` (39 rows; the 2025 Act and Rules 2026 in the Gazette, the 1961 Act in thc with
every amendment since searched).

### What the reading found

1. **The law moved after the copies the repo designed from.** FA 2026 s.96 replaced 2025 s.428 from 1-4-2026: the fee is now
   a fixed ₹1,000 (income ≤ ₹5,00,000) or ₹5,000, not "not exceeding ₹1,000". FA 2021 s.60 had already made 1961 s.234F a flat
   ₹5,000 (≤ ₹5,00,000: at most ₹1,000); thc still shows ₹5,000 / ₹10,000. FA 2021 s.59 widened s.234C's carve-out to dividend
   income. `DR 15` C1's table and sketch predate all three, and its sketch measures s.425 against assessed tax, which is wrong:
   s.425 uses *tax due on returned income* (row 19). Both are errata for `DR 15`.
2. **Three different bases, not one.** s.423 / 234A: tax **less advance tax**, TDS and reliefs. s.424 / 234B and s.425 / 234C:
   tax less TDS and reliefs, advance tax **not** deducted (rows 12, 13, 19). One shared "assessed" figure would be wrong twice.
3. **Rules 2026 r.269:** the base is rounded **down** to ₹100; "month or part of a month" counts a part as a whole month.
   2025 s.423 says only "number of months", so r.269(b) does not reach it by its words — **OQ-39**. The 1961 counterpart,
   Rules 1962 r.119A, has **no Gazette copy** in the repo (row 39).
4. **Every input exists already:** `FilingFacts` carries the due date, the furnishing date, dated advance-tax payments, TDS and
   dated self-assessment payments (`input/FilingFacts.java`); `EngineInputs.asOfDate()` (`:100`) gives the end date for a
   return not yet furnished; `Individual.dateOfBirth` gives the s.403(3) / 207(2) age. No new entered fact is needed.

### Choices (recommended first)

**C1 — scope.** **(a) Build** the liability and instalment schedule (s.403 – 406, 408 / 207 – 211), s.423 / 234A, s.424 / 234B,
s.425 / 234C without the carve-out, and s.428 / 234F. **Not built, each a notice and a `notIncluded` line:** the s.425(4) /
234C carve-out (`MAY_LOWER`; needs the tax on each income by instalment — place **W-112a**); an Assessing Officer's order
(s.407, s.409 / 210(3) – (6), 218; no fact can state one); s.426 / 234D and s.437 / 244A (they need a refund's dates — place
**W-112b**, not read this session); s.428(b) – (d) (revised return, audit, accountant's report: no such filing modelled).
(b) Build the carve-out now with capital gains' sale dates; dividends carry no date, so it would be half a rule.

**C2 — when nothing is filed.** **(a) Interest runs to `asOfDate`, as if filed and paid that day**, and the trace says so;
s.424(4) / 234B(2) stops it on each self-assessment payment at its date. (b) Refuse until a furnishing date is entered —
honest but useless for planning, which is the product.

**C3 — OQ-39, a part month under 2025 s.423.** **(a) Count it as a whole month**, as 1961 s.234A, 2025 s.424 and s.426 and
r.269(b) do; recorded as a reading, with an `ATTENTION` notice on the line. (b) Count whole months only, by the bare words.

**C4 — TY 2025-26 rounding without r.119A.** **(a) Apply r.269's base rounding and part-month rule to both years** and say on
the TY 2025-26 line that the 1962 rule is unread; you name the Department page trustworthy or it stays a reading.
(b) Block the TY 2025-26 rows until a Gazette r.119A is found.

**C5 — what is rounded.** **(a) `round.netpayable` rounds balance + interest + fee** (s.516 / 288B: "any amount payable"); the
three stay separate lines and `tax.netpayable` stays the bill, so elections are untouched (D-436 C1a). (b) Round only the tax.

### Worked by hand — TY 2025-26 (1961 Act)

Bill ₹1,00,000; TDS ₹20,000; advance tax ₹30,000 on 15-9-2025 and ₹20,000 on 15-3-2026; due 31-7-2026; filed and paid
31-8-2026; total income above ₹5,00,000; under 60.
- **Liable:** 1,00,000 − 20,000 = 80,000 ≥ 10,000. Schedule: 12,000 / 36,000 / 60,000 / 80,000 cumulative.
- **234B:** assessed 80,000; paid 50,000 < 72,000 (90 %); 30,000 × 1 % × 5 months (April – August) = **1,500**.
- **234C:** June 12,000 short, below 12 % (9,600) → 12,000 × 3 % = 360; September paid 30,000 ≥ 36 % (28,800) → nil; December
  30,000 × 3 % = 900; March 30,000 × 1 % = 300 → **1,560**.
- **234A:** 1,00,000 − 50,000 − 20,000 = 30,000 × 1 % × 1 month (August) = **300**. **234F:** **5,000**.
- **Payable:** 30,000 + 3,360 + 5,000 = **38,360** (already a multiple of ₹10).
- **Not filed, as of 6-11-2026 (C2a):** 234A 1-8 to 6-11 = 4 months → 1,200; 234B April to 6-11 = 8 months → 2,400; 234C 1,560;
  fee 5,000; payable **40,160**.
- **Edges:** 60 during the year, no business → not liable, 234B and 234C nil, payable **35,300**; bill − TDS ₹9,999 → not
  liable, ₹10,000 → liable; base ₹30,050 → interest on 30,000; filed on the due date → no 234A, no fee; income ₹5,00,000 →
  fee 1,000 (TY 2026-27 fixed; TY 2025-26 "at most", shown as that maximum with a notice). **TY 2026-27:** the same facts a year on give the same figures; filed 5-9-2027 → s.423 2 months under C3a.

### Five roles

- **Architect:** all in `taxengine` (no new module edge); law as rows; the three bases are three named measures, not one.
- **Product:** the planning user gets "pay ₹X by 15 December"; a late filer sees interest grow and a fee that does not. Old
  computations unchanged; a new one shows more, which is the fix. README and `api-collection` gain the response fields.
- **Engineer:** `FilingFacts` unchanged; no clock (`asOfDate` only); nodes under 60-line methods.
- **Automation:** each case above as a test written red first; bite checks: 90 % → 89 %, the 12 % harbour off, ₹100 rounding
  off, part month off — each must fail, then restored. Targeted, then unit and integration once each.
- **Manual:** compute a draft with the T-A facts over HTTP and expect `38,360`; the same unfiled, expect `40,160` on 6-11-2026.
